
# Project Plan Template: CloudFront Disaster Recovery Planning

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, ensuring the resilience of your cloud infrastructure is paramount. The CloudFront Disaster Recovery Planning project aims to develop a comprehensive disaster recovery plan specifically tailored for AWS CloudFront distributions. The primary objective is to identify potential risks and create strategies that will mitigate downtime during unexpected outages. This project will involve a thorough assessment of current configurations, determining critical assets, and establishing recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO). Key outcomes will include detailed procedures for data backup, failover mechanisms, and regular testing of the recovery plan to ensure its effectiveness. By the end of this project, clients will possess a robust disaster recovery plan that not only safeguards their CloudFront distributions but also enhances overall business continuity and operational resilience. This plan will empower organizations to respond swiftly to incidents, minimizing disruption and maintaining service availability.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the CloudFront Disaster Recovery Planning project includes the following key activities:

- **Risk Assessment**: Identify and analyze potential risks that could impact AWS CloudFront distributions.
- **Current Configuration Review**: Evaluate existing CloudFront configurations and associated resources.
- **Critical Asset Identification**: Determine which assets are critical for business operations and require prioritization in the recovery plan.
- **Establish RTO and RPO**: Define acceptable recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) for critical assets.
- **Backup Procedures**: Develop procedures for data backup, including frequency and storage solutions.
- **Failover Mechanisms**: Outline failover strategies to ensure continuity of service during outages.
- **Testing and Validation**: Create a schedule for regular testing of the disaster recovery plan to validate its effectiveness and make necessary adjustments.
- **Documentation**: Produce comprehensive documentation of the disaster recovery plan, including all procedures and protocols.
