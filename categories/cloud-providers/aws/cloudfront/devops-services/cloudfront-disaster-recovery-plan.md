
# CloudFront Disaster Recovery Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, ensuring business continuity is paramount, especially when leveraging cloud services like AWS CloudFront. The objective of this project is to develop a comprehensive disaster recovery plan tailored specifically for AWS CloudFront. This plan will outline strategies to mitigate risks associated with outages or failures, ensuring that your business can quickly recover and maintain operations. Key outcomes will include a thorough risk assessment, clearly defined recovery time objectives (RTO), and recovery point objectives (RPO). Additionally, the project will detail procedures for data backup and restoration, as well as guidelines for testing the disaster recovery plan to ensure its effectiveness. By the end of this project, you will have a robust, actionable plan that not only safeguards your operations but also enhances your overall cloud strategy. This plan will empower your organization to respond effectively to disruptions, minimizing downtime and protecting critical business functions.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of this project includes the following key activities:

- **Risk Assessment**: Identify potential risks and vulnerabilities associated with AWS CloudFront services.
- **Define RTO and RPO**: Establish clear recovery time objectives and recovery point objectives tailored to business needs.
- **Backup Procedures**: Develop detailed procedures for data backup, including frequency, storage solutions, and access protocols.
- **Restoration Procedures**: Outline step-by-step procedures for data restoration in the event of an outage or failure.
- **Testing Guidelines**: Create a framework for regularly testing the disaster recovery plan to ensure its effectiveness and relevance.
- **Documentation**: Compile all findings and procedures into a comprehensive disaster recovery plan document.
- **Training**: Provide training sessions for relevant stakeholders to ensure understanding and preparedness for executing the plan.
