
# Project Plan Template: AppStream 2.0 Pilot Program for SMBs

## 1. Executive Summary

The AWS AppStream 2.0 Pilot Program for small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) aims to empower organizations to explore and evaluate the capabilities of AWS AppStream 2.0. The primary objective of this project is to provide a structured evaluation process that allows participants to assess the potential benefits of cloud-based application and desktop delivery tailored to their unique needs. The program will begin with an initial consultation to understand the specific requirements of each business, followed by a strategic planning phase that outlines the implementation steps. Participants will receive hands-on support during the deployment of AppStream 2.0, ensuring a seamless transition and integration with their existing systems. Furthermore, ongoing operational support will be provided to monitor performance and optimize usage, enabling businesses to fully leverage the advantages of cloud technology. By the conclusion of the pilot program, SMBs will gain a comprehensive understanding of how AWS AppStream 2.0 can enhance their operational efficiency and drive productivity, ultimately positioning them for future growth and success.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the AppStream 2.0 Pilot Program includes the following key components:

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a thorough assessment of the SMB's current infrastructure, applications, and specific needs related to cloud-based solutions.
  
- **Strategic Planning**: Develop a customized implementation plan that outlines the steps necessary for deploying AWS AppStream 2.0, including timelines, resources, and responsibilities.

- **Deployment Support**: Provide hands-on assistance during the deployment phase, ensuring that the AppStream 2.0 service is integrated smoothly with the SMB's existing systems.

- **Training and Documentation**: Offer training sessions for staff to familiarize them with the AppStream 2.0 platform and provide comprehensive documentation for reference.

- **Ongoing Operational Support**: Monitor the performance of the deployed solution, offering optimization recommendations and troubleshooting assistance as needed.

- **Evaluation and Reporting**: At the end of the pilot program, conduct a review session to evaluate the outcomes and provide a detailed report on the benefits realized through the use of AWS AppStream 2.0.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completion
  - Deliverable: Consultation report outlining the SMB's needs and objectives.

- **Milestone 2**: Strategic Planning Document
  - Deliverable: Customized implementation plan with timelines and resource allocation.

- **Milestone 3**: Deployment Phase Initiation
  - Deliverable: Successful deployment of AppStream 2.0 with initial user access.

- **Milestone 4**: Training Sessions Completed
  - Deliverable: Training materials and session feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Ongoing Support Initiated
  - Deliverable: Support ticket system established and operational.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Evaluation and Reporting
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report detailing the pilot program outcomes and recommendations for future use.
