
# AppStream 2.0 DevOps Training Program Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The AppStream 2.0 DevOps Training Program aims to equip DevOps teams with the essential skills and knowledge required to efficiently manage and optimize AWS AppStream 2.0 environments. This comprehensive training program is designed to enhance the capabilities of participants through a blend of theoretical knowledge and practical, hands-on experience. Key objectives include mastering environment setup, application deployment, and performance optimization within AppStream 2.0. The program will feature expert-led sessions that explore advanced features, ensuring that teams can leverage the full potential of this powerful service. By the end of the training, participants will be adept at applying best practices, troubleshooting common issues, and enhancing collaboration and automation within their DevOps practices. Ultimately, the program aims to lead to faster and more reliable application deployment, significantly improving operational efficiency and effectiveness.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the AppStream 2.0 DevOps Training Program includes the following components:

- **Training Modules**: Development of training modules covering:
  - Environment setup
  - Application deployment
  - Performance optimization
  - Advanced features of AppStream 2.0

- **Hands-on Labs**: Creation of lab exercises that simulate real-world scenarios, allowing participants to practice:
  - Best practices for environment management
  - Troubleshooting common issues
  - Collaboration techniques within DevOps teams

- **Expert-led Sessions**: Organization of sessions led by industry experts to provide insights into:
  - Advanced AppStream 2.0 functionalities
  - Real-world case studies and applications

- **Assessment and Certification**: Implementation of assessments to evaluate participant understanding and provide certification upon successful completion of the program.

- **Feedback Mechanism**: Establishment of a feedback mechanism to gather participant insights for continuous improvement of the training program.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of training module development
  - **Deliverable**: Training materials and resources

- **Milestone 2**: Execution of hands-on labs
  - **Deliverable**: Lab exercises and participant feedback

- **Milestone 3**: Conducting expert-led sessions
  - **Deliverable**: Session recordings and participant evaluations

- **Milestone 4**: Final assessments and certification
  - **Deliverable**: Assessment results and certificates issued

- **Milestone 5**: Program review and feedback collection
  - **Deliverable**: Feedback report and recommendations for future training
