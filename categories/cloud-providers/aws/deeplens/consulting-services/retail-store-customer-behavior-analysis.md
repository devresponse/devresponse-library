
# Project Plan Template: Retail Store Customer Behavior Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary

The Retail Store Customer Behavior Analysis project aims to leverage AWS DeepLens technology to provide retailers with actionable insights into customer behavior. The primary objective of this project is to enhance the understanding of customer interactions within the retail environment, thereby enabling retailers to optimize their operations and improve customer experience. This project will involve deploying advanced video analytics to track foot traffic patterns, identify high-traffic product areas, and analyze customer engagement levels. Key outcomes will include a comprehensive assessment of the retail space, the development of tailored AI models, and the integration of these models with AWS DeepLens for real-time data processing. By ensuring that the solution is optimized for accuracy and efficiency, we will empower retailers to make informed decisions that enhance customer experience and drive sales. The project will culminate in a detailed report outlining findings and recommendations for future improvements, providing a roadmap for ongoing enhancements in customer engagement strategies.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Retail Space**: Conduct a thorough evaluation of the retail environment to understand layout, customer flow, and product placement.
- **Development of AI Models**: Create customized AI models tailored to the specific needs of the retailer, focusing on customer behavior analytics.
- **Integration with AWS DeepLens**: Implement the developed AI models with AWS DeepLens technology to facilitate real-time data processing and analytics.
- **Video Analytics Deployment**: Set up video analytics systems to monitor foot traffic, product engagement, and customer interactions.
- **Data Collection and Analysis**: Collect data over a specified period to analyze customer behavior patterns and engagement levels.
- **Reporting**: Generate a comprehensive report detailing findings, insights, and actionable recommendations for the retailer.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Retail Space Assessment  
  **Deliverable**: Assessment Report outlining current layout and customer flow patterns.

- **Milestone 2**: Development of AI Models  
  **Deliverable**: Customized AI Models ready for integration with AWS DeepLens.

- **Milestone 3**: Integration with AWS DeepLens  
  **Deliverable**: Fully integrated system with operational video analytics.

- **Milestone 4**: Data Collection Period  
  **Deliverable**: Collected data set for analysis.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Report Submission  
  **Deliverable**: Comprehensive report with findings and recommendations.
