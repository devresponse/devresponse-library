
# Project Plan Template: AWS RDS Data Archiving and Retention Policy Setup

## 1. Executive Summary

The AWS RDS Data Archiving and Retention Policy Setup project aims to establish a robust framework for managing data within your organization, ensuring compliance with industry regulations while optimizing storage costs. In today's data-driven environment, the efficient management of data is paramount for businesses leveraging AWS RDS. This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of your current data usage patterns, allowing our expert team to recommend best practices for data lifecycle management. Key outcomes include the development of a tailored archiving and retention policy, the implementation of automated solutions to streamline the archiving process, and the provision of thorough documentation and training for your team. By enhancing your data management strategy, this project will not only reduce unnecessary storage expenses but also ensure that your organization remains compliant with relevant regulations, ultimately contributing to improved operational efficiency and data governance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Analysis of Current Data Usage**: Conduct a thorough assessment of existing data storage and usage patterns within the AWS RDS environment.
- **Policy Development**: Create a comprehensive data archiving and retention policy that aligns with industry regulations and organizational needs.
- **Best Practices Recommendations**: Provide recommendations for data lifecycle management, including data classification, archiving frequency, and retention periods.
- **Implementation of Automated Solutions**: Develop and implement automated processes for data archiving to enhance efficiency and reduce manual intervention.
- **Documentation**: Prepare detailed documentation outlining the archiving and retention procedures, including roles and responsibilities.
- **Training**: Conduct training sessions for your team to ensure they are equipped to maintain and manage the new policy effectively.
