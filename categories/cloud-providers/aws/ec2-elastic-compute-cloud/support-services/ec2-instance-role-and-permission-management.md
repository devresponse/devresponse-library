
# Project Plan Template: EC2 Instance Role and Permission Management

## 1. Executive Summary

The EC2 Instance Role and Permission Management project aims to enhance the security and operational efficiency of your AWS environment by configuring and managing IAM (Identity and Access Management) roles and permissions specifically for EC2 instances. The primary objective is to establish a secure framework that adheres to the principle of least privilege, ensuring that each EC2 instance is granted only the permissions necessary to perform its designated tasks. This project will involve a comprehensive assessment of your current IAM setup to identify potential security risks and gaps. We will implement best practices for role assignment and permission management, ensuring that your AWS resources are protected against unauthorized access. Key outcomes will include detailed documentation of the configurations made, along with actionable recommendations for ongoing management and monitoring. By the conclusion of this project, you will have a robust IAM strategy that not only enhances the security posture of your AWS resources but also facilitates efficient operations, ultimately contributing to the overall success of your cloud initiatives.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current IAM Setup**: Review existing IAM roles, policies, and permissions associated with EC2 instances.
- **Risk Identification**: Identify potential security risks and compliance gaps within the current IAM configuration.
- **Role and Permission Configuration**: Implement IAM roles and permissions based on the principle of least privilege tailored to each EC2 instance's specific tasks.
- **Best Practices Implementation**: Apply industry best practices for IAM role assignment and permission management.
- **Documentation**: Provide detailed documentation outlining the configurations made, including role descriptions, permission sets, and rationale for decisions.
- **Recommendations**: Offer recommendations for ongoing management and monitoring of IAM roles and permissions.
- **Training and Support**: Provide training sessions for your team on managing IAM roles and permissions effectively.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
