
# Project Plan Template: EC2 Backup and Disaster Recovery Planning

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, ensuring the integrity and availability of your data is paramount. The EC2 Backup and Disaster Recovery Planning project aims to design and implement a comprehensive backup and disaster recovery plan specifically tailored for your EC2 instances. The primary objective of this project is to safeguard critical data and applications against potential threats and failures by establishing a robust multi-tiered backup strategy. This will involve a thorough assessment of your current infrastructure, identifying critical data and applications, and leveraging AWS services such as Amazon S3 for storage, AWS Backup for automated backups, and AWS CloudFormation for infrastructure as code. Key outcomes will include the development of a disaster recovery strategy that defines recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO), minimizing downtime and data loss. The final deliverable will consist of detailed documentation of the backup and recovery processes, along with a consultation session to ensure your team is well-equipped to manage the implementation effectively.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Infrastructure**: Conduct a comprehensive review of existing EC2 instances, applications, and data storage solutions.
- **Identification of Critical Data and Applications**: Collaborate with stakeholders to determine which data and applications are essential for business continuity.
- **Design of Multi-Tiered Backup Strategy**: Develop a backup strategy that includes:
  - Daily, weekly, and monthly backup schedules.
  - Use of Amazon S3 for secure and scalable storage.
  - Implementation of AWS Backup for automated backup processes.
- **Disaster Recovery Strategy Development**: Establish RTO and RPO metrics to guide recovery efforts.
- **Documentation**: Create detailed documentation outlining backup and recovery processes, including step-by-step instructions and best practices.
- **Training and Consultation**: Provide a consultation session for your team to ensure they understand the processes and tools involved in managing backups and disaster recovery.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of infrastructure assessment
  - **Deliverable**: Assessment report detailing current EC2 instances and applications.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Identification of critical data and applications
  - **Deliverable**: List of critical data and applications with stakeholder approval.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Design of multi-tiered backup strategy
  - **Deliverable**: Backup strategy document outlining schedules and AWS services to be used.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Development of disaster recovery strategy
  - **Deliverable**: Document detailing RTO and RPO metrics and recovery procedures.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final documentation and training session
  - **Deliverable**: Comprehensive documentation of backup and recovery processes and a training session for the team.
