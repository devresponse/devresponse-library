
# Project Plan Template: EC2 Compliance and Governance Review

## 1. Executive Summary

The EC2 Compliance and Governance Review project is designed to provide a comprehensive assessment of your AWS EC2 configurations and operational practices. The primary objective of this project is to ensure that your cloud infrastructure aligns with industry standards and governance policies, particularly in relation to compliance regulations such as GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI-DSS. Our expert consultants will conduct a thorough evaluation of your current EC2 setup, identifying any gaps in compliance and providing actionable recommendations to enhance your governance framework. Key outcomes of this project include a detailed report outlining our findings, a consultation session to discuss the results, and a strategic roadmap for implementing necessary changes. By the conclusion of this project, you will have a clear understanding of your compliance status and the steps required to achieve full governance alignment, ultimately ensuring that your cloud resources are secure and optimized for performance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current EC2 Configurations**: Review existing EC2 instances, security groups, IAM roles, and network configurations.
- **Compliance Gap Analysis**: Identify gaps in compliance with relevant regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, PCI-DSS) and best practices.
- **Governance Framework Evaluation**: Assess the current governance framework and its effectiveness in managing cloud resources.
- **Recommendations Report**: Provide a detailed report with findings, including specific recommendations for compliance improvements and governance enhancements.
- **Consultation Session**: Conduct a session to discuss the findings and recommendations with key stakeholders.
- **Implementation Roadmap**: Develop a roadmap outlining the steps needed to implement the recommended changes and achieve compliance.
