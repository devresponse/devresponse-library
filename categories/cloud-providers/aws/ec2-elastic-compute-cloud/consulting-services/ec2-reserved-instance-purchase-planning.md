
# Project Plan Template: EC2 Reserved Instance Purchase Planning

## 1. Executive Summary

The EC2 Reserved Instance Purchase Planning project aims to provide expert consulting services to businesses seeking to optimize their AWS EC2 usage through strategic purchasing of Reserved Instances. The primary objective is to empower clients with a deep understanding of their workload patterns and future usage predictions, enabling informed decision-making regarding reserved instance purchases. This project will involve a thorough analysis of the client's current EC2 usage, assessing workload predictability, and delivering tailored recommendations for the optimal number and type of reserved instances to acquire. Key outcomes include a comprehensive review of pricing models, potential savings, and the implications of various reservation terms. Additionally, the consultant will assist in formulating a purchasing strategy that aligns with the client's budget and operational needs, ensuring that the investment in reserved instances maximizes cost efficiency over the long term. Ultimately, this project will equip clients with actionable insights and a clear roadmap for their EC2 Reserved Instance purchases.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a kickoff meeting with the client to understand their business objectives, current EC2 usage, and specific needs regarding Reserved Instances.
  
- **Data Collection**: Gather relevant data on the client's existing EC2 usage patterns, including instance types, utilization rates, and historical usage trends.
  
- **Workload Analysis**: Analyze the collected data to identify workload predictability and usage patterns, focusing on peak and off-peak periods.
  
- **Recommendations Development**: Develop tailored recommendations for the optimal number and type of Reserved Instances to purchase based on the analysis.
  
- **Pricing Model Review**: Provide a comprehensive review of AWS pricing models for Reserved Instances, including standard, convertible, and scheduled options.
  
- **Savings Assessment**: Calculate potential savings associated with different purchasing strategies and reservation terms.
  
- **Purchasing Strategy Creation**: Assist the client in creating a purchasing strategy that aligns with their budget and operational needs.
  
- **Final Report**: Prepare a detailed report summarizing findings, recommendations, and the purchasing strategy.
  
- **Consultation Session**: Conduct a follow-up consultation session to discuss the report and answer any questions the client may have.
