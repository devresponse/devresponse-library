
# Project Plan Template: EC2 Cost Optimization Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

The EC2 Cost Optimization Audit aims to provide businesses with a comprehensive analysis of their AWS EC2 instances, identifying potential cost-saving opportunities. This project will involve a meticulous examination of current usage patterns, including peak usage times and underutilized resources. By assessing the types of instances being utilized, we will evaluate their alignment with the organization's operational needs. Furthermore, we will explore reserved instance strategies to uncover savings through long-term commitments. The key outcomes of this audit will be a detailed report containing actionable recommendations tailored to the specific requirements of the business. This will empower organizations to maximize their cloud investment while minimizing unnecessary expenses. Ultimately, the EC2 Cost Optimization Audit is essential for businesses seeking to enhance their cloud efficiency and achieve significant cost reductions.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a kickoff meeting with stakeholders to understand business objectives and gather preliminary data.
  
- **Data Collection**: Gather detailed usage data from AWS EC2 instances, including instance types, usage patterns, and billing information.

- **Usage Analysis**: Analyze the collected data to identify peak usage times, underutilized resources, and any anomalies in usage patterns.

- **Instance Evaluation**: Assess the types of instances in use and evaluate their alignment with the business's operational needs.

- **Reserved Instance Strategy**: Explore potential savings through reserved instance strategies, including recommendations for future purchases.

- **Recommendations Development**: Develop actionable recommendations based on the analysis, focusing on cost-saving opportunities and efficiency improvements.

- **Final Report Preparation**: Compile findings and recommendations into a comprehensive report for stakeholders.

- **Presentation of Findings**: Present the final report to stakeholders, highlighting key insights and recommendations.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
