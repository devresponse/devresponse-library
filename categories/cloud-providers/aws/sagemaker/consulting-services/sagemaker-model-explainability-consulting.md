
# Project Plan Template: Sagemaker Model Explainability Consulting

## 1. Executive Summary

The Sagemaker Model Explainability Consulting project aims to enhance the interpretability and transparency of machine learning models deployed within AWS Sagemaker. In today's data-driven landscape, understanding how models make decisions is essential for building trust among stakeholders and ensuring compliance with regulatory standards. This project will focus on implementing advanced explainability techniques, specifically SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) and LIME (Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations), to provide clear insights into model predictions. Our expert consultants will collaborate closely with your team to identify the models in use and tailor the explainability methods to fit your specific needs. Key outcomes include the development of visualizations that simplify complex model behaviors, a robust framework for ongoing model explainability, and improved stakeholder confidence in the decision-making processes driven by machine learning. By the conclusion of this project, your organization will not only achieve enhanced transparency but also see an improvement in model performance, ultimately leading to more informed business decisions.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a comprehensive review of existing machine learning models deployed in AWS Sagemaker to identify which models require explainability enhancements.
  
- **Selection of Explainability Techniques**: Based on the assessment, select appropriate explainability techniques (SHAP, LIME) tailored to the specific models and business requirements.

- **Implementation**: Apply the selected explainability techniques to the identified models, ensuring integration with existing workflows and systems.

- **Visualization Development**: Create user-friendly visualizations that effectively communicate model behaviors and predictions to stakeholders.

- **Training and Documentation**: Provide training sessions for your team on how to interpret the explainability outputs and maintain the framework. Deliver comprehensive documentation outlining the processes and methodologies used.

- **Feedback and Iteration**: Gather feedback from stakeholders and iterate on the visualizations and explanations to ensure clarity and usability.

- **Final Review and Handover**: Conduct a final review of the project deliverables with your team, ensuring all objectives have been met and provide a formal handover of the project materials.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 4**: Development of Visualizations  
  **Deliverable**: A set of visualizations that effectively communicate model predictions and behaviors.

- **Milestone 5**: Training and Documentation
