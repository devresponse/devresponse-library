## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Requirements Gathering**: Conduct workshops with {{Client Name}}'s stakeholders to gather detailed requirements regarding data sources, transformation rules, and output formats.
  
- **Analysis and Design**: Analyze the gathered requirements and design custom AWS Glue jobs that meet the specified ETL needs. This includes defining data schemas, transformation logic, and integration points with existing data architecture.

- **Development**: Develop the custom AWS Glue jobs based on the approved design. This will involve coding, testing, and validating the jobs to ensure they meet performance and quality standards.

- **Deployment**: Deploy the developed AWS Glue jobs into the production environment, ensuring seamless integration with existing systems.

- **Performance Optimization**: Monitor the performance of the deployed jobs and make necessary adjustments to optimize processing times and resource utilization.

- **Documentation and Training**: Provide comprehensive documentation of the developed jobs and conduct training sessions for {{Client Name}}'s staff to ensure they can effectively manage and maintain the ETL processes.

- **Support and Maintenance**: Offer post-deployment support for a specified period to address any issues and ensure the smooth operation of the AWS Glue jobs.
