
# Project Plan Template: Topic Modeling for Market Research Reports

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Topic Modeling for Market Research Reports" project aims to leverage the advanced natural language processing capabilities of AWS Comprehend to extract and analyze key themes and trends from a collection of market research reports. The primary objective is to provide businesses with valuable insights that can inform strategic decision-making. This project will involve a comprehensive data preparation phase, where the reports will be cleaned and formatted to ensure optimal analysis. Following this, AWS Comprehend will be utilized to identify and categorize topics, resulting in a detailed report that highlights significant findings. Key outcomes include a deeper understanding of market dynamics and actionable insights that can drive business strategies. Our dedicated team will ensure a seamless implementation process, presenting results in a clear and concise manner for easy stakeholder comprehension. Ultimately, this project will empower businesses to make informed decisions based on robust data analysis.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Collection**: Gather a comprehensive set of market research reports relevant to the client's industry.
- **Data Preparation**: 
  - Clean and format the reports to remove any irrelevant information.
  - Standardize the data for consistency in analysis.
- **Topic Modeling**:
  - Utilize AWS Comprehend to perform topic modeling on the prepared data.
  - Identify and categorize key themes and trends emerging from the reports.
- **Analysis and Reporting**:
  - Generate a comprehensive report detailing the significant findings from the topic modeling.
  - Include visualizations and summaries to enhance understanding.
- **Presentation**:
  - Prepare a presentation for stakeholders to communicate the insights and recommendations derived from the analysis.
- **Feedback and Revisions**:
  - Gather feedback from stakeholders and make necessary revisions to the report and presentation.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Data Collection
  - Deliverable: Compiled dataset of market research reports.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Data Preparation
  - Deliverable: Cleaned and formatted dataset ready for analysis.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Topic Modeling
  - Deliverable: Initial topic modeling results from AWS Comprehend.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Analysis and Reporting
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report detailing findings and insights.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Presentation to Stakeholders
  - Deliverable: Presentation slides summarizing key insights and recommendations.
