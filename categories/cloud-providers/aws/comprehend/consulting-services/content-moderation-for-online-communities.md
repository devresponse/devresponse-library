
# Project Plan Template: Content Moderation for Online Communities

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, maintaining a safe and respectful online community is paramount. The objective of this project is to implement a robust content moderation system utilizing AWS Comprehend, a powerful natural language processing service. This system will analyze user-generated content in real-time, identifying and flagging inappropriate or harmful material such as hate speech, harassment, and spam. The project will begin with a thorough assessment of existing community guidelines to ensure alignment with the moderation system. Key outcomes include the successful configuration of AWS Comprehend to meet these standards, comprehensive training for staff on interpreting analysis results, and the integration of these insights into the moderation workflow. Ultimately, this project aims to foster positive interactions and engagement within the online community, ensuring it remains a safe space for all users.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Existing Guidelines**: Review and analyze current community guidelines to identify areas for improvement and alignment with the content moderation system.
  
- **Configuration of AWS Comprehend**: Set up and customize AWS Comprehend to effectively identify and flag inappropriate content based on the established community guidelines.
  
- **Integration with Moderation Workflow**: Develop a seamless process for integrating AWS Comprehend analysis results into the existing moderation workflow, ensuring that flagged content is reviewed and acted upon promptly.
  
- **Training Sessions**: Conduct training sessions for moderation staff on how to interpret the results from AWS Comprehend and apply them in real-time moderation scenarios.
  
- **Monitoring and Evaluation**: Establish metrics for evaluating the effectiveness of the content moderation system and make necessary adjustments based on feedback and performance data.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the assessment of existing community guidelines.
  - **Deliverable**: Assessment report with recommendations for alignment.

- **Milestone 2**: Configuration of AWS Comprehend completed.
  - **Deliverable**: Documented configuration settings and guidelines.

- **Milestone 3**: Integration of AWS Comprehend into the moderation workflow.
  - **Deliverable**: Workflow integration plan and documentation.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of training sessions for moderation staff.
  - **Deliverable**: Training materials and attendance records.

- **Milestone 5**: Monitoring and evaluation metrics established.
  - **Deliverable**: Evaluation report with initial findings and recommendations.
