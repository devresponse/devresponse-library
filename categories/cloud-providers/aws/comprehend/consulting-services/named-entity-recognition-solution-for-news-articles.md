
# Project Plan Template: Named Entity Recognition for News Articles

## 1. Executive Summary

The Named Entity Recognition (NER) for News Articles project aims to develop a robust solution utilizing AWS Comprehend to extract and categorize entities from diverse news articles. The primary objective is to enhance media monitoring capabilities by accurately identifying key entities such as individuals, organizations, and geographical locations within the text. This project will commence with a thorough analysis of the client's specific requirements, ensuring that the NER model is tailored to the unique characteristics of the news articles being processed. Key outcomes include the implementation of efficient data ingestion processes, streamlined entity extraction workflows, and seamless integration of results into the client's existing media monitoring systems. Furthermore, we will provide optimization strategies to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of the entity recognition process, ensuring scalability and adaptability for future needs. Ultimately, this project is designed to deliver a high-quality, automated solution that not only streamlines the monitoring of news content but also empowers the client to respond proactively to emerging trends and events.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Requirement Analysis**: Conduct a comprehensive analysis of the client's needs to define the specific entities to be recognized and categorized.
  
- **Configuration of AWS Comprehend**: Tailor the NER model using AWS Comprehend to fit the unique characteristics of the news articles, including custom entity types if necessary.

- **Data Ingestion Processes**: Develop and implement processes for ingesting news articles from various sources into the NER system.

- **Entity Extraction Workflows**: Design and implement workflows for extracting identified entities from the ingested articles.

- **Integration**: Integrate the extracted entity data into the client's existing media monitoring systems, ensuring compatibility and ease of use.

- **Optimization Strategies**: Provide strategies for optimizing the NER process to improve accuracy and efficiency, including feedback loops and model retraining.

- **Documentation and Training**: Deliver comprehensive documentation and training sessions for the client's team to ensure effective use of the NER solution.

- **Support and Maintenance**: Offer ongoing support and maintenance services post-implementation to address any issues and ensure the system remains effective.
