
# Project Plan Template: Legal Document Classification System

## 1. Executive Summary

The Legal Document Classification System project aims to revolutionize the management of legal documents through the implementation of a robust DevOps solution utilizing AWS Comprehend. The primary objective is to automate the classification and organization of legal documents by leveraging advanced natural language processing capabilities. This project will provide a comprehensive overview of the legal document landscape, enabling businesses to extract relevant keywords and categorize documents efficiently. Key outcomes include the establishment of an AWS environment, seamless integration of AWS Comprehend for document analysis, and the development of a user-friendly interface for clients to upload and manage their legal documents. Furthermore, the project will incorporate automated workflows for document processing, significantly enhancing efficiency and accuracy in classification. By streamlining document management processes, businesses will reduce manual effort and improve accessibility to critical legal information. The project will adhere to Agile methodologies, ensuring timely delivery and the flexibility to adapt to client feedback throughout the development process.
