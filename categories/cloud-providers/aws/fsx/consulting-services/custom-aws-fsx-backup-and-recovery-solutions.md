
# Project Plan Template: Custom AWS FSX Backup and Recovery Solutions

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom AWS FSX Backup and Recovery Solutions project aims to enhance data integrity and ensure business continuity for organizations utilizing AWS FSX. In today's digital landscape, the risk of data loss can have severe implications for operational efficiency and customer trust. This project will involve a thorough assessment of the current infrastructure to identify potential vulnerabilities and risks. The consultant will develop a tailored backup strategy that incorporates automated backups, versioning, and secure storage options, ensuring that data is consistently protected. Additionally, a comprehensive disaster recovery plan will be created, detailing recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) to facilitate swift recovery from data loss incidents. By the conclusion of this project, your organization will possess a robust backup and recovery framework that not only enhances data security but also fortifies operational resilience. Key outcomes include improved data management practices, reduced risk of data loss, and empowered staff through training and documentation.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Infrastructure**
  - Evaluate existing AWS FSX configurations and data management practices.
  - Identify potential risks and vulnerabilities in the current setup.

- **Development of Backup Strategy**
  - Design a tailored backup solution that includes:
    - Automated backup schedules
    - Data versioning strategies
    - Secure storage options for backups

- **Creation of Disaster Recovery Plan**
  - Define Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO).
  - Outline procedures for data recovery in the event of a loss.

- **Documentation and Training**
  - Provide comprehensive documentation of the backup and recovery processes.
  - Conduct training sessions for your team to ensure effective management and maintenance of the backup solutions.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Infrastructure Assessment Completion**
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing current infrastructure and identified risks.

- **Milestone 2: Backup Strategy Development**
  - Deliverable: Documented backup strategy including automated schedules and storage solutions.

- **Milestone 3: Disaster Recovery Plan Creation**
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive disaster recovery plan outlining RTO and RPO.

- **Milestone 4: Documentation and Training Delivery**
  - Deliverable: Training materials and completion of training sessions for the team.
