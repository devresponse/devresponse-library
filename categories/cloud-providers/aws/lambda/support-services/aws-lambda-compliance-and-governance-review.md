
# Project Plan Template: AWS Lambda Compliance and Governance Review

## 1. Executive Summary

The AWS Lambda Compliance and Governance Review project aims to provide a thorough evaluation of your AWS Lambda configurations, ensuring they meet both industry standards and your organization's governance policies. The primary objective is to identify any gaps or areas for improvement in your current setup, focusing on security, performance, and compliance. Our experienced team will conduct a comprehensive analysis of your Lambda functions, permissions, and associated resources. Key outcomes of this project include a detailed report that outlines our findings, actionable recommendations for remediation, and a strategic roadmap for implementing necessary changes. By enhancing your cloud infrastructure's reliability and security, this project will ensure that your AWS Lambda functions operate within the required compliance frameworks, ultimately supporting your organization's operational goals and risk management strategies.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the AWS Lambda Compliance and Governance Review includes the following activities:

- **Initial Assessment**: Review existing AWS Lambda configurations and associated resources.
- **Compliance Evaluation**: Assess current setups against industry standards and organizational governance policies.
- **Security Analysis**: Evaluate permissions and access controls for Lambda functions to identify vulnerabilities.
- **Performance Review**: Analyze the performance metrics of Lambda functions to ensure optimal operation.
- **Reporting**: Compile a detailed report that includes:
  - Findings from the assessment
  - Recommendations for remediation
  - A roadmap for implementing changes
- **Stakeholder Presentation**: Present findings and recommendations to key stakeholders for feedback and approval.
