## 1. Executive Summary

The "Rekognition for Historical Image Archiving" project aims to leverage the advanced capabilities of AWS Rekognition to enhance the organization and accessibility of historical image archives. Our primary objective is to analyze a vast collection of historical images using machine learning algorithms, enabling automatic tagging with relevant metadata such as {{date}}, {{location}}, and {{subject}}. This initiative will streamline the archiving process, making it significantly easier to search and retrieve images for research and preservation purposes.
