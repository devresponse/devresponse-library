
# Project Plan Template: CloudTrail Log Retention Policy Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The CloudTrail Log Retention Policy Design project aims to establish a comprehensive framework for managing AWS CloudTrail logs within your organization. The primary objective is to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements while optimizing operational efficiency. This project will involve a detailed analysis of your current logging practices and an assessment of applicable regulatory obligations. Key outcomes include the development of a tailored log retention policy that defines the duration for which logs should be retained, the establishment of procedures for log archival, and the implementation of automated processes for log deletion. Additionally, our expert consultants will provide comprehensive documentation that outlines the policy and offers guidelines for ongoing management and compliance checks. By enhancing your cloud operations and ensuring adherence to industry standards and best practices, this project will position your organization for improved governance and risk management in the cloud environment.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Current State Assessment**: Conduct a thorough analysis of existing logging practices and compliance requirements.
- **Regulatory Review**: Identify relevant regulations and standards that impact log retention policies.
- **Policy Development**: Design a tailored log retention policy that includes:
  - Duration of log retention
  - Procedures for log archival
  - Automated processes for log deletion
- **Documentation**: Create comprehensive documentation that includes:
  - The finalized log retention policy
  - Guidelines for ongoing management
  - Compliance check procedures
- **Implementation Support**: Assist in the implementation of the log retention policy, including necessary configurations in AWS CloudTrail.
- **Training**: Provide training sessions for relevant staff on the new log retention policy and procedures.
- **Review and Feedback**: Gather feedback from stakeholders and make necessary adjustments to the policy.
