
# Project Plan Template: CloudTrail Log Retention Policy Manager

## 1. Executive Summary

The CloudTrail Log Retention Policy Manager project aims to establish a robust solution for managing and enforcing log retention policies for AWS CloudTrail across diverse environments. The primary objective is to analyze existing log retention requirements and design a scalable policy management framework that aligns with both organizational and regulatory standards. Key outcomes of this project include the implementation of automated processes that ensure compliance, a user-friendly interface for monitoring log retention status, and the generation of comprehensive reports. Furthermore, the project will provide alerts to stakeholders regarding any discrepancies in log retention, thereby enhancing accountability. To empower teams, thorough documentation and training materials will be developed, ensuring effective utilization of the system. By streamlining log management processes, this initiative will significantly enhance operational efficiency and guarantee that critical log data is retained according to established policies.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Analysis Phase**: 
  - Conduct a thorough assessment of current log retention requirements.
  - Identify gaps in existing processes and compliance issues.

- **Design Phase**: 
  - Develop a scalable policy management framework tailored to AWS CloudTrail.
  - Create a user-friendly interface for monitoring and reporting.

- **Implementation Phase**: 
  - Automate log retention processes to ensure compliance with policies.
  - Integrate alert mechanisms for stakeholders regarding log retention discrepancies.

- **Documentation and Training**: 
  - Produce comprehensive documentation detailing system functionalities and processes.
  - Develop training materials and conduct training sessions for relevant teams.

- **Testing and Validation**: 
  - Perform rigorous testing of the solution to ensure reliability and effectiveness.
  - Validate compliance with organizational and regulatory standards.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the analysis phase
  - Deliverable: Analysis report outlining current log retention requirements and identified gaps.

- **Milestone 2**: Design framework approval
  - Deliverable: Approved design document for the policy management framework.

- **Milestone 3**: Implementation of automated processes
  - Deliverable: Fully functional automated log retention management system.

- **Milestone 4**: Documentation and training completion
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive documentation and training materials delivered to teams.

- **Milestone 5**: Final testing and validation
  - Deliverable: Testing report confirming system reliability and compliance.
