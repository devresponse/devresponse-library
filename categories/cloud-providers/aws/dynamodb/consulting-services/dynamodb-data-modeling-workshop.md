
# Project Plan Template: DynamoDB Data Modeling Workshop

## 1. Executive Summary

The **DynamoDB Data Modeling Workshop** aims to equip teams with the essential skills and knowledge necessary for effective data modeling in AWS DynamoDB. This interactive workshop is designed to provide participants with a comprehensive understanding of NoSQL databases, focusing on the principles that underpin their design and implementation. Key objectives include teaching the importance of single-table design and exploring strategies to optimize query patterns for enhanced performance. Through hands-on exercises, participants will learn how to structure data efficiently, ensuring that their models align with business requirements. By the conclusion of the workshop, attendees will possess the expertise to implement robust data models in DynamoDB, ultimately driving better data management practices within their organizations. This workshop is particularly beneficial for developers, data architects, and database administrators seeking to deepen their knowledge of cloud-based database solutions.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the **DynamoDB Data Modeling Workshop** includes the following components:

- **Pre-Workshop Assessment**: Evaluate participants' current knowledge and skills related to NoSQL databases and AWS DynamoDB.
- **Workshop Content Development**: Create tailored workshop materials, including presentations, hands-on exercises, and case studies that reflect real-world scenarios.
- **Workshop Delivery**: Conduct a 2-day interactive workshop that covers:
  - Introduction to NoSQL databases and their advantages.
  - Detailed exploration of AWS DynamoDB features and capabilities.
  - Best practices for single-table design and data structuring.
  - Techniques for optimizing query patterns and performance.
  - Hands-on exercises to reinforce learning and practical application.
- **Post-Workshop Support**: Provide participants with access to additional resources and a follow-up Q&A session to address any lingering questions or challenges.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Pre-Workshop Assessment

  - Deliverable: Successful completion of the 2-day workshop with participant feedback collected.

- **Milestone 4**: Post-Workshop Support
  - Deliverable: Access to additional resources and completion of follow-up Q&A session.
