
# Project Plan Template: Custom Monitoring Dashboard for Certificate Health

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, maintaining the integrity and security of SSL/TLS certificates is paramount for any business leveraging AWS Certificate Manager. The objective of this project is to develop a custom monitoring dashboard that provides real-time insights into the health and status of your SSL/TLS certificates. This dashboard will feature key metrics such as expiration dates, renewal reminders, and compliance status, ensuring that your certificates are always up to date and functioning correctly. Key outcomes of this project include enhanced visibility into certificate management, reduced risk of certificate-related outages, and improved compliance with security standards. Our team will collaborate closely with you to understand your specific requirements and tailor the dashboard to meet your unique needs. By implementing robust monitoring solutions that integrate seamlessly with AWS Certificate Manager, we will enable proactive management of your certificates. Additionally, comprehensive documentation and training will be provided to ensure your team can effectively utilize the dashboard for ongoing monitoring and maintenance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Requirements Gathering**: Conduct workshops with stakeholders to gather detailed requirements for the monitoring dashboard.
- **Dashboard Design**: Create wireframes and design mockups for the dashboard interface, incorporating user feedback.
- **Development**: Build the custom monitoring dashboard, integrating with AWS Certificate Manager to pull real-time data on SSL/TLS certificates.
- **Key Features**:
  - Display of expiration dates for all certificates.
  - Automated renewal reminders.
  - Compliance status indicators.
  - User-friendly interface for easy navigation and access to key metrics.
- **Testing**: Perform thorough testing of the dashboard to ensure functionality, usability, and performance.
- **Documentation**: Develop user manuals and technical documentation for the dashboard.
- **Training**: Provide training sessions for your team to ensure effective use of the dashboard.
- **Support**: Offer post-launch support for a specified period to address any issues or questions.
