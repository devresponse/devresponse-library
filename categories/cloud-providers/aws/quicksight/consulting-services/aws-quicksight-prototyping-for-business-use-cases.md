
# Project Plan Template: AWS QuickSight Prototyping for Business Use Cases

## 1. Executive Summary

The AWS QuickSight Prototyping for Business Use Cases project aims to create functional prototypes tailored to specific business needs, enabling clients to validate their use cases through interactive dashboards. This project will provide a comprehensive overview of the capabilities of AWS QuickSight, allowing clients to visualize key metrics and insights that are critical for informed decision-making. The primary objective is to engage in initial consultations to gather detailed requirements, followed by the rapid development of dashboards that reflect these needs. Throughout the project, we will facilitate iterative feedback sessions to refine the prototypes, ensuring alignment with the client's strategic goals. By the conclusion of this project, clients will possess validated prototypes that not only showcase the power of AWS QuickSight but also deliver actionable insights, empowering them to make data-driven decisions effectively.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct meetings with stakeholders to gather requirements and understand specific business use cases.
- **Prototype Development**: Rapidly develop interactive dashboards in AWS QuickSight based on gathered requirements.
- **Iterative Feedback Sessions**: Schedule regular sessions with stakeholders to review prototypes and gather feedback for refinement.
- **Final Deliverables**: Provide a set of validated prototypes that demonstrate the capabilities of AWS QuickSight and include documentation on how to use the dashboards effectively.
- **Training and Support**: Offer training sessions for client teams on utilizing the dashboards and interpreting the insights generated.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Requirements Document
- **Milestone 2**: First Prototype Development
  - Deliverable: Initial Dashboard Prototype
- **Milestone 3**: First Iterative Feedback Session
  - Deliverable: Feedback Summary Report
- **Milestone 4**: Final Prototype Development
  - Deliverable: Final Dashboard Prototype
- **Milestone 5**: Training and Support Session
  - Deliverable: Training Materials and Session Feedback
