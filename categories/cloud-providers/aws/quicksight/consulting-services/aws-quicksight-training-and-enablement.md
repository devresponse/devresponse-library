
# Project Plan Template: AWS QuickSight Training and Enablement

## 1. Executive Summary

The AWS QuickSight Training and Enablement project aims to empower your team with the essential skills and knowledge required to effectively utilize AWS QuickSight. This comprehensive training program is designed to enhance your team's ability to create insightful dashboards and reports that facilitate data-driven decision-making. The project will consist of a series of hands-on workshops tailored to meet the specific needs of your business, ensuring that the training is both relevant and applicable. Key outcomes include improved analytical capabilities, the creation of actionable insights from data, and the provision of best practice guidelines and user guides for ongoing reference. By the conclusion of this training, participants will be equipped to leverage AWS QuickSight to its fullest potential, ultimately transforming data into strategic business advantages.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Training Needs Assessment**: Conduct an initial assessment to identify the specific training needs of the team.
- **Workshop Development**: Design and develop customized workshop content that aligns with the identified needs.
- **Hands-On Workshops**: Facilitate a series of interactive workshops covering:
  - Introduction to AWS QuickSight
  - Data preparation and ingestion
  - Creating dashboards and reports
  - Advanced analytics features
  - Best practices for data visualization
- **Resource Provision**: Provide participants with user guides and best practice documentation for future reference.
- **Feedback and Evaluation**: Collect feedback from participants to evaluate the effectiveness of the training and make necessary adjustments for future sessions.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Training Needs Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report outlining training needs and objectives.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Development of Workshop Content
  - Deliverable: Customized workshop materials and agenda.

- **Milestone 3**: Conducting Workshops
  - Deliverable: Completion of all scheduled workshops with participant engagement metrics.

- **Milestone 4**: Distribution of Resources
  - Deliverable: User guides and best practice documentation provided to all participants.

- **Milestone 5**: Feedback Collection and Analysis
  - Deliverable: Summary report of participant feedback and recommendations for future training.
