
# Project Plan Template: KPI Development and Tracking in AWS QuickSight

## 1. Executive Summary

The KPI Development and Tracking in AWS QuickSight project aims to empower organizations by establishing a robust framework for monitoring key performance indicators (KPIs) that align with their strategic objectives. This project will involve a thorough analysis of the existing data landscape and business goals, ensuring that the selected metrics are not only relevant but also actionable. Our team of expert consultants will collaborate closely with your stakeholders to design intuitive and visually appealing dashboards that provide real-time insights into performance metrics. Key outcomes of this project include the establishment of a comprehensive KPI framework, the development of user-friendly dashboards, and the implementation of ongoing monitoring processes. By the end of this project, your organization will be equipped to make informed, data-driven decisions that enhance overall performance and drive sustainable growth.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a kickoff meeting with stakeholders to understand business objectives and current data landscape.
- **KPI Identification**: Collaborate with your team to identify and prioritize KPIs that align with business goals.
- **Data Analysis**: Analyze existing data sources and determine data quality and availability for KPI tracking.
- **Dashboard Design**: Create visually appealing and intuitive dashboards in AWS QuickSight that display the identified KPIs.
- **Implementation**: Set up the dashboards in AWS QuickSight, ensuring proper data connections and visualizations.
- **Training**: Provide training sessions for your team on how to use and interpret the dashboards effectively.
- **Ongoing Support**: Establish a plan for ongoing monitoring and adjustments to ensure KPIs remain relevant and actionable.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and agreed-upon project objectives.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Identification of KPIs
  - Deliverable: Document listing prioritized KPIs with rationale for selection.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Data Analysis Report
  - Deliverable: Report detailing data sources, quality assessment, and recommendations.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Dashboard Design Mockups
  - Deliverable: Visual mockups of proposed dashboards for stakeholder review.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Dashboard Implementation
  - Deliverable: Fully functional dashboards deployed in AWS QuickSight.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Training Completion
  - Deliverable: Training materials and attendance records from training sessions.
  
- **Milestone 7**: Ongoing Support Plan
  - Deliverable: Document outlining the support plan for KPI monitoring and adjustments.
