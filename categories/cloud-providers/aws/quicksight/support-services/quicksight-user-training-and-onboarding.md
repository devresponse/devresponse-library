
# Project Plan Template: Quicksight User Training and Onboarding

## 1. Executive Summary

The Quicksight User Training and Onboarding project aims to empower teams with the knowledge and skills necessary to effectively utilize AWS Quicksight for data visualization. This comprehensive training initiative will cover essential features, best practices, and advanced functionalities of the platform. The project will provide interactive sessions that encourage participant engagement through practical exercises, ensuring that team members can apply what they learn in real-world scenarios. Key outcomes include the development of insightful dashboards and reports, which will facilitate data-driven decision-making within the organization. By the end of the training, participants will receive resources and documentation to support their ongoing use of Quicksight, fostering a culture of continuous learning and improvement in data analytics.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Quicksight User Training and Onboarding project includes the following key components:

- **Training Needs Assessment**: Conduct an initial assessment to identify specific training needs and objectives for each team.
- **Curriculum Development**: Create a tailored training curriculum that covers:
  - Introduction to AWS Quicksight
  - Key features and functionalities
  - Best practices for data visualization
  - Advanced techniques for creating dashboards and reports
- **Interactive Training Sessions**: Facilitate a series of interactive training sessions, including:
  - Hands-on exercises and practical applications
  - Group discussions and Q&A sessions
- **Resource Provision**: Develop and distribute comprehensive training materials, including:
  - User guides and documentation
  - Access to recorded training sessions
- **Post-Training Support**: Offer ongoing support and resources to ensure successful implementation of learned skills, including:
  - Follow-up Q&A sessions
  - Access to a dedicated support channel for questions and troubleshooting

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Training Needs Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report outlining team needs and objectives
- **Milestone 2**: Development of Training Curriculum
  - Deliverable: Finalized training curriculum and materials
- **Milestone 3**: Conducting Interactive Training Sessions
  - Deliverable: Completion of all scheduled training sessions with participant feedback
- **Milestone 4**: Distribution of Resources and Documentation
  - Deliverable: Access to training materials and recorded sessions
- **Milestone 5**: Post-Training Support Initiation
  - Deliverable: Launch of support channel and follow-up sessions
