
# Project Plan Template: Quicksight Audit and Health Check

## 1. Executive Summary

The Quicksight Audit and Health Check project aims to conduct a comprehensive evaluation of your AWS Quicksight environment, focusing on its configuration and performance. The primary objective is to identify inefficiencies and potential issues that may impede optimal performance. Our expert team will meticulously analyze your data sources, dashboards, and user permissions, ensuring that every aspect of your Quicksight setup is scrutinized. The key outcomes of this project include a detailed report that outlines our findings, actionable recommendations for enhancements, and best practices tailored to your specific use case. Additionally, we will facilitate a consultation session to discuss the audit results, providing guidance on implementing the suggested improvements. By the end of this project, you will be empowered to leverage AWS Quicksight effectively, enhancing your data visualization capabilities and ensuring alignment with industry standards.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a preliminary review of the existing AWS Quicksight environment to understand the current setup and identify key areas of focus.
  
- **Data Source Analysis**: Evaluate the data sources connected to Quicksight, checking for efficiency, relevance, and performance issues.

- **Dashboard Review**: Analyze existing dashboards for usability, performance, and adherence to best practices in data visualization.

- **User Permissions Audit**: Review user permissions and access controls to ensure compliance with security standards and optimal user experience.

- **Findings Report**: Compile a detailed report summarizing the findings from the assessment, including identified inefficiencies and potential issues.

- **Actionable Recommendations**: Provide tailored recommendations for enhancements and best practices based on the audit findings.

- **Consultation Session**: Conduct a consultation session to discuss the report, answer questions, and guide the implementation of suggested improvements.
