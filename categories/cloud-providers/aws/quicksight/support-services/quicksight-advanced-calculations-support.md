
# Project Plan Template: Quicksight Advanced Calculations Support

## 1. Executive Summary

The Quicksight Advanced Calculations Support project aims to provide expert assistance to users seeking to enhance their data visualization capabilities within AWS Quicksight through the development of advanced calculations. The primary objective is to deliver personalized support that enables clients to create complex calculated fields and custom formulas tailored to their unique data analysis requirements. This project will involve a thorough examination of the client's existing datasets, identification of key metrics, and the formulation of advanced calculations that yield deeper insights into their data. By collaborating closely with clients to understand their data structure and analytical goals, our experienced professionals will ensure that the resulting calculations are not only accurate but also optimized for performance. Key outcomes of this project include a robust set of calculated fields that empower clients to make informed decisions based on their data, ultimately enhancing their overall data visualization experience in AWS Quicksight.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a kickoff meeting with the client to understand their specific needs, data structure, and analytical goals.
- **Data Review**: Perform a comprehensive review of existing datasets to identify strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities for advanced calculations.
- **Key Metrics Identification**: Work with the client to identify key metrics that are critical for their business analysis.
- **Advanced Calculations Development**: Develop complex calculated fields and custom formulas tailored to the client's data analysis needs.
- **Performance Optimization**: Ensure that all calculations are optimized for performance to facilitate efficient data visualization.
- **Training and Documentation**: Provide training sessions for the client’s team on how to utilize the new calculated fields effectively, along with detailed documentation.
- **Feedback and Iteration**: Gather feedback from the client and make necessary adjustments to the calculations based on their input.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and project scope confirmation document.

- **Milestone 2**: Data Review Completed
  - Deliverable: Data review report highlighting key findings and recommendations.

- **Milestone 3**: Key Metrics Identified
  - Deliverable: Document outlining identified key metrics and their relevance.

- **Milestone 4**: Advanced Calculations Developed
  - Deliverable: A set of advanced calculated fields and custom formulas.

- **Milestone 5**: Performance Optimization Completed
  - Deliverable: Performance report demonstrating the efficiency of the calculations.

- **Milestone 6**: Training and Documentation Delivered
  - Deliverable: Training materials and user documentation.

- **Milestone 7**: Project Closure and Feedback
  - Deliverable: Final project report and feedback summary.
