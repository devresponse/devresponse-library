
# Project Plan: DevOps Training for AWS CodeCommit and CI/CD

## 1. Executive Summary

The "DevOps Training for AWS CodeCommit and CI/CD" project aims to provide comprehensive hands-on training sessions designed for teams seeking to enhance their skills in utilizing AWS CodeCommit and implementing Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) pipelines. This project will focus on equipping participants with practical experience in version control using AWS CodeCommit, understanding its features, and effectively integrating it with various CI/CD tools. The training will delve into best practices for automating the software development lifecycle, including code integration, testing, and deployment processes. By the conclusion of the training sessions, participants will possess the knowledge and skills necessary to streamline their development workflows, minimize deployment errors, and foster improved collaboration between development and operations teams. Ultimately, this training is tailored for organizations aiming to optimize their DevOps practices and enhance overall productivity, thereby positioning them for success in a competitive landscape.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of this project includes the following key components:

- **Training Sessions**: Conduct a series of hands-on training sessions focused on AWS CodeCommit and CI/CD practices.
- **Curriculum Development**: Develop a comprehensive curriculum that covers:
  - Introduction to AWS CodeCommit
  - Version control concepts and best practices
  - Setting up and configuring AWS CodeCommit
  - Integrating AWS CodeCommit with CI/CD tools (e.g., AWS CodePipeline, Jenkins)
  - Automating the software development lifecycle
  - Testing and deployment strategies
- **Practical Exercises**: Provide participants with practical exercises to reinforce learning and ensure hands-on experience.
- **Q&A Sessions**: Facilitate interactive Q&A sessions to address participants' queries and provide additional insights.
- **Feedback and Assessment**: Gather feedback from participants and assess their understanding through quizzes and practical assessments.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Curriculum Development Completion
  - Deliverable: Finalized training curriculum document

- **Milestone 3**: Conducting Training Sessions
