
# Project Plan Template: AWS CodeCommit Training for Development Teams

## 1. Executive Summary

The AWS CodeCommit Training for Development Teams project aims to deliver comprehensive training sessions designed to enhance the skills and efficiency of development teams in utilizing AWS CodeCommit. This project will provide participants with a thorough understanding of essential topics, including repository creation, branch management, pull requests, and best practices for version control. By engaging in practical exercises that simulate real-world scenarios, participants will have the opportunity to apply their knowledge in a controlled environment, ensuring they gain hands-on experience. Additionally, the training will cover the integration of AWS CodeCommit with other AWS services and tools, enabling teams to streamline their workflows and improve collaboration. The key outcomes of this project include increased productivity, enhanced project outcomes, and a well-equipped team capable of leveraging AWS CodeCommit effectively. Ultimately, this training will empower development teams to adopt best practices in version control, leading to more efficient project delivery and improved team dynamics.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the AWS CodeCommit Training for Development Teams includes the following key components:

- **Training Needs Assessment**: Conduct an assessment to identify the specific training needs of the development teams.
- **Curriculum Development**: Create a tailored curriculum that covers:
  - Introduction to AWS CodeCommit
  - Repository creation and management
  - Branch management strategies
  - Pull request workflows
  - Best practices for version control
  - Integration with other AWS services (e.g., AWS Lambda, AWS CodePipeline)
- **Training Delivery**: Facilitate interactive training sessions, including:
  - Live demonstrations
  - Hands-on exercises
  - Group discussions and Q&A sessions
- **Training Materials**: Provide comprehensive training materials, including:
  - Slide decks
  - User guides
  - Access to recorded sessions
- **Post-Training Support**: Offer follow-up support to address any questions or challenges faced by participants after the training.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Training Needs Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report outlining the training needs of the development teams.

- **Milestone 2**: Development of Training Curriculum
  - Deliverable: Finalized curriculum document detailing the training topics and structure.

- **Milestone 3**: Delivery of Training Sessions
  - Deliverable: Conducted training sessions with participant feedback collected.

- **Milestone 4**: Distribution of Training Materials
  - Deliverable: Provision of training materials to all participants.

- **Milestone 5**: Post-Training Support Initiation
  - Deliverable: Follow-up support plan and schedule for addressing participant queries.
