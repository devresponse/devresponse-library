
# Project Plan Template: CodeCommit Repository Branching Strategy Consultation

## 1. Executive Summary

The CodeCommit Repository Branching Strategy Consultation project aims to provide expert guidance on developing and implementing effective branching strategies for AWS CodeCommit repositories. The primary objective is to enhance the workflow of your development team and ensure that code management practices are both efficient and scalable. This project will begin with a comprehensive assessment of your current repository structure and branching practices, allowing us to identify key areas for improvement. We will then recommend best practices tailored specifically to your development needs, focusing on strategies for feature branching, release management, and team collaboration. Additionally, we will assist in configuring AWS CodeCommit to support the proposed strategies. By the conclusion of this consultation, you will receive a clear and actionable branching strategy that aligns with your project goals, ultimately enhancing your overall cloud operations and fostering a more productive development environment.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Practices**: 
  - Review existing repository structure and branching strategies.
  - Conduct interviews with team members to understand workflows and pain points.
  
- **Recommendations for Best Practices**: 
  - Develop tailored strategies for feature branching, release management, and collaboration.
  - Provide documentation outlining recommended practices and workflows.

- **Implementation Support**: 
  - Assist in configuring AWS CodeCommit to align with the new branching strategy.
  - Offer guidance on setting up necessary permissions and access controls.

- **Training and Knowledge Transfer**: 
  - Conduct training sessions for team members on the new branching strategies and tools.
  - Provide ongoing support for a defined period post-implementation to address any questions or issues.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current Practices Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing findings and areas for improvement.

- **Milestone 2**: Delivery of Best Practices Recommendations
  - Deliverable: Document outlining tailored branching strategies and workflows.

- **Milestone 3**: Configuration of AWS CodeCommit
  - Deliverable: Configured repository settings and permissions based on recommendations.

- **Milestone 4**: Training Sessions Conducted
  - Deliverable: Training materials and session feedback report.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Review and Support
  - Deliverable: Final report summarizing the project outcomes and ongoing support plan.
