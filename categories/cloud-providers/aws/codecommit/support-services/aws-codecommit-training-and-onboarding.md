
# Project Plan Template: AWS CodeCommit Training and Onboarding

## 1. Executive Summary

The AWS CodeCommit Training and Onboarding project aims to provide comprehensive training sessions and onboarding support for teams that are new to AWS CodeCommit. The primary objective is to equip participants with the essential skills and knowledge required to effectively utilize AWS CodeCommit for their version control needs. This project will deliver a structured training program that covers critical topics such as repository creation, branch management, pull requests, and integration with CI/CD pipelines. Participants will engage in hands-on exercises designed to reinforce their learning and ensure they can confidently navigate the platform. Additionally, the onboarding support will include best practices for collaboration and version control workflows, which will empower teams to enhance their productivity and streamline their development processes. By the end of this training, participants will possess a solid understanding of AWS CodeCommit and be well-prepared to implement it in their projects, ultimately leading to improved efficiency and collaboration within their development teams.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of this project includes the following key components:

- **Training Sessions**: 
  - Conduct a series of training sessions tailored for teams new to AWS CodeCommit.
  - Cover essential topics including:
    - Repository creation
    - Branch management
    - Pull requests
    - Integration with CI/CD pipelines

- **Hands-On Exercises**: 
  - Provide practical exercises that allow participants to apply their learning in real-world scenarios.
  - Ensure that participants can navigate AWS CodeCommit confidently.

- **Onboarding Support**: 
  - Offer guidance on best practices for collaboration and version control workflows.
  - Assist teams in developing effective strategies to enhance productivity.

- **Feedback and Assessment**: 
  - Collect feedback from participants to assess the effectiveness of the training.
  - Make necessary adjustments to the training content based on participant input.
