
# Project Plan Template: AWS Config Custom Remediation Actions

## 1. Executive Summary

The AWS Config Custom Remediation Actions project aims to enhance compliance and operational efficiency within your AWS environment by developing and implementing tailored remediation actions. The primary objective is to automate the resolution of compliance issues that may arise, thereby streamlining your compliance processes and improving cloud governance. The project will commence with a comprehensive assessment of your existing AWS Config setup, identifying specific areas where custom remediation can be effectively applied. Following this assessment, our team will design and develop bespoke remediation actions that align with your unique compliance requirements. The final phase will involve the implementation of these actions, accompanied by thorough testing to ensure their functionality. Additionally, we will provide detailed documentation and training to empower your team to manage and adjust these actions as necessary. Key outcomes of this project include improved compliance adherence, reduced manual intervention, and enhanced operational efficiency, ultimately contributing to a more robust cloud governance framework.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment Phase**: 
  - Conduct a comprehensive review of the current AWS Config setup.
  - Identify compliance gaps and areas for potential remediation.

- **Design Phase**: 
  - Collaborate with stakeholders to define specific compliance requirements.
  - Develop custom remediation actions tailored to identified compliance issues.

- **Development Phase**: 
  - Utilize AWS Systems Manager to create and implement the designed remediation actions.
  - Ensure that the actions are scalable and maintainable.

- **Testing Phase**: 
  - Conduct thorough testing of the implemented remediation actions to ensure they function as intended.
  - Validate that the actions effectively resolve compliance issues.

- **Documentation and Training**: 
  - Provide comprehensive documentation detailing the remediation actions and their management.
  - Conduct training sessions for your team to ensure they are equipped to handle adjustments and maintenance.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the Assessment Phase
  - Deliverable: Assessment report outlining compliance gaps and remediation opportunities.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of the Design Phase
  - Deliverable: Design document detailing the custom remediation actions.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of the Development Phase
  - Deliverable: Implemented remediation actions within the AWS environment.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of the Testing Phase
  - Deliverable: Testing report confirming the functionality of the remediation actions.

- **Milestone 5**: Completion of Documentation and Training
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive documentation and training materials provided to the team.
