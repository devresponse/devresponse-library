
# Project Plan Template: AWS Config Governance Policy Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The AWS Config Governance Policy Design project aims to establish a robust governance framework for your cloud resources, ensuring compliance with operational standards and regulatory requirements. This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of your existing AWS environment to identify gaps in compliance and operational practices. Our expert consultants will collaborate closely with your team to understand specific business needs and regulatory obligations. The key outcomes of this project include the development of tailored governance policies that align with industry best practices, detailed documentation outlining these policies, implementation guidelines, and monitoring strategies for ongoing compliance. Additionally, we will conduct training sessions for your staff, empowering them to effectively manage and enforce the newly established policies. By the conclusion of this project, your organization will benefit from enhanced security and compliance posture, ensuring that your AWS resources are governed effectively.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the current AWS environment to identify compliance gaps and operational inefficiencies.
- **Requirements Gathering**: Collaborate with stakeholders to gather specific business requirements and regulatory obligations.
- **Policy Development**: Design tailored governance policies that adhere to compliance standards and best practices.
- **Documentation**: Create comprehensive documentation that includes:
  - Governance policies
  - Implementation guidelines
  - Monitoring strategies
- **Implementation Support**: Assist in the implementation of the governance policies within the AWS environment.
- **Training Sessions**: Provide training for staff to ensure they are equipped to manage and enforce the governance policies effectively.
- **Ongoing Support**: Offer post-implementation support to address any questions or challenges that arise.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Assessment

- **Milestone 6**: Training Sessions Completion

- **Milestone 7**: Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Project Closure Report summarizing outcomes and next steps.
