
# Project Plan: AWS Service Catalog for DevOps Teams

## 1. Executive Summary

The AWS Service Catalog for DevOps Teams project aims to establish a tailored service catalog that streamlines the deployment of pre-approved infrastructure and application templates. The primary objective is to enhance operational efficiency by enabling DevOps teams to quickly access the resources they need while maintaining adherence to governance and compliance standards. This project will begin with a thorough analysis of the specific requirements of the DevOps teams, allowing us to identify the necessary portfolios and products to include in the catalog. Following this, we will create a structured portfolio that organizes the various products for ease of use and accessibility. Robust access controls will be implemented to manage permissions, ensuring that only authorized personnel can deploy specific resources. The project will culminate in comprehensive documentation and training sessions designed to empower teams to utilize the Service Catalog effectively. Key outcomes include reduced deployment times, improved resource management, and enhanced compliance with organizational standards.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Requirements Analysis**: Conduct interviews and workshops with DevOps teams to gather specific needs and expectations.
- **Portfolio Development**: Identify and categorize necessary portfolios and products for the AWS Service Catalog.
- **Template Creation**: Develop pre-approved infrastructure and application templates that meet the identified requirements.
- **Access Control Implementation**: Establish robust access controls to manage permissions for deploying resources.
- **Documentation**: Create comprehensive documentation detailing the usage of the Service Catalog, including guidelines and best practices.
- **Training Sessions**: Conduct training sessions for DevOps teams to ensure they are equipped to utilize the Service Catalog effectively.
- **Feedback Loop**: Establish a mechanism for ongoing feedback and improvements to the Service Catalog post-implementation.
