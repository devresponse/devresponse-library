
# Project Plan Template: EKS Cost Optimization Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary

The EKS Cost Optimization Analysis project aims to provide a comprehensive evaluation of your AWS Elastic Kubernetes Service (EKS) clusters to identify cost-saving opportunities. The primary objective of this project is to analyze your current EKS configurations, resource utilization, and billing patterns to uncover inefficiencies and recommend actionable optimizations. By examining various factors such as instance types, scaling policies, and workload distribution, our team will deliver tailored recommendations that align with your specific environment. Key outcomes will include a detailed report outlining findings, suggested optimizations, and a roadmap for implementation. This will ensure that your operational expenses are minimized while maintaining the performance and reliability of your services. Ultimately, the project seeks to empower your organization with insights that lead to smarter resource allocation and cost-effective management of your EKS infrastructure.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a preliminary review of the existing EKS setup, including cluster configurations, node types, and current usage patterns.
- **Data Collection**: Gather data on resource utilization, billing history, and performance metrics over a defined period.
- **Analysis**: 
  - Evaluate instance types and their appropriateness for workloads.
  - Review scaling policies to ensure they align with demand.
  - Analyze workload distribution across clusters and nodes.
- **Cost-Saving Opportunities**: Identify potential areas for cost reduction, including:
  - Recommendations for reserved instances and spot instances.
  - Suggestions for resource allocation adjustments.
  - Insights into optimizing scaling policies.
- **Final Report**: Compile a comprehensive report that includes:
  - A detailed breakdown of findings.
  - Actionable recommendations for cost optimization.
  - A roadmap for implementing suggested changes.
- **Presentation**: Deliver a presentation to stakeholders summarizing key findings and recommendations.
