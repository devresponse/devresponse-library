
# Project Plan Template: EKS Cost Optimization Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

The EKS Cost Optimization Audit project aims to deliver a comprehensive evaluation of your current AWS Elastic Kubernetes Service (EKS) usage. The primary objective is to identify cost-saving opportunities through a detailed analysis of your EKS configurations, resource utilization, and billing patterns. Our expert team will conduct a thorough assessment of your workloads, pinpointing underutilized resources and recommending best practices for optimizing your EKS environment. Key outcomes of this project include a clear roadmap for cost optimization tailored to your specific needs, actionable strategies for implementation, and a reduction in operational expenses while ensuring optimal performance and scalability. By the end of this audit, you will have a strategic plan that not only enhances your cost efficiency but also aligns with your business goals.
