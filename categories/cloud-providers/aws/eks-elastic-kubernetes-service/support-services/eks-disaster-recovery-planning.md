
# Project Plan Template: EKS Disaster Recovery Planning

## 1. Executive Summary

The EKS Disaster Recovery Planning project aims to develop a robust and comprehensive disaster recovery plan tailored specifically for your AWS Elastic Kubernetes Service (EKS) environment. In today's digital landscape, the resilience of cloud infrastructure is critical to maintaining business continuity. This project will begin with a thorough assessment of your current architecture, identifying potential risks and vulnerabilities that could impact operations. Key objectives include establishing effective recovery strategies that align with your business goals, outlining clear procedures for data backup, restoration processes, and failover mechanisms. The project will also focus on minimizing downtime during unexpected outages, ensuring that your organization can quickly recover and maintain service availability. Additionally, we will conduct training sessions for your team, equipping them with the knowledge and skills necessary to execute the disaster recovery plan effectively. By the end of this project, you will have a tailored disaster recovery plan that not only safeguards your operations but also enhances your overall cloud strategy, ensuring peace of mind and operational resilience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Architecture**: 
  - Review existing EKS setup and configurations.
  - Identify critical components and dependencies within the architecture.
  
- **Risk Identification**: 
  - Conduct a risk assessment to identify potential threats and vulnerabilities.
  - Evaluate the impact of various disaster scenarios on business operations.

- **Recovery Strategy Development**: 
  - Establish recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO).
  - Develop strategies for data backup, restoration, and failover mechanisms.

- **Documentation**: 
  - Create a comprehensive disaster recovery plan document.
  - Include detailed procedures for executing the recovery strategies.

- **Training Sessions**: 
  - Conduct training for your team on the disaster recovery plan.
  - Provide hands-on exercises and simulations to ensure preparedness.

- **Plan Review and Updates**: 
  - Schedule periodic reviews of the disaster recovery plan.
  - Update the plan as necessary based on changes in architecture or business objectives.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current Architecture Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing findings and recommendations.

- **Milestone 2**: Risk Assessment Completion
  - Deliverable: Risk assessment report outlining identified risks and their potential impact.

- **Milestone 3**: Development of Recovery Strategies
  - Deliverable: Documented recovery strategies including RTO and RPO.

- **Milestone 4**: Finalization of Disaster Recovery Plan
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive disaster recovery plan document.

- **Milestone 5**: Training Sessions Conducted
  - Deliverable: Training materials and session feedback report.

- **Milestone 6**: Plan Review and Update Schedule Established
  - Deliverable: Schedule for periodic reviews and updates.
