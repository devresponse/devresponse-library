
# Project Plan Template: Custom Vocabulary Development for AWS Transcribe

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Vocabulary Development for AWS Transcribe project aims to enhance transcription accuracy in specialized fields such as healthcare, legal, and technology by creating tailored vocabularies. The primary objective is to develop a comprehensive vocabulary list that reflects the unique terminology and jargon used in these sectors, thereby improving the effectiveness of AWS Transcribe for the client. This project will involve a detailed analysis of industry-specific language, ensuring that the custom vocabularies are not only accurate but also relevant to the client's specific needs. Key outcomes include the delivery of a robust vocabulary list, ongoing management, and updates to the vocabularies as industry terms evolve. By collaborating closely with the client, we will ensure that the transcription service remains effective and reliable, ultimately maximizing the benefits of AWS Transcribe for their unique industry requirements.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Analysis of Industry-Specific Terminology**: Conduct a thorough review of the specialized language used in the healthcare, legal, and technology sectors. This will involve researching common terms, phrases, and jargon that are critical for accurate transcription.

- **Development of Custom Vocabulary Lists**: Create comprehensive vocabulary lists tailored to the client's specific industry needs. This will include terms that are frequently used in the client's context, ensuring that the vocabulary is relevant and effective.

- **Collaboration with Client**: Engage in regular meetings with the client to gather insights on their specific challenges and requirements. This collaboration will help refine the vocabulary lists and ensure they meet the client's expectations.

- **Ongoing Management and Updates**: Establish a plan for the ongoing management of the vocabularies, including regular updates to incorporate new terms as they emerge in the industry. This will ensure that the transcription service remains current and effective.

- **Final Review and Approval**: Present the final vocabulary lists to the client for review and approval, making any necessary adjustments based on their feedback.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Industry Terminology Analysis  
  **Deliverable**: Detailed report on industry-specific terminology used in healthcare, legal, and technology sectors.

- **Milestone 2**: Draft Custom Vocabulary Lists  
  **Deliverable**: Initial draft of custom vocabulary lists tailored to the client's needs.

- **Milestone 3**: Client Review and Feedback  
  **Deliverable**: Summary of client feedback and any required changes to the vocabulary lists.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Vocabulary Lists Submission  
  **Deliverable**: Finalized custom vocabulary lists approved by the client.

- **Milestone 5**: Ongoing Management Plan  
  **Deliverable**: A documented plan for the ongoing management and updates of the vocabularies.
