
# Project Plan Template: AWS Transcribe for Accessibility Compliance

## 1. Executive Summary

The AWS Transcribe for Accessibility Compliance project aims to empower organizations to enhance the accessibility of their audio and video content, ensuring compliance with critical standards such as the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) and the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). The primary objective is to leverage AWS Transcribe technology to generate accurate captions and transcripts, making content accessible to individuals with hearing impairments. This project will begin with a comprehensive analysis of the client's existing audio and video materials, identifying areas for improvement and compliance gaps. Following this, we will implement AWS Transcribe to produce high-quality transcripts and captions, ensuring they are formatted appropriately for various platforms. Key outcomes include improved accessibility of content, increased audience reach, and fulfillment of legal requirements. By the end of this project, organizations will be equipped with the tools and knowledge necessary to maintain ongoing compliance and enhance their overall content strategy.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the client's existing audio and video materials to identify compliance gaps and accessibility needs.
- **Implementation of AWS Transcribe**: Set up and configure AWS Transcribe to generate accurate captions and transcripts for the identified content.
- **Quality Assurance**: Review and edit the generated transcripts and captions to ensure accuracy and adherence to accessibility standards.
- **Best Practices Guidance**: Provide clients with best practices for captioning, including timing, formatting, and platform-specific requirements.
- **Final Output Delivery**: Deliver the final transcripts and captions in the required formats for various platforms (e.g., web, social media, etc.).
- **Training and Support**: Offer training sessions for client staff on how to use AWS Transcribe and maintain accessibility compliance in future projects.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing findings and recommendations.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Implementation of AWS Transcribe
  - Deliverable: Configured AWS Transcribe environment and initial batch of transcripts.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Quality Assurance Review

- **Milestone 4**: Best Practices Guidance
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive guide on captioning best practices.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Output Delivery
  - Deliverable: Final transcripts and captions delivered in required formats.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Training and Support Completion
  - Deliverable: Training session completion report and support documentation.
