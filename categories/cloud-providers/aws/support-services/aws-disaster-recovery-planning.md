
# Project Plan Template: AWS Disaster Recovery Planning

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, ensuring business continuity is paramount, especially when leveraging AWS cloud environments. The AWS Disaster Recovery Planning project aims to develop a comprehensive disaster recovery plan tailored specifically for AWS infrastructures. The primary objective is to conduct a detailed analysis of current AWS setups and identify critical business functions that require protection. Key outcomes of this project will include the establishment of effective backup strategies, the outlining of failover mechanisms to ensure minimal downtime during unexpected outages, and the definition of clear recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) that align with the organization's business needs. This project will culminate in a strategic guide that not only details the disaster recovery plan but also provides actionable steps for implementation. By the end of this project, your organization will be equipped with the necessary tools and strategies to mitigate risks associated with unforeseen disruptions, ensuring a robust and resilient AWS environment.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current AWS Infrastructure**: 
  - Conduct a thorough review of existing AWS setups, including services in use, configurations, and current backup solutions.
  
- **Identification of Critical Business Functions**: 
  - Collaborate with stakeholders to identify and prioritize critical business functions that must be maintained during a disaster.

- **Development of Backup Strategies**: 
  - Design and recommend effective backup strategies tailored to the identified critical functions, including frequency, storage solutions, and data integrity checks.

- **Establishment of Failover Mechanisms**: 
  - Outline failover mechanisms that ensure minimal downtime, including automated failover processes and manual intervention protocols.

- **Definition of RTO and RPO**: 
  - Work with stakeholders to define clear recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) for each critical function.

- **Documentation of the Disaster Recovery Plan**: 
  - Create a comprehensive document that includes all findings, strategies, and procedures for disaster recovery.

- **Implementation Guidance**: 
  - Provide actionable steps and recommendations for implementing the disaster recovery plan within the organization.

- **Training and Support**: 
  - Offer training sessions for key personnel on the disaster recovery plan and ongoing support during the implementation phase.
