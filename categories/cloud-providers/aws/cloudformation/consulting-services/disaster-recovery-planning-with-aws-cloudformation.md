
# Project Plan Template: Disaster Recovery Planning with CloudFormation

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, ensuring business continuity is paramount. The Disaster Recovery Planning project aims to leverage AWS CloudFormation to create robust and scalable disaster recovery solutions tailored to your organization's needs. The primary objective of this project is to conduct a comprehensive assessment of your current infrastructure and recovery requirements, followed by the design and implementation of a customized CloudFormation template. This template will automate the deployment of your disaster recovery environment, ensuring that all critical components are replicated and ready for failover. Key outcomes include a validated recovery plan that minimizes data loss and downtime, detailed documentation for ongoing management, and support for your team to effectively handle disaster recovery processes. By the end of this project, your organization will be equipped with a reliable disaster recovery strategy that enhances resilience against unexpected disruptions.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment Phase**: 
  - Conduct a thorough analysis of the existing infrastructure.
  - Identify critical applications and data that require protection.
  - Gather recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) from stakeholders.

- **Design Phase**: 
  - Develop a customized AWS CloudFormation template based on the assessment findings.
  - Ensure the template includes all necessary resources for a complete disaster recovery environment.
  - Review the design with stakeholders for feedback and approval.

- **Implementation Phase**: 
  - Deploy the CloudFormation template in a test environment.
  - Configure all components to ensure they are ready for failover.
  - Conduct rigorous testing of the disaster recovery plan, including failover and failback scenarios.

- **Documentation and Training**: 
  - Create detailed documentation outlining the disaster recovery process and procedures.
  - Provide training sessions for your team to ensure they are equipped to manage the disaster recovery environment.

- **Ongoing Support**: 
  - Offer support for a specified period post-implementation to address any issues and provide guidance.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the Assessment Phase
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing current infrastructure and recovery requirements.

- **Milestone 2**: Approval of the CloudFormation Template Design
  - Deliverable: Customized CloudFormation template design document.

- **Milestone 3**: Successful Deployment in Test Environment
  - Deliverable: Deployed CloudFormation template with all components configured.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Testing Phase
  - Deliverable: Testing report with results of failover and failback scenarios.

- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of Documentation and Training
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive documentation and training materials.

- **Milestone 6**: End of Ongoing Support Period
  - Deliverable: Support closure report summarizing assistance provided.
