
# Project Plan Template: Elastic Beanstalk Compliance Automation

## 1. Executive Summary

The Elastic Beanstalk Compliance Automation project aims to enhance the compliance posture of your AWS Elastic Beanstalk environments by automating the necessary checks to adhere to critical industry standards such as {{GDPR}}, {{HIPAA}}, and {{PCI DSS}}. This initiative will involve the implementation of tailored AWS Config rules that align with your specific compliance requirements, ensuring that your environments are continuously monitored for compliance violations. The key outcomes of this project include the establishment of comprehensive audit trails that provide transparency and accountability, essential for regulatory audits. By automating compliance checks, your organization can significantly reduce the manual effort involved in compliance management, allowing your teams to focus on core development activities while maintaining a secure and compliant environment. Ultimately, this project will deliver a robust compliance framework that not only meets regulatory requirements but also enhances the overall reliability and security of your applications.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Environment**: Conduct a thorough assessment of the existing AWS Elastic Beanstalk environments to identify compliance gaps and requirements.
  
- **Development of AWS Config Rules**: Design and implement AWS Config rules tailored to meet the compliance standards of {{GDPR}}, {{HIPAA}}, and {{PCI DSS}}.

- **Automation of Compliance Checks**: Automate the compliance checks to ensure continuous monitoring and reporting of compliance status.

- **Audit Trail Implementation**: Establish comprehensive audit trails that log compliance checks, violations, and remediation actions taken.

- **Training and Documentation**: Provide training sessions for relevant stakeholders and create detailed documentation on compliance processes and tools used.

- **Ongoing Support and Maintenance**: Offer ongoing support and maintenance for the compliance automation framework to ensure it remains effective and up-to-date with regulatory changes.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the assessment of the current environment.
  - **Deliverable**: Assessment report detailing compliance gaps and requirements.

- **Milestone 2**: Development and implementation of AWS Config rules.
  - **Deliverable**: Documented AWS Config rules and deployment confirmation.

- **Milestone 3**: Automation of compliance checks.
  - **Deliverable**: Automated compliance check system operational and tested.

- **Milestone 4**: Implementation of audit trails.
  - **Deliverable**: Audit trail system operational with initial logs generated.

- **Milestone 5**: Training and documentation completion.
  - **Deliverable**: Training materials and user documentation delivered.

- **Milestone 6**: Project closure and handover.
  - **Deliverable**: Final project report and handover of all documentation and systems.
