
# Project Plan Template: ECS Custom Metrics and Alerts Setup

## 1. Executive Summary

The ECS Custom Metrics and Alerts Setup project aims to establish a comprehensive monitoring system for AWS Elastic Container Service (ECS) utilizing Amazon CloudWatch. The primary objective is to enhance the visibility of application performance and health by configuring custom metrics and alerts tailored to your specific deployment. This project will involve a thorough analysis of the current ECS setup, identifying key performance indicators (KPIs) that are critical to your operations. By implementing a robust alerting mechanism, the project will ensure that your team is promptly notified of any critical events, such as resource utilization thresholds or application errors. The key outcomes of this project include improved application performance, reduced downtime, and actionable insights for ongoing monitoring practices. Ultimately, this proactive approach will empower your team to address issues swiftly, ensuring optimal operation of your ECS applications.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Analysis of Current ECS Deployment**: 
  - Review existing ECS architecture and configurations.
  - Identify key performance indicators (KPIs) relevant to your applications.

- **Configuration of Custom Metrics**: 
  - Set up CloudWatch to track identified KPIs.
  - Ensure metrics are tailored to meet the specific needs of your applications.

- **Establishment of Alerts**: 
  - Configure alerts for critical events, including but not limited to:
    - Resource utilization thresholds (CPU, memory, etc.).
    - Application errors and performance degradation.
  - Define alert notification channels (e.g., email, SMS, Slack).

- **Documentation and Reporting**: 
  - Provide a comprehensive report detailing:
    - Configured metrics and their purpose.
    - Alert thresholds and notification settings.
    - Recommendations for ongoing monitoring practices.

- **Training and Support**: 
  - Conduct a training session for your team on how to interpret metrics and respond to alerts.
  - Offer support for a defined period post-implementation to address any questions or issues.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of current ECS deployment analysis
  - **Deliverable**: Analysis report with identified KPIs.

- **Milestone 2**: Configuration of custom metrics in CloudWatch
  - **Deliverable**: Documented metrics setup.

- **Milestone 3**: Establishment of alerts for critical events
  - **Deliverable**: Alert configuration documentation.

- **Milestone 4**: Delivery of comprehensive report
  - **Deliverable**: Final report including metrics, alerts, and recommendations.

- **Milestone 5**: Training session for the team
  - **Deliverable**: Training materials and session feedback.
