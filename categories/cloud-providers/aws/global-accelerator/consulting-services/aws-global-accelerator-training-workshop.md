
# Project Plan Template: AWS Global Accelerator Training Workshop

## 1. Executive Summary

The AWS Global Accelerator Training Workshop aims to empower teams with the knowledge and skills necessary to effectively utilize and manage AWS Global Accelerator. This comprehensive workshop will provide participants with a deep understanding of the fundamental concepts, architecture, features, and benefits of AWS Global Accelerator. Through hands-on training and practical exercises, attendees will learn how to set up and configure the service to optimize application performance and availability. Key outcomes of the workshop include enhanced capabilities in monitoring and troubleshooting, enabling teams to leverage AWS Global Accelerator to its fullest potential. By the conclusion of the session, participants will be equipped with actionable strategies to improve their cloud infrastructure and operations, ultimately maximizing their investment in AWS services. This workshop is an invaluable resource for businesses seeking to enhance their operational efficiency and service delivery through advanced cloud solutions.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the AWS Global Accelerator Training Workshop includes the following components:

- **Pre-Workshop Preparation**: 
  - Conduct a needs assessment to tailor the workshop content to the participants' skill levels and organizational requirements.
  - Develop training materials, including presentations, handouts, and practical exercise guides.

- **Workshop Delivery**:
  - Introduction to AWS Global Accelerator: Overview of its purpose, architecture, and key features.
  - Hands-on Training: Participants will engage in practical exercises to set up and configure AWS Global Accelerator.
  - Best Practices: Discussion on monitoring, troubleshooting, and optimizing performance.
  - Q&A Session: Address participant queries and provide additional insights.

- **Post-Workshop Support**:
  - Provide access to recorded sessions and supplementary materials.
  - Offer a follow-up consultation to address any further questions or challenges faced by participants.
