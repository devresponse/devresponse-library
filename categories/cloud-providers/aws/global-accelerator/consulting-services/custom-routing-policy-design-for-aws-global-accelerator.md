
# Project Plan Template: Custom Routing Policy Design for AWS Global Accelerator

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Routing Policy Design for AWS Global Accelerator project aims to enhance the performance of your applications by implementing tailored routing policies that ensure efficient traffic distribution. This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of your existing infrastructure and traffic patterns, allowing our team of AWS experts to identify areas for improvement. We will work closely with your team to understand your specific requirements and business objectives, ensuring that the routing policies we design align with your goals. Key outcomes of this project include the development of detailed documentation for the routing policies, implementation guidelines, and best practices for ongoing management. Additionally, we will provide support during the implementation phase to facilitate a smooth transition and optimize performance. By the conclusion of this project, you will possess a robust routing strategy that maximizes the advantages of AWS Global Accelerator, resulting in improved application responsiveness and an enhanced user experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the current infrastructure and traffic patterns.
- **Requirements Gathering**: Collaborate with your team to gather specific requirements and objectives.
- **Design Custom Routing Policies**: Develop tailored routing policies based on the analysis and requirements.
- **Documentation**: Create detailed documentation of the routing policies, including implementation guidelines and best practices.
- **Implementation Support**: Provide support during the implementation phase to ensure a smooth transition.
- **Performance Monitoring**: Establish metrics for monitoring the performance of the new routing policies post-implementation.
- **Training**: Offer training sessions for your team on managing and optimizing the routing policies.
