
# Project Plan Template: AWS Global Accelerator Performance Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

The AWS Global Accelerator Performance Audit aims to deliver a comprehensive evaluation of your current AWS Global Accelerator setup. The primary objective of this project is to identify potential bottlenecks and inefficiencies that may hinder the performance of your global applications. Our expert team will conduct an in-depth analysis of your configurations, focusing on routing policies, health checks, and endpoint configurations. The key outcomes of this audit will include a detailed report highlighting areas for improvement, along with actionable recommendations tailored to your specific needs. By optimizing your AWS Global Accelerator setup, we aim to enhance your cloud performance and reliability, ensuring low latency and high availability for your applications. Following the audit, a follow-up consultation will be provided to discuss our findings and assist you in implementing the suggested improvements effectively.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Review existing AWS Global Accelerator configurations.
- **Routing Policies Evaluation**: Analyze current routing policies for efficiency and effectiveness.
- **Health Checks Review**: Examine health check configurations to ensure they are functioning optimally.
- **Endpoint Configuration Analysis**: Assess endpoint configurations for potential improvements.
- **Performance Bottleneck Identification**: Identify any bottlenecks or inefficiencies impacting performance.
- **Actionable Recommendations**: Develop tailored recommendations based on the audit findings.
- **Comprehensive Report**: Prepare a detailed report summarizing the audit process, findings, and recommendations.
- **Follow-Up Consultation**: Conduct a consultation session to discuss the report and assist with implementation strategies.
