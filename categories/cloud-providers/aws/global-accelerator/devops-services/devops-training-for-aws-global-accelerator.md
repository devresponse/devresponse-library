
# Project Plan: DevOps Training for AWS Global Accelerator

## 1. Executive Summary

The "DevOps Training for AWS Global Accelerator" project aims to develop a comprehensive training program specifically designed for teams eager to integrate AWS Global Accelerator into their DevOps practices. The primary objective of this initiative is to equip participants with the essential knowledge and skills required to enhance deployment speed, improve system reliability, and foster collaboration among team members. The training will encompass key concepts and best practices, ensuring that teams can seamlessly incorporate AWS Global Accelerator into their existing workflows. By engaging in hands-on exercises and real-world scenarios, participants will gain practical experience that will empower them to optimize their software development and IT operations effectively. Additionally, the program will include assessments to measure understanding and retention of the material, ensuring that teams are well-prepared to implement these methodologies in their projects. Ultimately, the project seeks to drive operational excellence and innovation within organizations by leveraging the capabilities of AWS Global Accelerator.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the "DevOps Training for AWS Global Accelerator" project includes the following key components:

- **Curriculum Development**: Design a training curriculum that covers essential concepts, best practices, and hands-on exercises related to AWS Global Accelerator.
- **Training Delivery**: Conduct training sessions that include lectures, interactive discussions, and practical exercises.
- **Hands-On Labs**: Create lab environments where participants can practice integrating AWS Global Accelerator into simulated workflows.
- **Assessment and Evaluation**: Develop assessments to evaluate participants' understanding and retention of the training material.
- **Feedback Mechanism**: Implement a feedback system to gather participant insights and improve future training sessions.
- **Documentation**: Provide comprehensive training materials, including guides, slides, and reference documents for participants.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Curriculum Development
  - Deliverable: Finalized training curriculum document
- **Milestone 2**: Training Session Scheduling
  - Deliverable: Schedule of training sessions and participant registration
- **Milestone 3**: Delivery of Training Sessions
  - Deliverable: Conducted training sessions with participant attendance records
- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Hands-On Labs
  - Deliverable: Lab environment setup and participant completion reports
- **Milestone 5**: Assessment Administration
  - Deliverable: Completed assessments and evaluation reports
- **Milestone 6**: Feedback Collection and Analysis
  - Deliverable: Feedback report summarizing participant insights
- **Milestone 7**: Final Documentation Delivery
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive training materials package
