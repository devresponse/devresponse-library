
# Project Plan Template: Custom Subdomain Management

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Subdomain Management project aims to enhance your DNS structure through the setup and management of custom subdomains within AWS Route 53. Our primary objective is to improve organization and performance by creating a tailored subdomain architecture that aligns with your specific business needs. The project will begin with a comprehensive assessment of your current DNS configuration, allowing our expert consultants to identify areas for improvement. Key outcomes include the successful implementation of custom subdomains, optimized for performance and security, as well as guidance on best practices for DNS record management. By the end of this project, you will benefit from a streamlined and efficient DNS structure that not only meets your operational requirements but also supports the overall effectiveness of your cloud services.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current DNS Configuration**: 
  - Review existing DNS settings and architecture.
  - Identify inefficiencies and areas for improvement.

- **Strategic Planning of Subdomain Architecture**: 
  - Collaborate with stakeholders to define subdomain requirements.
  - Develop a comprehensive plan for subdomain structure.

- **Implementation of Custom Subdomains**: 
  - Create and configure custom subdomains in AWS Route 53.
  - Ensure proper settings for optimal performance and security.

- **Best Practices Guidance**: 
  - Provide recommendations on managing DNS records, including TTL settings and routing policies.
  - Offer training sessions for your team on DNS management.

- **Documentation and Support**: 
  - Deliver detailed documentation of the new DNS structure.
  - Provide ongoing support for a specified period post-implementation.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of DNS Configuration Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report outlining current DNS configuration and recommendations.

- **Milestone 2**: Approval of Subdomain Architecture Plan
  - Deliverable: Strategic plan document detailing the proposed subdomain structure.

- **Milestone 3**: Implementation of Custom Subdomains
  - Deliverable: Successfully configured custom subdomains in AWS Route 53.

- **Milestone 4**: Delivery of Best Practices Documentation
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive guide on DNS record management and best practices.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Review and Support Handoff
  - Deliverable: Final project report and transition to support phase.
