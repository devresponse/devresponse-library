
# Project Plan Template: Data Residency and Sovereignty Compliance Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, ensuring compliance with data residency and sovereignty requirements is crucial for businesses operating in multiple jurisdictions. The objective of this project is to develop a comprehensive strategy utilizing AWS Macie to identify and manage sensitive data stored in non-compliant regions. This project will involve a thorough assessment of current data storage practices, pinpointing areas of non-compliance and potential risks. Key outcomes include the implementation of robust controls to restrict data movement, ensuring adherence to local regulations, and providing ongoing support and guidance to maintain compliance as your business evolves. By focusing on tailored solutions, this project will empower your organization to navigate the complexities of data governance effectively, ultimately safeguarding your data assets and enhancing your organization's reputation in the marketplace.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment Phase**: 
  - Conduct a comprehensive review of current data storage practices.
  - Identify sensitive data and assess its compliance with local regulations.
  - Evaluate existing data governance frameworks and policies.

- **Strategy Development**: 
  - Develop a tailored data residency and sovereignty compliance strategy.
  - Utilize AWS Macie for data discovery and classification.
  - Identify areas of non-compliance and recommend corrective actions.

- **Implementation Phase**: 
  - Implement controls to restrict data movement to non-compliant regions.
  - Establish monitoring mechanisms to ensure ongoing compliance.
  - Provide training and resources for staff on data governance best practices.

- **Ongoing Support**: 
  - Offer continuous support and guidance to adapt to evolving regulations.
  - Conduct periodic reviews and updates to the compliance strategy.
  - Facilitate workshops and training sessions to keep staff informed.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the Assessment Phase
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing current practices and compliance gaps.

- **Milestone 2**: Development of Compliance Strategy
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Compliance Strategy Document.

- **Milestone 3**: Implementation of Controls
  - Deliverable: Implementation Report outlining controls and monitoring mechanisms.

- **Milestone 4**: Training and Resources Provided
  - Deliverable: Training Materials and Attendance Records from workshops.

- **Milestone 5**: Ongoing Support Initiated
  - Deliverable: Support Plan and Schedule for periodic reviews.
