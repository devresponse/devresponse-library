
# Proactive Insider Threat Detection Program Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, safeguarding sensitive data from insider threats is paramount. The Proactive Insider Threat Detection Program aims to establish a comprehensive insider threat detection framework utilizing AWS Macie, a powerful tool for monitoring and analyzing user behavior. This project will begin with a thorough assessment of your organization's current security posture, identifying potential vulnerabilities that could be exploited by insiders. Our expert consultants will configure AWS Macie to establish anomaly detection rules tailored to your organization's specific needs, ensuring that any unusual behavior is promptly flagged. Key outcomes of this project include the development of detailed response playbooks that guide your team in effectively addressing detected threats, as well as training sessions designed to equip your staff with the knowledge and skills necessary to recognize and respond to insider threats proactively. By the end of this project, your organization will have a robust framework in place to mitigate risks associated with insider threats, significantly enhancing your overall cloud security strategy.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment Phase**: 
  - Conduct a comprehensive evaluation of the current security posture.
  - Identify potential vulnerabilities related to insider threats.
  
- **Configuration Phase**: 
  - Set up AWS Macie for your organization.
  - Develop and implement anomaly detection rules customized to your specific environment.

- **Response Playbook Development**: 
  - Create detailed response playbooks that outline procedures for addressing detected threats.
  - Ensure playbooks are aligned with your organization's existing incident response protocols.

- **Training and Knowledge Transfer**: 
  - Conduct training sessions for staff on recognizing and responding to insider threats.
  - Provide ongoing support and resources to ensure long-term success.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Security Posture Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing vulnerabilities and recommendations.

- **Milestone 2**: Configuration of AWS Macie
  - Deliverable: Configured AWS Macie instance with tailored anomaly detection rules.

- **Milestone 3**: Development of Response Playbooks
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive response playbooks for insider threat incidents.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Staff Training Sessions
  - Deliverable: Training materials and attendance records.
