
# Project Plan Template: AWS DeepRacer Reinforcement Learning Workshop

## 1. Executive Summary

The AWS DeepRacer Reinforcement Learning Workshop aims to equip participants with a deep understanding of reinforcement learning principles through hands-on experience with AWS DeepRacer. This interactive workshop will provide an overview of the fundamental concepts of reinforcement learning, the architecture of AWS DeepRacer, and effective strategies for optimizing racing performance. Participants will engage in practical activities, including setting up their AWS DeepRacer environment, training their models, and competing in races against fellow attendees. By the conclusion of the workshop, participants will have acquired valuable insights and practical skills that will enable them to leverage AWS DeepRacer for their own machine learning projects. This workshop is tailored for teams or individuals eager to enhance their knowledge and skills in the rapidly evolving field of machine learning, ultimately fostering innovation and practical application in their respective domains.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Workshop Preparation**
  - Develop workshop curriculum and materials.
  - Set up AWS DeepRacer environments for participants.
  - Prepare necessary hardware and software requirements.

- **Workshop Delivery**
  - Conduct an introductory session on reinforcement learning principles.
  - Provide an overview of AWS DeepRacer architecture.
  - Facilitate hands-on activities for participants:
    - Setting up AWS DeepRacer environments.
    - Training reinforcement learning models.
    - Racing simulations and competitions.

- **Post-Workshop Support**
  - Provide participants with access to workshop materials and resources.
  - Offer a Q&A session to address any lingering questions.
  - Gather feedback from participants to improve future workshops.
