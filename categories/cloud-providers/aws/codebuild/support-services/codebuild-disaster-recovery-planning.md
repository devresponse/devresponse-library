
# Project Plan Template: CodeBuild Disaster Recovery Planning

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's fast-paced digital landscape, ensuring the resilience of your AWS CodeBuild projects is paramount. The primary objective of the CodeBuild Disaster Recovery Planning project is to create a comprehensive disaster recovery plan tailored specifically for AWS CodeBuild environments. This project will provide a detailed assessment of the current CodeBuild setup, identifying potential vulnerabilities and risks associated with unexpected failures or outages. Key outcomes will include the development of a step-by-step recovery strategy, defining recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO), and establishing robust backup procedures. Additionally, we will create detailed documentation for quick reference during a disaster, empowering your team with the knowledge and tools necessary to minimize downtime and data loss. Ultimately, this project aims to ensure business continuity and operational efficiency, allowing your organization to navigate disruptions with confidence and resilience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current CodeBuild Setup**: Conduct a thorough review of the existing AWS CodeBuild environment, including configurations, integrations, and dependencies.
- **Risk Identification**: Identify potential vulnerabilities and risks that could lead to service disruptions or data loss.
- **Recovery Strategy Development**: Create a detailed recovery strategy that includes:
  - Definition of Recovery Time Objectives (RTO)
  - Definition of Recovery Point Objectives (RPO)
  - Step-by-step procedures for recovery
- **Backup Procedures**: Establish and document backup procedures to ensure data integrity and availability.
- **Documentation Creation**: Develop comprehensive documentation that outlines the disaster recovery plan, including roles and responsibilities, communication protocols, and recovery steps.
- **Training and Knowledge Transfer**: Provide training sessions for the team to ensure they are familiar with the disaster recovery plan and procedures.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current Setup Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing current configurations and identified vulnerabilities.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Risk Assessment and Recovery Strategy Development
  - Deliverable: Document outlining RTO, RPO, and recovery procedures.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Backup Procedures Established
  - Deliverable: Backup procedure documentation.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Documentation and Training
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive disaster recovery plan document and training materials.
