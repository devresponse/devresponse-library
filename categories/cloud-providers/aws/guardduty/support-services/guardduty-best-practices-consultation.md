
# GuardDuty Best Practices Consultation Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The GuardDuty Best Practices Consultation project aims to enhance the implementation of AWS GuardDuty within your organization. Our primary objective is to conduct a comprehensive review of your current GuardDuty setup, identifying areas for improvement and ensuring alignment with AWS best practices for cloud security. The project will involve a detailed analysis of your threat detection capabilities and the effectiveness of your existing security policies. Key outcomes include a tailored set of actionable recommendations designed to optimize your GuardDuty usage, empowering your team with the knowledge and tools necessary to protect your cloud resources effectively. Additionally, we will provide a detailed report summarizing our findings and recommendations, along with a follow-up consultation to address any questions or concerns. By the end of this project, you will have a clear understanding of how to leverage GuardDuty to its fullest potential, ensuring robust security for your cloud environment.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the GuardDuty Best Practices Consultation includes the following key activities:

- **Initial Assessment**: Review the current GuardDuty setup and configurations.
- **Threat Detection Analysis**: Evaluate the effectiveness of existing threat detection capabilities.
- **Security Policy Assessment**: Analyze current security policies in relation to GuardDuty best practices.
- **Recommendations Development**: Provide tailored recommendations for optimizing GuardDuty usage.
- **Reporting**: Compile a detailed report summarizing findings, recommendations, and best practices.
- **Follow-up Consultation**: Conduct a follow-up session to discuss the report and address any questions.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Reporting
