
# Project Plan Template: Real-Time Threat Intelligence with GuardDuty

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Real-Time Threat Intelligence with GuardDuty" project aims to enhance your organization's cybersecurity posture by integrating AWS GuardDuty with external threat intelligence feeds. The primary objective is to leverage GuardDuty's advanced machine learning capabilities alongside curated threat intelligence sources to create a comprehensive security framework. This integration will significantly improve real-time threat detection and response capabilities, allowing your organization to proactively identify and mitigate potential threats. Key outcomes include a thorough assessment of the current AWS environment, the selection of appropriate threat intelligence feeds, and the effective configuration of GuardDuty to utilize these feeds. Additionally, we will conduct training sessions for your team to ensure they can interpret alerts and respond effectively. Ultimately, this project will streamline incident response processes and bolster your organization's confidence in its cloud security.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current AWS Environment**
  - Review existing AWS configurations and security measures.
  - Identify gaps in current threat detection capabilities.

- **Selection of Threat Intelligence Feeds**
  - Research and evaluate potential external threat intelligence sources.
  - Recommend the most suitable feeds based on organizational needs.

- **Configuration of AWS GuardDuty**
  - Set up GuardDuty within the AWS environment.
  - Integrate selected threat intelligence feeds into GuardDuty.
  - Customize alert settings to align with organizational priorities.

- **Training and Knowledge Transfer**
  - Develop training materials tailored to your team’s needs.
  - Conduct training sessions to ensure understanding of GuardDuty alerts and incident response protocols.
  - Provide ongoing support and resources for team members post-training.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Completion of AWS Environment Assessment**
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing current configurations and identified gaps.

- **Milestone 2: Selection of Threat Intelligence Feeds**
  - Deliverable: Recommendation report outlining selected threat intelligence sources.

- **Milestone 3: Configuration of GuardDuty**
  - Deliverable: Documented configuration settings and integration process.

- **Milestone 4: Training Sessions**
  - Deliverable: Training materials and completion of training sessions with team feedback.
