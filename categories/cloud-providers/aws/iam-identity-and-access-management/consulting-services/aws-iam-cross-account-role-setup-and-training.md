
# Project Plan Template: AWS IAM Cross-Account Role Setup and Training

## 1. Executive Summary

The AWS IAM Cross-Account Role Setup and Training project aims to establish secure cross-account roles within AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM) to enhance collaboration between multiple AWS accounts. The primary objective is to assess the current IAM setup, identify necessary permissions, and configure roles that allow users from one account to access resources in another account securely. Key outcomes of this project include a comprehensive evaluation of existing IAM configurations, the implementation of best practices for role management, and the delivery of detailed training sessions for your team. By the end of this project, your organization will have a robust IAM framework that not only improves cloud security posture but also facilitates seamless collaboration across AWS accounts. The training will empower your team with the knowledge and skills required to manage permissions effectively, ensuring that they can leverage the new setup to its fullest potential.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current IAM Setup**: 
  - Review existing IAM roles, policies, and permissions across all relevant AWS accounts.
  - Identify gaps and areas for improvement in the current configuration.

- **Role Configuration**:
  - Define the necessary cross-account roles based on the assessment findings.
  - Configure IAM roles with appropriate permissions to enable secure access between accounts.
  - Implement best practices for role naming conventions and policy management.

- **Training Development**:
  - Create training materials that cover the following topics:
    - Overview of AWS IAM and cross-account roles.
    - Best practices for managing permissions and access.
    - Hands-on exercises to practice role management and access configurations.

- **Training Delivery**:
  - Conduct training sessions for your team, including:
    - Interactive workshops and hands-on exercises.
    - Q&A sessions to address specific concerns and scenarios.
    - Distribution of documentation and resources for future reference.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current IAM Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing current IAM setup and identified gaps.

- **Milestone 2**: Configuration of Cross-Account Roles
  - Deliverable: Documented IAM roles and policies configured for cross-account access.

- **Milestone 3**: Development of Training Materials
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive training materials, including presentations and hands-on exercises.

- **Milestone 4**: Delivery of Training Sessions
  - Deliverable: Completion of training sessions with feedback collected from participants.
