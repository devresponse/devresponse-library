
# Project Plan Template: Service Control Policies (SCP) Implementation

## 1. Executive Summary

The Service Control Policies (SCP) Implementation project aims to enhance the governance and access control mechanisms of organizations utilizing AWS by designing and implementing tailored SCPs. The primary objective is to conduct a comprehensive assessment of the current AWS Organizations setup, identifying opportunities to strengthen security and compliance through effective policy management. By collaborating closely with your team, we will define clear and actionable policies that align with your business objectives, ensuring that only authorized actions are permitted across your AWS accounts. Key outcomes of this project include the successful implementation of SCPs, comprehensive documentation of the policies, and strategic recommendations for ongoing management and monitoring. This initiative will empower your organization to maintain a secure and compliant cloud environment, ultimately facilitating better resource management and risk mitigation.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment Phase**: 
  - Conduct a thorough review of the existing AWS Organizations setup.
  - Identify current governance and access control challenges.
  - Gather requirements from key stakeholders to understand specific needs.

- **Design Phase**: 
  - Develop tailored SCPs based on the assessment findings.
  - Ensure alignment with organizational objectives and compliance requirements.
  - Create a draft of the SCPs for review and feedback.

- **Implementation Phase**: 
  - Deploy the approved SCPs across the AWS accounts.
  - Conduct testing to ensure policies are functioning as intended.
  - Provide training to the internal team on managing and monitoring SCPs.

- **Documentation Phase**: 
  - Compile comprehensive documentation of the implemented SCPs.
  - Include guidelines for ongoing management and monitoring.
  - Deliver a final report summarizing the project outcomes and recommendations.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the Assessment Phase
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing current setup and identified challenges.

- **Milestone 2**: Approval of SCP Designs
  - Deliverable: Draft SCPs document for stakeholder review.

- **Milestone 3**: Successful Implementation of SCPs
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of SCP deployment and testing results.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Documentation and Training
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive documentation and training materials provided to the team.
