## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Direct Connect Compliance and Governance Assessment includes the following key activities:

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a kickoff meeting with stakeholders to understand current AWS Direct Connect configurations and compliance concerns.
- **Documentation Review**: Analyze existing documentation related to AWS Direct Connect, including architecture diagrams, data transfer protocols, and security policies.
- **Compliance Gap Analysis**: Evaluate the current setup against relevant regulations (e.g., {{regulation_1}}, {{regulation_2}}, {{regulation_3}}) and internal governance policies to identify compliance gaps.
- **Security Assessment**: Review security measures in place, including access controls, encryption methods, and monitoring practices.
- **Data Transfer Protocol Evaluation**: Assess the efficiency and security of data transfer protocols utilized in the AWS Direct Connect setup.
- **Reporting**: Compile findings into a comprehensive report that includes:
  - Summary of compliance gaps identified
  - Recommendations for remediation
  - Best practices for ongoing compliance and governance
- **Presentation of Findings**: Present the report to stakeholders and discuss recommendations and next steps.
