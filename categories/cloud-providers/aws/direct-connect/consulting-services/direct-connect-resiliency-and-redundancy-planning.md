
# Project Plan Template: Direct Connect Resiliency and Redundancy Planning

## 1. Executive Summary

The Direct Connect Resiliency and Redundancy Planning project aims to design and implement a robust AWS Direct Connect architecture that enhances business continuity through redundancy. The primary objective is to assess the current infrastructure to identify vulnerabilities that could lead to potential downtime, thereby ensuring uninterrupted service delivery for businesses reliant on AWS Direct Connect. This project will deliver a comprehensive plan that includes configurations for multiple Direct Connect connections, failover strategies, and monitoring solutions. Key outcomes will include a fully implemented architecture, tested failover mechanisms, and seamless integration with existing systems. Additionally, the consultant will provide thorough documentation and training for the client's IT team, empowering them to effectively manage and maintain the new architecture. This initiative is critical for safeguarding operations and ensuring that clients can rely on consistent and reliable connectivity.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment Phase**: 
  - Conduct a thorough assessment of the current AWS Direct Connect infrastructure.
  - Identify potential vulnerabilities and risks that could lead to downtime.
  
- **Design Phase**: 
  - Develop a comprehensive architecture plan that includes:
    - Multiple Direct Connect connections.
    - Failover strategies to ensure business continuity.
    - Monitoring solutions to proactively manage connectivity.
  
- **Implementation Phase**: 
  - Set up the designed architecture in the AWS environment.
  - Test failover mechanisms to ensure they function as intended.
  - Ensure seamless integration with existing systems and workflows.
  
- **Documentation and Training**: 
  - Create detailed documentation outlining the architecture, configurations, and operational procedures.
  - Provide training sessions for the client's IT team to ensure they are equipped to manage and maintain the new architecture effectively.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the assessment phase.
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing vulnerabilities and recommendations.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of the architecture design.
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive architecture plan including configurations and strategies.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of the implementation phase.
  - Deliverable: Fully operational AWS Direct Connect architecture with tested failover mechanisms.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Delivery of documentation and training.
  - Deliverable: Documentation package and completion of training sessions for the IT team.
