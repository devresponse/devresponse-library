
# Project Plan Template: Direct Connect Traffic Segmentation and Prioritization

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Direct Connect Traffic Segmentation and Prioritization" project aims to enhance network performance for critical workloads by implementing effective traffic segmentation and prioritization strategies for AWS Direct Connect. The primary objective is to assess the current network architecture and identify key workloads that require prioritized bandwidth. This project will deliver a comprehensive strategy that segments traffic based on workload importance, ensuring that mission-critical applications receive the necessary resources for optimal performance. Key outcomes include a detailed report outlining the implemented strategies, configuration settings, and recommendations for ongoing management. By optimizing network performance, businesses will be better equipped to support their most important workloads, ultimately leading to improved operational efficiency and reliability.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Network Architecture**: 
  - Review existing AWS Direct Connect configurations.
  - Identify critical workloads and their bandwidth requirements.
  
- **Traffic Segmentation Strategy Development**: 
  - Develop a comprehensive strategy for segmenting traffic based on workload importance.
  - Define criteria for prioritizing bandwidth allocation.

- **Configuration Guidance**: 
  - Provide detailed instructions on configuring AWS Direct Connect settings to enforce the developed strategies.
  - Recommend best practices for traffic flow management.

- **Monitoring and Adjustment Recommendations**: 
  - Outline methods for monitoring traffic flows.
  - Provide guidelines for adjusting configurations as necessary to maintain optimal performance.

- **Final Deliverable**: 
  - A detailed report that includes:
    - Implemented strategies.
    - Configuration settings.
    - Recommendations for ongoing management.
