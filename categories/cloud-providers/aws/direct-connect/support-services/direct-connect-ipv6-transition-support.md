
# Project Plan Template: Direct Connect IPv6 Transition Support

## 1. Executive Summary

The Direct Connect IPv6 Transition Support project aims to facilitate organizations in migrating their AWS Direct Connect connections to support IPv6, addressing the growing demand for this protocol. As businesses increasingly rely on cloud infrastructure, ensuring compatibility with IPv6 is crucial for future-proofing their operations. This project will provide a comprehensive assessment of the current Direct Connect setup, identifying potential challenges and implementing necessary changes for seamless connectivity. Key outcomes include a fully operational AWS Direct Connect configuration that supports IPv6, guidance on best practices for implementation, thorough testing to validate the new setup, and ongoing support to resolve any post-transition issues. By the conclusion of this project, clients will benefit from enhanced cloud service robustness and scalability, positioning them for success in an evolving digital landscape.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment Phase**: 
  - Conduct a thorough review of the existing AWS Direct Connect setup.
  - Identify potential challenges and risks associated with the transition to IPv6.
  - Document current configurations and dependencies.

- **Planning Phase**: 
  - Develop a detailed transition plan outlining the steps required for migration.
  - Define best practices for IPv6 implementation tailored to the client's environment.
  - Schedule necessary resources and establish a timeline for the transition.

- **Implementation Phase**: 
  - Execute the migration plan, making the necessary changes to support IPv6.
  - Configure AWS Direct Connect to ensure compatibility with IPv6.
  - Collaborate with client IT teams to ensure alignment and understanding of changes.

- **Testing Phase**: 
  - Conduct thorough testing of the new IPv6 configuration.
  - Validate connectivity and performance metrics to ensure optimal operation.
  - Document test results and address any identified issues.

- **Support Phase**: 
  - Provide ongoing support post-transition to address any arising issues.
  - Offer training sessions for client staff on managing the new IPv6 setup.
  - Establish a feedback loop for continuous improvement and support.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Assessment Phase
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing current setup and identified challenges.

- **Milestone 2**: Approval of Transition Plan
  - Deliverable: Detailed Transition Plan document including timelines and resource allocation.

- **Milestone 3**: Successful Implementation of IPv6 Configuration
  - Deliverable: Updated AWS Direct Connect configuration supporting IPv6.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Testing Phase
  - Deliverable: Testing Report with validation results and any necessary adjustments.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Support and Training Session
  - Deliverable: Training materials and session completion report.
