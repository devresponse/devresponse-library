
# Project Plan Template: AWS Athena Disaster Recovery Planning

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, ensuring data availability and business continuity is paramount for organizations utilizing AWS Athena. The primary objective of this project is to develop a comprehensive disaster recovery plan tailored specifically for AWS Athena. This plan will encompass a thorough risk assessment, identification of critical data and workloads, and the establishment of effective strategies for data backup and restoration. Key outcomes include the analysis of potential threats to data integrity and availability, the outlining of recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO), and the establishment of protocols for regular testing and updates of the disaster recovery plan. By the end of this project, clients will receive a detailed, actionable plan that not only safeguards their data but also enhances their operational resilience during unforeseen outages. This proactive approach aims to minimize downtime and ensure that organizations can swiftly recover from any disruptions, thereby maintaining trust and reliability with their stakeholders.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of this project includes the following key components:

- **Risk Assessment**: Conduct a comprehensive analysis of potential risks and threats to AWS Athena data and workloads.
- **Critical Data Identification**: Identify and categorize critical data and workloads that require prioritization in the disaster recovery plan.
- **Backup Strategies**: Develop strategies for regular data backups, including frequency, storage solutions, and methods of data retrieval.
- **Restoration Procedures**: Outline clear procedures for data restoration, ensuring minimal disruption to business operations.
- **RTO and RPO Definition**: Establish specific recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) tailored to the client's operational needs.
- **Testing Protocols**: Create protocols for regular testing of the disaster recovery plan to ensure its effectiveness and relevance.
- **Plan Updates**: Develop a schedule for periodic reviews and updates of the disaster recovery plan to adapt to changing business needs and technological advancements.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Risk Assessment Report
  - Deliverable: Detailed report outlining identified risks and threats.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Identification of Critical Data and Workloads
  - Deliverable: List of critical data and workloads with categorization.

- **Milestone 3**: Development of Backup and Restoration Strategies
  - Deliverable: Documented strategies for data backup and restoration.

- **Milestone 4**: Definition of RTO and RPO
  - Deliverable: Document specifying RTO and RPO for critical workloads.

- **Milestone 5**: Creation of Testing and Update Protocols
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive testing and update protocols for the disaster recovery plan.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Disaster Recovery Plan Delivery
  - Deliverable: Complete disaster recovery plan document, including all components developed throughout the project.
