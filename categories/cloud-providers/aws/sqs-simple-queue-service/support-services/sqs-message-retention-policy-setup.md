
# Project Plan Template: SQS Message Retention Policy Setup

## 1. Executive Summary

The SQS Message Retention Policy Setup project aims to provide expert assistance in establishing and configuring message retention policies for AWS SQS (Simple Queue Service). The primary objective of this project is to ensure that your message retention settings are aligned with your business requirements while adhering to relevant data retention standards. This will involve a comprehensive assessment of your current SQS setup, followed by a detailed analysis of your specific retention needs. Our team of experts will guide you through the configuration process, ensuring that all settings are optimized for both performance and compliance. Key outcomes of this project include enhanced operational efficiency, improved data governance, and a clear documentation of the configuration steps taken along with best practices for ongoing management. By the end of this project, you will have a robust message retention policy that meets your organizational needs and regulatory obligations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment Phase**: 
  - Conduct a comprehensive review of the current SQS setup.
  - Identify existing message retention settings and their alignment with business requirements.
  
- **Analysis Phase**: 
  - Gather specific retention needs from stakeholders.
  - Analyze compliance requirements related to data retention standards.

- **Configuration Phase**: 
  - Configure message retention policies in AWS SQS based on the analysis.
  - Optimize settings for performance and compliance.

- **Documentation Phase**: 
  - Create detailed documentation outlining the configuration steps taken.
  - Provide best practices for ongoing management and monitoring of message retention policies.

- **Training Phase**: 
  - Offer training sessions for relevant staff on managing and maintaining the new retention policies.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Assessment Phase
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing current SQS setup and findings.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Analysis Phase
  - Deliverable: Analysis report outlining specific retention needs and compliance requirements.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Configuration Phase
  - Deliverable: Configured SQS message retention policies and settings.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Documentation Phase
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive documentation of configuration steps and best practices.

- **Milestone 5**: Completion of Training Phase
  - Deliverable: Training materials and session completion report.
