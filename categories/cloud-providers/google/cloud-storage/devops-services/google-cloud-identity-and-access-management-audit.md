
# Project Plan: Google Cloud Identity and Access Management Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

The Google Cloud Identity and Access Management (IAM) Audit project aims to conduct a thorough examination of your organization's IAM policies to ensure they align with industry best practices and comply with relevant security standards. This project will provide a comprehensive assessment of your current access controls, identifying potential vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, and over-permissioned roles that could expose critical resources to unauthorized access. The key objectives include reviewing IAM roles, policies, and permissions, analyzing compliance with industry standards, and delivering actionable recommendations to enhance your security posture. By the end of this audit, your organization will have a clearer understanding of its IAM landscape, enabling you to strengthen cloud security, maintain regulatory compliance, and effectively leverage Google Cloud Storage. The expected outcomes include a detailed audit report, a prioritized list of remediation actions, and a roadmap for implementing best practices in IAM management.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Google Cloud IAM Audit project includes the following key activities:

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct an initial review of existing IAM policies and configurations.
- **Role and Policy Review**: Analyze all IAM roles and policies to identify over-permissioned roles and misconfigurations.
- **Compliance Analysis**: Evaluate IAM settings against industry standards such as NIST, ISO 27001, and GDPR.
- **Vulnerability Identification**: Identify potential vulnerabilities within the IAM setup that could lead to unauthorized access.
- **Recommendations**: Provide actionable recommendations for remediation, including best practices for role management and policy configurations.
- **Final Report**: Deliver a comprehensive audit report summarizing findings, recommendations, and a roadmap for implementation.
