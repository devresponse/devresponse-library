
# Project Plan Template: Kubernetes Chaos Engineering on GKE

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Kubernetes Chaos Engineering on GKE" project aims to implement chaos engineering practices within Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) to rigorously test and enhance the resilience and fault tolerance of applications and infrastructure. The primary objective is to simulate various failure scenarios that could potentially disrupt services, thereby identifying weaknesses within the system. By employing tools such as Chaos Monkey or Gremlin, we will conduct chaos experiments that will reveal vulnerabilities and inform strategies to mitigate risks. This project will also integrate chaos engineering practices into the existing CI/CD pipeline, ensuring that resilience testing becomes a continuous process. Key outcomes include a robust documentation of processes and results, as well as empowering your team with the knowledge and tools necessary to proactively address system vulnerabilities. Ultimately, this initiative will ensure that applications can withstand unexpected disruptions while maintaining high availability and performance, thereby enhancing overall operational excellence.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**: 
  - Kick-off meeting with stakeholders to define project goals and expectations.
  - Establish communication channels and project governance.

- **Assessment and Planning**:
  - Review existing architecture and identify critical components for chaos engineering.
  - Define chaos engineering hypotheses and scenarios to be tested.

- **Tool Selection and Setup**:
  - Evaluate and select appropriate chaos engineering tools (e.g., Chaos Monkey, Gremlin).
  - Set up the selected tools within the GKE environment.

- **Chaos Experiment Design**:
  - Design chaos experiments based on identified scenarios.
  - Develop scripts and configurations for executing chaos tests.

- **Integration with CI/CD Pipeline**:
  - Integrate chaos engineering practices into the CI/CD pipeline.
  - Ensure automated chaos tests are triggered during deployment processes.

- **Execution of Chaos Experiments**:
  - Conduct chaos experiments as per the defined schedule.
  - Monitor system behavior and collect data during experiments.

- **Analysis and Reporting**:
  - Analyze the results of chaos experiments to identify weaknesses.
  - Document findings and recommendations for system improvements.

- **Knowledge Transfer and Training**:
  - Conduct training sessions for the team on chaos engineering principles and practices.
  - Provide comprehensive documentation on processes, tools, and outcomes.

- **Project Closure**:
  - Final review meeting with stakeholders to present findings and recommendations.
  - Obtain feedback and finalize project documentation.
