
# Project Plan Template: GKE and AKS Training and Knowledge Transfer

## 1. Executive Summary

The GKE and AKS Training and Knowledge Transfer project aims to equip internal teams with the essential skills required to manage and optimize Kubernetes clusters on Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) and Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS). The primary objective of this project is to deliver a comprehensive training program that combines theoretical knowledge with practical, hands-on workshops. Participants will explore critical topics such as cluster architecture, deployment strategies, scaling applications, and performance monitoring. By engaging in real-world scenarios, team members will gain the confidence and expertise needed to navigate the complexities of cloud-native technologies. Key outcomes include enhanced team capabilities, improved operational efficiency, and the creation of detailed documentation that serves as a long-term resource. This initiative is designed to foster innovation within organizations by empowering teams to leverage the full potential of Kubernetes environments.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the GKE and AKS Training and Knowledge Transfer project includes the following components:

- **Training Program Development**: Design and develop a structured training curriculum that covers essential Kubernetes concepts and practices.
- **Hands-On Workshops**: Conduct a series of interactive workshops focusing on:
  - Cluster architecture and setup
  - Deployment strategies for applications
  - Scaling applications effectively
  - Monitoring and performance optimization
- **Real-World Simulations**: Create practical exercises that simulate real-world scenarios to enhance learning and application of skills.
- **Documentation**: Provide comprehensive training materials and documentation that participants can reference post-training.
- **Feedback and Assessment**: Implement mechanisms for participant feedback and assessment to ensure the training meets its objectives and to identify areas for improvement.
