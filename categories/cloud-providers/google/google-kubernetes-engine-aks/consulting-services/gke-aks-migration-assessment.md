
# Project Plan Template: GKE and AKS Migration Assessment

## 1. Executive Summary

The GKE and AKS Migration Assessment project aims to provide businesses with a comprehensive evaluation of their existing workloads and the potential advantages of transitioning to cloud-native solutions. The primary objective is to conduct an in-depth workload analysis to identify applications and services that are optimal for migration to either Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) or Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS). Our team of experts will meticulously assess associated risks, including potential downtime, data integrity issues, and compatibility challenges, ensuring that clients are fully informed of the implications of migration. The key outcome of this project will be a detailed migration roadmap that outlines the necessary steps for a successful transition. This roadmap will include timelines, cost estimates, and resource requirements, empowering clients to make informed decisions about their cloud strategy. By the end of this assessment, businesses will have a clear understanding of the migration process, enabling them to leverage the benefits of cloud-native solutions effectively.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Workload Analysis**: Conduct a thorough evaluation of current applications and services to determine their suitability for migration to GKE or AKS.
- **Risk Assessment**: Identify and analyze potential risks associated with migration, including downtime, data integrity, and compatibility challenges.
- **Migration Roadmap Development**: Create a comprehensive roadmap that outlines the steps necessary for migration, including:
  - Recommended cloud-native solutions (GKE or AKS)
  - Detailed timelines for each phase of the migration
  - Cost estimates for the migration process
  - Resource requirements, including personnel and technology
- **Stakeholder Engagement**: Collaborate with key stakeholders to gather insights and ensure alignment with business objectives.
- **Final Report**: Deliver a detailed report summarizing findings, recommendations, and the migration roadmap.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Workload Analysis
  - Deliverable: Workload Analysis Report
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Risk Assessment
  - Deliverable: Risk Assessment Document
- **Milestone 3**: Development of Migration Roadmap
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Migration Roadmap
- **Milestone 4**: Final Presentation to Stakeholders
  - Deliverable: Presentation Slides and Final Report
