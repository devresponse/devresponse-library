
# Project Plan Template: Persistent Disk Compliance and Governance Review

## 1. Executive Summary

The Persistent Disk Compliance and Governance Review project aims to conduct a thorough evaluation of your Google Persistent Disk usage to ensure compliance with industry regulations and organizational governance policies. The primary objective is to assess current data storage practices, identify potential compliance risks, and provide actionable recommendations to address any identified gaps. This project will encompass a detailed review of data security measures, access controls, and retention policies to ensure they align with regulatory standards. Key outcomes include a comprehensive compliance report that outlines findings, risks, and recommendations, as well as expert guidance for maintaining ongoing adherence to relevant policies. By the conclusion of this project, stakeholders will have peace of mind knowing that their cloud storage solutions are compliant and secure, thereby mitigating risks associated with non-compliance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Practices**: Review existing Google Persistent Disk usage, including data storage practices and configurations.
- **Compliance Risk Identification**: Identify potential risks related to compliance with industry regulations and organizational policies.
- **Data Security Evaluation**: Assess current data security measures, including encryption, access controls, and authentication processes.
- **Retention Policy Review**: Evaluate existing data retention policies to ensure they meet regulatory requirements.
- **Recommendations Development**: Provide actionable recommendations to address identified compliance gaps and enhance governance practices.
- **Compliance Report Preparation**: Compile findings and recommendations into a detailed compliance report for stakeholders.
- **Expert Guidance**: Offer ongoing support and guidance to ensure continued compliance post-review.
