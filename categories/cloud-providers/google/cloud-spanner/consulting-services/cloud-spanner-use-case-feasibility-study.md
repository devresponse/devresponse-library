## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Cloud Spanner Use Case Feasibility Study includes the following key activities:

- **Initial Consultation**: Engage with the client to understand their current database setup, business requirements, and specific use cases.
- **Data Collection**: Gather relevant data on performance metrics, scalability needs, and cost considerations.
- **Feasibility Analysis**: Conduct a detailed analysis of Google Cloud Spanner, focusing on its capabilities in relation to the client's requirements.
- **Comparison with Alternatives**: Evaluate alternative database solutions to provide a comprehensive comparison.
- **Report Preparation**: Compile findings into a detailed report that includes insights, recommendations, and a clear rationale for the proposed solution.
- **Presentation of Findings**: Present the findings to the client, addressing any questions and providing further clarification as needed.
