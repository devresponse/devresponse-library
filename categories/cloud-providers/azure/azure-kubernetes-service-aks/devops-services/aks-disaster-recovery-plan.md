
# Project Plan: AKS Disaster Recovery Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's cloud-centric world, ensuring the resilience of applications is paramount. The AKS Disaster Recovery Plan project aims to design and implement a comprehensive disaster recovery strategy specifically tailored for Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS) clusters. The primary objective of this project is to safeguard applications against potential disruptions through robust backup and restore strategies. This project will involve a thorough assessment of the current AKS architecture, identifying critical components, and establishing automated backup processes. Key outcomes include the development of detailed documentation outlining recovery procedures, conducting rigorous testing to validate the effectiveness of the plan, and providing training sessions for your team to ensure seamless execution during an actual disaster scenario. By the end of this project, you will have a reliable disaster recovery framework that enhances operational continuity and minimizes downtime, ultimately ensuring that your business can respond effectively to any unforeseen events.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current AKS Architecture**: 
  - Review existing AKS configurations and deployments.
  - Identify critical components and dependencies within the architecture.

- **Backup Strategy Development**: 
  - Design automated backup processes for AKS clusters, including persistent volumes and configuration data.
  - Establish retention policies and backup schedules.

- **Disaster Recovery Documentation**: 
  - Create comprehensive documentation detailing recovery procedures.
  - Include step-by-step instructions for restoring services and data.

- **Testing and Validation**: 
  - Conduct disaster recovery drills to test the effectiveness of the backup and restore processes.
  - Validate the recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO).

- **Training Sessions**: 
  - Provide training for your team on the disaster recovery plan and procedures.
  - Ensure team members are familiar with the tools and processes involved in executing the plan.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of AKS Architecture Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing current architecture and identified critical components.

- **Milestone 2**: Development of Backup Strategy
  - Deliverable: Documented backup strategy including automated processes and retention policies.

- **Milestone 3**: Creation of Disaster Recovery Documentation
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive recovery procedures document.

- **Milestone 4**: Testing and Validation of Disaster Recovery Plan
  - Deliverable: Testing report with results and recommendations for improvements.

- **Milestone 5**: Completion of Training Sessions
  - Deliverable: Training materials and attendance records.
