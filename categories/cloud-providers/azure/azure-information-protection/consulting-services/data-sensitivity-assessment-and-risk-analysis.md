
# Project Plan Template: Data Sensitivity Assessment and Risk Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary

The Data Sensitivity Assessment and Risk Analysis project aims to provide a comprehensive evaluation of your organization's data classification processes and the sensitivity of your data. In today's digital landscape, understanding the sensitivity of your data is crucial for maintaining robust security measures. This project will focus on identifying sensitive data types, assessing their exposure to potential threats, and analyzing your current Azure Information Protection configurations. Our expert consultants will deliver a detailed report that outlines our findings and provides actionable recommendations for enhancing your data protection strategies. Key outcomes include improved data classification, identification of vulnerabilities, and a strategic roadmap for compliance with industry standards. Ultimately, our goal is to empower your organization with the insights needed to safeguard critical information and ensure robust security measures are in place.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Data Classification Processes**: Review and evaluate existing data classification methodologies within the organization.
- **Identification of Sensitive Data Types**: Conduct interviews and workshops to identify and categorize sensitive data types relevant to the organization.
- **Risk Analysis**: Assess potential threats and vulnerabilities associated with identified sensitive data.
- **Evaluation of Azure Information Protection Configurations**: Analyze current configurations and settings within Azure Information Protection to identify gaps and vulnerabilities.
- **Reporting**: Develop a comprehensive report detailing findings, including:
  - Summary of sensitive data identified
  - Risk assessment results
  - Recommendations for enhancing data protection strategies
- **Presentation of Findings**: Conduct a presentation session with stakeholders to discuss findings and recommendations.
