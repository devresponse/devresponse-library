
# Project Plan Template: Azure Information Protection Multi-Tenant Support

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Information Protection Multi-Tenant Support project aims to provide specialized assistance for organizations leveraging Azure Information Protection (AIP) in multi-tenant environments. The primary objective is to ensure that security policies are effectively configured and consistently applied across all tenants. This project will involve a comprehensive assessment of existing AIP configurations, identifying potential vulnerabilities, and implementing best practices for data protection. Key outcomes include enhanced security compliance, streamlined administration processes, and improved understanding of AIP functionalities among administrative staff through targeted training sessions. By engaging our expert team, organizations can focus on their core business operations while we manage the complexities associated with AIP, ultimately ensuring that sensitive data remains secure across all tenants.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current AIP Configurations**: Conduct a thorough review of existing Azure Information Protection settings across all tenants to identify gaps and vulnerabilities.
  
- **Implementation of Best Practices**: Develop and implement best practices for AIP configuration to enhance data protection and compliance with security standards.
  
- **Multi-Tenant Security Policy Application**: Ensure that security policies are uniformly applied across all tenants to maintain consistency and security integrity.
  
- **Training Sessions**: Organize and conduct training sessions for administrative staff to improve their understanding of AIP functionalities, management, and best practices.
  
- **Ongoing Support and Maintenance**: Provide ongoing support to address any issues that arise post-implementation and ensure continuous compliance with evolving security standards.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the initial assessment of AIP configurations.
  - **Deliverable**: Assessment report detailing current configurations and identified vulnerabilities.

- **Milestone 2**: Implementation of recommended best practices.
  - **Deliverable**: Documented best practices and updated AIP configurations across all tenants.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of training sessions for administrative staff.
  - **Deliverable**: Training materials and feedback report from participants.

- **Milestone 4**: Final review and ongoing support plan.
  - **Deliverable**: Final project report and a plan for ongoing support and maintenance.
