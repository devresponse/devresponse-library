
# Project Plan Template: Azure Site Recovery for Critical Application Protection

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Site Recovery for Critical Application Protection project aims to implement a robust disaster recovery solution that safeguards essential applications within your organization. The primary objective is to assess the current infrastructure and identify critical applications that require protection against potential disruptions. By leveraging Azure Site Recovery (ASR), we will develop a comprehensive disaster recovery plan tailored to your business needs, ensuring that critical applications can be swiftly restored in the event of an outage. Key outcomes of this project include the successful configuration of ASR, establishment of replication processes, and thorough testing to validate the recovery process. Additionally, we will provide training for your IT staff to effectively manage and maintain the ASR solution. Our ultimate goal is to minimize downtime, protect business operations, and enable your organization to fully leverage the capabilities of Azure's cloud infrastructure.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment Phase**: 
  - Conduct a thorough assessment of the current IT infrastructure.
  - Identify critical applications that require disaster recovery solutions.
  - Evaluate existing backup and recovery processes.

- **Planning Phase**: 
  - Develop a tailored disaster recovery plan based on the assessment findings.
  - Define recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) for each critical application.
  - Document the disaster recovery strategy and processes.

- **Implementation Phase**: 
  - Configure Azure Site Recovery for the identified critical applications.
  - Set up replication for the applications to ensure data integrity and availability.
  - Conduct thorough testing of the recovery process to validate the effectiveness of the ASR configuration.

- **Training Phase**: 
  - Provide comprehensive training sessions for IT staff on managing and maintaining the ASR solution.
  - Develop user manuals and documentation for ongoing reference.

- **Support Phase**: 
  - Offer post-implementation support for a defined period to address any issues that may arise.
  - Conduct a review meeting to assess the success of the implementation and gather feedback.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the Assessment Phase
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing critical applications and current infrastructure evaluation.

- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of the Disaster Recovery Plan
  - Deliverable: Documented disaster recovery plan including RTO and RPO definitions.

- **Milestone 3**: Successful Configuration of Azure Site Recovery
  - Deliverable: Configured ASR environment ready for replication.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Testing Phase
  - Deliverable: Testing report validating the recovery process.

- **Milestone 5**: Training Completion
  - Deliverable: Training materials and session completion certificates for IT staff.

- **Milestone 6**: Post-Implementation Review
  - Deliverable: Review meeting notes and feedback report.
