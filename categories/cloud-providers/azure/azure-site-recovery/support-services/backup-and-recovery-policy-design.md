
# Project Plan Template: Backup and Recovery Policy Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Backup and Recovery Policy Design project aims to develop a comprehensive set of policies tailored specifically for Azure Site Recovery solutions. The primary objective is to ensure that {{Organization Name}} can effectively safeguard its data and maintain business continuity in the event of a disaster or system failure. This project will involve a thorough assessment of the current infrastructure to identify critical data assets, establishing Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO), and creating guidelines for regular backups, data retention, and testing recovery procedures. Key outcomes will include a robust framework that enhances the Azure Site Recovery implementation, ensuring systems resilience and data security. By the end of this project, {{Organization Name}} will have a practical and effective backup and recovery policy that not only meets compliance requirements but also aligns with the organization's operational needs and risk management strategies.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Infrastructure**: Conduct a detailed analysis of the existing IT infrastructure to identify critical data assets and dependencies.
  
- **Define RTO and RPO**: Collaborate with stakeholders to establish Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO) tailored to business needs.

- **Policy Development**: Create comprehensive backup and recovery policies that include:
  - Guidelines for regular backups
  - Data retention policies
  - Testing procedures for recovery

- **Implementation Plan**: Develop a step-by-step implementation plan for the policies, including roles and responsibilities.

- **Training and Documentation**: Provide training sessions for relevant staff and create documentation to support the policies.

- **Review and Feedback**: Facilitate a review process with stakeholders to gather feedback and make necessary adjustments.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current Infrastructure Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report

- **Milestone 2**: Establishment of RTO and RPO
  - Deliverable: RTO and RPO Documentation

- **Milestone 3**: Draft of Backup and Recovery Policies
  - Deliverable: Policy Draft

- **Milestone 4**: Implementation Plan Development
  - Deliverable: Implementation Plan Document

- **Milestone 5**: Training Sessions Conducted
  - Deliverable: Training Materials and Attendance Records

- **Milestone 6**: Final Review and Approval of Policies
  - Deliverable: Final Policy Document
