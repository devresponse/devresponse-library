
# Project Plan Template: Cross-Platform Recovery Orchestration

## 1. Executive Summary

The Cross-Platform Recovery Orchestration project aims to design a robust system that orchestrates recovery processes across various platforms, utilizing the capabilities of Azure Site Recovery. The primary objective is to create a seamless integration that ensures business continuity and minimizes downtime during recovery operations. This project will facilitate automated failover and failback processes, allowing organizations to maintain operational efficiency regardless of the platform in use. Key outcomes include the development of a centralized dashboard for monitoring recovery status, customizable recovery plans tailored to specific business needs, and comprehensive documentation to guide users through the orchestration process. By implementing this solution, businesses can significantly enhance their disaster recovery strategies, ensuring that critical applications and data remain accessible even in the face of unforeseen disruptions. The successful execution of this project will empower organizations to respond swiftly to incidents, thereby safeguarding their operational integrity and enhancing overall resilience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Cross-Platform Recovery Orchestration project includes the following key components:

- **System Design and Architecture**: Develop a comprehensive architecture that supports recovery orchestration across multiple platforms.
- **Integration with Azure Site Recovery**: Leverage Azure Site Recovery capabilities to facilitate automated failover and failback processes.
- **Centralized Dashboard Development**: Create a user-friendly dashboard that provides real-time monitoring of recovery status and system health.
- **Customizable Recovery Plans**: Design and implement customizable recovery plans that can be tailored to meet specific business requirements.
- **Documentation and Training**: Provide thorough documentation and training materials to guide users through the orchestration process and ensure effective utilization of the system.
- **Testing and Validation**: Conduct rigorous testing to validate the functionality and reliability of the recovery orchestration system.
- **Deployment and Support**: Oversee the deployment of the system and provide ongoing support to ensure smooth operation.
