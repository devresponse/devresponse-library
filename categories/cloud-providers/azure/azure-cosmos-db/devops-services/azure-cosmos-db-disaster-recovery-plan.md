
# Azure Cosmos DB Disaster Recovery Plan

## Executive Summary
In today's fast-paced digital landscape, ensuring business continuity is paramount, especially when it comes to managing critical databases like Azure Cosmos DB. This project aims to develop a comprehensive disaster recovery plan tailored specifically for Azure Cosmos DB, addressing potential outages and ensuring minimal disruption to operations. The primary objective is to safeguard data integrity and availability through meticulous risk assessments and robust recovery strategies. Key outcomes will include detailed procedures for data backup and restoration, clearly defined roles and responsibilities, and established communication protocols. Additionally, the plan will incorporate testing procedures to validate the effectiveness of the recovery strategies. By implementing this disaster recovery plan, businesses can enhance their resilience against unforeseen disruptions, maintain operational integrity, and ensure a swift recovery in the event of a disaster.

## Detailed Scope of Work
1. **Risk Assessment**
   - Identify potential risks and vulnerabilities associated with Azure Cosmos DB.
   - Evaluate the impact of these risks on business operations.

2. **Recovery Strategies**
   - Develop strategies for data backup, restoration, and failover processes.
   - Define recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO).

3. **Procedures for Data Backup and Restoration**
   - Document step-by-step procedures for backing up data in Azure Cosmos DB.
   - Outline restoration processes to ensure quick recovery.

4. **Roles and Responsibilities**
   - Define the roles of team members involved in disaster recovery.
   - Assign responsibilities for executing the disaster recovery plan.

5. **Communication Protocols**
   - Establish communication channels for notifying stakeholders during a disaster.
   - Create templates for incident reporting and updates.

6. **Testing Procedures**
   - Develop a schedule for regular testing of the disaster recovery plan.
   - Document the testing process and criteria for success.
