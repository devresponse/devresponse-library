
# Project Plan Template: Container Image Lifecycle Management Consulting

## 1. Executive Summary

The Container Image Lifecycle Management Consulting project aims to empower organizations with the knowledge and tools necessary to effectively manage their container images within Azure Container Registry. In today's cloud-centric environment, the efficient management of container images is paramount for operational efficiency and security. This project will provide a comprehensive analysis of current container image practices, followed by tailored recommendations for versioning strategies that ensure consistency and reliability. Key outcomes will include the development of robust retention policies that align with business needs, optimizing storage costs while maintaining compliance. Additionally, we will implement effective cleanup strategies to remove outdated or unused images, enhancing both performance and security. By the conclusion of this consulting engagement, your organization will possess a clear roadmap for managing container images efficiently, ensuring that your cloud infrastructure remains robust and agile.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Current State Assessment**: Conduct a thorough analysis of existing container image practices within the organization.
- **Versioning Strategy Development**: Provide tailored recommendations for versioning strategies that ensure consistency and reliability in container image management.
- **Retention Policy Creation**: Develop comprehensive retention policies that align with business needs, focusing on optimizing storage costs and maintaining compliance.
- **Cleanup Strategy Implementation**: Design and implement effective strategies for the cleanup of outdated or unused container images.
- **Roadmap Development**: Create a detailed roadmap for ongoing management of container images, including best practices and guidelines for future operations.
- **Training and Knowledge Transfer**: Provide training sessions for relevant staff to ensure understanding and adherence to new practices and policies.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current State Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing findings and recommendations.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Development of Versioning Strategy
  - Deliverable: Document outlining the proposed versioning strategy and its implementation plan.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Creation of Retention Policies
  - Deliverable: Retention Policy Document that includes guidelines and compliance measures.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Implementation of Cleanup Strategies
  - Deliverable: Cleanup Strategy Report with actionable steps and timelines.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Roadmap Presentation
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Roadmap Document and presentation to stakeholders.
