
# Project Plan Template: IoT Data Retention and Archiving Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary

The IoT Data Retention and Archiving Strategy project aims to develop a comprehensive framework for managing IoT data within the Azure environment. As businesses increasingly rely on IoT solutions, effective data management becomes paramount to ensure compliance with regulations such as GDPR and CCPA. This project will focus on assessing current data usage patterns and identifying retention requirements to optimize storage costs through efficient data lifecycle management. Key outcomes will include a detailed strategy document that outlines best practices for data retention, implementation guidelines for archiving solutions, and recommendations for ongoing monitoring and adjustments to the strategy. By the end of this project, stakeholders will have a robust plan that not only meets regulatory requirements but also enhances data accessibility for future analysis and reporting.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Data Usage Patterns**: Analyze existing IoT data flows and storage practices to understand how data is currently being utilized.
- **Identification of Retention Requirements**: Collaborate with stakeholders to determine the necessary retention periods for various types of IoT data, considering regulatory compliance and business needs.
- **Development of Data Lifecycle Management Strategies**: Propose strategies for data lifecycle management that optimize storage costs while ensuring compliance and accessibility.
- **Archiving Solutions Proposal**: Identify and recommend archiving solutions that align with the retention requirements and data accessibility needs.
- **Documentation**: Create a comprehensive strategy document that includes all findings, recommendations, and guidelines for implementation.
- **Implementation Guidelines**: Develop step-by-step guidelines for executing the proposed data retention and archiving strategy.
- **Monitoring and Adjustment Recommendations**: Provide recommendations for ongoing monitoring of the strategy's effectiveness and adjustments based on evolving business needs.
