
# Project Plan Template: Key Vault Disaster Recovery Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, ensuring the availability and security of sensitive data is paramount. The Key Vault Disaster Recovery Plan project aims to design and implement a comprehensive disaster recovery strategy specifically for Azure Key Vault. The primary objective is to maintain business continuity during unexpected outages, ensuring that critical secrets, keys, and certificates remain accessible and secure. This project will involve a thorough assessment of the current infrastructure, identification of potential risks, and the development of a robust recovery strategy that includes backup solutions, failover procedures, and regular testing protocols. By leveraging Azure's capabilities, we will ensure that your organization can swiftly recover from disruptions, minimizing downtime and safeguarding your operations. The key outcomes of this project will include a detailed disaster recovery plan, complete documentation, and guidelines for implementation tailored to your specific business needs, ultimately enhancing your organization's resilience against unforeseen incidents.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Infrastructure**: 
  - Evaluate existing Azure Key Vault configurations and integrations.
  - Identify critical assets and dependencies related to Key Vault.

- **Risk Identification**: 
  - Conduct a risk analysis to identify potential threats and vulnerabilities.
  - Prioritize risks based on impact and likelihood.

- **Disaster Recovery Strategy Development**: 
  - Design a comprehensive disaster recovery strategy that includes:
    - Backup solutions for secrets, keys, and certificates.
    - Failover procedures to ensure minimal disruption.
    - Regular testing protocols to validate the recovery plan.

- **Documentation**: 
  - Create detailed documentation outlining the disaster recovery plan.
  - Develop guidelines for implementation and maintenance of the plan.

- **Training and Support**: 
  - Provide training sessions for relevant staff on the disaster recovery procedures.
  - Offer ongoing support during the implementation phase.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current Infrastructure Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing findings and recommendations.

- **Milestone 2**: Risk Analysis Report
  - Deliverable: Document outlining identified risks and prioritization.

- **Milestone 3**: Disaster Recovery Strategy Document
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive disaster recovery strategy including backup and failover procedures.

- **Milestone 4**: Documentation and Guidelines
  - Deliverable: Final documentation of the disaster recovery plan and implementation guidelines.

- **Milestone 5**: Training Completion
  - Deliverable: Training session completion report and feedback.
