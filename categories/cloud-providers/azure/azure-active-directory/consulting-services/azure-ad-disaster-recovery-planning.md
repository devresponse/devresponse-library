
# Project Plan Template: Azure AD Disaster Recovery Planning

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, ensuring the security and availability of your Azure Active Directory (AD) is paramount for maintaining business operations. The objective of this project is to develop a comprehensive disaster recovery plan tailored specifically for your Azure AD environment. This project will provide a detailed overview of strategies for data backup, failover procedures, and recovery processes to minimize downtime during outages or security incidents. Key outcomes will include a thorough assessment of your current Azure AD setup, identification of potential risks, and the creation of a detailed roadmap for recovery. Furthermore, the project will encompass failover testing to validate the effectiveness of the plan, along with comprehensive documentation to guide your team through the recovery process. By the end of this project, you will have a robust disaster recovery strategy that ensures business continuity and protects your critical identity management systems.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of this project includes the following key activities:

- **Assessment of Current Azure AD Setup**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the existing Azure AD configuration, including user roles, permissions, and security settings.
- **Risk Identification**: Identify potential risks and vulnerabilities that could impact the availability and security of the Azure AD environment.
- **Disaster Recovery Strategy Development**: Create a tailored disaster recovery strategy that includes:
  - Data backup procedures
  - Failover procedures
  - Recovery processes
- **Failover Testing**: Execute failover testing to validate the effectiveness of the disaster recovery plan and ensure that all procedures function as intended.
- **Documentation**: Develop comprehensive documentation that outlines the disaster recovery plan, including step-by-step recovery procedures, roles and responsibilities, and contact information for key personnel.
- **Training and Support**: Provide training sessions for your team to ensure they are familiar with the disaster recovery plan and can execute it effectively in the event of an incident.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current Azure AD Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing the current setup and identified risks.

- **Milestone 2**: Development of Disaster Recovery Strategy
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive disaster recovery plan document.

- **Milestone 3**: Execution of Failover Testing
  - Deliverable: Failover testing report with results and recommendations.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Documentation and Training
  - Deliverable: Complete documentation package and training materials.
