
# Project Plan Template: Azure AD Tenant Health Assessment

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure AD Tenant Health Assessment project aims to provide a comprehensive evaluation of your Azure Active Directory (AD) environment. Our primary objective is to identify any misconfigurations or vulnerabilities within your tenant that could potentially expose your organization to security risks. Through a detailed analysis of your Azure AD configuration, security settings, and operational practices, our expert consultants will assess your current setup against industry best practices and standards. The key outcomes of this project include a thorough report that highlights our findings, along with specific, actionable recommendations tailored to enhance your Azure AD security posture and operational efficiency. By undertaking this assessment, organizations can ensure that their Azure AD is configured correctly and securely, ultimately supporting their broader business objectives and safeguarding sensitive information.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a kickoff meeting to understand the client's specific needs and objectives for the assessment.
- **Data Collection**: Gather relevant data regarding the current Azure AD configuration, including user accounts, roles, permissions, and security settings.
- **Configuration Review**: Analyze the collected data to identify misconfigurations, vulnerabilities, and areas that do not align with best practices.
- **Security Assessment**: Evaluate the security settings and policies in place, including multi-factor authentication, conditional access policies, and identity protection measures.
- **Operational Practices Review**: Assess operational practices related to user management, access controls, and incident response procedures.
- **Reporting**: Compile findings into a detailed report that includes:
  - Summary of the assessment
  - Identified vulnerabilities and misconfigurations
  - Recommendations for improvement
  - Best practices for ongoing management and monitoring
- **Presentation of Findings**: Conduct a presentation session with stakeholders to discuss the report and recommendations.
