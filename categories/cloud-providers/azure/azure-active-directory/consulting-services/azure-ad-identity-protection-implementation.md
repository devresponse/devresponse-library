
# Project Plan Template: Azure AD Identity Protection Implementation

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure AD Identity Protection Implementation project aims to significantly enhance your organization's security posture by deploying the advanced identity protection features of Azure Active Directory (Azure AD). The primary objective of this project is to conduct a comprehensive assessment of your current identity management setup, followed by the configuration of Azure AD Identity Protection to effectively monitor and mitigate identity-based risks. Key outcomes of this project include the establishment of tailored risk policies that align with your organization's specific needs, the implementation of real-time alerts for suspicious activities, and the provision of training and documentation to empower your team. By the conclusion of this project, your organization will possess a robust identity protection framework that not only reduces the risk of unauthorized access but also enhances overall security. This proactive approach will ensure that your organization is well-equipped to respond to identity-related threats, fostering a secure environment for your operations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment Phase**: 
  - Conduct a thorough evaluation of the existing identity management setup.
  - Identify vulnerabilities and areas for improvement in the current system.

- **Configuration Phase**: 
  - Configure Azure AD Identity Protection features, including risk policies, user risk levels, and sign-in risk levels.
  - Set up alerts for real-time notifications of suspicious activities.

- **Policy Development**: 
  - Collaborate with your team to establish risk policies tailored to your organization's specific requirements.
  - Document the policies and procedures for ongoing management.

- **Training and Documentation**: 
  - Provide comprehensive training sessions for your team on managing and responding to identity risks.
  - Develop user-friendly documentation that outlines procedures and best practices.

- **Implementation Support**: 
  - Offer ongoing support during the implementation phase to ensure a smooth transition.
  - Conduct a post-implementation review to assess the effectiveness of the identity protection framework.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Assessment Phase
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing current identity management setup and identified vulnerabilities.

- **Milestone 2**: Configuration of Azure AD Identity Protection
  - Deliverable: Configured Azure AD Identity Protection with tailored risk policies and alerts.

- **Milestone 3**: Training Completion
  - Deliverable: Training materials and completion certificates for team members.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Documentation
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive documentation outlining policies, procedures, and best practices.

- **Milestone 5**: Post-Implementation Review
  - Deliverable: Review Report assessing the effectiveness of the implemented identity protection framework.
