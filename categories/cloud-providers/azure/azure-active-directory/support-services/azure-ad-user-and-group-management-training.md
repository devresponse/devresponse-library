
# Project Plan Template: Azure AD User and Group Management Training

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure AD User and Group Management Training project aims to empower IT administrators with the essential skills required to effectively manage users, groups, and roles within Azure Active Directory (Azure AD). This comprehensive training program will provide participants with a thorough understanding of Azure AD's user and group management features, emphasizing best practices for delegation, permissions, and access control. Through a combination of theoretical knowledge and hands-on exercises, participants will engage in real-world scenarios that will enable them to apply their learning in practical settings. Key outcomes of this project include the ability to create and manage user accounts, assign appropriate roles, and implement robust security measures to safeguard sensitive information. By the conclusion of the training, organizations will enhance their Azure AD management capabilities, leading to improved security and productivity across their IT operations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Azure AD User and Group Management Training project includes the following key components:

- **Training Content Development**: Create comprehensive training materials covering Azure AD user and group management features, best practices, and security measures.
- **Training Delivery**: Conduct interactive training sessions, including lectures, demonstrations, and hands-on exercises.
- **Participant Engagement**: Facilitate group discussions and Q&A sessions to encourage participant interaction and knowledge sharing.
- **Assessment and Feedback**: Implement assessments to evaluate participant understanding and gather feedback for continuous improvement.
- **Post-Training Support**: Provide resources and support for participants after the training to reinforce learning and address any ongoing questions.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Training Content Development
  - Deliverable: Finalized training materials and resources.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Conducting the Training Sessions
  - Deliverable: Successful delivery of training sessions with participant engagement metrics.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Assessment and Feedback Collection
  - Deliverable: Compiled assessment results and participant feedback report.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Post-Training Support Initiation
  - Deliverable: Distribution of post-training resources and support contact information.
