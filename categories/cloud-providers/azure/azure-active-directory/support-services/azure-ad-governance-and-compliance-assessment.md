
# Project Plan Template: Azure AD Governance and Compliance Assessment

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure AD Governance and Compliance Assessment project aims to deliver a thorough evaluation of the Azure Active Directory (AD) environment within your organization. The primary objective is to identify any existing gaps in governance and compliance with applicable regulatory standards and internal policies. This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of your current Azure AD setup, focusing on critical areas such as user access controls, role assignments, and security configurations. Our team of seasoned experts will meticulously assess these components to ensure that your Azure AD environment adheres to best practices for security and efficiency. The key outcomes of this assessment will include a detailed report that outlines our findings and provides actionable recommendations to enhance your compliance posture. By implementing our proposed solutions, your organization will not only meet regulatory requirements but also strengthen its overall identity management strategy, thereby mitigating potential risks associated with non-compliance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Azure AD Environment**  
  - Review existing user access controls and permissions.
  - Analyze role assignments and their alignment with organizational policies.
  - Evaluate security configurations and compliance with regulatory standards.

- **Gap Analysis**  
  - Identify discrepancies between current practices and best practices.
  - Highlight areas of non-compliance with relevant regulations.

- **Recommendations**  
  - Provide actionable insights to address identified gaps.
  - Suggest improvements for user access management and security configurations.

- **Reporting**  
  - Compile findings into a comprehensive report.
  - Present the report to stakeholders, including key recommendations and next steps.
