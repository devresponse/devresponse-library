
# Project Plan Template: Azure AD Licensing and Cost Optimization

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure AD Licensing and Cost Optimization project aims to conduct a thorough analysis of your organization's Azure Active Directory (AD) licensing requirements. The primary objective is to evaluate the current licensing structure, usage patterns, and compliance needs to identify potential areas for cost savings. Our expert team will deliver a comprehensive report detailing existing licenses, assessing their effectiveness, and recommending the most suitable licensing options tailored to your business needs. Key outcomes of this project include maximizing the value of your Azure AD investment, minimizing unnecessary expenses, and ensuring compliance with industry standards. By the end of this project, your organization will have a clear understanding of its licensing landscape, enabling informed decision-making and efficient cloud operations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Current Licensing Assessment**: Review and document the existing Azure AD licensing structure.
- **Usage Pattern Analysis**: Analyze usage patterns to identify underutilized licenses and potential areas for optimization.
- **Compliance Evaluation**: Assess compliance needs based on industry standards and organizational requirements.
- **Cost Savings Identification**: Identify opportunities for cost savings through license consolidation or reallocation.
- **Recommendations Report**: Provide a detailed report with findings, recommendations for optimal licensing options, and a roadmap for implementation.
- **Stakeholder Review**: Conduct a review session with key stakeholders to discuss findings and recommendations.
- **Implementation Support**: Offer guidance and support during the implementation of recommended licensing changes.
