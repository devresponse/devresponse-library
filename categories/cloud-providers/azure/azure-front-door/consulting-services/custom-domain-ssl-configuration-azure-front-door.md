
# Project Plan Template: Custom Domain and SSL Configuration for Azure Front Door

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Custom Domain and SSL Configuration for Azure Front Door project is to enhance the security and branding of your applications through the comprehensive setup and configuration of custom domains and SSL certificates. This project will provide a tailored solution that aligns with your specific business requirements, ensuring seamless integration with Azure Front Door. Our expert consultants will guide you through the entire process, from obtaining SSL certificates to configuring them for optimal performance and reliability. Key outcomes of this project include improved application security, enhanced brand visibility, and adherence to industry compliance standards. By entrusting us with these technical details, you can focus on your core business operations while we ensure that your applications are secure and efficiently managed.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a detailed assessment of your business requirements and existing infrastructure.
- **Custom Domain Setup**: Assist in the selection and configuration of custom domains for Azure Front Door.
- **SSL Certificate Acquisition**: Guide you through the process of obtaining SSL certificates from a trusted Certificate Authority (CA).
- **SSL Configuration**: Configure SSL certificates within Azure Front Door to ensure secure connections.
- **Best Practices Guidance**: Provide recommendations for managing domains and SSL certificates effectively.
- **Testing and Validation**: Perform thorough testing to ensure that custom domains and SSL configurations are functioning correctly.
- **Documentation**: Deliver comprehensive documentation outlining the configuration process, best practices, and troubleshooting tips.
- **Post-Implementation Support**: Offer ongoing support for a specified period to address any issues or questions that may arise.
