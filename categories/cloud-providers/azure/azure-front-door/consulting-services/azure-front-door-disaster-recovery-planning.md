
# Project Plan Template: Azure Front Door Disaster Recovery Planning

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Front Door Disaster Recovery Planning project aims to develop a robust disaster recovery strategy tailored to the unique needs of {{Client Name}}. In today's digital landscape, ensuring business continuity is paramount, particularly during unexpected outages or failures. This project will provide a comprehensive assessment of the current infrastructure, identifying potential risks and establishing recovery objectives that align with business priorities. By leveraging Azure Front Door's advanced features, including global load balancing, SSL offloading, and application acceleration, we will design a resilient architecture that minimizes downtime and maximizes performance. Key outcomes of this project will include a detailed disaster recovery plan document, step-by-step activation procedures, testing protocols, and ongoing maintenance recommendations. Ultimately, this initiative will empower {{Client Name}} to enhance cloud resilience and ensure seamless operations in the face of adversity, safeguarding critical business functions and maintaining customer trust.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Infrastructure**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the existing IT infrastructure, including applications, databases, and network configurations.
  
- **Risk Identification**: Identify potential risks and vulnerabilities that could impact business operations during a disaster scenario.
  
- **Recovery Objectives**: Establish Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO) tailored to the specific needs of {{Client Name}}.
  
- **Design of Disaster Recovery Architecture**: Utilize Azure Front Door features to design a resilient architecture that supports the defined recovery objectives.
  
- **Documentation**: Create a comprehensive disaster recovery plan document that includes:
  - Overview of the disaster recovery strategy
  - Step-by-step procedures for activation
  - Testing protocols to validate the effectiveness of the plan
  - Ongoing maintenance recommendations to ensure the plan remains current and effective
  
- **Training and Support**: Provide training sessions for key personnel on the disaster recovery procedures and ongoing support during the implementation phase.
