
# Project Plan: Custom Health Probes for Azure Front Door

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Custom Health Probes for Azure Front Door" project aims to design and implement tailored health probes that enhance the monitoring capabilities of your Azure Front Door configuration. The primary objective is to ensure the performance and availability of your backend services through proactive health checks. By defining specific health check parameters and configuring the probes to meet your unique requirements, we will enable real-time monitoring that allows for early detection of potential issues. Key outcomes of this project include improved operational efficiency, enhanced reliability of services, and increased user satisfaction. Additionally, comprehensive documentation will be provided to facilitate the ongoing management and adjustment of the probes, ensuring that your monitoring solution remains effective and adaptable over time. This project represents a strategic investment in your infrastructure, aimed at delivering a robust solution that supports your business objectives.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Requirements Gathering**: Collaborate with stakeholders to define specific health check parameters and performance metrics.
- **Design Phase**: Create a detailed design for the custom health probes, ensuring alignment with Azure Front Door best practices.
- **Implementation**: Configure and deploy the health probes within the Azure environment, tailored to the defined requirements.
- **Testing**: Conduct thorough testing of the health probes to ensure they function as intended and provide accurate monitoring.
- **Documentation**: Develop comprehensive documentation that includes setup instructions, management guidelines, and troubleshooting tips.
- **Training**: Provide training sessions for relevant team members on how to manage and adjust the health probes as needed.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of requirements gathering
  - Deliverable: Requirements Specification Document
- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of design
  - Deliverable: Design Document for Health Probes
- **Milestone 3**: Successful implementation of health probes
  - Deliverable: Deployed Health Probes in Azure Environment
- **Milestone 4**: Completion of testing phase
  - Deliverable: Testing Report and Validation
- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of documentation and training
  - Deliverable: User Manual and Training Session Completion
