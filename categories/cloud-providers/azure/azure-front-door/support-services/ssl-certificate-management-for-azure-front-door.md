
# Project Plan Template: SSL Certificate Management for Azure Front Door

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, securing web applications is paramount. The SSL Certificate Management for Azure Front Door project aims to provide a comprehensive solution for managing SSL certificates, ensuring secure communication channels for your applications. The primary objective of this project is to procure SSL certificates from trusted Certificate Authorities, install them on your Azure Front Door configuration, and manage ongoing renewals to prevent any lapses in security. Key outcomes include a fully operational SSL setup that adheres to industry best practices, enhanced security posture, and increased trust from users. Our expert team will guide you through the validation processes and compliance requirements, ensuring that your applications not only meet security standards but also provide a seamless user experience. By the end of this project, you will have a robust SSL management system in place, safeguarding your digital assets and reinforcing your commitment to security.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Procurement of SSL Certificates**: 
  - Identify and select trusted Certificate Authorities.
  - Purchase SSL certificates based on the specific needs of your applications.

- **Installation of SSL Certificates**: 
  - Configure Azure Front Door to integrate the purchased SSL certificates.
  - Ensure proper installation and functionality of the certificates.

- **Ongoing Renewal Management**: 
  - Set up reminders and processes for timely renewal of SSL certificates.
  - Monitor certificate expiration dates and initiate renewals as needed.

- **Best Practices Guidance**: 
  - Provide documentation and training on SSL certificate management.
  - Advise on compliance with industry standards and validation processes.

- **Support and Maintenance**: 
  - Offer ongoing support for any SSL-related issues.
  - Regularly review and update SSL configurations as necessary.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of SSL Certificate Procurement
  - Deliverable: List of purchased SSL certificates and confirmation from Certificate Authorities.

- **Milestone 2**: Successful Installation of SSL Certificates on Azure Front Door
  - Deliverable: Documentation of installation process and verification of SSL functionality.

- **Milestone 3**: Implementation of Renewal Management Processes
  - Deliverable: Renewal management plan with scheduled reminders and processes.

- **Milestone 4**: Delivery of Best Practices Documentation
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive guide on SSL certificate management and compliance.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Review and Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Project closure report including all documentation and feedback.
