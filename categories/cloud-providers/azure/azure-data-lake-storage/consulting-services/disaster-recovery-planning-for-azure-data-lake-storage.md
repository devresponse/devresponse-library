
# Project Plan Template: Disaster Recovery Planning for Data Lake

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's data-driven landscape, ensuring the availability and integrity of your data is paramount. The objective of this project is to develop a comprehensive disaster recovery plan specifically tailored for Azure Data Lake Storage. This project will provide a thorough assessment of your current data architecture, identify potential risks, and design a robust recovery strategy that aligns with your business objectives. Key outcomes will include a detailed plan encompassing data backup solutions, recovery point objectives (RPO), recovery time objectives (RTO), and explicit procedures for data restoration. Additionally, we will offer guidance on best practices for monitoring and testing the disaster recovery plan to ensure its effectiveness. By the end of this project, you will have a clear, actionable plan that safeguards your data assets and supports business continuity in the face of unforeseen events.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Data Architecture**: 
  - Review existing data storage solutions and configurations.
  - Identify vulnerabilities and potential risks associated with data loss.

- **Risk Analysis**: 
  - Conduct a risk assessment to determine the likelihood and impact of various disaster scenarios.
  - Prioritize risks based on their potential impact on business operations.

- **Development of Recovery Strategy**: 
  - Define Recovery Point Objectives (RPO) and Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) tailored to business needs.
  - Design a comprehensive data backup solution that meets the defined RPO and RTO.

- **Procedures for Data Restoration**: 
  - Create detailed documentation outlining step-by-step procedures for data restoration.
  - Include roles and responsibilities for team members during a disaster recovery event.

- **Best Practices for Monitoring and Testing**: 
  - Provide recommendations for ongoing monitoring of the disaster recovery plan.
  - Outline a testing schedule to ensure the plan remains effective and up-to-date.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current Data Architecture Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing findings and recommendations.

- **Milestone 2**: Risk Analysis Completion
  - Deliverable: Risk Assessment Document outlining identified risks and prioritization.

- **Milestone 3**: Recovery Strategy Development
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Recovery Strategy Document including RPO and RTO.

- **Milestone 4**: Data Restoration Procedures Documentation
  - Deliverable: Detailed Data Restoration Procedures Manual.

- **Milestone 5**: Best Practices and Testing Recommendations
  - Deliverable: Best Practices Guide for Monitoring and Testing the Disaster Recovery Plan.
