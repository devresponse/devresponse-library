
# Project Plan Template: Azure Quantum Use Case Feasibility Study

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Quantum Use Case Feasibility Study aims to provide a comprehensive analysis of how Azure Quantum can be effectively utilized within the {{industry/organization}}. The primary objectives of this project include assessing current operational challenges, identifying potential quantum computing applications, and evaluating their feasibility in terms of technical implementation and business impact. By engaging with stakeholders, the consultant will gather insights and analyze existing data to benchmark against industry standards. The key outcomes of this study will be a prioritized list of use cases, a detailed roadmap for implementation, and actionable recommendations for maximizing the benefits of Azure Quantum. This study is crucial for organizations looking to innovate and maintain a competitive edge in a rapidly evolving technological landscape. Ultimately, the findings will empower decision-makers to strategically invest in quantum computing technologies that align with their organizational goals.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Operational Challenges**: Conduct interviews and surveys with stakeholders to identify pain points and inefficiencies in current processes.
- **Identification of Potential Quantum Computing Applications**: Research and analyze various use cases relevant to the {{industry/organization}} that could benefit from quantum computing.
- **Feasibility Evaluation**: Assess the technical and business feasibility of identified use cases, including resource requirements, potential ROI, and alignment with organizational strategy.
- **Stakeholder Engagement**: Facilitate workshops and discussions with key stakeholders to validate findings and gather additional insights.
- **Data Analysis**: Analyze existing data and benchmark against industry standards to support recommendations.
- **Final Report Preparation**: Compile findings into a comprehensive report that includes prioritized use cases, a roadmap for implementation, and strategic recommendations.
