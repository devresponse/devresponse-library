
# Project Plan Template: Azure Policy Change Management Process

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Policy Change Management Process project aims to establish a comprehensive framework for managing updates to Azure Policies effectively. The primary objective of this project is to create a structured approach that allows organizations to implement changes to their Azure Policies with minimal disruption to existing workflows. This will involve the identification of key stakeholders, a thorough assessment of current policies, and the development of a change management process that adheres to organizational standards and regulatory requirements. Key outcomes of this project include the creation of a change request template, guidelines for impact analysis, effective communication strategies for stakeholders, and a rollback plan to address any unforeseen issues. By the end of this project, clients will possess a clear, actionable change management process that enhances their ability to adapt to policy updates while maintaining operational integrity. This structured approach will not only streamline the change management process but also ensure compliance and reduce risks associated with policy changes.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Stakeholder Identification**: Identify and engage all relevant stakeholders, including IT teams, compliance officers, and business unit leaders, to gather input and ensure alignment.
  
- **Current Policy Assessment**: Conduct a comprehensive review of existing Azure Policies to identify areas that require updates or modifications.

- **Change Request Template Development**: Create a standardized template for submitting change requests, ensuring it captures all necessary information for review and approval.

- **Impact Analysis Guidelines**: Develop guidelines to assess the potential impact of proposed changes on existing workflows, systems, and compliance requirements.

- **Communication Strategies**: Formulate a communication plan to keep stakeholders informed throughout the change management process, including regular updates and feedback mechanisms.

- **Rollback Plan Creation**: Design a rollback plan to be implemented in case of unforeseen issues arising from policy changes, ensuring a quick return to previous states if necessary.

- **Training and Documentation**: Provide training sessions and documentation for stakeholders to ensure understanding and adherence to the new change management process.
