
# Project Plan Template: Azure Policy Disaster Recovery Planning

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Policy Disaster Recovery Planning project aims to develop a comprehensive disaster recovery plan that ensures operational continuity and compliance in a cloud-centric environment. The primary objective is to integrate Azure Policy into the disaster recovery framework, allowing for seamless recovery processes that adhere to compliance requirements. This project will involve a thorough assessment of existing Azure Policy configurations, identification of critical resources, and the establishment of recovery objectives tailored to the organization's needs. Key outcomes include a validated disaster recovery plan that incorporates policy testing in simulated environments, ensuring that all policies function as intended during recovery scenarios. The final deliverable will consist of detailed documentation and actionable insights that empower the organization to effectively navigate the complexities of disaster recovery within Azure, thereby enhancing preparedness for unforeseen events.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Azure Policy Configurations**: 
  - Review existing policies and configurations.
  - Identify gaps and areas for improvement.

- **Identification of Critical Resources**: 
  - Determine essential resources that require protection.
  - Prioritize resources based on business impact analysis.

- **Establishment of Recovery Objectives**: 
  - Define Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO).
  - Align objectives with business continuity requirements.

- **Policy Testing and Validation**: 
  - Create simulated disaster recovery environments.
  - Conduct testing of Azure Policies to ensure compliance and functionality.

- **Documentation and Reporting**: 
  - Compile findings and recommendations into a comprehensive report.
  - Provide actionable insights for ongoing policy management and disaster recovery planning.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current Azure Policy Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing current configurations and identified gaps.

- **Milestone 2**: Identification of Critical Resources
  - Deliverable: List of critical resources with prioritization based on business impact.

- **Milestone 3**: Establishment of Recovery Objectives
  - Deliverable: Document outlining defined RTO and RPO for critical resources.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Policy Testing and Validation
  - Deliverable: Testing report with results and recommendations for policy adjustments.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Documentation and Insights Report
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive disaster recovery plan document with actionable insights.
