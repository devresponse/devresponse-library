
# Project Plan Template: Azure Policy Governance Framework Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Policy Governance Framework Design project aims to establish a robust governance framework tailored to {{organization_name}}'s specific needs and regulatory obligations. This project will involve a thorough analysis of the current Azure environment to identify gaps in policy compliance and governance. The primary objective is to design a hierarchical structure for Azure Policies that aligns with {{organization_name}}'s organizational goals, ensuring that policies are effectively categorized and prioritized. Key outcomes include the definition of role assignments to ensure that the right stakeholders have appropriate access and responsibilities within the Azure environment, as well as the development of compliance workflows to facilitate ongoing monitoring and reporting. This comprehensive approach will not only enhance {{organization_name}}'s cloud management capabilities but also streamline operations and improve overall efficiency, ensuring compliance with both internal and external regulations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Current Environment Assessment**: Conduct a detailed analysis of the existing Azure environment to identify compliance gaps and governance issues.
- **Policy Framework Design**: Develop a hierarchical structure for Azure Policies that aligns with organizational goals, including categorization and prioritization of policies.
- **Role Assignment Definition**: Define roles and responsibilities for stakeholders to ensure appropriate access and accountability within the Azure environment.
- **Compliance Workflow Development**: Create workflows for ongoing monitoring and reporting to ensure compliance with internal and external regulations.
- **Documentation**: Provide comprehensive documentation of the governance framework, including policies, roles, and workflows.
- **Training and Support**: Offer training sessions for stakeholders to ensure understanding and effective implementation of the governance framework.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current Environment Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing identified gaps and recommendations.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Design of Policy Framework
  - Deliverable: Hierarchical Policy Structure Document.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Role Assignments Defined
  - Deliverable: Role Assignment Matrix.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Compliance Workflows Developed
  - Deliverable: Compliance Workflow Documentation.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Documentation and Training
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Governance Framework Document and Training Materials.
