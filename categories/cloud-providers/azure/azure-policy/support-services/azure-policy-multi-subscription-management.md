
# Project Plan Template: Azure Policy Multi-Subscription Management

## 1. Executive Summary
The Azure Policy Multi-Subscription Management project aims to establish a robust governance framework across multiple Azure subscriptions. The primary objective is to implement Azure Policies that enforce compliance and best practices tailored to {{organization_name}}'s specific needs. This project will commence with a comprehensive assessment of the current Azure environment to identify areas requiring policy enforcement. Following this assessment, we will create and assign policies across the designated subscriptions, ensuring alignment with compliance requirements. Key outcomes include streamlined cloud management processes, enhanced security, and a unified governance model across all subscriptions. By the end of the project, {{organization_name}} will benefit from improved operational efficiency and a significant reduction in risks associated with non-compliance. This initiative not only aims to meet regulatory standards but also to foster a culture of accountability and proactive management within the organization.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work
- **Assessment Phase**: Conduct a thorough evaluation of the existing Azure environment to identify compliance gaps and areas for policy enforcement.
- **Policy Development**: Create Azure Policies tailored to the specific compliance requirements of {{organization_name}}. This includes defining rules and effects that align with best practices.
- **Policy Assignment**: Assign the developed policies across the designated Azure subscriptions, ensuring that each subscription adheres to the established governance framework.
- **Compliance Tracking Setup**: Implement compliance tracking mechanisms to monitor adherence to the assigned policies. This will involve configuring Azure Policy compliance dashboards and alerts.
- **Reporting Mechanism**: Develop detailed reporting capabilities to provide insights into policy compliance status, including regular updates and exception reports.
- **Training and Documentation**: Provide training sessions for relevant stakeholders and create comprehensive documentation outlining policy management processes and compliance tracking.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the Assessment Phase
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing compliance gaps and recommendations.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Development of Azure Policies
  - Deliverable: Documented Azure Policies ready for implementation.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Policy Assignment Across Subscriptions
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of policy assignments and initial compliance checks.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Compliance Tracking Mechanism Established
  - Deliverable: Functional compliance tracking dashboard and alert system.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Reporting Mechanism in Place
  - Deliverable: Regular compliance reports and exception handling documentation.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Training and Documentation Completed
  - Deliverable: Training materials and policy management documentation distributed to stakeholders.
