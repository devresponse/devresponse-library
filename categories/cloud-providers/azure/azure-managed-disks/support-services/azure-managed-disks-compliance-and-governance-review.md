
# Project Plan Template: Azure Managed Disks Compliance and Governance Review

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Managed Disks Compliance and Governance Review project aims to conduct a thorough assessment of your Azure Managed Disks to ensure compliance with relevant industry standards and your organization's governance policies. This project will provide a comprehensive evaluation of data residency requirements, confirming that your data is stored in accordance with local regulations. Additionally, we will assess the encryption protocols currently in place to safeguard sensitive information and review access controls to verify that only authorized personnel can access critical data. The key outcomes of this project will include a detailed report outlining our findings, identification of compliance gaps, and actionable recommendations for remediation strategies. By the end of this review, your organization will have enhanced insights into its cloud security posture, ensuring that your Azure Managed Disks are fully aligned with best practices and compliance requirements.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Data Residency Requirements**
  - Review current data storage locations and compliance with local regulations.
  - Identify any potential risks associated with data residency.

- **Evaluation of Encryption Protocols**
  - Analyze existing encryption methods for data at rest and in transit.
  - Recommend improvements to enhance data protection.

- **Review of Access Controls**
  - Assess current access control measures and policies.
  - Identify unauthorized access risks and recommend enhancements.

- **Compliance Gap Analysis**
  - Conduct a thorough analysis to identify gaps in compliance with industry standards.
  - Document findings and provide a risk assessment.

- **Reporting and Recommendations**
  - Deliver a comprehensive report detailing findings, compliance gaps, and suggested remediation strategies.
  - Provide a presentation to stakeholders summarizing key insights and recommendations.
