
# Project Plan Template: Azure Managed Disks Cost Optimization Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Managed Disks Cost Optimization Audit aims to deliver a comprehensive analysis of your current Azure Managed Disks usage, focusing on enhancing efficiency and reducing costs. This project will involve a meticulous examination of existing disk configurations, including their sizes and performance tiers. Our expert team will identify opportunities for resizing disks to align more closely with workload requirements, recommend tier adjustments to optimize both performance and cost, and highlight any unused or underutilized resources that can be decommissioned to further minimize expenses. Key outcomes of this audit will include actionable insights and a clear roadmap for implementing cost-saving measures. By the conclusion of this project, you will have a detailed understanding of your Azure Managed Disks usage, enabling you to make informed decisions that ensure your cloud operations remain efficient and budget-friendly.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a preliminary review of current Azure Managed Disks configurations and usage patterns.
- **Data Collection**: Gather detailed metrics on disk sizes, performance tiers, and utilization rates.
- **Analysis**: Analyze the collected data to identify:
  - Opportunities for resizing disks.
  - Recommendations for tier adjustments.
  - Identification of unused or underutilized resources.
- **Recommendations Report**: Develop a comprehensive report outlining findings, recommendations, and a roadmap for implementation.
- **Presentation**: Present findings and recommendations to stakeholders, ensuring clarity and understanding of proposed actions.
