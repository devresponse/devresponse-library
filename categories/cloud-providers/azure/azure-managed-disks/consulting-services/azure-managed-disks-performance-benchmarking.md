
# Project Plan Template: Azure Managed Disks Performance Benchmarking

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Managed Disks Performance Benchmarking project aims to deliver a thorough evaluation of your current Azure Managed Disks configuration, with the objective of identifying performance bottlenecks that may be affecting your cloud operations. This project will leverage industry-standard benchmarking tools and methodologies to assess critical performance metrics such as Input/Output Operations Per Second (IOPS), throughput, and latency. By conducting this comprehensive analysis, our team of experts will provide you with a detailed report that outlines our findings and offers actionable recommendations tailored to your specific setup. The key outcomes of this project include enhanced performance and efficiency of your Azure Managed Disks, empowering your organization with the insights needed to optimize your cloud infrastructure and effectively meet your business needs.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a kickoff meeting to understand the current Azure Managed Disks setup and specific performance concerns.
- **Data Collection**: Gather relevant data on existing configurations, workloads, and performance metrics.
- **Benchmarking Analysis**: Utilize industry-standard tools to measure IOPS, throughput, and latency across various scenarios.
- **Identification of Bottlenecks**: Analyze the collected data to identify performance bottlenecks and areas for improvement.
- **Reporting**: Compile a comprehensive report detailing findings, including visual representations of performance metrics.
- **Recommendations**: Provide actionable recommendations for optimizing Azure Managed Disks configuration based on the analysis.
- **Follow-up Consultation**: Schedule a follow-up meeting to discuss the report and recommendations, and address any questions or concerns.
