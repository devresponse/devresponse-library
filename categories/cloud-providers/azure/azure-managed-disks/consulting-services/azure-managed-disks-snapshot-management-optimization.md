
# Project Plan Template: Azure Managed Disks Snapshot Management Optimization

## 1. Executive Summary
The Azure Managed Disks Snapshot Management Optimization project aims to enhance the operational efficiency of snapshot management practices while significantly reducing associated costs. This project will involve a thorough assessment of the current snapshot management processes, identifying inefficiencies and areas for improvement. The consultant will focus on automating the snapshot creation processes, establishing effective retention policies, and implementing cleanup procedures for obsolete snapshots. Key outcomes include a comprehensive strategy that aligns with Azure best practices, ensuring the client can maintain a streamlined and cost-effective cloud storage solution. The final deliverables will consist of detailed documentation of the implemented strategies and actionable recommendations for ongoing management and optimization. By the end of this project, the client will experience improved operational efficiency, reduced costs, and a more effective approach to managing Azure Managed Disks snapshots.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work
- **Assessment of Current Practices**: Conduct a detailed review of existing snapshot management practices for Azure Managed Disks.
- **Identification of Improvement Areas**: Analyze the assessment results to identify inefficiencies and potential areas for optimization.
- **Automation of Snapshot Creation**: Develop and implement automated processes for creating snapshots to enhance efficiency.
- **Establishment of Retention Policies**: Define and implement effective retention policies to ensure only necessary snapshots are retained.
- **Cleanup Processes**: Design and implement procedures for the regular cleanup of obsolete snapshots.
- **Documentation**: Provide comprehensive documentation of the strategies implemented, including workflows and policies.
- **Recommendations for Ongoing Management**: Deliver actionable recommendations for the client to maintain and optimize snapshot management practices in the future.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current Practices Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing current snapshot management practices.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Identification of Improvement Areas
  - Deliverable: Report outlining identified inefficiencies and proposed areas for improvement.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Automation Implementation
  - Deliverable: Automated snapshot creation process documentation and implementation report.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Retention Policies Established
  - Deliverable: Documented retention policies and guidelines for snapshot management.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Cleanup Processes Implemented
  - Deliverable: Cleanup process documentation and implementation report.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Final Documentation and Recommendations
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive final report including all documentation and recommendations for ongoing management.
