
# Project Plan Template: Azure Managed Disks Cost Optimization Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Managed Disks Cost Optimization Audit aims to empower organizations to maximize their investment in Azure cloud services by identifying and implementing cost-saving opportunities within their managed disks configuration. This project will provide a comprehensive analysis of the current Azure Managed Disks setup, focusing on critical areas such as disk types, unused resources, and storage tiers. Our expert consultants will evaluate usage patterns and performance metrics to uncover inefficiencies that may be leading to unnecessary costs. The key outcomes of this audit will include a detailed report outlining our findings, actionable recommendations for optimizing the managed disks setup, and strategies to enhance overall cloud cost management. By the end of this project, organizations will have a clear understanding of how to leverage Azure's capabilities more effectively, ensuring that they are not only reducing costs but also maintaining optimal performance levels.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a kickoff meeting with stakeholders to understand current Azure Managed Disks usage and objectives.
- **Data Collection**: Gather relevant data on existing managed disks configurations, including disk types, sizes, and usage metrics.
- **Analysis**: Perform a thorough analysis of the collected data to identify:
  - Unused or underutilized disks
  - Opportunities for changing disk types or storage tiers
  - Patterns in disk usage that may indicate inefficiencies
- **Recommendations**: Develop tailored recommendations based on the analysis, focusing on:
  - Cost-saving strategies
  - Best practices for Azure Managed Disks management
  - Potential performance enhancements
- **Reporting**: Prepare a comprehensive audit report that includes:
  - Summary of findings
  - Detailed recommendations
  - Actionable steps for implementation
- **Presentation**: Present the findings and recommendations to stakeholders, allowing for Q&A and discussion of next steps.
