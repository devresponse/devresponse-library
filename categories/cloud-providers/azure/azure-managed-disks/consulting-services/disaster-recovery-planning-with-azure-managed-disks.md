
# Project Plan Template: Disaster Recovery Planning with Azure Managed Disks

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, ensuring business continuity is paramount. The objective of this project is to design and implement a comprehensive disaster recovery plan utilizing Azure Managed Disks. This project will provide a robust framework for safeguarding critical data and ensuring operational integrity in the face of unexpected disruptions. Our expert team will conduct a thorough assessment of your current infrastructure to identify essential components that require protection. Key outcomes of this project include the establishment of geo-redundant storage to protect against regional outages, the configuration of snapshots for point-in-time backups, and the automation of failover processes for seamless transitions to backup systems. By tailoring our approach to meet your specific needs, we aim to enhance your disaster recovery strategy, ensuring that your organization remains resilient and capable of maintaining accessibility to vital information at all times.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment Phase**: 
  - Conduct a comprehensive review of the existing infrastructure.
  - Identify critical components and data that require protection.
  - Evaluate current backup and disaster recovery processes.

- **Design Phase**: 
  - Develop a disaster recovery plan tailored to the organization’s needs.
  - Design geo-redundant storage solutions using Azure Managed Disks.
  - Plan for the configuration of snapshots for point-in-time backups.

- **Implementation Phase**: 
  - Set up Azure Managed Disks and geo-redundant storage.
  - Configure snapshots and establish backup schedules.
  - Automate failover processes to ensure seamless transitions.

- **Testing Phase**: 
  - Conduct failover testing to validate the disaster recovery plan.
  - Review and refine processes based on testing outcomes.

- **Training and Documentation**: 
  - Provide training sessions for relevant staff on the new disaster recovery processes.
  - Deliver comprehensive documentation outlining the disaster recovery plan and procedures.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Infrastructure Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing critical components and current processes.

- **Milestone 2**: Approval of Disaster Recovery Plan Design
  - Deliverable: Documented Disaster Recovery Plan including geo-redundant storage and snapshot configurations.

- **Milestone 3**: Implementation of Azure Managed Disks and Backup Solutions
  - Deliverable: Fully configured Azure Managed Disks and automated failover processes.

- **Milestone 4**: Successful Completion of Testing Phase
  - Deliverable: Testing Report with results and recommendations for improvements.

- **Milestone 5**: Training and Documentation Completion
  - Deliverable: Training materials and comprehensive documentation for ongoing reference.
