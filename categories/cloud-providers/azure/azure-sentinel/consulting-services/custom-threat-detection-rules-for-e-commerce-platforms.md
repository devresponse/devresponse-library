
# Project Plan Template: Custom Threat Detection Rules for E-Commerce Platforms

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, e-commerce platforms are increasingly vulnerable to various security threats that can undermine customer trust and business integrity. The primary objective of this project is to develop and implement custom threat detection rules within Azure Sentinel, specifically designed for e-commerce businesses. This project will begin with a comprehensive assessment of the current security posture of {{Client Name}}, identifying potential vulnerabilities and risks related to payment fraud, account takeovers, and unauthorized access to sensitive customer data. Our team of Azure Sentinel experts will create bespoke detection rules that align with the unique business objectives and operational needs of {{Client Name}}. Key outcomes include enhanced threat detection capabilities, improved incident response times, and comprehensive documentation and training for internal teams. By leveraging Azure Sentinel's advanced features, we aim to fortify the security framework of {{Client Name}}, ensuring the protection of customer data and compliance with industry standards.
