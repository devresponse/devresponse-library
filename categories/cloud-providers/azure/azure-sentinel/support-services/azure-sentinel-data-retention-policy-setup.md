
# Project Plan Template: Azure Sentinel Data Retention Policy Setup

## 1. Executive Summary
The Azure Sentinel Data Retention Policy Setup project aims to establish a comprehensive data retention strategy that aligns with compliance requirements and optimizes storage costs for your organization. This project will involve a thorough assessment of current data retention practices, identifying areas for improvement, and implementing tailored policies that meet business objectives. Key outcomes include defining retention periods for various data types, establishing automated processes for data archiving and deletion, and ensuring compliance with relevant regulations. The project will also encompass the documentation of implemented policies and training for your team to ensure ongoing compliance and efficiency. By the end of this project, your organization will have a robust data retention strategy that enhances the effectiveness of your Azure Sentinel environment, ultimately leading to improved data management and regulatory adherence.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work
- **Assessment Phase**: Conduct a comprehensive review of existing data retention practices within Azure Sentinel.
- **Policy Development**: Identify areas for improvement and develop tailored data retention policies that align with business objectives.
- **Retention Period Definition**: Define specific retention periods for different types of data, ensuring compliance with industry regulations.
- **Automation Implementation**: Establish automated processes for data archiving and deletion to streamline data management.
- **Compliance Check**: Ensure that all configurations comply with relevant regulations and standards.
- **Documentation**: Create detailed documentation of the implemented data retention policies for future reference.
- **Training**: Provide training sessions for your team to ensure they understand the new policies and can maintain compliance effectively.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the assessment phase.
  - Deliverable: Assessment report outlining current practices and areas for improvement.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Development of tailored data retention policies.
  - Deliverable: Documented data retention policies for various data types.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Implementation of automated processes.
  - Deliverable: Configured automation for data archiving and deletion.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Compliance verification.
  - Deliverable: Compliance report confirming adherence to regulations.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Documentation and training.
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive documentation and training materials for team members.
