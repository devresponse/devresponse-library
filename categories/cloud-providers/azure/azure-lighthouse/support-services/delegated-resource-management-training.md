
# Project Plan Template: Delegated Resource Management Training

## 1. Executive Summary

The Delegated Resource Management Training project aims to equip IT teams with the necessary skills and knowledge to effectively utilize Azure Lighthouse for managing resources across multiple tenants. The primary objective is to enhance operational efficiency and collaboration within teams by mastering delegated resource management capabilities. This comprehensive training will cover essential topics such as setting up delegated access, monitoring resource usage, and troubleshooting common issues. By the end of the training sessions, participants will be proficient in leveraging Azure Lighthouse, leading to improved cloud management strategies and overall performance. Key outcomes include increased team competency in resource management, enhanced collaboration across different tenants, and a significant reduction in operational challenges related to resource allocation and monitoring.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Delegated Resource Management Training project includes the following components:

- **Training Needs Assessment**: Conduct an initial assessment to identify the specific needs and skill levels of the IT teams participating in the training.
- **Curriculum Development**: Create a tailored training curriculum that covers:
  - Introduction to Azure Lighthouse
  - Best practices for setting up delegated access
  - Techniques for monitoring resource usage
  - Troubleshooting common issues
- **Training Delivery**: Implement a blended learning approach that includes:
  - Interactive workshops
  - Hands-on exercises
  - Detailed documentation and resources
- **Post-Training Support**: Provide ongoing support and resources to ensure teams can apply what they have learned effectively.
- **Evaluation and Feedback**: Collect feedback from participants to assess the effectiveness of the training and identify areas for improvement.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Training Needs Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing participant needs and skill levels.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Development of Training Curriculum

- **Milestone 5**: Post-Training Support Initiation
  - Deliverable: Schedule and outline of ongoing support resources.
