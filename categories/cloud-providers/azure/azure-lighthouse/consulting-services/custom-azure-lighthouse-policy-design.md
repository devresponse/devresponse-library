
# Project Plan Template: Custom Azure Lighthouse Policy Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Azure Lighthouse Policy Design project aims to enhance the security, compliance, and operational efficiency of your Azure environment through tailored policy design and implementation. Our primary objective is to conduct a comprehensive assessment of your current Azure setup, identifying specific areas that require improvement. By collaborating closely with your team, we will gather insights to understand your unique business requirements. The key outcomes of this project will include the development of custom Azure Lighthouse policies that not only bolster security but also streamline operational processes. Additionally, we will provide detailed documentation and implementation guidelines to ensure your team is well-equipped to manage and maintain these policies. Ultimately, this project is designed to maximize your investment in Azure while ensuring adherence to best practices and regulatory standards.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment Phase**: 
  - Conduct a thorough evaluation of the current Azure environment.
  - Identify gaps in compliance, security, and operational efficiency.
  - Engage with stakeholders to gather insights on specific business needs.

- **Policy Design Phase**: 
  - Develop custom Azure Lighthouse policies tailored to the identified requirements.
  - Ensure policies align with industry best practices and regulatory standards.

- **Implementation Phase**: 
  - Provide guidelines for the implementation of the designed policies.
  - Assist in the deployment of the policies within the Azure environment.

- **Documentation Phase**: 
  - Create comprehensive documentation detailing the policies and their intended use.
  - Develop a user manual for ongoing management and maintenance of the policies.

- **Review and Training Phase**: 
  - Conduct a review session with your team to discuss the policies and their implementation.
  - Provide training to ensure your team is confident in managing the new policies.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the Assessment Phase
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report outlining current state and identified gaps.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of the Policy Design Phase
  - Deliverable: Draft of custom Azure Lighthouse policies for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of the Implementation Phase
  - Deliverable: Implementation Guidelines Document.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of the Documentation Phase
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Policy Documentation and User Manual.

- **Milestone 5**: Completion of the Review and Training Phase
  - Deliverable: Review Session and Training Completion Certificate.
