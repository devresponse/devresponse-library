## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Account Creation**: Assist in creating an Azure account, ensuring all necessary subscriptions and services are activated.
- **Permissions and Settings**: Configure account settings and permissions to align with organizational policies and compliance requirements.
- **Container Setup**: Establish containers within Azure Blob Storage to facilitate organized data storage.
- **Access Policies Configuration**: Develop and implement access policies to ensure data security and restrict access to authorized users only.
- **Testing and Validation**: Conduct thorough testing to validate the setup and ensure that all configurations meet the specified requirements.
- **Documentation**: Provide detailed documentation of the setup process, configurations, and best practices for ongoing management.
- **Training**: Offer training sessions for relevant staff to ensure they are equipped to manage and utilize the Azure Blob Storage effectively.
