## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Analysis of Existing Data Structures**: Conduct a thorough review of the current data architecture and identify key data points relevant to business objectives.
  
- **Requirements Gathering**: Collaborate with stakeholders to understand specific needs and objectives for the custom queries.
  
- **Query Development**: Design and develop custom queries tailored to extract insights from Azure Digital Twins data, ensuring they meet the identified requirements.
  
- **Performance Optimization**: Optimize queries for performance and scalability to ensure they can handle future data growth and integration needs.
  
- **Testing and Validation**: Implement a testing phase to validate the accuracy and reliability of the queries, making adjustments as necessary based on feedback.
  
- **Documentation**: Provide comprehensive documentation detailing the queries developed, including usage instructions and best practices for integration.
  
- **Training and Support**: Offer training sessions for relevant staff to ensure they can effectively utilize the new queries and understand the insights generated.
