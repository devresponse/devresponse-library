
# Project Plan Template: Data Asset Lifecycle Management

## 1. Executive Summary

The Data Asset Lifecycle Management project aims to implement a comprehensive strategy utilizing Azure Purview to enhance the management of data assets throughout their lifecycle. The primary objective is to establish clear policies for data retention, archiving, and deletion, ensuring compliance with industry standards and regulatory requirements. This project will begin with a thorough assessment of current data management practices, identifying gaps and opportunities for improvement. Following this assessment, tailored policies will be developed to align with best practices. Technical support will be provided during the implementation phase to ensure Azure Purview is configured correctly, automating the processes of data management. Furthermore, training sessions will be conducted for your team, equipping them with the necessary skills to manage and maintain the lifecycle policies effectively. By the end of this project, your organization will benefit from a robust framework for managing data assets, enhancing compliance, and optimizing operational efficiency.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment Phase**: 
  - Conduct a comprehensive review of current data management practices.
  - Identify gaps in data retention, archiving, and deletion policies.
  - Gather stakeholder input to understand specific needs and requirements.

- **Policy Development**: 
  - Develop tailored data management policies that align with industry standards and regulatory requirements.
  - Create documentation outlining the policies for data retention, archiving, and deletion.

- **Implementation Support**: 
  - Configure Azure Purview to automate data management processes.
  - Ensure integration with existing data systems and workflows.

- **Training and Knowledge Transfer**: 
  - Conduct training sessions for your team on managing and maintaining the lifecycle policies.
  - Provide ongoing support and resources for continuous improvement.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the Assessment Phase
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing current practices and identified gaps.

- **Milestone 2**: Development of Data Management Policies
  - Deliverable: Policy Documentation outlining retention, archiving, and deletion strategies.

- **Milestone 3**: Implementation of Azure Purview
  - Deliverable: Configured Azure Purview environment with automated processes.

- **Milestone 4**: Training Completion
  - Deliverable: Training Materials and Attendance Records for team sessions.
