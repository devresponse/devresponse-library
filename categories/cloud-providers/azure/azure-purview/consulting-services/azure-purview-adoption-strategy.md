
# Project Plan Template: Azure Purview Adoption Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Purview Adoption Strategy project is designed to facilitate the successful implementation and utilization of Azure Purview within {{Organization Name}}. The primary objective of this project is to create a comprehensive strategy that ensures stakeholder engagement, effective change management, and the demonstration of tangible benefits associated with data governance. This project will involve conducting workshops and interviews to gather insights from key personnel, identifying potential challenges, and developing a tailored adoption plan. Key outcomes will include a clear communication strategy, training sessions for staff, and performance metrics to measure success. Ultimately, this initiative aims to foster a culture of data governance that empowers teams to leverage Azure Purview effectively, enhancing data management and compliance across the organization. By aligning the adoption strategy with organizational goals, we aim to ensure that Azure Purview becomes an integral part of the data management framework, driving efficiency and accountability.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Stakeholder Engagement**: Identify and engage key stakeholders to ensure buy-in and support for the Azure Purview implementation.
- **Workshops and Interviews**: Conduct a series of workshops and interviews to gather insights on current data management practices and identify potential challenges.
- **Change Management**: Develop a change management plan that addresses the transition to Azure Purview, including strategies to mitigate resistance and promote acceptance.
- **Adoption Plan Development**: Create a tailored adoption plan that includes:
  - Training sessions for end-users and administrators.
  - Communication strategies to keep stakeholders informed throughout the process.
  - Performance metrics to evaluate the effectiveness of the adoption strategy.
- **Implementation Support**: Provide ongoing support during the implementation phase to address any issues and ensure a smooth transition.
- **Post-Implementation Review**: Conduct a review after implementation to assess the success of the adoption strategy and identify areas for improvement.

- **Milestone 6**: Post-implementation review
