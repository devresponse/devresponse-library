
# Project Plan Template: Custom Classification Rules Development

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's data-driven environment, organizations must ensure that their sensitive information is properly classified and managed. The objective of the Custom Classification Rules Development project is to create tailored classification rules within Azure Purview that align with your unique business requirements. This project will involve a collaborative effort between our expert consultants and your team to identify specific types of sensitive data that need to be detected and categorized. Key outcomes include the development and implementation of effective classification rules that enhance data visibility and ensure compliance with regulatory standards. Additionally, the project will encompass thorough testing of these rules to validate their effectiveness and accuracy. By the conclusion of this project, your organization will possess a robust set of custom classification rules that not only optimize your data governance strategy but also significantly improve your overall cloud operations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a comprehensive assessment of your current data classification processes and identify gaps in compliance and visibility.
- **Stakeholder Engagement**: Collaborate with key stakeholders to gather requirements and understand the specific types of sensitive data that need classification.
- **Rule Development**: Design and develop custom classification rules tailored to your business needs within Azure Purview.
- **Implementation**: Deploy the developed classification rules into your Azure Purview environment.
- **Testing and Validation**: Perform thorough testing of the classification rules to ensure they accurately detect and categorize sensitive data.
- **Training and Documentation**: Provide training sessions for your team on the new classification rules and deliver comprehensive documentation for future reference.
- **Project Closure**: Conduct a final review with stakeholders to ensure all objectives have been met and gather feedback for continuous improvement.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing current processes and identified gaps.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Stakeholder Requirements Gathering
  - Deliverable: Requirements Document outlining specific classification needs.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Development of Custom Classification Rules
  - Deliverable: Set of developed classification rules ready for implementation.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Implementation of Rules in Azure Purview
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of successful deployment of classification rules.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Testing and Validation Completion
  - Deliverable: Testing Report validating the effectiveness and accuracy of the rules.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Training and Documentation Delivery
  - Deliverable: Training materials and user documentation provided to the team.
  
- **Milestone 7**: Project Closure and Review
  - Deliverable: Final Project Review Report summarizing outcomes and feedback.
