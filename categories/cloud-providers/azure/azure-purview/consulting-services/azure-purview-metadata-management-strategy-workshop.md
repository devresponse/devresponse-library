
# Project Plan Template: Azure Purview Metadata Strategy Workshop

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Purview Metadata Management Strategy Workshop aims to empower organizations to effectively manage their metadata using Azure Purview. This interactive workshop will provide your team with the knowledge and tools necessary to define a comprehensive metadata management strategy tailored to your specific business needs. Our expert consultants will guide participants through the identification of critical metadata elements essential for operations, the establishment of classification rules for proper categorization, and the development of protocols to maintain metadata consistency across various departments. Key outcomes of the workshop include a clear roadmap for implementing Azure Purview, enhanced data governance, and optimized cloud operations. By the end of the workshop, your organization will be equipped to harness the full potential of Azure Purview, ensuring that metadata management practices align with industry best practices. This workshop is ideal for teams seeking to elevate their metadata management capabilities and drive operational efficiency.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Workshop Preparation**
  - Conduct pre-workshop assessment to understand current metadata practices and challenges.
  - Develop customized workshop materials based on the assessment findings.

- **Workshop Agenda**
  - Introduction to Azure Purview and its capabilities.
  - Identification of critical metadata elements relevant to your organization.
  - Setting up classification rules for effective metadata categorization.
  - Establishing protocols for maintaining metadata consistency across departments.
  - Interactive sessions for hands-on practice and real-world application.
  - Q&A session to address specific concerns and queries from participants.

- **Post-Workshop Deliverables**
  - Comprehensive report summarizing workshop findings and recommendations.
  - Customized roadmap for implementing Azure Purview within the organization.
  - Follow-up consultation to assist with initial implementation steps.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of pre-workshop assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report outlining current metadata practices and areas for improvement.

- **Milestone 2**: Conducting the Azure Purview Metadata Management Strategy Workshop
  - Deliverable: Workshop materials and participant feedback forms.

- **Milestone 3**: Delivery of post-workshop report and roadmap
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report with recommendations and implementation roadmap.

- **Milestone 4**: Follow-up consultation session
  - Deliverable: Summary of follow-up consultation and next steps for implementation.
