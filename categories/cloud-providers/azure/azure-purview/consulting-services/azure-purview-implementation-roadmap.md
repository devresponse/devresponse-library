
# Azure Purview Implementation Roadmap Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Purview Implementation Roadmap project aims to provide a structured and strategic approach to integrating Azure Purview into your organization's data governance framework. The primary objective of this project is to enhance data management practices by leveraging Azure Purview's capabilities, ensuring compliance, data quality, and improved decision-making processes. Our team of experts will conduct a thorough assessment of your current data governance practices, identifying strengths and areas for improvement. Engaging with key stakeholders across your organization will be crucial to gather insights and ensure alignment with business objectives. Based on this analysis, we will develop a phased implementation plan that outlines specific milestones, timelines, and resource requirements. The key outcomes of this project will include a comprehensive roadmap for Azure Purview implementation, improved data governance practices, and empowered teams capable of effectively utilizing Azure Purview for ongoing data management and compliance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Azure Purview Implementation Roadmap project includes the following key activities:

- **Assessment of Current Data Governance Practices**: Conduct interviews and workshops with stakeholders to evaluate existing data governance frameworks and practices.
- **Stakeholder Engagement**: Identify and engage key stakeholders across the organization to gather insights and ensure alignment with business objectives.
- **Gap Analysis**: Analyze the findings from the assessment to identify gaps and areas for improvement in current data governance practices.
- **Development of Implementation Roadmap**: Create a phased implementation plan that includes:
  - Specific milestones and timelines
  - Resource requirements
  - Risk assessment and mitigation strategies
- **Training and Enablement**: Develop training materials and conduct workshops to empower teams with the knowledge and skills necessary for effective use of Azure Purview.
- **Ongoing Support and Evaluation**: Establish a framework for ongoing support and evaluation of Azure Purview's implementation and effectiveness.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

The following milestones and deliverables will be achieved throughout the project:

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current State Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing current data governance practices and identified gaps.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Stakeholder Engagement and Insights Gathering
  - Deliverable: Stakeholder Engagement Summary Report.

- **Milestone 3**: Development of Implementation Roadmap
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Implementation Roadmap Document.

- **Milestone 4**: Training and Enablement Sessions
  - Deliverable: Training Materials and Workshop Completion Report.

- **Milestone 5**: Establishment of Ongoing Support Framework
  - Deliverable: Support and Evaluation Framework Document.
