
# Azure Purview Training Program Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Purview Training Program aims to empower employees with the essential knowledge and skills required to effectively utilize Azure Purview within the organization. This comprehensive training initiative is designed to develop tailored training materials that cover the core functionalities and best practices of Azure Purview. The program will include interactive workshops led by experienced consultants, providing participants with the opportunity to apply their learning in real-world scenarios. Hands-on exercises will be integrated into the training to reinforce understanding and facilitate the practical application of the concepts covered. By the conclusion of the program, employees will be equipped to leverage Azure Purview to enhance data governance, compliance, and overall cloud operations. This will ensure that the organization maximizes its investment in Azure technologies, leading to improved data management and operational efficiency.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Azure Purview Training Program includes the following key components:

- **Needs Assessment**: Conduct a thorough assessment of the organization's current knowledge and usage of Azure Purview to tailor the training program effectively.
- **Development of Training Materials**: Create customized training materials that cover the core functionalities, best practices, and use cases of Azure Purview.
- **Interactive Workshops**: Organize a series of interactive workshops led by experienced consultants, focusing on practical application and real-world scenarios.
- **Hands-on Exercises**: Incorporate hands-on exercises to reinforce learning and ensure participants can apply their knowledge effectively.
- **Feedback and Evaluation**: Implement a feedback mechanism to evaluate the effectiveness of the training and make necessary adjustments for future sessions.
- **Certification**: Provide participants with a certification upon successful completion of the training program.
