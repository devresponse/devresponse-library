
# Project Plan Template: Azure Purview Custom Reporting and Dashboard Creation

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Purview Custom Reporting and Dashboard Creation project aims to enhance data visibility and compliance tracking through the design and implementation of tailored reports and dashboards within the Azure Purview environment. The primary objective is to create intuitive visualizations that provide stakeholders with critical insights into metadata, data lineage, and compliance metrics. By gathering requirements from key stakeholders, we will ensure that the dashboards meet their specific needs and facilitate informed decision-making. Key outcomes of this project include improved data governance, enhanced compliance monitoring, and user-friendly reporting capabilities. Regular feedback sessions will be conducted to refine the dashboards, ensuring they remain aligned with the client's evolving requirements. The final deliverables will encompass comprehensive documentation and training materials, empowering users to effectively leverage the new reporting capabilities.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Requirements Gathering**: Conduct interviews and workshops with key stakeholders to identify specific reporting needs and compliance metrics.
- **Dashboard Design**: Create wireframes and prototypes for the dashboards based on gathered requirements, ensuring they are intuitive and user-friendly.
- **Implementation**: Develop and implement the custom reports and dashboards within the Azure Purview environment.
- **User Testing**: Facilitate user testing sessions to gather feedback on the dashboards and make necessary adjustments.
- **Documentation**: Prepare comprehensive documentation detailing the dashboard functionalities, data sources, and usage guidelines.
- **Training**: Develop training materials and conduct training sessions for end-users to ensure they can effectively utilize the new reporting capabilities.
- **Feedback Loop**: Establish a process for ongoing feedback to continuously improve the dashboards post-implementation.
