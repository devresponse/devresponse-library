
# Project Plan Template: Azure Purview Data Governance Policy Setup

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Purview Data Governance Policy Setup project aims to establish a comprehensive framework for data governance within your organization. The primary objective is to configure and implement data governance policies that align with your organizational goals and comply with relevant regulatory standards. This project will involve a thorough assessment of your current data management practices, followed by the development of tailored governance policies that address critical areas such as data classification, access controls, and compliance monitoring. Key outcomes of this project include a robust data governance framework that enhances data management workflows, improved compliance with regulatory requirements, and the provision of documentation and training for your team. By the conclusion of this project, your organization will be equipped with the necessary tools and knowledge to maintain effective data governance practices, ensuring that your data assets are managed securely and efficiently.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a comprehensive review of current data management practices to identify gaps and areas for improvement.
- **Policy Development**: Create tailored data governance policies that include:
  - Data classification standards
  - Access control mechanisms
  - Compliance monitoring procedures
- **Implementation**: Configure Azure Purview to support the newly developed governance policies.
- **Documentation**: Provide detailed documentation outlining the governance framework, policies, and procedures.
- **Training**: Conduct training sessions for your team to ensure they understand and can effectively implement the governance policies.
- **Support**: Offer ongoing support during the initial implementation phase to address any questions or challenges that arise.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing current practices and recommendations.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Development of Data Governance Policies
  - Deliverable: Draft of tailored governance policies for review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Implementation of Policies in Azure Purview
  - Deliverable: Configured Azure Purview environment with governance policies applied.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Documentation Delivery
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive documentation package including policies and procedures.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Training Completion
  - Deliverable: Training session materials and attendance records.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Project Closure and Support Handoff
  - Deliverable: Final project report and support transition plan.
