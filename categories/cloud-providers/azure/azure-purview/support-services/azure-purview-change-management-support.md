
# Project Plan Template: Azure Purview Change Management Support

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Purview Change Management Support project aims to facilitate seamless transitions and updates within your Azure Purview environments. Our primary objective is to ensure that all necessary changes are identified, planned, and implemented with minimal disruption to your operations. This project will involve a thorough analysis of your current Azure Purview setup, stakeholder consultations, and risk assessments to ensure that modifications align with your organizational goals. Key outcomes include enhanced operational efficiency, a robust framework for managing future updates, and a comprehensive post-implementation review process. By leveraging best practices in change management, our expert team will work closely with you to maintain the integrity and performance of your cloud services, ultimately supporting your organization's growth and adaptability in a rapidly evolving digital landscape.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Stakeholder Consultations**: Engage with key stakeholders to gather requirements and expectations regarding the changes to be implemented.
- **Change Identification**: Analyze the current Azure Purview environment to identify necessary changes and improvements.
- **Risk Assessment**: Conduct a thorough risk assessment to identify potential challenges and develop mitigation strategies.
- **Change Planning**: Create a detailed change management plan that outlines the steps for implementation, including timelines and resource allocation.
- **Testing**: Implement a testing phase to ensure that all changes function as intended and do not disrupt existing services.
- **Implementation**: Execute the change management plan, ensuring that all modifications are made according to the established timeline.
- **Post-Implementation Review**: Conduct a review after implementation to assess the effectiveness of the changes and gather feedback from stakeholders.
- **Documentation**: Provide comprehensive documentation of all changes made, including lessons learned and recommendations for future updates.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Stakeholder Consultations
  - Deliverable: Stakeholder Requirements Document
- **Milestone 2**: Change Identification and Risk Assessment
  - Deliverable: Change Identification Report and Risk Assessment Document
- **Milestone 3**: Change Management Plan Finalization
  - Deliverable: Approved Change Management Plan
- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Testing Phase
  - Deliverable: Testing Results Report
- **Milestone 5**: Implementation of Changes
  - Deliverable: Implementation Completion Report
- **Milestone 6**: Post-Implementation Review
  - Deliverable: Post-Implementation Review Document
