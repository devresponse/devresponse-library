
# Project Plan Template: Azure Purview Disaster Recovery Planning

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Purview Disaster Recovery Planning project aims to develop a comprehensive disaster recovery strategy tailored specifically for organizations utilizing Azure Purview. In today's digital landscape, the availability of data and business continuity are critical to operational success. This project will begin with a thorough assessment of the current Azure Purview setup, identifying potential risks and vulnerabilities that could impact data accessibility. Based on the findings, a customized disaster recovery strategy will be crafted, encompassing backup solutions, failover procedures, and clearly defined recovery time objectives (RTOs) and recovery point objectives (RPOs). The project will also include the creation of detailed documentation and training sessions to equip your staff with the necessary skills to implement the disaster recovery plan effectively. By the conclusion of this project, your organization will have a robust disaster recovery plan in place, ensuring that data remains accessible and operations continue smoothly, even in the face of unexpected disruptions.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment Phase**: 
  - Conduct a comprehensive evaluation of the current Azure Purview setup.
  - Identify potential risks, vulnerabilities, and areas for improvement.
  
- **Strategy Development**: 
  - Design a customized disaster recovery strategy based on the assessment findings.
  - Define backup solutions tailored to your data architecture.
  - Establish failover procedures to ensure minimal downtime.
  - Determine Recovery Time Objectives (RTOs) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPOs).

- **Documentation**: 
  - Create detailed documentation outlining the disaster recovery plan.
  - Include step-by-step procedures for executing the plan.

- **Training**: 
  - Conduct training sessions for staff to ensure they are well-prepared to implement the disaster recovery plan.
  - Provide ongoing support and resources for staff as needed.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the Assessment Phase
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing risks and vulnerabilities.

- **Milestone 2**: Development of the Disaster Recovery Strategy
  - Deliverable: Customized Disaster Recovery Strategy Document.

- **Milestone 3**: Documentation of Procedures
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Disaster Recovery Plan Document.

- **Milestone 4**: Training Sessions
  - Deliverable: Training Materials and Completion Certificates for participants.
