
# Project Plan Template: Azure CDN Cost Optimization Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure CDN Cost Optimization Analysis project aims to conduct a thorough examination of your current Azure Content Delivery Network (CDN) usage, with the primary objective of identifying potential cost-saving opportunities. This project will provide a comprehensive overview of your CDN configuration, usage patterns, and performance metrics, allowing us to pinpoint areas where costs can be optimized without sacrificing service quality. Our expert team will analyze your traffic patterns, caching strategies, and resource allocation to deliver actionable insights and tailored recommendations. By the conclusion of this project, you will receive a detailed report that outlines our findings and suggested optimizations, empowering you to make informed decisions that enhance both performance and cost-effectiveness. The key outcomes of this project include improved efficiency in CDN usage, significant cost savings, and the maintenance of high service standards, ultimately ensuring that your CDN remains reliable and effective in meeting your business needs.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Current CDN Configuration Review**: Analyze the existing setup of your Azure CDN, including endpoints, caching rules, and custom domains.
- **Usage Pattern Analysis**: Evaluate historical usage data to identify trends in traffic, peak usage times, and geographic distribution of users.
- **Performance Metrics Evaluation**: Assess performance metrics such as latency, throughput, and error rates to understand the current service quality.
- **Cost Analysis**: Review billing statements and usage reports to identify cost drivers and areas where expenses can be reduced.
- **Recommendations Development**: Provide actionable recommendations based on the analysis, including potential changes to caching strategies, resource allocation, and traffic management.
- **Final Report Preparation**: Compile findings and recommendations into a comprehensive report, including visualizations and executive summaries for stakeholders.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Kick-off Meeting**: Schedule and conduct a project kick-off meeting with stakeholders to align on objectives and expectations. 
- **Completion of Current CDN Configuration Review**: Deliver a summary of the current CDN configuration and any immediate observations.
- **Submission of Usage Pattern Analysis**: Present findings from the usage pattern analysis, including traffic trends and peak usage times.
- **Performance Metrics Report**: Provide a detailed report on performance metrics evaluated during the analysis phase.
- **Cost Analysis Findings**: Share insights from the cost analysis, highlighting key cost drivers and potential savings.
- **Final Report Delivery**: Submit the comprehensive final report containing all findings, recommendations, and visualizations.
