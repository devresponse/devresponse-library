
# Project Plan Template: Azure CDN Content Purge Automation

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure CDN Content Purge Automation project aims to streamline and automate the content purge processes within your Azure Content Delivery Network (CDN). The primary objective of this project is to develop efficient scripts and workflows that will identify and remove outdated or unwanted content from your CDN, ensuring that users consistently receive the most relevant and up-to-date information. By automating the content management process, we will enhance operational efficiency and significantly reduce the manual effort required for regular purging tasks. Our team of experts will collaborate closely with your organization to understand specific requirements, configure necessary settings, and implement robust solutions that adhere to best practices for Azure CDN management. Key outcomes of this project include a fully functional automated system that is easy to maintain and scale, improved content relevance for end-users, and a reduction in the time and resources spent on manual content management. Ultimately, this project will empower your organization to adapt quickly to evolving content needs while maintaining high standards of service delivery.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Requirements Gathering**: Collaborate with stakeholders to gather and document specific requirements for the content purge automation.
- **Script Development**: Create scripts that will automate the identification and removal of outdated or unwanted content from the Azure CDN.
- **Workflow Design**: Design workflows that will facilitate the automated purging process, including scheduling and triggering mechanisms.
- **Testing and Validation**: Conduct thorough testing of the scripts and workflows to ensure they function correctly and meet the defined requirements.
- **Deployment**: Implement the automated system within the Azure environment, ensuring minimal disruption to existing services.
- **Documentation**: Provide comprehensive documentation detailing the system architecture, configuration settings, and operational guidelines.
- **Training**: Offer training sessions for your team to ensure they are equipped to manage and maintain the automated system effectively.
- **Support and Maintenance**: Establish a support plan for ongoing maintenance and troubleshooting post-deployment.
