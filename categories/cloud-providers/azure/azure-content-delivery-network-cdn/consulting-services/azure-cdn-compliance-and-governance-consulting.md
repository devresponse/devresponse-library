
# Project Plan Template: Azure CDN Compliance and Governance Consulting

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure CDN Compliance and Governance Consulting project aims to assist organizations in achieving compliance with essential industry regulations, including GDPR and HIPAA, while utilizing Azure's Content Delivery Network (CDN) services. In today's digital landscape, the importance of adhering to regulatory requirements cannot be overstated, as non-compliance can lead to significant legal and financial repercussions. This project will provide a comprehensive assessment of the current Azure CDN setup, identifying potential compliance gaps and risks that may exist. Key outcomes include tailored recommendations for optimizing CDN configurations, the development of governance frameworks that align with business objectives, and the creation of detailed documentation outlining compliance strategies. By the end of this project, organizations will have a clear roadmap for implementation and ongoing support to ensure that their Azure CDN remains compliant as regulations evolve.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment Phase**: 
  - Conduct a thorough review of the current Azure CDN setup.
  - Identify compliance gaps related to GDPR, HIPAA, and other relevant regulations.
  - Evaluate existing data handling and storage practices.

- **Recommendations Phase**: 
  - Provide tailored recommendations for optimizing CDN configurations.
  - Suggest best practices for secure data handling and storage.

- **Governance Framework Development**: 
  - Assist in developing governance frameworks that align with business objectives.
  - Create policies and procedures to ensure ongoing compliance.

- **Documentation**: 
  - Prepare detailed documentation of compliance strategies.
  - Develop a roadmap for implementation of recommendations.

- **Ongoing Support**: 
  - Offer support to ensure compliance as regulations evolve.
  - Provide training and resources for staff on compliance best practices.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the Assessment Phase
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing compliance gaps and risks.

- **Milestone 2**: Delivery of Recommendations
  - Deliverable: Recommendations Document outlining optimization strategies.

- **Milestone 3**: Development of Governance Framework
  - Deliverable: Governance Framework Document including policies and procedures.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Documentation and Roadmap
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Compliance Strategy Document and Implementation Roadmap.

- **Milestone 5**: Ongoing Support Initiation
  - Deliverable: Support Plan outlining ongoing compliance maintenance and training.
