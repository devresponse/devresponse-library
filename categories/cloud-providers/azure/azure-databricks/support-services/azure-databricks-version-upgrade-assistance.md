
# Project Plan Template: Azure Databricks Version Upgrade Assistance

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Databricks Version Upgrade Assistance project aims to facilitate a seamless transition to the latest version of Azure Databricks, ensuring that your organization can leverage new features, enhance performance, and maintain compatibility with other Azure services. This project will provide comprehensive guidance and support throughout the upgrade process, starting with a thorough assessment of your current environment. Our expert team will identify potential compatibility issues and develop a tailored upgrade plan that addresses your specific needs. Key outcomes include a successful upgrade execution, optimized configurations for the new version, and robust post-upgrade support to resolve any issues that may arise. Ultimately, our goal is to minimize disruption to your operations while enhancing your overall cloud experience, enabling you to fully utilize the capabilities of the latest Azure Databricks release.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Environment**: Conduct a detailed analysis of the existing Azure Databricks setup, including configurations, integrations, and usage patterns.
  
- **Compatibility Analysis**: Identify potential compatibility issues with the new version, including dependencies on other Azure services and custom configurations.

- **Tailored Upgrade Plan**: Develop a customized upgrade plan that outlines the steps necessary for a successful transition, including timelines, resource allocation, and risk management strategies.

- **Execution of Upgrade**: Assist in the execution of the upgrade process, ensuring that all configurations are optimized for the new version and that best practices are followed.

- **Post-Upgrade Support**: Provide ongoing support after the upgrade to address any issues that may arise and to assist users in fully utilizing the new features and enhancements.

- **Training and Documentation**: Offer training sessions and comprehensive documentation to ensure that your team is well-equipped to operate within the upgraded environment.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current Environment Assessment  
  **Deliverable**: Assessment Report detailing current configurations and potential issues.

- **Milestone 2**: Compatibility Analysis Completion  
  **Deliverable**: Compatibility Analysis Report outlining identified issues and recommendations.

- **Milestone 3**: Tailored Upgrade Plan Approval  
  **Deliverable**: Customized Upgrade Plan document approved by stakeholders.

- **Milestone 4**: Successful Execution of Upgrade  
  **Deliverable**: Confirmation of upgrade completion and optimized configurations.

- **Milestone 5**: Post-Upgrade Support Initiation  
  **Deliverable**: Support Plan outlining ongoing assistance and training sessions.
