
# Azure Bastion Deployment Strategy Workshop Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Azure Bastion Deployment Strategy Workshop aims to equip businesses with the essential knowledge and tools required for a successful deployment of Azure Bastion. This interactive workshop will provide a comprehensive assessment of your current cloud infrastructure, identifying gaps and opportunities for enhancement. We will work closely with your team to understand your specific security requirements, ensuring that the deployment aligns with industry best practices and compliance standards. Key outcomes of this workshop include a customized roadmap that outlines the necessary steps for implementation, including timelines, resource allocation, and key milestones. By the end of the workshop, participants will have a clear understanding of how to optimize their Azure Bastion deployment for maximum efficiency and security, supported by expert insights and strategies from our seasoned consultants.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Workshop Assessment**: Conduct a preliminary assessment of the client's existing cloud infrastructure to identify current capabilities and areas for improvement.
  
- **Workshop Delivery**: Facilitate an interactive workshop that covers:
  - Overview of Azure Bastion and its benefits.
  - Security requirements and compliance considerations.
  - Best practices for Azure Bastion deployment.
  - Hands-on exercises to reinforce learning.

- **Customized Roadmap Development**: Post-workshop, develop a tailored roadmap that includes:
  - Step-by-step implementation plan.
  - Resource allocation and responsibilities.
  - Timelines for each phase of deployment.
  - Key milestones to track progress.

- **Follow-Up Support**: Provide follow-up consultations to address any questions and assist with the initial phases of deployment.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Pre-Workshop Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing current infrastructure and identified gaps.

- **Milestone 2**: Conducting the Workshop
  - Deliverable: Workshop materials, including presentation slides and handouts.

- **Milestone 3**: Delivery of Customized Roadmap
  - Deliverable: A comprehensive roadmap document outlining the implementation steps, timelines, and resource allocations.

- **Milestone 4**: Follow-Up Consultation
  - Deliverable: Summary report of follow-up discussions and any additional recommendations.
