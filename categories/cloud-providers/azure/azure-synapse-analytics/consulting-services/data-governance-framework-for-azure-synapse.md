
# Project Plan Template: Data Governance Framework for Azure Synapse

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's data-driven landscape, establishing a robust data governance framework is essential for organizations leveraging Azure Synapse Analytics. The primary objective of this project is to create a comprehensive governance framework that aligns with industry best practices and regulatory requirements. This framework will encompass policies, procedures, and standards that ensure data quality, security, and compliance throughout the data lifecycle. Key outcomes include a thorough assessment of current data management practices, identification of gaps, and the formulation of tailored strategies to address these gaps. Our collaborative approach with your team will ensure that the framework is not only effective but also practical and easy to implement. Ultimately, the final deliverable will empower your organization to manage data responsibly, mitigate risks, and enhance decision-making capabilities, fostering a culture of data stewardship and accountability.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Data Management Practices**: Conduct a comprehensive review of existing data governance policies, procedures, and practices within the organization.
  
- **Gap Analysis**: Identify gaps in current practices against industry standards and regulatory requirements, focusing on data quality, security, and compliance.

- **Framework Development**: Design a tailored data governance framework that includes:
  - Policies for data management
  - Procedures for data quality assurance
  - Standards for data security and compliance

- **Implementation Plan**: Develop a step-by-step implementation plan that outlines how the framework will be rolled out, including training and support for staff.

- **Stakeholder Engagement**: Collaborate with key stakeholders to ensure buy-in and alignment with organizational goals.

- **Documentation**: Provide comprehensive documentation of the governance framework, including all policies, procedures, and standards.

- **Review and Feedback**: Facilitate review sessions with stakeholders to gather feedback and make necessary adjustments to the framework.
