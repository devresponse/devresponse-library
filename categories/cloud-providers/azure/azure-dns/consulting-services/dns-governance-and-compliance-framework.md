
# Project Plan Template: DNS Governance and Compliance Framework

## 1. Executive Summary

The DNS Governance and Compliance Framework project aims to establish a comprehensive structure for managing Azure DNS effectively. The primary objective of this project is to provide organizations with guidelines and best practices that ensure DNS management aligns with industry standards and regulatory requirements. This initiative will involve a thorough assessment of current DNS practices to identify compliance gaps and develop policies that govern DNS operations. Key outcomes of the project include the creation of a robust framework that mitigates risks associated with DNS mismanagement, enhances security, and supports the overall digital transformation strategy of the organization. By implementing this framework, stakeholders will be equipped with the necessary tools and knowledge to monitor adherence to established policies, ensuring that all DNS operations are compliant and secure. Training sessions will be conducted to ensure that all relevant personnel understand compliance requirements, thereby fostering a culture of accountability and vigilance in DNS management.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current DNS Practices**: Conduct a comprehensive review of existing DNS management practices within the organization to identify strengths and weaknesses.
  
- **Compliance Gap Analysis**: Identify gaps in compliance with industry standards and regulatory requirements related to DNS management.
  
- **Policy Development**: Create a set of policies that govern DNS operations, ensuring alignment with best practices and compliance requirements.
  
- **Procedure Creation**: Develop detailed procedures for monitoring adherence to the established policies, including roles and responsibilities for stakeholders.
  
- **Training and Awareness**: Design and implement training programs for all relevant personnel to ensure understanding and compliance with the new DNS governance framework.
  
- **Monitoring and Reporting**: Establish a framework for ongoing monitoring and reporting of DNS compliance, including metrics for success and regular review processes.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the assessment of current DNS practices.
  - **Deliverable**: Assessment Report detailing findings and recommendations.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of compliance gap analysis.
  - **Deliverable**: Gap Analysis Report highlighting areas of non-compliance.

- **Milestone 3**: Development of DNS governance policies.
  - **Deliverable**: Policy Document outlining governance structures and procedures.

- **Milestone 4**: Creation of monitoring procedures.
  - **Deliverable**: Monitoring Procedures Document detailing compliance checks.

- **Milestone 5**: Implementation of training programs.
  - **Deliverable**: Training Materials and Attendance Records.

- **Milestone 6**: Establishment of monitoring and reporting framework.
  - **Deliverable**: Monitoring Framework Document and Initial Compliance Report.
