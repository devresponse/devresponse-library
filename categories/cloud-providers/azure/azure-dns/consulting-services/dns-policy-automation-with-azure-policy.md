
# Project Plan Template: DNS Policy Automation with Azure Policy

## 1. Executive Summary

The DNS Policy Automation with Azure Policy project aims to enhance the governance and compliance of DNS configurations within your organization. The primary objective is to automate the enforcement of DNS policies, ensuring that all configurations align with established organizational standards and best practices. This project will involve a thorough assessment of the current DNS setup, identifying gaps and areas for improvement. The consultant will develop a comprehensive policy framework that includes custom Azure Policies tailored to your specific needs. Key outcomes of this project include automated compliance checks, remediation actions, and the provision of training and documentation for your team. By the conclusion of this project, your organization will benefit from a robust system that not only maintains DNS compliance but also reduces operational risks and enhances overall efficiency.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current DNS Setup**: 
  - Review existing DNS configurations and practices.
  - Identify compliance gaps and areas for improvement.

- **Development of Policy Framework**: 
  - Create a comprehensive DNS policy framework aligned with best practices.
  - Develop custom Azure Policies tailored to organizational needs.

- **Automation of Compliance Checks**: 
  - Implement automated compliance checks to monitor DNS configurations.
  - Establish remediation actions for non-compliant configurations.

- **Training and Documentation**: 
  - Provide training sessions for your team on managing and monitoring Azure Policies.
  - Develop detailed documentation outlining policy management processes and best practices.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current DNS Setup Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing findings and recommendations.

- **Milestone 2**: Development of DNS Policy Framework
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive policy framework document.

- **Milestone 3**: Implementation of Custom Azure Policies
  - Deliverable: Custom Azure Policies deployed in the Azure environment.

- **Milestone 4**: Automation of Compliance Checks and Remediation Actions
  - Deliverable: Automated compliance checks and remediation processes established.

- **Milestone 5**: Training and Documentation Delivery
  - Deliverable: Training sessions conducted and documentation provided to the team.
