
# Video Contest Campaign Management Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Video Contest Campaign Management project aims to create an engaging and interactive platform that encourages users to showcase their creativity while promoting the {{brand_name}} organically. The primary objective is to develop a comprehensive contest strategy that includes defining contest rules, selecting themes, and establishing judging criteria. By creating promotional materials, we will generate excitement and encourage participation across various social media platforms, maximizing reach and engagement. Key outcomes of this project include enhanced brand visibility, increased user engagement, and the fostering of a sense of community among participants. The campaign will not only highlight the creativity of participants but also leverage their networks to amplify the {{brand_name}} message, ensuring a fair and transparent voting process while monitoring submissions and facilitating community interaction.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Contest Strategy Development**
  - Define contest rules and regulations
  - Select engaging themes for the contest
  - Establish clear judging criteria

- **Promotional Material Creation**
  - Design eye-catching graphics and videos
  - Develop engaging copy for social media posts
  - Create a dedicated landing page for contest details

- **Campaign Launch**
  - Schedule and execute the launch across selected social media platforms
  - Utilize organic reach strategies to promote the contest

- **Submission Monitoring**
  - Set up a system to track and manage submissions
  - Facilitate community interaction through comments and shares

- **Voting Process Management**
  - Implement a transparent voting system
  - Monitor voting to ensure fairness and integrity

- **Post-Campaign Analysis**
  - Collect data on participation and engagement
  - Analyze feedback and outcomes to assess campaign success
