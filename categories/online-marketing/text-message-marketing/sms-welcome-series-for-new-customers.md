
# Project Plan Template: SMS Welcome Series for New Customers

## 1. Executive Summary

The SMS Welcome Series for New Customers project aims to create a structured and engaging series of SMS messages designed to welcome new customers and guide them through the onboarding process. The primary objective of this project is to enhance customer engagement and satisfaction by providing timely and relevant information about the products or services purchased. The series will consist of a well-crafted welcome message followed by a sequence of informative follow-up messages that address common questions and encourage interaction. Additionally, a feedback request will be included to ensure customers feel valued and supported throughout their journey. Key outcomes of this project include improved customer retention rates, increased customer loyalty, and ultimately, a boost in sales. By implementing this SMS Welcome Series, businesses can create a positive first impression and foster a lasting relationship with their new customers.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Message Development**: 
  - Create a welcome message that introduces the brand and expresses gratitude for the customer's purchase.
  - Develop a series of 3-5 follow-up messages that provide essential information about the product/service, usage tips, and answers to frequently asked questions.
  - Design a feedback request message to gather insights on the customer experience and areas for improvement.

- **Target Audience Identification**: 
  - Define the target audience for the SMS Welcome Series, focusing on new customers who have made a recent purchase.

- **SMS Platform Selection**: 
  - Research and select an SMS marketing platform that aligns with the project requirements and budget.

- **Implementation Plan**: 
  - Create a timeline for the rollout of the SMS Welcome Series, including message scheduling and frequency.

- **Performance Metrics**: 
  - Establish key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure the success of the SMS Welcome Series, such as open rates, response rates, and customer feedback.
