
# Project Plan Template: WhatsApp Business Commerce Setup

## 1. Executive Summary

The WhatsApp Business Commerce Setup project aims to empower businesses to leverage WhatsApp as a dynamic social commerce platform. The primary objective is to establish a fully functional WhatsApp Business account tailored specifically for e-commerce, enabling businesses to effectively showcase their products and engage with customers. This project encompasses the configuration of a comprehensive business profile, integration of product catalogs, and implementation of automated messaging features to enhance customer interaction. Key outcomes include improved customer engagement, streamlined communication, and increased sales through direct messaging. By the conclusion of this project, clients will possess a robust WhatsApp Business account that not only highlights their offerings but also facilitates prompt and efficient customer interactions, ultimately driving business growth.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Account Setup**: 
  - Create and configure a WhatsApp Business account.
  - Verify the business phone number and complete the registration process.

- **Business Profile Configuration**: 
  - Set up a professional business profile including business name, description, address, and website link.
  - Upload a recognizable profile picture and cover image.

- **Product Catalog Integration**: 
  - Develop and integrate a product catalog showcasing the client's offerings.
  - Ensure product details, images, and pricing are accurately represented.

- **Automated Messaging Features**: 
  - Configure automated greeting messages for new customers.
  - Set up quick replies for frequently asked questions.
  - Implement away messages for after-hours inquiries.

- **Best Practices Guidance**: 
  - Provide training on effective customer engagement strategies via WhatsApp.
  - Share tips on managing customer inquiries and maintaining a professional tone.

- **Testing and Quality Assurance**: 
  - Conduct thorough testing of all features to ensure functionality.
  - Gather feedback from the client and make necessary adjustments.

- **Final Review and Handover**: 
  - Present the completed WhatsApp Business account to the client.
  - Provide documentation and resources for ongoing management.
