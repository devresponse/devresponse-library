- **Content Strategy Development**
  - Develop a content calendar tailored to {{chosen_platform}}.

- **Growth Hacks Implementation**
  - Identify and implement innovative growth hacks to enhance visibility.
  - Develop strategies for leveraging user-generated content and collaborations.
  - Create a plan for utilizing paid advertising effectively.

- **Performance Measurement**
  - Define key performance indicators (KPIs) for measuring success.
  - Establish a reporting framework to track progress and adjust strategies as needed.
  - Provide recommendations for ongoing optimization based on performance data.
