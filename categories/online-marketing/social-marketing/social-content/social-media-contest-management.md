
# Project Plan Template: Social Media Contest Management

## 1. Executive Summary

The Social Media Contest Management project aims to create an engaging and interactive contest that enhances brand visibility and boosts follower engagement across various social media platforms. The primary objective is to develop a unique contest concept that resonates with the brand's identity and marketing goals, ensuring that it captures the attention of the target audience. Key outcomes of this project include increased follower engagement, heightened brand awareness, and a measurable increase in website traffic. The project will encompass the establishment of clear contest rules to promote transparency, the creation of compelling promotional content, and the implementation of a robust strategy for monitoring entries and selecting winners. Ultimately, this initiative seeks to foster a sense of community among followers, encouraging them to actively participate and share their experiences, thereby strengthening their connection to the brand.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Concept Development**: Brainstorm and finalize a unique contest idea that aligns with the brand's identity and marketing objectives.
- **Contest Rules**: Draft clear and concise contest rules to ensure transparency and fairness for all participants.
- **Promotional Content Creation**: Design eye-catching graphics and write engaging copy for promotional materials to be shared across social media platforms.
- **Platform Selection**: Identify and select appropriate social media platforms for contest promotion based on target audience analysis.
- **Entry Monitoring**: Develop a strategy for tracking contest entries and ensuring compliance with contest rules.
- **Winner Selection**: Establish a fair and exciting process for selecting winners, including criteria and methods for announcement.
- **Post-Contest Engagement**: Plan follow-up activities to maintain engagement with participants and promote continued interaction with the brand.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Contest Concept Finalization
  - Deliverable: Approved contest concept document
- **Milestone 2**: Contest Rules Draft
  - Deliverable: Finalized contest rules document
- **Milestone 3**: Promotional Content Creation
  - Deliverable: Set of graphics and copy for social media posts
- **Milestone 4**: Contest Launch
  - Deliverable: Live contest on selected social media platforms
- **Milestone 5**: Entry Monitoring and Winner Selection
  - Deliverable: Report on entries and announcement of winners
- **Milestone 6**: Post-Contest Engagement Strategy
  - Deliverable: Plan for follow-up engagement activities
