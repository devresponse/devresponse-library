
# Project Plan Template: Social Media Ad Compliance Review

## 1. Executive Summary

The Social Media Ad Compliance Review project is designed to ensure that social media advertisements meet the stringent policies and guidelines established by platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, and LinkedIn. The primary objective of this project is to conduct a thorough evaluation of ad content, visuals, and targeting parameters to identify any compliance issues that could lead to penalties, ad rejections, or account suspensions. By meticulously analyzing each advertisement, we aim to provide actionable recommendations that will enhance adherence to platform standards. Key outcomes of this project include a comprehensive report detailing compliance findings, suggested adjustments for optimization, and strategies to safeguard the client's brand reputation. Ultimately, this review will maximize advertising effectiveness and ensure that the client's campaigns are not only compliant but also strategically positioned for success in a competitive digital landscape.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Ad Content Review**: Analyze the textual content of advertisements for compliance with platform-specific guidelines.
- **Visuals Assessment**: Evaluate images and videos used in ads to ensure they meet the required standards and do not violate any policies.
- **Targeting Parameters Analysis**: Review audience targeting settings to confirm they align with platform rules and best practices.
- **Compliance Issues Identification**: Document any potential compliance issues found during the review process.
- **Recommendations Development**: Provide actionable recommendations for adjustments to enhance compliance and effectiveness.
- **Final Report Compilation**: Create a detailed report summarizing findings, compliance issues, and suggested improvements.
- **Client Presentation**: Present the final report to the client, discussing findings and recommendations.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Kick-off Meeting**: Schedule and conduct a project kick-off meeting with stakeholders. 
  - **Deliverable**: Meeting notes and project timeline.
  
- **Ad Review Phase**: Complete the review of all advertisements.
  - **Deliverable**: Draft report of compliance findings.

- **Recommendations Phase**: Develop and finalize recommendations based on the review.
  - **Deliverable**: Recommendations document.

- **Final Report Preparation**: Compile the final report including all findings and recommendations.
  - **Deliverable**: Final compliance review report.

- **Client Presentation**: Present the final report to the client and gather feedback.
  - **Deliverable**: Presentation slides and feedback summary.
