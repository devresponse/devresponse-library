
# Project Plan Template: Custom Press Release Pitch for Product Launch

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of this project is to create a custom press release pitch that effectively communicates the unique features and benefits of {{Product Name}} during its launch. In today's competitive market, a well-crafted press release is not just a formality; it is a strategic tool that can significantly influence public perception and media coverage. This project will begin with an in-depth analysis of {{Product Name}}, including its market positioning and key messages that need to be conveyed. We will conduct comprehensive research to identify the most relevant media outlets and journalists who align with our target audience. The key outcomes of this project include a compelling press release pitch that captures attention, encourages media coverage, and ultimately drives awareness and interest in {{Product Name}}. By ensuring that the right story reaches the right audience, we aim to maximize the impact of the product launch and establish a strong foothold in the market.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with stakeholders to gather information about {{Product Name}}, its unique selling points, and the desired messaging.
- **Market Research**: Conduct research to identify target audiences, relevant media outlets, and key journalists who cover similar products.
- **Content Development**: Draft the press release pitch, ensuring it highlights the unique features, benefits, and market positioning of {{Product Name}}.
- **Review and Revisions**: Present the draft to stakeholders for feedback and make necessary revisions to ensure alignment with the brand’s voice and objectives.
- **Finalization**: Prepare the final version of the press release pitch, including any additional materials or media kits if necessary.
- **Distribution Strategy**: Develop a strategy for distributing the press release to the identified media outlets and journalists.
- **Follow-Up Plan**: Create a follow-up plan to engage with media contacts post-distribution and track coverage.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary report of the consultation and key insights gathered.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Market Research Completed
  - Deliverable: List of identified media outlets and journalists with contact information.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Draft Press Release Pitch Completed
  - Deliverable: First draft of the press release pitch for review.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Review and Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised press release pitch based on stakeholder feedback.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Press Release Pitch Delivered
  - Deliverable: Finalized press release pitch ready for distribution.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Distribution Strategy Developed
  - Deliverable: Document outlining the distribution plan and follow-up strategy.
