
# Project Plan Template: Award Nomination Press Release Pitch

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Award Nomination Press Release Pitch" project aims to craft a compelling press release that announces our brand's nomination or win for a prestigious industry award. The primary objective is to highlight our brand's key achievements and innovative contributions, while emphasizing the significance of the award within our industry. This project will involve thorough research on the award's background, selection criteria, and the competitive landscape to ensure that our narrative is both informative and engaging. By targeting relevant media outlets, we aim to enhance our brand's visibility and credibility, ultimately reaching a wider audience of potential customers and stakeholders. The key outcomes of this project include a polished press release ready for distribution and a strategic pitching plan designed to maximize media coverage. This initiative will not only celebrate our accomplishments but also position our brand favorably in the eyes of the media and the public.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct in-depth research on the award, including its history, selection criteria, and previous winners.
  - Analyze the competitive landscape to identify key differentiators for our brand.
  
- **Content Development**:
  - Draft the press release, ensuring it captures the essence of our brand's achievements and the significance of the award.
  - Incorporate quotes from key stakeholders, such as executives or industry experts, to add credibility and depth to the narrative.
  
- **Media Targeting**:
  - Identify and compile a list of relevant media outlets, journalists, and influencers who cover industry awards and related topics.
  - Develop a media outreach strategy tailored to each outlet to maximize the chances of coverage.
  
- **Review and Finalization**:
  - Conduct internal reviews and revisions of the press release to ensure clarity, accuracy, and impact.
  - Finalize the press release and prepare it for distribution.
  
- **Pitching Plan**:
  - Create a strategic pitching plan that outlines the timing, methods, and key messages for reaching out to media contacts.
  - Prepare follow-up strategies to engage with journalists post-distribution.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings on the award and competitive landscape.

- **Milestone 2**: Drafting of Press Release
  - Deliverable: First draft of the press release for internal review.

- **Milestone 3**: Finalization of Press Release
  - Deliverable: Polished press release ready for distribution.

- **Milestone 4**: Media Targeting and Outreach Strategy
  - Deliverable: List of targeted media outlets and a detailed outreach strategy.

- **Milestone 5**: Execution of Pitching Plan
  - Deliverable: Completed media outreach and follow-up report.
