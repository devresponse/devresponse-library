
# Project Plan Template: Tech Product Launch Press Release Pitch

## 1. Executive Summary

The Tech Product Launch Press Release Pitch project aims to strategically craft and pitch a compelling press release for the launch of {{Product Name}}, a new tech product designed to revolutionize {{specific industry or market segment}}. The primary objective is to create a narrative that effectively highlights the unique features and benefits of {{Product Name}}, ensuring it resonates with our target audience of tech enthusiasts, industry professionals, and potential customers. By tailoring the press release specifically for technology-focused media outlets and journalists, we aim to maximize the likelihood of media coverage and engagement. 

Key outcomes of this project include a polished press release that captures the essence of {{Product Name}}, a comprehensive list of targeted media contacts, and a summary of the outreach efforts undertaken. This project is essential for tech companies looking to make a significant impact in the market, attract attention from key industry players, and ultimately drive product adoption and sales. Through thorough research and a strategic outreach plan, we will position {{Product Name}} as a must-have solution in the tech landscape.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**
  - Identify and compile a list of relevant technology-focused media outlets and journalists.
  - Analyze recent trends and news in the tech industry to inform the press release narrative.
  
- **Press Release Development**
  - Draft a compelling press release that includes:
    - A catchy headline
    - An engaging lead paragraph
    - Detailed product features and benefits
    - Quotes from key stakeholders (e.g., product developers, executives)
    - A strong call to action
  - Review and revise the press release based on feedback from stakeholders.

- **Outreach Strategy**
  - Develop a targeted outreach strategy that includes:
    - Personalized email pitches to selected media contacts
    - Follow-up communication plan
    - Tracking and documenting outreach efforts
  
- **Final Deliverables**
  - A polished press release ready for distribution.
  - A comprehensive list of targeted media contacts.
  - A summary report of outreach efforts, including responses and media coverage achieved.
