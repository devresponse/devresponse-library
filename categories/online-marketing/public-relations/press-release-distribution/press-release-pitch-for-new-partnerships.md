
# Project Plan Template: Press Release Pitch for New Partnerships

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Press Release Pitch for New Partnerships" project aims to craft a compelling narrative that announces a strategic partnership between {{Organization A}} and {{Organization B}}. The primary objective is to highlight the innovative aspects of this collaboration and its potential impact on the industry, thereby maximizing media exposure and enhancing brand visibility for both organizations. This project will involve thorough research to identify relevant media contacts and develop key messaging points that resonate with journalists specializing in business and industry news. The expected outcome is a polished press release draft that captures the attention of the target audience, encouraging coverage and engagement from media outlets. By effectively communicating the benefits of the partnership, this project seeks to position both organizations as leaders in their respective fields and foster a positive public perception.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**:
  - Identify and compile a list of relevant media contacts and outlets that focus on business and industry news.
  - Analyze previous press releases and media coverage related to similar partnerships to identify best practices and effective messaging strategies.

- **Messaging Development**:
  - Collaborate with stakeholders from both organizations to gather insights on the partnership's benefits and innovative aspects.
  - Develop key messaging points that clearly articulate the value proposition of the partnership.

- **Drafting the Press Release**:
  - Create a structured outline for the press release, ensuring it includes all essential elements such as a catchy headline, subhead, body content, quotes from key stakeholders, and a boilerplate for both organizations.
  - Write and refine the press release draft, incorporating feedback from stakeholders to ensure clarity and impact.

- **Final Review and Approval**:
  - Conduct a final review of the press release for accuracy, tone, and adherence to brand guidelines.
  - Obtain necessary approvals from both organizations before distribution.
