
# Project Plan Template: Cultural Sensitivity PR Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

The Cultural Sensitivity PR Audit aims to enhance the inclusivity and cultural awareness of your company's public relations materials. In today's diverse and interconnected world, it is essential for businesses to communicate effectively and respectfully with a broad audience. This project will involve a thorough examination of your existing public relations assets, including press releases, marketing collateral, and social media content. Our expert consultants will assess these materials for cultural appropriateness, identifying any potential biases or insensitivities that could alienate target audiences. The key outcomes of this audit will include a detailed report outlining our findings, along with actionable recommendations for improving cultural sensitivity in your communications. By implementing these recommendations, your brand will not only resonate with a wider audience but also enhance public perception, ultimately driving engagement and loyalty. This project is designed to position your company as a leader in cultural sensitivity within the industry.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a kickoff meeting to understand the specific needs and objectives of the audit. Gather relevant materials and establish communication channels.
  
- **Material Collection**: Compile all public relations materials, including:
  - Press releases
  - Marketing collateral
  - Social media content
  - Any other relevant communication materials

- **Cultural Sensitivity Assessment**: 
  - Review each material for cultural appropriateness.
  - Identify potential biases, stereotypes, or insensitivities.
  - Evaluate the inclusivity of language and imagery used.

- **Stakeholder Interviews**: Conduct interviews with key stakeholders to gather insights on current perceptions and expectations regarding cultural sensitivity.

- **Reporting**: 
  - Prepare a comprehensive report detailing findings from the audit.
  - Include specific examples of identified issues and areas for improvement.
  - Provide actionable recommendations for enhancing cultural sensitivity in future communications.

- **Presentation of Findings**: Schedule a meeting to present the findings and recommendations to the relevant stakeholders.

- **Follow-Up Support**: Offer a follow-up consultation to assist in the implementation of recommendations and address any questions.
