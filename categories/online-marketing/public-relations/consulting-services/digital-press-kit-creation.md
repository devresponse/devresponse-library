
# Project Plan Template: Digital Press Kit Creation

## 1. Executive Summary

The Digital Press Kit Creation project aims to develop a comprehensive digital press kit that effectively communicates the brand story of {{Client Name}}. In today's competitive landscape, a well-crafted press kit is essential for capturing the attention of media outlets and influencers. The project will involve a thorough consultation phase to understand the unique aspects of {{Client Name}}'s business, followed by the creation of engaging content that includes a company overview, high-resolution images, press releases, and relevant media coverage. Key outcomes of this project include a polished digital press kit that not only informs but also inspires journalists to share {{Client Name}}'s narrative. By enhancing visibility and engagement in the market, this digital press kit will serve as a vital tool for media outreach and brand promotion.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct initial meetings with {{Client Name}} to gather information about the brand, target audience, and key messages.
  - Identify unique selling points and brand values to be highlighted in the press kit.

- **Content Development**:
  - Create an engaging company overview that encapsulates the brand's mission, vision, and values.
  - Develop high-quality press releases tailored to current news and events related to {{Client Name}}.
  - Curate relevant media coverage and testimonials to enhance credibility.

- **Visual Design**:
  - Design visually appealing layouts for the digital press kit that align with {{Client Name}}'s brand identity.
  - Incorporate high-resolution images and graphics that complement the written content.

- **Final Review and Revisions**:
  - Present the draft digital press kit to {{Client Name}} for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on client input to ensure satisfaction with the final product.

- **Delivery**:
  - Provide the final digital press kit in a user-friendly format suitable for distribution to media contacts.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Consultation Phase
  - Deliverable: Summary report of consultation findings and agreed-upon key messages.

- **Milestone 2**: Draft Content Development
  - Deliverable: Draft versions of the company overview, press releases, and media coverage.

- **Milestone 3**: Visual Design Completion
  - Deliverable: Initial design mockups of the digital press kit.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Review and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised digital press kit based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Polished digital press kit ready for distribution.
