
# Project Plan Template: Ad Compliance Checker

## 1. Executive Summary

The Ad Compliance Checker project aims to develop a comprehensive tool designed to ensure that advertisements comply with platform policies and industry regulations. The primary objective is to minimize the risk of ad disapproval by identifying potential compliance issues before ads are deployed. This tool will leverage advanced algorithms and machine learning techniques to analyze various elements of advertisements, including text, images, and keywords, ensuring they align with the guidelines set forth by advertising platforms. Key outcomes of the project include enhanced efficiency of ad campaigns, improved return on investment for businesses, and a significant reduction in the time and resources spent on ad revisions. By implementing thorough testing and validation phases, we will ensure the tool's reliability and effectiveness in real-world scenarios, ultimately empowering businesses to optimize their advertising strategies and achieve better results.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Requirements Gathering**: 
  - Conduct stakeholder interviews to identify specific compliance requirements.
  - Document the necessary platform policies and industry regulations.

- **Tool Development**:
  - Design and develop the Ad Compliance Checker tool using advanced algorithms and machine learning techniques.
  - Implement features for analyzing ad content, including text, images, and keywords.

- **Testing and Validation**:
  - Conduct unit testing to ensure individual components function correctly.
  - Perform integration testing to verify that all components work together seamlessly.
  - Execute user acceptance testing (UAT) with stakeholders to validate the tool against real-world scenarios.

- **Deployment**:
  - Prepare the tool for deployment, including necessary documentation and user training materials.
  - Launch the Ad Compliance Checker tool for use by the target audience.

- **Post-Deployment Support**:
  - Provide ongoing support and maintenance for the tool.
  - Gather user feedback for future enhancements and updates.
