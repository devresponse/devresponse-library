
# Project Plan Template: Competitor Analysis for Shopping Ads

## 1. Executive Summary

The primary objective of the Competitor Analysis for Shopping Ads project is to conduct a thorough examination of key competitors within the online advertising landscape, specifically focusing on their Shopping Ads campaigns. This project aims to gather and analyze critical data regarding competitors' product visibility strategies, pricing models, and ad placements. By reviewing various aspects such as ad formats, keywords, and audience targeting techniques, we will identify successful trends and strategies that can be leveraged to enhance your own Shopping Ads campaigns. The key outcomes of this project will include a comprehensive report that features visual representations of the gathered data, key findings, and strategic recommendations tailored to optimize your advertising efforts. Ultimately, the insights derived from this analysis will empower your team to make informed decisions that could lead to increased product sales and improved overall campaign performance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Competitor Identification**: Identify key competitors in the online advertising space relevant to Shopping Ads.
- **Data Collection**: Gather data on competitors' Shopping Ads campaigns, including:
  - Product visibility strategies
  - Pricing models
  - Ad placements
  - Ad formats
  - Keywords used
  - Audience targeting techniques
- **Data Analysis**: Analyze the collected data to identify trends, successful strategies, and areas for improvement.
- **Visual Representation**: Create visual representations (charts, graphs, etc.) to illustrate key findings and insights.
- **Report Compilation**: Compile a comprehensive report that includes:
  - Executive summary of findings
  - Detailed analysis of competitors
  - Strategic recommendations for optimizing Shopping Ads campaigns
- **Presentation of Findings**: Present the findings and recommendations to stakeholders, ensuring clarity and actionable insights.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of competitor identification

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of data analysis

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of visual representations
  - **Deliverable**: Visual aids to support findings
- **Milestone 5**: Final report compilation
  - **Deliverable**: Comprehensive report with findings and recommendations
- **Milestone 6**: Presentation of findings
  - **Deliverable**: Presentation to stakeholders
