
# Project Plan Template: Shopping Ads Performance Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

The Shopping Ads Performance Audit project is designed to provide a comprehensive evaluation of your current Shopping Ads campaigns. The primary objective is to analyze critical components such as bid strategies, targeting parameters, and ad copy effectiveness to identify strengths and weaknesses in your existing approach. By conducting this audit, we aim to deliver data-driven recommendations that will enhance campaign performance, optimize visibility, and ultimately increase sales. The project will culminate in a detailed report that outlines key findings, actionable insights, and strategic recommendations tailored to your specific business goals. Our commitment is to ensure that your advertising budget is utilized effectively, leading to improved return on investment (ROI) and overall campaign success. This fixed project will maintain transparency and accountability throughout the process, ensuring that all stakeholders are informed and aligned with the project's objectives.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a kickoff meeting to understand the client's current Shopping Ads setup, goals, and expectations.
  
- **Data Collection**: Gather relevant data from existing Shopping Ads campaigns, including performance metrics, bid strategies, targeting parameters, and ad copy.

- **Campaign Analysis**: Perform a thorough analysis of the collected data to identify trends, strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.

- **Competitor Benchmarking**: Analyze competitor Shopping Ads strategies to identify industry best practices and potential opportunities.

- **Reporting**: Develop a comprehensive report that includes:
  - Key findings from the analysis
  - Actionable insights
  - Strategic recommendations tailored to the client's business goals

- **Presentation**: Present the findings and recommendations to the client, allowing for discussion and feedback.

- **Follow-up Support**: Provide a follow-up consultation to address any questions and assist with the implementation of recommendations.
