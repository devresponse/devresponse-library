
# Project Plan: Google My Business Custom Attributes Setup

## 1. Executive Summary

The Google My Business Custom Attributes Setup project aims to enhance the visibility and engagement of local businesses through the strategic optimization of their Google My Business (GMB) profiles. In today's competitive market, a well-optimized GMB profile is crucial for attracting potential customers. This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of the current GMB profile to identify and implement custom attributes that resonate with the target audience. Key objectives include highlighting specific features such as accessibility options, payment methods, and unique services offered by the business. By optimizing these attributes, we aim to improve the business's local search rankings, making it easier for customers to discover and choose the business over competitors. The expected outcomes include increased customer engagement, improved visibility in search results, and a GMB profile that effectively communicates the business's unique offerings. Ultimately, this project will ensure that the GMB profile not only meets customer needs but also stands out in a crowded marketplace.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with stakeholders to understand business goals and target audience.
- **Current Profile Analysis**: Review the existing GMB profile to assess current attributes and performance metrics.
- **Attribute Identification**: Identify relevant custom attributes that align with the business's offerings and customer preferences.
- **Implementation Plan**: Develop a step-by-step plan for adding and optimizing custom attributes within the GMB profile.
- **Execution**: Implement the identified custom attributes into the GMB profile.
- **Testing and Validation**: Verify that the custom attributes are correctly displayed and functioning as intended.
- **Training Session**: Provide training for staff on how to manage and update the GMB profile moving forward.
- **Performance Monitoring**: Establish metrics for monitoring the impact of the custom attributes on customer engagement and search visibility.
