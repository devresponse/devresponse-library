
# Project Plan: Google My Business Keyword Research

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Google My Business Keyword Research project is to conduct a thorough analysis of local search terms that potential customers are using to find services similar to those offered by {{Your Business Name}}. This project aims to identify high-impact keywords that will enhance the visibility of your Google My Business profile, thereby improving search rankings and increasing customer engagement. The project will involve a detailed examination of search volume, competition, and relevance to ensure that the selected keywords align with your business goals. Key outcomes will include a comprehensive report detailing the keyword research findings, along with actionable recommendations for incorporating these keywords into your Google My Business profile. By strategically optimizing your profile with the identified keywords, we aim to drive more traffic to your website or physical location, ultimately leading to increased customer acquisition and retention.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Keyword Research**: Conduct extensive research to identify relevant local search terms that potential customers are using.
- **Analysis**: Evaluate the search volume, competition, and relevance of identified keywords.
- **Reporting**: Compile findings into a detailed report that includes:
  - List of high-impact keywords
  - Analysis of search volume and competition
  - Recommendations for keyword integration into Google My Business profile
- **Implementation Guidance**: Provide step-by-step instructions on how to incorporate the identified keywords into the Google My Business profile effectively.
- **Review and Feedback**: Schedule a review session to discuss the findings and recommendations with the client.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of initial keyword research
  - Deliverable: Preliminary list of potential keywords
- **Milestone 2**: Analysis of keywords for search volume and competition
  - Deliverable: Detailed analysis report
- **Milestone 3**: Final report preparation
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive keyword research report with recommendations
- **Milestone 4**: Review session with client
  - Deliverable: Feedback and adjustments based on client input
