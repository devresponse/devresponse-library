
# Project Plan Template: Google My Business Competitor Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary

The Google My Business Competitor Analysis project aims to provide a comprehensive evaluation of competitors' Google My Business profiles to identify their strengths and weaknesses. In today's competitive market, understanding how your business compares to others is essential for success. This project will focus on key elements such as business descriptions, customer reviews, photos, and engagement metrics. By conducting this analysis, we will uncover opportunities for improvement that can enhance your local SEO strategy. Additionally, we will compare service offerings, pricing strategies, and promotional tactics employed by competitors. The ultimate goal is to deliver actionable insights that will optimize your Google Business Profile, drive more traffic to your business, and increase customer engagement. The project will culminate in a detailed report outlining findings and tailored recommendations to meet your specific business needs.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Competitor Identification**: Identify top competitors within the local market based on industry relevance and customer demographics.
- **Profile Evaluation**: Analyze competitors' Google My Business profiles, focusing on:
  - Business descriptions
  - Customer reviews and ratings
  - Photos and visual content
  - Engagement metrics (e.g., Q&A, posts)
- **Strengths and Weaknesses Analysis**: Assess the strengths and weaknesses of each competitor's profile.
- **Service Offerings Comparison**: Compare the range of services offered by competitors.
- **Pricing Strategy Analysis**: Evaluate pricing strategies used by competitors.
- **Promotional Tactics Review**: Analyze promotional tactics and marketing strategies employed by competitors.
- **Actionable Insights Development**: Develop actionable insights and recommendations for optimizing your Google Business Profile.
- **Final Report Preparation**: Compile findings and recommendations into a comprehensive report tailored to your business needs.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Competitor Identification Completed
  - Deliverable: List of identified competitors
- **Milestone 2**: Profile Evaluation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of profile evaluations for each competitor
- **Milestone 3**: Strengths and Weaknesses Analysis Completed
  - Deliverable: SWOT analysis for each competitor
- **Milestone 4**: Service Offerings and Pricing Strategy Analysis Completed
  - Deliverable: Comparative analysis report
- **Milestone 5**: Promotional Tactics Review Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of promotional tactics used by competitors
- **Milestone 6**: Actionable Insights and Recommendations Developed
  - Deliverable: List of actionable insights
- **Milestone 7**: Final Report Prepared and Delivered
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report with findings and tailored recommendations
