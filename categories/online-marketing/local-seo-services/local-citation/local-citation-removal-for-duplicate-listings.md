
# Project Plan Template: Local Citation Removal for Duplicate Listings

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Local Citation Removal for Duplicate Listings" project aims to enhance local SEO performance by identifying and eliminating duplicate or outdated business listings across various online directories. In today's competitive digital landscape, accurate and consistent business information is crucial for improving visibility in local search results. This project will begin with a comprehensive audit of existing citations on platforms such as Google My Business, Yelp, and other relevant directories. Our primary objective is to pinpoint duplicates and outdated entries that may confuse both search engines and potential customers. By systematically removing or updating these citations, we will ensure that your business information is accurate and consistent across all platforms. Key outcomes of this project include a cleaner citation profile, improved local search rankings, and increased customer engagement. Ultimately, this initiative will contribute to a stronger online presence and drive more traffic to your business.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Audit Existing Citations**: Conduct a thorough review of all current business listings across major platforms, including but not limited to Google My Business, Yelp, Yellow Pages, and local directories.
  
- **Identify Duplicates and Outdated Listings**: Utilize specialized tools and manual checks to identify duplicate entries and outdated information that may mislead customers and search engines.
  
- **Develop a Removal and Update Strategy**: Create a detailed plan for removing duplicates and updating outdated listings, ensuring compliance with each platform's guidelines.
  
- **Execute Removal and Updates**: Implement the removal and update strategy, including contacting directory support teams where necessary to facilitate the removal of duplicates.
  
- **Verification and Quality Assurance**: After updates are made, conduct a follow-up audit to verify that all changes have been successfully implemented and that the citations are now accurate.
  
- **Reporting**: Provide a comprehensive report detailing the actions taken, including a summary of removed duplicates, updated listings, and the overall impact on local search visibility.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the initial audit of existing citations.
  - **Deliverable**: Audit report detailing current citations and identified duplicates.

- **Milestone 2**: Development of the removal and update strategy.
  - **Deliverable**: Strategy document outlining the approach for removing duplicates and updating listings.

- **Milestone 3**: Execution of the removal and update strategy.
  - **Deliverable**: Confirmation of completed actions, including any correspondence with directory support teams.

- **Milestone 4**: Verification and quality assurance.
  - **Deliverable**: Verification report confirming the accuracy of updated citations.

- **Milestone 5**: Final project report.
  - **Deliverable**: Comprehensive report summarizing the project outcomes and impact on local search visibility.
