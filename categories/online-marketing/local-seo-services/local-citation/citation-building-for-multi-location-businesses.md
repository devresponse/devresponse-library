
# Project Plan Template: Citation Building for Multi-Location Businesses

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Citation Building for Multi-Location Businesses" project aims to enhance the online visibility of businesses with multiple locations through the creation and optimization of local citations. In today's competitive market, having accurate and consistent information across various online platforms is crucial for attracting potential customers. This project will involve a comprehensive approach to researching relevant local directories, submitting business details, and verifying listings for each location. Key outcomes include improved search rankings, increased website traffic, and a stronger online presence for each business location. By the end of this project, we will ensure that all citations are regularly monitored and updated, providing a robust online representation that drives customer engagement and growth.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: Identify relevant local directories and citation sources for each business location.
- **Data Collection**: Gather accurate business information, including name, address, phone number (NAP), website URL, and business description for each location.
- **Submission Process**: Submit business details to identified directories, ensuring compliance with each platform's guidelines.
- **Verification**: Confirm the accuracy of listings through verification processes, including email confirmations and phone verifications where applicable.
- **Monitoring and Updates**: Establish a schedule for regular monitoring of citations to ensure ongoing accuracy and make updates as necessary.
- **Reporting**: Provide regular updates and reports on citation status, including newly created listings, updates made, and any discrepancies found.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the research phase.
  - **Deliverable**: List of identified local directories and citation sources.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Data collection for all business locations.
  - **Deliverable**: Compiled document containing NAP and other relevant business information.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Submission of business details to local directories.
  - **Deliverable**: Confirmation of submissions and links to live listings.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Verification of listings.
  - **Deliverable**: Verification report detailing confirmed listings and any issues encountered.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Ongoing monitoring and updates.
  - **Deliverable**: Monthly reports on citation accuracy and updates made.
