
# Local Citation Cleanup Service Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Local Citation Cleanup Service aims to enhance your business's online presence by addressing and rectifying inaccuracies in your business listings across various online directories. In today's digital landscape, having consistent and accurate information is crucial for local SEO rankings, as discrepancies can hinder potential customers from finding your business. This project will involve a comprehensive audit of your existing citations, identifying inconsistencies in critical details such as business name, address, and phone number. Our dedicated team will systematically correct these discrepancies across all relevant platforms, ensuring that your business information is accurate and uniform. By the end of this project, you will have a clean and optimized set of local citations, which will not only improve your search engine visibility but also foster trust with your customers. This service is essential for businesses looking to solidify their local footprint and attract more clientele, ultimately leading to increased sales and customer loyalty.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to understand their business, goals, and current citation status.
- **Comprehensive Citation Audit**: 
  - Gather existing citations from various online directories.
  - Identify discrepancies in business name, address, phone number, and other critical details.
- **Correction Plan Development**: 
  - Create a detailed plan for correcting identified discrepancies.
  - Prioritize citations based on their impact on local SEO.
- **Implementation of Corrections**: 
  - Systematically update and correct business information across all relevant platforms.
  - Ensure consistency across all directories.
- **Verification and Quality Assurance**: 
  - Conduct a follow-up audit to verify that all corrections have been made.
  - Ensure that all listings are live and accurate.
- **Final Reporting**: 
  - Provide a comprehensive report detailing the changes made and the current status of citations.
  - Include recommendations for ongoing citation management.
