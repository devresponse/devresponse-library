
# Project Plan Template: Customer Journey Mapping Workshop

## 1. Executive Summary
The Customer Journey Mapping Workshop aims to provide participants with a comprehensive understanding of the customer experience across various touchpoints. The primary objective of this workshop is to facilitate a collaborative environment where teams can visualize the customer journey, identify critical pain points, and explore opportunities for enhancement. Through guided discussions and hands-on exercises, participants will engage with mapping tools to ensure that all aspects of the customer experience are thoroughly considered. Key outcomes include the creation of a clear, actionable map of the customer journey and tailored strategies designed to improve customer satisfaction and retention. By the end of the workshop, participants will not only gain insights into customer interactions but also be equipped with the knowledge necessary to drive brand loyalty and foster business growth.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work
- **Workshop Preparation**
  - Define objectives and outcomes with stakeholders.
  - Develop workshop materials, including presentations, handouts, and mapping tools.
  - Identify and invite participants from relevant departments (e.g., marketing, sales, customer service).

- **Workshop Execution**
  - Conduct an introductory session to outline the purpose and structure of the workshop.
  - Facilitate collaborative activities to visualize the customer journey across various touchpoints.
  - Guide participants in identifying critical pain points and opportunities for enhancement.
  - Utilize mapping tools to create a visual representation of the customer journey.
  - Encourage discussions to foster a deeper understanding of customer interactions.

- **Post-Workshop Activities**
  - Compile workshop findings and insights into a comprehensive report.
  - Develop tailored strategies based on the identified opportunities for improvement.
  - Schedule a follow-up meeting to discuss the implementation of strategies and gather feedback.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1:** Workshop Preparation Completed
  - Deliverable: Finalized workshop materials and participant list.

- **Milestone 2:** Workshop Conducted
  - Deliverable: Completed customer journey maps and documented insights.

- **Milestone 3:** Post-Workshop Report Delivered
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report detailing findings, strategies, and next steps.
