
# Project Plan Template: ABM Account Scoring Model Development

## 1. Executive Summary

The ABM Account Scoring Model Development project aims to create a robust scoring model that will enable businesses to effectively rank and prioritize their target accounts. The primary objective of this project is to develop a comprehensive framework that utilizes various data points, including firmographics, behavioral insights, and historical engagement metrics, to assess the potential value of each account and their likelihood to convert. By implementing this scoring model, businesses will be able to focus their marketing efforts on high-potential accounts, ensuring that resources are allocated efficiently. Key outcomes of this project include a thorough analysis of existing data, the establishment of scoring criteria tailored to the specific needs of the business, and the creation of a user-friendly dashboard for ongoing account evaluation. Ultimately, the final deliverable will empower marketing teams to make informed decisions, enhance their account-based marketing strategies, and drive higher conversion rates.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Collection and Analysis**
  - Gather existing data on target accounts, including firmographics, behavioral insights, and historical engagement metrics.
  - Conduct a thorough analysis of the collected data to identify trends and patterns.

- **Development of Scoring Criteria**
  - Collaborate with stakeholders to define scoring criteria based on the analysis.
  - Establish a scoring framework that quantifies the potential value and likelihood to convert for each account.

- **Dashboard Creation**
  - Design and develop a user-friendly dashboard that visualizes the scoring model.
  - Ensure the dashboard allows for easy updates and ongoing evaluation of accounts.

- **Testing and Validation**
  - Test the scoring model with a subset of accounts to validate its effectiveness.
  - Gather feedback from marketing teams and make necessary adjustments.

- **Training and Documentation**
  - Provide training sessions for marketing teams on how to use the scoring model and dashboard.
  - Create comprehensive documentation outlining the scoring criteria, model usage, and best practices.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Data Collection Completed**
  - Deliverable: Compiled dataset of target accounts.

- **Milestone 2: Data Analysis Report**
  - Deliverable: Report detailing insights and trends from the data analysis.

- **Milestone 3: Scoring Criteria Established**
  - Deliverable: Document outlining the scoring criteria and framework.

- **Milestone 4: Dashboard Developed**
  - Deliverable: User-friendly dashboard for account scoring.

- **Milestone 5: Testing and Validation Completed**
  - Deliverable: Validation report and feedback summary.

- **Milestone 6: Training and Documentation Delivered**
  - Deliverable: Training materials and comprehensive documentation.
