
# Project Plan: Affiliate Onboarding Process Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Affiliate Onboarding Process Design project aims to create a comprehensive and user-friendly onboarding experience for new affiliates. The primary objective is to develop an onboarding strategy that clearly outlines the necessary steps for affiliates to understand their roles, access essential resources, and effectively promote our brand. This project will involve a thorough analysis of current onboarding practices to identify gaps and propose enhancements that streamline the onboarding journey. Key outcomes will include the creation of instructional materials, a welcome kit, and an automated onboarding workflow that ensures timely communication and support. By focusing on clarity and engagement, this project will empower new affiliates to become productive partners in our affiliate marketing program, ultimately driving better results for our business. The successful implementation of this onboarding process will not only enhance affiliate satisfaction but also improve overall program performance and ROI.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Current State Analysis**: Review existing onboarding practices and materials to identify strengths and weaknesses.
- **Gap Analysis**: Conduct interviews and surveys with current affiliates to gather feedback on their onboarding experience.
- **Onboarding Strategy Development**: Create a detailed onboarding strategy that includes:
  - Step-by-step onboarding process
  - Roles and responsibilities of affiliates
  - Key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure onboarding success
- **Instructional Materials Creation**: Develop user-friendly instructional materials, including:
  - Training videos
  - Written guides
  - FAQs
- **Welcome Kit Design**: Create a welcome kit that includes:
  - Branded merchandise
  - Essential resources and tools
  - Contact information for support
- **Automated Onboarding Workflow**: Design and implement an automated workflow that includes:
  - Email sequences for onboarding communication
  - Timely reminders for key tasks
  - Support channels for affiliates
- **Testing and Feedback**: Pilot the onboarding process with a select group of new affiliates and gather feedback for improvements.
- **Final Implementation**: Roll out the refined onboarding process to all new affiliates.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current State Analysis
  - Deliverable: Analysis report outlining strengths and weaknesses of current onboarding practices.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Gap Analysis Completion
  - Deliverable: Summary of affiliate feedback and identified gaps in the onboarding process.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Onboarding Strategy Document
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive onboarding strategy document including process steps and KPIs.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Instructional Materials and Welcome Kit
  - Deliverable: Complete set of instructional materials and a finalized welcome kit.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Automated Onboarding Workflow Implementation
  - Deliverable: Fully functional automated onboarding workflow with email sequences and reminders.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Pilot Testing and Feedback Collection
  - Deliverable: Feedback report from pilot testing with recommendations for adjustments.
  
- **Milestone 7**: Final Implementation
  - Deliverable: Official launch of the new onboarding process for all new affiliates.
