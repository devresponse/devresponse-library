
# Project Plan Template: The Basics of Programmatic Advertising

## 1. Executive Summary

The project "The Basics of Programmatic Advertising" aims to provide a comprehensive introductory lesson on the essential elements of programmatic advertising, a pivotal component of contemporary digital marketing. The primary objective is to equip participants with a thorough understanding of automated ad buying processes, including the various targeting options that enable marketers to effectively reach their desired audiences. This project will cover the mechanics of real-time bidding, demonstrating how advertisers can compete for ad space in mere milliseconds. Additionally, we will explore optimization strategies that enhance campaign performance and yield better results. Designed for both novices seeking to learn the fundamentals and experienced marketers looking to refine their skills, this lesson will culminate in actionable insights and practical knowledge. By the end of the project, participants will be empowered to implement programmatic strategies effectively in their marketing efforts, ultimately driving improved outcomes for their campaigns.
