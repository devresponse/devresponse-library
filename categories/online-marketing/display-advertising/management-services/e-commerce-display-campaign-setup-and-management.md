
# E-Commerce Display Campaign Setup Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The E-Commerce Display Campaign Setup project is designed to empower e-commerce businesses by enhancing their online visibility and driving sales through targeted display advertising. The primary objective of this project is to create a comprehensive advertising strategy that effectively reaches high-intent shoppers, ensuring that ads resonate with the target audience. This initiative will involve a detailed analysis of the client’s target demographic, allowing our team to craft visually compelling ads that align with the brand's identity. We will set up campaigns on leading platforms such as the Google Display Network and Facebook Audience Network, employing industry best practices for ad placements and audience targeting. Continuous monitoring and optimization of the campaigns will be conducted to maximize conversions and return on investment. Key outcomes include increased brand awareness, improved customer engagement, and measurable sales growth, ultimately providing clients with a structured approach to achieving their business goals.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Target Audience Analysis**: Conduct a thorough analysis to identify high-intent shoppers and understand their behaviors and preferences.
- **Ad Creation**: Design visually compelling display ads that reflect the brand's identity and appeal to the target audience.
- **Campaign Setup**: Configure campaigns on major platforms, including Google Display Network and Facebook Audience Network, ensuring optimal ad placements and targeting settings.
- **Monitoring and Optimization**: Implement a continuous monitoring process to track campaign performance metrics, making necessary adjustments to improve effectiveness and ROI.
- **Reporting**: Provide regular performance reports detailing key metrics, insights, and recommendations for future campaigns.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Target Audience Analysis
  - Deliverable: Target Audience Report
- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of Ad Designs
  - Deliverable: Set of Display Ads
- **Milestone 3**: Campaign Setup on Platforms
  - Deliverable: Live Campaigns on Google Display Network and Facebook Audience Network
- **Milestone 4**: First Performance Review
  - Deliverable: Performance Report with Optimization Recommendations
- **Milestone 5**: Final Campaign Review and Reporting
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Campaign Performance Report
