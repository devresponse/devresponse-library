
# Retargeting Campaign Performance Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

The Retargeting Campaign Performance Audit aims to enhance the effectiveness of your existing retargeting efforts by providing a comprehensive analysis of key performance metrics. In today's competitive online landscape, it is essential to re-engage potential customers who have previously interacted with your brand. This project will focus on evaluating critical aspects such as click-through rates, conversion rates, and audience segmentation. Our expert team will conduct a thorough examination of your current ad creatives, targeting strategies, and budget allocation. By identifying both strengths and weaknesses, we will deliver actionable recommendations tailored to improve your campaign's performance. The final report will encompass insights on best practices, optimization strategies, and potential areas for expansion, ensuring that your retargeting efforts yield maximum ROI. Ultimately, this audit will empower your marketing team with the knowledge and tools necessary to refine your approach and achieve greater success in your retargeting campaigns.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Review existing retargeting campaign materials, including ad creatives, targeting parameters, and budget allocation.
- **Performance Metrics Analysis**: Analyze key performance metrics such as click-through rates, conversion rates, and audience engagement levels.
- **Audience Segmentation Review**: Evaluate the effectiveness of current audience segmentation strategies and identify potential new segments.
- **Competitor Benchmarking**: Conduct a comparative analysis of competitor retargeting strategies to identify industry best practices.
- **Recommendations Development**: Provide tailored recommendations based on the analysis, focusing on optimization strategies and areas for potential expansion.
- **Final Report Preparation**: Compile findings, insights, and recommendations into a comprehensive report for presentation to stakeholders.
