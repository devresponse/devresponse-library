
# Project Plan Template: Music Licensing for Audio Ads

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the "Music Licensing for Audio Ads" project is to secure the necessary music licenses that will enable the creation of engaging and legally compliant audio advertisements. This project aims to identify suitable music tracks that resonate with the brand's identity and message while ensuring all licensing agreements are thoroughly negotiated and documented. The key outcomes include a comprehensive set of music licenses that not only enhance the quality of audio advertisements but also safeguard the brand against potential copyright infringements. By the conclusion of this project, stakeholders will have peace of mind knowing that all music used in their audio ads is legally licensed, allowing them to focus on crafting compelling content without the burden of legal complications.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Identify music genres and styles that align with the brand's identity.
  - Compile a list of potential music tracks for consideration.
  
- **Evaluation Phase**: 
  - Assess the suitability of each track based on brand messaging and target audience.
  - Shortlist tracks that meet the criteria for licensing.

- **Negotiation Phase**: 
  - Reach out to rights holders for each shortlisted track.
  - Negotiate terms of use, including duration, territory, and payment structure.

- **Documentation Phase**: 
  - Draft and finalize licensing agreements.
  - Ensure all licenses are properly documented and stored for future reference.

- **Review Phase**: 
  - Conduct a final review of all licenses to ensure compliance with legal standards.
  - Provide a summary report of all acquired licenses and their respective terms.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: List of potential music tracks for licensing.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Evaluation Phase
  - Deliverable: Shortlist of tracks selected for negotiation.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Negotiation Phase
  - Deliverable: Draft licensing agreements for each selected track.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Documentation Phase
  - Deliverable: Finalized licensing agreements and documentation.

- **Milestone 5**: Completion of Review Phase
  - Deliverable: Summary report of all acquired licenses and compliance check.
