
# Community Manager Onboarding Guide Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Community Manager Onboarding Guide project aims to provide a comprehensive resource for new community managers, equipping them with essential tools and knowledge to foster engagement and build strong relationships within their digital communities. This project will create a structured onboarding guide that covers best practices for community engagement, effective communication strategies, and methods for measuring community success. Key outcomes include a well-organized document that outlines understanding community dynamics, setting engagement goals, utilizing various online platforms, and troubleshooting common challenges. By ensuring that new community managers can seamlessly integrate into their roles, this guide will contribute positively to the community's growth and health. Additionally, the final document will serve as a reference point for ongoing training and development, promoting consistency in engagement practices across the organization.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Community Manager Onboarding Guide includes the following key components:

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct research on best practices in community management, including engagement strategies, communication methods, and success metrics.
- **Content Development**: Create detailed sections covering:
  - Understanding community dynamics
  - Setting engagement goals
  - Utilizing various online platforms (e.g., forums, social media, newsletters)
  - Troubleshooting common challenges faced by community managers
- **Review and Feedback**: Collaborate with experienced community managers and stakeholders to gather feedback on the draft guide.
- **Finalization**: Incorporate feedback and finalize the guide, ensuring clarity and usability.
- **Distribution**: Develop a plan for distributing the guide to new community managers and relevant stakeholders.
- **Training Sessions**: Organize training sessions to walk new community managers through the guide and answer any questions.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Analysis
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing best practices and strategies.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Draft of the Community Manager Onboarding Guide
  - Deliverable: First draft of the guide for review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Feedback Collection and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised draft incorporating feedback from stakeholders.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Guide Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized Community Manager Onboarding Guide ready for distribution.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Training Session Completion
  - Deliverable: Conducted training sessions with new community managers.
