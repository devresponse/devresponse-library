
# Project Plan: Customer Journey Mapping via Social Listening

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Customer Journey Mapping via Social Listening" project aims to leverage social listening data to create a comprehensive understanding of the customer journey. In today's competitive market, businesses must prioritize enhancing customer experience and improving retention rates. This project will analyze digital conversations and sentiments surrounding the brand to identify key touchpoints that significantly influence customer decisions and perceptions. By synthesizing this data, we will provide actionable insights that enable businesses to tailor their marketing strategies effectively, address customer pain points, and foster stronger relationships with their audience. The final deliverable will be a detailed report that outlines the customer journey, highlights key insights, and offers recommendations for optimizing customer interactions at each identified touchpoint. Ultimately, this project seeks to empower businesses to make informed decisions that enhance customer satisfaction and loyalty.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Phase 1: Research and Data Collection**
  - Identify relevant social media platforms and online forums for data collection.
  - Utilize social listening tools to gather data on customer conversations, sentiments, and trends related to the brand.
  - Compile a comprehensive dataset for analysis.

- **Phase 2: Data Analysis**
  - Analyze the collected data to identify key themes, sentiments, and customer pain points.
  - Map out the customer journey based on the identified touchpoints and interactions.

- **Phase 3: Reporting and Recommendations**
  - Develop a detailed report that includes:
    - A visual representation of the customer journey.
    - Key insights derived from the analysis.
    - Actionable recommendations for optimizing customer interactions at each touchpoint.
  - Present findings to stakeholders and gather feedback for final adjustments.
