
# Project Plan Template: Sourcing Recruitment for Online Advocacy Campaigns

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Sourcing Recruitment for Online Advocacy Campaigns" project aims to strategically source and recruit skilled professionals who will play a vital role in enhancing community engagement and driving social change through advocacy campaigns. The primary objective is to identify and attract candidates who not only possess the necessary skills but also resonate with the values and mission of the campaigns. Key outcomes include the successful recruitment of a diverse and passionate team capable of executing targeted outreach, managing recruitment processes, and effectively promoting campaign objectives. By collaborating closely with campaign managers, the project will develop tailored recruitment strategies and compelling job descriptions, utilizing various online platforms to reach top talent. Ultimately, this initiative seeks to build a strong team that can engage with the community and drive impactful social change.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Candidate Identification**: Research and identify potential candidates with relevant skills and experience in advocacy and community engagement.
- **Outreach Strategy Development**: Collaborate with campaign managers to create outreach strategies that resonate with target communities.
- **Job Description Creation**: Develop clear and compelling job descriptions that align with the campaign's mission and values.
- **Online Recruitment Platforms**: Utilize various online platforms (e.g., LinkedIn, job boards, social media) to attract and engage potential candidates.
- **Diversity and Inclusion Focus**: Implement strategies to ensure a diverse pool of candidates, promoting inclusivity in the recruitment process.
- **Candidate Screening and Interviews**: Manage the screening process, conduct interviews, and assess candidates based on their alignment with campaign goals.
- **Onboarding Process**: Facilitate a smooth onboarding process for selected candidates, ensuring they are well-integrated into the team and understand campaign objectives.
