
# Project Plan Template: Sourcing Event Moderators for Live Streams

## 1. Executive Summary

The primary objective of the "Sourcing Event Moderators for Live Streams" project is to identify and recruit skilled moderators who can effectively manage live streaming events. In today's digital landscape, live streaming serves as a crucial platform for audience engagement and community building. This project aims to enhance the quality of live stream events by ensuring that interactions remain seamless and engaging. Key outcomes include the successful recruitment of moderators who possess strong communication skills, a deep understanding of online community dynamics, and the ability to think on their feet. By fostering a positive atmosphere during broadcasts, addressing viewer questions, and enforcing community guidelines, these moderators will play an essential role in creating a welcoming environment for participants. Ultimately, this initiative will contribute to the growth and engagement of the online community, ensuring that live events are not only informative but also enjoyable for all attendees.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the current market to identify the demand for live stream moderators and the specific skills required.
- **Job Description Development**: Create detailed job descriptions outlining the responsibilities, qualifications, and skills needed for the moderator role.
- **Sourcing Candidates**: Utilize various platforms (social media, job boards, and professional networks) to source potential candidates.
- **Screening Process**: Develop a screening process that includes reviewing resumes, conducting initial interviews, and assessing candidates' communication skills and community management experience.
- **Final Selection**: Select the top candidates based on predefined criteria and conduct final interviews to ensure they align with the project objectives.
- **Training and Onboarding**: Design a training program to onboard selected moderators, covering community guidelines, engagement strategies, and technical aspects of live streaming.
- **Feedback and Evaluation**: Implement a feedback mechanism to evaluate the performance of moderators during live events and make necessary adjustments.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of market research and analysis
  - **Deliverable**: Market analysis report
- **Milestone 2**: Development of job descriptions
  - **Deliverable**: Finalized job descriptions for moderators
- **Milestone 3**: Sourcing and screening of candidates
  - **Deliverable**: List of shortlisted candidates
- **Milestone 4**: Final selection of moderators
  - **Deliverable**: Signed contracts with selected moderators
- **Milestone 5**: Completion of training and onboarding
  - **Deliverable**: Training materials and completion certificates for moderators
- **Milestone 6**: Implementation of feedback and evaluation process
  - **Deliverable**: Feedback report and performance evaluation metrics
