
# Project Plan Template: Recruitment for Multilingual Community Managers

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the "Recruitment for Multilingual Community Managers" project is to identify and recruit skilled professionals who can effectively engage with diverse audiences across various online platforms. This project aims to build a robust pipeline of candidates fluent in at least two languages, ensuring they can communicate and connect with community members from different cultural backgrounds. The recruitment process will leverage targeted outreach, professional networks, and specialized job boards to source candidates. Key outcomes include a shortlist of qualified candidates who possess experience in community management, a strong understanding of online engagement strategies, and the ability to foster inclusive environments. By successfully executing this project, we aim to enhance the client's online community's growth and engagement, ultimately contributing to a more vibrant and inclusive digital space.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Candidate Sourcing**: 
  - Conduct targeted outreach to multilingual professionals through various channels, including social media, professional networks, and specialized job boards.
  - Develop and distribute job postings that clearly outline the requirements and responsibilities of the community manager roles.

- **Candidate Evaluation**:
  - Screen applications to identify candidates who meet the language proficiency and community management experience criteria.
  - Conduct initial interviews to assess candidates' understanding of online engagement strategies and their ability to foster inclusive environments.

- **Shortlisting Candidates**:
  - Create a shortlist of qualified candidates based on evaluation criteria.
  - Prepare candidate profiles highlighting their skills, experience, and language capabilities for client review.

- **Client Collaboration**:
  - Regularly update the client on the recruitment progress and gather feedback on shortlisted candidates.
  - Facilitate the interview process between the client and shortlisted candidates.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Project Kickoff
  - Deliverable: Project initiation meeting notes and recruitment strategy outline.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Candidate Sourcing Completion
  - Deliverable: List of sourced candidates and job postings shared with the client.

- **Milestone 3**: Candidate Evaluation Completion
  - Deliverable: Summary report of candidate evaluations and initial interview outcomes.

- **Milestone 4**: Shortlist Presentation
  - Deliverable: Final shortlist of qualified candidates presented to the client, including detailed profiles.

- **Milestone 5**: Client Interviews
  - Deliverable: Schedule and facilitate interviews between the client and shortlisted candidates.
