
# Project Plan Template: Sourcing Recruitment for Community Engagement Coaches

## 1. Executive Summary

The project "Sourcing Recruitment for Community Engagement Coaches" aims to identify and recruit skilled professionals who can effectively train and guide teams in engaging with online communities. The primary objective is to empower organizations with the expertise needed to develop tailored training programs that focus on best practices in community management. By sourcing candidates with a proven track record in community engagement, we will ensure that the selected coaches can foster meaningful interactions and enhance overall community engagement. Key outcomes of this project include the successful recruitment of qualified coaches, the development of impactful training materials, and the establishment of a framework for ongoing community engagement. Ultimately, this initiative seeks to build vibrant online communities that drive brand loyalty and customer satisfaction, thereby contributing to the long-term success of the organization.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of this project includes the following key activities:

- **Candidate Sourcing**: Identify potential candidates through various channels, including job boards, social media, and professional networks.
- **Screening and Shortlisting**: Review resumes and applications to shortlist candidates based on their experience and qualifications in community engagement.
- **Interview Process**: Conduct structured interviews to assess candidates' skills, knowledge, and ability to deliver effective coaching sessions.
- **Assessment of Coaching Ability**: Implement practical assessments to evaluate candidates' coaching methodologies and their effectiveness in engaging teams.
- **Final Selection**: Select the top candidates who demonstrate the necessary expertise and fit for the role.
- **Onboarding and Training**: Facilitate onboarding sessions for the selected coaches, providing them with the necessary resources and information about the organization’s community engagement goals.
- **Feedback and Evaluation**: Establish a feedback mechanism to evaluate the performance of the coaches and the effectiveness of the training programs they develop.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of candidate sourcing
  - Deliverable: List of potential candidates
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of screening and shortlisting
  - Deliverable: Shortlist of candidates for interviews
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of the interview process
  - Deliverable: Interview feedback reports for each candidate
- **Milestone 4**: Final selection of Community Engagement Coaches
  - Deliverable: Offer letters for selected candidates
- **Milestone 5**: Onboarding of selected coaches
  - Deliverable: Onboarding materials and training schedule
- **Milestone 6**: Implementation of training programs
  - Deliverable: Training program outlines and materials
- **Milestone 7**: Evaluation of coaching effectiveness
  - Deliverable: Feedback reports and recommendations for improvement
