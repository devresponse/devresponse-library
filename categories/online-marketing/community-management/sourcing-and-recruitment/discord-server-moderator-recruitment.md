
# Project Plan Template: Discord Server Moderator Recruitment

## 1. Executive Summary

The Discord Server Moderator Recruitment project aims to establish a dedicated team of moderators who will enhance user engagement and maintain a safe online environment within our Discord community. In today's digital landscape, the importance of a vibrant and secure online community cannot be overstated. This project will focus on sourcing and recruiting skilled moderators who possess strong communication skills, a comprehensive understanding of Discord functionalities, and a genuine passion for community management. The key objectives include creating a detailed job description, identifying potential candidates through various sourcing channels, conducting thorough interviews, and ultimately hiring individuals who align with our community values. The expected outcomes of this project are a well-defined recruitment process, a team of qualified moderators, and an improved user experience that fosters positive interactions and adherence to community guidelines.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Job Description Creation**: Develop a comprehensive job description that outlines the responsibilities, qualifications, and expectations for the moderator role.
  
- **Candidate Sourcing**: Utilize various channels to source candidates, including social media platforms, job boards, and community forums. Engage with potential candidates through outreach and networking.

- **Application Process**: Set up an application process that includes submission of resumes, cover letters, and any relevant experience in community management.

- **Interview Process**: Conduct structured interviews to assess candidates' communication skills, understanding of Discord, and their approach to community management.

- **Selection Criteria**: Establish clear criteria for evaluating candidates, including their ability to foster engagement, manage discussions, and enforce community guidelines.

- **Onboarding**: Develop an onboarding process for selected moderators, including training on community guidelines, Discord functionalities, and best practices for moderation.

- **Feedback Mechanism**: Implement a feedback mechanism to continuously assess the performance of moderators and make necessary adjustments to the recruitment process.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Job Description  
  **Deliverable**: Finalized job description document.

- **Milestone 2**: Candidate Sourcing  
  **Deliverable**: List of sourced candidates and outreach records.

- **Milestone 3**: Application Review  
  **Deliverable**: Summary of applications received and shortlisted candidates.

- **Milestone 4**: Conduct Interviews  
  **Deliverable**: Interview notes and candidate evaluations.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Selection  
  **Deliverable**: List of hired moderators and offer letters.

- **Milestone 6**: Onboarding Completion  
  **Deliverable**: Onboarding materials and training completion records.
