
# Project Plan Template: Curated Playlist Research Report

## 1. Executive Summary

The Curated Playlist Research Report aims to empower artists by providing a comprehensive overview of playlists that align with their specific musical genres. This project is designed to conduct extensive research across various streaming platforms to identify popular playlists that cater to diverse styles of music. The key objectives of this project include compiling a detailed report that lists these playlists, along with essential contact information for the curators, enabling artists to reach out directly for potential placements. By leveraging curated playlists, artists can significantly enhance their visibility and engagement in the competitive music landscape. The final report will be delivered in a user-friendly format, ensuring that artists can easily navigate and utilize the information effectively. The expected outcomes include increased exposure for artists, potential collaborations with playlist curators, and ultimately, a growth in their fanbase.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Identify and analyze popular playlists across major streaming platforms such as Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music.
  - Categorize playlists based on musical genres, audience demographics, and engagement metrics.
  
- **Data Compilation**: 
  - Create a comprehensive list of identified playlists, including the following details:
    - Playlist name
    - Genre
    - Number of followers
    - Engagement metrics (e.g., average streams)
    - Curator contact information (if available)
  
- **Report Development**: 
  - Compile the research findings into a structured report.
  - Ensure the report is visually appealing and easy to navigate.
  - Include a summary section for quick reference.

- **Review and Feedback**: 
  - Present the draft report to stakeholders for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on feedback received.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Deliver the final report in a user-friendly format (PDF or interactive document).
  - Provide a brief presentation to explain the findings and how to utilize the report effectively.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research findings document

- **Milestone 2**: Data Compilation
  - Deliverable: Draft report containing playlist details

- **Milestone 3**: Review and Feedback
  - Deliverable: Revised report based on stakeholder feedback

- **Milestone 4**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Final Curated Playlist Research Report
