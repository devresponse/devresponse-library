
# Deezer Editorial Playlist Submission Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Deezer Editorial Playlist Submission project aims to empower artists by streamlining their submission process to Deezer's editorial team. The primary objective is to ensure that artists' tracks are effectively considered for inclusion in curated playlists, which is essential for enhancing their visibility on one of the leading music streaming platforms. This project will provide a comprehensive review of the artist's track, allowing us to craft a compelling submission that emphasizes the unique aspects of their music. Key outcomes include increased chances of playlist inclusion, improved artist engagement, and a stronger promotional presence on Deezer. By offering guidance on best practices for submission and assisting in the preparation of promotional materials such as artist bios and high-quality cover art, we aim to maximize the potential for artists to gain traction and drive more streams through curated playlists.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Track Review**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the artist's track to identify its strengths and unique qualities.
- **Submission Crafting**: Develop a compelling submission narrative that highlights the artist's music and aligns with Deezer's editorial criteria.
- **Best Practices Guidance**: Provide artists with insights and recommendations on the submission process, including timing and strategy.
- **Promotional Material Assistance**: Help artists gather necessary promotional materials, including:
  - Artist bios
  - High-quality cover art
  - Press releases or additional promotional content
- **Submission Follow-Up**: Offer support in following up with Deezer's editorial team to ensure the submission is acknowledged and considered.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Detailed project brief and timeline
- **Milestone 2**: Track Review Completion
  - Deliverable: Review report with feedback and recommendations
- **Milestone 3**: Submission Package Development
  - Deliverable: Completed submission narrative and promotional materials
- **Milestone 4**: Submission to Deezer
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of submission sent to the artist
- **Milestone 5**: Follow-Up Report
  - Deliverable: Summary of follow-up actions and responses from Deezer
