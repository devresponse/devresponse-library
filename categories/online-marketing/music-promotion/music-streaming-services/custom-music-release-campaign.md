
# Custom Music Release Campaign Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Music Release Campaign aims to provide artists with a comprehensive promotional strategy that maximizes the impact of their new music release. This project will focus on creating a multi-faceted approach that begins with a pre-save campaign, encouraging fans to save the new track ahead of its official release. By developing engaging teaser content, including eye-catching visuals and compelling messaging, we will generate buzz and anticipation across various social media platforms. Furthermore, targeted advertising will be implemented on popular streaming services to effectively reach the artist's desired audience. The campaign will be tailored to the artist's unique style and goals, ensuring that every aspect resonates with their target demographic. Key outcomes of this project include increased visibility for the artist, enhanced engagement with their audience, and a measurable boost in streaming numbers upon release. Ultimately, this campaign will empower the artist to connect more deeply with their fans and establish a strong presence in the competitive music streaming landscape.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Save Campaign Development**
  - Design and implement a pre-save landing page.
  - Create promotional materials to encourage pre-saves.
  
- **Teaser Content Creation**
  - Develop visual assets (graphics, videos) for social media.
  - Craft compelling messaging for posts and ads.
  
- **Social Media Strategy**
  - Identify key platforms for promotion (Instagram, TikTok, Twitter, etc.).
  - Schedule and manage posts leading up to the release.
  
- **Targeted Advertising**
  - Conduct audience research to define target demographics.
  - Create and manage ad campaigns on streaming services and social media.
  
- **Performance Tracking and Reporting**
  - Set up analytics to track engagement and pre-save metrics.
  - Provide a final report detailing campaign performance and outcomes.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Campaign Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Project kickoff meeting and campaign timeline.
  
- **Milestone 2: Pre-Save Campaign Launch**
  - Deliverable: Live pre-save landing page and promotional materials.
  
- **Milestone 3: Teaser Content Rollout**
  - Deliverable: Scheduled social media posts and visual assets.
  
- **Milestone 4: Advertising Campaign Launch**
  - Deliverable: Active targeted ad campaigns on selected platforms.
  
- **Milestone 5: Campaign Conclusion**
  - Deliverable: Final performance report and analysis.
