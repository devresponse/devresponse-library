
# Project Plan Template: Cross-Domain Tracking Issue Resolution

## 1. Executive Summary

The Cross-Domain Tracking Issue Resolution project aims to address and resolve critical tracking issues that impede the accurate collection of user data across multiple domains. In today's digital landscape, understanding user behavior is paramount for effective online marketing strategies. This project will provide a comprehensive analysis of existing tracking setups, identifying issues related to referral exclusions, cookie settings, and session continuity. Our primary objective is to ensure seamless user tracking as they navigate between different domains or subdomains. Key outcomes include the implementation of necessary adjustments to tracking codes, proper configuration of cookie settings, and the establishment of correct referral exclusions. By the conclusion of this project, clients will benefit from a robust cross-domain tracking system that enables them to gain valuable insights into user journeys, ultimately optimizing their online marketing efforts and enhancing overall business performance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Analysis of Existing Tracking Setups**: Conduct a thorough review of current tracking implementations across all relevant domains and subdomains.
- **Identification of Issues**: Identify specific problems related to referral exclusions, cookie settings, and session continuity that affect data accuracy.
- **Implementation of Adjustments**: Make necessary adjustments to tracking codes to ensure proper functionality across domains.
- **Configuration of Cookie Settings**: Adjust cookie settings to facilitate session continuity and prevent data loss during user navigation.
- **Referral Exclusions Setup**: Ensure that referral exclusions are correctly configured to maintain data integrity.
- **Testing and Validation**: Conduct extensive testing to validate that cross-domain tracking is functioning as intended.
- **Documentation and Reporting**: Provide detailed documentation of changes made and a final report summarizing the project outcomes and recommendations for future tracking strategies.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the analysis of existing tracking setups.
  - **Deliverable**: Analysis report outlining current tracking configurations and identified issues.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Implementation of adjustments to tracking codes and cookie settings.
  - **Deliverable**: Updated tracking codes and cookie configuration documentation.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Successful configuration of referral exclusions.
  - **Deliverable**: Referral exclusions setup confirmation report.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Completion of testing and validation of cross-domain tracking.
  - **Deliverable**: Testing report confirming successful tracking across domains.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final documentation and project closure.
  - **Deliverable**: Comprehensive project report including all findings, changes made, and recommendations.
