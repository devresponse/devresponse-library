
# Project Plan Template: Referral Spam Filtering Fixes

## 1. Executive Summary
The Referral Spam Filtering Fixes project aims to enhance the integrity of web analytics data by identifying and resolving issues related to referral spam. In the digital marketing landscape, accurate data is paramount for informed decision-making, and referral spam can significantly distort this data. This project will conduct a thorough analysis of the current analytics setup to pinpoint sources of spam traffic, followed by the implementation of effective filtering techniques. Key outcomes include a comprehensive report detailing identified spam sources, methods used for filtering, and recommendations for maintaining ongoing data integrity. By the end of this project, stakeholders will benefit from a cleaner, more reliable dataset that will empower strategic marketing decisions and optimize resource allocation. The ultimate objective is to ensure that marketing strategies are based on accurate data, leading to improved performance and ROI.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work
- **Initial Assessment**: Review the current web analytics setup to understand existing configurations and identify potential vulnerabilities to referral spam.
- **Data Analysis**: Conduct a detailed analysis of traffic sources to identify patterns and sources of referral spam.
- **Filtering Techniques**: Implement effective filtering techniques to exclude spam traffic from analytics reports. This may include:
  - Setting up filters in Google Analytics or other analytics tools.
  - Utilizing server-side filtering methods.
  - Adjusting tracking codes as necessary.
- **Reporting**: Prepare a comprehensive report that includes:
  - A list of identified spam sources.
  - A description of the filtering methods implemented.
  - Recommendations for ongoing monitoring and maintenance of data integrity.
- **Training**: Provide training for relevant team members on how to maintain the integrity of analytics data and recognize potential spam in the future.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Assessment
  - Deliverable: Initial Assessment Report
