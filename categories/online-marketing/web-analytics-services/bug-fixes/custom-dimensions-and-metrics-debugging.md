
# Project Plan: Custom Dimensions and Metrics Debugging

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's data-driven landscape, accurate tracking of custom dimensions and metrics is essential for businesses to make informed decisions. The objective of the "Custom Dimensions and Metrics Debugging" project is to identify and resolve issues related to the tracking of custom dimensions and metrics within your web analytics tools. This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of the current setup to pinpoint discrepancies and misconfigurations that may hinder the accurate tracking of key performance indicators (KPIs) and user attributes. Our expert team will provide actionable recommendations and implement necessary fixes to ensure that your analytics tools capture the right data effectively. Key outcomes of this project include a robust web analytics setup that enhances your ability to measure success, improved accuracy in data reporting, and a solid foundation for your online marketing strategies. By the end of this project, you will be equipped with the insights and tools needed to make informed decisions based on reliable data.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a thorough review of the current web analytics setup, including custom dimensions and metrics configurations.
- **Data Quality Analysis**: Identify discrepancies in data collection and reporting, focusing on custom dimensions and metrics.
- **Configuration Review**: Evaluate the current configurations in web analytics tools to identify misconfigurations that may affect data accuracy.
- **Recommendations Report**: Provide a detailed report outlining the findings from the assessment and analysis, including actionable recommendations for fixes.
- **Implementation of Fixes**: Execute the necessary changes to the web analytics setup to resolve identified issues and ensure accurate tracking.
- **Testing and Validation**: Conduct thorough testing to validate that the implemented fixes are functioning correctly and that data is being captured accurately.
- **Final Review and Documentation**: Provide comprehensive documentation of the changes made and conduct a final review with stakeholders to ensure satisfaction with the outcomes.
