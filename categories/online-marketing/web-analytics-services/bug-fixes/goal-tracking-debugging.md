
# Project Plan Template: Goal Tracking Debugging

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Goal Tracking Debugging" project aims to enhance the accuracy and reliability of goal tracking setups within your web analytics tools. In today's digital landscape, precise goal tracking is vital for evaluating the success of online marketing strategies. This project will involve a comprehensive audit of your current goal tracking configurations to identify any broken conversion tracking or misconfigured goal funnels that may be obstructing your ability to collect trustworthy data. Our expert team will deliver a detailed report outlining the identified issues, along with actionable recommendations for fixes. The key outcomes of this project include a fully functional goal tracking setup that aligns with your business objectives, improved data integrity, and the ability to make informed decisions based on accurate metrics. By the conclusion of this project, you will be equipped to leverage data effectively for future marketing campaigns, ultimately driving better results and optimizing your marketing efforts.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- Conduct a thorough audit of the existing goal tracking configurations within your web analytics tools.
- Identify any broken conversion tracking setups and misconfigured goal funnels.
- Analyze the impact of these issues on data collection and reporting.
- Provide a comprehensive report detailing the identified issues, including:
  - Description of each issue
  - Potential impact on business objectives
  - Recommended fixes and best practices for goal tracking
- Implement the recommended fixes to ensure a fully functional goal tracking setup.
- Conduct a follow-up review to verify that all issues have been resolved and that the goal tracking is functioning as intended.
- Provide training and documentation to ensure your team can maintain the goal tracking setup moving forward.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1:** Completion of the initial audit of goal tracking configurations
  - **Deliverable:** Audit report outlining identified issues
- **Milestone 2:** Presentation of findings and recommendations
  - **Deliverable:** Comprehensive report with actionable recommendations
- **Milestone 3:** Implementation of recommended fixes
  - **Deliverable:** Updated goal tracking setup
- **Milestone 4:** Follow-up review and verification
  - **Deliverable:** Confirmation report of successful implementation
- **Milestone 5:** Training session for the team
  - **Deliverable:** Training materials and documentation
