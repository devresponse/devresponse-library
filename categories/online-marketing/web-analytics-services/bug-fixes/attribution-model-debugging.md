
# Project Plan: Attribution Model Debugging

## 1. Executive Summary

The Attribution Model Debugging project aims to enhance the accuracy and reliability of your web analytics tools by debugging and optimizing the current attribution models. Accurate attribution is crucial for understanding the effectiveness of various marketing channels, which in turn maximizes return on investment (ROI). This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of existing attribution models to identify discrepancies and issues that may lead to inaccurate data reporting. Our expert team will collaborate closely with your internal stakeholders to implement necessary adjustments and enhancements. By the conclusion of this project, you will possess a refined attribution model that accurately reflects the contributions of each marketing channel. This will empower your organization to make informed decisions and optimize marketing strategies effectively, ultimately driving better performance and increased ROI.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a thorough review of the current attribution models in use, including data sources, methodologies, and reporting structures.
- **Data Analysis**: Analyze existing data to identify discrepancies, inaccuracies, and areas for improvement within the attribution models.
- **Stakeholder Interviews**: Engage with key stakeholders to gather insights on current challenges and expectations regarding attribution accuracy.
- **Model Optimization**: Develop and implement adjustments to the attribution models based on findings from the analysis and stakeholder feedback.
- **Testing and Validation**: Test the refined models to ensure they accurately reflect marketing channel contributions and validate results with stakeholders.
- **Documentation**: Provide comprehensive documentation of the new attribution models, including methodologies, data sources, and reporting guidelines.
- **Training and Support**: Offer training sessions for your team to ensure they understand the new models and can effectively utilize the insights generated.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing current attribution models and identified issues.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Data Analysis
  - Deliverable: Data Analysis Report highlighting discrepancies and areas for improvement.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Stakeholder Interviews Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary Report of stakeholder insights and expectations.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Model Optimization Implemented
  - Deliverable: Updated Attribution Model with documented changes.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Testing and Validation Completed
  - Deliverable: Validation Report confirming the accuracy of the refined models.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Documentation and Training Delivered
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Documentation and Training Materials.
