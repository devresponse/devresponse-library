
# Project Plan Template: E-commerce Tracking Setup for Google Analytics

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's competitive online marketplace, understanding customer behavior is crucial for driving sales and optimizing marketing strategies. The objective of this project is to set up enhanced e-commerce tracking in Google Analytics, enabling businesses to gain valuable insights into product performance, sales funnels, and customer purchase behavior. This project will involve configuring tracking for product impressions, clicks, and transactions, as well as implementing enhanced features such as checkout behavior analysis and product list performance. Key outcomes include the ability to accurately track customer interactions with products, identify bottlenecks in the sales funnel, and make data-driven decisions to improve marketing efforts and user experience. By providing a comprehensive view of the e-commerce landscape, this project aims to ultimately increase conversion rates and drive revenue growth for the business.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with stakeholders to understand business goals and specific tracking needs.
- **Account Setup**: Ensure that Google Analytics is properly set up and linked to the e-commerce platform.
- **Tag Configuration**: Implement necessary tags for tracking product impressions, clicks, and transactions using Google Tag Manager.
- **Enhanced E-commerce Features**: Configure enhanced e-commerce features, including:
  - Checkout behavior analysis
  - Product list performance tracking
  - Promotion tracking
- **Testing and Validation**: Conduct thorough testing to ensure all tags and triggers are firing correctly and data is being captured accurately.
- **Reporting Setup**: Create custom reports and dashboards in Google Analytics to visualize key metrics.
- **Training**: Provide training sessions for stakeholders on how to interpret the data and utilize the insights for decision-making.
- **Documentation**: Deliver comprehensive documentation outlining the setup process, configurations, and best practices for ongoing tracking.
