
# Project Plan Template: Customer Feedback Request Automation

## 1. Executive Summary

The Customer Feedback Request Automation project aims to establish a seamless and efficient email workflow that automatically reaches out to customers following their purchase or service experience. The primary objective of this project is to solicit valuable feedback and reviews from customers, thereby enhancing engagement and providing insights into customer satisfaction. By implementing a series of personalized email templates triggered by specific customer actions, such as completing a purchase or receiving a service, we will create a robust system that fosters timely communication. Key outcomes include improved response rates, actionable insights for service enhancement, and a strengthened relationship between the business and its customers. This project is essential for companies looking to leverage customer feedback for continuous improvement and better service delivery.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Define project goals and objectives.
  - Identify stakeholders and establish communication channels.

- **Requirements Gathering**
  - Conduct interviews and surveys with stakeholders to gather feedback requirements.
  - Analyze existing customer feedback processes.

- **Design Phase**
  - Develop a series of personalized email templates tailored to different customer actions.
  - Create a workflow diagram outlining the email automation process.

- **Implementation Phase**
  - Set up the email automation system using the chosen platform (e.g., Mailchimp, HubSpot).
  - Integrate tracking mechanisms to monitor email open rates, click-through rates, and response rates.

- **Testing Phase**
  - Conduct thorough testing of the email automation workflow to ensure functionality.
  - Gather feedback from a small group of customers to refine email content and timing.

- **Launch Phase**
  - Roll out the email automation system to all customers.
  - Monitor initial performance and make adjustments as necessary.

- **Post-Launch Review**
  - Analyze data collected from the email automation system.
  - Prepare a report summarizing feedback received and recommendations for future improvements.
