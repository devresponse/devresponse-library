
# Project Plan Template: Keyword Cannibalization Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

The Keyword Cannibalization Audit project aims to enhance the SEO performance of your website by identifying and addressing instances of keyword cannibalization. In the competitive landscape of search engine optimization, multiple pages targeting the same keywords can dilute your site's authority and hinder its visibility in search results. This project will provide a comprehensive audit of your existing content, focusing on pinpointing overlapping keywords and assessing their impact on your search engine rankings. Key outcomes include a detailed report of identified issues, actionable recommendations for content consolidation, optimization of page titles and meta descriptions, and strategies for implementing redirects where necessary. By resolving these issues, we aim to ensure that each page effectively targets unique keywords, ultimately driving more targeted traffic to your site and improving overall search engine performance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with stakeholders to understand the current SEO strategy and specific concerns regarding keyword performance.
  
- **Content Inventory**: Compile a comprehensive list of all existing content on the website, including URLs, titles, and targeted keywords.

- **Keyword Analysis**: Utilize SEO tools to analyze the existing keywords across the website, identifying instances of keyword overlap and cannibalization.

- **Impact Assessment**: Evaluate the impact of identified keyword cannibalization on search engine rankings and organic traffic.

- **Recommendations Report**: Develop a detailed report outlining findings, including:
  - Instances of keyword cannibalization
  - Suggested content consolidation strategies
  - Optimized page titles and meta descriptions
  - Redirect implementation strategies

- **Presentation of Findings**: Schedule a meeting to present the findings and recommendations to stakeholders, allowing for discussion and feedback.

- **Implementation Support**: Provide guidance and support during the implementation of recommended changes, if required.
