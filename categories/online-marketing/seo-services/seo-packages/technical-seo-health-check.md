
# Project Plan Template: Technical SEO Health Check

## 1. Executive Summary

The Technical SEO Health Check project aims to provide a comprehensive audit of your website's technical aspects to ensure optimal search engine performance. The primary objective is to identify and rectify issues that may hinder search engine indexing, user experience, and overall site performance. This project will encompass a thorough analysis of broken links, crawl errors, and page speed performance. Key outcomes include a detailed report that outlines identified issues, actionable recommendations for fixes, and a roadmap for ongoing SEO improvements. By addressing these critical technical elements, businesses will enhance their online visibility, improve user experience, and drive increased organic traffic, thereby maintaining a competitive edge in the ever-evolving digital landscape.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Discuss project objectives and gather information about the website.
  
- **Website Audit**: 
  - Conduct a comprehensive technical SEO audit, including:
    - Identification of broken links.
    - Analysis of crawl errors.
    - Assessment of page speed performance.
    - Review of mobile responsiveness.
    - Evaluation of site architecture and URL structure.
  
- **Reporting**: 
  - Compile findings into a detailed report that includes:
    - Summary of identified issues.
    - Prioritized list of actionable recommendations.
    - Suggested timelines for implementation.
  
- **Follow-Up Consultation**: 
  - Present findings and recommendations to the client.
  - Discuss next steps and potential ongoing support.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Project brief and objectives document.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Website Audit
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive audit report detailing findings.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Delivery of Recommendations
  - Deliverable: Actionable recommendations document.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Follow-Up Consultation
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and agreed next steps.
