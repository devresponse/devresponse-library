
# Project Plan Template: Competitor Brand Mentions and Sentiment Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary

The primary objective of the Competitor Brand Mentions and Sentiment Analysis project is to provide a comprehensive understanding of how your brand is perceived in comparison to its competitors within the digital landscape. This project will involve a meticulous analysis of brand mentions across various online platforms, including social media, blogs, forums, and review sites. By leveraging advanced analytical tools, we will track these mentions and evaluate the sentiment associated with them—categorizing them as positive, negative, or neutral. The key outcomes of this project will include identifying trends in customer perception, highlighting areas for improvement, and uncovering opportunities for differentiation. Ultimately, the insights gained will empower your brand to refine its positioning strategy, ensuring it resonates more effectively with the target audience. The culmination of this project will be a detailed report that encapsulates our findings and provides actionable recommendations aimed at enhancing your brand's market presence.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Competitor Identification**: Identify key competitors in the market to be analyzed.
- **Data Collection**: Utilize advanced tools to gather data on brand mentions across selected online platforms, including social media, blogs, forums, and review sites.
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Analyze the sentiment of the collected mentions, categorizing them into positive, negative, and neutral sentiments.
- **Trend Identification**: Identify trends in customer perception over time, focusing on how these trends compare to competitors.
- **Reporting**: Compile findings into a comprehensive report that includes:
  - Overview of brand mentions and sentiment analysis.
  - Comparative analysis with competitors.
  - Identification of strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats (SWOT analysis).
  - Actionable recommendations for brand positioning and improvement.
- **Presentation**: Present findings and recommendations to stakeholders, ensuring clarity and understanding of insights.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of competitor identification
  - Deliverable: List of identified competitors
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of data collection
  - Deliverable: Dataset of brand mentions and associated sentiment
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of sentiment analysis
  - Deliverable: Sentiment analysis report
- **Milestone 4**: Completion of trend identification
  - Deliverable: Trend analysis report
- **Milestone 5**: Final report compilation
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report with findings and recommendations
- **Milestone 6**: Presentation to stakeholders
  - Deliverable: Presentation slides summarizing key insights and recommendations
