
# Project Plan Template: Duplicate Content Analysis and Resolution

## 1. Executive Summary

The Duplicate Content Analysis and Resolution project aims to enhance the SEO performance of your website by identifying and resolving instances of duplicate content. Duplicate content can significantly hinder search engine rankings, leading to decreased visibility and potential penalties. This project will involve a comprehensive audit of your website using advanced SEO tools to pinpoint duplicate pages, titles, and meta descriptions. The key outcomes of this project include a detailed report of identified duplicate content, actionable recommendations for resolution, and the implementation of best practices such as canonical tags, 301 redirects, or content rewriting. By addressing these issues, we aim to improve your website's authority and visibility in search engines, ultimately driving increased traffic and user engagement. Our objective is to safeguard your site against potential penalties and enhance its overall SEO health, ensuring a robust online presence.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with stakeholders to understand specific concerns and objectives related to duplicate content.
- **Website Audit**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the website using advanced SEO tools to identify duplicate content, including:
  - Duplicate pages
  - Duplicate titles
  - Duplicate meta descriptions
- **Reporting**: Compile findings into a comprehensive report detailing:
  - Instances of duplicate content
  - Potential impact on SEO performance
- **Recommendations**: Provide actionable recommendations for resolving identified issues, which may include:
  - Implementing canonical tags
  - Setting up 301 redirects
  - Rewriting or consolidating content
- **Implementation Support**: Offer guidance and support during the implementation of recommended changes.
- **Final Review**: Conduct a follow-up audit to ensure all duplicate content issues have been resolved and assess improvements in SEO performance.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
