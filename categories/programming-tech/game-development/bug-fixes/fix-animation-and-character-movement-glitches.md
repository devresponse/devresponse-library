
# Project Plan: Fix Animation and Character Movement Glitches

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Fix Animation and Character Movement Glitches" project aims to enhance the overall gaming experience by diagnosing and rectifying existing animation and character movement issues. The primary objective is to conduct a comprehensive analysis of the current character animations and movement mechanics to identify any discrepancies or bugs that disrupt gameplay fluidity. By examining the physics interactions between characters and their environments, the project seeks to ensure that movements are not only visually appealing but also functionally sound. Key outcomes include improved realism in character actions, seamless gameplay, and heightened player immersion. Regular updates and communication will be prioritized to align with the client's vision and requirements, ensuring that the final deliverables meet or exceed expectations. This project is essential for maintaining player engagement and satisfaction, ultimately contributing to the game's success in a competitive market.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Analysis Phase**: 
  - Conduct a thorough review of existing character animations and movement mechanics.
  - Identify and document all glitches, discrepancies, and bugs affecting gameplay.
  - Analyze physics interactions between characters and their environments.

- **Implementation Phase**: 
  - Develop a plan for rectifying identified issues, including specific adjustments and optimizations.
  - Implement changes to enhance the realism of character actions.
  - Test the updated animations and movement mechanics to ensure functionality and visual appeal.

- **Quality Assurance**: 
  - Conduct extensive testing to verify that all glitches have been resolved.
  - Gather feedback from a focus group of players to assess improvements.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on feedback received.

- **Communication and Reporting**: 
  - Provide regular updates to the client regarding progress and findings.
  - Schedule weekly meetings to discuss any challenges and align on project direction.
  - Deliver a final report summarizing the work completed, issues resolved, and recommendations for future improvements.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Analysis Phase
  - Deliverable: Detailed report on identified glitches and discrepancies.

- **Milestone 2**: Implementation of Adjustments
  - Deliverable: Updated character animations and movement mechanics.

- **Milestone 3**: Quality Assurance Testing
  - Deliverable: Testing report and player feedback summary.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Project Review
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive final report including all findings, adjustments made, and recommendations.
