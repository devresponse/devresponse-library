
# Project Plan Template: Zombie Outbreak Tactics

## 1. Executive Summary

The project "Zombie Outbreak Tactics" aims to create an innovative tactical strategy game set in a post-apocalyptic world dominated by zombies. The primary objective is to immerse players in a rich narrative where they assume the role of a leader, guiding a diverse group of survivors through various challenging scenarios. The game will feature turn-based combat mechanics, allowing players to strategically plan their moves against relentless hordes of zombies. Additionally, players will engage in base building, fortifying their stronghold, managing resources, and making critical decisions that directly impact their group's survival. Key outcomes of this project include a fully developed game that offers an engaging and immersive experience, rigorous testing to ensure quality, and a polished final product ready for market release. By focusing on tactical thinking and resource management, "Zombie Outbreak Tactics" will not only entertain but also challenge players, making it a standout title in the gaming industry.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for "Zombie Outbreak Tactics" includes the following key components:

- **Concept Design**: Develop the game concept, including storyline, character design, and gameplay mechanics.
- **Game Development**: 
  - **Coding**: Implement the game logic, user interface, and AI for zombies and survivors.
  - **Art Creation**: Design and create 2D/3D assets, including characters, environments, and animations.
- **Sound Design**: Create immersive sound effects and background music to enhance the gaming experience.
- **Testing**: Conduct rigorous testing phases, including alpha and beta testing, to identify and fix bugs, ensure gameplay balance, and gather player feedback.
- **Marketing Strategy**: Develop a marketing plan to promote the game prior to launch, including social media campaigns and partnerships with influencers.
- **Launch Preparation**: Prepare for the game launch, including distribution channels and post-launch support.
