
# Project Plan Template: Haunted Manor Escape

## 1. Executive Summary

The Haunted Manor Escape project aims to create an immersive horror puzzle game that captivates players through a chilling atmosphere and intricate gameplay. The primary objective is to design a game that not only challenges players' critical thinking and problem-solving skills but also engages them emotionally with a compelling narrative. Players will find themselves trapped in a mysterious, haunted manor, where they must solve a series of challenging puzzles to unlock doors and ultimately escape. Each puzzle will be intricately designed to enhance the narrative, revealing pieces of the manor's dark history and the fate of its previous inhabitants. Key outcomes include the development of atmospheric visuals that evoke fear and suspense, strategically placed jump scares to maintain tension, and a polished final product that delivers a captivating gaming experience. The project encompasses all stages of game development, from initial concept design to final testing and launch, ensuring a high-quality product that resonates with players.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Concept Development**: 
  - Brainstorming sessions to outline the game's theme, mechanics, and narrative.
  - Creation of character profiles and backstory for the manor and its inhabitants.

- **Game Design**: 
  - Development of gameplay mechanics, including puzzle types and difficulty levels.
  - Design of the user interface (UI) and user experience (UX) to ensure intuitive navigation.

- **Art and Visuals**: 
  - Creation of atmospheric graphics that reflect the horror theme.
  - Development of character models, environment assets, and animations.

- **Sound Design**: 
  - Composition of an eerie soundtrack to enhance the immersive experience.
  - Implementation of sound effects for puzzles, jump scares, and ambient noises.

- **Programming**: 
  - Development of the game engine and coding of gameplay mechanics.
  - Integration of graphics, sound, and user interface elements.

- **Testing**: 
  - Conducting alpha and beta testing phases to gather player feedback.
  - Iterative improvements based on testing results to ensure a polished final product.

- **Launch and Marketing**: 
  - Development of a marketing strategy to promote the game prior to launch.
  - Coordination of the launch event and post-launch support.

- **Milestone 4**: Beta Version Release

- **Milestone 5**: Final Release
  - Deliverable: Polished final version of the game ready for launch.
