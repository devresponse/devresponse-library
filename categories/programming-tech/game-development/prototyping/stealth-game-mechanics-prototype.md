
# Project Plan Template: Stealth Game Mechanics Prototype

## 1. Executive Summary

The Stealth Game Mechanics Prototype project is designed to create a functional prototype that effectively demonstrates the key gameplay mechanics essential for a stealth-based game. The primary objective of this project is to develop a small, interactive level that showcases critical elements such as player visibility, enemy patrol patterns, and stealth takedown mechanics. By integrating basic enemy AI capable of patrolling designated paths and reacting to player actions, the prototype will provide a realistic environment for testing gameplay concepts. Additionally, stealth indicators will be incorporated to give players feedback on their visibility and proximity to enemies. This prototype serves as a vital tool for validating ideas and making necessary adjustments before moving into full-scale production. The key outcomes will include a playable prototype that highlights the core mechanics of the stealth game, offering valuable insights for further development and refinement of gameplay concepts.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Prototype Development**: Create a playable prototype that includes:
  - A small, interactive level designed for stealth gameplay.
  - Basic enemy AI with patrol patterns and reaction capabilities.
  - Player visibility mechanics that affect gameplay.
  - Stealth takedown mechanics for player interaction.
  - Integration of stealth indicators for player feedback.

- **Testing and Iteration**: Conduct playtesting sessions to gather feedback on gameplay mechanics and make necessary adjustments based on player interactions.

- **Documentation**: Provide comprehensive documentation outlining the design choices, gameplay mechanics, and feedback received during testing.

- **Final Review**: Present the prototype to stakeholders for review and gather insights for further development.
