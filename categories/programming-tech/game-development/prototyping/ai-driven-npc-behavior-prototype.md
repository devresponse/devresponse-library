
# Project Plan Template: AI-Driven NPC Behavior Prototype

## 1. Executive Summary

The AI-Driven NPC Behavior Prototype project aims to develop a functional prototype that simulates non-player character (NPC) behavior using artificial intelligence. The primary objective of this project is to create NPCs that exhibit basic decision-making capabilities, enabling them to navigate their environment effectively through advanced pathfinding algorithms. This prototype will not only focus on NPC navigation but also on their ability to interact with players and the surrounding environment, thereby enhancing the overall gameplay experience. Key outcomes of this project include a robust prototype that showcases the potential of AI-driven NPCs, providing valuable insights into player interactions and NPC behavior dynamics. By the end of this project, clients will have a testing ground for various gameplay mechanics, allowing game developers to validate their concepts and refine their designs before moving into full-scale production. This prototype will serve as a critical tool for innovation in game development, ensuring that the final product meets player expectations and enhances engagement.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct a thorough analysis of existing NPC behaviors in current games and identify areas for improvement using AI.
- **Design Phase**: Create design documents outlining the NPC behavior models, decision-making processes, and interaction mechanics.
- **Development Phase**:
  - Implement pathfinding algorithms to enable NPC navigation.
  - Develop AI models for decision-making capabilities.
  - Program interaction mechanics for NPCs to engage with players and the environment.
- **Testing Phase**: Conduct iterative testing to refine NPC behaviors and ensure they meet gameplay requirements.
- **Feedback Loop**: Gather feedback from stakeholders and potential users to make necessary adjustments.
- **Documentation**: Provide comprehensive documentation detailing the prototype's features, usage, and potential applications.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Analysis
  - Deliverable: Research report on existing NPC behaviors and proposed improvements.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of Design Documents
  - Deliverable: Design documents outlining NPC behavior models and interaction mechanics.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Development Phase
  - Deliverable: Functional prototype demonstrating NPC navigation and interaction capabilities.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Testing Phase
  - Deliverable: Testing report with feedback and adjustments made to the prototype.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Prototype Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized AI-Driven NPC Behavior Prototype along with comprehensive documentation.
