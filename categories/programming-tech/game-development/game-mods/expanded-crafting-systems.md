
# Project Plan Template: Expanded Crafting Systems

## 1. Executive Summary

The Expanded Crafting Systems project aims to significantly enhance the existing crafting mechanics within the video game {{Game Name}}. The primary objective is to provide players with a richer and more engaging experience by introducing a variety of new crafting recipes that utilize diverse materials. This enhancement will allow players to create unique items and tools, thereby fostering creativity and exploration within the game world. Key outcomes of this project include the integration of new materials sourced from different in-game environments, which will encourage players to interact more deeply with their surroundings. Additionally, we will refine the crafting mechanics to ensure a seamless user experience, potentially incorporating features such as crafting tiers, skill levels, and time-based crafting processes. By the end of this project, players will enjoy a dynamic and rewarding crafting system that not only enhances gameplay but also encourages creativity and exploration.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: 
  - Conduct a thorough analysis of the existing crafting system.
  - Gather player feedback to identify pain points and desired features.

- **Design New Crafting Recipes**: 
  - Develop a list of new crafting recipes that utilize a variety of materials.
  - Ensure recipes are balanced and enhance gameplay.

- **Integration of New Materials**: 
  - Identify new materials to be introduced into the game.
  - Design methods for players to source these materials from different environments.

- **Refinement of Crafting Mechanics**: 
  - Redesign the user interface for the crafting system.
  - Implement crafting tiers and skill levels to add depth to the crafting experience.
  - Explore time-based crafting processes to introduce strategic elements.

- **Testing and Feedback**: 
  - Conduct playtesting sessions to gather feedback on the new crafting system.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on player feedback.

- **Documentation and Training**: 
  - Create comprehensive documentation for the new crafting system.
  - Provide training materials for players to understand the new features.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Analysis
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and recommendations.

- **Milestone 2**: Design of New Crafting Recipes
  - Deliverable: Document outlining new recipes and their materials.

- **Milestone 3**: Integration of New Materials
  - Deliverable: List of new materials and their sourcing methods.

- **Milestone 4**: Refinement of Crafting Mechanics
  - Deliverable: Updated user interface and mechanics documentation.

- **Milestone 5**: Playtesting and Feedback
  - Deliverable: Playtesting report with feedback and proposed changes.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Documentation and Training Materials
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive guide for players on the new crafting system.
