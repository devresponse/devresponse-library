
# Project Plan Template: ISO 27001 Implementation

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the ISO 27001 Implementation project is to establish a robust Information Security Management System (ISMS) that complies with the ISO 27001 standard. This project aims to enhance the organization's information security posture by conducting a thorough assessment of current security practices, identifying gaps, and developing tailored policies and procedures. Key outcomes include a comprehensive risk assessment, effective asset management strategies, and a well-defined incident response plan. Additionally, we will provide training sessions to cultivate a culture of security awareness among staff, ensuring that all employees understand their roles in maintaining compliance. By the conclusion of this project, the organization will possess a fully functional ISMS that not only meets ISO 27001 requirements but also fortifies its overall cybersecurity framework, thereby reducing risks and enhancing stakeholder confidence.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a comprehensive review of current information security practices and policies.
- **Gap Analysis**: Identify discrepancies between existing practices and ISO 27001 requirements.
- **Policy Development**: Create and document policies and procedures that align with ISO 27001 standards, including:
  - Information security policy
  - Risk assessment and treatment plan
  - Asset management procedures
  - Incident response plan
- **Implementation Support**: Assist in the implementation of the developed policies and procedures.
- **Training and Awareness**: Conduct training sessions for staff to promote security awareness and compliance with the ISMS.
- **Monitoring and Review**: Establish a framework for ongoing monitoring and review of the ISMS to ensure continuous improvement and compliance.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing current practices and identified gaps.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Gap Analysis
  - Deliverable: Gap Analysis Report outlining discrepancies and recommendations.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Development of Policies and Procedures
  - Deliverable: Documented ISMS Policies and Procedures aligned with ISO 27001.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Implementation of ISMS
  - Deliverable: Implementation Plan and evidence of policy deployment.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Staff Training Sessions
  - Deliverable: Training Materials and Attendance Records.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Final Review and Handover
  - Deliverable: Final ISMS Report and Continuous Improvement Plan.
