
# Smartwatch Safety Tracker for Seniors Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Smartwatch Safety Tracker for Seniors project aims to develop a user-friendly smartwatch application tailored specifically for the elderly population. This innovative app will incorporate essential features such as fall detection, which utilizes advanced sensors to identify falls and automatically alert designated contacts or emergency services. Additionally, the app will include an emergency SOS function, allowing seniors to send immediate alerts with their location to family members or caregivers in case of an emergency. To further enhance the app's utility, medication reminders will be integrated, ensuring that users receive timely notifications for their medication schedules. The project will involve thorough research into user needs, design intuitive interfaces, and rigorous testing to ensure reliability and ease of use. Our goal is to create a comprehensive safety solution that empowers seniors to live independently while providing peace of mind to their families. Key outcomes include improved safety for seniors, enhanced communication with caregivers, and increased adherence to medication schedules.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: Conduct surveys and focus groups with seniors and caregivers to identify specific needs and preferences.
- **Design Phase**: Create wireframes and prototypes of the smartwatch application, focusing on user-friendly interfaces that cater to the elderly.
- **Development Phase**: 
  - Implement fall detection algorithms using advanced sensors.
  - Develop the emergency SOS feature, including location tracking capabilities.
  - Integrate medication reminder functionalities with customizable schedules.
- **Testing Phase**: 
  - Perform usability testing with target users to gather feedback and make necessary adjustments.
  - Conduct rigorous testing of all features to ensure reliability and accuracy.
- **Deployment Phase**: Launch the application on selected smartwatch platforms and provide user training materials.
- **Post-Launch Support**: Offer ongoing support and updates based on user feedback and technological advancements.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and user needs.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of Design Phase
  - Deliverable: Wireframes and prototypes approved by stakeholders.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Development Phase
  - Deliverable: Fully functional smartwatch application with all features implemented.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Testing Phase
  - Deliverable: Testing report detailing usability findings and adjustments made.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Application Launch
  - Deliverable: Official launch of the Smartwatch Safety Tracker for Seniors app on designated platforms.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Post-Launch Review
  - Deliverable: Review report with user feedback and recommendations for future updates.
