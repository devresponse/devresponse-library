
# Project Plan Template: Customer Feedback Collector

## 1. Executive Summary

The Customer Feedback Collector project aims to develop a sophisticated rule-based chatbot that will facilitate the collection of customer feedback through structured surveys and predefined questions. This project is designed to engage users in a conversational manner, guiding them through a series of questions tailored to gather valuable insights about their experiences with the business. The primary objective is to automate the feedback collection process, ensuring a consistent and efficient approach to understanding customer needs and preferences. Key outcomes include the design of an intuitive conversational flow, the creation of a user-friendly interface, and the integration of analytics tools to interpret the collected data. By leveraging this chatbot, businesses will gain actionable insights that can drive improvements in their products and services, ultimately enhancing customer satisfaction and loyalty.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Chatbot Design**: Develop the conversational flow of the chatbot, ensuring it is engaging and user-friendly.
- **Questionnaire Development**: Create structured surveys that include various types of feedback mechanisms such as ratings, open-ended responses, and multiple-choice questions.
- **User Interface Creation**: Design a visually appealing and intuitive interface for users to interact with the chatbot.
- **Integration of Analytics Tools**: Implement analytics tools to collect, analyze, and interpret the feedback data gathered by the chatbot.
- **Testing and Quality Assurance**: Conduct thorough testing of the chatbot to ensure functionality, usability, and reliability.
- **Deployment**: Launch the chatbot on the designated platforms (e.g., website, mobile app).
- **Training and Documentation**: Provide training for staff on how to use the chatbot and interpret the analytics, along with comprehensive documentation.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of chatbot design and conversational flow
  - Deliverable: Design document outlining the chatbot's conversation structure

- **Milestone 2**: Development of questionnaires and feedback mechanisms
  - Deliverable: Finalized list of questions and feedback types

- **Milestone 3**: User interface design completion
  - Deliverable: Prototype of the chatbot interface

- **Milestone 4**: Integration of analytics tools
  - Deliverable: Functional analytics dashboard for data interpretation

- **Milestone 5**: Testing and quality assurance phase
  - Deliverable: Testing report with identified issues and resolutions

- **Milestone 6**: Deployment of the chatbot
  - Deliverable: Live chatbot on the specified platforms

- **Milestone 7**: Training and documentation delivery
  - Deliverable: Training session completed and documentation provided
