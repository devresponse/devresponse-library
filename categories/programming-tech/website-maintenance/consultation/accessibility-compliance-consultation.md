
# Accessibility Compliance Consultation Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Accessibility Compliance Consultation project aims to empower businesses to enhance their online presence by ensuring their websites are accessible to all users, including those with disabilities. In today's digital landscape, compliance with accessibility standards is not only a legal obligation but also a moral imperative that reflects a commitment to inclusivity. This project will provide a comprehensive assessment of your current website, identifying barriers to accessibility and offering tailored recommendations based on the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG). Key outcomes include a detailed report outlining the findings of the assessment, a prioritized list of actionable recommendations, and a clear roadmap for implementation. By the conclusion of this consultation, businesses will be equipped with the knowledge and tools necessary to create a user-friendly online environment for everyone, thereby enhancing their brand reputation and fostering an inclusive digital community.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a kickoff meeting to understand the client's specific needs and objectives regarding accessibility.
- **Website Assessment**: Perform a thorough evaluation of the client's website to identify accessibility barriers, including but not limited to:
  - Visual impairments
  - Hearing impairments
  - Motor disabilities
  - Cognitive disabilities
- **Standards Review**: Analyze the website against key accessibility standards, particularly the WCAG 2.1 guidelines.
- **Reporting**: Compile findings into a detailed report that includes:
  - Identified accessibility issues
  - Severity ratings for each issue
  - Recommended solutions and best practices
- **Action Plan Development**: Create a prioritized action plan that outlines steps for remediation, including timelines and responsible parties.
- **Follow-Up Consultation**: Schedule a follow-up meeting to discuss the report and action plan, ensuring the client understands the recommendations and next steps.
