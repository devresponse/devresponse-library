## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Custom Video and Media Player project includes the following components:

- **Requirements Gathering**: Collaborate with stakeholders to identify specific needs and desired features for the media player.
- **Design Phase**: Create wireframes and prototypes that reflect the branding and functionality requirements.
- **Development**: Build the media player with advanced controls, including play, pause, volume adjustment, and navigation features.
- **Analytics Integration**: Implement analytics tools to track user engagement and performance metrics.
- **Branding Customization**: Ensure the media player reflects {{Client Name}}'s branding through color schemes, logos, and overall design aesthetics.
- **Testing and Quality Assurance**: Conduct thorough testing to identify and resolve any issues, ensuring a high-quality final product.
- **Deployment**: Launch the media player on the designated platforms and provide support during the initial rollout.
- **Post-Launch Support**: Offer ongoing maintenance and updates as needed to ensure optimal performance.
