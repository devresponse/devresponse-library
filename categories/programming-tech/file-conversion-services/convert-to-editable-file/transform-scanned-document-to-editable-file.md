
# Project Plan Template: Scanned Document to Editable File

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Scanned Document to Editable File" project aims to provide a seamless solution for converting scanned documents into editable formats such as Microsoft Word or Excel. The primary objective is to enhance productivity for businesses and individuals who frequently handle scanned documents. By employing advanced Optical Character Recognition (OCR) technology, we will meticulously analyze each scanned document to ensure that all text, images, and formatting are accurately captured and converted. The key outcomes of this project include the delivery of clean, well-structured documents that retain the original layout, enabling users to make necessary edits effortlessly. Additionally, the project will incorporate rigorous quality checks to guarantee that the converted files meet high standards of accuracy and usability. Ultimately, this service will empower clients to streamline their workflows and improve their document management processes.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Document Analysis**: Review each scanned document to assess its content, layout, and any potential challenges in conversion.
- **OCR Processing**: Utilize advanced OCR technology to extract text, images, and formatting from scanned documents.
- **Editing and Formatting**: Ensure that the extracted content is accurately formatted in the editable file, maintaining the original layout as closely as possible.
- **Quality Assurance**: Implement a multi-step quality check process to verify the accuracy and usability of the converted files.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the client with the final editable documents in the requested formats (e.g., Microsoft Word, Excel).
- **Client Feedback**: Gather feedback from the client to ensure satisfaction and make any necessary adjustments.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Document Analysis  
  **Deliverable**: Analysis report outlining the challenges and requirements for each document.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of OCR Processing  
  **Deliverable**: Initial editable files generated from the scanned documents.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Editing and Formatting  
  **Deliverable**: Well-structured editable documents ready for client review.

- **Milestone 4**: Quality Assurance Completion  
  **Deliverable**: Quality assurance report confirming the accuracy and usability of the converted files.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery  
  **Deliverable**: Final editable documents delivered to the client along with a feedback form.

## 4. Pricing and Payment Terms

- **Pricing Structure**: The pricing for the project will be based on the number of pages and complexity of the scanned documents. A detailed quote will be provided after the initial document analysis.

- **Payment Terms**: 
  - 50% upfront payment upon project initiation.
  - 50% upon final delivery of the editable files.
  
- **Additional Costs**: Any additional services requested by the client, such as expedited processing or extra revisions, will be billed separately.

- **Payment Methods**: Payments can be made via bank transfer, credit card, or other mutually agreed-upon methods.
