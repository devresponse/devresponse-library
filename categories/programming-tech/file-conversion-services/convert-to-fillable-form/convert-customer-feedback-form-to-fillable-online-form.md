
# Project Plan Template: Convert Customer Feedback Form to Fillable Online Form

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the project "Convert Customer Feedback Form to Fillable Online Form" is to transform a static customer feedback form into a dynamic, fillable online format that enhances user engagement and streamlines data collection. The current feedback form, which may exist in PDF or paper format, will be thoroughly analyzed to understand its content and structure. The new online form will be developed using user-friendly software, incorporating features such as dropdown menus, checkboxes, and text fields to facilitate an intuitive user experience. Furthermore, the form will be optimized for mobile devices, ensuring accessibility for all users. Key outcomes of this project include improved response rates, a seamless transition from a static document to an interactive online form, and enhanced data collection processes for the client. By focusing on user experience and functionality, the project aims to deliver a solution that meets the client's requirements and significantly improves the efficiency of gathering customer feedback.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Analysis of Current Form**: Review the existing customer feedback form to identify key elements and areas for improvement.
- **Design of Fillable Form**: Create a user-friendly design that includes:
  - Dropdown menus for multiple-choice questions
  - Checkboxes for selection options
  - Text fields for open-ended responses
- **Development**: Utilize appropriate software to build the fillable online form.
- **Mobile Optimization**: Ensure the form is fully responsive and accessible on mobile devices.
- **Testing**: Conduct thorough testing to verify functionality, ease of use, and compatibility across different devices and browsers.
- **Feedback and Revisions**: Gather feedback from stakeholders and make necessary revisions to the form.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the completed fillable online form to the client along with documentation on usage and maintenance.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the analysis of the current form
  - **Deliverable**: Analysis report outlining key findings and recommendations
- **Milestone 2**: Design approval of the fillable form
  - **Deliverable**: Wireframe/mockup of the proposed online form
- **Milestone 3**: Development of the fillable form
  - **Deliverable**: Functional online form ready for testing
- **Milestone 4**: Completion of testing phase
  - **Deliverable**: Testing report with identified issues and resolutions
- **Milestone 5**: Final delivery of the fillable online form
  - **Deliverable**: Completed form and usage documentation
