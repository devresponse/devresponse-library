
# Project Plan Template: Convert Employee Evaluation Form to Fillable Digital Form

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the "Convert Employee Evaluation Form to Fillable Digital Form" project is to transform a traditional paper-based employee evaluation form into a modern, user-friendly digital format. This project aims to enhance the efficiency of performance reviews by enabling employees and managers to complete evaluations electronically. The project will involve a comprehensive analysis of the existing form to identify key fields and sections that require interactivity, ensuring that the final product is fully functional and accessible online. Key outcomes include improved data accuracy, streamlined data collection for HR purposes, and a seamless user experience across various devices and platforms. By converting the evaluation form into a fillable digital format, we anticipate significant time savings and increased engagement from both employees and managers during the evaluation process.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Analysis of Existing Form**: Review the current employee evaluation form to identify key fields, sections, and areas for improvement.
- **Design Requirements**: Gather requirements from stakeholders to determine the desired features and functionalities of the digital form.
- **Prototype Development**: Create a prototype of the fillable digital form for stakeholder review and feedback.
- **Form Development**: Develop the final version of the digital form, incorporating interactivity and ensuring compatibility with various devices and platforms.
- **Testing**: Conduct thorough testing of the digital form to ensure functionality, usability, and data accuracy.
- **Deployment**: Launch the fillable digital form and provide access to employees and managers.
- **Training and Support**: Offer training sessions and support materials to assist users in navigating the new digital form.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the analysis of the existing form.
  - **Deliverable**: Analysis report detailing key fields and suggested improvements.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of design requirements.
  - **Deliverable**: Document outlining design specifications and stakeholder requirements.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Prototype development and stakeholder feedback.
  - **Deliverable**: Interactive prototype of the fillable digital form.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Development of the final digital form.
  - **Deliverable**: Fully functional fillable digital form ready for testing.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Completion of testing phase.
  - **Deliverable**: Testing report summarizing results and any necessary adjustments.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Deployment of the digital form.
  - **Deliverable**: Live access to the fillable digital form for users.
  
- **Milestone 7**: Training and support completion.
  - **Deliverable**: Training materials and support documentation provided to users.
