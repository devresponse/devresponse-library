
# Project Plan Template: Convert School Enrollment Form to Fillable Digital Form

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the "Convert School Enrollment Form to Fillable Digital Form" project is to transform a conventional paper-based school enrollment form into an interactive, fillable digital format. This project aims to streamline the registration process for both parents and school administrators, enhancing efficiency and accuracy in data collection. By creating a user-friendly digital form, we will enable parents to easily input their information, ensuring that all necessary data is collected accurately and efficiently. The key outcomes of this project include a thorough review of the existing enrollment form, the development of fillable fields, and the implementation of validation rules to maintain data integrity. Ultimately, the project will result in a digital form that can be accessed online, significantly improving the overall enrollment experience and reducing administrative workload.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Review Existing Form**: Conduct a comprehensive analysis of the current school enrollment form to identify all required fields and information.
- **Design Fillable Fields**: Create an interactive design for the fillable fields, ensuring they are user-friendly and accessible.
- **Implement Validation Rules**: Establish necessary validation rules for each field to ensure data integrity and accuracy during submission.
- **Testing**: Conduct thorough testing of the fillable form to identify any issues or areas for improvement before final deployment.
- **User Training**: Provide training materials and sessions for school administrators on how to manage and process the digital enrollment forms.
- **Deployment**: Launch the fillable digital form on the school’s website, ensuring it is easily accessible to parents.
- **Feedback Collection**: Gather feedback from users to identify any further enhancements or adjustments needed post-launch.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the review of the existing enrollment form.
  - **Deliverable**: Documented analysis report of the current form.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Design and approval of the fillable form layout.
  - **Deliverable**: Prototype of the fillable digital form.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Implementation of validation rules and testing.
  - **Deliverable**: Fully functional fillable form with validation rules in place.
  
- **Milestone 4**: User training and documentation.
  - **Deliverable**: Training materials and session completion report.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Deployment of the fillable form on the school’s website.
  - **Deliverable**: Live link to the fillable digital form.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Collection of user feedback and final adjustments.
  - **Deliverable**: Feedback report and list of adjustments made.
