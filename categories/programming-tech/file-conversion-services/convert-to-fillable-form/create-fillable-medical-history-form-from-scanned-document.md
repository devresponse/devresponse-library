
# Project Plan Template: Create Fillable Medical History Form from Scanned Document

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the "Create Fillable Medical History Form from Scanned Document" project is to transform a scanned medical history form into a fully interactive, fillable digital format. This project aims to enhance the efficiency of data collection within the clinic and improve workflow processes. By analyzing the existing scanned document, we will identify key fields that require user input, ensuring that the final fillable form is user-friendly and accessible across various devices. Key outcomes include increased patient engagement, reduced administrative burden on clinic staff, and seamless integration of collected data into the clinic's data management system. This transformation will not only streamline operations but also ensure compliance with relevant data protection regulations, ultimately leading to improved operational efficiency and patient satisfaction.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Document Analysis**: Review the scanned medical history form to identify all necessary fields for user input, including text fields, checkboxes, and dropdown menus.
- **Field Design**: Create a list of all fields that need to be included in the fillable form, ensuring they are intuitive and easy to navigate.
- **Form Development**: Utilize appropriate software tools to develop the fillable form, ensuring it is compatible with various devices (desktop, tablet, mobile).
- **User Testing**: Conduct user testing sessions with clinic staff and patients to gather feedback on the form's usability and accessibility.
- **Revisions**: Implement necessary revisions based on feedback received during user testing to enhance the form's functionality.
- **Integration**: Ensure that the fillable form can be seamlessly integrated into the clinic's existing data management system for efficient data collection and storage.
- **Compliance Check**: Verify that the final product adheres to relevant data protection regulations to safeguard patient information.
- **Training**: Provide training sessions for clinic staff on how to use the new fillable form and integrate it into their workflow.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Document Analysis  
  **Deliverable**: Document analysis report outlining identified fields and design considerations.

- **Milestone 2**: Field Design Approval  
  **Deliverable**: Approved list of fields and design mockups for the fillable form.

- **Milestone 3**: Form Development Completion  
  **Deliverable**: Fully developed fillable medical history form ready for testing.

- **Milestone 4**: User Testing Completion  
  **Deliverable**: User testing report with feedback and suggested revisions.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Form Revision  
  **Deliverable**: Revised fillable form incorporating user feedback.

- **Milestone 6**: Integration and Compliance Check  
  **Deliverable**: Confirmation of successful integration and compliance with data protection regulations.

- **Milestone 7**: Staff Training Completion  
  **Deliverable**: Training materials and completion report for clinic staff training sessions.
