
# Mobile App Feature Prioritization Workshop Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Mobile App Feature Prioritization Workshop aims to facilitate a collaborative environment where key stakeholders, including product managers, developers, and marketing teams, can come together to identify and prioritize essential features and updates for the mobile application. The primary objective of this workshop is to analyze user feedback and current market trends while aligning our findings with the overarching business objectives of {{Client Name}}. By employing prioritization techniques such as the MoSCoW method and the Kano model, we will ensure that the focus remains on features that provide the highest value to both users and the business. Key outcomes of the workshop will include a comprehensive roadmap of prioritized features, actionable insights, and strategic recommendations for implementation. This structured approach will not only enhance the user experience but also ensure that the mobile app remains competitive in a rapidly evolving market.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Mobile App Feature Prioritization Workshop includes the following key activities:

- **Pre-Workshop Preparation:**
  - Conduct stakeholder interviews to gather initial insights.
  - Collect and analyze existing user feedback and market research data.
  - Prepare workshop materials, including presentations and prioritization frameworks.

- **Workshop Facilitation:**
  - Introduce participants to the workshop objectives and agenda.
  - Present findings from the pre-workshop analysis.
  - Facilitate discussions using prioritization techniques (MoSCoW and Kano model).
  - Encourage collaborative brainstorming to generate new feature ideas.

- **Post-Workshop Deliverables:**
  - Compile a comprehensive report summarizing workshop discussions and outcomes.
  - Develop a prioritized feature roadmap with actionable insights.
  - Provide recommendations for implementation strategies.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1:** Pre-Workshop Preparation Completed
  - Deliverable: Stakeholder interview summaries and analysis report.

- **Milestone 2:** Workshop Conducted
  - Deliverable: Workshop agenda and participant feedback forms.

- **Milestone 3:** Post-Workshop Report Delivered
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report including prioritized feature roadmap and recommendations.
