
# Project Plan Template: Fix App Notifications Not Displaying on Lock Screen

## 1. Executive Summary

The primary objective of the "Fix App Notifications Not Displaying on Lock Screen" project is to diagnose and resolve the critical issue of mobile app notifications failing to appear on users' lock screens. In today's fast-paced digital environment, timely notifications are essential for maintaining user engagement and satisfaction. This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of the app's notification settings, a thorough review of the codebase for potential bugs, and extensive testing across various devices and operating systems to replicate the issue. Key outcomes include the restoration of reliable notification functionality on lock screens, a detailed report outlining findings and proposed solutions, and implementation steps to rectify the identified issues. Additionally, the freelancer will provide best practice guidelines for notification management to prevent future occurrences. Ultimately, this project aims to enhance the overall user experience and engagement with the mobile application, ensuring that users receive timely and relevant notifications.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Analysis of Notification Settings**: Review the app's current notification settings to identify any misconfigurations or issues that may prevent notifications from appearing on the lock screen.
  
- **Codebase Review**: Conduct a thorough examination of the app's codebase to identify potential bugs or errors affecting notification delivery and display.

- **Cross-Device Testing**: Test the app on various mobile devices and operating systems to replicate the issue of notifications not displaying on the lock screen.

- **Documentation of Findings**: Compile a detailed report of the analysis, including identified issues, potential causes, and recommendations for fixes.

- **Proposed Solutions**: Develop a set of proposed solutions to address the identified issues, including code modifications and configuration changes.

- **Implementation Steps**: Outline clear steps for implementing the proposed solutions, including any necessary testing and validation processes.

- **Best Practices Guidance**: Provide guidance on best practices for notification management to ensure optimal functionality and prevent future issues.
