
# Project Plan: Squarespace Website Accessibility Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

The Squarespace Website Accessibility Audit project aims to ensure that your website is fully accessible to all users, including those with disabilities. In today's digital landscape, accessibility is not just a best practice; it is a legal requirement and a moral obligation. This project will involve a comprehensive audit of your Squarespace website, focusing on compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG). Our expert team will meticulously analyze your site to identify potential barriers that may hinder user experience for individuals with visual, auditory, motor, or cognitive disabilities. 

The key outcomes of this project include a detailed report that outlines identified accessibility issues, along with actionable recommendations for improvements. By enhancing your website's inclusivity, we aim to ensure that all users can navigate and interact with your content seamlessly. By the end of this audit, you will have a clear understanding of your site's accessibility status and the necessary steps to achieve compliance, ultimately fostering a more inclusive online environment.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with stakeholders to understand specific accessibility concerns and objectives.
  
- **Website Analysis**: Perform a thorough review of the Squarespace website, focusing on:
  - Visual elements (color contrast, text size, alt text for images)
  - Auditory elements (transcripts for audio content, captions for videos)
  - Navigation (keyboard accessibility, logical structure)
  - Cognitive considerations (clear language, consistent layout)

- **Compliance Assessment**: Evaluate the website against WCAG 2.1 standards to identify areas of non-compliance.

- **Reporting**: Compile a detailed report that includes:
  - Summary of findings
  - Specific accessibility issues identified
  - Prioritized recommendations for remediation
  - Best practices for ongoing accessibility maintenance

- **Follow-Up Consultation**: Present findings to stakeholders and discuss the implementation of recommendations.
