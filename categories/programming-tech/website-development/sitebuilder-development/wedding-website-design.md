
# Project Plan Template: Wedding Website Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Wedding Website Design project aims to create a bespoke online platform that serves as a central hub for all wedding-related information. The primary objective is to design a user-friendly website that reflects the couple's unique style and preferences while providing essential features such as RSVP functionality, detailed event information, photo galleries, and a gift registry. Key outcomes include a visually appealing website that enhances guest engagement, simplifies the RSVP process, and facilitates gift-giving. By following a structured timeline with regular updates and feedback sessions, we will ensure that the final product aligns with the couple's vision and meets their expectations. This project not only aims to streamline wedding planning but also to create a memorable online experience for guests.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Website Design and Development**
  - Create a custom design that reflects the couple's style and preferences.
  - Develop a responsive website compatible with various devices (desktop, tablet, mobile).
  
- **RSVP Functionality**
  - Implement an RSVP form for guests to confirm their attendance.
  - Include options for meal preferences and additional comments.

- **Event Information**
  - Provide detailed information about the wedding ceremony and reception, including date, time, and location.
  - Include maps and directions to the venue.

- **Photo Galleries**
  - Create sections for photo galleries to showcase engagement photos and wedding moments.
  - Allow guests to upload their photos post-event.

- **Gift Registry Integration**
  - Integrate a gift registry feature to streamline the gift-giving process.
  - Provide links to various retailers for guest convenience.

- **Regular Updates and Feedback**
  - Schedule regular meetings with the couple to provide updates and gather feedback.
  - Ensure alignment with the couple's vision throughout the project.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Project initiation meeting and requirements gathering.
  
- **Milestone 2: Design Mockups**
  - Deliverable: Presentation of initial design mockups for feedback.

- **Milestone 3: Development Phase**
  - Deliverable: Completion of website development, including all features.

- **Milestone 4: Testing and Quality Assurance**
  - Deliverable: Conduct thorough testing to ensure functionality and user experience.

- **Milestone 5: Final Review and Launch**
  - Deliverable: Final review meeting with the couple and website launch.
