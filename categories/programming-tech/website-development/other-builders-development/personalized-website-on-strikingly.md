
# Project Plan: Personalized Website on Strikingly

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Personalized Website on Strikingly project is to create a unique and engaging online presence for individuals or small businesses. This project aims to leverage the Strikingly platform to deliver a fully functional website that reflects the client's branding and meets their specific needs. The project will begin with initial consultations to gather detailed requirements, followed by the development of design drafts for client feedback. Key outcomes include the integration of custom forms for lead generation and seamless social media connections to enhance audience engagement. By the end of this project, the client will receive a visually appealing website that not only showcases their brand identity but also serves as a powerful tool for customer interaction and business growth. The structured approach ensures that all client specifications are met, resulting in a final product that stands out in a competitive digital landscape.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct meetings with the client to understand their vision, branding, and specific requirements for the website.
  
- **Design Drafts**: Create initial design drafts based on the gathered requirements, incorporating client branding elements and preferred aesthetics.

- **Client Feedback**: Present design drafts to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments based on their input.

- **Website Development**: Build the website on the Strikingly platform, ensuring all features are implemented as per the agreed design.

- **Integration of Custom Forms**: Develop and integrate custom forms for lead generation and customer inquiries to facilitate user engagement.

- **Social Media Integration**: Ensure seamless integration of social media platforms to enhance connectivity and promote audience interaction.

- **Testing and Quality Assurance**: Conduct thorough testing of the website to ensure functionality, responsiveness, and user experience.

- **Final Review and Launch**: Present the final website to the client for approval and make any last-minute adjustments before the official launch.

- **Post-Launch Support**: Provide a specified period of post-launch support to address any issues or additional requests from the client.
