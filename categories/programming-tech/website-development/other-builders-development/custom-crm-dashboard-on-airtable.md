
# Project Plan Template: Custom CRM Dashboard on Airtable

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Custom CRM Dashboard on Airtable project is to develop a tailored Customer Relationship Management (CRM) solution that aligns with the unique business processes of {{Client Name}}. This project aims to create an intuitive and user-friendly dashboard that not only enhances the management of customer relationships but also streamlines operational workflows through automation. Key outcomes include the integration of advanced data visualization tools for insightful analysis of customer interactions and business metrics, as well as the incorporation of team collaboration features to improve communication among team members. By the end of this project, {{Client Name}} will have a powerful tool that empowers them to make data-driven decisions, enhances productivity, and ultimately drives business growth.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Requirements Gathering**: Conduct meetings with stakeholders to gather detailed requirements and understand the specific needs of {{Client Name}}.
- **Dashboard Design**: Create wireframes and design prototypes for the CRM dashboard, ensuring alignment with the client's branding and user experience expectations.
- **Airtable Setup**: Configure Airtable to support the custom dashboard, including the creation of necessary tables, fields, and relationships.
- **Workflow Automation**: Develop automated workflows within Airtable to streamline operations, including task management, notifications, and data entry processes.
- **Data Visualization**: Implement advanced data visualization tools to provide insights into customer interactions and key business metrics.
- **Team Collaboration Tools**: Integrate collaboration features such as comments, mentions, and shared views to enhance team communication.
- **User Training**: Provide training sessions for {{Client Name}}’s team to ensure they are proficient in using the new dashboard.
- **Testing and Feedback**: Conduct thorough testing of the dashboard and gather feedback from stakeholders to make necessary adjustments.
- **Final Delivery**: Deliver the completed CRM dashboard, along with documentation and support resources.
