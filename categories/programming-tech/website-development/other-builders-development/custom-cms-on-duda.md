
# Project Plan Template: Custom CMS on Duda

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom CMS on Duda project aims to develop a tailored content management system that empowers businesses to create and manage their online presence effortlessly. The primary objective is to deliver a user-friendly platform that allows clients to update their website content without requiring extensive technical knowledge. This project will leverage Duda's unique design capabilities, providing advanced design options that enable businesses to stand out in a competitive digital landscape. Key outcomes include a fully functional CMS that meets the specific needs of the client, enhanced team collaboration tools for seamless communication, and a visually appealing interface that enhances user experience for both administrators and end-users. By focusing on intuitive design and ease of use, this project will ultimately contribute to the client's success in managing their online content effectively.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Conduct a kickoff meeting with stakeholders to define project goals and expectations.
  - Gather requirements through interviews and surveys with key stakeholders.

- **Design Phase**
  - Create wireframes and mockups for the CMS interface.
  - Develop a design prototype that showcases advanced design options available on the Duda platform.

- **Development Phase**
  - Set up the Duda environment and configure the CMS according to the agreed specifications.
  - Implement core features, including:
    - User-friendly content editing tools.
    - Advanced design customization options.
    - Team collaboration features for workflow management.
  
- **Testing Phase**
  - Conduct thorough testing of the CMS to ensure functionality, usability, and performance.
  - Gather feedback from stakeholders and make necessary adjustments.

- **Deployment Phase**
  - Launch the CMS on the client's domain.
  - Provide training sessions for administrators and end-users on how to utilize the CMS effectively.

- **Post-Launch Support**
  - Offer ongoing support and maintenance for a specified period.
  - Collect user feedback for future enhancements.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1:** Project Kickoff

- **Milestone 4:** Testing Sign-off
  - Deliverable: Testing report and feedback documentation.

- **Milestone 5:** CMS Launch
  - Deliverable: Live CMS on the client's domain.

- **Milestone 6:** Training Completion
  - Deliverable: Training materials and session completion report.

- **Milestone 7:** Post-Launch Review
  - Deliverable: User feedback report and recommendations for future enhancements.
