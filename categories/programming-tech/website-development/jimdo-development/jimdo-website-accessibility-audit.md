
# Jimdo Website Accessibility Audit Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Jimdo Website Accessibility Audit project aims to ensure that your website is fully accessible to all users, including those with disabilities. In today's digital landscape, accessibility is not only a legal requirement but also a moral imperative that enhances user experience and broadens audience reach. This project will involve a comprehensive audit of your Jimdo website, assessing it against established standards such as the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG). Our expert team will meticulously evaluate the website's design, content, and functionality to identify any barriers that may hinder accessibility. The key outcomes of this project include a detailed report outlining our findings and actionable recommendations for improvements, as well as the implementation of necessary adjustments to enhance accessibility. By the conclusion of this project, your Jimdo website will be more inclusive, user-friendly, and compliant with accessibility regulations, ultimately improving the experience for all visitors and fostering a more equitable online environment.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with stakeholders to understand specific accessibility concerns and goals.
- **Accessibility Audit**: Perform a thorough evaluation of the website, including:
  - Design elements (color contrast, font size, etc.)
  - Content structure (headings, lists, alt text for images)
  - Functional aspects (keyboard navigation, form accessibility)
- **Compliance Assessment**: Measure the website against WCAG standards to identify compliance gaps.
- **Reporting**: Create a comprehensive report detailing:
  - Findings from the audit
  - Specific areas of non-compliance
  - Recommendations for improvements
- **Implementation Support**: Provide guidance and support for implementing recommended changes, including:
  - Adjustments to design and content
  - Testing for accessibility post-implementation
- **Final Review**: Conduct a follow-up assessment to ensure all changes meet accessibility standards.
