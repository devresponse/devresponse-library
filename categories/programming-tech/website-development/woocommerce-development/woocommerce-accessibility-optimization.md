
# WooCommerce Accessibility Optimization Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The WooCommerce Accessibility Optimization project aims to enhance the accessibility of your eCommerce platform, ensuring compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) and creating an inclusive shopping experience for all users, including those with disabilities. In today's digital landscape, accessibility is not just a legal requirement but a vital component of customer satisfaction and business growth. This project will begin with a comprehensive audit of your current WooCommerce store to identify accessibility barriers. Key outcomes will include improved keyboard navigation, enhanced color contrast, and the provision of alternative text for images. By implementing these changes, we aim to broaden your customer base and ensure that every user can navigate and purchase with ease. Ultimately, this project will not only meet legal standards but also foster a more inclusive environment that reflects your commitment to accessibility.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Accessibility Audit**: Conduct a thorough assessment of the current WooCommerce store to identify areas of non-compliance with WCAG.
- **Recommendations Report**: Provide a detailed report outlining the findings from the audit, including specific recommendations for improvements.
- **Implementation of Changes**:
  - Improve keyboard navigation to ensure all functionalities are accessible without a mouse.
  - Enhance color contrast across the website to meet accessibility standards.
  - Add alternative text for all images to provide context for screen readers.
  - Ensure that forms are accessible and properly labeled.
  - Test the website with various assistive technologies to ensure compatibility.
- **User Testing**: Conduct user testing sessions with individuals who have disabilities to gather feedback on the implemented changes.
- **Final Review and Adjustments**: Make necessary adjustments based on user feedback and conduct a final review to ensure all accessibility standards are met.
- **Documentation and Training**: Provide documentation on the changes made and offer training for staff on maintaining accessibility standards moving forward.
