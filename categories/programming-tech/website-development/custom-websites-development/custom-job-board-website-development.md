
# Custom Job Board Website Development Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Job Board Website Development project aims to create a fully functional and user-friendly platform tailored to the specific needs of {{Client Name}}. The primary objective is to develop a unique job board where employers can effortlessly post job openings and job seekers can submit their resumes with ease. - **Testing Phase**:
  - Conduct thorough testing, including functional, usability, and performance testing.
  - Gather feedback from stakeholders and make necessary adjustments.
- **Deployment**:
