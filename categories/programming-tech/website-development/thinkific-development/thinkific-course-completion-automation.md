
# Thinkific Course Completion Automation Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Thinkific Course Completion Automation project is designed to enhance the online learning experience by automating key workflows within the Thinkific platform. In today's competitive online education landscape, it is essential to provide a seamless and engaging learning journey for students. This project aims to streamline the course completion process by implementing automated triggers that send congratulatory emails to learners upon successful course completion. This not only fosters a sense of achievement but also encourages further engagement with additional content. Furthermore, we will establish mechanisms to unlock subsequent courses or content based on specific completion criteria, ensuring that learners are guided smoothly through their educational pathways. Our dedicated team will collaborate closely with you to understand your unique requirements and tailor the automation to fit your course structure. The key outcomes of this project include improved learner satisfaction, increased retention rates, and a significant reduction in manual administrative tasks. By the end of this project, you will have a fully functional automation system that enhances the overall learning experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Requirements Gathering**: Conduct meetings with stakeholders to gather specific requirements for the automation process.
- **System Design**: Create a detailed design document outlining the automation workflows, including triggers for congratulatory emails and content unlocking mechanisms.
- **Implementation**: Develop and configure the automation workflows within the Thinkific platform.
- **Testing**: Perform thorough testing of the automation system to ensure all workflows function as intended and meet the specified requirements.
- **Training**: Provide training sessions for your team on how to manage and utilize the new automation features effectively.
- **Launch**: Deploy the automation system and monitor its performance during the initial rollout phase.
- **Post-Launch Support**: Offer ongoing support for a specified period to address any issues or questions that may arise.
