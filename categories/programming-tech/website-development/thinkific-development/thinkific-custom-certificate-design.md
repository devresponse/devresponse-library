
# Thinkific Custom Certificate Design Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Thinkific Custom Certificate Design project aims to create bespoke course completion certificates for clients utilizing the Thinkific platform. The primary objective is to design visually appealing and professionally branded certificates that reflect the unique identity of each client's educational offerings. This project will begin with an initial consultation to gather essential information regarding the client's branding guidelines, including color schemes, logos, and typography preferences. Following this, our design team will produce several mockups for client review, ensuring alignment with their vision. The final approved design will be implemented within the Thinkific platform, making the certificates easily accessible for students upon course completion. Key outcomes of this project include enhanced course offerings, improved student satisfaction, and increased engagement, ultimately contributing to the client's brand reputation and educational impact.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss branding guidelines, including:
  - Color schemes
  - Logos
  - Typography preferences
  - Overall design vision

- **Design Mockups**: Create multiple design mockups based on the consultation, including:
  - At least three distinct certificate designs
  - Variations in layout and style to provide options

- **Client Review**: Present the mockups to the client for feedback and revisions, including:
  - Collecting client input on design elements
  - Making necessary adjustments based on feedback

- **Final Design Approval**: Finalize the design based on client feedback and obtain formal approval.

- **Implementation**: Integrate the approved certificate design into the Thinkific platform, ensuring:
  - Certificates are automatically generated upon course completion
  - Accessibility for students in their course dashboard

- **Testing**: Conduct thorough testing to ensure:
  - Certificates display correctly
  - All branding elements are accurately represented

- **Training and Support**: Provide the client with guidance on how to manage and issue certificates within Thinkific.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed

- **Milestone 2**: Design Mockups Created

- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approved

- **Milestone 5**: Implementation Completed
  - Deliverable: Certificates integrated into the Thinkific platform

- **Milestone 6**: Testing Completed

- **Milestone 7**: Training and Support Provided
