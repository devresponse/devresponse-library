
# Project Plan Template: Thinkific Student Dashboard Customization

## 1. Executive Summary

The Thinkific Student Dashboard Customization project aims to enhance the usability and engagement of the student learning experience on the Thinkific platform. The primary objective is to tailor the dashboard to better meet the needs of learners, ensuring it is intuitive and user-friendly. This project will involve a comprehensive redesign of the dashboard layout, improved navigation, and the integration of additional features that facilitate easier access to course materials, progress tracking, and communication with instructors. By focusing on user experience, we anticipate a significant increase in student satisfaction and retention rates. Key outcomes include a fully customized dashboard that aligns with the client's vision, improved user engagement metrics, and positive feedback from students regarding their learning experience. The project will follow a structured approach, including initial consultations to gather requirements, design mockups for client approval, and final implementation of the customized dashboard. Regular feedback loops will be established to ensure alignment with the client's expectations throughout the project lifecycle.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct meetings with stakeholders to gather requirements and understand the specific needs of students and instructors.
  
- **Research and Analysis**: Analyze existing dashboard usage data and gather feedback from current users to identify pain points and areas for improvement.

- **Design Mockups**: Create design mockups of the proposed dashboard layout, including navigation improvements and new feature integrations.

- **Client Approval**: Present design mockups to the client for feedback and approval before proceeding to development.

- **Development**: Implement the approved designs into the Thinkific platform, ensuring all features are functional and user-friendly.

- **Testing**: Conduct thorough testing of the customized dashboard to identify any issues or areas for further enhancement.

- **Feedback Loop**: Establish regular feedback sessions with the client and select users to gather insights and make necessary adjustments.

- **Final Implementation**: Launch the customized dashboard for all students, ensuring a smooth transition and providing support as needed.

- **Post-Launch Review**: Conduct a review session with stakeholders to assess the project's success and gather feedback for future improvements.
