
# Project Plan Template: Thinkific Course Previews and Free Trials

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Thinkific Course Previews and Free Trials" project aims to enhance the user acquisition strategy by implementing course preview and free trial options on the Thinkific platform. In today's competitive online education landscape, it is essential to attract potential students effectively. This project will enable prospective learners to experience course content before making a purchase decision, thereby increasing engagement and conversion rates. The key objectives include configuring course settings for previews, designing promotional content that resonates with the target audience, and integrating free trial options that encourage sign-ups. By providing a seamless onboarding experience, we aim to align the setup with your branding and ensure user-friendliness. The anticipated outcomes include increased user engagement, higher conversion rates, and a more robust online presence in the educational market.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Course Preview Configuration**: 
  - Set up course settings on the Thinkific platform to enable previews for selected courses.
  - Determine the duration and content of the previews to maximize user interest.

- **Promotional Content Design**: 
  - Create engaging promotional materials, including graphics, videos, and copy that highlight course benefits.
  - Develop a marketing strategy to promote the previews and free trials across various channels.

- **Free Trial Integration**: 
  - Implement free trial options for courses, allowing users to access course materials for a limited time.
  - Configure automated email sequences to nurture leads during the trial period.

- **User Experience Testing**: 
  - Conduct user testing to ensure the setup is intuitive and aligns with branding.
  - Gather feedback from users to make necessary adjustments before the official launch.

- **Launch and Monitor**: 
  - Officially launch the course previews and free trial options.
  - Monitor user engagement and conversion metrics post-launch to assess effectiveness.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Course Preview Configuration
  - Deliverable: Configured course settings for previews on the Thinkific platform.

- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of Promotional Content
  - Deliverable: Completed promotional materials ready for distribution.

- **Milestone 3**: Integration of Free Trial Options
  - Deliverable: Fully functional free trial setup with automated email sequences.

- **Milestone 4**: User Experience Testing Completion
  - Deliverable: User feedback report and adjustments made based on findings.

- **Milestone 5**: Official Launch
  - Deliverable: Launch announcement and promotional campaign executed.

- **Milestone 6**: Post-Launch Monitoring
  - Deliverable: Report on user engagement and conversion metrics.
