
# Thinkific Custom Onboarding Process Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Thinkific Custom Onboarding Process project aims to create a tailored onboarding experience for new students enrolling in online courses. The primary objective of this project is to enhance the initial engagement of students with the Thinkific platform, ensuring they have a clear understanding of how to navigate their learning environment effectively. This project will involve a thorough analysis of the current onboarding procedures, identifying areas for improvement, and developing a comprehensive onboarding workflow. Key outcomes include the creation of welcome emails, instructional videos, and interactive tutorials that will guide students through their first steps. Additionally, we will implement feedback mechanisms to continuously enhance the onboarding experience based on student input. By the end of this project, new students will feel welcomed, informed, and ready to engage with their courses, leading to higher retention rates and improved learning outcomes.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Analysis of Current Onboarding Procedures**: Review existing onboarding materials and processes to identify strengths and weaknesses.
- **Stakeholder Interviews**: Conduct interviews with current students and instructors to gather insights on their onboarding experiences.
- **Development of Onboarding Workflow**: Create a step-by-step onboarding process that includes:
  - Welcome emails that introduce students to the platform.
  - Instructional videos that demonstrate key features of Thinkific.
  - Interactive tutorials that engage students in hands-on learning.
- **Feedback Mechanisms**: Design and implement surveys and feedback forms to gather student input on the onboarding experience.
- **Pilot Testing**: Run a pilot of the new onboarding process with a select group of students to gather initial feedback and make necessary adjustments.
- **Final Implementation**: Roll out the refined onboarding process to all new students.
- **Monitoring and Evaluation**: Establish metrics to evaluate the effectiveness of the onboarding process and make ongoing improvements based on student feedback.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the analysis of current onboarding procedures.
  - **Deliverable**: Report summarizing findings and recommendations.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of stakeholder interviews.
  - **Deliverable**: Summary of insights gathered from interviews.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Development of the new onboarding workflow.
  - **Deliverable**: Comprehensive onboarding plan including emails, videos, and tutorials.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Implementation of feedback mechanisms.
  - **Deliverable**: Feedback forms and survey templates.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Completion of pilot testing.
  - **Deliverable**: Pilot test report with feedback and adjustments.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Final implementation of the onboarding process.
  - **Deliverable**: Fully operational onboarding process for new students.
  
- **Milestone 7**: Monitoring and evaluation of the onboarding process.
  - **Deliverable**: Evaluation report with metrics and recommendations for future improvements.
