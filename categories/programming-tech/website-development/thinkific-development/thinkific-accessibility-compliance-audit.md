
# Thinkific Accessibility Compliance Audit Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Thinkific Accessibility Compliance Audit aims to evaluate online courses and pages hosted on the Thinkific platform for adherence to established accessibility standards, specifically the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG). This project will provide a comprehensive review of your Thinkific platform, identifying areas that may not meet accessibility requirements. Our objectives include assessing course content, navigation, multimedia elements, and overall user experience to ensure inclusivity for all learners, including those with disabilities. Key outcomes of this audit will include a detailed report outlining our findings, actionable recommendations for improvements, and a roadmap for enhancing the accessibility of your online courses. By prioritizing accessibility, you not only expand your reach but also demonstrate a commitment to inclusive education, ensuring that your content is usable by a diverse audience and compliant with legal standards.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with stakeholders to understand specific needs and expectations regarding accessibility.
- **Platform Review**: Perform a thorough review of the Thinkific platform, including:
  - Course content (text, images, documents)
  - Navigation structure and usability
  - Multimedia elements (videos, audio)
  - Overall user experience
- **Compliance Assessment**: Evaluate the identified areas against WCAG standards to determine compliance levels.
- **Reporting**: Compile findings into a comprehensive report that includes:
  - Summary of compliance status
  - Detailed analysis of identified issues
  - Prioritized recommendations for improvements
- **Follow-up Consultation**: Present findings to stakeholders and discuss actionable steps for implementation.
