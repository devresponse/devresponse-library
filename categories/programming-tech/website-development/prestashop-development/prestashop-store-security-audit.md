
# Prestashop Store Security Audit Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Prestashop Store Security Audit project is to conduct a comprehensive evaluation of the security measures in place for your online store. In today's digital landscape, the protection of business and customer data is of utmost importance. This project aims to identify potential vulnerabilities within your Prestashop store that could be exploited by malicious actors. Our expert team will perform a detailed analysis of the store's architecture, configurations, and third-party modules, ensuring that all security protocols are assessed against industry best practices. Key outcomes of this project include a thorough understanding of your store's security posture, a detailed report of identified vulnerabilities, and actionable recommendations for enhancing security measures. By the end of this audit, you will have a clear roadmap for improving your store's defenses, ultimately providing peace of mind to both you and your customers.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with stakeholders to understand specific security concerns and objectives.
- **Architecture Review**: Analyze the overall structure of the Prestashop store, including server configurations and hosting environment.
- **Configuration Assessment**: Review the settings and configurations of the Prestashop platform to ensure they align with security best practices.
- **Third-Party Module Evaluation**: Assess all installed third-party modules for known vulnerabilities and compliance with security standards.
- **Security Protocol Evaluation**: Evaluate existing security protocols, including SSL certificates, firewalls, and access controls.
- **Vulnerability Scanning**: Conduct automated and manual scans to identify potential security weaknesses.
- **Compliance Check**: Ensure that the store complies with relevant regulations and standards (e.g., GDPR, PCI-DSS).
- **Reporting**: Compile findings into a comprehensive report detailing vulnerabilities, risks, and recommendations for remediation.
- **Follow-Up Consultation**: Present findings to stakeholders and discuss actionable steps for enhancing security.
