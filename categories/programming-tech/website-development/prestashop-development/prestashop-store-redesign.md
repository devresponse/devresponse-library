
# Prestashop Store Redesign Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The **Prestashop Store Redesign** project aims to completely overhaul an existing online store to enhance its visual appeal, usability, and overall user experience. The primary objective is to conduct a thorough analysis of the current store layout, identifying key areas for improvement in navigation, product presentation, and the checkout process. By incorporating modern design principles, the redesigned store will not only be visually attractive but also user-friendly across all devices. Key outcomes of this project include an optimized homepage layout, refined product pages, an enhanced shopping cart experience, and full responsiveness of the site. Furthermore, we will implement best practices for SEO and performance optimization to increase visitor traffic and conversion rates. This project will be executed in close collaboration with the client to ensure that their vision and branding are seamlessly integrated into the new design, ultimately leading to a more engaging and effective online shopping experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Current Store Analysis**: 
  - Review existing store layout and functionality.
  - Identify pain points in navigation, product presentation, and checkout processes.

- **Design Planning**:
  - Develop wireframes and mockups for the new store layout.
  - Incorporate modern design principles and best practices for user experience.

- **Homepage Redesign**:
  - Optimize the layout for better visual appeal and usability.
  - Include featured products, promotions, and easy navigation options.

- **Product Page Enhancement**:
  - Refine product presentation with high-quality images and detailed descriptions.
  - Implement user reviews and ratings for products.

- **Shopping Cart Experience**:
  - Enhance the shopping cart interface for ease of use.
  - Streamline the checkout process to minimize cart abandonment.

- **Responsive Design Implementation**:
  - Ensure the site is fully responsive across all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile).

- **SEO and Performance Optimization**:
  - Implement best practices for on-page SEO.
  - Optimize site speed and performance metrics.

- **Client Collaboration**:
  - Regular meetings with the client to gather feedback and ensure alignment with their vision.
  - Incorporate client branding elements into the design.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current Store Analysis
  - Deliverable: Analysis report with identified areas for improvement.

- **Milestone 2**: Approval of Design Mockups
  - Deliverable: Wireframes and mockups for the new store layout.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Homepage Redesign
  - Deliverable: Fully functional redesigned homepage.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Product Page Enhancements
  - Deliverable: Redesigned product pages with optimized content.

- **Milestone 5**: Completion of Shopping Cart and Checkout Process
  - Deliverable: Enhanced shopping cart and streamlined checkout process.

- **Milestone 6**: Full Site Responsiveness Testing
  - Deliverable: Responsive design tested across multiple devices.

- **Milestone 7**: SEO and Performance Optimization
  - Deliverable: SEO report and performance metrics post-optimization.

- **Milestone 8**: Final Review and Launch
  - Deliverable: Live site with all enhancements implemented.
