
# Prestashop Store Accessibility Enhancements Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Prestashop Store Accessibility Enhancements" project aims to significantly improve the accessibility of your online store, ensuring compliance with the latest accessibility standards, specifically WCAG 2.1. This initiative is driven by the objective to create an inclusive shopping experience for all users, including those with disabilities. The project will begin with a comprehensive audit of the current store setup to identify areas that require enhancement. Key outcomes include improved navigation, enhanced color contrast, and better text readability, which will facilitate easier access and interaction for all customers. Furthermore, our team will provide thorough training and documentation to empower your staff in maintaining these enhancements. By prioritizing accessibility, we not only comply with legal standards but also expand your customer base and enhance overall user satisfaction, ultimately contributing to the growth and success of your online business.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Audit and Assessment**: Conduct a thorough audit of the current Prestashop store to identify accessibility barriers.
- **Recommendations Report**: Provide a detailed report outlining the findings from the audit and specific recommendations for enhancements.
- **Implementation of Changes**:
  - Improve navigation structures to ensure ease of use for all users.
  - Enhance color contrast across the website to meet WCAG 2.1 standards.
  - Optimize text readability, including font size, style, and spacing adjustments.
- **Testing**: Perform usability testing with diverse user groups, including individuals with disabilities, to ensure the effectiveness of the enhancements.
- **Training and Documentation**: Develop and deliver training sessions for your team, along with comprehensive documentation to support ongoing maintenance of accessibility features.
- **Final Review and Feedback**: Conduct a final review of the implemented changes and gather feedback for any additional adjustments.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the Accessibility Audit
  - Deliverable: Audit Report with Findings and Recommendations
- **Milestone 2**: Implementation of Accessibility Enhancements
  - Deliverable: Updated Prestashop Store with Enhanced Accessibility Features
- **Milestone 3**: Usability Testing Completion
  - Deliverable: Usability Testing Report
- **Milestone 4**: Training and Documentation Delivery
  - Deliverable: Training Session Completion and Documentation Provided
- **Milestone 5**: Final Review and Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Final Review Report and Feedback Summary
