
# Prestashop Store Augmented Reality (AR) Integration Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Prestashop Store Augmented Reality (AR) Integration project aims to revolutionize the online shopping experience by incorporating cutting-edge AR technology into an existing Prestashop store. The primary objective of this project is to enable customers to visualize products in their own environment using their mobile devices or computers prior to making a purchase. This innovative approach is expected to significantly enhance customer engagement, reduce return rates, and ultimately drive sales growth. Key outcomes of the project include the successful selection and implementation of suitable AR tools and frameworks, customization of the Prestashop platform to seamlessly integrate these features, and comprehensive testing to ensure optimal functionality. Additionally, the project will provide training for the client's team, empowering them to manage and update AR content effectively. By leveraging AR technology, we aim to create a more interactive and satisfying shopping experience that meets the evolving needs of today's consumers.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Conduct a kickoff meeting with stakeholders to outline project goals and expectations.
  - Define project roles and responsibilities.

- **AR Tools and Framework Selection**
  - Research and evaluate potential AR tools and frameworks suitable for integration with Prestashop.
  - Present findings and recommendations to stakeholders for approval.

- **Customization of Prestashop**
  - Develop a detailed plan for customizing the Prestashop platform to support AR features.
  - Implement the approved AR tools and frameworks into the Prestashop store.
  - Ensure compatibility with existing store functionalities.

- **Testing and Quality Assurance**
  - Conduct thorough testing of AR features across various devices and browsers.
  - Identify and resolve any issues or bugs that arise during testing.
  - Gather feedback from a select group of users to refine the AR experience.

- **Training and Documentation**
  - Develop training materials and user guides for the client's team.
  - Conduct training sessions to ensure the team is equipped to manage and update AR content.

- **Project Closure**
  - Review project outcomes against initial objectives.
  - Conduct a final meeting with stakeholders to present results and gather feedback.
  - Provide a project closure report summarizing key achievements and lessons learned.
