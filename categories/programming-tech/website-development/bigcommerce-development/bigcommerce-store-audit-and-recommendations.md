
# BigCommerce Store Audit and Recommendations Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The BigCommerce Store Audit and Recommendations project aims to enhance the performance and user experience of your e-commerce platform. In today's competitive online marketplace, it is vital for businesses to ensure their stores are optimized for both functionality and customer engagement. This project will involve a comprehensive audit of your existing BigCommerce store, focusing on critical areas such as site speed, mobile responsiveness, SEO optimization, user interface design, and overall functionality. Our expert team will conduct a thorough analysis of your store's current setup, identifying potential issues and assessing alignment with e-commerce best practices. The key outcomes of this project will include a detailed report outlining actionable recommendations tailored to your specific needs, enhancements to improve site performance, strategies to increase conversion rates, and an overall elevation of the customer experience. By the end of this project, you will possess a clear roadmap for optimizing your BigCommerce store, ultimately maximizing its potential in the digital marketplace.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Site Speed Analysis**: Evaluate the loading times of your store pages and identify bottlenecks affecting performance.
- **Mobile Responsiveness Review**: Assess the mobile version of your store to ensure it provides an optimal user experience across various devices.
- **SEO Optimization Check**: Analyze current SEO practices, including keyword usage, meta tags, and content structure, to improve search engine visibility.
- **User Interface Design Evaluation**: Review the design elements of your store to ensure they are user-friendly and visually appealing.
- **Functionality Assessment**: Test all features and functionalities of the store to identify any issues that may hinder user experience.
- **Competitor Benchmarking**: Compare your store's performance and features against key competitors in the e-commerce space.
- **Actionable Recommendations**: Provide a detailed report with prioritized recommendations for enhancements based on audit findings.
