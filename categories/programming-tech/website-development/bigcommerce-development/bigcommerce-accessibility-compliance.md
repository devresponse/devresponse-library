
# Project Plan: BigCommerce Accessibility Compliance

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, ensuring that your e-commerce store is accessible to all users, including those with disabilities, is not just a legal requirement but also a moral imperative. The objective of the BigCommerce Accessibility Compliance project is to make your online store compliant with accessibility standards such as WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines). This project will involve a comprehensive audit of your BigCommerce store to identify areas that require improvement. Our team of experts will provide actionable recommendations and implement necessary changes to enhance accessibility features, including keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility, and color contrast adjustments. Key outcomes include an expanded customer base, improved user experience, and increased customer satisfaction and loyalty. By prioritizing accessibility, your BigCommerce store will be fully compliant and ready to provide an inclusive shopping experience for all users, ensuring that no one is left behind in the digital marketplace.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a thorough audit of the current BigCommerce store to evaluate existing accessibility features and compliance with WCAG standards.
  
- **Recommendations Report**: Develop a detailed report outlining areas for improvement, including specific recommendations for enhancing accessibility features.

- **Implementation Plan**: Create a step-by-step plan for implementing the recommended changes, including timelines and resource allocation.

- **Accessibility Enhancements**: Implement necessary changes to improve accessibility, such as:
  - Enhancing keyboard navigation capabilities.
  - Ensuring compatibility with screen readers.
  - Adjusting color contrast for better visibility.
  - Adding alternative text for images and multimedia content.

- **Testing and Validation**: Perform rigorous testing to ensure that all implemented changes meet accessibility standards and function correctly across various devices and browsers.

- **Final Review and Reporting**: Conduct a final review of the BigCommerce store to confirm compliance and provide a comprehensive report detailing the changes made and their impact on accessibility.
