
# Project Plan Template: Big Cartel Custom Discount and Promotion Features

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Big Cartel Custom Discount and Promotion Features" project aims to enhance the e-commerce capabilities of your Big Cartel store by implementing tailored discount and promotion features that align with your business objectives. The primary objective is to create a seamless shopping experience that not only encourages customer engagement but also drives sales growth. This project will focus on developing tiered discount structures, enabling customers to receive varying discounts based on their purchase amounts, and implementing flash sales to create urgency and excitement around your products. Additionally, a user-friendly coupon code system will be integrated to facilitate promotional campaigns. By ensuring that these features are functional and aesthetically aligned with your brand, we aim to exceed your expectations and deliver a final product that enhances user experience and boosts overall sales performance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of this project includes the following key components:

- **Tiered Discount Structures**: Development of a system that allows for multiple discount levels based on the total purchase amount. This will encourage larger purchases and reward loyal customers.
  
- **Flash Sales Implementation**: Creation of a mechanism for time-limited sales that generate urgency among customers, encouraging them to make quick purchasing decisions.
  
- **Coupon Code System**: Integration of a user-friendly interface for customers to apply coupon codes during checkout, facilitating promotional campaigns and enhancing customer engagement.
  
- **Brand Aesthetic Alignment**: Ensuring that all new features are designed to match the existing brand aesthetic and user experience of the Big Cartel store.
  
- **Testing and Quality Assurance**: Conducting thorough testing of all new features to ensure functionality, usability, and compatibility with existing systems.
  
- **Collaboration and Feedback**: Ongoing collaboration with the client to gather feedback and make necessary adjustments throughout the project lifecycle.
