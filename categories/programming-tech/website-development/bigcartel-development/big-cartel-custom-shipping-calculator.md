
# Big Cartel Custom Shipping Calculator Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's competitive e-commerce landscape, providing customers with accurate shipping estimates is crucial for enhancing their shopping experience and reducing cart abandonment. The objective of the Big Cartel Custom Shipping Calculator project is to develop a tailored shipping calculator for your Big Cartel store that allows customers to input their location, select product weight, and choose their preferred delivery speed to receive an accurate shipping cost estimate. This project will integrate the calculator seamlessly into your existing Big Cartel platform, ensuring it aligns with your store's design and functionality. Key outcomes include improved customer satisfaction, a streamlined checkout process, and ultimately, increased sales and enhanced store performance. By implementing this feature, we aim to provide a robust solution that meets your business needs while fostering a more engaging shopping experience for your customers.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Requirements Gathering**: Collaborate with stakeholders to gather detailed requirements for the shipping calculator, including desired features and user experience.
  
- **Design**: Create wireframes and design mockups for the shipping calculator that align with the existing Big Cartel store design.

- **Development**: 
  - Develop the shipping calculator functionality, allowing users to input their location, select product weight, and choose delivery speed.
  - Integrate the calculator with the existing Big Cartel platform, ensuring compatibility and responsiveness.

- **Testing**: 
  - Conduct thorough testing to ensure the accuracy and reliability of the shipping cost estimates.
  - Perform user acceptance testing (UAT) with selected customers to gather feedback and make necessary adjustments.

- **Deployment**: Launch the custom shipping calculator on the Big Cartel store, ensuring all features are functioning as intended.

- **Training and Support**: Provide training for staff on how to manage and update the shipping calculator, along with ongoing support for any issues that may arise post-launch.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Requirements Gathering Completion
  - Deliverable: Documented requirements and feature list.

- **Milestone 4**: Testing Completion
  - Deliverable: Testing report with feedback and adjustments made.

- **Milestone 5**: Deployment
  - Deliverable: Live shipping calculator on the Big Cartel store.

- **Milestone 6**: Training and Support Initiation
