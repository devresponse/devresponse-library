
# Project Plan Template: Custom Product Page Development for Big Cartel

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Product Page Development project for Big Cartel aims to significantly enhance the online shopping experience for users by implementing advanced features that cater to modern e-commerce demands. The primary objectives of this project include the development of custom product pages that incorporate image zoom functionality, allowing customers to examine product details closely. Additionally, the integration of product videos will enable potential buyers to gain a comprehensive understanding of the products, effectively showcasing their features and benefits. Another key outcome is the incorporation of dynamic pricing, which will reflect real-time changes based on inventory levels or promotional offers, thereby encouraging conversions and increasing sales. The project will also involve rigorous testing to ensure compatibility across various devices and browsers, ensuring a seamless user experience. Ultimately, the deliverables will consist of fully functional, visually appealing product pages optimized for performance, aimed at driving higher engagement and sales for the client's e-commerce platform.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Feature Development**:
  - Implement image zoom functionality on product pages.
  - Integrate product videos for enhanced product showcasing.
  - Develop dynamic pricing features that update based on inventory and promotions.

- **Design**:
  - Create visually appealing layouts for product pages that align with the client's branding.
  - Ensure responsive design for compatibility across devices (desktop, tablet, mobile).

- **Testing**:
  - Conduct thorough testing across multiple browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
  - Perform user acceptance testing (UAT) to gather feedback and make necessary adjustments.

- **Deployment**:
  - Prepare the product pages for launch on the Big Cartel platform.
  - Provide documentation and training for the client's team on managing the new features.

- **Post-Launch Support**:
  - Offer a support period for troubleshooting and addressing any issues that arise after deployment.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Project Kickoff

- **Milestone 2**: Design Approval

- **Milestone 4**: Testing Phase
  - Deliverable: Testing report including UAT feedback and resolutions.

- **Milestone 5**: Deployment
  - Deliverable: Live product pages on the Big Cartel platform.

- **Milestone 6**: Post-Launch Review
  - Deliverable: Final project report and feedback session with the client.
