
# Project Plan: Big Cartel Analytics Dashboard Integration

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's competitive e-commerce landscape, having access to real-time data is crucial for making informed business decisions. The objective of the Big Cartel Analytics Dashboard Integration project is to seamlessly integrate a custom analytics dashboard into your Big Cartel store. This integration will empower you to monitor sales trends, customer behavior, and key performance metrics through a user-friendly interface. Key outcomes of this project include enhanced visibility into essential data points such as conversion rates, average order value, and customer demographics. Additionally, the dashboard will feature customizable reporting capabilities, allowing you to filter data by date ranges, product categories, and more. By providing you with the tools to track performance effectively, this integration will facilitate data-driven decision-making, ultimately leading to increased sales and improved customer satisfaction. Our dedicated team will ensure that the integration process is smooth, secure, and tailored to meet your specific business needs.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Requirements Gathering**: Collaborate with stakeholders to identify specific analytics needs and desired metrics.
- **Dashboard Design**: Create wireframes and design prototypes for the analytics dashboard, ensuring a user-friendly interface.
- **Data Integration**: Develop the backend integration to pull data from the Big Cartel store, ensuring data accuracy and security.
- **Custom Reporting Features**: Implement customizable reporting options, allowing users to filter data by date ranges, product categories, and other relevant metrics.
- **Testing and Quality Assurance**: Conduct thorough testing to ensure the dashboard functions correctly and meets all requirements.
- **User Training**: Provide training sessions for users to familiarize them with the dashboard features and functionalities.
- **Deployment**: Launch the analytics dashboard on the Big Cartel store, ensuring a smooth transition.
- **Post-Launch Support**: Offer ongoing support and maintenance to address any issues and implement future enhancements.
