
# Big Cartel Custom Checkout Experience Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Big Cartel Custom Checkout Experience project aims to revolutionize the checkout process of your Big Cartel store by creating a more user-friendly and efficient system. The primary objective is to redesign the existing checkout interface, integrating custom fields that allow for personalized customer input. This will not only enhance the user experience but also minimize friction during the checkout process, leading to higher conversion rates. Key outcomes of this project include a streamlined checkout process, the incorporation of diverse payment options, and a design that reflects your brand's unique identity. By focusing on these elements, we aim to increase customer satisfaction and loyalty, ultimately driving sales and fostering a positive shopping experience. The successful implementation of this project will position your Big Cartel store as a leader in customer-centric e-commerce solutions.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**
  - Conduct user research to identify pain points in the current checkout process.
  - Analyze competitor checkout experiences to gather insights and best practices.

- **Design Phase**
  - Create wireframes and prototypes for the new checkout interface.
  - Develop custom fields for personalized customer input (e.g., gift messages, special requests).
  - Ensure the design aligns with the brand's identity and aesthetic.

- **Development Phase**
  - Implement the new checkout interface on the Big Cartel platform.
  - Integrate custom fields and ensure they are functional and user-friendly.
  - Streamline the checkout steps to reduce the number of clicks and information required.

- **Payment Integration**
  - Research and integrate diverse payment options (e.g., credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay).
  - Ensure compliance with security standards for payment processing.

- **Testing and Quality Assurance**
  - Conduct thorough testing of the new checkout experience to identify and resolve any issues.
  - Gather feedback from a select group of users to refine the process.

- **Launch and Post-Launch Support**
  - Launch the new checkout experience on the Big Cartel store.
  - Provide ongoing support and monitoring to address any post-launch issues.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Research Completion**
  - Deliverable: User research report and competitor analysis findings.
  
- **Milestone 2: Design Approval**
  - Deliverable: Wireframes and prototypes for the new checkout interface approved by stakeholders.

- **Milestone 3: Development Completion**
  - Deliverable: Fully functional checkout interface with integrated custom fields and payment options.

- **Milestone 4: Testing Completion**
  - Deliverable: Testing report with identified issues and resolutions.

- **Milestone 5: Launch**
  - Deliverable: New checkout experience live on the Big Cartel store.

- **Milestone 6: Post-Launch Review**
  - Deliverable: Post-launch report detailing performance metrics and user feedback.
