
# Big Cartel Social Media Integration Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Big Cartel Social Media Integration project aims to enhance the e-commerce capabilities of your Big Cartel store by integrating it with leading social media platforms, including Instagram, Facebook, and Pinterest. In today's digital landscape, social media is not just a communication tool but a vital sales channel that can significantly boost online visibility and sales. This project will enable seamless product tagging in social media posts, allowing customers to view product details and make purchases directly from their favorite platforms. Additionally, we will implement social sharing features that encourage users to share your products with their networks, thereby increasing engagement and reach. Our approach will prioritize user-friendliness and ensure that the integration aligns with your brand's aesthetic, providing a cohesive shopping experience. By the end of this project, your Big Cartel store will be fully equipped to leverage social media as a powerful sales channel, driving traffic and increasing conversions.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Platform Selection**: Identify and confirm the social media platforms for integration (Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest).
- **Integration Development**: 
  - Develop and implement product tagging features for each selected platform.
  - Create a user-friendly interface for managing social media posts and product tags.
- **Social Sharing Features**: 
  - Implement sharing buttons on product pages to facilitate easy sharing on social media.
  - Develop customizable sharing options that reflect the brand's identity.
- **Testing and Quality Assurance**: 
  - Conduct thorough testing of all features to ensure functionality and user experience.
  - Gather feedback from a select group of users to refine the integration.
- **Training and Documentation**: 
  - Provide training sessions for your team on how to utilize the new features effectively.
  - Create comprehensive documentation outlining the integration process and best practices.
- **Launch and Post-Launch Support**: 
  - Officially launch the integrated features.
  - Offer post-launch support for a defined period to address any issues or questions.
