
# GoDaddy Custom Forms Development Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The GoDaddy Custom Forms Development project aims to enhance website functionality through the creation of tailored forms that meet specific business needs. In today's digital landscape, effective communication and data collection are paramount, and this project seeks to address those needs by developing custom forms for your GoDaddy website. The primary objectives include designing user-friendly contact forms, detailed surveys for customer feedback, and lead generation forms to capture potential clients. Key outcomes will involve improved user engagement, streamlined data collection processes, and a visually appealing interface that aligns with your branding. By collaborating closely with stakeholders, we will ensure that each form is optimized for performance and usability, ultimately enhancing the overall visitor experience and supporting your business objectives.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Requirements Gathering**: Conduct meetings with stakeholders to identify specific needs and preferences for the forms.
- **Form Design**: Create wireframes and mockups for each form, ensuring alignment with branding guidelines.
- **Development**: Build the custom forms using GoDaddy's platform, ensuring compatibility and responsiveness across devices.
- **Testing**: Perform thorough testing of each form to ensure functionality, usability, and performance.
- **Deployment**: Launch the forms on the GoDaddy website, ensuring proper integration with existing systems.
- **Training**: Provide training sessions for staff on how to manage and analyze form submissions.
- **Support and Maintenance**: Offer ongoing support for a specified period post-launch to address any issues or updates.
