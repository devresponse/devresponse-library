
# GoDaddy Website Accessibility Audit Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The GoDaddy Website Accessibility Audit project aims to ensure that your website is compliant with the latest ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act) and WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) standards. In today's digital landscape, accessibility is not merely a legal obligation but a fundamental aspect of user experience that promotes inclusivity. This project will involve a comprehensive evaluation of your website to identify any barriers that may hinder users with disabilities from accessing your content. Our expert team will conduct a detailed analysis of your site's design, navigation, content, and functionality. The key outcomes of this project include a thorough report of our findings, actionable recommendations for enhancing accessibility, and an improved user experience that broadens your audience reach. By addressing these accessibility issues, your website will not only comply with legal standards but also demonstrate a commitment to inclusivity, ultimately benefiting all users.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with stakeholders to understand specific accessibility concerns and objectives.
- **Website Evaluation**: Conduct a thorough audit of the website, including:
  - Design analysis (color contrast, font size, etc.)
  - Navigation assessment (keyboard accessibility, logical flow)
  - Content review (alt text for images, text alternatives for non-text content)
  - Functionality testing (forms, multimedia, etc.)
- **Compliance Assessment**: Evaluate the website against ADA and WCAG standards.
- **Reporting**: Compile findings into a comprehensive report that includes:
  - Identified accessibility barriers
  - Compliance status
  - Prioritized recommendations for remediation
- **Follow-up Consultation**: Present findings to stakeholders and discuss actionable steps for improvement.
