
# Simvoly Digital Agency Website Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Simvoly Digital Agency Website project is to create a professional and visually appealing online presence for a digital agency using the Simvoly platform. This website will function as a digital storefront, effectively showcasing the agency's services, past projects, and facilitating client inquiries through integrated contact forms. Key outcomes include the development of dedicated service pages that detail the agency's offerings, case studies that highlight successful projects, and client testimonials that build trust and credibility. The design will prioritize user experience and responsiveness, ensuring accessibility across various devices. Collaboration with the client will be essential to gather content, images, and branding guidelines, ensuring that the final product aligns with the agency's vision and goals. The project timeline is fixed, with clearly outlined deliverables to ensure timely completion and satisfaction of all stakeholders.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Website Design and Development**
  - Create a visually appealing homepage that reflects the agency's brand identity.
  - Develop dedicated service pages for each of the agency's offerings.
  - Implement case study pages to showcase successful projects.
  - Integrate client testimonials to enhance credibility.

- **Content Gathering and Integration**
  - Collaborate with the client to gather necessary content, including text, images, and branding guidelines.
  - Ensure all content is optimized for SEO to improve search engine visibility.

- **Functionality Implementation**
  - Set up integrated contact forms for client inquiries.
  - Ensure the website is responsive and accessible on various devices (desktop, tablet, mobile).

- **Testing and Quality Assurance**
  - Conduct thorough testing to ensure all features function correctly.
  - Perform user experience testing to gather feedback and make necessary adjustments.

- **Launch and Post-Launch Support**
  - Launch the website and monitor for any immediate issues.
  - Provide post-launch support for a specified period to address any concerns.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Project kickoff meeting and finalized project timeline.
  
- **Milestone 2: Content Collection**
  - Deliverable: Gathered content, images, and branding guidelines from the client.

- **Milestone 3: Design Mockups**
  - Deliverable: Initial design mockups for client review and feedback.

- **Milestone 4: Development Completion**
  - Deliverable: Fully developed website ready for testing.

- **Milestone 5: Testing Phase**
  - Deliverable: Completed testing report and adjustments made based on feedback.

- **Milestone 6: Website Launch**
  - Deliverable: Live website and launch announcement.

- **Milestone 7: Post-Launch Review**
  - Deliverable: Post-launch support report and feedback collection.
