
# Project Plan Template: Wix Forum Setup and Customization

## 1. Executive Summary

The Wix Forum Setup and Customization project aims to create an engaging online community through the establishment of a user-friendly forum on your Wix website. The primary objective is to foster interaction and discussion among users, enhancing the overall user experience. This project will begin with a comprehensive consultation to identify your specific needs and goals for the forum. Key outcomes include a tailored forum layout that aligns with your website's branding, the establishment of categories and threads for organized discussions, and the implementation of user permissions to facilitate smooth interactions. Additionally, we will customize the forum's visual elements, including themes, colors, and logos, to ensure it is visually appealing and consistent with your brand identity. Finally, we will provide training to equip you with the necessary skills to manage and moderate the forum effectively, ensuring that you can cultivate a vibrant and engaged community. 

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct a detailed consultation to understand the client's specific needs and objectives for the forum.
  - Gather information on desired features, branding elements, and user engagement strategies.

- **Forum Setup**:
  - Create a user-friendly forum layout that aligns with the existing website design.
  - Set up categories and threads to facilitate organized discussions.
  - Configure user permissions to ensure appropriate access levels for different user roles.

- **Customization**:
  - Implement customization options, including themes, colors, and logos, to enhance the visual appeal of the forum.
  - Ensure that the forum is responsive and accessible across various devices.

- **Training and Support**:
  - Provide training sessions on how to manage and moderate the forum effectively.
  - Offer ongoing support for a specified period post-launch to address any questions or issues.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the consultation phase
  - Deliverable: Document outlining client requirements and forum objectives.

- **Milestone 2**: Forum setup and initial configuration
  - Deliverable: Live forum with categories and threads established.

- **Milestone 3**: Customization of the forum's visual elements
  - Deliverable: Customized forum that reflects the client's branding.

- **Milestone 4**: Training and handover
  - Deliverable: Training materials and a recorded session for client reference.
