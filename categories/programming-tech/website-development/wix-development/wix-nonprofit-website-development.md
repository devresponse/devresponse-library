
# Project Plan Template: Wix Nonprofit Website Development

## 1. Executive Summary

The Wix Nonprofit Website Development project aims to create a fully functional and visually appealing website tailored specifically for nonprofit organizations. Our primary objective is to empower your nonprofit by establishing a robust digital presence that enhances engagement and drives support for your cause. The project will involve close collaboration with your team to understand your mission, values, and goals, ensuring that the website accurately reflects your organization's identity. Key outcomes include the development of user-friendly donation forms to facilitate easy contributions from supporters, volunteer sign-up forms to encourage community involvement, and mobile optimization to ensure accessibility for all users. Regular updates and feedback sessions will be integral to the process, ensuring that the final product not only meets but exceeds your expectations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a series of meetings to gather information about the nonprofit's mission, values, and specific requirements for the website.
- **Design Phase**: Create wireframes and design mockups that align with the nonprofit's branding and objectives.
- **Development Phase**: Build the website on the Wix platform, incorporating essential features such as:
  - User-friendly donation forms
  - Volunteer sign-up forms
  - Blog or news section for updates
  - Event calendar
  - Contact forms
- **Mobile Optimization**: Ensure the website is fully responsive and accessible on various devices.
- **Testing**: Conduct thorough testing to identify and resolve any issues before the website goes live.
- **Launch**: Officially launch the website and provide training to the nonprofit's staff on how to manage and update the site.
- **Post-Launch Support**: Offer ongoing support for a specified period to address any questions or issues that may arise.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Summary report of gathered requirements and objectives.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Approval of Design Mockups
  - Deliverable: Finalized design mockups for the website.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Website Development
  - Deliverable: Fully functional website on the Wix platform.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Successful Testing Phase
  - Deliverable: Testing report and resolution of identified issues.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Website Launch
  - Deliverable: Live website and training session for staff.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Post-Launch Support Period
  - Deliverable: Support documentation and follow-up report after the support period.
