
# Wix Website Accessibility Audit Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Wix Website Accessibility Audit project aims to ensure that your website is compliant with accessibility standards, thereby enhancing user experience for all visitors, including those with disabilities. In today's digital landscape, accessibility is not merely a best practice but a legal requirement in many jurisdictions. This project will involve a thorough evaluation of your Wix website against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG). Our team of experts will identify barriers that may hinder users from effectively navigating and interacting with your site. The key outcomes of this project will include a detailed report highlighting specific accessibility issues related to visual elements, navigation, and content structure, along with actionable recommendations for improvement. By investing in this audit, you are not only fulfilling legal obligations but also promoting inclusivity and ensuring that your website is user-friendly for everyone.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with stakeholders to understand specific accessibility concerns and objectives.
  
- **Website Evaluation**: Perform a comprehensive audit of the Wix website, focusing on:
  - Visual elements (color contrast, text size, alt text for images)
  - Navigation (keyboard accessibility, logical structure)
  - Content structure (headings, lists, and links)

- **Compliance Assessment**: Evaluate the website against WCAG 2.1 standards to identify areas of non-compliance.

- **Reporting**: Compile a detailed report that includes:
  - Summary of findings
  - Specific issues identified
  - Prioritized recommendations for remediation

- **Follow-Up Consultation**: Present findings to stakeholders and discuss the recommendations in detail.

- **Implementation Support**: Offer guidance on implementing recommended changes, if required.
