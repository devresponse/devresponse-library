
# Project Plan Template: Online Course Platform on Strikingly

## 1. Executive Summary

The Online Course Platform project aims to create a comprehensive and engaging learning environment utilizing the Strikingly website development platform. The primary objective is to design a visually appealing and user-friendly interface that allows educators to effectively list and manage their courses. Key outcomes include the implementation of functionalities for video hosting, enabling instructors to upload and manage course content seamlessly. Additionally, a robust student registration system will be developed to facilitate easy enrollment and track student progress. The platform will be responsive and optimized for various devices, ensuring an engaging learning experience for all users. Throughout the project, we will prioritize user experience and accessibility, ensuring that both instructors and students can navigate the platform with ease. Regular updates and feedback sessions will be scheduled to align the project with the client's vision and requirements, ultimately delivering a high-quality online course platform that meets the needs of educators and learners alike.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Platform Design and Development**
  - Create a visually appealing and intuitive user interface.
  - Ensure responsive design for optimal viewing on various devices (desktop, tablet, mobile).
  
- **Course Management Features**
  - Develop functionalities for educators to list, edit, and manage their courses.
  - Implement video hosting capabilities for course content delivery.
  
- **Student Registration and Management**
  - Design a user-friendly registration system for students.
  - Implement tracking features for student progress and course completion.
  
- **User Experience and Accessibility**
  - Conduct user testing to gather feedback on usability.
  - Ensure compliance with accessibility standards to cater to all users.
  
- **Regular Updates and Feedback Sessions**
  - Schedule bi-weekly meetings with stakeholders to review progress and gather feedback.
  - Make iterative improvements based on user feedback and project requirements.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kick-off**
  - Deliverable: Project initiation document and stakeholder agreement.
  
- **Milestone 2: Design Mockups**
  - Deliverable: Visual design mockups for the platform interface.
  
- **Milestone 3: Development Phase**
  - Deliverable: Completed platform with core functionalities (course listing, video hosting, registration).
  
- **Milestone 4: User Testing**
  - Deliverable: User feedback report and list of identified improvements.
  
- **Milestone 5: Final Launch**
  - Deliverable: Fully functional online course platform live and accessible to users.
