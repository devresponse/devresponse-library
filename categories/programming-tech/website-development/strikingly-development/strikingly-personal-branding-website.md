
# Project Plan Template: Strikingly Personal Branding Website

## 1. Executive Summary

The Strikingly Personal Branding Website project aims to create a visually stunning and highly functional online presence that effectively showcases your professional skills and achievements. In today's digital landscape, a strong personal brand is crucial for professionals seeking to differentiate themselves and attract potential clients or employers. This project will involve designing a personalized website using the Strikingly platform, which will serve as a central hub for your professional journey. Key outcomes include a user-friendly interface, mobile responsiveness, and SEO optimization, ensuring that your website is not only visually appealing but also easily discoverable. The website will feature dedicated sections for your expertise, a portfolio of your work, and testimonials from clients and colleagues, all tailored to reflect your unique personality and professional ethos. By the end of this project, you will have a polished online presence that effectively communicates your brand and engages visitors, ultimately enhancing your professional opportunities.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Conduct a kickoff meeting to understand client requirements and expectations.
  - Define project goals, target audience, and key messaging.

- **Design Phase**
  - Develop wireframes and mockups for the website layout.
  - Create a color palette and select typography that reflects the client's brand identity.
  - Design sections for expertise, portfolio, testimonials, and contact information.

- **Development Phase**
  - Build the website using the Strikingly platform.
  - Ensure mobile responsiveness and cross-browser compatibility.
  - Implement SEO best practices, including keyword optimization and meta tags.

- **Content Creation**
  - Collaborate with the client to gather content for each section of the website.
  - Write engaging copy that highlights the client's skills and achievements.
  - Collect and format testimonials from clients and colleagues.

- **Testing and Review**
  - Conduct thorough testing of the website for functionality and user experience.
  - Gather feedback from the client and make necessary revisions.

- **Launch and Training**
  - Launch the website and ensure all features are functioning correctly.
  - Provide training to the client on how to manage and update the website.

- **Post-Launch Support**
  - Offer a support period for troubleshooting and minor adjustments.
