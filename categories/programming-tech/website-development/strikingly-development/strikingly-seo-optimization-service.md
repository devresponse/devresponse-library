
# Strikingly SEO Optimization Service Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Strikingly SEO Optimization Service aims to enhance the online visibility and performance of websites built on the Strikingly platform. In today’s digital landscape, a visually appealing website is not sufficient; it must also be optimized for search engines to attract organic traffic. This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of the current website, focusing on critical areas such as meta tags, keyword integration, and overall site performance. Our objective is to implement best practices for on-page SEO, ensuring that the website not only captivates visitors with its design but also ranks higher in search engine results. Key outcomes include improved search engine rankings, increased organic traffic, and a seamless user experience that keeps visitors engaged. By optimizing content and enhancing loading speeds, we aim to provide businesses of all sizes with the tools they need to improve their online presence and drive more traffic to their Strikingly website.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Website Analysis**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the current website to identify areas for improvement, including:
  - Review of existing meta tags and descriptions
  - Keyword research and integration
  - Assessment of site performance metrics (loading speed, mobile responsiveness, etc.)

- **On-Page SEO Implementation**: 
  - Optimize meta tags and descriptions based on keyword research
  - Enhance content with relevant keywords while maintaining readability
  - Improve internal linking structure to enhance navigation

- **Technical SEO Improvements**:
  - Optimize images and other media for faster loading times
  - Implement structured data markup to enhance search visibility
  - Ensure mobile optimization and cross-browser compatibility

- **Performance Monitoring**: 
  - Set up tracking tools (e.g., Google Analytics, Google Search Console) to monitor website performance post-optimization
  - Provide a report on key performance indicators (KPIs) such as organic traffic growth and search rankings

- **Client Training**: Offer training sessions for the client on maintaining SEO best practices and utilizing tools for ongoing optimization.
