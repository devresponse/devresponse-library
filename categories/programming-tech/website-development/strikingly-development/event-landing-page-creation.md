
# Event Landing Page Creation Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Event Landing Page Creation project aims to design and develop a visually stunning and highly functional landing page tailored specifically for your upcoming event. In today's digital landscape, an effective landing page is essential for capturing the interest of potential attendees and converting them into participants. This project will leverage the Strikingly platform to create a page that highlights the unique features of your event, including an engaging layout, compelling visuals, and critical information such as the event date, time, location, and agenda. Key outcomes of this project include increased visitor engagement, streamlined registration through integrated RSVP forms, and a user-friendly interface that ensures accessibility across devices. Our commitment to multiple revisions guarantees that the final product aligns perfectly with your vision, ultimately enhancing the overall success of your event.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Design Phase**: 
  - Create wireframes and mockups for the landing page layout.
  - Develop a color scheme and typography that aligns with the event branding.
  
- **Content Development**:
  - Gather and curate content including event details, speaker bios, and agenda.
  - Create compelling visuals and graphics to enhance the page's appeal.

- **Development Phase**:
  - Build the landing page using the Strikingly platform.
  - Integrate essential features such as RSVP forms and social media links.

- **Testing and Revisions**:
  - Conduct usability testing to ensure functionality across devices.
  - Implement feedback and make necessary revisions to meet client expectations.

- **Launch**:
  - Finalize the landing page and prepare for launch.
  - Monitor initial performance metrics post-launch for any immediate adjustments.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of wireframes and mockups
  - Deliverable: Approved design mockups

- **Milestone 2**: Content development and approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized content and visuals

- **Milestone 3**: Development of the landing page
  - Deliverable: Functional landing page on the Strikingly platform

- **Milestone 4**: Usability testing and revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised landing page based on testing feedback

- **Milestone 5**: Launch of the landing page
  - Deliverable: Live landing page with monitoring setup
