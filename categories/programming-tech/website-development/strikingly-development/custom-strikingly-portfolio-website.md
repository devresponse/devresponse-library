
# Project Plan Template: Custom Strikingly Portfolio Website

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital age, having a standout portfolio is essential for professionals looking to showcase their skills and achievements. The objective of this project is to design and develop a custom portfolio website using the Strikingly platform, renowned for its user-friendly interface and visually appealing templates. This project aims to create a unique online presence that reflects your personal style and professional identity. Key outcomes include a visually striking layout, integration of multimedia elements, and mobile optimization, ensuring that the website is accessible across all devices. Additionally, the website will be optimized for search engines to enhance online visibility. By the end of this project, you will possess a polished and professional portfolio website that effectively showcases your work and elevates your personal brand in the digital landscape.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting to understand your vision, preferences, and specific requirements for the portfolio website.
- **Design Phase**: Create a visually striking layout that aligns with your personal brand. This includes selecting color schemes, fonts, and overall aesthetics.
- **Content Development**: Collaborate with you to gather and organize content, including text, images, and multimedia elements that highlight your skills and accomplishments.
- **Website Development**: Utilize the Strikingly platform to build the website, ensuring that all elements are integrated seamlessly.
- **Mobile Optimization**: Ensure that the website is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices, providing an excellent user experience across all platforms.
- **SEO Optimization**: Implement SEO best practices to enhance the website's visibility on search engines, including keyword integration and meta tags.
- **Review and Revisions**: Present the initial version of the website for your feedback and make necessary revisions based on your input.
- **Final Launch**: Prepare the website for launch, ensuring all functionalities are working correctly and that the site is ready for public viewing.
- **Post-Launch Support**: Provide a period of support for any issues or adjustments needed after the website goes live.
