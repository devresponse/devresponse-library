
# Site123 Custom Widgets Development Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Site123 Custom Widgets Development project aims to create and integrate tailored widgets that will significantly improve the functionality and user engagement of your Site123 website. The primary objective of this project is to conduct a comprehensive analysis of your current website needs, followed by the design and development of custom widgets that align with your brand and enhance user experience. Key outcomes include the delivery of interactive forms, social media feeds, and dynamic content displays that cater to your specific requirements. Our dedicated team will collaborate closely with you throughout the process to ensure that the developed widgets not only meet your expectations but also elevate the overall user experience on your website. By the end of this project, you will have a set of custom widgets seamlessly integrated into your existing Site123 framework, thoroughly tested for optimal performance, ultimately leading to increased user engagement and satisfaction.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Needs Assessment**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the current website to identify specific needs and desired functionalities.
- **Design Phase**: Create design mockups for the custom widgets, ensuring alignment with your brand identity and user experience goals.
- **Development Phase**: Develop the custom widgets based on approved designs, focusing on functionality and performance.
- **Integration**: Seamlessly integrate the developed widgets into the existing Site123 framework.
- **Testing**: Perform comprehensive testing to ensure all widgets function correctly and meet performance standards.
- **Feedback and Revisions**: Gather feedback from stakeholders and make necessary revisions to the widgets.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the final set of custom widgets along with documentation and support for implementation.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Needs Assessment
  - Deliverable: Needs Assessment Report
- **Milestone 2**: Approval of Widget Designs
  - Deliverable: Design Mockups
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Widget Development
  - Deliverable: Developed Widgets
- **Milestone 4**: Successful Integration into Site123
  - Deliverable: Integrated Widgets
- **Milestone 5**: Completion of Testing Phase
  - Deliverable: Testing Report
- **Milestone 6**: Final Delivery and Documentation
  - Deliverable: Final Widget Package and User Guide
