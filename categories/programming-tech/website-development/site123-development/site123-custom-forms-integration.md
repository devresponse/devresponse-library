
# Site123 Custom Forms Integration Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Site123 Custom Forms Integration project is designed to enhance the interactivity and functionality of your Site123 website by creating and integrating tailored forms that effectively collect user data. The primary objective of this project is to conduct a thorough analysis of your specific requirements to design forms that align with your business objectives. Key outcomes include the development of user-friendly and visually appealing forms that seamlessly integrate into your existing website structure. Our team will implement validation checks to ensure data accuracy and provide a smooth user experience. Additionally, we will set up notifications for form submissions and provide guidance on managing the collected data. This project aims to deliver a comprehensive solution that not only meets your immediate needs but also enhances user engagement on your website, ultimately contributing to improved customer satisfaction and business growth.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Requirements Analysis**: Conduct meetings with stakeholders to gather detailed requirements for the custom forms.
- **Form Design**: Create wireframes and prototypes of the forms for client review and feedback.
- **Development**: Build the custom forms using Site123's integration capabilities, ensuring they are responsive and accessible.
- **Validation Implementation**: Integrate validation checks to ensure data accuracy and completeness.
- **User Experience Testing**: Perform usability testing to ensure forms are intuitive and user-friendly.
- **Integration**: Seamlessly integrate the forms into the existing Site123 website structure.
- **Notification Setup**: Configure email notifications for form submissions to designated stakeholders.
- **Data Management Guidance**: Provide documentation and training on how to manage and utilize the collected data effectively.
- **Post-Launch Support**: Offer support for a specified period after launch to address any issues or questions.
