
# Project Plan Template: OsCommerce GDPR Compliance Implementation

## 1. Executive Summary

The OsCommerce GDPR Compliance Implementation project aims to ensure that your online store adheres to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) requirements, which are essential for protecting customer data and maintaining trust in today's digital marketplace. The primary objective of this project is to integrate key compliance features into your OsCommerce platform, including a cookie consent banner, mechanisms for data access requests, and an updated privacy policy. By conducting a comprehensive assessment of your current store setup, our expert team will identify areas that require adjustments to meet legal standards. The key outcomes of this project will include enhanced customer trust, improved data management practices, and a fully compliant online store that safeguards user information. Ultimately, this initiative will not only fulfill legal obligations but also position your business as a responsible entity in the eyes of your customers.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Setup**: Conduct a thorough review of the existing OsCommerce store to identify compliance gaps related to GDPR.
- **Cookie Consent Banner Integration**: Implement a user-friendly cookie consent banner that informs visitors about data collection practices and allows them to accept or decline cookies.
- **Data Access Request Mechanism**: Develop functionality that enables customers to request access to their personal data, including viewing, downloading, and managing their information.
- **Privacy Policy Update**: Revise the current privacy policy to ensure it accurately reflects data handling practices and complies with GDPR requirements.
- **Testing and Quality Assurance**: Perform rigorous testing of all implemented features to ensure they function correctly and meet compliance standards.
- **Training and Documentation**: Provide training for your team on managing GDPR compliance features and create documentation for ongoing reference.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the initial assessment report.
  - **Deliverable**: Detailed report outlining compliance gaps and recommended actions.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Integration of the cookie consent banner.
  - **Deliverable**: Live implementation of the cookie consent banner on the website.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Development of data access request functionality.
  - **Deliverable**: Fully functional data access request feature available to customers.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Updated privacy policy.
  - **Deliverable**: Revised privacy policy published on the website.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Completion of testing and quality assurance.
  - **Deliverable**: Testing report confirming all features are compliant and functional.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Training and documentation delivery.
  - **Deliverable**: Training session completed and documentation provided to the team.
