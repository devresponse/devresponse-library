
# Opencart GDPR Compliance Services Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Opencart GDPR Compliance Services project aims to ensure that online businesses utilizing the Opencart platform are fully compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). In today's digital landscape, compliance is not just a legal obligation but also a critical factor in building customer trust and enhancing the overall shopping experience. This project will provide a comprehensive assessment of the current Opencart setup, identifying gaps in compliance and implementing necessary features to align with GDPR requirements. Key outcomes include the integration of a cookie consent mechanism, the establishment of a streamlined process for data access requests, and the updating of privacy policies to reflect current practices and legal obligations. By the end of this project, clients will have a fully compliant Opencart store that not only meets regulatory standards but also fosters transparency and trust with their customers.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Opencart Setup**: 
  - Review existing data handling practices.
  - Identify areas of non-compliance with GDPR.

- **Integration of Cookie Consent Mechanism**: 
  - Implement a user-friendly cookie consent banner.
  - Ensure users are informed about data collection practices.

- **Establishment of Data Access Request Process**: 
  - Develop a clear procedure for customers to request access to their personal data.
  - Create templates for data access requests and responses.

- **Updating Privacy Policy**: 
  - Revise the existing privacy policy to include GDPR-compliant language.
  - Ensure the policy reflects current data practices and legal obligations.

- **Training and Support**: 
  - Provide training for staff on GDPR compliance and data handling.
  - Offer ongoing support for any compliance-related queries.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current Setup Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing compliance gaps.

- **Milestone 2**: Integration of Cookie Consent Mechanism
  - Deliverable: Functional cookie consent banner on the Opencart store.

- **Milestone 3**: Implementation of Data Access Request Process
  - Deliverable: Documented process and templates for data access requests.

- **Milestone 4**: Updated Privacy Policy
  - Deliverable: Revised privacy policy document.

- **Milestone 5**: Staff Training and Support
  - Deliverable: Training session completion and support documentation.
