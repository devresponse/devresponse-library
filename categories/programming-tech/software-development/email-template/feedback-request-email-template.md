
# Project Plan: Feedback Request Email Template

## 1. Executive Summary

The Feedback Request Email Template project aims to create a professional and visually appealing email template designed to effectively solicit customer feedback and reviews. The primary objective is to develop a template that aligns seamlessly with the client's brand identity, ensuring consistency in communication across all customer touchpoints. This project will encompass both design and coding elements, focusing on a responsive layout that functions flawlessly across various devices and email clients. Key outcomes include a fully coded HTML email template that incorporates strategically placed call-to-action buttons, personalized greetings, and sections for customer testimonials. Additionally, the design will adhere to best practices in email marketing, featuring clear subject lines and engaging visuals to enhance open and response rates. The final deliverable will not only be the email template itself but also a comprehensive user guide to facilitate easy implementation into the client's email marketing platform.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct a review of existing email templates and gather insights on best practices for feedback solicitation.
- **Brand Alignment**: Collaborate with the client to understand their brand guidelines, including color schemes, fonts, and overall style.
- **Design Phase**:
  - Create wireframes for the email layout.
  - Develop a visually appealing design that includes personalized greetings and customer testimonial sections.
  - Incorporate strategically placed call-to-action buttons to encourage feedback.
- **Coding Phase**:
  - Convert the approved design into a fully coded HTML email template.
  - Ensure the template is responsive and compatible with major email clients and devices.
- **Testing**: Conduct thorough testing to ensure functionality, responsiveness, and compatibility.
- **Documentation**: Prepare a user guide detailing how to implement the template into the client’s email marketing platform.
- **Feedback and Revisions**: Gather feedback from the client and make necessary revisions to the template.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Analysis
  - Deliverable: Research report and analysis summary.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Brand Alignment Confirmation
  - Deliverable: Document outlining brand guidelines and design preferences.

- **Milestone 3**: Design Phase Completion
  - Deliverable: Wireframes and final design mockups.

- **Milestone 4**: Coding Phase Completion
  - Deliverable: Fully coded HTML email template.

- **Milestone 5**: Testing Completion
  - Deliverable: Testing report confirming functionality and compatibility.

- **Milestone 6**: User Guide Delivery
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive user guide for template implementation.

- **Milestone 7**: Final Review and Approval
  - Deliverable: Client approval of the final email template and documentation.
