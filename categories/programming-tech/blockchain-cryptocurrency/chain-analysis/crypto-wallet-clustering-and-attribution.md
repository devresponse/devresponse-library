
# Project Plan Template: Crypto Wallet Clustering and Attribution

## 1. Executive Summary

The Crypto Wallet Clustering and Attribution project aims to leverage advanced chain analysis techniques to systematically cluster cryptocurrency wallets and attribute them to specific entities or individuals. The primary objective is to meticulously analyze transaction patterns across various blockchain networks, thereby identifying relationships between wallets and uncovering the underlying ownership structures. This project will employ sophisticated clustering algorithms to interpret the gathered data, providing actionable insights that can inform strategic decisions for businesses and individuals. Key outcomes include a comprehensive report detailing ownership structures, activity patterns, and potential risks associated with digital assets. By the end of this project, stakeholders will gain a deeper understanding of cryptocurrency transaction dynamics, enabling them to make informed decisions and mitigate risks effectively.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Collection**: 
  - Gather transaction data from various blockchain networks, including but not limited to Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other relevant cryptocurrencies.
  - Ensure compliance with data privacy regulations and ethical standards during data collection.

- **Data Preprocessing**: 
  - Clean and preprocess the collected data to ensure accuracy and reliability.
  - Identify and remove any outliers or irrelevant data points that may skew the analysis.

- **Clustering Analysis**: 
  - Apply advanced clustering algorithms (e.g., K-means, DBSCAN) to group wallets based on transaction patterns.
  - Analyze the clusters to identify potential ownership and relationships between wallets.

- **Attribution Analysis**: 
  - Develop methodologies to attribute clustered wallets to specific entities or individuals.
  - Validate attribution results through cross-referencing with known data sources.

- **Reporting**: 
  - Prepare a comprehensive report detailing findings, including ownership structures and activity patterns.
  - Provide actionable insights and recommendations for stakeholders based on the analysis.

- **Presentation**: 
  - Present findings to stakeholders through a detailed presentation, highlighting key insights and strategic implications.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Data Collection
  - Deliverable: Data collection report summarizing the sources and types of data gathered.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Data Preprocessing
  - Deliverable: Cleaned and preprocessed dataset ready for analysis.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Clustering Analysis
  - Deliverable: Clustering analysis report detailing the identified wallet clusters.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Attribution Analysis
  - Deliverable: Attribution analysis report with identified entities and individuals linked to wallet clusters.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Report and Presentation
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive final report and presentation summarizing all findings and recommendations.
