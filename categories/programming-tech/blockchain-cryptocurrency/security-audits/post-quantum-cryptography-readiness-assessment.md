
# Project Plan Template: Post-Quantum Cryptography Readiness Assessment

## 1. Executive Summary

As quantum computing technology progresses, the imperative for blockchain systems to adapt and secure themselves against potential quantum threats has never been more critical. The objective of the Post-Quantum Cryptography Readiness Assessment project is to conduct a thorough evaluation of existing blockchain systems to identify vulnerabilities that may arise from quantum algorithms. This project will provide a comprehensive overview of the current cryptographic algorithms in use, pinpointing those susceptible to quantum decryption techniques. Key outcomes will include a detailed risk assessment, a strategic roadmap for transitioning to post-quantum cryptographic solutions, and a consultation session to discuss findings and next steps. By proactively addressing these vulnerabilities, we aim to ensure the resilience and security of blockchain infrastructures in the face of future technological advancements.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Blockchain Systems**: 
  - Identify and document the existing blockchain systems in use.
  - Evaluate the cryptographic algorithms currently implemented within these systems.

- **Vulnerability Analysis**: 
  - Conduct a detailed analysis to determine which algorithms are susceptible to quantum attacks.
  - Assess the potential impact of quantum decryption on the integrity and confidentiality of blockchain data.

- **Recommendations for Transition**: 
  - Develop a set of recommendations for transitioning to post-quantum cryptographic solutions.
  - Provide guidance on best practices for implementing these recommendations within the existing blockchain infrastructure.

- **Final Report**: 
  - Compile findings into a comprehensive report that includes:
    - Risk assessment results.
    - A roadmap for implementation of recommended changes.
    - A summary of the consultation session.

- **Consultation Session**: 
  - Schedule and conduct a session with stakeholders to discuss findings, address questions, and outline next steps.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the initial assessment of current blockchain systems.
  - **Deliverable**: Documented overview of existing systems and algorithms.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of vulnerability analysis.
  - **Deliverable**: Detailed report on vulnerabilities and potential impacts.

- **Milestone 3**: Development of recommendations for post-quantum cryptographic solutions.
  - **Deliverable**: Recommendations report.

- **Milestone 4**: Final report compilation and review.
  - **Deliverable**: Comprehensive final report including risk assessment and roadmap.

- **Milestone 5**: Conduct consultation session with stakeholders.
  - **Deliverable**: Summary of consultation session and agreed next steps.
