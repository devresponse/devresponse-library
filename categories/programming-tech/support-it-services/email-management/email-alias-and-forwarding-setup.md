
# Project Plan: Email Alias and Forwarding Setup

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's fast-paced business environment, effective email management is essential for maintaining clear communication and enhancing productivity. The Email Alias and Forwarding Setup project aims to optimize your organization's email system by implementing tailored email aliases and forwarding rules. The primary objective is to analyze the current email setup, identify key areas for improvement, and create a streamlined communication process that directs emails to the appropriate team members efficiently. Key outcomes include the establishment of department-specific email aliases, the implementation of automated forwarding rules based on predefined criteria, and a comprehensive report detailing the changes made. By the end of this project, your organization will benefit from a well-organized email system that promotes efficiency, reduces the risk of overlooked messages, and enhances overall responsiveness across teams.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Current Email System Analysis**: Conduct a thorough assessment of the existing email setup to identify inefficiencies and areas for improvement.
- **Email Alias Creation**: Develop and implement email aliases for various departments or functions within the organization to ensure proper email routing.
- **Forwarding Rules Setup**: Establish forwarding rules that automatically direct incoming messages based on specific criteria, such as keywords or sender addresses.
- **Testing and Validation**: Test the new email aliases and forwarding rules to ensure they function correctly and meet organizational needs.
- **Training and Documentation**: Provide training sessions for team members on the new email management system and deliver comprehensive documentation outlining the changes made.
- **Post-Implementation Support**: Offer support for a specified period after implementation to address any issues or adjustments needed.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current Email System Analysis  
  **Deliverable**: Analysis report detailing findings and recommendations.

- **Milestone 2**: Creation of Email Aliases  
  **Deliverable**: List of created email aliases and their corresponding departments/functions.

- **Milestone 3**: Implementation of Forwarding Rules  
  **Deliverable**: Document outlining the established forwarding rules and criteria.

- **Milestone 4**: Testing and Validation  
  **Deliverable**: Testing report confirming the functionality of aliases and forwarding rules.

- **Milestone 5**: Training and Documentation  
  **Deliverable**: Training materials and user documentation provided to team members.

- **Milestone 6**: Post-Implementation Support  
  **Deliverable**: Support log documenting any issues addressed during the support period.
