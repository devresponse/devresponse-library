
# Project Plan Template: Green Screen Setup and Configuration

## 1. Executive Summary

The Green Screen Setup and Configuration project aims to provide streamers with a comprehensive solution for enhancing their streaming environment through professional-grade green screen installation and configuration. The primary objective is to understand the unique needs of each client, ensuring that the selected green screen material and size perfectly fit their streaming setup. This project will deliver key outcomes, including the successful configuration of chroma key settings within the streaming software, which is essential for achieving optimal background removal and visual effects. Additionally, expert guidance on lighting adjustments will be provided to eliminate shadows and enhance the overall stream quality. By the end of this project, streamers—whether novice or experienced—will have a polished and professional look that significantly boosts viewer engagement and overall streaming experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a detailed consultation with the client to assess their specific needs, streaming environment, and desired outcomes.
  
- **Material Selection**: Assist the client in selecting the appropriate green screen material (e.g., fabric, collapsible, or painted wall) and size based on their space and streaming requirements.
  
- **Installation**: Provide guidance on the installation process, ensuring that the green screen is set up correctly and securely in the designated area.
  
- **Chroma Key Configuration**: Configure the chroma key settings within the client's streaming software (e.g., OBS, Streamlabs) to ensure optimal background removal and visual effects.
  
- **Lighting Guidance**: Offer expert advice on lighting adjustments to eliminate shadows and enhance the overall quality of the stream, including recommendations for lighting equipment if necessary.
  
- **Final Review**: Conduct a final review of the setup with the client to ensure satisfaction and make any necessary adjustments.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Consultation report summarizing client needs and recommendations.

- **Milestone 2**: Selection of Green Screen Material and Size
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of selected green screen material and size.

- **Milestone 3**: Installation of Green Screen
  - Deliverable: Installed green screen ready for use.

- **Milestone 4**: Configuration of Chroma Key Settings
  - Deliverable: Documented settings for chroma key configuration.

- **Milestone 5**: Lighting Adjustment Recommendations
  - Deliverable: Lighting setup guide tailored to the client's environment.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Review and Adjustments
  - Deliverable: Client satisfaction confirmation and final adjustments made.
