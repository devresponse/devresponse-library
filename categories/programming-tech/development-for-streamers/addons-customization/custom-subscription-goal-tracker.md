
# Custom Subscription Goal Tracker Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Subscription Goal Tracker project aims to develop an engaging and visually appealing tool specifically designed for streamers to showcase their subscription goals in real-time. The primary objective is to enhance viewer interaction and motivation by providing a dynamic visual representation of the streamer's progress towards their subscription targets. This tracker will feature customizable animations and progress bars, allowing streamers to tailor the tool to fit their unique branding and style. By integrating seamlessly with popular streaming platforms, the tracker will ensure ease of use and functionality, making it accessible for streamers of all technical backgrounds. Key outcomes of this project include increased viewer engagement, improved subscription rates, and a more immersive streaming experience. Ultimately, the Custom Subscription Goal Tracker will empower streamers to effectively communicate their goals to their audience, fostering a sense of community and encouraging viewer participation.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct market research to identify the needs and preferences of streamers regarding subscription goal tracking tools.
- **Design Phase**:
  - Create wireframes and mockups for the tracker interface.
  - Develop customizable animations and progress bar designs.
- **Development Phase**:
  - Build the tracker application with a focus on seamless integration with popular streaming platforms (e.g., Twitch, YouTube).
  - Implement a user-friendly interface that allows streamers to update goals and customize the appearance easily.
- **Testing Phase**:
  - Conduct usability testing with a group of streamers to gather feedback and make necessary adjustments.
  - Perform technical testing to ensure compatibility with various streaming platforms.
- **Launch Phase**:
  - Deploy the Custom Subscription Goal Tracker to the market.
  - Provide documentation and support for users to facilitate onboarding.
- **Post-Launch Support**:
  - Offer ongoing maintenance and updates based on user feedback and technological advancements.
