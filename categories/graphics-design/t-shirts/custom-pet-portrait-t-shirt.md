
# Project Plan: Custom Pet Portrait T-Shirt

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Pet Portrait T-Shirt project aims to create a unique and personalized product that celebrates the bond between pet owners and their furry companions. Our objective is to provide customers with high-quality t-shirts featuring stunning hand-drawn or digital portraits of their pets. This project will involve collaboration with talented graphic designers who will work closely with clients to capture the essence of their pets, ensuring that every detail is meticulously represented. Key outcomes include delivering a product that not only meets but exceeds customer expectations through multiple design revisions, high-quality fabric printing, and a seamless ordering experience. By offering a whimsical or realistic portrayal of pets, we aim to cater to diverse customer preferences, making these t-shirts perfect for personal use or as thoughtful gifts. Ultimately, this project seeks to enhance customer satisfaction and foster a deeper connection between pet lovers and their beloved animals.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision for the pet portrait t-shirt, including style preferences (whimsical or realistic) and any specific details they want to include.
  
- **Design Creation**: The graphic designer will create initial sketches or digital drafts based on the client's input. This phase will include:
  - Hand-drawn or digital portrait options.
  - Color palette selection.
  - T-shirt style and color selection.

- **Client Review**: Present the initial designs to the client for feedback. This phase allows for:
  - Up to three rounds of revisions based on client feedback.
  - Adjustments to ensure the design aligns with the client's vision.

- **Final Approval**: Once the client is satisfied with the design, obtain final approval to proceed with production.

- **Production**: Coordinate with a printing company to produce the t-shirts, ensuring:
  - High-quality fabric is used.
  - Accurate color reproduction of the design.
  
- **Quality Assurance**: Inspect the final products for quality and accuracy before shipping.

- **Delivery**: Package and ship the t-shirts to the client, ensuring timely delivery.

- **Post-Project Follow-Up**: Conduct a follow-up with the client to gather feedback and ensure satisfaction with the final product.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Client brief document outlining design preferences.

- **Milestone 2**: Initial Design Drafts Created
  - Deliverable: Three initial design options presented to the client.

- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback Received
  - Deliverable: Documented feedback and requested revisions.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approved
  - Deliverable: Final design file ready for production.

- **Milestone 5**: T-Shirts Produced
  - Deliverable: Completed t-shirts ready for quality assurance.

- **Milestone 6**: Quality Assurance Completed
  - Deliverable: Quality assurance report confirming product standards.

- **Milestone 7**: T-Shirts Delivered
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of delivery and client receipt.
