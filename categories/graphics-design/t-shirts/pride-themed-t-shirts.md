
# Project Plan: Pride-Themed T-Shirts

## 1. Executive Summary

The Pride-Themed T-Shirts project aims to design a vibrant and inclusive series of t-shirts that celebrate LGBTQ+ pride and diversity. The primary objective is to create eye-catching graphics that resonate with the spirit of pride, ensuring that each design reflects the values of inclusivity, love, and acceptance. This project will involve close collaboration with the client to fully understand their vision and incorporate essential elements such as rainbow colors, symbols of diversity, and empowering messages. Key outcomes include multiple design concepts, revisions based on client feedback, and the delivery of final files prepared for print. Ultimately, this project presents an opportunity to create meaningful merchandise that not only promotes pride but also supports the LGBTQ+ community, fostering a sense of belonging and acceptance among individuals.
