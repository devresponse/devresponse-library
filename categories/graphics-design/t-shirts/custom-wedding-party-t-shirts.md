
# Project Plan Template: Custom Wedding Party Tees

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Wedding Party Tees project aims to create unique, custom-designed t-shirts for wedding parties, including bridesmaids, groomsmen, and family members. The primary objective is to foster a sense of unity and celebration among the wedding party while ensuring that each member feels included and stylish. This project will involve close collaboration with the couple to capture their vision, incorporating elements such as wedding colors, themes, and personal touches that reflect their personality. Key outcomes include the delivery of multiple design concepts, the opportunity for revisions based on feedback, and the final provision of high-quality, print-ready designs. By the end of this project, the wedding party will have matching t-shirts that not only enhance the celebration but also serve as cherished keepsakes of the special day.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the couple to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific themes or colors they wish to incorporate into the t-shirt designs.
  
- **Design Development**: Our graphic designers will create multiple design concepts based on the initial consultation. Each design will reflect the couple's personality and wedding theme.
  
- **Review and Feedback**: Present the design concepts to the couple for review. Gather feedback and make necessary revisions to ensure the designs meet their expectations.
  
- **Finalization of Designs**: Once the couple approves the designs, we will finalize them and prepare them for printing.
  
- **Production Coordination**: Coordinate with printing vendors to ensure high-quality production of the t-shirts.
  
- **Delivery**: Ensure timely delivery of the finished t-shirts to the couple or designated location prior to the wedding date.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of the couple's vision and preferences.

- **Milestone 2**: Design Concepts Developed
  - Deliverable: Presentation of 3-5 design concepts for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Feedback and Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised designs based on couple's feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Designs Approved
  - Deliverable: Final print-ready designs.

- **Milestone 5**: Production Completed
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of t-shirt production and quality check.

- **Milestone 6**: Delivery of T-Shirts
  - Deliverable: T-shirts delivered to the couple or designated location.
