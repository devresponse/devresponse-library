
# Project Plan Template: Custom Baby Shower T-Shirts

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Baby Shower T-Shirts project aims to create a delightful collection of t-shirt designs that celebrate the joyous occasion of welcoming a new life. The primary objective is to develop charming and playful designs that resonate with themes such as 'Mom-to-Be', 'Team Baby', and other celebratory motifs. This project will not only focus on the aesthetic appeal of the designs but also ensure they are suitable for various sizes and styles, catering to all guests attending the baby shower. Key outcomes include the delivery of high-resolution graphics ready for printing, along with mockups that visualize the designs on actual t-shirts. Collaboration with the client will be integral to ensure that the final designs align with their vision and the overall theme of the event. Ultimately, this project aims to provide memorable keepsakes that guests will cherish long after the celebration, enhancing the overall experience of the baby shower.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Concept Development**: Conduct research on current trends in baby shower themes and t-shirt designs. Develop initial concepts based on themes such as 'Mom-to-Be', 'Team Baby', and other celebratory motifs.
  
- **Design Creation**: Create a series of vibrant and appealing t-shirt designs. Ensure designs are adaptable for various sizes and styles, including options for both adults and children.

- **Client Collaboration**: Schedule regular meetings with the client to present design concepts, gather feedback, and make necessary adjustments to align with their vision.

- **Mockup Development**: Produce high-quality mockups to visualize how the designs will look on actual t-shirts. This will aid in client approval and provide a clear representation of the final product.

- **Final Deliverables**: Prepare and deliver high-resolution graphics suitable for printing, along with the finalized mockups.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Concept Development
  - **Deliverable**: Initial design concepts presented to the client for feedback.

- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of T-Shirt Designs
  - **Deliverable**: Approved designs ready for mockup creation.

- **Milestone 3**: Mockup Presentation
  - **Deliverable**: High-quality mockups presented to the client for final approval.

- **Milestone 4**: Delivery of Final Graphics
  - **Deliverable**: High-resolution graphics and mockups delivered to the client.
