
# Project Plan Template: Pop Culture Mashup Tee

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Pop Culture Mashup Tee" project aims to create a unique t-shirt design that seamlessly blends various elements from popular culture into a single, cohesive graphic. The primary objective is to design a visually striking t-shirt that resonates with fans of iconic symbols, characters, and themes from movies, music, video games, and television shows. This project will involve thorough research to identify the most recognizable and beloved elements of pop culture, ensuring that the final design is both original and appealing. Key outcomes include the delivery of initial sketches, a digital mockup, and finalized design files in the appropriate formats for printing. By the end of this project, we aim to produce a wearable piece of art that not only showcases the designer's creativity but also connects with a diverse audience of pop culture enthusiasts.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Identify and select iconic symbols, characters, and themes from various pop culture phenomena.
  - Analyze current trends in t-shirt design and popular culture to ensure relevance.

- **Design Phase**: 
  - Create initial sketches that incorporate selected elements.
  - Develop a digital mockup that showcases the design in a realistic t-shirt format.

- **Review Phase**: 
  - Present initial sketches and digital mockup to stakeholders for feedback.
  - Revise the design based on feedback received.

- **Finalization Phase**: 
  - Prepare final design files in required formats (e.g., AI, PSD, PNG).
  - Ensure the design is suitable for printing, considering color schemes, typography, and overall composition.

- **Delivery Phase**: 
  - Provide all final design files to the client.
  - Offer recommendations for printing and merchandising options.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report outlining selected pop culture elements.

- **Milestone 2**: Initial Sketches Completed
  - Deliverable: Set of initial sketches for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Digital Mockup Completed
  - Deliverable: Digital mockup of the t-shirt design.

- **Milestone 4**: Feedback Review and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised design based on stakeholder feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Design Files Delivered
  - Deliverable: Finalized design files in required formats.
