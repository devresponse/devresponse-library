
# Project Plan Template: Humorous Meme Tees

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Humorous Meme Tees" project aims to capitalize on the growing popularity of memes as a form of expression in today's digital culture. The primary objective is to create a series of custom-designed t-shirts that feature humorous graphics and witty captions, appealing to a broad audience. This project will involve thorough research into trending memes and popular culture references to ensure that the designs are not only relevant but also resonate with potential customers. Key outcomes include the development of multiple design concepts, the incorporation of client feedback through revisions, and the delivery of final print-ready files. By blending creativity with humor, this project seeks to produce t-shirts that are not just clothing items but conversation starters, fostering a sense of community among meme enthusiasts.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Identify trending memes and popular culture references.
  - Analyze target audience preferences and demographics.
  
- **Design Phase**: 
  - Create multiple design concepts for t-shirts.
  - Develop eye-catching graphics and witty captions that align with selected memes.
  
- **Client Review**: 
  - Present initial design concepts to the client for feedback.
  - Revise designs based on client input and preferences.
  
- **Finalization**: 
  - Prepare final designs in various formats suitable for print.
  - Ensure designs are adaptable to different t-shirt styles and colors.
  
- **Production Preparation**: 
  - Collaborate with printing vendors to ensure quality and accuracy.
  - Create a style guide for t-shirt production.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report on trending memes and target audience insights.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Initial Design Concepts
  - Deliverable: Presentation of 5-10 design concepts for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised designs based on client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized designs ready for print.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Production Preparation
  - Deliverable: Style guide and production files sent to printing vendor.
