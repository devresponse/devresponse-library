
# Project Plan: Custom Graduation T-Shirts

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Graduation T-Shirts project aims to celebrate the achievements of graduates by creating unique and eye-catching t-shirt designs that encapsulate the spirit of their academic journey. The primary objective is to design custom t-shirts that feature the graduating class year, school mascots, and personalized elements that reflect the pride and excitement of graduation. This project will involve collaboration with talented graphic designers who will work closely with stakeholders to understand their vision and incorporate school colors, logos, and themes into the designs. Key outcomes include the delivery of high-quality graphics that resonate with graduates and their families, ensuring that each t-shirt serves as a memorable keepsake. The final products will cater to both individual graduates and larger groups, allowing everyone to wear their accomplishments with pride. By the end of this project, we aim to provide a range of designs that not only celebrate graduation but also foster a sense of community and belonging among graduates.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Design Consultation**: Conduct initial meetings with stakeholders to gather requirements, including preferred colors, logos, themes, and any specific design ideas.
- **Design Development**: Create multiple design concepts based on the consultation feedback. Each design will incorporate the graduating class year, school mascots, and personalized elements.
- **Revisions**: Present the initial designs to stakeholders for feedback. Make necessary revisions based on their input to ensure satisfaction.
- **Final Design Approval**: Obtain final approval on the selected designs from stakeholders.
- **Production Preparation**: Prepare the final designs for production, ensuring all specifications meet printing requirements.
- **Production**: Coordinate with printing vendors to produce the t-shirts, ensuring quality control throughout the process.
- **Distribution**: Organize the distribution of the t-shirts to individual graduates and larger groups, ensuring timely delivery before graduation ceremonies.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Design Consultation
  - **Deliverable**: Summary of requirements and design preferences
- **Milestone 2**: Design Concepts Presentation
  - **Deliverable**: Three initial design concepts for review
- **Milestone 3**: Design Revisions
  - **Deliverable**: Revised designs based on stakeholder feedback
- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approval
  - **Deliverable**: Approved final designs ready for production
- **Milestone 5**: Production Completion
  - **Deliverable**: Completed t-shirts ready for distribution
- **Milestone 6**: Distribution of T-Shirts
  - **Deliverable**: Confirmation of delivery to all graduates
