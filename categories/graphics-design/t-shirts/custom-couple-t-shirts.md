
# Project Plan: Custom Couple T-Shirts

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Couple T-Shirts project aims to create a unique set of matching t-shirts designed specifically for couples, capturing the essence of their relationship through either romantic or humorous themes. The primary objective is to deliver a product that resonates with the couple's personality and style, making it ideal for special occasions such as anniversaries, weddings, or simply celebrating love in a fun way. The project will involve close collaboration with the client to understand their vision, preferences, and any specific elements they wish to incorporate into the designs. Key outcomes include multiple design concepts, revisions based on client feedback, and the delivery of final print-ready files suitable for various t-shirt printing methods. By focusing on personalized designs, this project not only aims to provide a tangible product but also to create lasting memories for couples.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific themes or messages they want to include in the t-shirt designs.
- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather inspiration based on the couple's relationship, interests, and style. This may include reviewing existing designs, color palettes, and typography.
- **Design Concepts**: Create multiple design concepts (minimum of {{number_of_concepts}}) that reflect the discussed themes, incorporating both graphics and text.
- **Client Review**: Present the design concepts to the client for feedback and discussion.
- **Revisions**: Make necessary revisions based on client feedback, allowing for up to {{number_of_revisions}} rounds of revisions.
- **Finalization**: Finalize the selected design(s) and prepare print-ready files in various formats suitable for different printing methods (e.g., screen printing, direct-to-garment).
- **Delivery**: Provide the final designs to the client along with any necessary guidelines for printing.
