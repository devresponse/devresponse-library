
# Project Plan: Vintage Band T-Shirt Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Vintage Band T-Shirt Design project aims to create a unique, retro-inspired t-shirt that encapsulates the essence of vintage band merchandise. The primary objective is to design a visually appealing product that resonates with fans of classic rock, pop, or alternative music, evoking a sense of nostalgia and artistic expression. This project will involve close collaboration with the client to ensure that the design aligns with their vision, whether it is inspired by a real band or a fictional concept. Key outcomes include multiple design concepts for client review, a finalized design suitable for screen printing, and detailed specifications for color, typography, and graphics. Ultimately, this project seeks to enhance the client's merchandise offerings and provide a standout piece that appeals to music lovers.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather references and inspiration from classic band merchandise, focusing on design elements that evoke nostalgia.
- **Client Consultation**: Conduct meetings with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific requirements for the design.
- **Concept Development**: Create multiple design concepts based on the client's input, incorporating elements such as typography, color schemes, and graphics.
- **Client Review**: Present the design concepts to the client for feedback and discussion.
- **Revisions**: Make necessary revisions based on client feedback, ensuring the final design meets their expectations.
- **Final Design Preparation**: Prepare the final design for screen printing, including detailed specifications for colors, typography, and graphics.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final design files in the required formats for production.

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Inspiration
  - Deliverable: Inspiration board and design references

- **Milestone 4**: Client Review Completed

- **Milestone 5**: Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised design concepts based on feedback
- **Milestone 6**: Final Design Prepared
  - Deliverable: Final design files with specifications for screen printing
- **Milestone 7**: Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Project summary report and client satisfaction survey
