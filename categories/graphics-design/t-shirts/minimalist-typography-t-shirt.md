
# Project Plan: Minimalist Typography T-Shirt

## 1. Executive Summary

The Minimalist Typography T-Shirt project aims to create a striking and modern t-shirt design that embodies the principles of minimalism through typography. The primary objective is to develop a unique graphic that utilizes clean lines and a limited color palette, effectively conveying a powerful message or brand identity. This project will involve close collaboration with the client to fully understand their vision, preferences, and any specific text or phrases they wish to incorporate into the design. Key outcomes include the delivery of multiple design concepts, allowing for client feedback and revisions, ensuring the final product aligns perfectly with the client's expectations. The end result will be a versatile t-shirt design suitable for casual wear and promotional merchandise, ideal for brands looking to make a statement with understated elegance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific text or phrases to be included in the design.
- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather inspiration from current design trends in minimalist typography and analyze competitors' designs.
- **Concept Development**: Create multiple design concepts based on the initial consultation and research findings.
- **Client Review**: Present the design concepts to the client for feedback and discussion.
- **Revisions**: Make necessary revisions based on client feedback, ensuring the design aligns with their expectations.
- **Final Design Approval**: Obtain final approval from the client on the chosen design.
- **Production Preparation**: Prepare the final design files for production, ensuring all specifications meet printing requirements.
- **Delivery**: Deliver the final design files to the client in the required formats.
