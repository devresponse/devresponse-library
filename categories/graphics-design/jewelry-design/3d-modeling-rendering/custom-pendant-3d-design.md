
# Project Plan Template: Custom Pendant 3D Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Pendant 3D Design project aims to create a bespoke 3D model that embodies the unique preferences and themes specified by the client. The primary objective is to transform the client's vision into a tangible design that can be visualized and produced. The project will commence with an initial consultation to gather insights into the client's desired style, materials, and any specific motifs or symbols they wish to incorporate. Following this, a series of preliminary sketches will be developed for the client's review, ensuring that their vision is accurately captured. Once a concept is approved, a high-quality 3D model will be created using advanced software, focusing on both aesthetics and production feasibility. The final deliverables will include the 3D model files in various formats suitable for 3D printing or rendering, along with a detailed presentation showcasing the design from multiple angles. Key outcomes of this project include a refined design that meets the client's expectations, a clear visualization of the custom jewelry piece, and a seamless transition into the production phase.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific requirements for the pendant design.
- **Research and Concept Development**: Gather inspiration and create preliminary sketches based on the client's input, focusing on style, materials, and motifs.
- **Client Review**: Present the preliminary sketches to the client for feedback and approval.
- **3D Modeling**: Develop a high-quality 3D model using advanced software, ensuring that the design is both aesthetically pleasing and feasible for production.
- **Final Review**: Present the 3D model to the client for final approval, making any necessary adjustments based on feedback.
- **Deliverables**: Provide the final 3D model files in various formats (e.g., STL, OBJ) suitable for 3D printing or rendering, along with a detailed presentation showcasing the design from multiple angles.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and design brief
- **Milestone 2**: Preliminary Sketches
  - Deliverable: Series of sketches for client review
- **Milestone 3**: Concept Approval

- **Milestone 4**: 3D Model Development
  - Deliverable: High-quality 3D model file
- **Milestone 5**: Final Review and Adjustments
  - Deliverable: Finalized 3D model and presentation
- **Milestone 6**: Project Completion
  - Deliverable: All final files and documentation delivered to the client
