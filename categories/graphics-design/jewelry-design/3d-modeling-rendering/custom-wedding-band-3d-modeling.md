
# Project Plan: Custom Wedding Band 3D Modeling

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Wedding Band 3D Modeling project aims to create a bespoke 3D model that embodies the unique style and preferences of the client. The primary objective is to deliver a personalized wedding band design that reflects the client's vision, ensuring that every detail is meticulously tailored to their specifications. The project will commence with an initial consultation to explore design ideas, preferred materials, and any specific engravings or motifs that the client wishes to incorporate. Following this, concept sketches will be developed to visualize the design direction, allowing for client feedback and adjustments. Once the concept is approved, the project will transition into the 3D modeling phase, utilizing advanced software to create a detailed and realistic representation of the wedding band. The final deliverable will consist of high-resolution 3D renderings from multiple angles, providing the client with a comprehensive view of the design before production. Key outcomes include a satisfied client with a personalized wedding band design, a streamlined design process, and high-quality visual assets that can be used for marketing or production purposes.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss their vision for the wedding band.
  - Gather information on design preferences, materials, and any specific engravings or motifs.

- **Concept Sketch Development**: 
  - Create a series of hand-drawn or digital sketches based on the client's input.
  - Present sketches to the client for feedback and revisions.

- **3D Modeling**: 
  - Utilize advanced 3D modeling software (e.g., Blender, Rhino, or SolidWorks) to create a detailed model of the approved design.
  - Ensure the model includes all specified features, such as engravings and material textures.

- **Rendering**: 
  - Produce high-resolution 3D renderings from multiple angles to showcase the design.
  - Prepare the renderings for presentation to the client.

- **Final Review and Adjustments**: 
  - Present the final 3D renderings to the client for approval.
  - Make any necessary adjustments based on client feedback.

- **Delivery of Final Assets**: 
  - Provide the client with the final 3D model files and high-resolution renderings.
  - Offer guidance on the next steps for production or 3D printing.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Summary of client preferences and design ideas.

- **Milestone 2**: Concept Sketches
  - Deliverable: A set of concept sketches for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: 3D Model Creation
  - Deliverable: Completed 3D model of the wedding band.

- **Milestone 4**: High-Resolution Renderings
  - Deliverable: Multiple high-resolution renderings from different angles.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Review and Delivery
  - Deliverable: Final 3D model files and renderings delivered to the client.
