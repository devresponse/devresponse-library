
# Project Plan Template: Geometric Jewelry Line Concept Sketches

## 1. Executive Summary

The Geometric Jewelry Line Concept Sketches project aims to create a series of innovative and visually striking concept sketches for a new jewelry line that emphasizes bold geometric shapes and patterns. The primary objective is to produce designs that not only resonate with modern aesthetics but also ensure functionality and wearability. This project will serve as a foundational visual guide for the jewelry pieces, capturing the essence of contemporary artistry. Key outcomes include multiple iterations of sketches that explore various geometric forms, such as triangles, hexagons, and abstract shapes. These sketches will enable the client to select and refine their preferred concepts, ultimately establishing a distinctive identity in the jewelry market. The project is tailored for clients targeting fashion-forward consumers who appreciate unique and edgy designs, ensuring that the final products stand out in a competitive landscape.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Inspiration Gathering**: Conduct research on current jewelry trends, focusing on geometric designs and modern aesthetics. Gather inspiration from various sources, including art, architecture, and nature.
  
- **Concept Development**: Create initial concept sketches that explore a variety of geometric shapes and patterns. This phase will include brainstorming sessions and rough drafts to capture diverse artistic expressions.

- **Client Review and Feedback**: Present the initial sketches to the client for feedback. Gather insights and preferences to refine the designs further.

- **Refinement of Designs**: Based on client feedback, refine the selected sketches, enhancing details and ensuring that each design aligns with the client's vision and brand identity.

- **Final Deliverables**: Prepare a comprehensive portfolio of the final sketches, including multiple iterations of each design. Ensure that the sketches are presented in a professional format suitable for client review and future production.
