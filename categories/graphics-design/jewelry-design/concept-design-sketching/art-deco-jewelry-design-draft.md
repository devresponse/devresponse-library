
# Project Plan Template: Art Deco Jewelry Design Draft

## 1. Executive Summary

The Art Deco Jewelry Design Draft project aims to create a unique concept sketch that encapsulates the essence of the Art Deco movement. The primary objective is to develop a design that features characteristic geometric patterns, bold lines, and vintage elegance, which are hallmarks of this iconic style. This project will involve thorough research into Art Deco motifs and styles to ensure that the final design is both authentic and original. Key outcomes include a detailed sketch that not only serves as a visual representation of the jewelry piece but also emphasizes functionality and wearability for modern consumers. By blending historical inspiration with contemporary design sensibilities, this project will provide clients with a compelling preview of the final product, facilitating informed decisions before moving into production.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct in-depth research on Art Deco motifs, styles, and historical context.
  - Analyze existing jewelry designs inspired by the Art Deco movement.
  
- **Concept Development**: 
  - Brainstorm and sketch initial ideas based on research findings.
  - Develop multiple design concepts that reflect the Art Deco aesthetic.

- **Design Refinement**: 
  - Select the most promising concept and refine it through iterative sketching.
  - Incorporate feedback from stakeholders to enhance the design.

- **Final Sketch Creation**: 
  - Produce a polished, detailed sketch of the final design.
  - Ensure the sketch highlights both aesthetic qualities and practical wearability.

- **Presentation**: 
  - Prepare a presentation of the final sketch for client review.
  - Include a rationale for design choices and how they align with the Art Deco movement.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings on Art Deco styles and motifs.

- **Milestone 2**: Initial Concept Development
  - Deliverable: A set of preliminary sketches showcasing various design ideas.

- **Milestone 3**: Design Refinement
  - Deliverable: A refined sketch based on selected concept and stakeholder feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Sketch Creation
  - Deliverable: A high-quality, detailed sketch of the final jewelry design.

- **Milestone 5**: Client Presentation
  - Deliverable: Presentation materials including the final sketch and design rationale.
