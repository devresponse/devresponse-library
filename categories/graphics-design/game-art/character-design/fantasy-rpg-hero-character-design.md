
# Project Plan Template: Fantasy RPG Hero Character Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Fantasy RPG Hero Character Design project aims to create a visually captivating hero character that aligns with the narrative and aesthetic of the fantasy RPG game. The primary objective is to develop a character that resonates with the target audience, enhancing player engagement and contributing to an immersive gaming experience. The project will begin with thorough research into the game's lore, ensuring that the character's design reflects the world it inhabits. Following this, multiple iterations of the character will be conceptualized and sketched, focusing on unique traits and attributes. The design will include detailed armor, weapons, and accessories, ensuring both visual appeal and functionality within the game. Key outcomes will include high-resolution character art, turnarounds, and color palettes, providing a comprehensive visual reference for animation and gameplay. Ultimately, this project seeks to deliver a memorable hero character that captivates players and enriches the overall gaming experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct in-depth research on the game's lore and aesthetic style.
  - Analyze existing characters and themes within the game to ensure alignment.
  
- **Concept Development**:
  - Create multiple character sketches and iterations.
  - Focus on unique traits, personality, and backstory that resonate with the target audience.
  
- **Design Finalization**:
  - Develop detailed designs for armor, weapons, and accessories.
  - Ensure designs are practical for animation and gameplay mechanics.
  
- **Deliverables**:
  - High-resolution character art.
  - Character turnarounds showcasing different angles.
  - Color palettes for the character's design.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and insights.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Concept Development
  - Deliverable: Presentation of character sketches and iterations for feedback.

- **Milestone 3**: Finalization of Character Design
  - Deliverable: Final character art, including detailed designs for armor, weapons, and accessories.

- **Milestone 4**: Submission of Final Deliverables
  - Deliverable: High-resolution character art, turnarounds, and color palettes.
