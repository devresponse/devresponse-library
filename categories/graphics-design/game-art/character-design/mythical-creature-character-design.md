
# Project Plan: Mythical Creature Character Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Mythical Creature Character Design project aims to create a unique and captivating character for a fantasy game, drawing inspiration from various elements of folklore, mythology, and imaginative concepts. The primary objective is to design a character that stands out in the gaming landscape, enhancing the overall player experience through visual storytelling. The project will commence with comprehensive research into different mythologies to gather inspiration and identify distinctive traits that can be integrated into the character's design. Following the research phase, initial sketches will be developed to explore diverse forms, colors, and features that align with the game's narrative and visual style. Once these sketches are refined and approved, detailed character sheets will be produced, showcasing front, side, and back views, along with color palettes and texture references. The key outcomes of this project include a finalized character design that captivates players, contributes to the game's immersive experience, and aligns with the overall artistic direction of the project.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct in-depth research into various mythologies and folklore.
  - Identify unique traits and characteristics of mythical creatures.
  - Compile a mood board of inspirations and references.

- **Concept Development**:
  - Create initial sketches based on research findings.
  - Explore various forms, colors, and features.
  - Present sketches for feedback and approval.

- **Character Sheet Creation**:
  - Develop detailed character sheets that include:
    - Front, side, and back views of the character.
    - Color palettes and texture references.
  - Ensure that the character design aligns with the game's narrative and visual style.

- **Final Review and Adjustments**:
  - Conduct a final review of the character design with stakeholders.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on feedback.
  - Prepare final files for delivery.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report and mood board.

- **Milestone 2**: Initial Sketches Approval
  - Deliverable: Set of initial sketches for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Character Sheet Development
  - Deliverable: Detailed character sheets including all required views and references.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Review and Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized character design files ready for integration into the game.
