
# Project Plan Template: Cyberpunk Hacker Character Concept

## 1. Executive Summary

The Cyberpunk Hacker Character Concept project aims to create a distinctive character that encapsulates the essence of a dystopian future, appealing to players through its design and narrative significance. The primary objective is to develop a unique hacker character equipped with intricate futuristic tech gear, showcasing their hacking prowess through a variety of gadgets and tools. The character will be adorned in neon-lit clothing, featuring vibrant colors that contrast sharply with the dark, gritty aesthetics typical of the cyberpunk genre. Key outcomes include a series of initial concept sketches, a detailed character design, and a carefully selected color palette. The project will culminate in a comprehensive character sheet that presents the character from multiple angles, along with close-ups of essential features. This character will not only serve as a visual focal point but will also enhance the immersive experience of the game, resonating with players through its rebellious attitude and dynamic design elements.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Inspiration Gathering**: 
  - Analyze existing cyberpunk aesthetics and character designs.
  - Gather references for futuristic tech gear, neon colors, and rebellious postures.

- **Initial Concept Sketches**: 
  - Create multiple rough sketches to explore different design directions.
  - Present sketches for feedback and select the most promising concepts.

- **Detailed Character Design**: 
  - Develop the chosen concept into a detailed character design.
  - Focus on the character's attire, tech gear, and overall silhouette.

- **Color Palette Selection**: 
  - Create a vibrant color palette that reflects the cyberpunk theme.
  - Ensure colors enhance the character's personality and fit within the game’s environment.

- **Final Character Sheet Creation**: 
  - Produce a comprehensive character sheet that includes:
    - Multiple angles of the character.
    - Close-ups of key features (e.g., facial expressions, tech gear).
    - Annotations highlighting design choices and character traits.

- **Revisions and Finalization**: 
  - Incorporate feedback from stakeholders.
  - Finalize the character design and prepare files for integration into the game.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Research and Inspiration Gathering
  - Deliverable: Research document with references and inspiration.

- **Milestone 2**: Initial Concept Sketches
  - Deliverable: Set of 5-10 rough sketches for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Detailed Character Design
  - Deliverable: Finalized character design illustration.

- **Milestone 4**: Color Palette Selection
  - Deliverable: Color palette document with swatches.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Character Sheet Creation
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive character sheet with multiple angles and close-ups.

- **Milestone 6**: Revisions and Finalization
  - Deliverable: Final character design files ready for game integration.
