
# Project Plan Template: Fantasy Merchant Character Concept

## 1. Executive Summary

The Fantasy Merchant Character Concept project aims to create a visually striking and memorable character that embodies the essence of a trader in a fantastical setting. The primary objective is to design a character that not only captivates players with its elaborate clothing and vibrant colors but also serves as a pivotal figure in the game's narrative. The character will showcase intricate details that reflect their status and expertise in commerce, while also incorporating unique wares that highlight the merchant's specialization. Key outcomes of this project include the development of initial sketches, a carefully selected color palette, and final character renderings that align with the game's overall aesthetic and story. By crafting a character with charm, wit, and a hint of mystery, we aim to enhance the player's experience and engagement within the game world. Ultimately, this project will contribute to a richer narrative experience, making the merchant an integral part of the player's journey.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Inspiration Gathering**: 
  - Conduct research on fantasy merchants in various media.
  - Collect reference images and materials that inspire character design.

- **Initial Sketches**: 
  - Create multiple rough sketches of the character, focusing on different poses and expressions.
  - Explore various clothing styles and accessories that reflect the merchant's status.

- **Color Palette Selection**: 
  - Develop a color palette that complements the character's design and the game's aesthetic.
  - Present color options for feedback and revisions.

- **Final Character Renderings**: 
  - Produce high-quality digital renderings of the character based on approved sketches and color palette.
  - Include detailed views of clothing, accessories, and unique wares.

- **Character Personality Development**: 
  - Define the character's backstory, traits, and dialogue style.
  - Create a character profile that outlines their role in the game and interactions with players.

- **Feedback and Revisions**: 
  - Present designs to stakeholders for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on input received.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Compile all design assets and documentation for final delivery.
  - Ensure all files are organized and accessible for the development team.
