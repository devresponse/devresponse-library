
# Project Plan Template: Space Explorer Character Concept

## 1. Executive Summary

The Space Explorer Character Concept project aims to design a captivating character that embodies the essence of adventure within a sci-fi gaming universe. The primary objective is to create a visually striking character equipped with a high-tech spacesuit that showcases advanced technology and functionality, suitable for various extraterrestrial environments. This project will commence with an initial brainstorming session to discuss the character's backstory, personality traits, and the specific visual elements that will make them stand out. Key outcomes include a series of sketches exploring different design options, focusing on the character's silhouette, color palette, and unique features such as exploration tools and gadgets. The final deliverable will be a polished character design, complete with detailed views and annotations that highlight the character's functionality and aesthetic appeal. This character will not only serve as a visual representation but also enhance the narrative and immersive experience of the game, ultimately contributing to player engagement and satisfaction.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Brainstorming Session**: Conduct a collaborative meeting with stakeholders to define the character's backstory, personality traits, and visual elements.
- **Research and Inspiration Gathering**: Collect references and inspirations from existing sci-fi media, focusing on character design, technology, and environments.
- **Sketch Development**: Create a series of preliminary sketches to explore different design options, including:
  - Character silhouette
  - Color palette
  - Unique features (exploration tools, gadgets)
- **Feedback and Iteration**: Present sketches to stakeholders for feedback and make necessary revisions based on input.
- **Final Character Design**: Develop a polished character design that includes:
  - Detailed views (front, side, back)
  - Annotations highlighting functionality and aesthetic appeal
- **Presentation of Final Deliverables**: Prepare a presentation to showcase the final character design to stakeholders.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Brainstorming Session
  - Deliverable: Document outlining character backstory and traits
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Research and Inspiration Gathering
  - Deliverable: Mood board with visual references
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Sketch Development
  - Deliverable: Series of preliminary sketches
- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Feedback and Iteration
  - Deliverable: Revised sketches based on stakeholder feedback
- **Milestone 5**: Completion of Final Character Design
  - Deliverable: Polished character design with detailed views and annotations
- **Milestone 6**: Presentation of Final Deliverables
  - Deliverable: Presentation showcasing the final character design
