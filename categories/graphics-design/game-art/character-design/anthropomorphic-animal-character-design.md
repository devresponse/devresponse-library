
# Project Plan: Anthropomorphic Animal Character Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Anthropomorphic Animal Character Design project aims to create a captivating character that seamlessly blends human-like traits with distinct animal features, serving as a compelling protagonist or sidekick for your game. The primary objective is to develop a character that not only enhances the visual appeal of the game but also fosters a deeper connection with players, thereby enriching their overall gaming experience. The project will commence with an in-depth analysis of the game's narrative and visual style, ensuring that the character aligns with the overarching aesthetic. Through collaborative brainstorming sessions, we will explore various animal types and their corresponding human traits, leading to the creation of initial sketches that capture the character's personality and charm. Following the establishment of a design direction, we will refine the chosen concept into a detailed character model, complete with color palettes, textures, and expressions. The key outcomes of this project will include high-quality character art assets that are ready for integration into your game, ultimately contributing to a more immersive and engaging player experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct meetings with stakeholders to understand the game's narrative, target audience, and visual style.
- **Brainstorming Sessions**: Facilitate collaborative sessions to explore various animal types and human traits that can be incorporated into the character design.
- **Concept Sketching**: Create initial sketches that highlight the character's personality, charm, and unique traits.
- **Design Direction Approval**: Present the sketches to stakeholders for feedback and approval on the design direction.
- **Character Model Development**: Refine the chosen concept into a detailed character model, including:
  - Color palettes
  - Textures
  - Expressions
- **Final Art Assets Creation**: Produce high-quality character art assets, ensuring they are optimized for integration into the game.
- **Review and Revisions**: Allow for a round of revisions based on stakeholder feedback before finalizing the assets.
- **Delivery**: Provide all final assets in the required formats for seamless integration into the game.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed

- **Milestone 4**: Design Direction Approved

- **Milestone 5**: Character Model Development Completed
  - Deliverable: Detailed character model with color palettes, textures, and expressions.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Art Assets Delivered
