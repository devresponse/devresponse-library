
# Project Plan Template: Historical Warrior Character Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Historical Warrior Character Design project aims to create a meticulously crafted character that embodies the essence of a specific historical warrior from a chosen era. The primary objective is to develop a character that is not only visually stunning but also historically accurate, enhancing the immersive experience for players in a gaming environment. This project will involve extensive research into the selected time period to ensure authenticity in attire, weaponry, and cultural elements. Key outcomes include the delivery of initial sketches for client feedback, detailed character models, and a comprehensive character sheet that showcases various poses and expressions. Ultimately, this project seeks to elevate the player's experience by providing a character that resonates with the historical context and fits seamlessly into the game's narrative and aesthetic.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct thorough research on the chosen historical period, focusing on:
    - Armor and weaponry
    - Cultural elements and attire
    - Historical context and significance of the warrior

- **Concept Development**:
  - Create initial sketches based on research findings.
  - Present sketches to the client for feedback and revisions.

- **Character Modeling**:
  - Develop detailed 3D character models incorporating feedback.
  - Create high-resolution textures that reflect the materials and colors of the era.

- **Character Sheet Creation**:
  - Design a comprehensive character sheet that includes:
    - Various poses and expressions
    - Detailed descriptions of attire and weapons
    - Background information that enhances the character's story

- **Final Review and Adjustments**:
  - Present the final character design and sheet to the client.
  - Make any necessary adjustments based on final feedback.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and insights.

- **Milestone 2**: Initial Sketches Submission
  - Deliverable: Set of initial character sketches for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Finalization of Character Model
  - Deliverable: Detailed 3D character model ready for integration.

- **Milestone 4**: Character Sheet Delivery
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive character sheet showcasing poses, expressions, and backstory.
