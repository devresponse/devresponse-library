
# Project Plan Template: Sci-Fi Space Station Interior Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Sci-Fi Space Station Interior Design project is to create a visually captivating and immersive environment that embodies futuristic aesthetics suitable for a variety of sci-fi game scenarios. This project aims to design an interior space that features sleek metallic surfaces, illuminated panels emitting a soft glow, and intricate machinery that adds depth and realism to the setting. The project overview includes close collaboration with the client to ensure that the design aligns with the overall game narrative and mechanics. Key outcomes will include multiple views of the interior space, ensuring comprehensive coverage of all angles for optimal player immersion. By delivering a high-tech atmosphere, this project will enhance the player's experience and engagement, making it a standout feature in the game.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Concept Development**
  - Conduct research on existing space station designs in sci-fi media.
  - Develop initial concepts and sketches based on client input and game requirements.

- **Design Creation**
  - Create detailed 3D models of the space station interior.
  - Design elements to include:
    - Sleek metallic surfaces
    - Illuminated panels with adjustable lighting
    - Complex machinery and control panels
    - Functional areas such as living quarters, control rooms, and recreational spaces

- **Client Collaboration**
  - Schedule regular meetings with the client to review progress and gather feedback.
  - Adjust designs based on client input to ensure alignment with the game narrative.

- **Final Deliverables**
  - Produce high-resolution renders of the interior from multiple angles.
  - Provide a comprehensive design document detailing materials, colors, and lighting schemes.
