
# Project Plan: Medieval Market Stall Props

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Medieval Market Stall Props" project aims to create a captivating series of props that will enhance the visual storytelling of gaming environments. This project focuses on developing intricately designed fruit baskets filled with various fruits, rustic barrels that evoke authenticity, detailed carts for transporting goods, and vibrant fabric canopies that provide shade and color to the market scene. Each prop will be crafted with meticulous attention to historical accuracy and artistic flair, ensuring they seamlessly fit into the medieval theme. The key outcomes of this project include a collection of high-quality, optimized props ready for integration into game engines, enriching the aesthetic appeal of the game. By creating an immersive experience for players, this project will transport them to a lively medieval marketplace, enhancing their engagement and enjoyment of the gaming experience. Ultimately, the project will not only serve as a visual enhancement but also contribute to the overall narrative and atmosphere of the game.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Medieval Market Stall Props project includes the following key components:

- **Research and Design**: Conduct thorough research on medieval market stalls, including historical references and artistic styles. Develop initial sketches and concepts for each prop.
  
- **Prop Development**:
  - **Fruit Baskets**: Design and create multiple fruit baskets filled with a variety of historically accurate fruits.
  - **Rustic Barrels**: Craft barrels that reflect the craftsmanship of the medieval period, ensuring they are visually appealing and functional.
  - **Transport Carts**: Develop detailed carts that can be used for transporting goods, incorporating realistic design elements.
  - **Fabric Canopies**: Create vibrant fabric canopies that provide shade and enhance the visual appeal of the market scene.

- **Prototyping**: Build prototypes of each prop to test design and functionality.

- **Optimization for Game Engines**: Ensure all props are optimized for use in various game engines, focusing on polygon count, texture resolution, and compatibility.

- **Final Review and Adjustments**: Conduct a final review of all props, making necessary adjustments based on feedback and testing.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Design Phase
  - Deliverable: Finalized sketches and design concepts for all props.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Prop Development
  - Deliverable: Finished models of fruit baskets, rustic barrels, transport carts, and fabric canopies.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Prototyping
  - Deliverable: Functional prototypes of all props for testing.

- **Milestone 4**: Optimization for Game Engines
  - Deliverable: All props optimized and ready for integration into game engines.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Review and Adjustments
  - Deliverable: Finalized props ready for delivery.
