
# Illustrated Storyboards for Children's Book Planning

## Executive Summary

The "Illustrated Storyboards for Children's Book Planning" project aims to provide children's book authors with a comprehensive storyboard service that enhances their storytelling process. The primary objective is to create a series of rough sketches that visualize the narrative, allowing authors to see key scenes, character interactions, and the overall flow of their story. By illustrating pivotal moments, this project will enable authors to refine their storytelling, ensuring that visual elements align with the intended message and tone of the book. Key outcomes include a set of sequential images that facilitate informed decisions about pacing, character development, and scene transitions. This service is particularly beneficial for authors who wish to thoughtfully consider every aspect of their book before proceeding to final illustrations, ultimately leading to a more engaging and cohesive final product.

## Detailed Scope of Work

1. **Initial Consultation**
   - Meet with the author to discuss the book's themes, characters, and key scenes.
   - Gather necessary materials, including the manuscript and any reference images.

2. **Storyboard Development**
   - Create rough sketches for each pivotal moment in the story.
   - Ensure that each sketch captures the essence of the narrative and aligns with the author's vision.

3. **Feedback and Revisions**
   - Present the initial storyboard to the author for feedback.
   - Make necessary revisions based on the author's input to refine the sketches.

4. **Finalization of Storyboard**
   - Prepare the final version of the storyboard, incorporating all revisions.
   - Deliver a digital copy of the storyboard to the author in a suitable format.

5. **Post-Delivery Support**
   - Offer a follow-up consultation to discuss the storyboard and any further needs the author may have.

## Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Initial Consultation Completed**
  - Deliverable: Summary document outlining the discussion points and agreed-upon project scope.

- **Milestone 2: First Draft of Storyboard Completed**
  - Deliverable: A series of rough sketches representing key scenes.

- **Milestone 3: Feedback Session Conducted**
  - Deliverable: Documented feedback from the author and a list of required revisions.

- **Milestone 4: Final Storyboard Delivered**
  - Deliverable: Finalized storyboard in digital format, ready for author use.

- **Milestone 5: Post-Delivery Consultation Completed**
  - Deliverable: Summary of the follow-up discussion and any additional recommendations.
