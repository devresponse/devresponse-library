
# Project Plan Template: Fantasy Erotica Character Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Fantasy Erotica Character Design project aims to create a series of custom NSFW fantasy characters that embody sensuality and imagination. The primary objective is to collaborate closely with the client to understand their unique vision, preferences, and specific requirements for each character. This project will involve a comprehensive design process, including initial concept sketches, detailed character illustrations, and carefully curated color palettes that enhance the characters' allure and appeal. Key outcomes include high-resolution digital and print-ready illustrations that resonate with the intended themes and narratives, ensuring that each character is not only visually captivating but also aligns with community standards and guidelines. By exploring the boundaries of fantasy art, this project will provide the client with unique and engaging characters suitable for various mediums, including games, stories, or personal collections.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and specific requirements for the characters.
- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather references and inspiration based on the client's input to inform the design process.
- **Concept Sketches**: Create a series of initial concept sketches for each character, focusing on different poses, expressions, and attire.
- **Client Feedback**: Present the concept sketches to the client for feedback and revisions.
- **Detailed Character Illustrations**: Develop detailed illustrations based on the approved concept sketches, incorporating intricate details and features.
- **Color Palette Development**: Create color palettes that enhance the characters' allure and align with the overall theme.
- **Final Revisions**: Make any necessary adjustments based on client feedback on the detailed illustrations and color palettes.
- **Final Deliverables**: Provide the final character designs in high-resolution formats suitable for both digital and print use.
