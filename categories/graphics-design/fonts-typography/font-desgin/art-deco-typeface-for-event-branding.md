
# Project Plan Template: Art Deco Typeface for Event Branding

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Art Deco Typeface for Event Branding" project aims to create a unique typeface inspired by the elegance and opulence of the 1920s Art Deco movement. This project is specifically tailored for high-profile events such as galas and themed parties, where visual identity plays a crucial role in setting the tone and enhancing the overall experience. The primary objective is to develop a versatile font that embodies intricate details, bold geometric shapes, and symmetrical designs, ensuring it resonates with the target audience. Key outcomes include a comprehensive research phase to ensure authenticity, a series of initial sketches and digital drafts, and a collaborative revision process based on client feedback. Ultimately, the final product will serve as a powerful branding tool, effectively capturing the essence of each event and elevating visual communication across various materials, including invitations, signage, and promotional content.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: Conduct in-depth research on Art Deco aesthetics, including historical context, key design elements, and existing typefaces.
- **Initial Sketches**: Create a series of hand-drawn sketches that explore different styles and characteristics of the Art Deco movement.
- **Digital Drafts**: Develop digital versions of selected sketches using font design software, ensuring scalability and versatility.
- **Client Feedback**: Present initial drafts to the client for feedback, focusing on aspects such as legibility, style, and overall impact.
- **Revisions**: Implement changes based on client feedback, refining the typeface to meet project objectives.
- **Finalization**: Prepare the final typeface files in various formats (OTF, TTF) and provide usage guidelines for branding applications.
- **Delivery**: Submit the final product along with a comprehensive branding guide that includes examples of usage across different materials.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase  
  **Deliverable**: Research report summarizing findings and design inspirations.

- **Milestone 2**: Submission of Initial Sketches  
  **Deliverable**: A portfolio of hand-drawn sketches showcasing various design concepts.

  **Deliverable**: Digital typeface drafts for client review.

  **Deliverable**: Documented feedback and notes for revisions.

- **Milestone 5**: Submission of Revised Typeface  
  **Deliverable**: Updated digital typeface files reflecting client feedback.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Delivery  
  **Deliverable**: Final typeface files and branding guide.
