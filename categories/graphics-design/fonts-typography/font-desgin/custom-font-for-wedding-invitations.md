
# Project Plan Template: Custom Font for Wedding Invitations

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the "Custom Font for Wedding Invitations" project is to create a bespoke typeface that embodies romance and elegance, specifically tailored for wedding stationery. This project will involve a collaborative design process, where the unique personality of the couple and the theme of their wedding will be reflected in the font. The key outcomes include the delivery of a complete font package that features various styles and weights, ensuring versatility for both digital and print applications. By incorporating decorative flourishes and calligraphic elements, the font will not only serve as a means of communication but will also enhance the overall aesthetic of the wedding materials. Regular consultations with the client will be conducted to ensure alignment with their vision, resulting in a final product that meets their expectations and elevates their wedding invitations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Inspiration Gathering**: 
  - Conduct research on current font trends in wedding stationery.
  - Gather inspiration from the couple's wedding theme, colors, and personal style.

- **Initial Concept Development**: 
  - Create initial sketches and concepts for the custom font.
  - Present concepts to the client for feedback and revisions.

- **Font Design**: 
  - Develop the font using design software, focusing on decorative flourishes and calligraphic elements.
  - Create multiple styles and weights of the font (e.g., regular, bold, italic).

- **Client Consultations**: 
  - Schedule regular meetings with the client to discuss progress and gather feedback.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on client input.

- **Finalization of Font Package**: 
  - Prepare the final font files in various formats (e.g., OTF, TTF).
  - Include a style guide that outlines usage, licensing, and installation instructions.

- **Delivery**: 
  - Deliver the complete font package to the client.
  - Provide ongoing support for any questions or issues related to the font.
