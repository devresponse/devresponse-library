
# Project Plan Template: Vintage-Inspired Typeface for Retro Branding

## 1. Executive Summary

The Vintage-Inspired Typeface for Retro Branding project aims to create a unique typeface that captures the essence of nostalgia while appealing to modern branding needs. The primary objective is to design a typeface that embodies the charm of historical typography trends, drawing inspiration from classic typefaces that have proven their longevity in the design world. This project will involve comprehensive research into vintage typography, followed by the development of initial sketches that will be refined into a versatile digital format. Key outcomes include a fully functional typeface suitable for various applications, such as branding, packaging, and advertising, alongside a detailed style guide that provides guidelines on usage, spacing, sizing, and typeface pairing. By the end of this project, brands will have a distinctive typeface that not only evokes nostalgia but also enhances their visual storytelling, ensuring a strong connection with their target audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct thorough research on historical typography trends.
  - Identify and analyze classic typefaces that have influenced modern design.
  
- **Concept Development**: 
  - Create initial sketches based on research findings.
  - Develop multiple design concepts for review.

- **Digital Refinement**: 
  - Select the most promising sketches and refine them into digital formats.
  - Ensure the typeface is versatile for various applications, including branding, packaging, and advertising.

- **Style Guide Creation**: 
  - Develop a comprehensive style guide that includes:
    - Font usage guidelines
    - Spacing and sizing recommendations
    - Pairing suggestions with other typefaces

- **Feedback and Revisions**: 
  - Present the typeface and style guide to stakeholders for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on feedback received.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Provide the final typeface files in various formats (e.g., OTF, TTF).
  - Deliver the completed style guide.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and inspirations.

- **Milestone 2**: Initial Concept Sketches
  - Deliverable: A collection of initial sketches for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Digital Typeface Development
  - Deliverable: Digital typeface files (preliminary version).

- **Milestone 4**: Style Guide Draft
  - Deliverable: Draft version of the style guide.

- **Milestone 5**: Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised typeface and style guide based on stakeholder feedback.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Final typeface files and completed style guide.
