
# Project Plan Template: Typography-Based Posters

## 1. Executive Summary

The Typography-Based Posters project aims to create unique hand-lettered posters that serve as compelling promotional materials for various events, advertising campaigns, or personal projects. The primary objective is to develop artistic typography that not only captures attention but also effectively communicates the intended message. This project will begin with a comprehensive consultation to understand the client's vision, target audience, and specific requirements. Following this, initial sketches will be presented for client feedback, ensuring alignment with their expectations. Upon approval, the final hand-lettered artwork will be digitized and delivered in high-resolution formats suitable for both print and digital use. Key outcomes include the delivery of visually stunning posters that encapsulate the essence of the event or brand, enhancing its memorability and impact. Ultimately, this project seeks to blend creativity with strategic communication, resulting in artwork that resonates with viewers and fulfills the client's promotional needs.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**
  - Conduct an initial meeting with the client to discuss their vision, target audience, and specific requirements.
  - Gather reference materials and examples of styles the client admires.

- **Design Phase**
  - Create initial sketches based on the consultation feedback.
  - Present sketches to the client for review and feedback.
  - Revise sketches as necessary based on client input.

- **Finalization Phase**
  - Once the sketches are approved, create the final hand-lettered artwork.
  - Digitize the artwork for high-resolution output.

- **Delivery Phase**
  - Provide the final artwork in various formats (e.g., PDF, PNG, JPEG) suitable for print and digital use.
  - Include a style guide outlining font usage, color palettes, and any other relevant design elements.

- **Post-Delivery Support**
  - Offer a limited period for revisions or adjustments based on client feedback after delivery.
  - Provide guidance on how to best utilize the posters for promotional purposes.
