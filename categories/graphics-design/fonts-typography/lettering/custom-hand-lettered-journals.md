
# Project Plan Template: Custom Hand-Lettered Journals

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Hand-Lettered Journals project aims to create unique, personalized journal covers that reflect the individual style and preferences of each client. The primary objective is to enhance the aesthetic appeal of journals while providing a personal touch that makes each piece truly one-of-a-kind. The project will begin with an initial consultation to discuss themes, color schemes, and specific elements that the client wishes to incorporate into their journal cover. Following this, the designer will present several hand-lettered cover concepts for the client to choose from. Once a concept is selected, the designer will finalize the artwork, ensuring it is polished and ready for print. The key outcomes of this project include the delivery of high-resolution digital files suitable for printing, along with a mockup of the journal cover. This project not only fulfills the artistic vision of the client but also results in a tangible product that they can cherish and use.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific elements they want to include in their journal cover.
- **Concept Development**: Create multiple hand-lettered cover concepts based on the client's input, focusing on themes, color schemes, and design elements.
- **Client Review**: Present the cover concepts to the client for feedback and selection of their preferred design.
- **Final Artwork Creation**: Refine the selected concept, ensuring all elements are polished and ready for print.
- **Deliverables**: Provide the client with high-resolution digital files of the final artwork, along with a mockup of the journal cover.
