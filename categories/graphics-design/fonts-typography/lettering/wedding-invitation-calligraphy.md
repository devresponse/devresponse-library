
# Project Plan Template: Wedding Invitation Calligraphy

## 1. Executive Summary

The Wedding Invitation Calligraphy project aims to create exquisite hand-lettered designs tailored specifically for wedding invitations and related stationery. The primary objective is to provide couples with a unique and artistic representation of their personal style and wedding theme, ensuring that each piece resonates with their love story. This project will encompass the design of custom wedding invitations, RSVP cards, and envelopes, all featuring elegant calligraphy that enhances the overall aesthetic of the wedding. Key outcomes include high-resolution digital files suitable for printing, allowing couples to showcase their individuality on their special day. The process will be collaborative, incorporating client preferences and ideas, resulting in personalized designs that reflect the couple's commitment and joy. By the end of this project, clients will receive beautifully crafted stationery that not only serves its functional purpose but also adds a touch of artistry and elegance to their wedding celebration.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the couple to discuss their vision, preferences, and wedding theme.
- **Design Concept Development**: Create initial design concepts based on the consultation, including color schemes, typography, and layout.
- **Client Review and Feedback**: Present the design concepts to the couple for feedback and make necessary revisions.
- **Final Design Approval**: Obtain final approval from the couple on the designs before proceeding to production.
- **Creation of Digital Files**: Produce high-resolution digital files for wedding invitations, RSVP cards, and envelopes.
- **Delivery of Final Files**: Provide the couple with the final digital files via email or a secure file-sharing platform.
- **Optional Printing Services**: Offer printing services for the stationery if requested by the couple.
