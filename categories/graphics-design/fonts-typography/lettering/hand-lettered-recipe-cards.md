
# Project Plan: Hand-Lettered Recipe Cards

## 1. Executive Summary

The Hand-Lettered Recipe Cards project aims to create custom, hand-lettered recipe cards that seamlessly blend artistic expression with practical use. The primary objective is to deliver unique, personalized cards that not only serve as functional kitchen tools but also as beautiful decorative pieces or thoughtful gifts. Each card will feature distinctive lettering styles tailored to the client's preferences, ensuring that the final product resonates with their individual taste. The project will involve an initial consultation phase to discuss the client's vision, preferred color schemes, and specific recipes to be featured. Key outcomes include high-resolution digital files suitable for printing, allowing clients to reproduce their cards while maintaining quality and charm. This project is ideal for individuals seeking to add a personal touch to their culinary creations or to present a special gift to friends and family. Ultimately, each hand-lettered card will be crafted with care, transforming recipes into works of art that reflect the client's personality and style.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific recipes they want to include.
  - Gather information on preferred color schemes, lettering styles, and any additional design elements.

- **Design Phase**: 
  - Create initial sketches based on the consultation feedback.
  - Present design options to the client for review and feedback.
  - Revise designs as necessary until the client is satisfied.

- **Finalization**: 
  - Prepare the final hand-lettered designs.
  - Convert designs into high-resolution digital files suitable for printing.

- **Delivery**: 
  - Provide the client with the final digital files via email or a secure file-sharing platform.
  - Offer guidance on printing options and recommendations for card stock.

- **Follow-Up**: 
  - Conduct a follow-up meeting to ensure client satisfaction and gather feedback for future projects.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Client brief document summarizing preferences and requirements.

- **Milestone 2**: Design Options Presented
  - Deliverable: Initial sketches and design options for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Final Design Approval
  - Deliverable: Client-approved final design.

- **Milestone 4**: High-Resolution Digital Files Delivered
  - Deliverable: Final digital files sent to the client.

- **Milestone 5**: Project Closure and Feedback
  - Deliverable: Follow-up meeting notes and feedback documentation.
