
# Project Plan Template: Hand-Lettered Maps

## 1. Executive Summary

The Hand-Lettered Maps project aims to create custom, hand-lettered maps that serve as both functional navigation tools and artistic expressions. Our objective is to blend artistry with practicality, producing maps that highlight specific locations, events, or imaginative realms through unique hand lettering techniques. Each map will be meticulously crafted to reflect the character and essence of the subject matter, whether it be a vibrant city, a whimsical event, or an entirely fictional world. Key outcomes of this project include delivering a beautifully designed map that resonates with the client's vision, ensuring alignment through initial consultations, sketches, and drafts. The final product will not only serve as a practical guide but also as a stunning piece of art, suitable for framing or digital use. This project is particularly suited for clients looking to enhance their branding, events, or creative endeavors with a personal touch.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Engage with the client to understand their vision, preferences, and specific requirements for the map.
- **Research and Concept Development**: Gather information about the locations or themes to be represented in the map. Develop initial concepts based on client input.
- **Sketching and Drafting**: Create preliminary sketches and drafts of the map, incorporating hand lettering and artistic elements. Present these drafts to the client for feedback.
- **Revisions**: Make necessary adjustments based on client feedback to ensure the map aligns with their expectations.
- **Final Design**: Complete the final version of the hand-lettered map, incorporating all agreed-upon elements and details.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final product in both physical and digital formats, ensuring high-quality resolution for printing and online use.
- **Post-Delivery Support**: Offer support for any questions or additional requests related to the final product.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client vision and requirements
- **Milestone 2**: Concept Development Completed
  - Deliverable: Initial concept sketches for client review
- **Milestone 3**: Draft Review Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised drafts based on client feedback
- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approved
  - Deliverable: Final hand-lettered map design
- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of Final Product
  - Deliverable: Physical and digital copies of the map
