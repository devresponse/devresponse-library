
# Project Plan Template: Hand-Lettered Greeting Cards

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Hand-Lettered Greeting Cards" project aims to create custom, hand-lettered greeting cards that cater to a variety of occasions, including birthdays, holidays, and expressions of gratitude. The primary objective is to deliver unique, artistic lettering that embodies the sentiment of each occasion, ensuring a personal touch that resonates with the recipient. This project will involve initial consultations with clients to capture their vision and preferences, followed by the creation of multiple design drafts for review. Key outcomes include high-resolution digital files suitable for printing, along with recommendations for card stock and printing options. Ultimately, this project seeks to provide visually stunning cards while creating a memorable experience for both the sender and the recipient, transforming each card into a cherished keepsake.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and specific occasions for the cards.
- **Design Drafts**: Create multiple design drafts based on the client's input, showcasing different styles and lettering techniques.
- **Client Feedback**: Present the drafts to the client for feedback and revisions. This may involve several rounds of adjustments to ensure satisfaction.
- **Final Design**: Finalize the selected design, ensuring it meets the client's expectations and is ready for production.
- **Digital Files**: Prepare high-resolution digital files of the final design, suitable for printing.
- **Recommendations**: Provide recommendations for card stock and printing options, including potential vendors and pricing.
- **Delivery**: Ensure timely delivery of digital files and any additional materials as agreed upon with the client.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client preferences and vision.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Design Drafts Created
  - Deliverable: Three design drafts presented to the client.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback Received
  - Deliverable: Documented feedback and requested revisions.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approved
  - Deliverable: Finalized design ready for production.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Digital Files Delivered
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital files sent to the client.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Recommendations Provided
  - Deliverable: List of recommended card stock and printing options.
