
# Project Plan Template: Brand Packaging Lettering

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Brand Packaging Lettering" project aims to create bespoke hand-lettered typography tailored specifically for product packaging. The primary objective is to develop a unique visual identity that resonates with the target audience and enhances the product's overall appeal. This project will begin with an initial consultation to gain a deep understanding of the brand's vision, values, and target market. Following this, our team will produce sketches and drafts, allowing for client feedback and necessary revisions. The key outcomes include high-resolution digital files that are versatile for both print and web use, ensuring that the final designs can be effectively utilized across various packaging formats. By incorporating artistic lettering that tells the brand's story, this project will help clients differentiate themselves in competitive markets such as food, cosmetics, or gift items, ultimately captivating consumers and driving brand loyalty.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss their brand vision, values, and target audience.
  - Gather information on existing branding elements and any specific requirements for the lettering.

- **Research and Inspiration**: 
  - Conduct research on industry trends and competitor packaging to inform design choices.
  - Create a mood board to visually represent the desired style and tone.

- **Sketching and Drafting**: 
  - Develop initial sketches based on the consultation and research findings.
  - Present drafts to the client for feedback and discussion.

- **Revisions**: 
  - Incorporate client feedback into the designs.
  - Provide up to three rounds of revisions to ensure client satisfaction.

- **Finalization**: 
  - Prepare the final hand-lettered typography designs.
  - Create high-resolution digital files in various formats suitable for print and web use.

- **Delivery**: 
  - Deliver the final files to the client along with a style guide that includes usage recommendations.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Consultation notes and project brief.

- **Milestone 2**: Research and Mood Board Creation
  - Deliverable: Mood board presentation.

- **Milestone 3**: Initial Sketches
  - Deliverable: Presentation of initial sketches for client feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Revisions and Final Drafts
  - Deliverable: Revised drafts based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Design Delivery
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital files and style guide.
