
# Project Plan Template: Custom Graffiti-Style Typography

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Graffiti-Style Typography project aims to create a unique and visually striking typography that embodies the essence of urban culture and street art. The primary objective is to develop a series of custom typography designs that can be utilized across various applications, including branding for urban businesses, merchandise such as t-shirts and stickers, and promotional materials like posters and flyers. This project will involve close collaboration with the client to ensure that the designs align with their vision and brand identity. Key outcomes include the delivery of multiple design concepts, the incorporation of client feedback through revisions, and the provision of final files in various formats suitable for both print and digital use. Ultimately, the goal is to produce typography that not only stands out but also effectively communicates the brand's message in a creative and engaging manner, resonating with the target audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Inspiration**: Conduct research on urban culture and existing graffiti styles to gather inspiration for the typography design.
- **Client Consultation**: Hold initial meetings with the client to understand their vision, brand identity, and specific requirements for the typography.
- **Concept Development**: Create multiple design concepts that reflect the client's brand and the essence of graffiti art.
- **Client Review and Feedback**: Present the initial concepts to the client and gather feedback for revisions.
- **Revisions**: Implement changes based on client feedback and refine the typography designs.
- **Finalization**: Prepare the final typography designs, ensuring they are suitable for various applications.
- **File Formats**: Deliver the final typography in multiple formats (e.g., AI, EPS, PNG, JPG) for both print and digital use.
- **Brand Guidelines**: Provide a brief guide on how to use the typography effectively in different contexts.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of research and inspiration gathering

- **Milestone 5**: Final design revisions completed
  - **Deliverable**: Revised typography designs
- **Milestone 6**: Delivery of final files

- **Milestone 7**: Completion of brand guidelines
  - **Deliverable**: Brand guidelines document
