
# Project Plan Template: Typography for Children's Books

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Typography for Children's Books" project aims to create captivating typography that is specifically tailored for young readers. The primary objective is to design typefaces and layouts that not only attract children's attention but also enhance their understanding and enjoyment of the text. This project will involve extensive research into age-specific design trends, ensuring that the typography is playful, colorful, and age-appropriate. Key outcomes include the development of custom typographic elements that resonate with children while maintaining clarity and readability. By integrating typography with illustrations and text, the project will deliver mockups that showcase a cohesive and engaging reading experience. Ultimately, this project seeks to foster a love for reading among children through visually appealing and thoughtfully designed typography.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct research on age-specific design trends in children's literature.
  - Analyze existing children's books to identify effective typography styles.
  
- **Typeface Selection**: 
  - Curate a list of suitable typefaces that are playful and engaging.
  - Evaluate typefaces for readability and clarity for young readers.
  
- **Custom Typography Development**: 
  - Design custom typographic elements that align with the themes of the stories.
  - Create playful and colorful typefaces that resonate with children.
  
- **Mockup Creation**: 
  - Develop mockups that integrate typography with illustrations and text.
  - Ensure that the final designs provide a cohesive and engaging reading experience.
  
- **Feedback and Revisions**: 
  - Present mockups to stakeholders for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on feedback received.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and recommendations.

- **Milestone 2**: Typeface Selection Finalized
  - Deliverable: Document listing selected typefaces with rationale for choices.

- **Milestone 3**: Custom Typography Developed
  - Deliverable: Set of custom typographic elements for review.

- **Milestone 4**: Mockups Completed
  - Deliverable: Mockups showcasing typography integrated with illustrations and text.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Finalized typography designs ready for publication.
