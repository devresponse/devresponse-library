
# Project Plan Template: Typographic Wedding Invitation Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Typographic Wedding Invitation Design project aims to create bespoke wedding invitations that encapsulate the unique style and theme of the couple. The primary objective is to leverage typography as a central design element, ensuring that the choice of fonts, spacing, and layout not only enhances readability but also adds an elegant touch to the overall aesthetic. This project will involve close collaboration with the clients to understand their vision, preferences, and any specific elements they wish to incorporate, such as color schemes, motifs, or personal messages. Key outcomes include the delivery of multiple design concepts, followed by revisions based on client feedback, culminating in a set of digital invitation designs that can be printed or shared online. Ultimately, this project seeks to capture the essence of the couple's love story through thoughtful typographic choices and layout aesthetics, ensuring complete client satisfaction.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the couple to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific elements they wish to include in the invitation design.
- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather inspiration and reference materials based on the couple's style and theme.
- **Typography Selection**: Curate a selection of fonts that align with the couple's aesthetic and enhance the overall design.
- **Design Concepts**: Create three distinct design concepts for the wedding invitation, incorporating typography, color schemes, and motifs as discussed.
- **Client Review**: Present the design concepts to the couple for feedback and selection of a preferred concept.
- **Revisions**: Make necessary revisions based on client feedback to refine the chosen design.
- **Final Design Delivery**: Provide the final digital invitation design in various formats suitable for printing and online sharing.
- **Post-Delivery Support**: Offer support for any additional requests or modifications after the final delivery.
