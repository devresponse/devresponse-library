
# Project Plan Template: Event Place Card Calligraphy

## 1. Executive Summary

The Event Place Card Calligraphy project aims to create exquisite, personalized place cards for various events, including weddings, parties, and corporate gatherings. The primary objective is to enhance the overall aesthetic of each occasion through elegant calligraphy that reflects the event's theme and style. By collaborating closely with the client, we will gather essential details such as the event theme, color palette, and specific text or names to be included on each card. The key outcomes of this project will include the delivery of high-resolution digital files suitable for printing, along with options for different sizes and layouts. Ultimately, this project seeks to provide a memorable touch to each event, ensuring that every guest feels special and welcomed, thereby elevating the overall guest experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Client Consultation**: Initial meeting with the client to discuss event details, including theme, color palette, and specific text requirements.
- **Design Concepts**: Creation of multiple design concepts for the place cards, showcasing various calligraphy styles and layouts.
- **Client Review**: Presentation of design concepts to the client for feedback and selection of the preferred design.
- **Finalization of Design**: Incorporation of client feedback to finalize the design of the place cards.
- **Production of Calligraphy**: Crafting the calligraphy for each place card, ensuring attention to detail and quality.
- **Digital File Preparation**: Creation of high-resolution digital files for printing, including various sizes and layouts as per client specifications.
- **Delivery**: Submission of final digital files to the client, along with any additional instructions for printing.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Client Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client requirements and preferences.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Design Concepts Presented
  - Deliverable: Three initial design concepts for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Design Finalization
  - Deliverable: Finalized design based on client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Calligraphy Production Completed
  - Deliverable: Completed calligraphy for all place cards.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Digital Files Delivered
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital files in various formats and sizes.
