
# Project Plan Template: Custom Envelope Addressing

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Envelope Addressing project aims to enhance the presentation of invitations for special occasions through exquisite calligraphy. Our primary objective is to provide a service that combines artistry with precision, ensuring that each envelope is a reflection of the event's theme and the host's unique style. This project will cater to various events, including weddings, corporate gatherings, and other memorable celebrations. Key outcomes include delivering beautifully addressed envelopes that not only meet but exceed client expectations, fostering a sense of elegance and anticipation among guests. By offering a variety of styles and scripts, we aim to accommodate diverse preferences while maintaining a commitment to quality and timely delivery. Ultimately, this project will allow clients to focus on their event planning, knowing that the finishing touches are in expert hands.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Service Description**: Provide custom envelope addressing services for various occasions, including weddings, corporate events, and celebrations.
- **Client Interaction**: Engage with clients to understand their specific needs, preferences, and themes for the event.
- **Design Options**: Offer a selection of calligraphy styles and scripts (e.g., classic, modern, whimsical) for clients to choose from.
- **Address Collection**: Clients will provide names and addresses, which will be formatted according to the chosen style.
- **Production Process**: Skilled calligraphers will meticulously address each envelope, ensuring high-quality craftsmanship.
- **Quality Assurance**: Implement a review process to ensure accuracy and quality before delivery.
- **Delivery**: Timely delivery of the addressed envelopes to the client, ready for mailing.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation with Client
  - Deliverable: Documented client preferences and event details.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Style Selection
  - Deliverable: Finalized calligraphy style and script selection by the client.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Address Formatting
  - Deliverable: Draft of the formatted addresses for client review.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Production of Addressed Envelopes
  - Deliverable: Completed envelopes ready for delivery.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Delivery of addressed envelopes to the client.
