
# Project Plan Template: Custom Calligraphy Wedding Vows

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Calligraphy Wedding Vows project aims to transform heartfelt wedding vows into exquisite handwritten calligraphy pieces, creating a stunning visual representation of love and commitment. This project will provide couples with a unique keepsake that captures the essence of their promises in a beautifully crafted format. The primary objective is to deliver a personalized calligraphy piece that reflects the couple's preferred style—be it classic, modern, or whimsical—while ensuring meticulous attention to detail in every stroke. Key outcomes include a digital proof for client approval, a finalized calligraphy piece suitable for framing, and a lasting work of art that couples can cherish for years to come. This project not only enhances the wedding experience but also serves as a meaningful reminder of the special day.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the couple to discuss their vision, preferred styles, and any specific requests for the calligraphy piece.
- **Drafting the Vows**: Collect the wedding vows from the couple and ensure they are finalized for the calligraphy process.
- **Design Options**: Present multiple design options based on the couple's preferences, including font styles, layouts, and color schemes.
- **Digital Proof Creation**: Create a digital proof of the calligraphy piece for the couple's review and approval.
- **Revisions**: Allow for up to {{number}} rounds of revisions based on feedback from the couple.
- **Final Production**: Once approved, produce the final handwritten calligraphy piece on high-quality paper.
- **Framing Options**: Provide framing options for the final piece, if requested.
- **Delivery**: Ensure timely delivery of the finished product to the couple.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of couple's preferences and finalized vows.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Design Options Presented
  - Deliverable: Visual mock-ups of design options for approval.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Digital Proof Delivered
  - Deliverable: Digital proof of the calligraphy piece for review.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Approval Received
  - Deliverable: Confirmation from the couple to proceed with final production.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Piece Completed
  - Deliverable: Handwritten calligraphy piece delivered to the couple.
