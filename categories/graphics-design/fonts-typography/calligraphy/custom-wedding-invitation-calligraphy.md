
# Project Plan Template: Custom Wedding Invitation Calligraphy

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Wedding Invitation Calligraphy project aims to create bespoke calligraphy that embodies the unique style and theme of the couple's special day. The primary objective is to collaborate closely with the client to capture their vision, preferences, and specific details, including names, dates, and venue information. This project will result in a collection of elegant scripts and styles that not only convey essential information but also serve as a beautiful keepsake for the couple and their guests. Key outcomes include high-resolution digital formats suitable for printing, a mock-up to visualize the final design, and a personalized experience that enhances the overall wedding stationery. By providing a distinctive artistic touch, this service will help couples make their invitations stand out, adding sophistication and artistry to their celebration.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the couple to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific details to be included in the invitations.
- **Design Proposal**: Create a design proposal that includes a selection of calligraphy styles and scripts for the couple to choose from.
- **Drafting**: Develop initial drafts of the calligraphy, incorporating the couple's feedback and making necessary adjustments.
- **Finalization**: Finalize the calligraphy design based on the couple's approval, ensuring all details are accurate and aesthetically pleasing.
- **Digital Delivery**: Provide the final calligraphy in high-resolution digital formats (e.g., PDF, PNG) suitable for printing.
- **Mock-up Creation**: Create a mock-up of the invitation to help the couple visualize the final product.
- **Revisions**: Allow for a specified number of revisions based on client feedback before final delivery.
- **Delivery Timeline**: Establish a timeline for each phase of the project to ensure timely completion.
