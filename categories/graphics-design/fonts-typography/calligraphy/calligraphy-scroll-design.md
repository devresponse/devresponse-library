
# Calligraphy Scroll Design Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Calligraphy Scroll Design project aims to create exquisite calligraphy scrolls that serve as captivating visual elements for various occasions. The primary objective is to design scrolls featuring intricate lettering and artistic embellishments tailored to fit the theme of each event or narrative. Key outcomes include delivering high-resolution digital formats suitable for printing or online sharing, ensuring that each scroll not only conveys messages but also enhances the overall aesthetic appeal of the occasion. The project will involve a collaborative process with clients, allowing their ideas and themes to be incorporated into the designs. Multiple design drafts will be provided, enabling revisions based on client feedback, ultimately culminating in a final product that embodies elegance and artistry. This project will cater to a diverse range of events, including weddings, anniversaries, storytelling sessions, and historical reenactments, ensuring a personalized touch that resonates with the intended audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, themes, and specific requirements for the scroll design.
- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather inspiration based on the client's preferences, event type, and historical context if applicable.
- **Design Drafts**: Create multiple initial design drafts incorporating the client's ideas and themes.
- **Client Feedback**: Present the drafts to the client and gather feedback for revisions.
- **Revisions**: Make necessary adjustments based on client feedback, ensuring alignment with their vision.
- **Final Design**: Develop the final design incorporating all agreed-upon elements and enhancements.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final designs in high-resolution digital formats suitable for printing and online sharing.
- **Post-Delivery Support**: Offer support for any additional requests or minor adjustments after delivery.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client requirements and vision.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Research and Inspiration Compiled
  - Deliverable: Inspiration board and design direction document.
  
- **Milestone 3**: First Draft Designs Completed
  - Deliverable: Three initial design drafts presented to the client.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Client Feedback Received
  - Deliverable: Documented feedback and requested revisions.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Revised Designs Completed
  - Deliverable: Updated designs based on client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Final Design Approved
  - Deliverable: Final design files ready for delivery.
  
- **Milestone 7**: Final Delivery Completed
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital files sent to the client.
