
# Project Plan Template: Personalized Greeting Card Calligraphy

## 1. Executive Summary

The Personalized Greeting Card Calligraphy project aims to create exquisite, custom-designed greeting cards that reflect the unique sentiments of our clients. The primary objective is to deliver a personalized experience that begins with an in-depth consultation to capture the client's vision, preferred styles, and specific messages for various occasions such as birthdays, weddings, and holidays. This project will culminate in the production of high-quality greeting cards that not only convey heartfelt messages but also serve as memorable keepsakes. Key outcomes include a selection of design drafts for client approval, meticulous craftsmanship in the final product, and the provision of high-resolution digital files for easy printing. By combining artistic flair with precision, we aim to exceed client expectations and create lasting impressions through our calligraphy services.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferred styles, and specific messages.
  - Gather information on the occasion and any thematic elements the client wishes to incorporate.

- **Design Phase**: 
  - Create several design drafts based on the consultation feedback.
  - Present the drafts to the client for review and selection.
  - Make necessary revisions based on client feedback until the final design is approved.

- **Production Phase**: 
  - Meticulously craft the final greeting cards using high-quality materials and calligraphy techniques.
  - Ensure that each card is unique and tailored to the client's specifications.

- **Delivery Phase**: 
  - Provide the client with high-resolution digital files of the final designs suitable for printing.
  - Offer guidance on printing options and recommendations for card stock and finishes.
