
# Project Plan Template: Handwritten Thank You Notes

## 1. Executive Summary

The Handwritten Thank You Notes project aims to create personalized, exquisite thank you notes that embody the essence of gratitude and appreciation. The primary objective is to provide clients with a unique product that reflects their individual style and message through the art of calligraphy. This project will involve meticulous attention to detail, ensuring that each note is crafted with care and precision, enhancing the overall sentiment expressed. Key outcomes include a collection of beautifully designed thank you notes, tailored to the client's specifications, and the option for matching envelopes. By offering a variety of styles, colors, and paper types, this project seeks to elevate the experience of expressing thanks, making each recipient feel valued and special. Ultimately, the project will not only fulfill the client's needs but also promote the timeless art of handwritten communication in a digital age.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Client Consultation**: Engage with clients to understand their specific needs, preferences, and the message they wish to convey through the thank you notes.
- **Design Options**: Present clients with a selection of calligraphy styles, color palettes, and paper types to choose from.
- **Drafting**: Create initial drafts of the thank you notes based on the client's selections and feedback.
- **Finalization**: Incorporate client feedback to finalize the designs, ensuring that the typography and overall aesthetic align with the client's vision.
- **Production**: Handcraft each thank you note with precision, utilizing high-quality materials and calligraphy techniques.
- **Quality Assurance**: Conduct a thorough review of each note to ensure consistency, quality, and adherence to the client's specifications.
- **Delivery**: Package the completed thank you notes and matching envelopes (if requested) for delivery to the client.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Client Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Client brief document outlining preferences and requirements.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Design Options Presented
  - Deliverable: Portfolio of design options for client selection.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Drafts Created
  - Deliverable: Initial drafts of thank you notes for client review.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Designs Approved
  - Deliverable: Finalized designs ready for production.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Production Completed
  - Deliverable: Completed set of handwritten thank you notes and envelopes.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Quality Assurance Completed
  - Deliverable: Quality assurance report confirming standards met.
  
- **Milestone 7**: Delivery of Final Product
  - Deliverable: Packaged thank you notes delivered to the client.
