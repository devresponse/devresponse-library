
# Project Plan: Sports Event Banner Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Sports Event Banner Design project aims to create visually striking and engaging web banners that effectively promote sports events and teams. In today's competitive sports landscape, capturing the attention of fans is crucial, and our project objectives focus on delivering custom-designed banners that highlight upcoming games, showcase team merchandise, and engage the audience. The project will involve a thorough understanding of the unique identity of each team or event, ensuring that team colors, logos, and relevant imagery are incorporated into the designs. Key outcomes include multiple banner sizes optimized for various online platforms, including social media, websites, and email marketing campaigns. By the end of this project, we aim to enhance visibility and drive engagement for sporting events, ultimately contributing to increased attendance and fan interaction.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Discovery**: 
  - Conduct meetings with stakeholders to understand the team's identity and event specifics.
  - Gather information on target audience preferences and competitor analysis.

- **Design Phase**:
  - Create initial design concepts based on gathered insights.
  - Develop multiple banner sizes (e.g., leaderboard, square, vertical) for different platforms.
  - Incorporate team colors, logos, and relevant imagery into the designs.

- **Review and Feedback**:
  - Present initial designs to stakeholders for feedback.
  - Revise designs based on feedback and conduct follow-up reviews.

- **Finalization**:
  - Prepare final designs in various formats (e.g., JPEG, PNG, GIF) for online use.
  - Ensure all banners are optimized for web performance and visual appeal.

- **Delivery**:
  - Provide the final banner designs to stakeholders along with usage guidelines.
  - Offer support for implementation on various platforms if needed.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Discovery Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and insights.

- **Milestone 2**: Initial Design Concepts
  - Deliverable: Presentation of 3-5 initial design concepts for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised designs based on stakeholder feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized banner designs approved by stakeholders.

- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of Final Assets
  - Deliverable: Complete set of banner designs in required formats.
