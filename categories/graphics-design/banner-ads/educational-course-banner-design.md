
# Project Plan Template: Educational Course Banner Design

## Executive Summary
The primary objective of the Educational Course Banner Design project is to create visually striking and engaging web banners that effectively promote online courses, webinars, and educational programs. This project aims to enhance the online visibility of educational offerings and attract a wider audience by crafting banners that resonate with the target demographic. The project will involve close collaboration with the client to understand the unique aspects of the courses, including branding elements, color schemes, and specific themes. Key outcomes will include the delivery of multiple banner sizes optimized for various online platforms, ensuring versatility and effectiveness in reaching potential learners. By incorporating compelling visuals, clear messaging, and strong calls-to-action, the banners will drive engagement and conversions, ultimately contributing to the success of the educational initiatives being promoted.

## Detailed Scope of Work
1. **Client Consultation**: 
   - Conduct initial meetings with the client to gather information about the courses, branding guidelines, and target audience.
   - Identify key themes and messages to be conveyed in the banners.

2. **Design Concept Development**:
   - Create initial design concepts based on client feedback and project objectives.
   - Present concepts to the client for review and approval.

3. **Banner Design**:
   - Design multiple web banners in various sizes (e.g., leaderboard, skyscraper, square) to ensure compatibility with different online platforms.
   - Incorporate compelling visuals, clear messaging, and strong calls-to-action in each banner.

4. **Revisions and Finalization**:
   - Gather feedback from the client on the initial designs and make necessary revisions.
   - Finalize the designs and prepare them for delivery.

5. **Delivery of Final Assets**:
   - Provide the client with the final banner designs in appropriate formats (e.g., JPEG, PNG, GIF).
   - Ensure all designs are optimized for web use and meet platform specifications.

## Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1**: Client Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client requirements and project objectives document.

- **Milestone 2**: Design Concept Presentation
  - Deliverable: Presentation of initial design concepts for client feedback.

- **Milestone 3**: Banner Design Completion
  - Deliverable: Draft versions of all banner designs for review.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised banner designs based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery of Assets
  - Deliverable: All final banner designs delivered in specified formats.
