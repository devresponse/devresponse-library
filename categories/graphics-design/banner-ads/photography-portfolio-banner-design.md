
# Project Plan Template: Photography Portfolio Banner Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Photography Portfolio Banner Design project aims to create visually captivating web banners that effectively showcase a photographer's unique portfolio. In today's digital landscape, a strong online presence is essential for attracting new clients, and this project seeks to fulfill that need. The primary objective is to design banners that not only reflect the photographer's individual style and aesthetic but also engage potential clients through compelling imagery and text. Key outcomes include a set of professionally designed banners optimized for various online platforms, ensuring functionality across different devices. By understanding the photographer's vision and incorporating their services into the design, we aim to enhance their online visibility and drive inquiries, ultimately contributing to their business growth.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the photographer to discuss their vision, style, and specific requirements for the banners.
- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather inspiration and analyze competitor banners to identify trends and effective design strategies.
- **Concept Development**: Create initial design concepts based on the consultation and research findings.
- **Design Creation**: Develop the final banner designs, incorporating selected imagery, typography, and branding elements.
- **Revisions**: Present the designs to the photographer for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Finalization**: Prepare the final banner files in various formats suitable for different online platforms (e.g., JPEG, PNG, GIF).
- **Delivery**: Provide the final banners to the photographer along with guidelines for optimal usage across platforms.

- **Milestone 5**: Revisions Completed
