
# Project Plan Template: Fitness Campaign Banner Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Fitness Campaign Banner Design project aims to create visually striking web banners specifically tailored for fitness brands. The primary objective is to design engaging graphics that effectively promote workout programs, gym memberships, and health products. By collaborating closely with the client, we will gain a deep understanding of the brand's identity, target audience, and specific campaign goals. The banners will be optimized for various online platforms, ensuring they capture attention and drive conversions. Key outcomes of this project include the delivery of multiple banner sizes and formats, providing versatility for different advertising channels. The final product will reflect creativity, an understanding of current fitness trends, and the ability to convey messages succinctly through visual elements. Additionally, the designer will incorporate client feedback through revisions, ensuring that the final designs align perfectly with the brand's vision and marketing objectives.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Discovery**: 
  - Conduct a kickoff meeting with the client to gather insights on brand identity, target audience, and campaign goals.
  - Analyze competitor banners and current fitness trends to inform design choices.

- **Design Phase**:
  - Create initial design concepts for web banners, focusing on key messages and visual appeal.
  - Develop multiple banner sizes and formats (e.g., leaderboard, skyscraper, square) to ensure compatibility across various platforms.

- **Client Review and Revisions**:
  - Present initial designs to the client for feedback.
  - Implement revisions based on client input, ensuring alignment with brand vision and campaign objectives.

- **Finalization**:
  - Prepare final banner designs in required formats (e.g., JPEG, PNG, GIF) for delivery.
  - Provide a style guide outlining design elements for future reference.

- **Delivery and Support**:
  - Deliver final assets to the client via a secure file-sharing platform.
  - Offer post-delivery support for any additional requests or minor adjustments.
