
# Project Plan Template: Online Contest Banner Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Online Contest Banner Design project aims to create visually striking and engaging banners that effectively promote online contests and giveaways. The primary objective is to design banners that capture the attention of potential participants, thereby encouraging them to engage with the contest and ultimately increasing overall participation. This project will involve a thorough understanding of the client's brand identity, target audience, and specific contest details to ensure that the banners are not only aesthetically pleasing but also aligned with the marketing goals. Key outcomes include the delivery of multiple design concepts for client review, the incorporation of client feedback into the final designs, and the optimization of these designs for various online platforms. By enhancing the client's online visibility and driving user engagement through compelling visual content, this project will contribute significantly to the success of the client's marketing efforts.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: 
  - Understand the client's brand identity and values.
  - Identify the target audience and their preferences.
  - Analyze competitors' banner designs for inspiration and differentiation.

- **Concept Development**: 
  - Create multiple initial design concepts for the banners.
  - Ensure designs align with the specific contest details and marketing goals.

- **Client Review and Feedback**: 
  - Present initial concepts to the client for feedback.
  - Gather and document client feedback for revisions.

- **Design Refinement**: 
  - Incorporate client feedback into the selected design(s).
  - Refine the design to enhance visual appeal and effectiveness.

- **Final Deliverables**: 
  - Deliver final banner designs in various formats optimized for online platforms (e.g., JPEG, PNG, GIF).
  - Provide guidelines for banner usage across different channels.

- **Post-Launch Support**: 
  - Offer support for any adjustments needed after the banners are launched.
  - Monitor engagement metrics to assess the effectiveness of the banners.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Analysis
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing brand identity, target audience, and competitor analysis.

- **Milestone 2**: Presentation of Initial Design Concepts
  - Deliverable: Three initial banner design concepts for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Documented client feedback and revised design(s).

- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized banner designs in required formats.

- **Milestone 5**: Post-Launch Review
  - Deliverable: Report on engagement metrics and any necessary adjustments.
