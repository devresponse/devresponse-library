
# Project Plan Template: Custom Illustrated Maps

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Custom Illustrated Maps" project aims to create unique and visually captivating maps tailored to the specific needs of our clients. The primary objective is to blend creativity with practical cartography, resulting in maps that serve various purposes, including fantasy world-building, travel guides, and promotional materials for events. This project will begin with a comprehensive consultation to understand the client's vision, target audience, and specific requirements. Key outcomes include the delivery of high-resolution digital maps that are not only aesthetically pleasing but also functional. By incorporating artistic elements that enhance the map's appeal, we will ensure that the final product meets the client's expectations and serves its intended purpose effectively. Our goal is to provide clients with a product that stands out in both print and online formats, making their projects more engaging and informative.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct an initial meeting with the client to gather information about their vision, target audience, and specific requirements.
  - Discuss the purpose of the map and any thematic elements that should be included.

- **Sketch Development**: 
  - Create initial sketches outlining the layout and key features of the map.
  - Present sketches to the client for feedback and approval.

- **Illustration Phase**: 
  - Develop detailed illustrations based on the approved sketches.
  - Incorporate artistic elements that enhance the map's aesthetic appeal while ensuring functionality.

- **Review and Revisions**: 
  - Present the detailed illustrations to the client for review.
  - Make necessary revisions based on client feedback.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Prepare the final product in high-resolution digital formats suitable for both print and online use.
  - Deliver the final maps along with any necessary usage guidelines.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the consultation phase
  - **Deliverable**: Consultation notes and project brief

- **Milestone 2**: Approval of initial sketches
  - **Deliverable**: Set of initial sketches for client review

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of detailed illustrations
  - **Deliverable**: Detailed illustrated maps for client review

- **Milestone 4**: Final revisions and approval
  - **Deliverable**: Revised maps based on client feedback

- **Milestone 5**: Final delivery of high-resolution digital maps
  - **Deliverable**: Final product delivered in agreed formats
