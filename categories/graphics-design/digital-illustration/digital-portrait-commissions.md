
# Project Plan Template: Digital Portrait Commissions

## 1. Executive Summary

The Digital Portrait Commissions project aims to provide clients with high-quality, custom digital portraits that encapsulate the essence of individuals. The primary objective is to create unique artwork that resonates with clients, whether based on specific photographs or detailed descriptions. This project will involve close collaboration between artists and clients to ensure that each portrait reflects the personality and characteristics of the subject. Key outcomes include delivering meticulously crafted digital portraits that serve as memorable keepsakes or gifts for loved ones. The process will encompass initial consultations to gather requirements, the creation of sketches for client approval, and the final delivery of high-resolution digital portraits suitable for both printing and online use. By focusing on quality and client satisfaction, this project seeks to establish a reputation for excellence in custom digital artwork.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific requirements for the portrait.
  - Gather reference materials, such as photographs or descriptions, to inform the artistic process.

- **Sketch Development**: 
  - Create initial sketches based on the gathered information.
  - Present sketches to the client for feedback and approval.
  - Make necessary revisions based on client input.

- **Final Artwork Creation**: 
  - Develop the final digital portrait using professional illustration software.
  - Ensure that the artwork captures the essence of the subject as discussed during the consultation.

- **Delivery**: 
  - Provide the finished digital portrait in multiple high-resolution formats (e.g., JPEG, PNG) suitable for printing and online use.
  - Offer options for additional formats or sizes upon request.

- **Client Follow-Up**: 
  - Conduct a follow-up with the client to ensure satisfaction with the final product.
  - Gather testimonials or feedback for future marketing efforts.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Documented client requirements and reference materials.

- **Milestone 2**: Sketches Presented for Approval
  - Deliverable: Initial sketches submitted to the client for feedback.

- **Milestone 3**: Final Artwork Completed
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital portrait delivered in agreed formats.

- **Milestone 4**: Client Follow-Up Conducted
  - Deliverable: Client feedback and testimonials collected.
