
# Project Plan Template: Educational Institution Course Brochure

## 1. Executive Summary

The Educational Institution Course Brochure project aims to create a visually appealing and informative brochure that effectively communicates the offerings of {{Educational Institution Name}}. The primary objective is to design a marketing tool that showcases the various courses available, outlines admission requirements, and highlights campus facilities. This brochure will serve as a vital asset in attracting prospective students and enhancing the institution's brand image. Key outcomes include a well-structured, easy-to-read brochure that encapsulates the essence of the institution's educational offerings. The project will involve thorough research, collaboration with stakeholders, and multiple design iterations based on client feedback. Ultimately, the final deliverable will be a polished, print-ready brochure that aligns with the institution's marketing strategy and resonates with its target audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct interviews with faculty and administration to gather course details.
  - Analyze competitor brochures for design inspiration and content structure.
  - Collect information on admission requirements and campus facilities.

- **Design Phase**: 
  - Develop multiple design concepts based on the research findings.
  - Create initial drafts of the brochure layout, including text and imagery.
  - Incorporate branding elements such as logos, colors, and fonts.

- **Review Phase**: 
  - Present design concepts to the client for feedback.
  - Revise designs based on client input and suggestions.
  - Conduct a second round of revisions if necessary.

- **Finalization Phase**: 
  - Prepare the final design for print, ensuring all specifications are met.
  - Deliver print-ready files in the required formats (PDF, etc.).
  - Provide a digital version of the brochure for online distribution.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and insights.

- **Milestone 2**: Presentation of Initial Design Concepts
  - Deliverable: Three distinct design concepts for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Review Phase
  - Deliverable: Revised brochure design based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Finalization and Delivery
  - Deliverable: Print-ready brochure files and digital version.
