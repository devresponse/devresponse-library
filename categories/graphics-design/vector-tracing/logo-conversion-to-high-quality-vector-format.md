
# Project Plan Template: Logo Conversion to High-Quality Vector Format

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's competitive market, having a high-quality logo is essential for brand recognition and professionalism. The objective of this project is to convert existing raster-based logos into high-quality vector graphics that are resolution-independent. This transformation ensures that logos can be resized without losing quality, making them suitable for various applications, from business cards to large banners. The project will involve meticulous tracing of the logo to capture every detail accurately, followed by refining the design to enhance its visual appeal.
