
# Project Plan: Interactive QR Code Wrap

## 1. Executive Summary

The Interactive QR Code Car Wrap project aims to create a visually striking vehicle wrap that not only promotes your brand but also engages potential customers through innovative technology. The primary objective is to design a wrap that prominently features a QR code, allowing passersby to easily scan and access your website or specific promotional content. This project will involve a comprehensive design process tailored to reflect your brand's identity, utilizing colors, graphics, and messaging that resonate with your target audience. Key outcomes include a high-resolution design ready for printing, ensuring that your vehicle becomes a mobile advertisement that captures attention and drives traffic to your online presence. By integrating technology with traditional advertising, this project seeks to enhance customer engagement and increase brand visibility in a competitive market.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct meetings with the client to understand branding goals, target audience, and specific requirements for the vehicle wrap.
- **Design Concept Development**: Create initial concept sketches that incorporate the client's branding elements, including colors, graphics, and messaging.
- **QR Code Integration**: Design a prominent QR code that links to the client's website or promotional content, ensuring it is easily scannable.
- **Revisions and Feedback**: Present the design concepts to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions to meet expectations.
- **Final Design Approval**: Obtain final approval from the client on the design before proceeding to the production phase.
- **High-Resolution File Preparation**: Prepare the final design in high-resolution format suitable for printing.
- **Production Coordination**: Collaborate with printing vendors to ensure the wrap is produced to specifications and installed correctly on the vehicle.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of branding goals and requirements
- **Milestone 2**: Concept Sketches Developed
  - Deliverable: Presentation of initial design concepts
- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised design based on client feedback
- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approval
  - Deliverable: Approved high-resolution design files
- **Milestone 5**: Production and Installation
  - Deliverable: Completed vehicle wrap installed on the client's vehicle
