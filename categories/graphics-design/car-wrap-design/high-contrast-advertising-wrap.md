
# High-Contrast Advertising Wrap Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The High-Contrast Advertising Wrap project aims to create a visually striking car wrap design that leverages bold colors and large typography to maximize visibility and impact. The primary objective is to transform your vehicle into an effective mobile advertisement that not only captures the attention of potential customers but also communicates your brand message clearly and effectively. The project will commence with an initial consultation to delve into your brand identity, target audience, and specific requirements for the vehicle wrap. Following this, we will present multiple design concepts that emphasize high contrast and readability, ensuring that your brand stands out in any traffic situation. Key outcomes include the delivery of print-ready files optimized for vehicle wrapping and a comprehensive style guide detailing color codes and typography specifications. Ultimately, this project seeks to enhance your brand's visibility and engagement through innovative design.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting to understand the client's brand identity, target audience, and specific requirements for the vehicle wrap.
- **Design Concept Development**: Create multiple design concepts that utilize high-contrast colors and large typography.
- **Client Review**: Present the design concepts to the client for feedback and selection.
- **Design Refinement**: Refine the selected design based on client feedback to ensure it meets expectations.
- **Final Deliverables**:
  - Print-ready files optimized for vehicle wrapping.
  - A style guide that includes color codes and typography specifications.
- **Project Closure**: Conduct a final review with the client to ensure satisfaction and gather feedback for future improvements.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client requirements and brand identity.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Design Concepts Presented
  - Deliverable: Three initial design concepts for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Design Selection and Feedback
  - Deliverable: Selected design concept and feedback documentation.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Refinement
  - Deliverable: Refined design ready for final approval.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Deliverables Provided
  - Deliverable: Print-ready files and style guide delivered to the client.
