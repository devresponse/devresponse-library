
# Project Plan Template: Real Estate Agent Wrap

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Real Estate Agent Wrap" project aims to design a custom car wrap that serves as a powerful marketing tool for real estate agents. The primary objective is to create a visually striking and professional design that effectively reflects the agent's branding, including their logos and color schemes. The wrap will prominently display essential contact details, ensuring potential clients can easily reach out. Additionally, the design will feature high-quality imagery of the agent's listed properties, capturing the attention of potential buyers and sellers. Key outcomes include multiple design concepts, revisions based on client feedback, and the delivery of print-ready files optimized for vehicle wrap printing. This comprehensive solution will enhance the mobile advertising efforts of real estate professionals, ultimately contributing to increased visibility and client engagement.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation:** Meet with the client to discuss branding, design preferences, and specific requirements for the car wrap.
- **Research and Concept Development:** Conduct research on effective car wrap designs in the real estate sector and develop initial design concepts.
- **Design Creation:** Create multiple design concepts that incorporate the agent's branding, contact information, and high-quality property imagery.
- **Client Review and Feedback:** Present the design concepts to the client for review and gather feedback for revisions.
- **Revisions:** Make necessary adjustments to the designs based on client feedback, ensuring alignment with their vision.
- **Final Design Approval:** Present the revised designs for final approval by the client.
- **Preparation of Print-Ready Files:** Optimize the final design for vehicle wrap printing, ensuring all elements are legible and visually appealing.
- **Delivery:** Provide the client with the final print-ready files and any additional guidelines for installation.
