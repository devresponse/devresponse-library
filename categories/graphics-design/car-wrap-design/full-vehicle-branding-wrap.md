
# Project Plan Template: Full Vehicle Branding Wrap

## 1. Executive Summary

The Full Vehicle Branding Wrap project aims to transform your vehicle into a striking mobile advertisement that effectively communicates your brand identity. The primary objective is to create a cohesive and visually appealing design that captures the essence of your business while resonating with your target audience. This project will involve a comprehensive consultation process to gather insights into your brand's messaging, values, and unique selling points. Key outcomes include the delivery of multiple design concepts for your review, high-resolution files ready for printing, and a final wrap that not only enhances your vehicle's appearance but also increases brand visibility on the road. By the end of this project, your vehicle will serve as a powerful marketing tool, making a memorable impression on potential customers and driving engagement with your brand.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct an initial meeting to discuss brand identity, target audience, and messaging.
  - Gather necessary materials, including logos, color schemes, and any existing branding guidelines.

- **Design Phase**: 
  - Develop multiple design concepts based on the information gathered during the consultation.
  - Ensure designs are tailored to fit the specific dimensions and contours of the vehicle.

- **Review and Feedback**: 
  - Present design concepts to the client for review.
  - Incorporate client feedback and make necessary revisions to finalize the design.

- **Production Phase**: 
  - Prepare high-resolution files suitable for printing.
  - Coordinate with printing vendors to ensure quality standards are met.

- **Installation Phase**: 
  - Schedule and oversee the installation of the vehicle wrap.
  - Conduct a final inspection to ensure the wrap is applied correctly and meets quality expectations.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary document of brand identity and project requirements.

- **Milestone 2**: Design Concepts Presented
  - Deliverable: Three initial design concepts for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Final Design Approval
  - Deliverable: Approved design ready for production.

- **Milestone 4**: High-Resolution Files Delivered
  - Deliverable: Files sent to the printing vendor.

- **Milestone 5**: Installation Completed
  - Deliverable: Final inspection report and client sign-off.
