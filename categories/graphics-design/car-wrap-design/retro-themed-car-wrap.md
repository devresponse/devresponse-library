
# Project Plan: Retro-Themed Car Wrap

## 1. Executive Summary

The Retro-Themed Car Wrap project aims to create a visually striking and nostalgic vehicle wrap that embodies the essence of vintage design while effectively promoting the {{brand_name}}. This project will leverage classic design elements, including nostalgic graphics and typography, to resonate with target audiences and enhance brand visibility. The project will commence with an initial consultation to align on the vision, brand identity, and specific themes or colors desired. Following this, the designer will present multiple concept sketches for review, ensuring that the final design captures the desired retro aesthetic. Upon approval of a concept, a high-resolution digital mockup will be created, tailored to the vehicle's dimensions. The final deliverables will include print-ready files and installation guidelines. By transforming the vehicle into a mobile advertisement, this project will not only enhance its appearance but also serve as a powerful marketing tool, increasing brand recognition and engagement on the road.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Discuss vision, brand identity, and specific themes/colors.
  - Gather input on preferred retro design elements and inspirations.

- **Concept Development**: 
  - Research vintage design trends and graphics.
  - Create and present {{number_of_concepts}} concept sketches for client review.

- **Design Approval**: 
  - Revise concepts based on client feedback.
  - Finalize the selected concept for high-resolution mockup.

- **Digital Mockup Creation**: 
  - Develop a high-resolution digital mockup of the car wrap.
  - Ensure alignment with branding and vehicle dimensions.

- **Final Deliverables**: 
  - Provide print-ready files for the car wrap.
  - Include installation guidelines for the wrap application.
