
# Project Plan Template: Tourism-Themed Car Wrap

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Tourism-Themed Car Wrap" project aims to create a visually captivating car wrap design tailored specifically for tourism companies. The primary objective is to develop a design that incorporates scenic imagery, including iconic landmarks, natural landscapes, and cultural elements that resonate with travelers. By effectively communicating the brand's message, the car wrap will evoke a sense of adventure and exploration, enhancing the visibility of the tourism business on the road. Key outcomes include high-resolution design files suitable for printing and a mockup of the car wrap applied to a vehicle for client approval. Collaboration with the client will be crucial to ensure the design aligns with their branding and marketing goals, ultimately helping tourism businesses attract more customers through eye-catching vehicle advertising.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct research on the tourism company's brand, target audience, and competitors to inform the design process.
- **Concept Development**: Create initial design concepts that incorporate scenic imagery and align with the client’s branding.
- **Client Collaboration**: Present concepts to the client for feedback and revisions, ensuring alignment with their marketing goals.
- **Design Finalization**: Refine the chosen design based on client feedback, ensuring it is optimized for visibility and impact.
- **High-Resolution File Preparation**: Prepare high-resolution files suitable for printing, ensuring all design elements are correctly formatted.
- **Mockup Creation**: Develop a mockup of the car wrap applied to a vehicle for client approval.
- **Final Review and Approval**: Conduct a final review with the client to obtain approval on the design and mockup.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of research and analysis
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings
- **Milestone 2**: Presentation of initial design concepts
  - Deliverable: Design concept presentation
- **Milestone 3**: Client feedback and revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised design concepts based on feedback
- **Milestone 4**: Final design approval
  - Deliverable: Approved design files
- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of high-resolution files and mockup
  - Deliverable: High-resolution design files and vehicle mockup
