
# Project Plan Template: Partial Car Wrap Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Partial Car Wrap Design project aims to create a custom vehicle wrap that effectively showcases your brand identity while enhancing the visual appeal of your vehicle. The primary objective is to develop a design that focuses on specific areas of the vehicle, such as the doors or rear, ensuring that key branding elements are prominently displayed. This project will begin with a comprehensive consultation to understand your branding needs, color preferences, and messaging. Following this, we will present initial design concepts for your review, allowing for constructive feedback and necessary revisions. The key outcomes of this project include a finalized design that aligns with your vision, delivered in the appropriate formats for printing. Ultimately, this project is designed for businesses seeking to maximize their visibility on the road through a professional and impactful vehicle wrap that serves as an effective marketing tool.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct an initial meeting to discuss branding needs, color preferences, and messaging.
  - Gather necessary materials, such as logos and existing branding guidelines.

- **Design Phase**:
  - Create initial design concepts focusing on specific areas of the vehicle.
  - Present concepts for client review and feedback.
  - Revise designs based on client input to ensure alignment with their vision.

- **Finalization Phase**:
  - Deliver the final design files in the appropriate formats for printing (e.g., AI, PDF, EPS).
  - Provide guidelines for installation and maintenance of the wrap.

- **Post-Project Support**:
  - Offer support for any questions regarding the design or installation process.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Summary of branding needs and preferences.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Initial Design Concepts
  - Deliverable: Presentation of 2-3 design concepts for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised design based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approval
  - Deliverable: Approved final design files.

- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of Final Files
  - Deliverable: Final design files delivered in required formats.
