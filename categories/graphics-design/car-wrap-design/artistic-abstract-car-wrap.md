
# Project Plan: Artistic Abstract Wrap

## 1. Executive Summary

The Artistic Abstract Car Wrap project aims to create a visually stunning and unique vehicle wrap that embodies creativity and artistic expression. The primary objective of this project is to develop an abstract design that incorporates vibrant colors and intricate patterns, ensuring that the final product stands out on the road. This project will involve a collaborative process with the client to ensure that the design reflects their brand identity while also appealing to a broad audience. Key outcomes include the delivery of multiple design concepts, client feedback sessions, and the finalization of a high-resolution design file suitable for printing. By transforming the vehicle into a mobile work of art, this project will enhance brand visibility and create a lasting impression on potential customers.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, brand identity, and specific requirements for the car wrap.
- **Design Development**: Create multiple design concepts based on the client's input, focusing on abstract art styles that utilize vibrant colors and intricate patterns.
- **Client Feedback**: Present the design concepts to the client and gather feedback for revisions.
- **Revisions**: Make necessary adjustments to the selected design based on client feedback, ensuring alignment with their vision.
- **Final Design Approval**: Obtain final approval from the client on the revised design.
- **High-Resolution File Preparation**: Prepare the final design in a high-resolution format suitable for printing and application on the vehicle.
- **Print Coordination**: Coordinate with a printing service to ensure the design is printed accurately and to specifications.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and design brief
- **Milestone 2**: Design Concepts Created
  - Deliverable: Three initial design concepts presented to the client
- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback Received
  - Deliverable: Documented feedback from the client
- **Milestone 4**: Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised design presented to the client
- **Milestone 5**: Final Design Approved
  - Deliverable: Final design file in high-resolution format
- **Milestone 6**: Print Coordination Completed
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of print order and timeline
