
# Project Plan Template: Charity Campaign Wrap

## 1. Executive Summary

The Charity Campaign Wrap project aims to create a visually striking and meaningful vehicle wrap that effectively promotes a charity or non-profit initiative. The primary objective of this project is to collaborate closely with the client, {{client_name}}, to understand the core message and values of the charity, ensuring that the design resonates with the target audience. This will involve brainstorming sessions to generate creative concepts, followed by the development of mock-ups that incorporate impactful visuals and compelling messaging. The final design will be tailored to fit the specific dimensions of the vehicle, ensuring a professional and polished appearance. The wrap will serve as a mobile advertisement, raising awareness and encouraging support for the charity's cause. Key outcomes include the delivery of high-resolution files suitable for printing, along with guidelines for installation. Ultimately, the goal is to create a wrap that not only attracts attention but also inspires action and engagement with the charity's mission.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with {{client_name}} to discuss the charity's mission, values, and target audience.
- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct research on similar campaigns and analyze effective messaging strategies.
- **Brainstorming Sessions**: Organize creative brainstorming sessions with the design team to generate concepts.
- **Design Development**: Create initial mock-ups based on selected concepts, incorporating feedback from the client.
- **Revisions**: Allow for up to {{number_of_revisions}} rounds of revisions based on client feedback.
- **Final Design Approval**: Present the final design to {{client_name}} for approval.
- **File Preparation**: Prepare high-resolution files suitable for printing, ensuring all specifications are met.
- **Installation Guidelines**: Develop and provide detailed installation guidelines for the vehicle wrap.
- **Project Closure**: Conduct a final review meeting with {{client_name}} to ensure satisfaction and gather feedback.
