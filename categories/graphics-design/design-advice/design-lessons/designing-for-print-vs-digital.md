
# Project Plan Template: Designing for Print vs. Digital

## 1. Executive Summary

The project "Designing for Print vs. Digital" aims to equip participants with a comprehensive understanding of the fundamental differences between designing for print and digital platforms. The primary objective is to enhance participants' design strategies by exploring critical aspects such as color modes, resolution requirements, typography, and layout considerations unique to each medium. This project will provide an engaging learning experience through practical exercises that allow participants to apply theoretical knowledge in real-world scenarios. Key outcomes include improved adaptability in design workflows, a clear grasp of how to tailor strategies for different formats, and the ability to meet the specific needs of print and digital outputs effectively. By the end of the project, participants will be empowered to make informed design choices that enhance the quality and effectiveness of their work across various platforms.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of this project includes the following key components:

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the differences between print and digital design, focusing on color modes, resolution, typography, and layout.
  
- **Curriculum Development**: Create a structured curriculum that outlines the learning objectives, key topics, and practical exercises for participants.
  
- **Workshop Sessions**: Organize interactive workshop sessions where participants can engage in hands-on activities to apply their knowledge.
  
- **Resource Materials**: Develop and distribute resource materials, including guides, checklists, and reference sheets that participants can use during and after the project.
  
- **Feedback and Assessment**: Implement a feedback mechanism to assess participants' understanding and application of the concepts learned throughout the project.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Analysis  
  - Deliverable: Research report detailing key distinctions between print and digital design.

- **Milestone 2**: Curriculum Development  
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive curriculum document outlining session topics and learning objectives.

- **Milestone 3**: Workshop Sessions  
  - Deliverable: Conduct a series of workshops with participant feedback collected after each session.

- **Milestone 4**: Resource Materials Distribution  
  - Deliverable: Provide participants with resource materials for ongoing reference.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Assessment and Feedback  
  - Deliverable: Summary report of participant assessments and feedback for future improvements.
