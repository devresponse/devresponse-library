
# Project Plan: Designing with Negative Space

## Executive Summary

The "Designing with Negative Space" project aims to educate participants on the essential design principle of negative space, which refers to the area surrounding and between the subjects of an image. The primary objective is to equip learners with the skills to effectively utilize negative space to enhance their designs, making them more engaging and aesthetically pleasing. This project will provide a comprehensive overview of various techniques for identifying and employing negative space across different design contexts, including logos, posters, and digital graphics. Key outcomes include improved composition and balance in participants' designs, the ability to convey messages effectively through strategic use of negative space, and the development of a keen eye for design aesthetics. By the end of the course, participants will have the confidence and knowledge to create visually compelling designs that capture attention and communicate effectively.

## Detailed Scope of Work

1. **Course Development**
   - Research and compile theoretical insights on negative space.
   - Create a curriculum that includes practical exercises and examples.
   - Develop instructional materials, including presentations and handouts.

2. **Workshop Delivery**
   - Schedule and organize workshops, including venue selection and logistics.
   - Conduct interactive sessions that encourage participant engagement.
   - Provide feedback and guidance during practical exercises.

3. **Assessment and Feedback**
   - Design assessment criteria to evaluate participants' understanding and application of negative space.
   - Collect feedback from participants to improve future iterations of the course.

4. **Marketing and Promotion**
   - Develop a marketing strategy to promote the course to potential participants.
   - Create promotional materials, including flyers and social media content.
