
# Project Plan Template: Design Critique Sessions

## 1. Executive Summary

The Design Critique Sessions project aims to provide aspiring designers with personalized feedback on their work through structured mentorship from experienced professionals. This initiative is designed to enhance the skills of mentees by focusing on critical aspects of design, such as composition, color theory, typography, and overall effectiveness. Each session will allow participants to present a selection of their design projects, fostering an interactive environment where constructive criticism is encouraged. Key outcomes include improved design skills, a clearer understanding of individual design styles, and actionable advice for future projects. By the end of the sessions, mentees will not only receive valuable insights but also develop a roadmap for their professional growth, making this mentorship experience an essential component of their design education.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Design Critique Sessions includes the following components:

- **Session Structure**: Each session will consist of a presentation by the mentee, followed by a critique from the mentor. Sessions will be scheduled weekly over a period of {{number_of_weeks}} weeks.
  
- **Preparation**: Mentees will prepare a portfolio of their design work, selecting {{number_of_projects}} projects for presentation. They will be encouraged to focus on specific aspects of each design for critique.

- **Feedback Mechanism**: The mentor will provide feedback that highlights both strengths and areas for improvement. This will include actionable advice and resources tailored to each mentee's needs.

- **Interactive Dialogue**: Each session will allow time for questions and discussions, fostering an open environment for learning and growth.

- **Outcome Assessment**: At the end of the sessions, mentees will complete a self-assessment reflecting on their learning and development, alongside a mentor assessment to provide additional insights.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Project Kick-off
  - Deliverable: Initial meeting to outline expectations and goals.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Mentee Preparation
  - Deliverable: Submission of selected design projects by mentees for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Conducting Sessions
  - Deliverable: Completion of {{number_of_sessions}} critique sessions, with documented feedback for each mentee.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Assessment
  - Deliverable: Collection of self-assessments and mentor assessments from all participants.

- **Milestone 5**: Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Final report summarizing outcomes, participant feedback, and recommendations for future sessions.
