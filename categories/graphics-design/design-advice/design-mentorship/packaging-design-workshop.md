
# Project Plan Template: Packaging Design Workshop

## 1. Executive Summary

The Packaging Design Workshop is an immersive mentorship experience designed for aspiring graphic designers who wish to refine their skills in the specialized field of packaging design. The primary objective of this workshop is to equip participants with the essential principles of aesthetics, functionality, and branding that are crucial for creating effective packaging solutions. Throughout the workshop, participants will engage in interactive sessions led by experienced design mentors, allowing them to learn through practical exercises, group discussions, and personalized feedback. Key outcomes include the development of a comprehensive packaging design project that participants can showcase in their portfolios, as well as a deeper understanding of the design process and industry standards. By the end of the workshop, attendees will not only enhance their technical skills but also gain confidence in their ability to conceptualize and execute packaging designs that resonate with consumers and effectively communicate brand messages.
