
# Project Plan Template: Design Thinking in Action

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Design Thinking in Action" mentorship program aims to immerse participants in the core principles of design thinking, fostering a human-centered approach to innovation and problem-solving. This program is structured to provide a comprehensive learning experience through interactive workshops and hands-on projects that stimulate creative thinking and collaboration. Participants will engage in activities that emphasize empathy with users, problem definition, ideation of solutions, prototyping concepts, and testing ideas in real-world scenarios. Led by experienced design mentors, each session will offer personalized feedback and guidance, ensuring that participants not only understand theoretical concepts but also apply them effectively in practical situations. Key outcomes include the development of a robust toolkit for addressing complex challenges, enhanced collaboration skills, and a mindset geared towards continuous improvement and innovation. By the conclusion of the program, participants will be equipped to tackle their own projects with confidence and creativity.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the "Design Thinking in Action" mentorship program includes the following components:

- **Workshops**: A series of interactive workshops focusing on each stage of the design thinking process, including empathy, definition, ideation, prototyping, and testing.
- **Hands-on Projects**: Participants will work on real-world projects that require the application of design thinking principles, allowing them to practice and refine their skills.
- **Mentorship Sessions**: Regular one-on-one and group mentorship sessions with experienced design professionals to provide personalized guidance and feedback.
- **Resource Materials**: Provision of comprehensive resource materials, including guides, templates, and case studies to support learning and application.
- **Evaluation and Feedback**: Continuous assessment of participant progress through feedback sessions and evaluations to ensure understanding and application of concepts.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Program Kick-off
  - Deliverable: Orientation session and introduction to design thinking principles.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Empathy and Definition Workshops
  - Deliverable: Participants submit empathy maps and problem statements for feedback.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Ideation and Prototyping Workshops
  - Deliverable: Participants present their ideation outcomes and initial prototypes.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Testing Phase
  - Deliverable: Participants conduct user testing and gather feedback on their prototypes.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Presentation and Evaluation
  - Deliverable: Participants present their final projects and receive evaluations from mentors.
