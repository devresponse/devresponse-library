
# Project Plan Template: Parametric Curtain Wall System Family

## 1. Executive Summary

The Parametric Curtain Wall System Family project aims to develop a comprehensive and adaptable BIM family tailored specifically for curtain wall systems. The primary objective is to create a versatile tool that allows architects and designers to customize panel sizes according to their unique project requirements, thereby enhancing design flexibility. This project will incorporate various frame materials, including aluminum, steel, and wood, providing users with a range of options to suit different architectural styles. Additionally, the family will offer multiple glazing choices, such as insulated, tinted, and reflective glass, to improve energy efficiency and aesthetic appeal. The key outcomes of this project include a fully functional BIM family that seamlessly integrates with popular architectural design software, streamlining workflows and improving the accuracy of architectural models. By delivering this innovative solution, we aim to empower architects and designers to visualize and implement their curtain wall designs more effectively, ultimately contributing to the success of their projects.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct a thorough analysis of existing curtain wall systems and identify key features and requirements for the BIM family.
- **Design Development**: Create parametric models that allow for adjustable panel sizes, accommodating various project specifications.
- **Material Specification**: Develop options for different frame materials (aluminum, steel, wood) and define their properties within the BIM family.
- **Glazing Options**: Integrate multiple glazing types (insulated, tinted, reflective) into the family, ensuring compatibility with the frame materials.
- **Testing and Validation**: Perform rigorous testing to ensure the functionality and performance of the BIM family across various architectural design software.
- **Documentation**: Prepare comprehensive documentation, including user guides and technical specifications, to assist users in implementing the family in their projects.
- **Training and Support**: Offer training sessions and ongoing support to users to facilitate the effective use of the BIM family.
