
# Custom Residential Door Family Creation Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Residential Door Family Creation project aims to develop a comprehensive Building Information Modeling (BIM) family specifically tailored for residential doors. The primary objective of this project is to create a detailed 3D model that offers a variety of customizable options, including dimensions, materials, and styles. By delivering a fully functional BIM family, this project will enhance the design process for architects and designers, ensuring accuracy in construction documentation. Key outcomes include adherence to industry standards and best practices, providing flexibility for adapting door specifications to meet unique project requirements. This service is crucial for visualizing both the aesthetic and functional aspects of residential doors, ultimately contributing to the overall success of architectural designs. The project will facilitate improved collaboration among stakeholders and streamline the integration of door specifications into architectural projects.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: 
  - Conduct a thorough analysis of existing residential door designs and BIM families.
  - Identify industry standards and best practices for door specifications.

- **Design Development**: 
  - Create initial sketches and concepts for the door family.
  - Develop a detailed 3D model incorporating customizable options such as dimensions, materials, and styles.

- **Modeling and Documentation**: 
  - Build the BIM family in accordance with industry standards.
  - Prepare comprehensive documentation, including user guides and technical specifications.

- **Quality Assurance**: 
  - Perform rigorous testing of the BIM family to ensure functionality and compatibility with various architectural software.
  - Gather feedback from stakeholders and make necessary adjustments.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Provide the completed BIM family along with all supporting documentation.
  - Conduct a presentation or training session for end-users to ensure proper utilization of the model.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Project Kick-off
  - Deliverable: Project initiation document and stakeholder meeting notes.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Research and Analysis
  - Deliverable: Research report detailing findings and recommendations.

- **Milestone 3**: Design Development Completion
  - Deliverable: Initial design concepts and sketches for review.

- **Milestone 4**: 3D Model Development
  - Deliverable: Completed 3D BIM family model.

- **Milestone 5**: Quality Assurance Testing
  - Deliverable: QA report and feedback summary.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Delivery and Training
  - Deliverable: Final BIM family, documentation, and training session completion.
