
# Project Plan Template: BIM Workflow Automation with Dynamo

## 1. Executive Summary

The "BIM Workflow Automation with Dynamo" project aims to provide an advanced training course tailored for professionals seeking to enhance their Building Information Modeling (BIM) capabilities through automation. This course will empower participants to leverage Dynamo, a powerful visual programming tool, to streamline and optimize their BIM workflows effectively. The project will encompass a comprehensive curriculum that includes creating custom scripts, automating repetitive tasks, and integrating Dynamo with various BIM software. Key outcomes of this training will include improved efficiency, reduced errors, and enhanced collaboration in BIM processes. By the end of the course, attendees will possess practical skills that can be immediately applied to their projects, making this training ideal for architects, engineers, and construction professionals eager to elevate their BIM expertise.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Course Development**: 
  - Design and develop course materials, including presentations, handouts, and exercises.
  - Create a comprehensive curriculum that covers essential topics related to Dynamo and BIM workflow automation.

- **Training Delivery**: 
  - Conduct live online training sessions over a specified duration.
  - Facilitate hands-on exercises and real-world examples to reinforce learning.

- **Participant Support**: 
  - Provide ongoing support to participants through Q&A sessions and discussion forums.
  - Offer additional resources and materials for further learning.

- **Assessment and Feedback**: 
  - Develop assessments to evaluate participant understanding and skills.
  - Gather feedback from participants to improve future iterations of the course.
