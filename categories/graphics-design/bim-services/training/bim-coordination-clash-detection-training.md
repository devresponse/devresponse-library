
# Project Plan Template: BIM Coordination and Clash Detection Training

## 1. Executive Summary

The BIM Coordination and Clash Detection Training course aims to equip participants with essential skills and knowledge necessary for effective Building Information Modeling (BIM) coordination. This comprehensive online training will cover the fundamental principles of BIM, focusing on the identification and resolution of clashes within design models. Participants will learn advanced techniques for utilizing BIM software tools to streamline the coordination process, enhance collaboration among project stakeholders, and minimize costly errors during construction. The course will include interactive modules, real-world case studies, and practical exercises to ensure participants can apply their learning effectively. By the end of the training, attendees will be proficient in conducting clash detection, interpreting results, and implementing solutions to improve project outcomes. Key outcomes include improved collaboration, reduced project delays, and enhanced overall project quality.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the BIM Coordination and Clash Detection Training includes the following components:

- **Course Development**: Create a comprehensive curriculum that covers BIM fundamentals, clash detection techniques, and software tools.
- **Interactive Modules**: Develop engaging online modules that include video lectures, quizzes, and discussion forums.
- **Real-World Case Studies**: Incorporate case studies that illustrate common clash detection scenarios and solutions.
- **Practical Exercises**: Design hands-on exercises that allow participants to practice clash detection using BIM software.
- **Assessment and Certification**: Implement assessments to evaluate participant understanding and provide certification upon successful completion.
- **Technical Support**: Offer ongoing technical support to participants throughout the training period.
