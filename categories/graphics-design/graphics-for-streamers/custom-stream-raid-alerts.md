
# Project Plan Template: Custom Stream Raid Alerts

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Stream Raid Alerts project aims to enhance the streaming experience by designing unique and visually striking raid alerts that resonate with the channel's brand identity. In the competitive landscape of streaming, it is crucial to create memorable moments that engage viewers and foster a sense of community. This project will deliver custom alerts that not only welcome incoming streamers and their audiences but also reflect the essence of the channel. Key outcomes include the development of animated alerts with tailored sound effects, ensuring they stand out during raids. By investing in these custom alerts, streamers will elevate their broadcasts, making them more inviting and enjoyable for both new and returning viewers. The final product will be delivered in a format compatible with popular streaming platforms, facilitating seamless integration into broadcasts and enhancing overall viewer engagement.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Concept Development**
  - Analyze the client's brand identity and streaming style.
  - Gather inspiration and examples of effective raid alerts.
  - Develop initial concepts for the raid alerts.

- **Design Phase**
  - Create custom graphics for the raid alerts that align with the client's branding.
  - Design animations that enhance the visual appeal of the alerts.
  - Select and produce sound effects that complement the animations.

- **Review and Revisions**
  - Present initial designs to the client for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on client input.
  - Finalize designs and animations.

- **Integration and Delivery**
  - Prepare the final product in formats compatible with popular streaming platforms (e.g., OBS, Streamlabs).
  - Provide documentation on how to integrate the alerts into the client's streaming setup.
  - Offer post-delivery support for any integration issues.
