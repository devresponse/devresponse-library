
# Project Plan: Custom Stream Schedule Graphics

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Stream Schedule Graphics project aims to enhance the professional image of streamers by providing visually appealing and brand-consistent graphics that effectively communicate streaming schedules. In the fast-paced world of streaming, maintaining audience engagement is crucial, and clear communication of streaming times is a key component of this engagement. The project will deliver a set of high-resolution graphics optimized for various platforms, including Twitch, YouTube, and Facebook Gaming. These graphics will not only display the streamer's schedule, including specific days and times, but will also incorporate branding elements such as logos and color schemes that align with the streamer's overall aesthetic. Key outcomes include increased viewer awareness of streaming schedules, improved audience retention, and a more polished and professional appearance across streaming platforms. By the end of this project, streamers will have a suite of graphics that not only inform but also attract and engage their audience, ultimately contributing to the growth of their viewer base.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Discovery**: 
  - Conduct a brief consultation with the streamer to understand their brand identity, preferred aesthetics, and specific requirements for the graphics.
  - Analyze competitors and industry standards for stream schedule graphics.

- **Design Development**:
  - Create initial design concepts for the stream schedule graphics, incorporating the streamer's branding elements.
  - Develop multiple design variations for review, including different layouts and color schemes.

- **Feedback and Revisions**:
  - Present the initial concepts to the streamer for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on the feedback received, ensuring the final designs meet the streamer's expectations.

- **Finalization**:
  - Prepare the final graphics in high-resolution formats suitable for various platforms (Twitch, YouTube, Facebook Gaming).
  - Ensure compatibility with different streaming setups by providing the graphics in multiple file formats (e.g., PNG, JPEG, PSD).

- **Delivery**:
  - Deliver the final graphics package to the streamer, including all formats and a style guide for future reference.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Discovery
  - Deliverable: Research report and design brief.

- **Milestone 2**: Initial Design Concepts
  - Deliverable: Presentation of 3-5 design concepts for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised designs based on streamer feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Graphics Package
  - Deliverable: High-resolution graphics in multiple formats, along with a style guide.
