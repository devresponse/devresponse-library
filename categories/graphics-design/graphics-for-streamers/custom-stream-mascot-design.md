
# Project Plan: Custom Stream Mascot Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Stream Mascot Design project aims to create a unique and engaging mascot that encapsulates the essence of {{streamer_name}}'s brand identity. In the competitive landscape of streaming, a distinctive mascot serves as a powerful tool for enhancing viewer engagement and fostering community loyalty. The project will commence with a comprehensive consultation to delve into the brand's core values, target audience, and preferred aesthetic style. Following this, our talented graphic designers will produce initial sketches and concepts, which will be refined based on feedback from {{streamer_name}}. The final deliverable will be a high-resolution mascot design, optimized for use across multiple platforms, including overlays, merchandise, and promotional materials. By the end of this project, {{streamer_name}} will have a visually striking mascot that not only represents their brand but also strengthens their connection with the audience, ultimately contributing to the growth of their streaming community.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct an initial meeting with {{streamer_name}} to gather insights on brand values, target audience, and preferred design styles.
  - Develop a creative brief summarizing the findings from the consultation.

- **Design Phase**:
  - Create initial sketches and concepts based on the creative brief.
  - Present the sketches to {{streamer_name}} for feedback and suggestions.
  - Revise the designs according to the feedback received.

- **Finalization Phase**:
  - Develop the final high-resolution mascot design.
  - Prepare the design files in various formats suitable for different platforms (e.g., PNG, SVG).
  - Deliver the final mascot design along with a style guide outlining usage recommendations.
