
# Project Plan: Custom Stream Branding Package

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Stream Branding Package aims to provide streamers with a comprehensive suite of graphic design elements that significantly enhance their online presence. This project focuses on creating a unique logo, eye-catching overlays, professional banners, and dynamic animations tailored to reflect the streamer's personal brand and resonate with their audience. The primary objective is to elevate the visual appeal of the stream, ensuring a cohesive identity across all platforms. Key outcomes include increased viewer engagement, improved brand recognition, and a memorable experience for the audience. By investing in this branding package, streamers will stand out in the competitive landscape, fostering a stronger connection with their viewers and enhancing their overall streaming experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Logo Design**: Create a unique and memorable logo that encapsulates the streamer's personal brand. This will involve:
  - Initial consultation to understand the streamer's vision and preferences.
  - Concept sketches and revisions based on feedback.
  - Final logo delivery in various formats (PNG, SVG, etc.).

- **Overlay Design**: Develop custom overlays that enhance viewer engagement during live streams. This includes:
  - Designing overlays for different streaming scenarios (e.g., gameplay, chatting).
  - Incorporating elements such as alerts, chat boxes, and social media handles.
  - Providing editable files for future adjustments.

- **Banner Creation**: Design professional banners for use on various platforms (Twitch, YouTube, etc.). This will involve:
  - Understanding the dimensions and requirements for each platform.
  - Creating visually appealing banners that promote the streamer's brand.
  - Delivering final designs in appropriate formats.

- **Dynamic Animations**: Produce animations that add flair to broadcasts, including:
  - Intro and outro animations for streams.
  - Transition animations between scenes.
  - Custom alerts for new followers, donations, etc.

- **Revisions and Feedback**: Allow for a specified number of revisions for each design element based on client feedback.
