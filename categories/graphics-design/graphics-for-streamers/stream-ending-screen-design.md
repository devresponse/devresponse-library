
# Project Plan: Stream Ending Screen Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Stream Ending Screen Design" project aims to create a visually captivating and brand-aligned ending screen for streamers, enhancing their audience's experience as the stream concludes. In the competitive landscape of streaming, it is essential to leave a lasting impression on viewers, and this project addresses that need by delivering a high-quality design that encapsulates the streamer's unique identity. The objectives include integrating the streamer's logo, color scheme, and thematic elements that resonate with their content. Additionally, the design will feature designated spaces for social media handles and a compelling call-to-action, encouraging viewers to follow or subscribe. Key outcomes of this project will include a polished, professional ending screen delivered in high-resolution formats suitable for various streaming platforms, ultimately elevating the streamer's brand presence and fostering viewer engagement.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Discovery**: 
  - Conduct a brief consultation with the streamer to gather insights on brand identity, preferred color schemes, and thematic elements.
  - Analyze competitor ending screens for inspiration and differentiation.

- **Design Concept Development**: 
  - Create initial design concepts based on the gathered information.
  - Present 2-3 design options for feedback.

- **Revisions and Finalization**: 
  - Incorporate feedback from the streamer to refine the chosen design.
  - Ensure the design includes space for social media handles and a call-to-action.

- **Delivery**: 
  - Provide the final design in multiple high-resolution formats (e.g., PNG, JPEG, and PSD) suitable for various streaming platforms.
  - Include a style guide outlining the color codes and font choices used in the design.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Discovery
  - Deliverable: Summary document of brand insights and competitor analysis.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Presentation of Design Concepts
  - Deliverable: 2-3 initial design concepts for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Final Design Approval
  - Deliverable: Refined design based on feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: High-resolution ending screen files and style guide.
