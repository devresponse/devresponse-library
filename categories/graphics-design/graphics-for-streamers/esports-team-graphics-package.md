
# Project Plan: Esports Team Graphics Package

## 1. Executive Summary

The Esports Team Graphics Package aims to provide esports teams with a comprehensive suite of graphic design elements that significantly enhance their brand identity and visual presence on streaming platforms. This project will focus on creating custom overlays that seamlessly integrate with popular streaming software, ensuring that the team's branding is prominently displayed during live broadcasts. In addition to overlays, the package will include the design of eye-catching promotional banners and unique logos that encapsulate the team's spirit and mission. The graphics will be tailored to meet the specific needs of each team, ensuring that all elements are cohesive and professionally executed. Key outcomes of this project include improved audience engagement, a stronger brand presence, and a competitive edge in the esports landscape. By investing in this graphics package, esports teams can elevate their streaming experience and effectively connect with their audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Custom Overlays**: Design and develop overlays that integrate with popular streaming software (e.g., OBS, Streamlabs). These overlays will include elements such as webcam frames, alerts for new followers/subscribers, and scoreboards.
  
- **Promotional Banners**: Create visually appealing banners for use on social media and during live streams. These banners will promote upcoming events, sponsorships, and team achievements.

- **Logo Design**: Develop a unique logo that reflects the team's identity and mission. This logo will be versatile for use across various platforms, including merchandise, social media, and streaming overlays.

- **Branding Guidelines**: Provide a comprehensive branding guide that outlines the color palette, typography, and usage of graphic elements to ensure consistency across all platforms.

- **Revisions and Feedback**: Allow for a specified number of revisions based on client feedback to ensure satisfaction with the final products.
