
# Project Plan: Twitch Panels Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Twitch Panels Design project aims to create custom-designed panels that enhance the visual appeal and functionality of a streaming channel. In the competitive landscape of online streaming, it is essential for streamers to present a professional and cohesive brand image to attract and retain viewers. This project will focus on designing panels that not only align with the streamer’s unique branding—incorporating their color scheme, logo, and overall theme—but also effectively communicate essential channel information. Key outcomes include high-resolution images optimized for Twitch, which will serve specific purposes such as 'About Me', 'Schedule', 'Social Media Links', and 'Donations'. By ensuring that each panel is visually appealing and informative, the project will elevate the professionalism of the channel, ultimately enhancing viewer engagement and satisfaction. The successful completion of this project will provide streamers with the tools they need to create a more organized and inviting channel experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: 
  - Conduct a review of existing Twitch channels to identify design trends and best practices.
  - Gather information on the streamer’s branding elements, including color schemes, logos, and themes.

- **Design Concept Development**: 
  - Create initial design concepts for each panel, ensuring they are cohesive and aligned with the streamer’s branding.
  - Present design concepts to the client for feedback and revisions.

- **Panel Design Creation**: 
  - Finalize designs based on client feedback.
  - Create high-resolution images for each panel, ensuring they meet Twitch’s specifications.

- **Optimization**: 
  - Optimize images for various devices to ensure clarity and quality across platforms.
  - Provide the final deliverables in multiple formats as required by the client.

- **Delivery and Support**: 
  - Deliver the final panel designs to the client.
  - Offer support for implementation and any necessary adjustments post-delivery.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Research and Analysis Completion
  - Deliverable: Research report and branding questionnaire results.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Design Concept Presentation
  - Deliverable: Initial design concepts for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Final Design Approval
  - Deliverable: Approved designs for all panels.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Deliverables Submission
  - Deliverable: High-resolution images of all panels in required formats.
