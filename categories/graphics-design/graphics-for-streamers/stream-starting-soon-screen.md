
# Project Plan Template: Stream Starting Soon Screen

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Stream Starting Soon Screen" project aims to create a visually captivating and engaging screen that serves as an introduction to your streaming sessions. In the competitive landscape of online streaming, it is essential to capture and maintain viewer attention right from the start. This project will focus on designing a screen that not only reflects your unique brand identity but also enhances viewer engagement during the waiting period. Key objectives include the development of eye-catching graphics, dynamic animations, and a cohesive color scheme that aligns with your overall streaming aesthetic. The final product will incorporate interactive elements such as countdown timers and social media handles, fostering a sense of connection with your audience. By delivering high-resolution formats suitable for various streaming platforms, we aim to ensure that your viewers are captivated from the moment they join your stream, ultimately contributing to a more professional and polished streaming experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: 
  - Analyze existing 'Starting Soon' screens from popular streamers.
  - Identify trends and best practices in graphic design for streaming.

- **Concept Development**: 
  - Create initial design concepts that reflect the unique brand identity of the streamer.
  - Develop a color palette and typography that aligns with the overall streaming aesthetic.

- **Graphic Design**: 
  - Design the main 'Starting Soon' screen layout.
  - Incorporate dynamic animations to enhance visual appeal.
  - Add interactive elements such as countdown timers and social media handles.

- **Review and Revisions**: 
  - Present initial designs to the client for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on client input.

- **Finalization**: 
  - Prepare the final design in high-resolution formats.
  - Ensure compatibility with various streaming platforms (e.g., Twitch, YouTube).

- **Delivery**: 
  - Provide the final deliverables to the client, including all necessary files and formats.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Analysis
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and design inspirations.

- **Milestone 2**: Concept Development Approval
  - Deliverable: Presentation of initial design concepts for client approval.

- **Milestone 3**: Graphic Design Completion
  - Deliverable: Draft version of the 'Starting Soon' screen for review.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Revisions and Approval
  - Deliverable: Final design approved by the client.

- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of Final Assets
  - Deliverable: High-resolution files delivered in multiple formats.
