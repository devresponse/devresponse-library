
# Project Plan Template: Urban Street Art

## 1. Executive Summary

The Urban Street Art project aims to create a visually striking album cover that embodies the essence of urban street art, specifically drawing inspiration from the vibrant world of graffiti. The primary objective is to design an album cover that not only reflects the unique style of the artist but also resonates with their target audience, ensuring the album stands out in a competitive market. This project will involve close collaboration between our professional designers and the client to fully understand their artistic vision and the message they wish to convey through their music. Key outcomes include the delivery of initial concept sketches, a series of revisions based on client feedback, and the final high-resolution artwork suitable for both digital and print formats. By blending creativity with cultural expression, this project presents a unique opportunity to produce an album cover that is both memorable and impactful, ultimately enhancing the artist's brand and market presence.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, musical themes, and any specific elements they want to incorporate into the album cover.
  
- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather visual references and inspiration from urban street art, graffiti styles, and current design trends to inform the creative process.
  
- **Concept Development**: Create initial concept sketches that reflect the discussed ideas and themes. Present these concepts to the client for feedback.
  
- **Revisions**: Based on client feedback, make necessary adjustments to the selected concept. This may involve color changes, texture adjustments, or layout modifications.
  
- **Final Design**: Develop the final high-resolution artwork, ensuring it meets all specifications for both digital and print formats.
  
- **Client Approval**: Present the final design to the client for approval. Make any last-minute adjustments as required.
  
- **Delivery**: Provide the final artwork files in various formats (JPEG, PNG, PDF) and resolutions suitable for different uses (social media, album printing, etc.).

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and project brief
  
- **Milestone 2**: Concept Sketches Presented
  - Deliverable: 3-5 initial concept sketches
  
- **Milestone 3**: Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised design based on client feedback
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approved
  - Deliverable: High-resolution final artwork
  
- **Milestone 5**: Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Final files delivered and project summary report
