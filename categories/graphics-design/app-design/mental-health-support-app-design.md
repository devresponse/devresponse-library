
# Mental Health Support App Design Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Mental Health Support App Design project aims to create a visually calming and user-friendly interface that fosters a sense of safety and support for users seeking mental health resources. The primary objective is to bridge the gap between users and licensed therapists through an engaging and accessible app. Key outcomes include the development of a soothing color palette, intuitive navigation, and engaging visual elements that promote a positive user experience. The app will feature chat functionality, appointment scheduling, and access to self-help materials, ensuring that users from diverse backgrounds can navigate the app with ease. By prioritizing accessibility and inclusivity, this project is crucial for enhancing user engagement and satisfaction, ultimately contributing to the well-being of individuals seeking mental health support. The final deliverables will include wireframes, high-fidelity mockups, and a comprehensive style guide to ensure consistency across all app screens.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Mental Health Support App Design project includes the following key components:

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct user research to understand the needs and preferences of the target audience, including surveys and interviews with potential users and mental health professionals.
- **Wireframing**: Create low-fidelity wireframes to outline the app's structure and layout, focusing on user flow and functionality.
- **Visual Design**: Develop high-fidelity mockups that incorporate a soothing color palette and engaging visual elements, ensuring a calming user experience.
- **Accessibility Review**: Ensure that the design meets accessibility standards (e.g., WCAG) to accommodate users with varying abilities.
- **Style Guide Creation**: Develop a comprehensive style guide that includes typography, color schemes, iconography, and UI components to maintain consistency throughout the app.
- **User Testing**: Conduct usability testing with a diverse group of users to gather feedback and make necessary adjustments to the design.
- **Final Deliverables**: Provide the final wireframes, high-fidelity mockups, and style guide to the development team for implementation.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of User Research  
  **Deliverable**: Research report summarizing user needs and preferences.

- **Milestone 2**: Approval of Wireframes  
  **Deliverable**: Low-fidelity wireframes for the app layout and user flow.

- **Milestone 3**: Approval of High-Fidelity Mockups  
  **Deliverable**: High-fidelity mockups showcasing the visual design of the app.

- **Milestone 4**: Style Guide Completion  
  **Deliverable**: Comprehensive style guide for app consistency.

- **Milestone 5**: User Testing Completion  
  **Deliverable**: Usability testing report with feedback and recommendations.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Project Handover  
  **Deliverable**: All final design assets delivered to the development team.
