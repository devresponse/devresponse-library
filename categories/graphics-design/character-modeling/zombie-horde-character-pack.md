
# Project Plan Template: Zombie Horde Character Pack

## 1. Executive Summary

The Zombie Horde Character Pack project aims to create a comprehensive collection of unique 3D zombie character models, each showcasing distinct levels of decay and diverse appearances. This project is designed to cater to the needs of horror game developers and filmmakers seeking high-quality assets to enhance their projects. The primary objective is to deliver a set of meticulously crafted zombie models that embody the eerie and unsettling characteristics typical of the horror genre. Key outcomes include a diverse range of zombie designs, each rigged for animation and optimized for performance in various digital environments. The final deliverables will encompass multiple variations of zombies, complete with unique textures, poses, and accessories, providing a rich and immersive experience for users. Collaboration and feedback will be integral throughout the design process to ensure that the final product aligns with the client's vision and expectations, ultimately contributing to the success of their horror-themed projects.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Zombie Horde Character Pack includes the following key components:

- **Research and Concept Development**: Conduct research on zombie archetypes and horror aesthetics to inform the design process. Develop initial concepts and sketches for the character models.
  
- **3D Modeling**: Create high-quality 3D models of zombies, ensuring each model reflects unique characteristics such as varying levels of decay, clothing styles, and physical features.

- **Texturing and Materials**: Apply detailed textures and materials to each model to enhance realism, including skin textures, clothing wear, and environmental effects.

- **Rigging and Animation**: Rig each character model for animation, allowing for a range of movements and poses. Develop a set of standard animations for idle, walking, and attacking behaviors.

- **Optimization**: Optimize models for performance in various digital environments, ensuring compatibility with popular game engines and rendering software.

- **Quality Assurance**: Conduct thorough testing of models and animations to ensure functionality and visual fidelity. Gather feedback from stakeholders and make necessary adjustments.

- **Final Deliverables**: Package the final character models, textures, and animations into a user-friendly format for distribution. Provide documentation and support materials for users.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Concept Development  
  **Deliverable**: Concept sketches and design document

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of 3D Modeling

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Texturing and Materials  
  **Deliverable**: Textured models ready for rigging

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Rigging and Animation  
  **Deliverable**: Rigged models with basic animations

- **Milestone 5**: Completion of Optimization  
  **Deliverable**: Optimized models for various platforms

- **Milestone 6**: Final Quality Assurance  
  **Deliverable**: QA report and final adjustments

- **Milestone 7**: Project Completion and Delivery  
  **Deliverable**: Final character pack, documentation, and support materials
