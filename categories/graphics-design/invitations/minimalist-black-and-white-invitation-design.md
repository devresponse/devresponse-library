
# Project Plan Template: Minimalist Black and White Invitation Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Minimalist Black and White Invitation Design project aims to create elegant and sophisticated invitations that embody a modern aesthetic through a refined black-and-white color scheme. The primary objective is to design invitations that not only convey essential event information but also serve as artistic representations that set the tone for various formal occasions, including weddings, corporate gatherings, and special celebrations. This project will involve close collaboration with the client to ensure their vision and preferences are fully realized. Key outcomes include the delivery of multiple design concepts, incorporation of client feedback through revisions, and provision of final files that are print-ready or suitable for digital distribution. Ultimately, this project seeks to elevate the invitation experience, making it a memorable part of the event.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and specific requirements for the invitations.
- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather inspiration and conduct research on current design trends in minimalist invitation design.
- **Concept Development**: Create three initial design concepts that reflect the client's preferences and the event's formality.
- **Client Review**: Present the design concepts to the client for feedback and selection of a preferred concept.
- **Revisions**: Based on client feedback, make necessary revisions to the selected design concept.
- **Finalization**: Prepare the final design, ensuring all elements are polished and aligned with the client's vision.
- **File Preparation**: Create print-ready files and digital versions of the invitation for distribution.
- **Delivery**: Deliver the final files to the client along with any necessary usage guidelines.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed

  - Deliverable: Revised design based on client feedback

  - Deliverable: Delivery of final files and project summary
