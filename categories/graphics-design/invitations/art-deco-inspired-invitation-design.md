
# Art Deco-Inspired Invitation Design Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Art Deco-Inspired Invitation Design project aims to create exquisite invitations that embody the elegance and sophistication of the Art Deco movement. The primary objective is to design invitations that incorporate distinctive geometric patterns, luxurious metallic accents, and vintage typography, capturing the essence of the Roaring Twenties. This project will focus on tailoring each invitation to reflect the specific theme of the client's event, whether it be a lavish Gatsby-themed party or an elegant corporate gathering. Key outcomes include the delivery of visually stunning invitations that not only convey essential event details but also serve as beautiful keepsakes for guests. Through close collaboration with the client, we will ensure that the final design aligns perfectly with their vision, resulting in invitations that set the tone for an unforgettable celebration.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to discuss their vision, event theme, and specific requirements for the invitations.
- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather inspiration from the Art Deco movement, focusing on geometric patterns, color palettes, and typography.
- **Design Concepts**: Create three initial design concepts for the client to review, incorporating feedback to refine the designs.
- **Final Design Selection**: Collaborate with the client to select the final design and make any necessary adjustments.
- **Production**: Prepare the final design for printing, ensuring high-quality materials and finishes are used.
- **Delivery**: Coordinate the printing and delivery of the invitations to the client, ensuring they arrive on time for the event.
- **Post-Event Follow-Up**: Gather feedback from the client regarding the invitations and their guests' reactions.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed

- **Milestone 5**: Invitations Delivered
