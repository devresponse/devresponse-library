
# Project Plan Template: Destination Wedding Invitation Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Destination Wedding Invitation Design project aims to create bespoke invitations that encapsulate the essence of the chosen wedding location while providing essential details for guests. The project will begin with an in-depth consultation to understand the couple's vision and the unique characteristics of the destination, whether it be a serene beach, a majestic mountain, or a culturally rich landmark. Key outcomes include a series of design drafts that incorporate local motifs, maps, and patterns, followed by revisions based on client feedback. The final product will be an exquisite invitation that not only informs but also excites guests about the upcoming celebration. This project seeks to ensure that the invitations serve as a beautiful prelude to the unforgettable experience that awaits, setting the perfect tone for the special day.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the couple to discuss their vision, preferences, and the destination's unique elements.
- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather inspiration based on the destination, including local culture, colors, and motifs.
- **Design Drafts**: Create initial design drafts that incorporate the discussed elements.
- **Client Review**: Present drafts to the couple for feedback and suggestions.
- **Revisions**: Make necessary revisions based on client feedback to refine the design.
- **Final Approval**: Obtain final approval from the couple on the design.
- **Production**: Prepare the final design for printing, including selecting paper quality and printing techniques.
- **Delivery**: Coordinate the delivery of the printed invitations to the couple.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client vision and destination elements.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Design Drafts Created
  - Deliverable: Three initial design drafts for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Client Review Completed
  - Deliverable: Feedback document from the couple.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised design drafts based on feedback.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Approval Received
  - Deliverable: Approved final design.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Invitations Printed
  - Deliverable: Printed invitations ready for delivery.
  
- **Milestone 7**: Invitations Delivered
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of delivery to the couple.
