
# Interactive Puzzle Invitation Design Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Interactive Puzzle Invitation Design project aims to revolutionize the traditional invitation experience by creating a unique and engaging puzzle format that captivates recipients. The primary objective is to design invitations that not only convey essential event details—such as date, time, and location—but also provide an interactive activity that enhances guest anticipation. This project will involve a collaborative process with clients to ensure that their vision and preferences are seamlessly integrated into the final product. Key outcomes include the delivery of high-quality, visually appealing puzzle invitations that reflect the essence of the client's event, whether it be a wedding, birthday party, or corporate gathering. By incorporating vibrant colors and thematic elements, we aim to create an invitation that serves as both a functional piece of information and a memorable keepsake, setting the tone for a delightful and engaging event experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Client Consultation**: Initial meetings to discuss the client's vision, preferences, and event details.
- **Design Concept Development**: Creation of multiple design concepts that incorporate the client's themes and colors.
- **Puzzle Design**: Development of the puzzle format, including the number of pieces and the layout of information.
- **Material Selection**: Choosing high-quality materials that ensure durability and visual appeal.
- **Prototype Creation**: Producing a prototype of the invitation for client review and feedback.
- **Revisions and Finalization**: Making necessary adjustments based on client feedback and finalizing the design.
- **Production**: Printing and assembling the invitations.
- **Quality Assurance**: Conducting a thorough quality check to ensure all invitations meet the established standards.
- **Delivery**: Shipping the completed invitations to the client or directly to the guests, as per the client's preference.
