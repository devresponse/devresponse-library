
# Project Plan Template: Retro Neon-Themed Party Invitations

## 1. Executive Summary

The Retro Neon-Themed Party Invitations project aims to design visually striking invitations that embody the vibrant and energetic essence of 80s aesthetics. The primary objective is to create invitations that not only provide essential event details but also evoke a sense of nostalgia and excitement among the guests. By utilizing a bold color palette dominated by electric pinks, greens, and blues, along with dynamic typography and iconic 80s graphics, we will ensure that each invitation captures the lively spirit of the era. Key outcomes of this project include the delivery of high-quality digital formats for online sharing and print-ready formats for physical distribution, ensuring versatility in how the invitations are presented. Ultimately, this project seeks to create a memorable first impression for guests, setting the tone for a fun and lively celebration.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Design Concept Development**: 
  - Research and gather inspiration from 80s aesthetics, including color schemes, typography, and graphic elements.
  - Create mood boards to visualize the design direction.

- **Invitation Design**:
  - Develop multiple design concepts featuring vibrant colors and bold typography.
  - Incorporate iconic 80s graphics such as geometric shapes and neon lights.
  - Ensure that each design includes essential event details (date, time, location, RSVP information).

- **Client Review and Feedback**:
  - Present initial design concepts to the client for feedback.
  - Revise designs based on client input and preferences.

- **Finalization of Designs**:
  - Finalize the selected design(s) after incorporating feedback.
  - Prepare digital formats suitable for online sharing (e.g., JPEG, PNG).
  - Prepare print-ready formats (e.g., PDF) for physical distribution.

- **Delivery**:
  - Deliver all finalized designs to the client in agreed formats.
  - Provide guidelines for printing and sharing the invitations.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Design Concept Development
  - Deliverable: Mood boards and design direction document.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Presentation of Initial Design Concepts
  - Deliverable: Three design concepts for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised designs based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Finalization of Designs
  - Deliverable: Finalized digital and print-ready invitation designs.

- **Milestone 5**: Project Delivery
  - Deliverable: All finalized designs delivered to the client.
