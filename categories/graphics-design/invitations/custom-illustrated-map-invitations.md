
# Project Plan: Custom Illustrated Map Invitations

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Illustrated Map Invitations project aims to create unique and visually engaging invitations that serve as both informative guides and artistic keepsakes for various events. The primary objective is to design hand-drawn maps that highlight key landmarks and personalized icons relevant to the event, whether it be a wedding, birthday, or corporate gathering. This project will involve a series of consultations with clients to capture their vision and preferences, followed by the development of drafts and revisions to ensure satisfaction. Key outcomes include a finalized invitation design that not only informs guests about the event location but also enhances the overall theme and ambiance of the occasion. By delivering a product that combines functionality with artistry, we aim to create a memorable experience for both the hosts and their guests.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to discuss their vision, event details, and any specific elements they want included in the map.
- **Research and Planning**: Gather information about the event location, including landmarks, routes, and any personalized icons that reflect the theme.
- **Draft Design**: Create initial sketches of the map and invitation layout for client review.
- **Revisions**: Incorporate client feedback into the design, making necessary adjustments to ensure the final product aligns with their expectations.
- **Final Design**: Produce the final version of the illustrated map invitation, ensuring high-quality graphics and print-ready formats.
- **Delivery**: Provide the client with digital files and/or printed invitations as per their preference.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and agreed-upon design direction.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Draft Design Submitted
  - Deliverable: Initial sketches of the map and invitation layout.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised design based on client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approved
  - Deliverable: Final illustrated map invitation in digital format.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of Invitations
  - Deliverable: Printed invitations and/or digital files sent to the client.
