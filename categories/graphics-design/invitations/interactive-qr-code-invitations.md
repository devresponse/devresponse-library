
# Project Plan Template: Interactive QR Code Invitations

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Interactive QR Code Invitations" project aims to revolutionize the traditional invitation process by integrating modern technology into the design. The primary objective is to create unique, visually appealing invitations that incorporate QR codes, allowing guests to easily access event details, RSVP forms, and personalized video messages from the hosts. This project will not only enhance guest engagement but also provide a memorable experience that reflects the theme and tone of the event. By collaborating closely with the client, we will ensure that each invitation is tailored to their vision, resulting in a product that captivates recipients and encourages participation. Key outcomes include the delivery of high-quality digital and print-ready invitations, increased guest interaction through QR code functionality, and a streamlined RSVP process that enhances event planning efficiency.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Client Consultation**: Conduct initial meetings with the client to gather requirements, understand the event theme, and discuss design preferences.
- **Design Concept Development**: Create multiple design concepts that incorporate QR codes in a stylish manner, ensuring alignment with the event's theme.
- **QR Code Integration**: Generate and test QR codes that link to event details, RSVP forms, and personalized messages.
- **Client Review and Feedback**: Present design concepts to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Final Design Approval**: Obtain final approval from the client on the selected design.
- **Production of Digital and Print-Ready Formats**: Prepare the final design in both digital formats (PDF, JPEG) and print-ready formats (CMYK, high resolution).
- **Delivery**: Provide the final product to the client via email and/or physical delivery, as per the client's preference.
- **Post-Delivery Support**: Offer support for any issues related to the QR code functionality or design adjustments after delivery.
