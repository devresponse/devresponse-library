
# Project Plan Template: Pop-Up 3D Invitation Cards

## 1. Executive Summary

The Pop-Up 3D Invitation Cards project aims to create captivating and interactive invitations that leave a lasting impression on recipients. Our primary objective is to design and produce stunning pop-up invitations that incorporate intricate paper engineering techniques, tailored to suit various occasions such as weddings, birthdays, and corporate events. The project will begin with initial consultations to capture the client's vision, followed by the development of sketches and prototypes that refine the design. Key outcomes include high-quality printed invitations that reflect the client's personal style or brand identity, ensuring each card is not only visually appealing but also a memorable keepsake for guests. By focusing on customization and creativity, this project promises to elevate any event, making it unforgettable from the very first glance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Meet with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and event details.
  - Gather information on themes, color schemes, and any specific requirements.

- **Design Development**: 
  - Create initial sketches based on the consultation feedback.
  - Develop paper engineering techniques to ensure the pop-up effect is both functional and aesthetically pleasing.

- **Prototyping**: 
  - Produce prototypes of the invitation cards for client review.
  - Incorporate client feedback to refine the design.

- **Final Design Approval**: 
  - Present the final design to the client for approval.
  - Make any necessary adjustments based on final feedback.

- **Production**: 
  - Print the approved designs using high-quality materials.
  - Ensure that all invitations are produced to the highest standards of craftsmanship.

- **Delivery**: 
  - Package and deliver the final invitations to the client, ready for distribution.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Client brief document outlining vision and requirements.

- **Milestone 2**: Design Sketches Completed
  - Deliverable: Set of initial sketches for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Prototype Development Completed
  - Deliverable: Physical prototypes of the invitation cards.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approval
  - Deliverable: Approved final design ready for production.

- **Milestone 5**: Production Completed
  - Deliverable: Batch of high-quality printed invitations.

- **Milestone 6**: Delivery of Invitations
  - Deliverable: Packaged invitations delivered to the client.
