
# Project Plan Template: Luxury Velvet Invitation Cards

## 1. Executive Summary

The Luxury Velvet Invitation Cards project aims to create exquisite invitations that embody elegance and sophistication. Our primary objective is to design and produce high-quality invitations utilizing velvet-textured paper, which provides a unique tactile experience that sets the tone for any upscale event. The project will focus on delivering multiple design concepts that incorporate refined typography, ensuring legibility while enhancing aesthetic appeal. Clients will have the option to enhance their invitations with metallic foil accents, adding a touch of glamour and luxury. Key outcomes include the delivery of production-ready files, a streamlined design process that incorporates client feedback, and invitations that serve not only as informative pieces but also as stunning works of art. Ultimately, our goal is to leave a lasting impression on guests and elevate the overall event experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather design inspirations and trends in luxury invitation design.
- **Concept Development**: Create multiple design concepts for client review, focusing on typography, layout, and color schemes.
- **Client Collaboration**: Schedule meetings with clients to discuss concepts, gather feedback, and make necessary revisions.
- **Design Refinement**: Finalize the chosen design based on client feedback, ensuring all elements align with the client's vision.
- **Production Preparation**: Prepare production-ready files, including specifications for velvet-textured paper and metallic foil accents.
- **Quality Assurance**: Conduct a final review of the designs to ensure all elements meet quality standards before printing.
- **Printing Coordination**: Collaborate with printing vendors to ensure high-quality production of the invitations.
- **Delivery**: Coordinate the delivery of the finished invitations to the client.
