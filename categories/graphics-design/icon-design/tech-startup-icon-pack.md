
# Project Plan Template: Tech Startup Icon Pack

## 1. Executive Summary

The Tech Startup Icon Pack project aims to create a comprehensive set of icons specifically designed for a tech startup. The primary objective is to develop icons that effectively represent various aspects of the startup, including its products, services, team members, and key funding milestones. By embodying the essence of innovation and professionalism, these icons will resonate with the target audience and enhance the overall user experience. The project will begin with brainstorming sessions to capture the startup's vision, followed by the creation of sketches and digital drafts. Throughout the design process, we will actively seek and incorporate feedback to ensure that the icons align with the brand's identity and values. The key outcomes of this project will include a high-resolution icon pack delivered in multiple formats, ready for use across various digital platforms, ultimately contributing to the startup's branding and marketing efforts.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with stakeholders to understand the startup's vision, values, and target audience.
- **Brainstorming Sessions**: Facilitate collaborative sessions to generate ideas and concepts for the icon designs.
- **Sketching**: Create initial sketches of the icons based on the ideas generated during brainstorming.
- **Digital Drafts**: Develop digital versions of the selected sketches, ensuring they are aligned with the brand's aesthetic.
- **Feedback Loops**: Present drafts to stakeholders for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Finalization**: Finalize the icon designs after multiple iterations and approvals.
- **Delivery**: Provide high-resolution icons in various formats (e.g., PNG, SVG, AI) for use across digital platforms.
- **Documentation**: Create a style guide that outlines the usage of the icons, including color codes and sizing recommendations.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Icon Designs Approved
  - Deliverable: Finalized icon designs

- **Milestone 7**: Icon Pack Delivered
