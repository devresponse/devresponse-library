
# Unisex Capsule Wardrobe Technical Design Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Unisex Capsule Wardrobe Technical Design project aims to create a cohesive collection of versatile and timeless garments suitable for all genders. The primary objective is to develop detailed technical drawings that accurately represent each piece in the collection, ensuring clarity for manufacturers and facilitating a smooth production process. This project will also deliver a comprehensive tech pack that includes essential specifications such as fabric types, color palettes, measurements, and construction details. Key outcomes include a well-rounded capsule wardrobe that emphasizes functionality and style, allowing for easy mixing and matching of pieces. By focusing on quality and design integrity, this project is ideal for fashion brands looking to streamline their production process while appealing to a diverse customer base. Ultimately, the project seeks to enhance the brand's market presence and customer satisfaction through innovative design and practical solutions.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**
  - Conduct market research to identify current trends in unisex fashion.
  - Analyze target demographics and their preferences for a capsule wardrobe.

- **Design Development**
  - Create initial sketches and concepts for the collection.
  - Develop detailed technical drawings for each garment, including front, back, and side views.

- **Tech Pack Creation**
  - Compile a comprehensive tech pack for each garment, including:
    - Fabric types and sourcing information
    - Color palettes and swatches
    - Detailed measurements and size specifications
    - Construction details and assembly instructions

- **Prototype Development**
  - Collaborate with manufacturers to create prototypes based on technical drawings and tech packs.
  - Conduct fittings and make necessary adjustments to designs.

- **Final Review and Adjustments**
  - Review prototypes and finalize designs based on feedback.
  - Ensure all technical drawings and tech packs are updated accordingly.

- **Delivery**
  - Provide final technical drawings and tech packs to the client.
  - Offer support during the initial production run, if necessary.
