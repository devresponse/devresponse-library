
# Project Plan: Streetwear Collection Technical Drawings

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Streetwear Collection Technical Drawings" project aims to create comprehensive technical drawings for a new line of streetwear garments, specifically hoodies, joggers, and graphic tees. The primary objective is to provide precise measurements and specifications that will serve as a blueprint for garment production. This project will encompass detailed front and back views of each item, including intricate stitching lines, fabric types, and unique design elements that embody the streetwear aesthetic. Key outcomes include a complete tech pack that ensures manufacturers have all the necessary information to produce high-quality garments that align with the designer's vision. By streamlining the production process while maintaining design integrity, this project is tailored for fashion brands seeking to enhance their operational efficiency and product quality.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct market research to understand current streetwear trends and consumer preferences.
- **Design Development**: Collaborate with designers to finalize garment designs, including color palettes and fabric selections.
- **Technical Drawings Creation**: Develop detailed technical drawings for each garment, including:
  - Front and back views
  - Accurate measurements and specifications
  - Stitching lines and construction details
  - Fabric types and care instructions
  - Unique design elements and embellishments
- **Tech Pack Compilation**: Assemble all technical drawings and specifications into a comprehensive tech pack.
- **Review and Revisions**: Facilitate a review process with stakeholders to ensure all details meet the designer's vision and make necessary revisions.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the completed tech pack to the client in both digital and print formats.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of market research and design development
  - Deliverable: Research report and finalized design sketches
- **Milestone 2**: Creation of technical drawings for all garments
  - Deliverable: Technical drawings for hoodies, joggers, and graphic tees
- **Milestone 3**: Compilation of the tech pack
  - Deliverable: Complete tech pack document
- **Milestone 4**: Review and approval process
  - Deliverable: Feedback report and revised tech pack
- **Milestone 5**: Final delivery of the tech pack
  - Deliverable: Final tech pack in digital and print formats
