
# Vehicle Wrap Mockup Design Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Vehicle Wrap Mockup Design project is designed to provide clients with high-quality, realistic mockups that effectively showcase their branding on various types of vehicles, including cars, trucks, and vans. The primary objective of this project is to enable businesses to visualize how their logos and designs will appear on actual vehicles before making a commitment to production. By utilizing advanced design software, we will ensure that the mockups are accurate in terms of color, scale, and detail. Key outcomes of this project include delivering multiple angles and perspectives of the vehicle wraps, which will allow clients to assess their designs from different viewpoints. This service is particularly beneficial for marketing campaigns, promotional events, and fleet branding, as it empowers clients to make informed decisions about their branding strategies. The final deliverables will be provided in high-resolution formats suitable for both digital and print use, ensuring versatility in presentation and maximizing the impact of their branding efforts.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Client Consultation**: Engage with clients to understand their branding requirements, vehicle types, and specific design elements they wish to incorporate.
- **Design Development**: Create initial design concepts based on client feedback, ensuring alignment with their branding guidelines.
- **Mockup Creation**: Utilize advanced design software to create realistic vehicle wrap mockups, including:
  - Cars
  - Trucks
  - Vans
- **Multiple Perspectives**: Provide clients with mockups from various angles to give a comprehensive view of the design.
- **Revisions**: Allow for a specified number of revisions based on client feedback to ensure satisfaction with the final design.
- **Final Deliverables**: Prepare high-resolution mockups in multiple formats (e.g., JPEG, PNG, PDF) for both digital and print use.
- **Client Approval**: Obtain final approval from the client before project closure.
