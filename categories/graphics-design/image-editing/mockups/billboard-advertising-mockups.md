
# Project Plan Template: Billboard Advertising Mockups

## 1. Executive Summary

The Billboard Advertising Mockups project aims to create high-quality, realistic mockups that enable clients to visualize their advertisements in various outdoor environments. The primary objective is to provide clients with a comprehensive view of how their designs will appear on different billboard formats, including standard, digital, and large-scale options. By showcasing advertisements in multiple perspectives and settings, we will empower clients to make informed decisions regarding their marketing strategies. Key outcomes of this project include the delivery of high-resolution mockups that are suitable for presentations and marketing purposes, ensuring that clients can effectively communicate their vision to stakeholders. This service is essential for enhancing the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and maximizing client satisfaction.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct market research to identify popular billboard formats and outdoor environments that resonate with target audiences.
- **Design Development**: Collaborate with clients to gather their advertisement designs and understand their vision for the mockups.
- **Mockup Creation**: Develop high-quality, realistic billboard mockups in various formats, including:
  - Standard billboards
  - Digital billboards
  - Large-scale billboards
- **Perspective and Setting Variations**: Create multiple perspectives and settings for each mockup to provide a comprehensive view of the advertisement in real-world scenarios.
- **Client Review and Feedback**: Present initial mockups to clients for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Final Deliverables**: Provide the final mockups in high-resolution formats suitable for presentations and marketing purposes.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of market research and analysis
  - **Deliverable**: Research report outlining popular billboard formats and environments
- **Milestone 2**: Client design collection and initial mockup drafts
  - **Deliverable**: Draft mockups for client review
- **Milestone 3**: Client feedback and revisions
  - **Deliverable**: Revised mockups based on client feedback
- **Milestone 4**: Final mockup delivery
  - **Deliverable**: High-resolution mockups in agreed formats
