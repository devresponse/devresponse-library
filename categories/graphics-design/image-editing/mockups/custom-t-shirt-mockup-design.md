
# Project Plan Template: Custom T-Shirt Mockup Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom T-Shirt Mockup Design project aims to deliver high-quality, realistic mockups of custom t-shirt designs for clients seeking to visualize their apparel concepts before production. This project is essential for fashion designers, brands, and individuals who want to present their t-shirt ideas in a professional manner. By providing multiple mockup variations that showcase different angles, colors, and styles, we ensure that clients can accurately assess how their designs will appear in real life. Key outcomes include tailored mockups that reflect the client's unique branding and design elements, delivered in high-resolution formats suitable for presentations, marketing, or online stores. Ultimately, this project will empower clients to make informed decisions about their apparel concepts, enhancing their confidence in moving forward with production.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Client Consultation**: Conduct an initial meeting with the client to understand their vision, branding, and design elements.
- **Design Creation**: Develop multiple mockup variations based on the client's specifications, including:
  - Different t-shirt styles (e.g., crew neck, v-neck, long sleeve)
  - A variety of colors and fabric textures
  - Multiple angles and perspectives (front, back, side)
- **Feedback and Revisions**: Present initial mockups to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Finalization**: Prepare the final mockups incorporating all approved changes.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final mockups in high-resolution formats (e.g., PNG, JPEG, PSD) suitable for various uses such as marketing, online stores, and presentations.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Client Consultation
  - Deliverable: Summary of client requirements and design preferences
- **Milestone 2**: Initial Mockup Designs
  - Deliverable: Presentation of multiple mockup variations for client review
- **Milestone 3**: Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised mockups based on client feedback
- **Milestone 4**: Final Mockup Delivery
  - Deliverable: High-resolution mockups delivered in agreed formats
