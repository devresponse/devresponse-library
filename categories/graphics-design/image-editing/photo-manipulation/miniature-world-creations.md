
# Miniature World Creations Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Miniature World Creations project aims to transform ordinary photographs into captivating miniature landscapes that evoke emotion and tell a story. Our primary objective is to elevate visual content for advertising campaigns, social media, or personal projects, making them memorable and impactful. This project will involve a comprehensive consultation to understand the client's vision, followed by the careful selection of images for transformation. Each photograph will be meticulously edited to incorporate tiny figures and imaginative elements, ensuring a seamless blend that creates a cohesive miniature world. Key outcomes include high-resolution final images suitable for both digital and print use, enhancing the client's ability to engage their audience and stand out in any medium. By focusing on creativity and attention to detail, we aim to deliver a unique visual experience that resonates with viewers and fulfills the client's objectives.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Initial meeting to discuss the client's vision and objectives.
  - Gathering of reference materials and inspiration from the client.
  
- **Image Selection**:
  - Client provides a selection of photographs for transformation.
  - Recommendations on the best images to achieve desired outcomes.

- **Creative Development**:
  - Conceptualization of miniature elements to be added to each image.
  - Sourcing or creating tiny figures and imaginative elements.

- **Editing Process**:
  - Detailed editing of selected images to incorporate miniature elements.
  - Ensuring seamless integration of added components for a cohesive look.
  - Multiple revisions based on client feedback.

- **Final Output**:
  - Delivery of high-resolution images in formats suitable for digital and print use.
  - Provision of a digital portfolio showcasing the transformed images.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the initial consultation and vision gathering.
  - **Deliverable**: Consultation summary document.

- **Milestone 2**: Final selection of images for transformation.
  - **Deliverable**: List of selected images confirmed by the client.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of the creative development phase.
  - **Deliverable**: Concept sketches or mood boards for client approval.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of the editing process.
  - **Deliverable**: Draft versions of edited images for client review.

- **Milestone 5**: Final delivery of high-resolution images.
  - **Deliverable**: Final images delivered in agreed formats.
