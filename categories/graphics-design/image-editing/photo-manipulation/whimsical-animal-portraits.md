
# Project Plan Template: Whimsical Animal Portraits

## 1. Executive Summary

The Whimsical Animal Portraits project aims to create enchanting and imaginative portraits of beloved pets or wildlife through advanced photo manipulation techniques. Our primary objective is to transform ordinary photographs into extraordinary works of art that reflect the unique personality of each animal and the creative vision of the client. By incorporating playful costumes, whimsical props, and surreal backgrounds, we will craft visually stunning pieces that evoke joy and creativity. Key outcomes include delivering high-resolution digital files suitable for printing or online sharing, allowing clients to showcase their portraits in various formats. This project is designed for pet owners who wish to celebrate their furry friends and wildlife enthusiasts seeking memorable art pieces that capture the essence of their subjects.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to understand their vision, preferences, and specific requirements for the portrait.
- **Photo Selection**: Assist the client in selecting the best photographs of their pets or wildlife for manipulation.
- **Concept Development**: Create a concept sketch or mood board that outlines the proposed elements, including costumes, props, and backgrounds.
- **Photo Manipulation**: Utilize advanced software to manipulate the selected photographs, incorporating the agreed-upon elements to create the final portrait.
- **Client Review**: Present the draft portrait to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments based on their input.
- **Finalization**: Complete the final portrait, ensuring all elements are polished and meet the client's expectations.
- **Delivery**: Provide the client with a high-resolution digital file of the final portrait, along with guidelines for printing and sharing.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Client brief document outlining vision and preferences.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Photo Selection Finalized
  - Deliverable: Selected photographs ready for manipulation.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Concept Development Completed
  - Deliverable: Concept sketch or mood board presented to the client.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Draft Portrait Created
  - Deliverable: Draft portrait for client review.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Portrait Delivered
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital file of the final portrait.
