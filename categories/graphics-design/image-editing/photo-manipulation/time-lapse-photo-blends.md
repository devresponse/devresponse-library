
# Project Plan Template: Time-Lapse Photo Blends

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Time-Lapse Photo Blends" project aims to harness the artistic technique of photo manipulation to create a stunning visual representation of time passing. The primary objective is to blend multiple images captured at different times throughout the day into a single cohesive artwork that highlights the transition of light, color, and atmosphere. This project will not only showcase the beauty of time-lapse photography but also cater to the client's specific vision, whether for personal enjoyment, advertising, or branding purposes. Key outcomes include the selection of the best images, meticulous adjustments to colors and lighting for consistency, and the seamless merging of these images to create a harmonious composition. The final deliverable will be a high-resolution digital file, ensuring versatility for both print and online applications. This project is designed to provide a visually striking piece that captures the essence of time, meeting the client's expectations and enhancing their visual storytelling.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Image Selection**: Collaborate with the client to select the most impactful images that represent various times of the day.
- **Color and Lighting Adjustments**: Adjust the colors and lighting of the selected images to ensure consistency across the final composition.
- **Photo Manipulation**: Utilize advanced photo manipulation techniques to blend the images seamlessly, creating a time-lapse effect.
- **Client Review**: Present the initial draft of the blended image to the client for feedback and revisions.
- **Final Adjustments**: Make necessary adjustments based on client feedback to achieve the desired outcome.
- **Delivery of Final Product**: Provide the client with a high-resolution digital file suitable for both print and online use.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of image selection
  - **Deliverable**: List of selected images for approval by the client.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of color and lighting adjustments
  - **Deliverable**: Preview of adjusted images for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of initial photo manipulation
  - **Deliverable**: Draft of the blended image for client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final adjustments based on client feedback
  - **Deliverable**: Revised blended image for final approval.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of final high-resolution digital file
  - **Deliverable**: High-resolution digital file in specified formats (e.g., JPEG, PNG, TIFF).
