
# Project Plan Template: Vintage Film Poster Edits

## 1. Executive Summary

The Vintage Film Poster Edits project aims to harness the art of photo manipulation to create visually stunning vintage-style film posters that resonate with the essence of classic cinema. The primary objective is to transform ordinary images into extraordinary visuals that evoke nostalgia and artistic flair, tailored specifically to meet the client's branding needs or personal preferences. This project will involve a meticulous selection of suitable images, the application of vintage filters, color adjustments, and the incorporation of typography that aligns with the vintage aesthetic. Key outcomes include a series of unique posters that not only capture the spirit of vintage cinema but also tell a story or convey a theme that is meaningful to the client. A collaborative approach will be adopted, ensuring that client feedback is integral to the creative process, ultimately resulting in memorable art pieces that enhance marketing materials for businesses or serve as cherished personal art for individuals.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Image Selection**: Collaborate with the client to select images that reflect the desired theme or story for the poster.
- **Photo Manipulation**: Apply various photo manipulation techniques, including:
  - Vintage filters to create an aged look.
  - Color adjustments to enhance the overall aesthetic.
  - Layering techniques to add depth and texture.
- **Typography Design**: Incorporate typography that complements the vintage style, including font selection and placement.
- **Client Review and Feedback**: Present initial drafts to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the final high-resolution digital files in multiple formats suitable for both print and digital use.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation and Image Selection
  - Deliverable: List of selected images and themes.
  
- **Milestone 2**: First Draft of Poster Designs
  - Deliverable: Initial poster drafts for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Revisions Based on Client Feedback
  - Deliverable: Revised poster designs reflecting client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Approval and Delivery
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital files of the final posters.
