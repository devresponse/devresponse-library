
# Project Plan Template: Horror-Themed Photo Manipulations

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Horror-Themed Photo Manipulations" project aims to create a series of visually striking and thematically cohesive images that evoke fear and intrigue. The primary objective is to transform ordinary photographs into haunting visuals that captivate and disturb viewers, making them ideal for marketing campaigns, social media, or personal projects. This project will involve the careful selection of base images and the incorporation of various horror elements, such as ghostly figures, dark landscapes, and unsettling textures. Key outcomes include a collection of unique images tailored to the client's specific requirements, ensuring that each manipulation is crafted with meticulous attention to detail. Clients can expect a collaborative process that includes revisions to align the final products with their vision, ultimately delivering a powerful visual experience that resonates with their target audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Image Selection**: Collaborate with the client to select base images that will serve as the foundation for the manipulations.
- **Concept Development**: Brainstorm and develop concepts for each manipulation, focusing on horror themes and elements.
- **Photo Manipulation**: Utilize advanced photo editing techniques to create haunting visuals, incorporating elements such as:
  - Ghostly figures
  - Dark landscapes
  - Unsettling textures
- **Revisions**: Provide a set number of revisions based on client feedback to ensure satisfaction with the final images.
- **Final Delivery**: Deliver high-resolution images in the required formats, along with any necessary usage rights and guidelines.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation and Image Selection
  - Deliverable: List of selected base images
- **Milestone 2**: Concept Development and Approval
  - Deliverable: Concept sketches or mood boards for client approval
- **Milestone 3**: First Draft of Photo Manipulations
  - Deliverable: Initial set of manipulated images for client review
- **Milestone 4**: Revisions and Final Adjustments
  - Deliverable: Revised images based on client feedback
- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery of High-Resolution Images
  - Deliverable: Final images delivered in specified formats
