
# Project Plan Template: Celebrity Mashup Posters

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Celebrity Mashup Posters" project aims to create imaginative and visually striking artwork that combines elements from various celebrity images into unique posters. The primary objective is to craft designs that not only capture the essence of the celebrities involved but also convey a playful and artistic flair. This project will involve selecting high-quality images, applying advanced photo manipulation techniques, and ensuring that the final designs are polished and ready for both print and digital use. Key outcomes include delivering multiple design concepts for client selection, incorporating feedback for revisions, and producing final products suitable for promotional materials, social media campaigns, or personal collections. By the end of this project, we aim to exceed client expectations and provide visually compelling artwork that resonates with audiences.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Image Selection**: Research and curate a collection of high-quality celebrity images that align with the project's artistic vision.
- **Concept Development**: Create initial design concepts that blend the selected celebrity images in innovative ways.
- **Photo Manipulation**: Utilize advanced photo editing software to manipulate images, ensuring a seamless integration of elements.
- **Design Refinement**: Present initial concepts to the client and gather feedback for revisions.
- **Finalization**: Make necessary adjustments based on client feedback and prepare the final designs for print and digital formats.
- **Delivery**: Provide the client with the final posters in various formats suitable for their intended use.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Image Selection
  - Deliverable: Curated list of selected celebrity images.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Initial Concept Development
  - Deliverable: Presentation of 3-5 design concepts for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised designs based on client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized posters ready for delivery in print and digital formats.
