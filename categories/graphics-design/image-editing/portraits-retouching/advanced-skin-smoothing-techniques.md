
# Project Plan Template: Advanced Skin Smoothing Techniques

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Advanced Skin Smoothing Techniques" project is designed to empower portrait retouchers with the expertise required to enhance skin quality in portrait photography while maintaining the natural texture of the subject. The primary objective of this project is to provide participants with a comprehensive understanding of advanced retouching techniques, including frequency separation, dodge and burn methods, and the application of advanced filters. Through detailed step-by-step tutorials, participants will learn how to effectively implement these techniques, supported by before-and-after examples that clearly demonstrate the transformative impact of each method. Additionally, the project will offer downloadable presets compatible with popular editing software such as Adobe Photoshop and Lightroom, facilitating efficient application of the learned techniques. By the conclusion of this project, participants will possess the skills necessary to achieve flawless skin retouching, ensuring that the authenticity of the subject's appearance is preserved. Key outcomes include enhanced retouching skills, increased confidence in applying advanced techniques, and the ability to produce high-quality portrait images that resonate with clients and audiences alike.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Overview**:
