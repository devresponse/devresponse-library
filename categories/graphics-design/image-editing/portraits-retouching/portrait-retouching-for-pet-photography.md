
# Project Plan Template: Portrait Retouching for Pet Photography

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Portrait Retouching for Pet Photography" project aims to deliver high-quality retouched portraits that beautifully capture the bond between pets and their owners. The primary objective is to enhance the visual appeal of each portrait through meticulous retouching techniques, ensuring that the final images reflect the love and connection shared between pets and their human companions. This project will focus on enhancing fur textures to achieve a soft and lifelike appearance, adjusting lighting to highlight unique features, and performing color correction for vibrant, true-to-life images. Additionally, background editing will be conducted to eliminate distractions, creating a cohesive composition that draws the viewer's eye to the subjects. The key outcomes of this project include polished images suitable for personal keepsakes or professional use, ultimately celebrating the special relationship between pets and their owners.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with clients to understand their vision and preferences for the portraits.
- **Image Selection**: Assist clients in selecting the best images for retouching from the initial shoot.
- **Fur Texture Enhancement**: Apply techniques to enhance the texture of the pet's fur, ensuring it appears soft and lifelike.
- **Lighting Adjustments**: Modify lighting to accentuate the unique features of both pets and their owners.
- **Color Correction**: Perform color adjustments to ensure images are vibrant and true to life.
- **Background Editing**: Remove distractions from the background to create a cohesive and focused composition.
- **Final Review**: Present the retouched images to the client for feedback and make any necessary adjustments.
- **Delivery of Final Images**: Provide clients with high-resolution images suitable for printing and digital use.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed

- **Milestone 4**: Final Adjustments Made

  - Deliverable: High-resolution final images delivered in preferred formats.
