
# Project Plan Template: Portrait Retouching for Corporate Headshots

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Portrait Retouching for Corporate Headshots" project aims to deliver high-quality, professionally retouched images that enhance the visual appeal and professionalism of corporate headshots. The primary objective is to meticulously retouch portraits by enhancing skin tones, smoothing blemishes, and correcting uneven lighting, thereby creating a polished and professional look that reflects the subject's best self. This project will also focus on implementing neutral backgrounds that complement the subject, ensuring that the attention remains on the individual. A critical aspect of this project is maintaining consistency across multiple portraits, which will be achieved by establishing standardized lighting and color correction protocols. The key outcomes will include a series of high-resolution images that are suitable for both digital and print use, allowing clients to present themselves effectively in any professional setting. By adhering to these standards, we aim to exceed client expectations and deliver a product that enhances their corporate image.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to understand their vision, preferences, and specific requirements for the headshots.
- **Photography Session**: Conduct a professional photography session, ensuring optimal lighting and background settings.
- **Image Selection**: Collaborate with the client to select the best images for retouching.
- **Retouching Process**:
  - Enhance skin tones for a natural look.
  - Smooth out blemishes and imperfections.
  - Correct uneven lighting and shadows.
  - Implement neutral backgrounds that do not distract from the subject.
- **Consistency Checks**: Ensure that all portraits maintain a cohesive look through standardized lighting and color correction.
- **Final Review**: Present the retouched images to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments.
- **Delivery**: Provide high-resolution images in both digital and print formats.
