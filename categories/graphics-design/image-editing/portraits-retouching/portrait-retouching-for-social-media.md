
# Project Plan Template: Portrait Retouching for Social Media

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Portrait Retouching for Social Media" project aims to enhance portrait images specifically tailored for social media platforms, ensuring that clients' visuals stand out in a competitive digital environment. The primary objective is to deliver high-quality, retouched images that meet the specific requirements of various social media channels, including Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn. Key outcomes of this project include resizing images to optimal dimensions, enhancing color vibrancy, and applying trendy filters that maintain a professional appearance. By focusing on these aspects, the project will help individuals and businesses elevate their online presence, attract attention, and present a polished image to their audience. The final deliverables will be high-resolution images suitable for both web and mobile use, enabling seamless uploads across all social media platforms.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Image Selection**: Clients will provide a selection of portrait images for retouching.
- **Resizing**: Each image will be resized according to the specifications of major social media platforms (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn).
- **Color Enhancement**: Vibrancy and saturation of colors will be adjusted to create eye-catching visuals.
- **Filter Application**: Trendy filters will be applied judiciously to enhance the modern appeal while ensuring a professional look.
- **Final Review**: Clients will have the opportunity to review the retouched images and request adjustments if necessary.
- **Delivery**: Final images will be delivered in high-resolution formats suitable for both web and mobile use.
