
# Project Plan Template: Minimalist Portrait Retouching Techniques

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Minimalist Portrait Retouching Techniques" project aims to equip participants with essential skills in portrait editing that emphasize a minimalist aesthetic. The primary objective is to provide a comprehensive guide that teaches subtle retouching techniques, ensuring that the beauty of portraits is enhanced without compromising their authenticity. Participants will learn to apply skin smoothing techniques that eliminate blemishes while preserving texture, perform eye enhancements to create captivating focal points, and adjust lighting to achieve a more dynamic and engaging look. Utilizing professional software tools, this project will focus on maintaining the natural essence of the subject throughout the retouching process. Key outcomes include the development of refined editing skills, the ability to create stunning portraits suitable for personal and professional use, and an understanding of minimalist principles in portrait retouching. This project is ideal for photographers, graphic designers, and anyone interested in enhancing their portrait editing capabilities.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the "Minimalist Portrait Retouching Techniques" project includes the following key components:

- **Curriculum Development**: Create a structured curriculum that covers essential topics such as skin smoothing, eye enhancement, and lighting adjustments.
- **Software Training**: Provide training on professional software tools (e.g., Adobe Photoshop, Lightroom) that will be used for retouching.
- **Hands-On Workshops**: Conduct interactive workshops where participants can practice techniques on sample portraits.
- **Resource Materials**: Develop and distribute resource materials, including guides, cheat sheets, and video tutorials.
- **Feedback and Assessment**: Implement a system for providing feedback on participants' work and assessing their progress throughout the project.
- **Final Project**: Participants will complete a final project showcasing their retouching skills, which will be reviewed and critiqued.
