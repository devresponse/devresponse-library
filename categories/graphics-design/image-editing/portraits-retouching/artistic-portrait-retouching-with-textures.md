
# Project Plan Template: Artistic Portrait Retouching with Textures

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Artistic Portrait Retouching with Textures" project aims to transform standard portrait photographs into stunning artistic masterpieces by integrating various textures and overlays. The primary objective is to enhance the subject's features while seamlessly blending artistic elements, resulting in visually striking portraits that maintain the integrity of the original image. Key outcomes include the application of techniques such as watercolor overlays, grunge textures, and abstract patterns, which will elevate the aesthetic appeal of the portraits. This service caters to individuals seeking to enhance personal photos and businesses aiming to establish a unique brand identity through compelling visual content. By prioritizing a collaborative approach, we ensure that the client's vision is at the forefront, leading to a final product that not only meets but exceeds expectations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Engage with the client to understand their vision, preferences, and specific requirements for the retouching process.
- **Image Selection**: Assist the client in selecting the best portrait photographs for retouching.
- **Texture and Overlay Selection**: Present a variety of textures and overlays (watercolor, grunge, abstract) for client approval.
- **Retouching Process**:
  - Enhance the subject's features using advanced retouching techniques.
  - Apply selected textures and overlays to the images.
  - Ensure a seamless blend between the original photograph and artistic elements.
- **Review and Feedback**: Present the initial retouched images to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the final retouched images in high-resolution format, suitable for both print and digital use.
- **Post-Project Support**: Offer a follow-up consultation to address any additional needs or adjustments.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Client brief document outlining vision and requirements.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Image Selection Finalized

- **Milestone 4**: Initial Retouched Images Presented
  - Deliverable: First draft of retouched images for client review.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Retouched Images Delivered
  - Deliverable: High-resolution final images in specified formats.
