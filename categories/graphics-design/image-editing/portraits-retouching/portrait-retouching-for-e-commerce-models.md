
# Project Plan Template: Portrait Retouching for E-Commerce Models

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Portrait Retouching for E-Commerce Models" project aims to enhance portrait images of models specifically for e-commerce platforms, ensuring that each photo meets the highest standards of visual appeal and brand consistency. The primary objective is to create visually stunning images that not only attract potential customers but also reflect the brand's identity. Key outcomes include meticulous skin smoothing to achieve a flawless appearance while preserving the model's natural features, and implementing color correction techniques to ensure vibrant and true-to-life skin tones. Additionally, background editing will be performed to eliminate distractions, providing a clean and professional look that emphasizes both the model and the product. The final images will be optimized for web use, ensuring fast loading times without compromising quality. This project is ideal for brands looking to elevate their online presence through high-quality imagery that resonates with their target audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Image Selection**: Collaborate with the client to select the images that require retouching.
- **Skin Retouching**: Apply skin smoothing techniques to create a flawless look while maintaining natural features.
- **Color Correction**: Adjust skin tones to ensure they are vibrant and true to life, aligning with the brand's aesthetic.
- **Background Editing**: Remove any distractions in the background to provide a clean and professional appearance.
- **Final Optimization**: Optimize images for web use, ensuring fast loading times without compromising quality.
- **Client Review**: Present the retouched images to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments.
- **Final Delivery**: Deliver the final images in the required formats and resolutions.
