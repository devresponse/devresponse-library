
# Project Plan Template: Portrait Retouching for Diverse Skin Tones

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Portrait Retouching for Diverse Skin Tones" project aims to enhance the beauty and authenticity of portraits by focusing on the unique characteristics of diverse skin tones. The primary objective is to utilize advanced retouching techniques that ensure accurate color representation while avoiding over-processing. This project will not only enhance the visual appeal of the portraits but also celebrate inclusivity and diversity in photography. Key outcomes include a portfolio of retouched images that highlight the natural beauty of each subject, showcasing a range of skin tones in a harmonious manner. By balancing highlights and shadows, the project will create depth and dimension, ensuring that every feature is presented beautifully. Ultimately, this project serves both personal and professional needs, providing clients with stunning portraits that reflect their individuality.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with clients to understand their vision, preferences, and specific requirements for retouching.
- **Image Selection**: Assist clients in selecting the portraits to be retouched, ensuring a diverse representation of skin tones.
- **Advanced Color Correction**: Implement techniques to accurately represent skin tones, focusing on maintaining natural hues and avoiding over-processing.
- **Balancing Highlights and Shadows**: Adjust lighting to create depth and dimension in the portraits, ensuring a well-rounded presentation of features.
- **Final Review and Revisions**: Present the initial retouched images to clients for feedback and make necessary adjustments based on their input.
- **Delivery of Final Images**: Provide clients with high-resolution digital files of the retouched portraits in their preferred formats.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Client brief and project outline document.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Image Selection Finalized
  - Deliverable: List of selected images for retouching.
  
- **Milestone 3**: First Round of Retouching Completed
  - Deliverable: Initial retouched images for client review.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Client Feedback Received
  - Deliverable: Document summarizing client feedback and requested revisions.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Retouching Completed
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital files of final retouched portraits.
