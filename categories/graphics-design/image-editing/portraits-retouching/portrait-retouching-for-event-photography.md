
# Project Plan Template: Portrait Retouching for Event Photography

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Portrait Retouching for Event Photography" project aims to deliver high-quality retouching services for portraits captured during significant events such as weddings, parties, and conferences. The primary objective is to enhance the overall appearance of the images while preserving the natural essence of the subjects. This project will utilize advanced techniques, including batch editing, to efficiently process multiple images, ensuring consistency across the portfolio. Special attention will be given to enhancing low-light photos, correcting exposure issues, and improving color balance to bring out the vibrancy of the portraits. Additionally, unwanted elements will be meticulously removed to create a polished final product. The key outcomes of this project include delivering stunning portraits that clients can cherish and use for personal or professional purposes, ultimately enhancing their memories of these significant events.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Image Selection**: Collaborate with the client to select the images that require retouching from the event.
- **Batch Editing**: Implement batch editing techniques to streamline the retouching process for multiple images.
- **Enhancement Techniques**: 
  - Adjust exposure and contrast for low-light photos.
  - Improve color balance and vibrancy to enhance the overall appearance.
  - Remove unwanted elements and distractions from the images.
- **Quality Assurance**: Conduct a thorough review of the retouched images to ensure they meet the client's expectations and maintain a natural look.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the client with the final retouched images in the desired format and resolution, along with any necessary usage rights.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation and Image Selection
  - Deliverable: List of selected images for retouching.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Batch Editing
  - Deliverable: First draft of retouched images for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Quality Assurance Review
  - Deliverable: Finalized retouched images after client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: High-resolution images delivered in the agreed format.
