
# Project Plan Template: Portrait Retouching for Fashion Photography

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Portrait Retouching for Fashion Photography" project aims to deliver high-quality retouched portraits specifically designed for the fashion industry. The primary objective is to enhance the aesthetic appeal of portraits to align with the standards of high-fashion visuals. This project will focus on meticulous makeup enhancement, ensuring that every detail is accentuated, alongside skin tone refinement to achieve a flawless and natural appearance. Additionally, we will incorporate dramatic lighting effects to add depth and dimension, creating striking visual impacts that captivate viewers. The final deliverables will consist of a series of polished portraits, ready for editorial use, showcasing the subject in the best possible light. This project is tailored for fashion brands, photographers, or models seeking to elevate their portfolios with stunning, professionally retouched images. Key outcomes include improved visual quality, enhanced brand representation, and increased marketability of the subjects featured.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to understand their vision, preferences, and specific requirements for the retouching project.
- **Image Selection**: Collaborate with the client to select the best images for retouching from the provided raw files.
- **Retouching Techniques**:
  - **Makeup Enhancement**: Apply digital makeup techniques to enhance facial features, ensuring a polished look.
  - **Skin Tone Refinement**: Adjust skin tones to achieve a natural and flawless appearance while maintaining authenticity.
  - **Lighting Effects**: Implement dramatic lighting techniques to create depth and dimension in the portraits.
- **Review and Feedback**: Present the initial retouched images to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments based on their input.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the client with the final high-resolution images in the specified formats, ready for editorial use.
