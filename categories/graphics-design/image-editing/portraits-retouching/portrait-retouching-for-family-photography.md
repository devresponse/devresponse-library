
# Project Plan Template: Portrait Retouching for Family Photography

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Portrait Retouching for Family Photography" project aims to deliver exceptional retouching services tailored specifically for family portraits. The primary objective is to enhance the visual appeal of family images while preserving the natural essence of each individual. This project will focus on harmonizing skin tones across all family members, ensuring a cohesive and authentic look. Distractions in the background will be expertly minimized or removed, allowing the viewer's attention to remain on the subjects. Furthermore, we will enhance group dynamics by adjusting composition and lighting, ensuring that every individual is highlighted appropriately while maintaining the integrity of the family unit. Key outcomes include polished and professional portraits that capture the essence of family togetherness, making them ideal for personal keepsakes or professional use. Clients can expect a seamless experience characterized by clear communication throughout the project, ensuring their vision is realized in the final images.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to understand their vision, preferences, and specific requirements for the retouching process.
- **Image Selection**: Assist the client in selecting the best images for retouching from the family photography session.
- **Skin Tone Harmonization**: Apply techniques to ensure consistent and natural skin tones across all family members.
- **Background Editing**: Remove or minimize distractions in the background to enhance focus on the subjects.
- **Lighting and Composition Adjustment**: Adjust lighting and composition to highlight each individual while maintaining the overall family dynamic.
- **Final Review**: Present the retouched images to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments based on their input.
- **Delivery of Final Images**: Provide the client with high-resolution digital files of the retouched portraits in their preferred format.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and agreed-upon vision document.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Image Selection Finalized
  - Deliverable: List of selected images for retouching.

- **Milestone 3**: First Draft of Retouched Images Delivered
  - Deliverable: Initial set of retouched images for client review.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Adjustments Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised images based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery of High-Resolution Images
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital files provided to the client.
