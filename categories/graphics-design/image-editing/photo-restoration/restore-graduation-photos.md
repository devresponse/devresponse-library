
# Project Plan Template: Restore Graduation Photos

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Restore Graduation Photos" project aims to preserve and enhance the cherished memories associated with graduation milestones through professional photo restoration services. This project will focus on repairing old graduation photographs that have suffered from wear and tear, including issues such as fading, discoloration, scratches, and tears. Our skilled professionals will utilize advanced digital techniques to meticulously analyze each image, restoring them to their original glory and ensuring that the vibrancy and details of these significant moments are revived. Key outcomes of this project include the delivery of high-quality restored images that are suitable for both printing and digital sharing, allowing clients to celebrate their academic achievements. By the end of this project, clients will have beautifully restored photographs that not only reflect their hard work and dedication but also serve as lasting mementos for years to come.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Image Collection**: Gather all graduation photographs from clients, ensuring proper documentation and condition assessment.
- **Initial Assessment**: Analyze each photograph to identify specific restoration needs, including fading, discoloration, scratches, and tears.
- **Restoration Process**:
  - **Digital Scanning**: Scan photographs at high resolution to create digital copies for restoration.
  - **Image Repair**: Utilize advanced software tools to repair damages, enhance colors, and restore details.
  - **Quality Enhancement**: Improve overall image quality, ensuring clarity and vibrancy.
- **Client Review**: Present restored images to clients for feedback and approval.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide clients with high-resolution digital files and printed copies of restored photographs.
- **Post-Project Support**: Offer guidance on how to care for restored photographs to ensure longevity.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Image Collection
  - **Deliverable**: Documented list of all photographs received from clients.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Initial Assessment
  - **Deliverable**: Assessment report detailing the condition and restoration needs of each photograph.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Restoration Process
  - **Deliverable**: Restored digital images ready for client review.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Client Review and Feedback
  - **Deliverable**: Client feedback report and any necessary revisions.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery of Restored Photographs
  - **Deliverable**: High-resolution digital files and printed copies delivered to clients.
