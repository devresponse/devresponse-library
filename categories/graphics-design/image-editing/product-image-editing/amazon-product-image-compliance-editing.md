
# Project Plan Template: Amazon Product Image Compliance Editing

## 1. Executive Summary

The Amazon Product Image Compliance Editing project aims to enhance the visibility and sales potential of products listed on Amazon by ensuring that all product images meet the platform's stringent guidelines. In the competitive landscape of e-commerce, high-quality images are crucial for attracting customers and driving conversions. This project will focus on editing product images to achieve compliance with Amazon's requirements, which include a pure white background, correct dimensions (minimum of 1000 pixels in height or width), and high resolution. Key outcomes of this project include the delivery of polished, distraction-free images that accurately represent the products, thereby improving customer experience and increasing the likelihood of purchase. The editing process will involve background removal, color adjustments, and sharpness enhancements, followed by a thorough review to ensure compliance with Amazon's latest standards. By the end of this project, clients will receive a set of high-quality images ready for immediate upload to their Amazon listings, ultimately leading to improved product visibility and sales performance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Image Assessment**: Review all submitted product images for initial compliance with Amazon's guidelines.
- **Background Removal**: Eliminate any distractions from the background to ensure a pure white backdrop.
- **Color Correction**: Adjust colors to accurately represent the product, ensuring they are true to life.
- **Resolution Enhancement**: Ensure all images meet the minimum resolution requirements of at least 1000 pixels in height or width.
- **Sharpness Adjustment**: Enhance the clarity and sharpness of images to improve visual appeal.
- **Final Review**: Conduct a comprehensive review of all edited images to confirm compliance with Amazon's latest standards.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final set of edited images in the required format for upload to Amazon listings.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Image Assessment
  - Deliverable: Report on initial compliance status of submitted images.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Editing Process
  - Deliverable: Edited images with background removal, color correction, and sharpness adjustments.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Final Review and Compliance Check
  - Deliverable: Compliance report confirming that all images meet Amazon's standards.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: High-quality images ready for upload, along with documentation of compliance.
