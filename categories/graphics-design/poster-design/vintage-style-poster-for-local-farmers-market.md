
# Project Plan: Vintage-Style Poster for Local Farmers Market

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Vintage-Style Poster for Local Farmers Market project is to create a visually appealing and nostalgic poster that effectively promotes the weekly farmers market. This project aims to evoke a sense of community and appreciation for local produce while highlighting the market's offerings. The poster will feature hand-drawn illustrations of fresh fruits and vegetables, emphasizing the organic and local nature of the products available. Retro typography will be employed to enhance the vintage aesthetic, ensuring that essential information such as the market's name, location, and schedule is prominently displayed. A catchy tagline will be included to further emphasize the freshness and locality of the products. The final design will be versatile, suitable for both digital and print formats, allowing for broad promotional use across various channels. Key outcomes include increased awareness of the farmers market, enhanced community engagement, and a visually striking representation of local agriculture that resonates with the target audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather references and inspiration for vintage-style posters, focusing on typography, color schemes, and illustration styles that evoke nostalgia.
- **Concept Development**: Create initial sketches and concepts for the poster layout, including placement of illustrations, text, and overall design elements.
- **Illustration**: Develop hand-drawn illustrations of fresh produce, ensuring they reflect the quality and variety available at the farmers market.
- **Typography Selection**: Choose retro typography that complements the vintage aesthetic and ensures readability of key information.
- **Design Execution**: Combine illustrations and typography into a cohesive poster design, adjusting colors and layouts as necessary.
- **Feedback and Revisions**: Present the initial design to stakeholders for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Finalization**: Prepare the final design for both digital and print formats, ensuring high-resolution files are available for various promotional uses.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final poster design files to the client, along with any necessary usage guidelines.
