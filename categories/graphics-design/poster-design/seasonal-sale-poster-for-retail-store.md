
# Project Plan Template: Seasonal Sale Poster for Retail Store

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Seasonal Sale Poster project is to design a visually striking and effective promotional poster that communicates the details of an upcoming seasonal sale for {{Retail Store Name}}. This project aims to capture the attention of potential customers through vibrant colors and dynamic typography, creating a sense of urgency and excitement around the sale. Key outcomes include a poster that clearly displays the sale dates, significant discounts, and featured products, all while aligning with the store's branding. The final design will be suitable for both print and digital distribution, maximizing reach and impact. Collaboration with the client will be essential to refine the design and ensure that all necessary information is accurately represented. The project will leverage creativity, marketing insight, and technical graphic design skills to produce a poster that not only looks appealing but also drives sales effectively.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: 
  - Analyze the target audience and current market trends.
  - Review existing branding materials of {{Retail Store Name}} to ensure consistency.

- **Concept Development**:
  - Brainstorm and sketch initial design concepts.
  - Present concepts to the client for feedback and approval.

- **Design Execution**:
  - Create a high-resolution digital poster design incorporating:
    - Sale dates
    - Discount percentages
    - Featured products
    - Store branding elements
  - Utilize vibrant colors and dynamic typography to evoke urgency.

- **Client Collaboration**:
  - Schedule regular check-ins with the client to gather feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on client input.

- **Finalization**:
  - Prepare the final design for both print and digital formats.
  - Ensure all files are optimized for various distribution channels.

- **Delivery**:
  - Deliver final files to the client in agreed formats (PDF, JPEG, etc.).
  - Provide a style guide for future reference.
