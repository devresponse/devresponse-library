
# Event Planning Brand Style Guide Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Event Planning Brand Style Guide project is to create a comprehensive and cohesive visual identity for an event planning business that resonates with clients and reflects the celebratory nature of the industry. This project will focus on designing a distinctive logo that embodies festivity and elegance, ensuring it stands out in a competitive market. Key outcomes include the development of vibrant and thematic color palettes that evoke excitement and align with various event themes, as well as the careful selection of typography that conveys sophistication while remaining approachable. The final deliverable will be a detailed guide outlining logo usage, color specifications, typography guidelines, and examples of application across different mediums. This guide will ensure consistency in branding for all marketing materials and platforms, ultimately enhancing brand recognition and client engagement.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Logo Design**: 
  - Research and conceptualization of logo ideas.
  - Creation of multiple logo drafts for client review.
  - Finalization of the logo based on client feedback.

- **Color Palette Development**: 
  - Research on color psychology and its impact on event themes.
  - Creation of vibrant and thematic color palettes suitable for various events (e.g., weddings, corporate gatherings).
  - Presentation of color palettes for client selection.

- **Typography Selection**: 
  - Research and selection of typography that conveys sophistication and approachability.
  - Development of a typography hierarchy for different applications (headings, body text, etc.).
  - Presentation of typography options for client approval.

- **Brand Style Guide Creation**: 
  - Compilation of all design elements into a cohesive style guide.
  - Detailed documentation of logo usage, color specifications, and typography guidelines.
  - Inclusion of examples of application across different mediums (e.g., business cards, social media, event signage).

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Logo Design
  - Deliverable: Final logo files in various formats (AI, PNG, JPG)

- **Milestone 2**: Approval of Color Palettes
  - Deliverable: Selected color palette documentation

- **Milestone 3**: Typography Selection Finalized
  - Deliverable: Typography guidelines document

- **Milestone 4**: Brand Style Guide Completion
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Brand Style Guide PDF
