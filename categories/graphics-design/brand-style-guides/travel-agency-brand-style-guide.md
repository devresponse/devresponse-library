
# Travel Agency Brand Style Guide Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Travel Agency Brand Style Guide project aims to establish a captivating and cohesive visual identity for travel agencies that inspires wanderlust and adventure. The primary objective of this project is to create a comprehensive brand style guide that encapsulates the essence of exploration and travel. This guide will serve as a foundational tool for travel agencies, providing them with the necessary elements to communicate their brand effectively across various platforms. Key outcomes include the design of a unique logo that reflects the spirit of adventure, a curated selection of earthy and vibrant color schemes that resonate with nature, and carefully chosen typography that evokes feelings of excitement. By delivering a complete brand style guide that includes logo specifications, color palette details, typography guidelines, and usage examples, we will ensure consistency across all marketing materials. Ultimately, this project will empower travel agencies to develop a powerful visual identity that attracts their target audience and enhances their brand narrative.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Logo Design**: 
  - Research and conceptualize a unique logo that embodies the spirit of travel and exploration.
  - Create multiple logo variations for client review and feedback.
  - Finalize the logo design based on client input.

- **Color Palette Development**: 
  - Curate a selection of earthy and vibrant color schemes that reflect themes of nature and adventure.
  - Provide color codes (HEX, RGB, CMYK) for each color in the palette.

- **Typography Guidelines**: 
  - Research and select typography that evokes feelings of exploration and excitement.
  - Provide font pairings and usage recommendations.

- **Brand Style Guide Compilation**: 
  - Compile all elements into a cohesive brand style guide document.
  - Include logo specifications, color palette details, typography guidelines, and usage examples.
  - Ensure the guide is visually appealing and easy to navigate.

- **Client Review and Revisions**: 
  - Present the draft brand style guide to the client for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on client input.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Deliver the final brand style guide in both digital and print-ready formats.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Research and Concept Development
  - Deliverable: Concept sketches and mood boards for logo and color palette.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Logo Design Completion
  - Deliverable: Finalized logo design and variations.

- **Milestone 3**: Color Palette and Typography Selection
  - Deliverable: Document detailing selected color palette and typography guidelines.

- **Milestone 4**: Draft Brand Style Guide
  - Deliverable: Draft version of the brand style guide for client review.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Brand Style Guide Delivery
  - Deliverable: Completed brand style guide in digital and print formats.
