
# Project Plan: Pet Care Brand Style Guide

## 1. Executive Summary

The Pet Care Brand Style Guide project aims to establish a unique and inviting visual identity for businesses in the pet care industry. The primary objective is to create a comprehensive guide that encapsulates the essence of warmth and friendliness, resonating with pet owners and caregivers alike. This project will begin with the development of a playful logo that embodies the spirit of pets and their caregivers, setting the tone for the brand's identity. Following the logo creation, we will curate a selection of soft and comforting color schemes that evoke feelings of trust and affection, essential for connecting with our target audience. Additionally, we will choose typography that is both approachable and easy to read, ensuring it appeals to a diverse audience of pet lovers. The final deliverable will be a comprehensive brand style guide that outlines the logo usage, color palette, typography guidelines, and examples of application across various platforms. This guide will serve as an essential tool for maintaining brand consistency and enhancing market presence in the competitive pet care sector, ultimately supporting businesses in building a loyal customer base.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Logo Development**: 
  - Research and brainstorm concepts that reflect the pet care industry.
  - Create initial logo drafts and gather feedback from stakeholders.
  - Finalize the logo design based on feedback.

- **Color Palette Selection**: 
  - Research color psychology related to pets and caregiving.
  - Develop a selection of soft and comforting color schemes.
  - Present color options to stakeholders for approval.

- **Typography Guidelines**: 
  - Identify and select fonts that are approachable and easy to read.
  - Create typography hierarchy for various applications (headlines, body text, etc.).
  - Compile typography guidelines for usage in the brand style guide.

- **Brand Style Guide Compilation**: 
  - Document all elements including logo usage, color palette, and typography.
  - Include examples of application across various platforms (digital, print, merchandise).
  - Design the layout of the brand style guide for clarity and ease of use.
