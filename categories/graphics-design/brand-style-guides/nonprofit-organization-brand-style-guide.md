
# Nonprofit Organization Brand Style Guide Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Nonprofit Organization Brand Style Guide project is to create a comprehensive and cohesive visual identity that effectively communicates the mission and values of the organization. This project will involve the design of a meaningful logo that encapsulates the essence of the nonprofit, ensuring it is both memorable and representative of its cause. Additionally, we will develop a compassionate color palette that evokes feelings of trust, warmth, and advocacy, carefully selecting hues that align with the nonprofit's goals. Typography will be chosen to reflect the organization’s personality, ensuring readability and emotional connection. The final brand style guide will serve as a comprehensive document that outlines logo usage, color specifications, typography guidelines, and additional visual elements. Key outcomes include a visually appealing and consistent brand identity that empowers the nonprofit to communicate its message effectively and build a recognizable brand presence in the community.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Logo Design**: 
  - Research and brainstorm logo concepts that reflect the nonprofit's mission.
  - Create initial sketches and digital drafts for review.
  - Finalize the logo design based on feedback.

- **Color Palette Development**: 
  - Analyze the emotional impact of colors and select a palette that aligns with the nonprofit's goals.
  - Provide color codes (RGB, HEX, CMYK) for digital and print use.

- **Typography Selection**: 
  - Choose fonts that reflect the organization’s personality and ensure readability.
  - Provide guidelines for font usage, including sizes and styles.

- **Brand Style Guide Creation**: 
  - Compile all visual elements into a cohesive document.
  - Include sections on logo usage, color specifications, typography guidelines, and additional visual elements.
  - Provide examples of application across various platforms and materials.
