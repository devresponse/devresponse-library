
# Automotive Brand Style Guide Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Automotive Brand Style Guide project aims to establish a distinctive and cohesive visual identity for automotive companies. The primary objective of this project is to create a comprehensive resource that encapsulates the essence of the brand, focusing on elements that resonate with the automotive industry. This guide will serve as a foundational tool for maintaining brand consistency across various platforms and touchpoints. Key outcomes include a bold logo design that symbolizes strength and innovation, a curated color palette featuring metallic tones that evoke speed and modernity, and carefully selected typography that enhances the brand's personality. The project will commence with an in-depth consultation to understand the client's vision, target audience, and market positioning. Ultimately, the final deliverable will be a detailed style guide that outlines the usage of these elements, ensuring recognition and consistency in the market.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a series of meetings with the client to gather insights on their vision, target audience, and market positioning.
  
- **Logo Design**: Develop a bold and innovative logo that reflects the brand's identity and values. This will include:
  - Concept sketches
  - Digital renderings
  - Client feedback and revisions
  
- **Color Palette Development**: Curate a color palette that includes metallic tones, ensuring it aligns with the brand's identity and evokes a sense of speed and modernity.
  
- **Typography Selection**: Choose typography that complements the logo and color palette, enhancing the overall brand personality.
  
- **Style Guide Creation**: Compile all elements into a comprehensive style guide that includes:
  - Logo usage guidelines
  - Color specifications
  - Typography hierarchy
  - Examples of application across various platforms (print, digital, merchandise)
  
- **Final Review and Revisions**: Present the style guide to the client for final feedback and make necessary adjustments.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Consultation Summary Document
- **Milestone 2**: Logo Design Finalized
  - Deliverable: Final Logo Files (vector and raster formats)
- **Milestone 3**: Color Palette Approved
  - Deliverable: Color Palette Document
- **Milestone 4**: Typography Selected
  - Deliverable: Typography Guidelines Document
- **Milestone 5**: Style Guide Completed
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Automotive Brand Style Guide
