
# NFT Portrait Commissions Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The NFT Portrait Commissions project aims to revolutionize the way personalized digital art is created and owned by leveraging the power of blockchain technology. The primary objective is to provide artists with a platform to transform clients' photos or concepts into stunning NFT portraits that encapsulate their unique vision and style. This project will facilitate a seamless process starting from an initial consultation, where clients can articulate their ideas and preferences, to the final creation of a high-quality digital portrait. Key outcomes include the delivery of personalized art pieces that are not only visually captivating but also securely minted as NFTs, ensuring their authenticity and uniqueness. By combining creativity with technology, NFT Portrait Commissions will cater to modern art enthusiasts and collectors, offering them exclusive digital assets that commemorate special moments or serve as unique gifts.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific elements they wish to incorporate into the portrait.
- **Concept Development**: Collaborate with the client to finalize the concept based on the consultation feedback.
- **Artwork Creation**: The artist will create a high-quality digital portrait based on the agreed-upon concept.
- **NFT Minting**: Once the artwork is completed, it will be minted as a non-fungible token (NFT) on the blockchain, ensuring its authenticity and uniqueness.
- **Delivery**: Provide the client with the digital portrait and the NFT, along with any necessary instructions for accessing and managing their NFT.
- **Post-Delivery Support**: Offer support to clients regarding the use and display of their NFT portraits, including guidance on digital wallets and NFT marketplaces.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Consultation notes and concept outline.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Concept Finalized
  - Deliverable: Finalized concept approval from the client.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Artwork Creation Completed
  - Deliverable: High-quality digital portrait file.
  
- **Milestone 4**: NFT Minting Completed
  - Deliverable: Minted NFT on the blockchain with proof of authenticity.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Digital portrait and NFT access instructions provided to the client.
