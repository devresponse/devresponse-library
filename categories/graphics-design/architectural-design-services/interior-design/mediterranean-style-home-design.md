
# Project Plan Template: Mediterranean Style Home Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Mediterranean Style Home Design project aims to create a stunning residence that embodies the warmth and charm of coastal living. The primary objective is to design a home that reflects the essence of Mediterranean architecture through the use of natural materials such as stone, wood, and terracotta. The project will focus on a harmonious blend of these materials, complemented by a warm, earthy color palette and textured walls that add depth and character to each space. Key outcomes include a layout that prioritizes open spaces for enhanced flow and functionality, ensuring that each area serves its purpose while maintaining aesthetic appeal. Strategic lighting will be incorporated to enhance the ambiance, creating a welcoming environment for relaxation and entertainment. Ultimately, this design will be tailored to the client's lifestyle, resulting in a captivating sanctuary that meets both visual and functional needs.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and lifestyle needs.
- **Site Analysis**: Assess the existing site conditions, including measurements, orientation, and environmental factors.
- **Concept Development**: Create initial design concepts that incorporate Mediterranean elements, including sketches and mood boards.
- **Material Selection**: Choose appropriate materials such as stone, wood, and terracotta, along with color palettes and finishes.
- **Space Planning**: Develop a detailed floor plan that emphasizes open spaces and functional layouts.
- **Lighting Design**: Plan and select lighting fixtures that enhance the ambiance and functionality of each room.
- **Final Design Presentation**: Present the final design to the client for approval, including 3D renderings and detailed specifications.
- **Project Management**: Oversee the implementation of the design, coordinating with contractors and suppliers to ensure quality and adherence to timelines.
- **Final Walkthrough**: Conduct a walkthrough with the client to ensure satisfaction and address any final adjustments.
