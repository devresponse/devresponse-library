
# Project Plan Template: Coastal Beach House Decor

## 1. Executive Summary

The Coastal Beach House Decor project aims to transform a beach house into a serene coastal retreat that embodies the essence of seaside living. The primary objective is to create a harmonious environment that reflects the natural beauty of the beach through the use of light, airy colors such as soft whites, sandy beiges, and ocean blues. Key outcomes include the successful integration of nautical accents, such as decorative items like ropes, shells, and marine-themed artwork, to enhance the coastal vibe. The project will also focus on selecting breezy fabrics for window treatments and upholstery, fostering a relaxed atmosphere ideal for family gatherings and relaxation. A comprehensive consultation will be conducted to understand the client's vision, followed by the development of a detailed design plan that includes mood boards, color palettes, and furniture layouts. Ultimately, the project will ensure that the beach house not only looks stunning but also feels inviting and functional.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a comprehensive meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and budget.
- **Design Concept Development**: Create mood boards that reflect the desired aesthetic, including color palettes and material selections.
- **Space Planning**: Develop furniture layouts that optimize space and functionality for family gatherings.
- **Selection of Furnishings and Decor**: Curate a list of furniture, fabrics, and decorative items that align with the coastal theme.
- **Implementation Plan**: Create a timeline for procurement and installation of selected items.
- **Final Review and Adjustments**: Present the final design to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments before execution.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Client Vision Document
- **Milestone 2**: Submission of Design Concept

- **Milestone 3**: Approval of Space Planning

- **Milestone 5**: Project Implementation

- **Milestone 6**: Final Review
  - Deliverable: Client Satisfaction Survey
