
# Project Plan: Mid-Century Modern Living Room

## 1. Executive Summary

The Mid-Century Modern Living Room project aims to create a stunning and functional living space that embodies the essence of mid-century modern design while seamlessly integrating contemporary elements. The primary objective is to curate a harmonious environment that showcases iconic furniture pieces, such as a sleek sofa, minimalist coffee table, and stylish accent chairs, all chosen for their aesthetic appeal and comfort. The project will strategically utilize bold colors and retro-inspired decor elements, including geometric patterns and vintage artwork, to enhance the vibrancy of the space. Additionally, a thoughtful lighting plan will incorporate a mix of ambient and task lighting, ensuring a warm and inviting atmosphere. Key outcomes include a cohesive design that reflects the mid-century modern style, functionality tailored to the client's lifestyle, and a personalized touch that resonates with their vision. This project will not only transform the living room but also create a space that is both timeless and contemporary.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Design Consultation**: Conduct initial meetings with the client to understand their vision, preferences, and requirements for the living room.
- **Space Planning**: Develop a layout that maximizes functionality while adhering to mid-century modern design principles.
- **Furniture Selection**: Source and select iconic mid-century modern furniture pieces, ensuring comfort and aesthetic appeal.
- **Color Palette Development**: Create a bold color scheme that enhances the vibrancy of the space, including paint selections and fabric choices.
- **Decor Elements**: Curate retro-inspired decor items, such as geometric patterns and vintage artwork, to complement the overall design.
- **Lighting Design**: Plan and specify a mix of ambient and task lighting solutions to create a warm and inviting atmosphere.
- **Implementation Plan**: Develop a detailed timeline for the procurement and installation of all design elements.
- **Final Walkthrough**: Conduct a final review with the client to ensure satisfaction and make any necessary adjustments.
