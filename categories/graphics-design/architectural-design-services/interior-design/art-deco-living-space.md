
# Project Plan Template: Art Deco Living Space

## 1. Executive Summary

The Art Deco Living Space project aims to create a stunning living environment that encapsulates the elegance and opulence of the Art Deco era. Our primary objective is to design a space that not only reflects the bold geometric patterns and rich color palettes characteristic of the 1920s and 1930s but also incorporates luxurious materials such as velvet, marble, and brass. This project will involve a comprehensive consultation process to fully understand the client's vision, followed by the development of detailed design plans and mood boards to visualize the concept. Key outcomes include the selection of statement furniture pieces, intricate lighting fixtures, and decorative art that pay homage to the iconic style of the period. Ultimately, the final design will enhance the aesthetic appeal of the living space while ensuring functionality and comfort, creating a perfect blend of style and practicality that meets the client's needs.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a series of meetings with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and requirements for the living space.
- **Design Concept Development**: Create mood boards and initial design sketches that reflect the Art Deco style, incorporating client feedback.
- **Material Selection**: Identify and source luxurious materials such as velvet, marble, and brass, as well as vintage accents that align with the design concept.
- **Furniture and Fixture Selection**: Curate a selection of statement furniture pieces and intricate lighting fixtures that embody the Art Deco aesthetic.
- **Space Planning**: Develop a detailed floor plan that optimizes the layout for functionality and flow while adhering to the design theme.
- **Final Design Presentation**: Present the complete design plan to the client, including 3D renderings and detailed specifications for all elements.
- **Execution Oversight**: Oversee the implementation of the design, ensuring that all elements are executed according to the approved plans and specifications.
- **Final Walkthrough and Adjustments**: Conduct a final walkthrough with the client to ensure satisfaction and make any necessary adjustments.
