
# Project Plan Template: Architectural Lighting Simulation

## 1. Executive Summary

The Architectural Lighting Simulation project aims to create a comprehensive 3D model that accurately depicts the interaction between natural and artificial lighting within a specified architectural space. The primary objective is to provide a realistic visualization that highlights how light influences both the aesthetics and functionality of the environment. By simulating various lighting conditions and times of day, this project will empower architects and lighting designers to make informed decisions during the design process. Key outcomes include high-quality visual outputs that can be utilized for presentations, client meetings, and iterative design discussions. Ultimately, this project seeks to enhance the design process, ensuring that the final architectural solutions are both visually appealing and functionally effective.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Define project goals and objectives.
  - Identify stakeholders and gather initial requirements.

- **3D Model Development**
  - Create detailed 3D models of the architectural space.
  - Incorporate architectural elements, materials, and textures.

- **Lighting Techniques Application**
  - Research and apply various lighting techniques (e.g., ambient, task, accent).
  - Simulate natural light sources based on geographical location and time of year.

- **Rendering Process**
  - Render high-quality visual outputs for different times of day and lighting conditions.
  - Ensure visualizations accurately represent the interplay of light and space.

- **Review and Feedback**
  - Present initial renderings to stakeholders for feedback.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on stakeholder input.

- **Final Deliverables**
  - Provide a comprehensive set of visualizations for use in presentations and design iterations.
  - Document the lighting simulation process and outcomes for future reference.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Project charter and stakeholder requirements document.
  
- **Milestone 2: Completion of 3D Model**
  - Deliverable: Detailed 3D model of the architectural space.

- **Milestone 3: Application of Lighting Techniques**
  - Deliverable: Documentation of lighting techniques applied and initial renderings.

- **Milestone 4: Final Rendering and Review**
  - Deliverable: High-quality visual outputs and feedback report.

- **Milestone 5: Project Closure**
  - Deliverable: Final project report including all visualizations and documentation.
