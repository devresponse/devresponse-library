
# Mediterranean Villa Mood Board Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Mediterranean Villa Mood Board project aims to create an inspiring visual presentation that encapsulates the essence of Mediterranean architecture and design. The primary objective is to develop a comprehensive mood board that serves as a foundational tool for clients seeking to visualize their villa concept. This project will showcase a harmonious blend of traditional and contemporary elements, featuring a curated selection of design components such as terracotta tiles, arched windows, and a palette of warm, earthy tones. These elements will reflect the inviting atmosphere of Mediterranean living, providing clients with a clear vision of their design direction. Key outcomes include a high-quality digital mood board that encompasses textures, materials, and color swatches, ensuring that all stakeholders are aligned with the project vision. By the end of this project, clients will have a visually compelling reference that guides the design process and fosters collaboration among all parties involved.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Inspiration Gathering**: 
  - Conduct research on Mediterranean architecture and design trends.
  - Collect images, textures, and materials that represent the Mediterranean style.

- **Mood Board Creation**:
  - Curate a selection of design elements, including:
    - Terracotta tiles
    - Arched windows
    - Color palettes featuring warm, earthy tones
  - Incorporate textures and materials that align with the Mediterranean aesthetic.

- **Digital Presentation**:
  - Design a high-quality digital mood board that is visually appealing and easy to navigate.
  - Ensure the mood board is formatted for easy sharing and referencing.

- **Stakeholder Review**:
  - Present the mood board to stakeholders for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on stakeholder input.

- **Final Delivery**:
  - Provide the final digital mood board in multiple formats for accessibility.
  - Include a brief guide on how to use the mood board throughout the design process.
