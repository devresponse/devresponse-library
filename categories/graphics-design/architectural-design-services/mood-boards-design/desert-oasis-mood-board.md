
# Project Plan: Desert Oasis Mood Board

## 1. Executive Summary

The Desert Oasis Mood Board project aims to create a visually captivating presentation that encapsulates the essence of a serene retreat inspired by desert landscapes. The primary objective is to develop a mood board that features a harmonious blend of warm, sandy tones and natural stone elements, reflecting the tranquility and beauty of the desert environment. This project will focus on minimalist architectural concepts that emphasize simplicity and elegance, allowing the natural surroundings to take center stage. Key outcomes include a carefully curated selection of images, textures, and color palettes that will serve as a powerful tool for clients to visualize their architectural vision. The final deliverable will not only inspire but also guide the design process, ensuring that all elements align with the intended aesthetic and functional goals of the project. By the end of this project, clients will have a comprehensive visual reference that encapsulates their design aspirations, facilitating effective communication and decision-making throughout the architectural development process.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Inspiration Gathering**
  - Conduct research on desert landscapes and architectural styles.
  - Collect images, textures, and color palettes that reflect the desired aesthetic.

- **Mood Board Creation**
  - Design a digital mood board that incorporates selected images and textures.
  - Ensure the mood board reflects the warm, sandy tones and natural stone elements.

- **Client Collaboration**
  - Present initial mood board concepts to the client for feedback.
  - Revise the mood board based on client input to ensure alignment with their vision.

- **Finalization and Delivery**
  - Finalize the mood board with all agreed-upon elements.
  - Deliver the completed mood board in both digital and print formats.
