
# Coastal Retreat Inspiration Board Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Coastal Retreat Inspiration Board project aims to encapsulate the serene and refreshing essence of coastal living through a meticulously curated visual representation. The primary objective is to create a mood board that features a harmonious blend of images, textures, and color swatches that embody the tranquility of beachside retreats. By focusing on light and airy color palettes, including soft blues, sandy beiges, and crisp whites, the project seeks to evoke a sense of calm and relaxation. Additionally, the incorporation of nautical decor elements such as driftwood accents, marine-inspired patterns, and natural materials like linen and jute will enhance the overall coastal theme. The key outcomes of this project include a comprehensive visual guide that inspires clients and effectively communicates the desired aesthetic for their architectural design projects. Ultimately, the final deliverable will serve as a pivotal reference point for discussions and decisions throughout the design process, ensuring alignment between client expectations and design execution.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Inspiration Gathering**: 
  - Conduct research on coastal design trends and gather inspiration from various sources, including magazines, websites, and social media platforms.
  
- **Image and Texture Selection**: 
  - Curate a collection of images that reflect the coastal theme, including landscapes, interiors, and decor elements.
  - Select textures that align with the mood board's aesthetic, focusing on natural materials and finishes.

- **Color Palette Development**: 
  - Develop a cohesive color palette that includes soft blues, sandy beiges, and crisp whites.
  - Create color swatches to visually represent the selected colors.

- **Mood Board Creation**: 
  - Design the mood board layout, ensuring a balanced composition of images, textures, and colors.
  - Utilize design software to create a digital version of the mood board.

- **Client Review and Feedback**: 
  - Present the initial mood board to the client for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on client input to ensure alignment with their vision.

- **Final Deliverable**: 
  - Prepare the final version of the Coastal Retreat Inspiration Board, including both digital and print-ready formats.
  - Provide a brief written explanation of the design choices and how they relate to the coastal theme.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 5**: Client Feedback Incorporated
  - Deliverable: Revised mood board based on client feedback.
