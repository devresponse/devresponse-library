
# Art Deco Revival Mood Board Project Plan

## Executive Summary
The Art Deco Revival Mood Board project aims to encapsulate the essence of the Art Deco movement, characterized by its bold geometric patterns, opulent materials, and vibrant color palettes. This project seeks to create a visually striking mood board that serves as a comprehensive guide for clients looking to embrace the glamour and sophistication of this iconic architectural style. The primary objective is to conduct extensive research into Art Deco elements, including color theory, material selection, and pattern design, to curate a collection of images, textures, and color swatches that evoke the luxurious feel of the era. Key outcomes include a polished presentation that not only inspires clients but also effectively communicates the desired aesthetic for their architectural endeavors. By the end of this project, clients will have a clear visual direction that aligns with their design aspirations, ensuring that future projects resonate with the timeless elegance of Art Deco.

## Detailed Scope of Work
1. **Research Phase**
   - Conduct in-depth research on the Art Deco movement, focusing on its historical context, key characteristics, and influential designers.
   - Analyze color theory relevant to the Art Deco palette, identifying key colors and their emotional impacts.
   - Explore material options that reflect the opulence of the Art Deco style, including metals, glass, and textiles.

2. **Mood Board Development**
   - Curate a collection of images that exemplify Art Deco design, including architecture, furniture, and decorative arts.
   - Select textures and materials that align with the identified color palette and design elements.
   - Create a cohesive layout for the mood board that highlights the interplay of colors, textures, and patterns.

3. **Presentation**
   - Design a visually appealing presentation of the mood board, ensuring clarity and impact.
   - Prepare a narrative to accompany the mood board, explaining the choices made and how they reflect the Art Deco aesthetic.
   - Schedule a presentation meeting with the client to showcase the mood board and gather feedback.

## Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1:** Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings on Art Deco elements.
  
- **Milestone 2:** Completion of Mood Board Development
  - Deliverable: Draft version of the mood board for internal review.
  
- **Milestone 3:** Finalization of Mood Board
  - Deliverable: Final mood board ready for client presentation.
  
- **Milestone 4:** Client Presentation
  - Deliverable: Presentation meeting with the client, including the mood board and accompanying narrative.
