
# Urban Green Space Mood Board Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Urban Green Space Mood Board project aims to create a visually compelling presentation that encapsulates innovative ideas for integrating green spaces into urban environments. The primary objective is to curate a selection of images, textures, and color palettes that highlight the beauty and functionality of rooftop gardens, vertical greenery, and sustainable landscaping solutions. This mood board will serve as an inspirational tool for architects, urban planners, and landscape designers, facilitating the visualization and communication of design concepts. Key outcomes include a comprehensive visual representation of current trends in urban greening, an emphasis on sustainability and ecological balance, and a collaborative process that ensures alignment with the client's vision. By the end of this project, stakeholders will have a powerful resource to guide their design decisions and promote greener urban spaces.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Inspiration Gathering**: 
  - Conduct research on current trends in urban green spaces.
  - Collect images, textures, and color palettes that reflect innovative designs.
  
- **Mood Board Development**: 
  - Create a digital mood board layout using design software.
  - Organize selected visuals into categories (e.g., rooftop gardens, vertical greenery, sustainable landscaping).
  
- **Client Collaboration**: 
  - Schedule feedback sessions with the client to review initial drafts.
  - Incorporate client feedback into the mood board design.
  
- **Finalization and Presentation**: 
  - Prepare the final mood board for presentation.
  - Create a brief report explaining the design choices and trends represented.
  
- **Delivery**: 
  - Provide the final mood board in both digital format and print-ready files.
  - Offer a presentation session to discuss the mood board with stakeholders.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Inspiration Gathering
  - Deliverable: A collection of curated images and materials.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Initial Mood Board Draft
  - Deliverable: First draft of the mood board for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback Session
  - Deliverable: Documented feedback and notes for revisions.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Mood Board Presentation
  - Deliverable: Finalized mood board and accompanying report.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Delivery of all project files and a summary report.
