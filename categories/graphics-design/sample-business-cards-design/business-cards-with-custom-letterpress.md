
# Project Plan: Business Cards with Custom Letterpress

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Business Cards with Custom Letterpress" project aims to create exquisite business cards that embody sophistication and professionalism through the traditional letterpress printing technique. The primary objective is to design and produce business cards that not only serve as a means of contact but also reflect the unique identity and values of your brand. The project will commence with a detailed consultation to understand your brand's essence, preferred color schemes, and any specific design elements you wish to incorporate. Following this, we will present several design concepts for your review and approval. Once a design is finalized, we will utilize high-quality materials for the letterpress printing process, ensuring that the final product is both visually appealing and durable. Key outcomes include a distinctive business card that leaves a lasting impression on clients and partners, enhancing your brand's visibility and professionalism in the marketplace.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Schedule an initial meeting with the client to discuss brand identity, preferences, and design elements.
  - Gather information on desired color schemes, typography, and any specific imagery or logos to be included.

- **Design Phase**: 
  - Create multiple design concepts based on the consultation feedback.
  - Present design concepts to the client for review and feedback.
  - Revise designs as necessary until the client approves a final design.

- **Production Phase**: 
  - Select high-quality materials suitable for letterpress printing.
  - Prepare the final design for printing, ensuring all specifications are met.
  - Execute the letterpress printing process, maintaining quality control throughout.

- **Delivery Phase**: 
  - Package the finished business cards professionally.
  - Arrange for timely delivery to the client.
  - Follow up with the client to ensure satisfaction with the final product.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Consultation notes and design brief.

- **Milestone 2**: Design Concepts Presentation
  - Deliverable: Three initial design concepts for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Final Design Approval
  - Deliverable: Approved final design ready for production.

- **Milestone 4**: Production Completion
  - Deliverable: Completed letterpress printed business cards.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Professionally packaged business cards delivered to the client.
