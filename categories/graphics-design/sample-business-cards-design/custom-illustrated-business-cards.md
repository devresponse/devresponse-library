
# Project Plan: Custom Illustrated Business Cards

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Illustrated Business Cards project aims to create bespoke business cards that encapsulate the unique identity and values of {{Client Name}}. Our primary objective is to deliver a product that not only serves as a networking tool but also reflects the essence of the brand. The project will commence with a comprehensive consultation to gather insights into the client's vision, target audience, and brand aesthetics. Following this, our skilled designers will produce multiple unique illustration concepts, allowing for a collaborative selection process. The key outcomes of this project include a finalized business card design that resonates with the brand's personality, high-resolution files for both print and digital applications, and a style guide to ensure brand consistency across all stationery. Ultimately, this project is designed for businesses that aspire to leave a lasting impression in their professional interactions.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct an in-depth consultation with {{Client Name}} to understand their vision, target audience, and brand aesthetics.
  - Gather information on preferred styles, colors, and any existing branding materials.

- **Design Phase**:
  - Develop multiple unique illustration concepts based on the consultation insights.
  - Present the concepts to {{Client Name}} for feedback and selection.

- **Refinement Phase**:
  - Refine the selected design based on {{Client Name}}'s feedback.
  - Ensure all details align with the client's expectations and brand identity.

- **Final Deliverables**:
  - Provide high-resolution files suitable for print and digital use.
  - Create a comprehensive style guide to maintain brand consistency across all stationery.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the Consultation Phase
  - Deliverable: Summary report of the consultation findings.

- **Milestone 2**: Presentation of Initial Design Concepts
  - Deliverable: Multiple unique illustration concepts for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Final Design Selection
  - Deliverable: Selected design for refinement.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approval
  - Deliverable: Approved design ready for production.

- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of Final Files and Style Guide
  - Deliverable: High-resolution files and style guide delivered to {{Client Name}}.
