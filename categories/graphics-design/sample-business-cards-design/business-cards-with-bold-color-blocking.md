
# Project Plan: Business Cards with Bold Color Blocking

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Business Cards with Bold Color Blocking" project aims to create visually striking business cards that leverage bold color blocking techniques to leave a lasting impression on potential clients and partners. The primary objective is to design business cards that not only capture attention but also embody the client's brand identity through a vibrant color palette. By collaborating closely with the client, we will ensure that their vision and preferences are integrated into the design, resulting in a product that reflects their values and professional image. Key outcomes of this project include the delivery of a unique and memorable business card design, provided in multiple formats suitable for both print and digital use. This versatility will enhance the client's professional presence in various networking situations, ultimately contributing to their business growth and brand recognition.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and specific elements they wish to include in the business card design.
- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather inspiration from current design trends, competitor analysis, and the client's industry to inform the design process.
- **Design Development**: Create multiple design concepts that incorporate bold color blocking techniques, ensuring alignment with the client's brand identity.
- **Client Review**: Present the design concepts to the client for feedback and revisions.
- **Final Design**: Refine the selected design based on client feedback and finalize the business card layout.
- **Format Preparation**: Prepare the final design in various formats (e.g., PDF, PNG, JPEG) for both print and digital use.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final business card design files to the client along with guidelines for printing and usage.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and design brief
- **Milestone 2**: Design Concepts Developed
  - Deliverable: Three initial design concepts presented to the client
- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback Received
  - Deliverable: Summary of client feedback and requested revisions
- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approved
  - Deliverable: Finalized business card design
- **Milestone 5**: Design Files Delivered
  - Deliverable: Final design files in multiple formats
