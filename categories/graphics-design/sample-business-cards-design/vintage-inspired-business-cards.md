
# Project Plan: Vintage-Inspired Business Cards

## 1. Executive Summary

The Vintage-Inspired Business Cards project aims to elevate your brand's identity through the creation of unique business cards that embody the charm and elegance of bygone eras. Our objective is to design business cards that not only reflect your brand's personality but also resonate with your target audience, leaving a lasting impression on clients. This project will involve a collaborative design process where we will incorporate carefully selected vintage fonts, textures, and color palettes that align with a retro aesthetic. Key outcomes include a distinctive business card design tailored to your specifications, a polished final product that captures the essence of vintage design, and a seamless feedback loop to ensure your vision is realized. By the end of this project, you will have a set of business cards that stand out in today's competitive market, enhancing your brand's visibility and appeal.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting to understand the client's brand identity, target audience, and specific design preferences.
- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather vintage design elements, fonts, textures, and color palettes that align with the client's vision.
- **Design Concepts**: Create three initial design concepts for the business cards, incorporating feedback from the client.
- **Revisions**: Allow for two rounds of revisions based on client feedback to refine the selected design concept.
- **Final Design Approval**: Present the final design for approval, ensuring it meets all specifications and expectations.
- **Print Preparation**: Prepare the final design files for printing, including specifications for size, paper type, and finish.
- **Delivery**: Coordinate with a printing service to produce the business cards and arrange for delivery to the client.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed

- **Milestone 5**: Final Design Approved
  - Deliverable: Final business card design ready for print.

- **Milestone 6**: Print Preparation Completed
  - Deliverable: Print-ready files delivered to the printing service.

- **Milestone 7**: Delivery of Business Cards
  - Deliverable: Physical business cards delivered to the client.
