
# Project Plan Template: Student and Entry-Level Resume Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the "Student and Entry-Level Resume Design" project is to create professional and visually appealing resumes tailored specifically for students and recent graduates. This project aims to highlight the client's educational achievements, relevant internships, and transferable skills that can attract potential employers. The designer will collaborate closely with the client to gather comprehensive information about their academic background, work experiences, and career aspirations. Key outcomes of the project include a polished resume that adheres to industry standards and best practices, ensuring that it stands out in a competitive job market. The final product will not only be aesthetically pleasing but also strategically structured to emphasize key qualifications and competencies. Additionally, the project will incorporate revisions based on client feedback to guarantee satisfaction with the final outcome, ultimately empowering the client to present themselves effectively to prospective employers.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Client Consultation**: Conduct an initial meeting with the client to discuss their background, career goals, and specific requirements for the resume.
- **Information Gathering**: Collect relevant information from the client, including educational history, work experience, skills, and any additional achievements.
- **Design Concept Development**: Create a design concept that aligns with the client's personal brand and industry standards.
- **Draft Creation**: Develop the first draft of the resume, focusing on layout, formatting, and content that highlights the client's strengths.
- **Client Review**: Present the draft to the client for feedback and suggestions for improvement.
- **Revisions**: Implement revisions based on client feedback to refine the resume.
- **Finalization**: Prepare the final version of the resume, ensuring it is polished and ready for distribution.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final resume in multiple formats (e.g., PDF, Word) as per the client's request.
