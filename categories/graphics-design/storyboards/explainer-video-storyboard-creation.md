
# Project Plan Template: Explainer Video Storyboard Creation

## 1. Executive Summary

The Explainer Video Storyboard Creation project aims to develop a comprehensive storyboard that simplifies and clarifies complex concepts for our clients. The primary objective is to create a visual narrative that effectively communicates key messages to the target audience, ensuring that the storyline flows logically and engagingly. This project will begin with an in-depth consultation to gather insights about the client's goals, audience, and essential messages. Following this, a series of sequential illustrations will be drafted, detailing visual elements, dialogue, and transitions. The key outcomes of this project include a polished storyboard that serves as a blueprint for video production, enhancing visual storytelling and making it easier for viewers to grasp core ideas. By the end of this project, clients will have a clear and compelling storyboard that aligns with their vision, ready for the next stages of video production.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: Conduct an in-depth meeting with the client to understand their objectives, target audience, and key messages.
- **Research and Analysis**: Analyze the information gathered during the consultation to identify themes and concepts that will resonate with the audience.
- **Storyboard Drafting**: Create a series of panels that outline:
  - Visual elements for each scene
  - Dialogue and narration
  - Transitions between scenes
- **Client Review**: Present the initial storyboard draft to the client for feedback and revisions.
- **Revisions**: Incorporate client feedback and make necessary adjustments to the storyboard.
- **Finalization**: Deliver the polished storyboard, ensuring it meets all project requirements and aligns with the client's vision.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the consultation phase
  - **Deliverable**: Consultation summary document
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of research and analysis
  - **Deliverable**: Research findings report
- **Milestone 3**: First draft of the storyboard
  - **Deliverable**: Initial storyboard panels
- **Milestone 4**: Client review and feedback session
  - **Deliverable**: Feedback document
- **Milestone 5**: Final storyboard delivery
  - **Deliverable**: Polished storyboard ready for production
