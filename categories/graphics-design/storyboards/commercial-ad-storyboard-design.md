
# Project Plan Template: Commercial Ad Storyboard Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Commercial Ad Storyboard Design project is to create a comprehensive and visually engaging storyboard that effectively communicates the brand's message to its target audience. This project will involve a detailed exploration of the brand's objectives through initial consultations, ensuring that the creative vision aligns with the marketing goals. The storyboard will consist of sequential illustrations that depict each shot, including detailed descriptions of camera angles, movements, and visual cues. Key outcomes of this project include a polished storyboard that serves as a vital tool for the advertisement's production, enhancing storytelling and visual impact. By providing a clear roadmap for the production team, we aim to facilitate a seamless execution of the creative vision, ultimately leading to a successful advertisement that resonates with viewers and drives brand engagement.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct meetings with the client to gather information about the brand, target audience, and key messages to be conveyed in the advertisement.
  
- **Research and Concept Development**: Analyze competitor advertisements and industry trends to inform the storyboard's creative direction.

- **Storyboard Creation**: Develop a series of sequential illustrations that depict each shot of the advertisement. This will include:
  - Visual representations of each scene
  - Detailed descriptions of camera angles and movements
  - Visual cues and notes for production considerations

- **Draft Review and Revisions**: Present initial drafts of the storyboard to the client for feedback. Incorporate revisions based on client input to refine the storyboard.

- **Final Delivery**: Provide the client with a polished, high-quality storyboard that includes all necessary details for the production team.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary report of brand objectives and key messages

- **Milestone 2**: Concept Development Completed
  - Deliverable: Concept proposal and research findings

- **Milestone 3**: First Draft of Storyboard Completed
  - Deliverable: Initial storyboard draft for client review

- **Milestone 4**: Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised storyboard based on client feedback

- **Milestone 5**: Final Storyboard Delivered
  - Deliverable: Polished storyboard ready for production
