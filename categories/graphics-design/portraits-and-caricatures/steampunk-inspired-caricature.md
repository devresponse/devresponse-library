
# Project Plan Template: Steampunk-Inspired Caricature

## 1. Executive Summary

The Steampunk-Inspired Caricature project aims to create a unique and whimsical piece of art that captures the essence of the subject while incorporating the intricate details characteristic of the steampunk genre. The primary objective is to blend artistry with elements such as gears, goggles, and Victorian-era fashion, resulting in a visually striking caricature that evokes nostalgia and creativity. This project will not only serve as a personal keepsake but also as a thoughtful gift, celebrating individuality in a fun and artistic manner. Key outcomes include the delivery of initial sketches for client feedback, followed by the final artwork in either digital or traditional format. The artist will ensure that the client's vision is fully realized, fostering a collaborative and engaging creative process.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific elements they wish to include in the caricature.
- **Research and Concept Development**: Explore steampunk aesthetics and gather reference materials to inspire the design.
- **Initial Sketches**: Create 2-3 initial sketches based on the consultation, incorporating feedback from the client.
- **Revisions**: Allow for up to two rounds of revisions on the initial sketches to ensure alignment with the client's vision.
- **Final Artwork Creation**: Develop the final caricature, integrating all agreed-upon elements and details.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final artwork in the chosen format (digital file or traditional medium) along with a high-resolution copy for printing.
- **Client Feedback**: Gather final feedback from the client to ensure satisfaction with the completed work.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client preferences and vision.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Initial Sketches Delivered
  - Deliverable: 2-3 initial sketches for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised sketches based on client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Artwork Delivered
  - Deliverable: Completed steampunk-inspired caricature in the agreed format.
