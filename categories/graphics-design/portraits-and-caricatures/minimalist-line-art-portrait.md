
# Project Plan: Minimalist Line Art Portrait

## 1. Executive Summary

The Minimalist Line Art Portrait project aims to create a unique and contemporary representation of the subject through minimalist line art techniques. The primary objective is to deliver a stylish piece of art that enhances the client's personal space or serves as a distinctive gift. This project will involve close collaboration between the artist and the client to ensure that the final artwork captures the essence of the subject while adhering to a clean and modern aesthetic. Key outcomes include a high-resolution digital file of the portrait, which will be suitable for both printing and online use. The process will encompass initial consultations to discuss the client's vision, followed by the creation of sketches and revisions based on feedback. Ultimately, this project is designed to provide a seamless experience from concept to completion, resulting in a stunning piece of art that reflects the client's style and personality.
