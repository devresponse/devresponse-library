
# Project Plan: Digital Oil Painting Portrait

## 1. Executive Summary

The Digital Oil Painting Portrait project aims to create a stunning digital representation of the subject that embodies elegance and artistry. The primary objective is to capture the essence of the individual through advanced digital painting techniques that mimic the rich textures and vibrant colors of traditional oil paintings. This project will not only serve as a beautiful piece of art but also as a personalized keepsake that reflects the client's preferences in color schemes, backgrounds, and specific features. Key outcomes include a high-resolution digital portrait suitable for both digital display and printing, along with a collaborative process that ensures client satisfaction through regular updates and feedback incorporation. Ultimately, this project seeks to provide clients with a unique and sophisticated artwork that commemorates special moments or enhances personal branding.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific features they want to highlight in the portrait.
- **Concept Development**: Create initial sketches and color palette options based on the client's input for approval.
- **Digital Painting Process**: Utilize advanced digital painting techniques to develop the portrait, focusing on texture, color, and detail to replicate the look of an oil painting.
- **Client Feedback**: Provide progress updates at key stages of the painting process and incorporate client feedback to refine the artwork.
- **Finalization**: Complete the digital portrait, ensuring all client specifications are met.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final artwork in high resolution, suitable for both digital use and printing, along with any additional formats requested by the client.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Client brief document outlining preferences and requirements.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Concept Development Completed
  - Deliverable: Approved sketches and color palette options.
  
- **Milestone 3**: First Draft of Digital Painting Completed
  - Deliverable: First draft of the portrait for client review.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised portrait based on client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Artwork Delivered
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital portrait in requested formats.
