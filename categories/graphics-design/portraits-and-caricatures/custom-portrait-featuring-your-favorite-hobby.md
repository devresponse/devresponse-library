
# Project Plan: Custom Portrait with Favorite Hobby

## Executive Summary
The "Custom Portrait with Favorite Hobby" project aims to create a unique artistic representation that celebrates individual passions through personalized portraits. The primary objective is to deliver a custom artwork that not only captures the subject's likeness but also integrates elements of their favorite hobby, whether it be painting, music, gardening, or any other interest. This project will involve close collaboration with clients to understand their vision and preferences, ensuring that the final piece is a true reflection of their personality. Key outcomes include a high-resolution digital portrait that serves as a cherished keepsake or a thoughtful gift. By focusing on artistic detail and client satisfaction, we aim to provide a memorable experience that highlights the intersection of art and personal passion.

## Detailed Scope of Work
1. **Initial Consultation**: 
   - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and favorite hobby.
   - Gather reference images and any specific details the client wishes to include in the portrait.

2. **Concept Development**:
   - Create initial sketches based on the consultation.
   - Present sketches to the client for feedback and adjustments.

3. **Draft Creation**:
   - Develop a detailed draft of the portrait incorporating the client's feedback.
   - Share the draft with the client for review and further modifications.

4. **Final Artwork**:
   - Complete the final portrait based on the approved draft.
   - Ensure the artwork is crafted with attention to detail and artistic flair.

5. **Delivery**:
   - Provide the final artwork in high-resolution digital format.
   - Optionally, discuss printing options for physical copies if desired.

## Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Client brief and reference images collected.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Concept Sketches Presented
  - Deliverable: Initial sketches for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Draft Completed
  - Deliverable: Detailed draft of the portrait for client feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Artwork Delivered
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital portrait provided to the client.
