
# Project Plan Template: Hand-Drawn Family Portrait

## 1. Executive Summary

The Hand-Drawn Family Portrait project aims to create a unique and personalized piece of art that captures the essence of each family member. The primary objective is to provide families with a beautiful, hand-drawn portrait that reflects their individual personalities and collective story. This project will begin with a detailed consultation, allowing families to share their vision and preferences. Key outcomes include a detailed sketch for feedback, followed by the final artwork that incorporates intricate details and vibrant colors. The final product will be delivered in high-resolution digital format, making it easy for families to print and display their cherished keepsake. Ultimately, this project not only serves as a stunning piece of art but also as a celebration of family bonds and memories.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation**: Schedule a meeting with the family to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific details they want included in the portrait.
- **Information Gathering**: Collect photographs and any other reference materials that will help the artist understand the family's dynamics and individual characteristics.
- **Sketch Creation**: The artist will create a detailed sketch based on the consultation and reference materials.
- **Feedback Session**: Present the sketch to the family for feedback and make necessary adjustments based on their input.
- **Final Artwork**: Once the sketch is approved, the artist will finalize the portrait, adding intricate details and vibrant colors.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final artwork in high-resolution digital format, ensuring it is suitable for printing and display.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Consultation notes and agreed vision for the portrait.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Sketch Creation
  - Deliverable: Detailed sketch of the family portrait for review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Feedback Session
  - Deliverable: Revised sketch based on family feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Artwork Completion
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital portrait delivered to the family.
