
# Project Plan Template: Celebrity Look-Alike Portrait

## 1. Executive Summary

The Celebrity Look-Alike Portrait project aims to provide clients with a unique artistic experience that merges their personal features with those of their favorite celebrities. The primary objective is to create a captivating digital portrait that not only reflects the client's individuality but also embodies the iconic traits of a renowned figure. This project overview highlights the collaborative nature of the process, where skilled artists engage with clients to understand their likeness and the celebrity they wish to emulate. Key outcomes include a high-quality digital portrait suitable for personal enjoyment, social media profiles, or as a memorable gift. By valuing client input throughout the creative process, we ensure that the final artwork resonates with the client’s vision while celebrating the allure of celebrity culture. This project is ideal for fans, gift-givers, or anyone seeking to add a touch of glamour to their life.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferred celebrity, and specific features to be highlighted in the portrait.
- **Reference Gathering**: Collect reference images of the client and the chosen celebrity to guide the artistic process.
- **Sketch Development**: Create preliminary sketches based on the gathered references and client feedback.
- **Client Review**: Present the sketches to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments.
- **Final Artwork Creation**: Develop the final digital portrait, incorporating all agreed-upon elements and adjustments.
- **Final Review**: Share the completed portrait with the client for final approval.
- **Delivery**: Provide the client with the high-resolution digital file of the portrait in various formats suitable for printing and online use.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completion
  - **Deliverable**: Documented client vision and preferences.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Reference Images Collection
  - **Deliverable**: Compiled reference images for the client and celebrity.

- **Milestone 3**: Preliminary Sketches Completion
  - **Deliverable**: Set of initial sketches for client review.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Artwork Completion
  - **Deliverable**: High-quality digital portrait ready for delivery.

- **Milestone 5**: Project Closure
  - **Deliverable**: Final approval from the client and delivery of digital files.
