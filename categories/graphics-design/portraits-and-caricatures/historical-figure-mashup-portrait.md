
# Project Plan Template: Historical Figure Mashup Portrait

## 1. Executive Summary

The Historical Figure Mashup Portrait project aims to create a unique and personalized artistic representation that merges the client's likeness with that of a renowned historical figure. This project offers an innovative way to celebrate personal identity while paying homage to iconic personalities from history, such as queens, kings, philosophers, and other notable figures. The primary objective is to deliver a high-quality digital portrait that captures the essence of both the client and the selected historical character. Key outcomes include a collaborative artistic process involving initial sketches, revisions, and a final polished piece that reflects the client's vision and the historical context of the chosen figure. This project not only serves as a personal keepsake but also as a thoughtful gift, making it a versatile offering for various occasions. By the end of the project, clients will receive a striking visual representation that embodies their individuality and connects them to the grandeur of history.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferred historical figure, and any specific features they wish to highlight.
- **Research and Selection**: Gather reference materials related to the chosen historical figure, including attire, characteristics, and historical context.
- **Sketch Development**: Create initial sketches that blend the client's likeness with the selected historical figure's attributes.
- **Client Review**: Present the sketches to the client for feedback and suggestions for revisions.
- **Revisions**: Make necessary adjustments based on client feedback to ensure satisfaction with the direction of the portrait.
- **Final Artwork Creation**: Develop the final digital portrait, incorporating all agreed-upon elements and details.
- **Final Review**: Present the completed portrait to the client for final approval.
- **Delivery**: Provide the client with the high-quality digital file of the portrait in various formats suitable for personal use and sharing.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client preferences and selected historical figure.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Research and Reference Materials Compiled
  - Deliverable: Collection of reference images and notes on the historical figure.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Initial Sketches Presented
  - Deliverable: Set of initial sketches for client review.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised sketches based on client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Digital Portrait Delivered
  - Deliverable: High-quality digital portrait file in multiple formats.
