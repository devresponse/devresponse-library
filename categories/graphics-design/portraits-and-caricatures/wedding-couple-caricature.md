
# Project Plan: Wedding Couple Caricature

## 1. Executive Summary

The Wedding Couple Caricature project aims to create a unique and playful artistic representation of the couple on their special day. This project will capture the joy and essence of the couple's relationship, providing a delightful keepsake that can be used for wedding invitations, thank-you cards, or as a cherished home display. The primary objective is to deliver a caricature that reflects the personalities and features of the couple, ensuring it resonates with their love story. Key outcomes include a finalized caricature that meets the couple's expectations, a collaborative process with skilled artists, and a memorable piece of art that serves as a conversation starter for years to come. By focusing on creativity and detail, we aim to provide a product that not only celebrates the wedding day but also becomes a treasured memento for the couple.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the couple to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific elements they want included in the caricature.
- **Character Sketches**: Create preliminary sketches based on the consultation to capture the couple's likeness and personality traits.
- **Feedback Loop**: Present the sketches to the couple for feedback and make necessary adjustments based on their input.
- **Final Artwork Creation**: Develop the final caricature artwork, incorporating all agreed-upon details and enhancements.
- **Digital and Print Options**: Provide the couple with both digital copies and print-ready files of the caricature.
- **Delivery**: Ensure timely delivery of the final product, along with any additional items such as prints or thank-you card designs if requested.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - **Deliverable**: Meeting notes and agreed-upon vision for the caricature.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Character Sketches Completed
  - **Deliverable**: Preliminary sketches for couple's review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Feedback Received and Adjustments Made
  - **Deliverable**: Revised sketches based on couple's feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Artwork Completed
  - **Deliverable**: Final caricature artwork in digital and print formats.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of Final Product
  - **Deliverable**: Completed caricature delivered to the couple.
