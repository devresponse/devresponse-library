
# Project Plan Template: Celebrity-Inspired Caricature

## 1. Executive Summary

The Celebrity-Inspired Caricature project aims to provide clients with a unique artistic experience that combines their likeness with the playful essence of their favorite celebrity. The primary objective is to create a one-of-a-kind caricature that not only captures the client's facial features but also embodies the distinctive style and persona of the chosen celebrity. This project will facilitate a collaborative process, allowing clients to actively participate in the artistic journey by providing input on specific features and stylistic elements they wish to emphasize. Key outcomes include high-resolution digital artwork that can be shared on social media or printed, ensuring that clients receive a memorable piece that serves as a personalized gift, a fun profile picture, or simply a delightful indulgence in creativity. Ultimately, this project seeks to blend artistry with personal expression, resulting in a caricature that resonates with both the client and the celebrity inspiration.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Client Consultation**: Initial meeting to discuss the client's vision, preferred celebrity, and any specific features to highlight.
- **Celebrity Selection**: Assist the client in selecting a celebrity that resonates with their personality and style.
- **Sketch Development**: Create preliminary sketches based on the client's likeness and chosen celebrity style for client review.
- **Feedback Loop**: Incorporate client feedback on sketches to refine the caricature.
- **Final Artwork Creation**: Produce the final caricature in a digital format, ensuring high-resolution quality.
- **Delivery**: Provide the completed caricature to the client via email or a secure download link.
- **Revisions**: Offer one round of minor revisions based on client feedback after the final delivery.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Client Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client preferences and celebrity selection.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Preliminary Sketches Delivered
  - Deliverable: Initial sketches for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Final Sketch Approval
  - Deliverable: Client-approved sketch ready for final artwork creation.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Caricature Delivered
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital caricature sent to the client.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Feedback and Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised caricature (if applicable) based on client feedback.
