
# Project Plan: Retro Pop Art Caricature

## 1. Executive Summary

The Retro Pop Art Caricature project aims to create a vibrant and playful digital illustration that captures the essence of the subject while embodying the energetic spirit of the retro pop art movement. This project will leverage bold colors, dynamic shapes, and iconic pop art elements to produce a unique piece of art that stands out as a statement work. The primary objective is to deliver a high-quality caricature that not only resembles the subject but also resonates with the fun and lively characteristics of retro pop art. Key outcomes include the delivery of initial sketches for client feedback, followed by the final artwork, ensuring that the client's vision is fully realized. Clients can utilize the final piece for various purposes, such as social media profiles, gifts, or personal keepsakes, making this project a versatile addition to their artistic collection.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific elements they want to include in the caricature.
- **Research & Inspiration**: Gather references and inspiration from retro pop art styles and techniques to inform the design process.
- **Sketch Development**: Create initial sketches based on the client's input and feedback, focusing on capturing the likeness and personality of the subject.
- **Client Feedback**: Present the sketches to the client for review and gather feedback for revisions.
- **Final Artwork Creation**: Develop the final digital illustration, incorporating the client's feedback and ensuring the use of bold colors and dynamic shapes.
- **Final Review**: Share the completed caricature with the client for final approval.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final high-resolution digital file in multiple formats suitable for various uses (e.g., social media, print).
- **Post-Project Follow-Up**: Check in with the client after delivery to ensure satisfaction and gather any testimonials or feedback.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and agreed-upon vision for the caricature.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Initial Sketches Developed
  - Deliverable: Set of initial sketches for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback Received
  - Deliverable: Documented feedback and requested revisions.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Artwork Completed
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital caricature in multiple formats.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Follow-up communication and feedback collection.
