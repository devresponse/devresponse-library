
# Project Plan: Holiday-Themed Family Caricature

## 1. Executive Summary

The Holiday-Themed Family Caricature project aims to create a unique and personalized piece of art that encapsulates the joy and warmth of the holiday season. Our objective is to deliver a custom caricature that not only celebrates family bonds but also serves as a delightful keepsake for holiday cards, gifts, or decorations. This project will involve close collaboration with clients to ensure that each caricature reflects the distinct personalities of family members while incorporating festive elements such as Santa hats, snowflakes, and a beautifully designed Christmas tree background. Key outcomes include a high-resolution digital file of the caricature, which will be suitable for both printing and online sharing. Ultimately, this project seeks to bring smiles and holiday cheer to families, creating a memorable artistic representation that they can cherish for years to come.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific elements they would like to include in the caricature.
  
- **Concept Development**: Create initial sketches based on the consultation, incorporating feedback from the client to refine the design.
  
- **Final Artwork Creation**: Develop the final caricature artwork, ensuring that it captures the essence of each family member and includes festive elements.
  
- **Client Review**: Present the final artwork to the client for review and make any necessary adjustments based on their feedback.
  
- **Delivery of Digital File**: Provide the client with a high-resolution digital file of the completed caricature, formatted for both print and online use.
  
- **Post-Delivery Support**: Offer assistance with any questions regarding printing or sharing the caricature online.
