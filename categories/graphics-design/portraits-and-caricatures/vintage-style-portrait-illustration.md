
# Project Plan Template: Vintage-Style Portrait Illustration

## 1. Executive Summary

The Vintage-Style Portrait Illustration project aims to create a unique and timeless piece of art that captures the essence of the subject while reflecting the artistic flair of classic 19th or early 20th-century illustrations. The primary objective is to deliver a high-resolution portrait that embodies nostalgia and charm, making it a cherished keepsake or a distinctive gift. The project will involve close collaboration with the client to ensure their vision and preferences are fully understood and incorporated into the final artwork. Key outcomes include initial sketches, iterative revisions based on client feedback, and the final rendering that meets the highest standards of quality for both digital and print formats. This project not only seeks to fulfill the client's artistic desires but also aims to create a lasting piece that resonates with viewers, evoking memories and emotions tied to the vintage aesthetic.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific details they wish to incorporate into the portrait.
- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather references and inspiration from classic 19th and early 20th-century illustrations to inform the artistic style.
- **Initial Sketches**: Create preliminary sketches based on the consultation and research, presenting them to the client for feedback.
- **Revisions**: Incorporate client feedback into the sketches, making necessary adjustments to align with their vision.
- **Final Rendering**: Produce the final high-resolution portrait illustration, ensuring it captures the desired vintage aesthetic.
- **Client Review**: Present the final artwork to the client for approval, allowing for any last-minute adjustments.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final artwork in both digital format and print-ready format, ensuring it meets the client's specifications.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Summary of client preferences and vision.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Research and Inspiration
  - Deliverable: Mood board or reference images for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Initial Sketches
  - Deliverable: Three preliminary sketches for client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised sketches based on client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Rendering
  - Deliverable: High-resolution final portrait illustration.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Client Review
  - Deliverable: Final artwork presented for approval.
  
- **Milestone 7**: Delivery
  - Deliverable: Final artwork delivered in digital and print-ready formats.
