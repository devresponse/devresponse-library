
# Project Plan Template: Personalized Graduation Caricature

## 1. Executive Summary

The Personalized Graduation Caricature project aims to create a unique and memorable piece of art that celebrates the achievements of graduates. This project will provide a one-of-a-kind caricature that captures the essence of the graduate in their cap and gown, proudly holding their diploma. Our talented artists will incorporate personalized elements that reflect the graduate's journey, interests, and accomplishments, ensuring that the final product is not just a likeness but a cherished keepsake. Key outcomes of this project include delivering high-resolution digital artwork that can be printed or shared online, enhancing the celebratory experience for graduates and their families. This caricature serves as a perfect gift for graduation parties, a surprise for proud parents, or a memorable addition to graduation scrapbooks, making it a significant milestone in the graduate's life. 

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Engage with the client to gather information about the graduate, including their interests, achievements, and any specific elements they would like included in the caricature.
- **Concept Development**: Create initial sketches based on the information gathered during the consultation. Present these concepts to the client for feedback and approval.
- **Artwork Creation**: Develop the final caricature artwork, incorporating all agreed-upon elements. This includes the graduate's likeness, attire, diploma, and any additional personalized features.
- **Revisions**: Allow for up to two rounds of revisions based on client feedback to ensure satisfaction with the final product.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the completed caricature in high-resolution digital format, suitable for printing and online sharing. Include instructions for printing and usage rights.
- **Follow-Up**: Conduct a follow-up with the client to ensure satisfaction and gather any testimonials or feedback for future projects.
