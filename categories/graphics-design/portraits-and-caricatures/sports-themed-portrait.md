
# Project Plan Template: Sports-Themed Portrait

## 1. Executive Summary

The Sports-Themed Portrait project aims to create a vibrant and dynamic representation of the subject engaged in their favorite sport. This project is designed for sports enthusiasts who wish to celebrate their passion through personalized art. The primary objective is to deliver a high-quality portrait that not only captures the likeness of the subject but also conveys the energy and excitement inherent in the sport. The artist will collaborate closely with the client to understand their specific preferences, including the sport, action pose, and any personal elements to be incorporated into the background. Key outcomes include a visually stunning artwork that serves as a cherished keepsake, a seamless collaborative process, and a final piece that aligns with the client's vision. By utilizing high-quality materials and techniques, the project will ensure that the final product is both aesthetically pleasing and meaningful to the client.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Meet with the client to discuss their vision, preferred sport, and any specific elements they wish to include.
  
- **Concept Development**: 
  - Create initial sketches based on the client's input and feedback.
  
- **Refinement**: 
  - Present the sketches to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments.
  
- **Final Artwork Creation**: 
  - Utilize high-quality materials to create the final portrait, ensuring attention to detail and vibrant colors.
  
- **Client Review**: 
  - Present the completed portrait to the client for final approval.
  
- **Delivery**: 
  - Package and deliver the final artwork to the client, ensuring it is ready for display.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client preferences and agreed-upon concepts.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Concept Sketches Developed
  - Deliverable: Initial sketches presented to the client for feedback.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Final Sketch Approval
  - Deliverable: Approved final sketch ready for artwork creation.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Artwork Completed
  - Deliverable: Completed portrait ready for client review.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Artwork Delivered
  - Deliverable: Final artwork delivered to the client.
