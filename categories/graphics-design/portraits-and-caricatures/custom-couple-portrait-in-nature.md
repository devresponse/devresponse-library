
# Project Plan Template: Custom Couple Portrait in Nature

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Custom Couple Portrait in Nature" project aims to create a stunning and personalized portrait that encapsulates the love and connection between a couple, set against a breathtaking natural backdrop. The primary objective is to deliver a high-quality digital illustration that resonates with the couple's unique story and preferences. This project will involve a detailed consultation process to ensure that the clients' vision is fully understood and incorporated into the artwork. Key outcomes include a beautifully crafted portrait that reflects the couple's personality, a seamless collaboration between the artist and clients, and a final product that serves as a cherished keepsake. By focusing on the couple's shared experiences and preferred natural settings—be it a serene forest, picturesque beach, or majestic mountain scene—the project will not only fulfill the clients' expectations but also create a lasting memory that they can treasure for years to come.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the clients to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific elements they wish to include in the portrait.
  - Gather information about the couple's relationship, favorite locations, and any significant symbols or themes they would like to incorporate.

- **Concept Development**:
  - Create initial sketches based on the consultation feedback.
  - Present the sketches to the clients for review and gather their input.

- **Background Selection**:
  - Discuss and finalize the natural backdrop (forest, beach, mountain) that best represents the couple's personality and shared experiences.

- **Draft Creation**:
  - Develop a detailed draft of the portrait, incorporating the agreed-upon elements and background.
  - Share the draft with the clients for feedback and make necessary adjustments.

- **Final Artwork**:
  - Produce the final high-quality digital illustration, ensuring all details are refined and polished.
  - Provide the final artwork in multiple formats suitable for printing and online sharing.

- **Delivery**:
  - Deliver the final digital files to the clients via email or a secure file-sharing platform.
  - Offer a follow-up consultation to discuss any additional requests or modifications.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and a summary of the couple's vision.

- **Milestone 2**: Concept Development Completed
  - Deliverable: Initial sketches presented to the clients.

- **Milestone 3**: Background Selection Finalized
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of the chosen natural backdrop.

- **Milestone 4**: Draft Creation Completed
  - Deliverable: Detailed draft of the portrait shared with the clients.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Artwork Delivered
  - Deliverable: High-quality digital illustration in multiple formats.
