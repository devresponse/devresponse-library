
# Project Plan: Custom Wedding Invitation Prompts

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Custom Wedding Invitation Prompts" project aims to develop tailored AI prompts that generate exquisite wedding invitation designs, reflecting the unique style and theme of each couple. The primary objective is to create visually stunning invitations that capture the essence of the couple's personal story, ensuring their special day is memorable and distinctive. The project will commence with an in-depth consultation to gather insights into the couple's vision, preferences, and specific elements they wish to incorporate. Following this, a series of carefully crafted AI prompts will be developed to inspire the creation of beautiful visuals. The final deliverables will include high-resolution designs suitable for both digital and print formats, allowing couples to share their invitations in a way that resonates with their individuality. Key outcomes include enhanced customer satisfaction, unique invitation designs, and a streamlined process for generating custom wedding invitations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct an initial meeting with the couple to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific elements they wish to include in their wedding invitations.
  - Gather information on the wedding theme, color palette, and any personal stories or symbols that should be incorporated.

- **Prompt Development**:
  - Analyze the information gathered during the consultation to create a series of tailored AI prompts.
  - Ensure prompts are designed to inspire a variety of styles and formats, catering to different tastes and preferences.

- **Design Creation**:
  - Utilize the AI prompts to generate multiple invitation design options.
  - Review and refine designs based on feedback from the couple.

- **Finalization and Delivery**:
  - Prepare the final designs in high-resolution formats suitable for both digital and print use.
  - Deliver the completed invitations to the couple, ensuring they meet all specified requirements.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive notes and a summary of the couple's vision and preferences.

- **Milestone 2**: AI Prompt Development
  - Deliverable: A set of tailored AI prompts ready for design generation.

- **Milestone 3**: Design Generation
  - Deliverable: Multiple invitation design options based on the AI prompts.

- **Milestone 4**: Review and Feedback
  - Deliverable: Revised designs based on couple's feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Design Delivery
  - Deliverable: High-resolution wedding invitation designs in digital and print formats.
