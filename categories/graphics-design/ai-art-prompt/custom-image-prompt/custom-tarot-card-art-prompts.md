
# Project Plan: Custom Tarot Card Art Prompts

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Tarot Card Art Prompts project aims to harness the power of AI technology to create unique and artistic tarot card designs tailored to the client's specific themes and preferences. The primary objective is to develop a series of custom AI prompts that will generate visually stunning graphics, ensuring that each tarot card design resonates with the client's vision and beliefs. This project will begin with an initial consultation to gather detailed requirements, allowing for a deep understanding of the client's artistic direction. Key outcomes include a comprehensive guide on effectively utilizing the AI prompts, alongside a collection of generated tarot card designs that reflect individual aesthetics. By merging art and technology, this project not only delivers personalized tarot cards but also opens up new avenues for creative expression, making it an ideal choice for those seeking to explore the mystical world of tarot through a modern lens.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, themes, symbols, and preferences for the tarot card designs.
  - Document all requirements and expectations to ensure alignment throughout the project.

- **Development of AI Prompts**: 
  - Create a series of custom AI prompts based on the gathered requirements.
  - Test and refine the prompts to ensure they produce high-quality and relevant tarot card designs.

- **Design Generation**: 
  - Utilize the developed AI prompts to generate a diverse range of tarot card designs.
  - Review the generated designs with the client to gather feedback and make necessary adjustments.

- **Final Deliverables**: 
  - Provide the client with a comprehensive guide on how to use the AI prompts effectively.
  - Deliver the final set of unique tarot card designs in high-resolution formats suitable for printing and digital use.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completion
  - Deliverable: Documented requirements and expectations from the client.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Development of AI Prompts
  - Deliverable: A set of tested and refined AI prompts ready for design generation.

- **Milestone 3**: Generation of Tarot Card Designs
  - Deliverable: A preliminary collection of tarot card designs for client review.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Review and Adjustments
  - Deliverable: Revised tarot card designs based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Project Completion
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive guide on using the prompts and final high-resolution tarot card designs.
