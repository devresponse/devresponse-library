
# Project Plan: Art Deco Logo Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Art Deco Logo Design project aims to create a distinctive logo that encapsulates the elegance and sophistication of the Art Deco movement. The primary objective is to develop a visual identity that not only reflects the opulence of the era but also resonates with modern audiences. This project will begin with an in-depth research phase to explore the key elements of Art Deco design, including bold geometric shapes, rich colors, and luxurious details. Following the research, brainstorming sessions will be conducted to generate innovative concepts that align with the client's vision. The selected design will be meticulously refined and executed using professional graphic design software, ensuring high-quality output. Key outcomes include the delivery of a versatile logo in various formats suitable for both digital and print applications, empowering businesses to establish a strong visual identity that stands out in a competitive market.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct a comprehensive analysis of the Art Deco movement, focusing on its historical context, design principles, and notable examples.
  - Gather inspiration from existing Art Deco logos and designs to inform the creative process.

- **Concept Development**:
  - Organize brainstorming sessions with the design team to generate a variety of logo concepts.
  - Create initial sketches and digital drafts based on the brainstormed ideas.

- **Design Refinement**:
  - Select the most promising concepts for further development.
  - Refine the chosen design, focusing on color palettes, typography, and geometric shapes that embody the Art Deco style.

- **Final Execution**:
  - Utilize professional graphic design software to create the final logo.
  - Ensure the logo is adaptable for various applications, including digital platforms and print materials.

- **Delivery**:
  - Provide the final logo in multiple formats (e.g., PNG, JPEG, SVG, EPS) to accommodate different use cases.
  - Include a style guide that outlines logo usage, color specifications, and typography recommendations.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing key findings and inspirations.

- **Milestone 2**: Concept Development Completion
  - Deliverable: Presentation of initial logo concepts for client feedback.

- **Milestone 3**: Design Refinement Completion
  - Deliverable: Refined logo design options for final selection.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Logo Execution
  - Deliverable: Final logo files in various formats and a comprehensive style guide.
