
# Cultural Heritage Logo Design Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Cultural Heritage Logo Design project aims to create a distinctive logo that embodies the essence of cultural heritage and traditions. The primary objective of this project is to develop a logo that resonates with the intended audience while accurately representing the unique cultural elements specified by the client. This will involve comprehensive research into the cultural symbols, motifs, and color palettes that reflect the client's heritage. Key outcomes include the delivery of initial concept sketches, digital drafts, and a final high-resolution logo suitable for both print and digital applications. Additionally, a style guide will be provided to ensure consistent usage of the logo across various platforms. By the end of this project, the client will have a professional and visually compelling logo that celebrates and promotes their cultural identity, enhancing their brand recognition and connection with their audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct in-depth research on the specific cultural elements that the client wishes to represent.
  - Identify relevant symbols, motifs, and color palettes that reflect the unique aspects of the culture.

- **Concept Development**: 
  - Create initial concept sketches based on research findings.
  - Present sketches to the client for feedback and discussion.

- **Digital Drafting**: 
  - Develop digital drafts of the selected concept.
  - Incorporate client feedback to refine the design.

- **Revisions**: 
  - Allow for a specified number of revisions based on client input.
  - Finalize the logo design after all revisions are completed.

- **Final Deliverables**: 
  - Provide a high-resolution logo in various formats (e.g., PNG, JPEG, SVG).
  - Create a comprehensive style guide outlining the logo's usage, including color codes, typography, and application examples.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report detailing cultural elements and design inspirations.

- **Milestone 2**: Initial Concept Sketches
  - Deliverable: Presentation of initial sketches to the client.

- **Milestone 3**: Digital Drafts
  - Deliverable: Digital drafts of the logo for client review.

- **Milestone 4**: Revisions and Finalization
  - Deliverable: Final logo design after revisions.

- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of Final Assets
  - Deliverable: High-resolution logo files and style guide.
