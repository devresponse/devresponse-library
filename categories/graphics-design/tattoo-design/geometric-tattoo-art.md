
# Project Plan Template: Geometric Tattoo Art

## 1. Executive Summary

The Geometric Tattoo Art project aims to create a unique and visually striking tattoo design that embodies modern aesthetics through the use of symmetry, intricate patterns, and abstract shapes. The primary objective is to collaborate closely with the client to develop a design that not only reflects their personal style but also serves as a standout piece of art on their skin. The project will commence with an initial consultation to gather insights into the client's ideas, preferences, and any specific elements they wish to incorporate. Following this, the artist will produce several design drafts, allowing for client feedback and revisions to ensure the final design aligns perfectly with the client's vision. Key outcomes of this project include a high-resolution digital file of the finalized tattoo design, which will be ready for application. This project presents an exciting opportunity for both the artist and the client, requiring a keen eye for detail and a strong understanding of geometric art principles.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss their ideas, preferences, and any specific elements they wish to include in the tattoo design.
  - Document the client's vision and requirements for the design.

- **Research and Inspiration**: 
  - Conduct research on geometric tattoo designs and gather inspiration that aligns with the client's preferences.
  - Create a mood board to visually represent the style and elements discussed during the consultation.

- **Design Drafts**: 
  - Develop three initial design drafts based on the client's input and the research conducted.
  - Ensure that each draft incorporates the principles of symmetry, intricate patterns, and abstract shapes.

- **Client Feedback**: 
  - Present the design drafts to the client for feedback.
  - Document the client's comments and suggestions for revisions.

- **Revisions**: 
  - Make necessary adjustments to the designs based on client feedback.
  - Provide the client with two rounds of revisions to refine the design further.

- **Final Design**: 
  - Create the final tattoo design incorporating all agreed-upon elements and revisions.
  - Prepare a high-resolution digital file of the final design for the client.

- **Delivery**: 
  - Deliver the final design file to the client in the agreed format (e.g., JPEG, PNG, or PDF).
  - Provide any additional information or guidelines for the application of the tattoo.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Documented client vision and preferences.

- **Milestone 2**: Design Drafts Created
  - Deliverable: Three initial design drafts presented to the client.

- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback Received
  - Deliverable: Documented feedback and suggestions for revisions.

- **Milestone 4**: Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Updated design drafts based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Design Delivered
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital file of the final tattoo design.
