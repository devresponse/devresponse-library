
# Project Plan Template: Custom Tarot Card Deck Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Tarot Card Deck Design project aims to create a unique and personalized tarot card deck that embodies the client's vision and energy. This project will involve close collaboration with talented graphic designers to ensure that each card reflects the client's personal or business themes. The key objectives include delivering a beautifully illustrated deck that incorporates original artwork and symbolism tailored to the client's intended message. The project will begin with initial consultations to gather ideas and preferences, followed by drafts and revisions to refine the designs. The final outcome will be a high-resolution digital product, ready for printing or online use, that stands out in any setting. By the end of this project, clients will possess a custom tarot deck that enhances their spiritual offerings, serves as a personal keepsake, or provides a thoughtful gift, ultimately resonating with their unique energy and intentions.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a detailed meeting with the client to discuss their vision, themes, and preferences for the tarot card deck.
- **Concept Development**: Create initial design concepts based on the client's input, including card layouts, color schemes, and symbolic elements.
- **Draft Creation**: Develop drafts of each tarot card, incorporating feedback from the client to ensure alignment with their vision.
- **Revisions**: Allow for multiple rounds of revisions based on client feedback to refine the designs and ensure satisfaction.
- **Final Design Approval**: Present the final designs to the client for approval before proceeding to the final delivery.
- **Digital Delivery**: Provide the completed tarot card designs in high-resolution digital formats suitable for printing and online use.
- **Post-Delivery Support**: Offer support for any questions or additional requests after the delivery of the final product.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completion
  - **Deliverable**: Summary of client vision and themes.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Concept Development Completion
  - **Deliverable**: Initial design concepts for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Draft Creation Completion
  - **Deliverable**: Drafts of all tarot cards for client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Revisions Completion
  - **Deliverable**: Revised designs based on client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Design Approval
  - **Deliverable**: Client-approved final designs.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Digital Delivery Completion
  - **Deliverable**: High-resolution digital files of the tarot card deck.
