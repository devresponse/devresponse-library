
# Project Plan Template: Custom Typography Portraits

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Typography Portraits project aims to transform cherished words and phrases into stunning works of art, providing a unique graphic design service that resonates with personal meaning. The primary objective is to create personalized portraits crafted entirely from typography, allowing clients to celebrate their favorite quotes, song lyrics, or special phrases in a visually striking manner. This project will not only enhance the aesthetic appeal of home decor but also serve as a heartfelt gift or personal keepsake. Key outcomes include a streamlined consultation process, the delivery of high-resolution artwork, and a high level of client satisfaction through tailored designs that reflect individual styles and preferences. By the end of this project, we aim to establish a reputation for quality and creativity in the custom art market, ensuring that each portrait captures the essence of the client's message in a memorable way.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Custom Typography Portraits project includes the following phases:

- **Consultation**: Engage with clients to understand their vision, preferred styles, and the specific words or phrases they wish to incorporate into the portrait.
- **Design Draft**: Create an initial draft of the typography portrait based on the consultation feedback. This draft will include layout options, font selections, and color schemes.
- **Client Review**: Present the draft to the client for feedback and revisions. This phase allows for two rounds of revisions to ensure the design aligns with the client's expectations.
- **Final Artwork**: Upon approval of the draft, finalize the typography portrait, ensuring it meets high-resolution standards suitable for both print and digital use.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final artwork to the client in their preferred format, along with usage guidelines for printing or digital display.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the initial consultation
  - **Deliverable**: Consultation notes and agreed-upon vision document
- **Milestone 2**: Submission of the design draft
  - **Deliverable**: Initial typography portrait draft for client review
- **Milestone 3**: Client feedback and revisions
  - **Deliverable**: Revised draft incorporating client feedback
- **Milestone 4**: Final approval of the artwork
  - **Deliverable**: Approved final design ready for production
- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of final artwork
  - **Deliverable**: High-resolution files sent to the client
