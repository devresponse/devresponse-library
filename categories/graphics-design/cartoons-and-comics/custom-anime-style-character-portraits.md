
# Project Plan: Custom Anime-Style Character Portraits

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Anime-Style Character Portraits project aims to provide clients with a unique and personalized artistic experience that captures their essence in a vibrant anime aesthetic. The primary objective is to create high-quality, custom portraits that not only enhance the client's online presence but also serve as thoughtful gifts for friends and loved ones. This project will involve a collaborative process, beginning with a detailed consultation to understand the client's ideas and preferences. Key outcomes include the delivery of a beautifully crafted, high-resolution digital portrait that meets the client's expectations and can be used across various platforms. By leveraging the skills of talented artists, we aim to immerse clients in the world of anime, ensuring that each portrait is a true reflection of their personality and style.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Schedule a one-on-one consultation with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific details they want to include in the portrait.
  - Gather reference images and inspiration from the client to guide the artistic process.

- **Draft Creation**: 
  - Artists will create an initial draft of the character portrait based on the consultation.
  - The draft will be presented to the client for feedback and revisions.

- **Revisions**: 
  - Allow for up to {{number}} rounds of revisions based on client feedback to ensure satisfaction with the draft.
  - Make necessary adjustments to colors, features, and overall composition as per client requests.

- **Finalization**: 
  - Once the client approves the final draft, the artist will finalize the portrait.
  - Deliver the completed portrait in high-resolution digital format suitable for various uses (e.g., social media, prints).

- **Post-Delivery Support**: 
  - Provide the client with tips on how to best use and display their new portrait.
  - Offer a follow-up consultation to gather feedback and discuss potential future projects.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Documented client preferences and reference materials.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Draft Creation
  - Deliverable: Initial draft of the character portrait for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised drafts based on client feedback (up to {{number}} rounds).

- **Milestone 4**: Final Portrait Delivery
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital portrait delivered to the client.

- **Milestone 5**: Post-Delivery Support
  - Deliverable: Follow-up consultation and feedback collection.
