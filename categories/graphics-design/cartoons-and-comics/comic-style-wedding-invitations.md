
# Project Plan: Comic-Style Wedding Invitations

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Comic-Style Wedding Invitations" project aims to create a unique and personalized set of wedding invitations that encapsulate the love story of {{Couple's Names}} through engaging comic strip illustrations. The primary objective is to transform traditional wedding invitations into a delightful narrative that reflects the couple's journey, from their first meeting to the proposal. This project will involve close collaboration with the couple to ensure that each comic panel resonates with their personalities and captures the essence of their relationship. Key outcomes include a series of high-quality digital invitations that can be printed or sent electronically, providing a memorable keepsake for both the couple and their guests. By integrating preferred color schemes, themes, and specific elements, this project will not only set the tone for the wedding but also create a lasting impression that celebrates the couple's unique story.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with {{Couple's Names}} to discuss their vision, preferences, and key moments to be illustrated.
- **Storyboarding**: Develop a storyboard that outlines the comic strip panels, ensuring a coherent narrative flow that captures the couple's love story.
- **Illustration Design**: Create detailed illustrations for each panel, incorporating the couple's personalities, preferred color schemes, and any specific elements they wish to include.
- **Review and Revisions**: Present the initial designs to {{Couple's Names}} for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Finalization**: Prepare the final high-resolution digital files of the comic-style wedding invitations for printing and electronic distribution.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final product in multiple formats (PDF, JPEG) suitable for both print and digital use.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completion
  - Deliverable: Summary of discussion and agreed-upon key moments.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Storyboard Approval
  - Deliverable: Storyboard draft for the comic strip.

- **Milestone 3**: First Draft of Illustrations
  - Deliverable: Initial set of comic panels for review.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised illustrations based on feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Product Delivery
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital invitations in multiple formats.
