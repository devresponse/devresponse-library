
# Project Plan: Custom Tarot Card Illustrations in Comic Style

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Custom Tarot Card Illustrations in Comic Style" project aims to create a unique and vibrant set of tarot cards that blend traditional tarot imagery with a modern, playful comic aesthetic. The primary objective is to design a cohesive series of illustrations that resonate with both the mystical themes of tarot and the contemporary tastes of today's audience. Each card will feature original artwork that captures the essence of its corresponding tarot symbol while infusing it with a fresh, cartoonish flair. Key outcomes of this project include the delivery of high-resolution digital files for printing, a comprehensive style guide detailing the color palettes and design elements used, and a collaborative process that ensures the client's vision is realized in each illustration. By the end of this project, the client will have a bespoke set of tarot cards that not only serves as a tool for divination but also as a visually striking piece of art.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Concept Development**: 
  - Conduct research on traditional tarot symbolism and imagery.
  - Develop initial concepts for each tarot card, focusing on how to reinterpret traditional elements in a comic style.
  
- **Illustration Creation**: 
  - Create unique illustrations for each of the {{number_of_cards}} tarot cards, ensuring a cohesive style throughout.
  - Incorporate feedback from the client during the illustration process to refine designs.

- **Finalization of Illustrations**: 
  - Prepare high-resolution digital files for each card, suitable for printing.
  - Ensure that all illustrations meet the required specifications for quality and detail.

- **Style Guide Development**: 
  - Create a style guide that outlines the color palettes, design elements, and artistic choices made throughout the project.
  - Include guidelines for future use of the illustrations and any potential merchandise.

- **Client Collaboration**: 
  - Schedule regular check-ins with the client to discuss progress, gather feedback, and make necessary adjustments.
  - Ensure that the final product aligns with the client's vision and expectations.
