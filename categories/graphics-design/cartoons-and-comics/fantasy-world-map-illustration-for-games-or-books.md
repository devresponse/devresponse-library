
# Project Plan Template: Fantasy World Map Illustration for Games or Books

## 1. Executive Summary

The Fantasy World Map Illustration project aims to create a captivating and intricately detailed map that serves as the foundation for games, novels, or tabletop RPG campaigns. The primary objective is to visually represent a fictional world, showcasing its geographical features, unique lore, and cultural elements. This project will involve close collaboration with the artist to ensure that the final illustration not only meets but exceeds the client's expectations. Key outcomes include a stunning visual representation of the world, complete with key landmarks, regions, and routes that enhance the storytelling experience. The map will be designed to engage and inspire the audience, making it an essential tool for any fantasy narrative. Through iterative feedback and revisions, we aim to achieve a masterpiece that resonates with the intended audience, whether for a sprawling epic or a concise tabletop setting.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Concept Development**: 
  - Gather inspiration and references for fantasy world maps.
  - Develop a concept based on the client's vision, including geographical features, cultural elements, and key landmarks.

- **Initial Sketches**: 
  - Create rough sketches of the map layout, including regions, routes, and significant locations.
  - Present sketches to the client for feedback and approval.

- **Detailed Illustration**: 
  - Begin the detailed illustration process, incorporating feedback from the initial sketches.
  - Include elements such as mountains, rivers, forests, cities, and other landmarks that reflect the world’s lore.

- **Color and Texture Application**: 
  - Apply color schemes and textures that enhance the visual appeal and thematic elements of the map.
  - Ensure that the color palette aligns with the overall tone of the project.

- **Final Revisions**: 
  - Present the near-final version to the client for review.
  - Make necessary revisions based on client feedback to ensure satisfaction.

- **Delivery of Final Artwork**: 
  - Provide the final high-resolution map in various formats suitable for print and digital use.
  - Include a brief guide on how to use the map within the context of the game or narrative.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Research and Concept Development 
  - Deliverable: Concept document with initial ideas and references.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Initial Sketches 
  - Deliverable: Rough sketches for client review and approval.

- **Milestone 3**: Detailed Illustration 
  - Deliverable: Detailed map illustration for client feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Color and Texture Application 
  - Deliverable: Colorized version of the map for review.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Revisions 
  - Deliverable: Near-final version of the map for final client approval.

- **Milestone 6**: Delivery of Final Artwork 
  - Deliverable: High-resolution final map in multiple formats.
