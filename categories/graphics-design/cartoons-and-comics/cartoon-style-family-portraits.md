
# Project Plan: Cartoon-Style Family Portraits

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Cartoon-Style Family Portraits" project aims to transform families into delightful, whimsical illustrations that capture their unique essence in a playful manner. This project is designed for families looking to celebrate their bond through art, making it an ideal gift for special occasions such as birthdays, anniversaries, or holidays, as well as a charming addition to home decor. Our talented illustrators will engage closely with clients to understand the distinctive characteristics and preferences of each family member, ensuring that the final artwork reflects their individuality. Key outcomes include high-resolution digital portraits that are ready for printing or online sharing, providing families with a joyful representation of their loved ones. The project not only fosters creativity but also strengthens family connections through artistic expression.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific characteristics they want to highlight in the portraits.
- **Character Design**: Create initial sketches based on the consultation, focusing on each family member's unique traits and personality.
- **Feedback Loop**: Present the sketches to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments to ensure satisfaction.
- **Final Illustration**: Develop the final cartoon-style portrait, incorporating all feedback and ensuring a cohesive and vibrant representation of the family.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final artwork in high-resolution digital format, suitable for both printing and online sharing.
- **Revisions**: Include one round of minor revisions post-delivery based on client feedback.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client preferences and vision.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Character Design Sketches Completed
  - Deliverable: Initial sketches for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Feedback Received and Revisions Made
  - Deliverable: Revised sketches based on client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Cartoon-Style Portrait Delivered
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital artwork in multiple formats (JPEG, PNG, etc.).
  
- **Milestone 5**: Client Satisfaction Confirmation
  - Deliverable: Client feedback and approval of the final artwork.
