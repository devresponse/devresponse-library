
# Project Plan Template: Customer Journey Map Infographic

## 1. Executive Summary

The Customer Journey Map Infographic project aims to create a visually engaging and informative infographic that outlines the various stages of a customer's interaction with a business. The primary objective of this project is to simplify complex information regarding customer behavior, enabling businesses to identify key touchpoints and areas for improvement. The infographic will illustrate the journey from initial awareness through consideration, purchase, and post-purchase engagement, utilizing design elements such as icons, flowcharts, and color coding. Key outcomes include a comprehensive understanding of customer interactions, enhanced marketing strategies, and improved customer service processes. By the end of this project, stakeholders will receive a versatile infographic that serves as a valuable tool for marketing teams, customer service departments, and business strategists, ultimately leading to optimized processes and enhanced customer experiences.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct interviews and surveys to gather data on customer touchpoints.
  - Analyze existing customer journey maps and relevant literature.
  - Identify key stages of the customer journey: Awareness, Consideration, Purchase, and Post-Purchase Engagement.

- **Design Phase**: 
  - Create initial design concepts based on research findings.
  - Develop icons, flowcharts, and color schemes that align with the brand identity.
  - Iterate designs based on client feedback and suggestions.

- **Finalization Phase**: 
  - Prepare the final infographic in multiple formats (PDF, PNG, and interactive web version).
  - Conduct a presentation to showcase the infographic and explain its components.
  - Provide a summary report detailing the research process and design decisions.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research Report summarizing customer touchpoints and journey stages.

- **Milestone 2**: Initial Design Concepts
  - Deliverable: Presentation of initial design concepts for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Design Iterations
  - Deliverable: Revised infographic design based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Infographic Delivery
  - Deliverable: Final infographic in multiple formats and a presentation summarizing the project.
