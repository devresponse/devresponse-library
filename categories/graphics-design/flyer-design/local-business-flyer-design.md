
# Project Plan: Local Business Flyer Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Local Business Flyer Design project aims to create a visually appealing and informative flyer specifically tailored for local businesses, including salons, cafes, and repair shops. The primary objective is to develop a powerful marketing tool that effectively attracts nearby customers by showcasing the unique offerings of each business. This project will involve close collaboration with the client to ensure that the flyer captures their vision and aligns with their branding elements, such as logos and color schemes. Key outcomes include a clear presentation of services offered, any special promotions or discounts, and essential contact information. The final deliverables will be provided in both print-ready and digital formats, ensuring versatility for various marketing channels. By the end of this project, local businesses will have a professional flyer that enhances their visibility and drives customer engagement.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Client Consultation**: Initial meetings with the client to understand their vision, branding, and specific requirements for the flyer.
- **Research and Analysis**: Analyze competitor flyers and local market trends to inform design choices.
- **Design Development**: Create multiple design concepts that incorporate the client's branding elements, including logos and color schemes.
- **Content Creation**: Collaborate with the client to draft compelling content that highlights services, promotions, and contact information.
- **Revisions**: Present design concepts to the client and incorporate feedback through a series of revisions until the final design is approved.
- **Final Deliverables**: Provide the completed flyer in both print-ready (PDF) and digital formats (JPEG/PNG) for online use.
- **Post-Delivery Support**: Offer guidance on how to effectively use the flyer across various marketing channels.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Client Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and initial design brief
- **Milestone 2**: Design Concepts Presented
  - Deliverable: Three initial design concepts for client review
- **Milestone 3**: Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised design based on client feedback
- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approved
  - Deliverable: Finalized flyer design
- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of Final Products
  - Deliverable: Print-ready and digital formats of the flyer
