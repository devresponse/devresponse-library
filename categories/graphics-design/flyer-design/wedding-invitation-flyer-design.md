
# Project Plan: Wedding Invitation Flyer Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Wedding Invitation Flyer Design project aims to create a visually stunning and personalized flyer that encapsulates the essence of the couple's special day. The primary objective is to design an invitation that reflects the wedding's theme and style, ensuring it resonates with the couple's vision and preferences. This project will involve close collaboration with the clients to gather their ideas, preferences, and any specific elements they wish to include. Key outcomes will include a beautifully designed flyer featuring essential details such as the wedding date, venue, and RSVP information, all presented in an elegant and harmonious manner. The final product will be delivered in high-resolution formats, suitable for both digital sharing and print distribution, allowing the couple to seamlessly invite their guests. By focusing on elegant typography, a cohesive color scheme, and relevant graphics, this project will not only meet but exceed the couple's expectations, creating a memorable first impression for their wedding celebration.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the couple to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific requirements for the flyer design.
- **Concept Development**: Create initial design concepts based on the consultation, including typography, color schemes, and graphics.
- **Design Iterations**: Present the initial concepts to the couple and gather feedback. Make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Final Design**: Develop the final design incorporating all feedback, ensuring all key elements (wedding date, venue, RSVP details) are included.
- **High-Resolution Output**: Prepare the final design in high-resolution formats suitable for both digital and print distribution (PDF, JPEG, etc.).
- **Delivery**: Provide the final files to the couple and ensure they are satisfied with the end product.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and design brief
- **Milestone 2**: Concept Development Completed
  - Deliverable: Three initial design concepts
- **Milestone 3**: Design Iterations Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised design based on client feedback
- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approved
  - Deliverable: Final design files in high-resolution formats
- **Milestone 5**: Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Client satisfaction confirmation and project feedback
