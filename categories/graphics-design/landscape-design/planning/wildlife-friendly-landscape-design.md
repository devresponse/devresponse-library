
# Wildlife-Friendly Landscape Design Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Wildlife-Friendly Landscape Design project aims to create a sustainable and aesthetically pleasing outdoor space that enhances the natural habitat for local wildlife. The primary objective is to design a landscape that attracts and supports pollinators, birds, and small mammals through the strategic selection of native plants. This project will involve a comprehensive site analysis to assess existing conditions and identify opportunities for improvement, ensuring that the design is tailored to the specific environment. Key outcomes include a detailed planting plan that specifies plant types and quantities, as well as recommendations for sustainable practices such as rain gardens and composting areas. Ultimately, this project will not only beautify the landscape but also promote biodiversity and ecological health, fostering a harmonious relationship between the environment and its inhabitants.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Site Analysis**: Conduct a thorough assessment of the existing landscape, including soil quality, sunlight exposure, and current flora and fauna.
- **Native Plant Selection**: Research and select native plants that attract pollinators, provide shelter, and offer food sources for local wildlife.
- **Design Development**: Create a landscape design that incorporates the selected plants, ensuring a balance between aesthetics and ecological function.
- **Planting Plan**: Develop a detailed planting plan that outlines the types and quantities of plants to be used, along with their placement in the landscape.
- **Sustainable Practices**: Recommend sustainable landscaping practices, such as the installation of rain gardens, composting areas, and other eco-friendly features.
- **Implementation Plan**: Outline the steps for executing the design, including timelines, resource allocation, and responsibilities.
- **Maintenance Guidelines**: Provide guidelines for ongoing maintenance to ensure the landscape remains wildlife-friendly and sustainable.
