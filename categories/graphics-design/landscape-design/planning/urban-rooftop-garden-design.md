
# Urban Rooftop Garden Design Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Urban Rooftop Garden Design project aims to transform underutilized rooftop spaces into vibrant, functional green areas that enhance the aesthetic appeal and environmental quality of urban buildings. The primary objective is to create a sustainable garden that not only beautifies the urban landscape but also contributes to biodiversity and improves air quality. The project will involve a thorough analysis of the rooftop's dimensions and structural integrity to ensure safety and feasibility. Key outcomes include a detailed garden layout that maximizes space while ensuring accessibility, expert selection of native and drought-resistant plant species, and the design of an efficient irrigation system. By the end of the project, the client will receive comprehensive design plans, a curated plant list, and irrigation schematics, equipping them with all necessary resources to bring their rooftop garden vision to life.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Site Analysis**: Conduct a thorough assessment of the rooftop's dimensions, structural integrity, and existing conditions.
- **Design Development**: Create a detailed layout plan that maximizes the use of space while ensuring accessibility and usability for all potential users.
- **Plant Selection**: Research and select appropriate native and drought-resistant plant species that thrive in urban environments, ensuring year-round beauty and sustainability.
- **Irrigation System Design**: Develop an efficient irrigation system that conserves water while maintaining the health of the selected plants.
- **Documentation**: Prepare detailed design plans, plant lists, and irrigation schematics for client review and implementation.
