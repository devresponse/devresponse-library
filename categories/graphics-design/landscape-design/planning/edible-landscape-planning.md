
# Edible Landscape Planning Project Plan Template

## 1. Executive Summary

The Edible Landscape Planning project aims to transform outdoor spaces into sustainable and aesthetically pleasing environments that provide fresh food sources. The primary objective is to conduct a thorough analysis of the existing landscape, focusing on soil quality, sunlight exposure, and climate conditions to ensure optimal growth for selected edible plants. By collaborating with homeowners, our expert landscape designers will curate a selection of fruits, vegetables, and herbs that are well-suited to the specific environment. Key outcomes include a detailed planting plan that maximizes both beauty and functionality, maintenance guidelines to support year-round growth, and seasonal planting suggestions. This project not only enhances the visual appeal of the property but also promotes sustainable living by integrating food production into everyday life. Ultimately, the Edible Landscape Planning project seeks to create a harmonious balance between nature and human habitation, fostering a deeper connection to the environment.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the homeowner to discuss project goals, preferences, and any specific requirements for the edible landscape.
  
- **Site Analysis**: Conduct a comprehensive assessment of the existing landscape, including:
  - Soil testing for quality and nutrient content
  - Evaluation of sunlight exposure throughout the day
  - Assessment of local climate conditions and microclimates
  
- **Plant Selection**: Identify suitable edible plants (fruits, vegetables, herbs) that thrive in the identified conditions, considering factors such as growth habits, seasonal availability, and homeowner preferences.

- **Design Development**: Create a visually appealing landscape design that incorporates the selected edible plants, ensuring a balance between aesthetics and functionality.

- **Planting Plan**: Provide a detailed planting plan that includes:
  - Layout of plant placements
  - Recommended planting times for each species
  - Maintenance guidelines for optimal growth
  
- **Implementation Support**: Offer guidance during the planting process, including tips on soil preparation and planting techniques.

- **Follow-Up Consultation**: Schedule a follow-up meeting to assess the initial growth and provide additional recommendations for care and maintenance.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

  - Deliverable: Final landscape design plan, including visual representations.

- **Milestone 5**: Planting Plan Delivery
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive planting plan with layout and maintenance guidelines.

- **Milestone 6**: Implementation Support
  - Deliverable: On-site guidance during the planting process.

- **Milestone 7**: Follow-Up Consultation
  - Deliverable: Follow-up report with growth assessment and additional recommendations.
