
# Commercial Landscape Master Plan Project Plan Template

## 1. Executive Summary

The Commercial Landscape Master Plan project aims to create a detailed and strategic landscape design tailored specifically for commercial properties. The primary objective of this project is to enhance the visual appeal and functionality of outdoor spaces, ensuring they are inviting and practical for both clients and employees. By conducting an initial consultation to understand the client's vision and requirements, we will gather essential insights that will guide the design process. Following this, a thorough site analysis will be performed, taking into account local climate and soil conditions to ensure the selected plants and materials thrive in their environment. The final deliverable will be a comprehensive master plan that outlines plant selections, hardscape elements, irrigation systems, and maintenance guidelines. Key outcomes of this project include improved curb appeal, a welcoming environment that reflects the brand identity of the business, and a sustainable landscape that requires minimal maintenance. This project is essential for businesses looking to enhance their outdoor spaces and create a lasting impression on clients and employees alike.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Meet with the client to discuss their vision, requirements, and budget.
  - Document client preferences and expectations.

- **Site Analysis**: 
  - Conduct a thorough assessment of the site, including existing vegetation, soil conditions, and climate factors.
  - Identify any site constraints or opportunities for improvement.

- **Research**: 
  - Investigate local climate conditions and soil types to inform plant selection.
  - Review zoning regulations and any other legal considerations.

- **Design Development**: 
  - Create conceptual designs based on the client's vision and site analysis.
  - Develop a detailed master plan that includes:
    - Plant selections (trees, shrubs, flowers)
    - Hardscape elements (paths, patios, walls)
    - Irrigation systems (drip, sprinkler)
    - Maintenance guidelines (seasonal care, pruning, fertilization)

- **Client Review and Revisions**: 
  - Present the master plan to the client for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on client input.

- **Final Deliverable**: 
  - Provide the client with a comprehensive master plan document, including all design elements and guidelines.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed

  - Deliverable: Conceptual design sketches for client review.

- **Milestone 5**: Client Review Completed
  - Deliverable: Feedback document from the client.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Master Plan Delivered
