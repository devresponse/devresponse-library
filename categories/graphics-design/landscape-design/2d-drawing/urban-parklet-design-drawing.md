
# Urban Parklet Design Drawing Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Urban Parklet Design Drawing project aims to create a comprehensive 2D drawing that encapsulates the essence of urban parklets. This project is designed to provide a visual representation of a functional and aesthetically pleasing outdoor space that enhances urban environments. The primary objective is to develop a detailed drawing that includes seating areas, planters, and pedestrian-friendly features, ensuring that the parklet is inviting and practical for community use. Key outcomes of this project will include a scaled drawing that adheres to industry standards, facilitating accurate construction and landscaping. By focusing on community interaction and enhancing public spaces, this project will serve as a vital tool for clients looking to promote social engagement and improve the quality of urban life.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct a thorough analysis of existing urban parklets and gather community input to inform design decisions.
- **Concept Development**: Create initial design concepts that reflect community needs and preferences.
- **Design Drafting**: Develop detailed 2D drawings that include:
  - Seating areas (benches, tables, etc.)
  - Planters and greenery
  - Pedestrian pathways and accessibility features
  - Lighting and safety elements
- **Revisions**: Incorporate feedback from stakeholders and make necessary adjustments to the design.
- **Finalization**: Prepare the final scaled drawing, ensuring compliance with local regulations and industry standards.
- **Documentation**: Provide a comprehensive report that includes design rationale, material specifications, and maintenance recommendations.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Analysis
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and community input.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Initial Concept Development
  - Deliverable: Presentation of design concepts for stakeholder review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Drafting of Detailed 2D Drawings
  - Deliverable: Preliminary 2D drawings showcasing design elements.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Stakeholder Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised drawings based on stakeholder feedback.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Scaled Drawing Submission
  - Deliverable: Finalized 2D drawing and comprehensive documentation.
