
# Project Plan Template: Urban Rooftop Garden Layout

## 1. Executive Summary

The Urban Rooftop Garden Layout project aims to design a comprehensive 2D layout that transforms urban rooftops into vibrant green spaces. The primary objective of this project is to create detailed drawings that effectively showcase the arrangement of planters, seating areas, and designated green zones. By focusing on maximizing space efficiency, the layout will ensure both aesthetic appeal and functionality, catering to the needs of urban dwellers seeking to enhance their living environments. Key outcomes will include a thorough analysis of sunlight exposure, accessibility, and the integration of sustainable practices. The final deliverable will provide clients with a clear visual representation of their rooftop garden, facilitating effective communication with contractors and ensuring that the design vision is accurately implemented. This project not only aims to beautify urban spaces but also to promote a serene environment amidst the hustle and bustle of city life, ultimately contributing to the well-being of its inhabitants.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Site Assessment**: Conduct a thorough assessment of the rooftop space, including measurements, existing structures, and environmental factors.
- **Design Concept Development**: Create initial design concepts that incorporate client preferences and sustainable practices.
- **2D Layout Creation**: Develop detailed 2D drawings that include:
  - Arrangement of planters and vegetation
  - Designated seating areas
  - Pathways and accessibility features
  - Integration of water features or irrigation systems
- **Client Review and Revisions**: Present the initial layout to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Final Deliverables**: Provide the client with finalized 2D drawings, including a legend and notes on plant selection and maintenance recommendations.
