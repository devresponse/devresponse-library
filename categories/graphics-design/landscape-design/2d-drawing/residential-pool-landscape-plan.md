
# Project Plan Template: Residential Pool Landscape Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Residential Pool Landscape Plan project aims to create a comprehensive 2D site plan specifically designed for residential pool areas. The primary objective is to provide homeowners with a detailed visual representation of their pool layout, surrounding decking, landscaping features, and the strategic placement of outdoor furniture. This project will enhance the aesthetic appeal of outdoor spaces while ensuring functionality and ease of movement. Key outcomes include the delivery of scaled drawings that accurately depict dimensions and spatial relationships, allowing for effective implementation of the design. By the end of this project, clients will receive a clear and professional guide that not only transforms their pool area into a beautiful oasis but also meets their practical needs for outdoor living. This project is particularly beneficial for homeowners seeking to elevate their outdoor experience and create a welcoming environment for family and friends.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and requirements for the pool landscape.
- **Site Analysis**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the existing site conditions, including measurements, topography, and sunlight exposure.
- **Concept Development**: Create initial design concepts that incorporate the client's feedback and preferences.
- **2D Site Plan Creation**: Develop detailed 2D drawings that include:
  - Pool layout with dimensions
  - Surrounding decking design
  - Landscaping features (plants, trees, shrubs)
  - Placement of outdoor furniture
- **Revisions**: Present the initial site plan to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions.
- **Final Deliverables**: Provide the client with the final 2D site plan in both digital and printed formats, including any additional notes or guidelines for implementation.
