
# Project Plan Template: Meditation Garden Site Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Meditation Garden Site Plan project aims to create a comprehensive 2D site plan that embodies tranquility and harmony, essential for meditation gardens. The primary objective is to design a serene environment that promotes relaxation and mindfulness through carefully planned pathways, water features, and seating areas. This project will involve collaboration with the client to capture their unique vision and preferences, ensuring the final design is personalized and functional. Key outcomes include a meticulously scaled site plan that accurately represents dimensions and spatial relationships, detailed annotations, and specifications to assist in the construction process. By focusing on these elements, the project will result in a peaceful retreat that effectively meets the needs of its users.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Site Analysis**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the proposed site, including topography, existing vegetation, and environmental factors.
- **Client Consultation**: Engage in discussions with the client to understand their vision, preferences, and any specific requirements for the meditation garden.
- **Design Development**:
  - Create conceptual sketches of the garden layout, including pathways, water features, and seating areas.
  - Develop a detailed 2D site plan that includes:
    - Scaled dimensions
    - Spatial relationships
    - Material specifications
- **Annotations and Specifications**: Provide detailed annotations on the site plan, including:
  - Plant selections
  - Construction materials
  - Maintenance considerations
- **Revisions**: Incorporate client feedback and make necessary revisions to the site plan to ensure satisfaction.
- **Final Deliverables**: Prepare and deliver the final 2D site plan, including all annotations and specifications, in both digital and printed formats.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Site Analysis
  - Deliverable: Site analysis report
- **Milestone 2**: Client Consultation and Conceptual Sketches
  - Deliverable: Conceptual sketches for client review
- **Milestone 3**: Draft of 2D Site Plan
  - Deliverable: Draft 2D site plan for client feedback
- **Milestone 4**: Final 2D Site Plan
  - Deliverable: Finalized 2D site plan with annotations and specifications
