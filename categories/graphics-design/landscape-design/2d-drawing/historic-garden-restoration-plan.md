
# Historic Garden Restoration Plan

## Executive Summary
The Historic Garden Restoration Plan aims to create a comprehensive 2D drawing that meticulously outlines the restoration of historic gardens. The primary objective of this project is to research and document the original layouts and features of the gardens, ensuring authenticity in the restoration process. By collaborating closely with the client, we will gather insights into their vision and requirements, which will guide the design to align with both historical accuracy and contemporary needs. Key outcomes of this project include detailed site plans that preserve historical elements while integrating modern updates to enhance functionality and aesthetics. The final deliverables will encompass annotations for plant selections and recommendations for materials that respect the garden's heritage. This project not only aims to restore the beauty of the historic gardens but also to create a sustainable and functional space that can be enjoyed by future generations.

## Detailed Scope of Work
1. **Research Phase**
   - Conduct thorough research on the historical significance of the gardens.
   - Gather archival materials, photographs, and historical documents related to the original garden layouts and features.
   - Interview stakeholders and historical experts to gain insights into the garden's history.

2. **Site Analysis**
   - Perform a detailed site analysis to assess the current condition of the gardens.
   - Identify existing plants, structures, and features that will be preserved or restored.
   - Evaluate environmental factors that may influence the restoration process.

3. **Design Development**
   - Create initial design concepts based on research findings and client input.
   - Develop detailed 2D drawings that outline the restoration plan, including layout, plant selections, and materials.
   - Incorporate modern updates that enhance the garden's functionality while respecting historical elements.

4. **Client Review and Revisions**
   - Present the design concepts to the client for feedback.
   - Make necessary revisions based on client input to ensure satisfaction with the final design.

5. **Final Deliverables**
   - Provide a comprehensive set of 2D drawings that include:
     - Detailed site plans
     - Annotations for plant selections
     - Recommendations for materials
   - Prepare a final report summarizing the restoration plan and its historical significance.

## Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1:** Completion of Research Phase  
  **Deliverable:** Research report and historical documentation.

- **Milestone 2:** Completion of Site Analysis  
  **Deliverable:** Site analysis report detailing current conditions and preservation recommendations.

- **Milestone 3:** Initial Design Concepts  
  **Deliverable:** Presentation of initial design concepts to the client.

- **Milestone 4:** Client Review and Feedback  
  **Deliverable:** Revised design based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 5:** Final Design and Documentation  
  **Deliverable:** Comprehensive 2D drawings and final report.
