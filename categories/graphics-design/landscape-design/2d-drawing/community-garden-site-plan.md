
# Community Garden Site Plan Project Plan Template

## 1. Executive Summary

The Community Garden Site Plan project aims to develop a comprehensive 2D site layout that visually represents the design and organization of a community garden. The primary objective is to create an inclusive space that fosters community interaction while promoting sustainability. This project will involve the design of designated plots for individual gardeners, clearly marked pathways for accessibility, and shared facilities such as tool sheds and compost areas. Key outcomes include a detailed, scaled drawing that adheres to local zoning regulations and incorporates feedback from community stakeholders. By engaging with the community throughout the process, we aim to ensure that the final design meets the needs and expectations of all participants, ultimately enhancing the gardening experience and fostering a sense of community ownership.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct meetings with community stakeholders to gather requirements and preferences for the garden layout.
- **Site Analysis**: Assess the proposed site for environmental factors, soil quality, sunlight exposure, and existing vegetation.
- **Design Development**: Create a preliminary design that includes:
  - Designated plots for individual gardeners
  - Pathways for accessibility
  - Shared facilities (tool sheds, compost areas, seating)
- **Community Feedback**: Present the preliminary design to stakeholders for feedback and suggestions.
- **Revisions**: Incorporate feedback into the final design.
- **Final Site Plan**: Produce a detailed, scaled 2D drawing that adheres to local zoning regulations.
- **Final Review Session**: Conduct a review meeting with stakeholders to ensure all elements meet expectations.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
