
# Project Plan Template: 3D Model of Outdoor Wedding Venue

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the "3D Model of Outdoor Wedding Venue" project is to create a highly detailed and visually immersive 3D model that encapsulates the elegance and romance of an outdoor wedding setting. This project aims to provide clients with a realistic representation of their envisioned venue, complete with meticulously crafted landscaping, vibrant floral arrangements, and thoughtfully arranged seating layouts. By collaborating closely with the client throughout the design process, we will ensure that the final model not only meets but exceeds their expectations. Key outcomes include a high-resolution 3D render that can be utilized for promotional materials, planning purposes, and client presentations. Ultimately, this project seeks to enhance the client's ability to visualize their special day, making the planning process more engaging and effective.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to gather requirements, preferences, and vision for the outdoor wedding venue.
- **Concept Development**: Create initial sketches and mood boards based on client input to establish the overall aesthetic and layout.
- **3D Modeling**: Develop a detailed 3D model that includes:
  - Landscaping features (trees, shrubs, flowers)
  - Seating arrangements (tables, chairs, etc.)
  - Pathways and other structural elements (gazebos, arches)
- **Client Review**: Present the initial 3D model to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments.
- **Final Render Creation**: Produce a high-resolution 3D render of the finalized model, ensuring it captures all details and client specifications.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final render in multiple formats suitable for various uses (digital presentations, print materials, etc.).
- **Post-Delivery Support**: Offer a follow-up consultation to address any additional client needs or modifications.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed

- **Milestone 5**: Final Render Completed
  - Deliverable: High-resolution 3D render delivered in specified formats.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Final project report and follow-up consultation.
