
# Project Plan Template: AI-Enhanced Real Estate Photo Editing

## 1. Executive Summary

The AI-Enhanced Real Estate Photo Editing project aims to revolutionize the way real estate photography is presented to potential buyers. By leveraging advanced AI image editing techniques, the project seeks to enhance the visual appeal of properties, thereby increasing buyer interest and facilitating quicker sales. The primary objectives include improving lighting, adjusting color balance, and sharpening details to create stunning visuals that highlight the best features of each property. Key outcomes will involve the delivery of high-quality images suitable for online listings, brochures, and promotional materials. The use of AI tools will not only automate the editing process but also optimize enhancements to ensure consistent quality and efficiency. A review phase will be incorporated to guarantee client satisfaction with the final outputs, ensuring that the images meet the expectations and requirements of the real estate market.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Image Collection**: Gather original photographs from the client, ensuring a diverse range of properties.
- **AI Image Editing**:
  - **Lighting Enhancement**: Utilize AI algorithms to adjust brightness and contrast for optimal visibility.
  - **Color Balance Adjustment**: Correct color discrepancies to ensure true-to-life representations of the properties.
  - **Detail Sharpening**: Apply sharpening techniques to highlight architectural features and textures.
- **Quality Assurance**: Implement a review process to assess the quality of edited images against project standards.
- **Client Review**: Present edited images to the client for feedback and necessary adjustments.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the final images in various formats suitable for online and print use.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Image Collection
  - Deliverable: Original photographs received from the client.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Initial AI Image Editing
  - Deliverable: First draft of edited images for internal review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Quality Assurance Review
  - Deliverable: Quality assessment report and adjustments made based on findings.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Client Review Phase
  - Deliverable: Edited images presented to the client for feedback.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized images delivered in requested formats.
