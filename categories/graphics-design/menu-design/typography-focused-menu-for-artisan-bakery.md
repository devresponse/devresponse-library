
# Project Plan Template: Typography-Focused Menu for Artisan Bakery

## 1. Executive Summary

The Typography-Focused Menu for Artisan Bakery project aims to create a visually striking and user-friendly menu that embodies the unique character and quality of the bakery's offerings. The primary objective is to design a menu that not only showcases the bakery's products but also enhances the overall customer experience through thoughtful typography and layout. By utilizing handcrafted fonts and a clean, modern design, the menu will facilitate easy navigation for customers, allowing them to explore the delicious offerings with ease. Key outcomes of this project include a comprehensive understanding of the bakery's vision and target audience, achieved through an initial consultation phase. The final deliverable will be a polished menu that aligns with the bakery's branding, featuring a harmonious color scheme that enhances readability. The project will also provide multiple design drafts and revisions based on client feedback, ensuring that the final product meets the bakery's expectations and resonates with its customers.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct meetings with the bakery team to gather insights on vision, target audience, and branding.
  - Analyze competitor menus and market trends to inform design choices.

- **Design Phase**:
  - Develop initial design concepts focusing on handcrafted typography and a clean layout.
  - Create a color palette that complements the bakery's branding and enhances readability.

- **Drafts and Revisions**:
  - Present multiple design drafts to the client for feedback.
  - Incorporate client feedback and make necessary revisions to the designs.

- **Finalization**:
  - Deliver the final menu design in various formats suitable for both print and digital use (e.g., PDF, JPEG, PNG).
  - Provide guidelines for future updates or modifications to the menu.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the Consultation Phase
  - Deliverable: Summary report of insights gathered from the bakery team.

- **Milestone 2**: Presentation of Initial Design Concepts
  - Deliverable: Three initial design drafts showcasing different typography and layout options.

- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised designs based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Menu Design Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized menu in multiple formats for print and digital use.
