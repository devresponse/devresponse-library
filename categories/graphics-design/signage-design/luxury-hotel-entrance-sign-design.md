
# Luxury Hotel Entrance Sign Design Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Luxury Hotel Entrance Sign Design project aims to create a visually stunning and sophisticated sign that embodies the essence of luxury and elegance. The primary objective is to design a sign that not only serves as a functional element but also acts as a key branding tool reflecting the hotel's identity and values. This project will utilize premium materials such as brushed metal, high-quality acrylic, or natural stone to ensure durability and a high-end appearance. The refined typography will be carefully selected to enhance readability while maintaining an upscale aesthetic. Key outcomes include a complete design package consisting of initial concept sketches, digital mock-ups, and final revisions based on client feedback. The final deliverable will be a ready-for-production design that stands out and leaves a lasting impression on guests and passersby alike. By focusing on quality and elegance, this project will significantly contribute to the hotel's overall branding strategy and guest experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**
  - Conduct market research on luxury hotel signage trends.
  - Analyze competitor signage and branding strategies.

- **Concept Development**
  - Create initial concept sketches based on client requirements.
  - Present concepts to the client for feedback and selection.

- **Design Execution**
  - Develop digital mock-ups of the selected concept.
  - Refine typography and material choices in collaboration with the client.

- **Revisions**
  - Incorporate client feedback into the design.
  - Provide up to three rounds of revisions to ensure client satisfaction.

- **Final Deliverables**
  - Prepare a complete design package, including:
    - Final design files in various formats (e.g., AI, PDF, PNG).
    - Material specifications and recommendations.
    - Installation guidelines and maintenance instructions.
