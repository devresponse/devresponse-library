
# Project Plan Template: Custom Wedding Welcome Signs

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Wedding Welcome Signs project aims to create an elegant and romantic welcome sign that embodies the unique style and theme of the couple's special day. The primary objective is to design a sign that prominently features the couple's names and wedding date, ensuring that guests feel warmly welcomed upon arrival. This project will involve close collaboration with the couple to gather their preferences regarding design elements, including theme colors and overall aesthetics. Key outcomes include a high-resolution digital and print-ready design that harmonizes with the wedding decor and enhances the overall ambiance of the celebration. By delivering a personalized and visually appealing welcome sign, this project will contribute to a memorable experience for both the couple and their guests.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the couple to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific themes or colors they wish to incorporate into the welcome sign.
- **Design Concept Development**: Create initial design concepts based on the information gathered during the consultation. This will include sketches and color palettes.
- **Feedback and Revisions**: Present the design concepts to the couple for feedback. Make necessary revisions based on their input to ensure the design aligns with their expectations.
- **Final Design Approval**: Obtain final approval from the couple on the design before proceeding to production.
- **Production**: Prepare the final design in a high-resolution format suitable for both digital and print use.
- **Delivery**: Provide the couple with the final product, including digital files and any physical prints as agreed upon.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of couple's preferences and design requirements.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Design Concepts Developed
  - Deliverable: Presentation of initial design concepts and color palettes.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Feedback and Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised design based on couple's feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Design Approved
  - Deliverable: Finalized design ready for production.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Production Completed
  - Deliverable: High-resolution digital files and physical prints delivered to the couple.
