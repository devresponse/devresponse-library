
# Project Plan Template: Typography-Focused Label Design for Craft Coffee Bags

## 1. Executive Summary

The Typography-Focused Label Design for Craft Coffee Bags project aims to create a distinctive and visually striking label that captures the essence of the brand while appealing to the target audience. The primary objective is to develop innovative typography that not only reflects the artisanal quality of the coffee but also enhances brand identity. This project will involve a comprehensive design process, including multiple concepts and revisions based on client feedback, ensuring that the final product aligns with the client's marketing strategy. Key outcomes include a modern aesthetic label that stands out on retail shelves, effectively communicates essential product information, and resonates with consumers. By collaborating closely with the client, we will ensure that the label design embodies their vision and values, ultimately contributing to increased brand recognition and sales.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to discuss brand values, target audience, and design preferences.
- **Research and Inspiration**: Conduct market research to gather insights on current design trends in the coffee industry and analyze competitor labels.
- **Concept Development**: Create three initial label design concepts focusing on typography, color schemes, and layout.
- **Client Review**: Present the concepts to the client for feedback and select one concept for further development.
- **Revisions**: Implement client feedback and make necessary revisions to the selected design.
- **Final Design**: Finalize the label design, ensuring it meets all functional requirements for print production.
- **Print-Ready Files**: Prepare and deliver print-ready files in the required formats.
- **Post-Launch Support**: Provide guidance on label application and any necessary adjustments after the initial print run.
