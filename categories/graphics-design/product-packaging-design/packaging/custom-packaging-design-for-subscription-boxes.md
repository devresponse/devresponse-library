
# Project Plan Template: Custom Packaging Design for Subscription Boxes

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Packaging Design for Subscription Boxes project aims to create bespoke packaging solutions that enhance the unboxing experience for customers. Our primary objective is to design packaging that not only protects the products but also reflects the unique identity of your brand. By focusing on the unboxing experience, we intend to make it memorable and engaging, fostering customer loyalty and encouraging repeat purchases. Key outcomes of this project include the development of custom inserts for product security, the use of branded tissue paper to add a touch of luxury, and cohesive design elements that unify the entire package. We will collaborate closely with your team to understand your brand values and target audience, ensuring that every design aspect resonates with your customers. The final deliverables will consist of comprehensive design files that are ready for production, facilitating a smooth transition from concept to reality.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Discovery**
  - Conduct brand analysis to understand brand values and target audience.
  - Review existing packaging and identify areas for improvement.

- **Concept Development**
  - Create initial design concepts for packaging.
  - Develop mood boards to visualize design direction.

- **Design Creation**
  - Design custom packaging that includes:
    - Unique box structure tailored for subscription boxes.
    - Custom inserts to securely hold products.
    - Branded tissue paper and other luxury elements.
    - Cohesive design elements that reflect brand identity.

- **Prototyping**
  - Develop prototypes of the packaging designs.
  - Conduct testing for functionality and durability.

- **Final Design and Production Files**
  - Refine designs based on feedback.
  - Prepare comprehensive design files for production.

- **Collaboration and Feedback**
  - Regular meetings with the client for updates and feedback.
  - Incorporate client feedback into design iterations.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Research and Discovery Completion**
  - Deliverable: Brand analysis report and existing packaging review.

- **Milestone 2: Concept Development Completion**
  - Deliverable: Initial design concepts and mood boards.

- **Milestone 3: Design Creation Completion**
  - Deliverable: Finalized packaging designs including all elements.

- **Milestone 4: Prototyping Completion**
  - Deliverable: Physical prototypes of the packaging.

- **Milestone 5: Final Design Approval**
  - Deliverable: Approved design files ready for production.
