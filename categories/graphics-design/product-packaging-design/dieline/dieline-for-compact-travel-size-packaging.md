
# Project Plan Template: Dieline for Compact Travel-Size Packaging

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Dieline for Compact Travel-Size Packaging" project aims to create a precise and innovative dieline that caters to the growing demand for convenient and portable packaging solutions. The primary objective is to design packaging that not only protects the product but also enhances its visual appeal on retail shelves, thereby attracting modern consumers. This project will involve a thorough understanding of the product specifications, branding guidelines, and target audience through close collaboration with the client. Key outcomes include a detailed dieline template that accommodates various product dimensions while ensuring structural integrity and ease of use. Additionally, the project will provide recommendations for materials and finishes that complement the design, ultimately helping brands make a strong impression in the competitive travel-size market. By focusing on both functionality and aesthetics, this project will deliver a comprehensive packaging solution that meets the needs of contemporary consumers.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct market research to understand current trends in travel-size packaging and consumer preferences.
- **Client Collaboration**: Engage with the client to gather product specifications, branding guidelines, and target audience insights.
- **Dieline Design**: Create a precise dieline that accommodates various product dimensions while ensuring structural integrity.
- **Visual Appeal**: Incorporate design elements that enhance the visual appeal of the packaging for retail display.
- **Material Recommendations**: Provide recommendations for suitable materials and finishes that align with the design and product requirements.
- **Prototype Development**: Develop prototypes to test the functionality and aesthetics of the dieline design.
- **Final Deliverables**: Deliver a detailed dieline template ready for production, along with a comprehensive report on material and finish recommendations.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of market research and analysis
  - **Deliverable**: Market research report
- **Milestone 2**: Initial client meeting to gather specifications and guidelines
  - **Deliverable**: Meeting notes and project brief
- **Milestone 3**: First draft of the dieline design
  - **Deliverable**: Dieline design draft
- **Milestone 4**: Prototype development and testing
  - **Deliverable**: Prototype samples and testing feedback
- **Milestone 5**: Finalization of the dieline design and material recommendations
  - **Deliverable**: Final dieline template and recommendations report
