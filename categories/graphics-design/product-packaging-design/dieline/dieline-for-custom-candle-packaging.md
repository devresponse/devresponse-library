
# Project Plan Template: Dieline for Custom Candle Packaging

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Dieline for Custom Candle Packaging" project aims to create a precise and functional dieline that not only protects the candle products during transit but also embodies the unique identity of the brand. This project will involve designing a dieline that accommodates various candle sizes and shapes, ensuring a snug fit that prevents movement and potential damage. Key objectives include enhancing the unboxing experience with easy-open features and visually appealing graphics that resonate with the brand's aesthetic. The final deliverable will be a production-ready dieline that seamlessly integrates into the manufacturing process. By combining technical expertise with creative design, this project seeks to deliver packaging that is both functional and visually captivating, ultimately leaving a lasting impression on customers and enhancing brand loyalty.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**
  - Conduct market research to understand current trends in candle packaging.
  - Analyze competitor packaging designs to identify opportunities for differentiation.

- **Design Development**
  - Create initial sketches and concepts for the dieline.
  - Develop a dieline that accommodates multiple candle sizes and shapes.
  - Incorporate brand elements, including logos, colors, and typography.

- **Prototyping**
  - Create a physical prototype of the dieline for testing.
  - Evaluate the prototype for functionality, durability, and aesthetic appeal.

- **Revisions and Finalization**
  - Gather feedback from stakeholders and make necessary revisions.
  - Finalize the dieline design based on feedback and testing results.

- **Production Preparation**
  - Prepare the final dieline in a format suitable for production.
  - Collaborate with manufacturers to ensure compatibility with their processes.
