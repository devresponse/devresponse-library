
# Project Plan Template: Dieline for Custom Tea Box Packaging

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the "Dieline for Custom Tea Box Packaging" project is to create a precise and functional dieline template that caters specifically to the packaging needs of custom tea products. This project aims to deliver a packaging solution that not only accommodates multiple tea bags but also aligns seamlessly with the client's branding guidelines. The key outcomes include a professional dieline file that meets industry standards, enhances shelf presence, and facilitates efficient production processes. The design will prioritize structural integrity and ease of assembly while incorporating creative branding elements that resonate with the target audience. Ultimately, this project seeks to provide a visually appealing and functional packaging solution that elevates the product's marketability and consumer appeal.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the client's branding guidelines and target audience preferences to inform the design process.
- **Dieline Design**: Create a detailed dieline template that accommodates multiple tea bags, ensuring the design is functional and visually appealing.
- **Material Specifications**: Identify suitable materials for the packaging that align with the client's sustainability goals and product requirements.
- **Structural Integrity**: Ensure the dieline design maintains structural integrity during production and transportation.
- **Assembly Instructions**: Provide clear assembly instructions to facilitate ease of assembly for production teams.
- **Client Review and Feedback**: Present the initial dieline design to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Final Deliverable**: Prepare and deliver the final dieline file in the required format, ensuring it meets all industry standards for production.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Analysis
  - **Deliverable**: Research report summarizing findings and design considerations.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Initial Dieline Design
  - **Deliverable**: First draft of the dieline template for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback and Revisions
  - **Deliverable**: Revised dieline template based on client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Dieline File Preparation
  - **Deliverable**: Final dieline file ready for production, including assembly instructions.
