
# Project Plan Template: Dieline for Custom Chocolate Bar Wrappers

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Dieline for Custom Chocolate Bar Wrappers" project aims to create a precise dieline template that is specifically tailored for custom chocolate bar wrappers. The primary objective of this project is to ensure that the wrapper fits the chocolate bar snugly while incorporating essential branding elements such as logos, colors, and typography. The project overview includes a thorough analysis of the chocolate bar dimensions, the type of material used for the wrapper, and any special features like windows or cut-outs that may enhance the product's appeal. Key outcomes will include a functional dieline that not only serves as a template for production but also elevates the visual presentation of the product, making it more attractive on retail shelves. The designer will work closely with the client to ensure adherence to branding guidelines and alignment with the overall marketing strategy. This project requires a blend of technical skills and creative design thinking to produce a high-quality dieline that meets and exceeds client expectations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: 
  - Gather information on the dimensions and specifications of the chocolate bar.
  - Analyze the material options for the wrapper and their implications on design.
  
- **Dieline Creation**: 
  - Develop a precise dieline template that accommodates the chocolate bar's dimensions.
  - Incorporate branding elements such as logos, colors, and typography into the dieline.
  
- **Design Features**: 
  - Consider any special features such as windows or cut-outs that enhance visual appeal.
  - Ensure that the dieline allows for easy production and assembly.
  
- **Client Collaboration**: 
  - Conduct regular meetings with the client to review progress and gather feedback.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on client input to align with branding guidelines.
  
- **Finalization and Delivery**: 
  - Prepare the final dieline template in the required format for production.
  - Deliver the final design along with any necessary documentation or guidelines for use.
