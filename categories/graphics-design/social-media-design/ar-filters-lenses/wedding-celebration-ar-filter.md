
# Project Plan Template: Wedding Celebration AR Filter

## 1. Executive Summary

The Wedding Celebration AR Filter project aims to design and develop a unique augmented reality (AR) filter that enhances the wedding experience for the couple and their guests. The primary objective is to create a personalized AR filter that reflects the couple's story through custom animations and hashtags, encouraging social media sharing and engagement. This project will involve close collaboration with the couple to gather essential details about their preferences, ensuring that the final product resonates with their vision. Key outcomes include a fully functional AR filter optimized for popular social media platforms, providing guests with an interactive and memorable way to celebrate the couple's special day. By integrating personalized elements, the AR filter will not only serve as a keepsake but also promote the wedding through increased social media visibility.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the couple to discuss their vision, preferences, and specific elements they wish to include in the AR filter.
- **Concept Development**: Create initial design concepts and animations that reflect the couple's story and wedding theme.
- **Design Approval**: Present the concepts to the couple for feedback and approval.
- **AR Filter Development**: Develop the AR filter using appropriate software and tools, ensuring it meets technical requirements for social media platforms.
- **Testing**: Conduct thorough testing of the AR filter to ensure functionality and user experience across various devices.
- **Final Adjustments**: Make necessary adjustments based on testing feedback and finalize the AR filter.
- **Launch and Support**: Assist the couple in launching the AR filter on their chosen social media platforms and provide support for any issues that may arise.
