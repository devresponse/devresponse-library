
# Project Plan Template: Event Promotion AR Filter

## 1. Executive Summary

The Event Promotion AR Filter project aims to create an engaging and interactive Augmented Reality (AR) filter designed specifically for {{Event Name}}. The primary objective is to enhance audience engagement and drive awareness and attendance for the event through innovative digital experiences. This project will leverage captivating elements such as a countdown timer to build anticipation, branded animations that resonate with the event's theme, and geotagged effects that encourage social media sharing. By integrating these features, we aim to provide users with a unique and memorable experience that aligns with your branding and event goals. Key outcomes include increased social media interactions, heightened event visibility, and a measurable uptick in attendance, ultimately contributing to the overall success of {{Event Name}}.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Concept Development**
  - Conduct a brainstorming session to gather ideas and themes related to {{Event Name}}.
  - Analyze target audience preferences and behaviors to tailor the AR filter effectively.

- **Design and Development**
  - Create initial design mockups for the AR filter, incorporating the countdown timer, branded animations, and geotagged effects.
  - Develop the AR filter using appropriate software and platforms (e.g., Spark AR, Lens Studio).
  - Ensure compatibility with major social media platforms (e.g., Instagram, Snapchat).

- **Testing and Quality Assurance**
  - Conduct thorough testing of the AR filter to ensure functionality and user experience.
  - Gather feedback from a select group of users and make necessary adjustments.

- **Launch and Promotion**
  - Coordinate with your marketing team to promote the AR filter leading up to {{Event Name}}.
  - Monitor user engagement and interactions post-launch.

- **Post-Event Analysis**
  - Analyze the performance of the AR filter in terms of user engagement and social media metrics.
  - Provide a report summarizing the outcomes and insights gained from the project.
