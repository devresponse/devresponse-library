
# Project Plan: AR Filters for Sports Teams

## 1. Executive Summary

The "AR Filters for Sports Teams" project aims to design and develop innovative augmented reality (AR) filters that enhance fan engagement and team spirit during game days. The primary objective is to create immersive experiences that resonate with fans by incorporating the team's logos, mascots, and unique interactive effects. These filters will be compatible with popular social media platforms, allowing fans to express their loyalty and enthusiasm in a modern way. Key outcomes include the successful design and deployment of AR filters, comprehensive testing to ensure functionality across various devices, and the creation of user guidelines to maximize engagement. This project not only serves as a fun tool for fans but also acts as a strategic marketing initiative for sports teams to connect with their audience, ultimately fostering a deeper sense of community and support.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Ideation**: Conduct brainstorming sessions with stakeholders to gather ideas and preferences for the AR filters. This will include reviewing existing AR filters in the market and identifying unique features that can be incorporated.
  
- **Design Phase**: Develop initial design concepts for the AR filters, focusing on the integration of team logos, mascots, and interactive elements. Create mockups and prototypes for review.

- **Development**: Utilize AR development tools to create the filters based on the approved designs. Ensure compatibility with major social media platforms such as Instagram and Snapchat.

- **Testing**: Perform rigorous testing of the AR filters across various devices to ensure seamless functionality and user experience. Gather feedback from a select group of fans for further refinement.

- **Deployment**: Finalize the AR filters and prepare them for launch. Develop a marketing strategy to promote the filters to fans.

- **User Guidelines**: Create comprehensive guidelines on how to use the AR filters effectively, including tips for sharing on social media and maximizing engagement.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Ideation
  - Deliverable: Document summarizing ideas and preferences gathered from stakeholders.

- **Milestone 2**: Approval of Design Concepts
  - Deliverable: Finalized design mockups for the AR filters.

- **Milestone 3**: Development of AR Filters
  - Deliverable: Fully functional AR filters ready for testing.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Testing Phase
  - Deliverable: Testing report including feedback and necessary adjustments.

- **Milestone 5**: Deployment of AR Filters
  - Deliverable: AR filters launched on social media platforms.

- **Milestone 6**: Delivery of User Guidelines
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive user manual for fans.
