
# Project Plan Template: AR Filters for Nonprofits

## 1. Executive Summary

The "AR Filters for Nonprofits" project aims to design and develop engaging augmented reality (AR) filters tailored specifically for nonprofit organizations. The primary objective is to create visually striking filters that can be utilized on popular social media platforms, thereby enhancing the digital presence of these organizations. By incorporating elements such as logos, slogans, and thematic visuals that resonate with the nonprofit's mission, we aim to foster a deeper connection with their audience. Key outcomes include increased user engagement, heightened awareness of the nonprofit's cause, and a boost in donations and volunteer sign-ups. The project will involve close collaboration with the nonprofit to ensure that the final product aligns with their branding and messaging, ultimately inspiring users to share their experiences and take action in support of the cause.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Discovery**
  - Conduct interviews with nonprofit stakeholders to understand their mission, goals, and target audience.
  - Analyze existing AR filters and trends in social media engagement to inform design choices.

- **Design Phase**
  - Create initial design concepts for AR filters, incorporating nonprofit branding elements.
  - Develop interactive features that encourage user participation and sharing.

- **Development Phase**
  - Build the AR filters using appropriate software and tools.
  - Test filters on various devices and social media platforms to ensure compatibility and functionality.

- **Collaboration and Feedback**
  - Present designs to the nonprofit for feedback and revisions.
  - Implement changes based on feedback to finalize the AR filters.

- **Launch and Promotion**
  - Develop a launch strategy in collaboration with the nonprofit, including promotional materials and social media campaigns.
  - Monitor user engagement and gather feedback post-launch for potential future iterations.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Research Completion**
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and insights from stakeholder interviews and market analysis.

- **Milestone 2: Design Approval**
  - Deliverable: Finalized design concepts for AR filters, approved by the nonprofit.

- **Milestone 3: Development Completion**
  - Deliverable: Fully functional AR filters ready for testing.

- **Milestone 4: Launch**
  - Deliverable: Successful launch of AR filters on selected social media platforms, accompanied by promotional materials.

- **Milestone 5: Post-Launch Review**
  - Deliverable: Report on user engagement metrics and feedback for future improvements.
