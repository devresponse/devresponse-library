
# Project Plan Template: Instagram Story Highlight Covers

## 1. Executive Summary

The Instagram Story Highlight Covers project aims to create a cohesive and visually appealing set of custom thumbnail designs that enhance the aesthetic of the client's Instagram profile. In today's visually-driven social media landscape, it is crucial for brands to present a professional appearance that resonates with their audience. This project will involve a collaborative process where the designer works closely with the client to understand their brand identity, including colors, themes, and style preferences. The key outcomes of this project include a set of unique highlight covers that align with the client's branding and are optimized for Instagram's specifications. By the end of the project, clients will not only have an elevated visual appeal for their profiles but also a more organized presentation of their content, ultimately engaging their audience more effectively.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to gather information about their brand identity, including colors, themes, and style preferences.
- **Research and Inspiration**: Analyze competitors and gather design inspiration that aligns with the client's vision.
- **Design Concepts**: Create 3 initial design concepts for the highlight covers based on the client's input.
- **Client Feedback**: Present the concepts to the client and gather feedback for revisions.
- **Revisions**: Make necessary adjustments to the selected design concept based on client feedback.
- **Final Design**: Deliver the final set of highlight covers, ensuring they are optimized for Instagram's specifications (1080 x 1920 pixels).
- **File Formats**: Provide the final designs in multiple formats (JPEG, PNG) for versatility in use.
- **Post-Delivery Support**: Offer a brief support session to guide the client on how to upload and utilize the highlight covers on their Instagram profile.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
