
# Project Plan Template: Non-Profit Campaign Video Intro

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Non-Profit Campaign Video Intro project is to create a captivating and emotionally resonant animation that serves as the opening sequence for non-profit campaign videos. This project aims to engage viewers immediately, utilizing powerful visuals and impactful text that succinctly conveys the mission of the non-profit organization. By fostering a sense of connection and urgency, the intro animation will inspire viewers to take action and support the cause. Key outcomes include a polished animation that integrates branding elements seamlessly, enhancing recognition and trust in the organization. The final product will not only set the tone for the campaign but also effectively communicate the organization's values and aspirations, ensuring that the audience feels compelled to engage with the content. The project will adhere to a structured timeline, allowing for meticulous crafting of all elements to align with the client's vision and objectives.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Concept Development**
  - Conduct interviews with stakeholders to understand the non-profit's mission and values.
  - Analyze existing campaign materials to ensure consistency in messaging and branding.
  - Develop initial concepts and storyboards for the intro animation.

- **Scriptwriting**
  - Write a compelling script that encapsulates the non-profit's mission and call to action.
  - Collaborate with the client for feedback and revisions.

- **Design and Animation**
  - Create visual assets that reflect the non-profit's branding and emotional appeal.
  - Develop the animation, ensuring smooth transitions and engaging visuals.
  - Integrate text elements that convey key messages effectively.

- **Review and Revisions**
  - Present the initial animation draft to the client for feedback.
  - Implement revisions based on client input and finalize the animation.

- **Delivery**
  - Provide the final intro animation in multiple formats suitable for various platforms.
  - Include a style guide for future reference on branding and messaging.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Research and Concept Development Completion**
  - Deliverable: Concept presentation and storyboard.

- **Milestone 2: Script Approval**
  - Deliverable: Finalized script document.

- **Milestone 3: Animation Draft Completion**
  - Deliverable: First draft of the intro animation.

- **Milestone 4: Final Animation Delivery**
  - Deliverable: Finalized intro animation in specified formats.
