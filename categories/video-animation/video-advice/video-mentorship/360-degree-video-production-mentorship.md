
# Project Plan Template: 360-Degree Video Production Mentorship

## 1. Executive Summary

The 360-Degree Video Production Mentorship is designed to empower aspiring video creators with the skills and knowledge necessary to excel in the realm of immersive video production. This comprehensive program aims to guide participants through the entire video creation process, from the initial setup of 360-degree cameras to the intricate techniques involved in post-production stitching. The mentorship will provide personalized, hands-on training, ensuring that each participant gains a thorough understanding of essential topics such as equipment selection, shot framing for 360-degree perspectives, and the use of advanced software for editing and stitching. Key outcomes of this mentorship include the development of a solid foundation in 360-degree video production, the completion of a unique video project that participants can showcase, and the acquisition of expert feedback that will enhance their confidence and skills. By the end of the program, participants will be well-equipped to create captivating 360-degree videos for various applications, including personal projects, marketing campaigns, and professional portfolios.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Program Duration**: {{Duration of the mentorship program (e.g., 6 weeks)}}
- **Target Audience**: Aspiring video creators with a keen interest in 360-degree video production.
- **Key Components**:
  - **Introductory Session**: Overview of 360-degree video production and its applications.
  - **Equipment Selection**: Guidance on choosing the right cameras, lenses, and accessories for 360-degree filming.
  - **Camera Setup**: Hands-on training on how to set up 360-degree cameras for optimal results.
  - **Shooting Techniques**: Instruction on framing shots and capturing immersive content.
  - **Post-Production**: Training on software tools for stitching and editing 360-degree videos.
  - **Feedback Sessions**: Regular reviews of participant projects with constructive feedback from mentors.
  - **Final Project**: Each participant will complete a 360-degree video project to showcase their skills.
