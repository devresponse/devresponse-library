
# Project Plan Template: Video Content Accessibility Review

## 1. Executive Summary

The Video Content Accessibility Review project aims to ensure that video materials are accessible to all audiences, aligning with established accessibility standards. In today's digital landscape, accessibility is not merely a best practice; it is a necessity that enhances viewer engagement and satisfaction. This project will involve a comprehensive evaluation of existing video content, focusing on the presence and accuracy of captions and subtitles, visual clarity, and the identification of potential barriers that may hinder accessibility. Key outcomes will include actionable insights and tailored recommendations to improve the accessibility of video content, ensuring compliance with legal standards and broadening audience reach. By prioritizing accessibility, organizations can create inclusive content that resonates with diverse viewers, ultimately leading to improved viewer satisfaction and engagement.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with stakeholders to understand the specific needs and objectives related to video accessibility.
  
- **Content Evaluation**: Review a specified number of video materials ({{number_of_videos}}) for:
  - Accuracy and presence of captions and subtitles.
  - Visual clarity, including color contrast and text legibility.
  - Identification of any barriers that may hinder viewer engagement.

- **Accessibility Standards Assessment**: Compare the evaluated videos against established accessibility standards (e.g., WCAG 2.1, ADA compliance).

- **Reporting**: Prepare a detailed report summarizing findings, including:
  - Areas of compliance and non-compliance.
  - Specific recommendations for improvement.
  - Best practices for future video content creation.

- **Follow-Up Consultation**: Schedule a follow-up meeting to discuss the report findings and recommendations with stakeholders.
