
# Project Plan Template: Employee Onboarding Training Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Employee Onboarding Training Video project aims to create a comprehensive and engaging video resource that will facilitate the onboarding process for new hires. The primary objective of this project is to develop a high-quality video that introduces new employees to the company's policies, culture, and expectations, ensuring they feel welcomed and informed from day one. This project will involve collaboration with key stakeholders to gather relevant content, including interviews with team members, insights into company values, and an overview of operational procedures. The key outcomes of this project include a polished final video that educates and inspires new employees, enhances employee retention and satisfaction, and contributes to a more cohesive workplace environment. By streamlining the onboarding process, this video will serve as an essential tool for integrating new hires into the company culture and aligning them with organizational goals.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Define project objectives and goals.
  - Identify key stakeholders and project team members.
  
- **Content Development**
  - Conduct interviews with team members and leadership to gather insights.
  - Collect existing onboarding materials and resources.
  - Develop a script that outlines the video content and flow.

- **Video Production**
  - Plan and schedule video shoots, including location and equipment needs.
  - Film interviews and other relevant footage.
  - Edit the video to ensure a polished and professional final product.

- **Review and Feedback**
  - Present the draft video to stakeholders for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on stakeholder input.

- **Final Delivery**
  - Produce the final version of the video.
  - Distribute the video to relevant departments and new hires.
  - Gather feedback from new hires on the effectiveness of the video.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kick-off**
  - Deliverable: Project charter and stakeholder list.
  
- **Milestone 2: Content Development Completion**
  - Deliverable: Finalized script and content outline.

- **Milestone 3: Video Production Completion**
  - Deliverable: Raw footage and initial video draft.

- **Milestone 4: Review and Feedback**
  - Deliverable: Feedback report and revised video draft.

- **Milestone 5: Final Video Delivery**
  - Deliverable: Completed Employee Onboarding Training Video.
