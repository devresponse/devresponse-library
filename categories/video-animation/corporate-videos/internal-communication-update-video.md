
# Project Plan Template: Internal Communication Update Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Internal Communication Update Video project aims to create a professional and engaging video that effectively conveys essential updates, announcements, or changes within {{Organization Name}}. The primary objective of this project is to enhance internal communication by ensuring that all team members are informed and aligned with the latest developments. This video will serve as a vital tool for fostering a sense of unity and promoting transparency within the organization. 

The project will involve close collaboration with key stakeholders to gather crucial information, crafting a compelling script that resonates with the audience, and producing a high-quality video that reflects {{Organization Name}}’s corporate identity. Key outcomes include improved employee engagement, a clearer understanding of organizational changes, and a strengthened internal culture. By utilizing visuals, graphics, and a clear narrative, this video will not only inform but also motivate the internal team, making it an invaluable asset for {{Organization Name}}’s corporate communication strategy.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**: 
  - Kick-off meeting with stakeholders to define objectives and gather initial input.
  
- **Information Gathering**: 
  - Collaborate with department heads to collect key updates and announcements.
  - Conduct interviews with selected employees for personal insights and testimonials.

- **Script Development**: 
  - Draft a compelling script that incorporates gathered information.
  - Review and revise the script based on feedback from stakeholders.

- **Video Production**: 
  - Plan and schedule video shoots, including location scouting and talent selection.
  - Capture high-quality video footage and audio.
  - Edit the video to include graphics, animations, and branding elements.

- **Review and Approval**: 
  - Present the first draft of the video to stakeholders for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on feedback and finalize the video.

- **Distribution**: 
  - Develop a distribution plan to share the video with all employees.
  - Monitor engagement and gather feedback post-release.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Project Kick-off Meeting
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and project charter.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Information Gathering
  - Deliverable: Summary report of key updates and insights.

- **Milestone 3**: Script Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized script document.

- **Milestone 4**: Video Production Completion
  - Deliverable: Raw video footage.

- **Milestone 5**: First Draft Review
  - Deliverable: First draft of the edited video.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Video Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized video ready for distribution.

- **Milestone 7**: Video Distribution
  - Deliverable: Distribution report and engagement metrics.
