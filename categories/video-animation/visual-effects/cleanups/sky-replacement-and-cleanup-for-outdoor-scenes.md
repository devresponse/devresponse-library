
# Project Plan Template: Sky Replacement and Cleanup for Outdoor Scenes

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Sky Replacement and Cleanup for Outdoor Scenes" project aims to transform outdoor footage by replacing dull or overexposed skies with vibrant, realistic alternatives that significantly enhance the visual appeal of the scenes. This project will not only focus on sky replacement but will also involve meticulous cleanup of unwanted elements within the footage, such as distracting objects or blemishes that detract from the overall quality. The primary objective is to create a polished final product that captivates the audience and elevates the storytelling aspect of the video. Key outcomes include a high-quality video that showcases enhanced outdoor scenes, ready for client review and approval. The project will involve initial assessments of the footage, selection of appropriate sky images, and detailed adjustments to ensure seamless integration, resulting in a visually stunning final product that meets the client's expectations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Review the provided outdoor footage to identify areas requiring sky replacement and cleanup.
- **Sky Selection**: Curate a collection of vibrant and realistic sky images that fit the mood and tone of the footage.
- **Sky Replacement**: Implement sky replacement techniques to ensure a natural look and feel, adjusting colors and lighting as necessary.
- **Cleanup Process**: Identify and remove unwanted elements within the scene, including distracting objects, blemishes, and any other imperfections.
- **Integration**: Ensure seamless integration of the new sky and cleaned-up elements with the existing footage, maintaining consistency in lighting and perspective.
- **Client Review**: Present the enhanced footage to the client for feedback and approval, making any necessary adjustments based on their input.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the client with the final high-quality video, ensuring all enhancements are polished and meet the project specifications.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Assessment Completed
  - Deliverable: Assessment report outlining areas for improvement.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Sky Selection Finalized
  - Deliverable: A curated selection of sky images for client approval.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Sky Replacement Completed
  - Deliverable: Draft video with replaced skies for initial review.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Cleanup Process Completed
  - Deliverable: Draft video with all unwanted elements removed.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Client Review and Feedback
  - Deliverable: Feedback report from the client.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Final Adjustments Made
  - Deliverable: Revised video incorporating client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 7**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: High-quality final video ready for distribution.
