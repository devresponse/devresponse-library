
# Project Plan Template: Object Tracking Cleanup for VFX Shots

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Object Tracking Cleanup for VFX Shots" project aims to enhance the quality of visual effects in video and animation by meticulously addressing object tracking issues. The primary objective is to identify and rectify artifacts or inconsistencies that may disrupt viewer immersion. This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of the provided footage, allowing us to pinpoint problematic areas that require attention. By employing advanced cleanup techniques, we will remove unwanted elements and ensure that the motion of objects appears fluid and natural. The key outcomes of this project include delivering polished VFX shots that not only meet industry standards but also elevate the overall production value. Ultimately, our goal is to refine the visual storytelling, ensuring that the final product captivates audiences and aligns with the client's vision. Through a thorough review process and rigorous quality assurance, we will guarantee that all deliverables are of the highest quality, contributing to a seamless viewing experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Footage Review**: Conduct a thorough review of the provided VFX footage to identify areas requiring cleanup.
- **Artifact Identification**: Analyze the footage to pinpoint specific artifacts or inconsistencies in object tracking.
- **Cleanup Techniques**: Apply advanced techniques to remove unwanted elements and correct tracking issues, ensuring that the motion of objects is fluid and natural.
- **Quality Assurance**: Implement a rigorous quality assurance process to verify that all deliverables meet the client's expectations and industry standards.
- **Client Feedback**: Present the cleaned-up shots to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments based on their input.
- **Final Delivery**: Deliver the final polished VFX shots in the agreed format, ensuring they align with the client's vision and requirements.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Client Review and Feedback
