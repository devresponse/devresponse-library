
# Project Plan Template: Product Placement Enhancement with CGI

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Product Placement Enhancement with CGI" project aims to revolutionize the integration of branded products into live-action footage through the innovative use of Computer-Generated Imagery (CGI). The primary objective is to create a seamless visual experience where products are naturally embedded within scenes, enhancing viewer engagement without appearing forced. This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of existing live-action footage, employing precise match moving techniques to accurately track camera movements, and developing high-quality CGI models of the products. Key outcomes include a polished final video that not only showcases the products realistically but also contributes to the narrative and aesthetic of the overall project. By leveraging sophisticated visual effects, this service is tailored for filmmakers and advertisers seeking to elevate their content and create memorable brand experiences that resonate with audiences.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with stakeholders to understand project requirements, target audience, and specific product details.
  
- **Footage Analysis**: Review the live-action footage to identify key scenes for product placement and assess camera movements.

- **Match Moving**: Utilize advanced match moving techniques to track the camera's movement throughout the selected scenes, ensuring accurate integration of CGI elements.

- **CGI Model Creation**: Design and develop high-quality 3D models of the products to be placed within the footage, ensuring they are visually appealing and realistic.

- **Integration**: Seamlessly integrate the CGI models into the live-action footage, adjusting lighting, shadows, and reflections to match the environment.

- **Review and Feedback**: Present the initial integration to stakeholders for feedback and make necessary adjustments based on their input.

- **Final Rendering**: Render the final video with all integrated elements, ensuring high quality and adherence to project specifications.

- **Delivery**: Provide the final polished video to stakeholders in the required formats.
