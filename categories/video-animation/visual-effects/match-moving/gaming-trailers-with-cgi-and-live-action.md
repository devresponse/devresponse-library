
# Project Plan Template: Gaming Trailers with CGI and Live Action

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Gaming Trailers with CGI and Live Action" project aims to create dynamic and engaging trailers that effectively showcase the unique gameplay mechanics and narrative aspects of the game. The primary objective is to captivate the audience and communicate the essence of the game through a seamless blend of CGI elements and live-action footage. This project will involve a comprehensive pre-production planning phase, where key scenes and storyboards will be developed to outline the narrative flow. Following this, we will film live-action footage that will serve as the foundation for the trailer. The integration of CGI characters and environments will be meticulously executed to ensure they match the lighting and perspective of the live footage, creating a cohesive visual experience. In the post-production phase, we will focus on editing, color grading, and sound design to enhance the overall impact of the trailer. The final deliverable will be a polished trailer ready for distribution across various platforms, designed to attract gamers and generate excitement about the game. Key outcomes include increased visibility for the game, heightened audience engagement, and a compelling promotional tool that effectively conveys the game's unique features.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production Planning**
  - Develop a project timeline and budget
  - Create storyboards outlining key scenes
  - Conduct casting for live-action actors
  - Scout and secure filming locations
  - Plan CGI elements and their integration with live footage

- **Production**
  - Film live-action footage according to the storyboard
  - Capture high-quality audio during filming
  - Ensure proper lighting and camera angles for CGI integration

- **Post-Production**
  - Integrate CGI characters and environments into the live-action footage
  - Edit the footage to create a cohesive narrative
  - Perform color grading to enhance visual appeal
  - Design and mix sound effects and music to complement the visuals
  - Finalize the trailer for distribution

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Completion of Pre-Production Planning**
  - Deliverable: Finalized storyboards and production schedule

- **Milestone 2: Filming of Live-Action Footage**
  - Deliverable: Raw footage of live-action scenes

- **Milestone 3: Integration of CGI Elements**
  - Deliverable: First draft of the trailer with CGI integrated

- **Milestone 4: Post-Production Completion**
  - Deliverable: Final polished trailer ready for distribution
