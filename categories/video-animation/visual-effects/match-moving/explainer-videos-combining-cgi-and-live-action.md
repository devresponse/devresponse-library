
# Project Plan Template: Explainer Videos with CGI and Live Action

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Explainer Videos with CGI and Live Action" project aims to create high-quality explainer videos that effectively blend Computer-Generated Imagery (CGI) with live-action footage. The primary objective is to produce visually captivating content that simplifies complex concepts for businesses, making their messages more accessible and engaging to their target audience. This project will involve a comprehensive approach, starting with initial consultations to understand the client's vision and objectives. Following this, we will engage in storyboarding and script development to outline the narrative effectively. The production phase will encompass filming live-action sequences, creating CGI elements, and seamlessly integrating both components to ensure a cohesive final product. Key outcomes include a polished explainer video that not only informs but also captivates viewers, thereby enhancing the client's brand presence and communication strategy. By the end of this project, clients will have a powerful tool to convey their messages clearly and engagingly, ultimately driving better audience engagement and understanding.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Schedule meetings with the client to discuss their vision, objectives, and target audience.
  - Gather necessary materials and references from the client.

- **Storyboarding and Script Development**: 
  - Develop a detailed storyboard outlining the visual and narrative flow of the video.
  - Create a script that aligns with the storyboard and effectively communicates the intended message.

- **Production Phase**: 
  - **Live-Action Filming**: 
    - Organize and schedule filming sessions with actors and locations.
    - Capture high-quality live-action footage as per the storyboard.
  - **CGI Creation**: 
    - Design and develop CGI elements that complement the live-action footage.
    - Ensure that CGI elements are visually consistent with the overall aesthetic of the video.

- **Integration and Editing**: 
  - Seamlessly integrate CGI and live-action footage during the editing process.
  - Apply color grading, sound design, and visual effects to enhance the final product.

- **Client Review and Revisions**: 
  - Present the draft video to the client for feedback.
  - Implement necessary revisions based on client input.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Deliver the final polished explainer video in the required formats.
  - Provide any additional assets or materials as agreed upon.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Consultation notes and project brief.

  - Deliverable: Finalized storyboard and script document.

  - Deliverable: Raw footage from filming sessions.

- **Milestone 4**: CGI Elements Completed
  - Deliverable: Rendered CGI assets ready for integration.

- **Milestone 5**: First Draft of Video Completed
  - Deliverable: Draft version of the explainer video for client review.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Video Delivered
  - Deliverable: Final polished explainer video in agreed formats.
