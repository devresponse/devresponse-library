
# Project Plan Template: Seamless CGI Integration for Product Commercials

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Seamless CGI Integration for Product Commercials" project aims to revolutionize the way products are showcased in commercials by seamlessly blending Computer-Generated Imagery (CGI) with live-action footage. Our primary objective is to create visually stunning and engaging content that captivates audiences and effectively communicates the product's unique features. This project will involve a close collaboration with the client to fully understand their vision and objectives, ensuring that the CGI elements enhance the storytelling experience rather than detract from it. Key outcomes will include the development of high-quality CGI assets that are meticulously integrated into the live-action scenes using advanced match-moving techniques. This will guarantee that all CGI elements are perfectly aligned with the real-world environment, resulting in a polished final product that elevates the commercial's visual appeal. Ultimately, our goal is to create a memorable and impactful advertisement that not only showcases the product innovatively but also resonates with viewers, driving engagement and sales.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a series of meetings with the client to gather requirements, understand the product, and define the creative vision.
- **Script Development**: Collaborate with the client to create a script that effectively integrates CGI elements into the narrative.
- **Storyboarding**: Develop detailed storyboards that outline the visual flow of the commercial, highlighting where CGI will be integrated.
- **CGI Asset Creation**: Design and produce CGI elements that align with the product and the overall aesthetic of the commercial.
- **Match Moving**: Utilize advanced match-moving techniques to ensure that CGI elements are accurately aligned with the live-action footage.
- **Filming**: Coordinate and execute the live-action shoot, ensuring that all necessary footage is captured for integration with CGI.
- **Post-Production**: Edit the footage, incorporating CGI elements and ensuring seamless integration, color grading, and sound design.
- **Client Review**: Present the initial cut to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Final Delivery**: Deliver the completed commercial in the required formats and resolutions, along with any additional assets as agreed upon.
