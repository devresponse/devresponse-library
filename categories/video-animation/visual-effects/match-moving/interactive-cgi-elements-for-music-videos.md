
# Project Plan Template: Interactive CGI Elements for Music Videos

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Interactive CGI Elements for Music Videos" project aims to revolutionize the artistic quality of music videos by integrating cutting-edge interactive CGI elements that harmoniously blend with live-action footage. The primary objective is to enhance the storytelling aspect of music videos, creating a captivating visual experience that resonates with audiences. By collaborating closely with the music video production team, we will gain a deep understanding of the artistic vision and narrative, ensuring that our CGI elements align perfectly with the intended message. Key outcomes of this project include the successful design and animation of unique CGI objects and environments that dynamically respond to the music, as well as the seamless integration of these elements through advanced match moving techniques. This project is tailored for artists eager to push the boundaries of traditional music videos, resulting in a memorable and immersive viewing experience that stands out in the competitive landscape of music entertainment.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production Phase**
  - Initial meetings with the music video production team to discuss artistic vision and narrative.
  - Development of a creative brief outlining the desired CGI elements and their interactions with live-action footage.
  - Storyboarding and conceptual design of CGI objects and environments.

- **Production Phase**
  - On-set collaboration with the filming crew to capture camera movements and angles.
  - Implementation of match moving techniques to track camera movements accurately.
  - Creation of CGI elements based on the approved designs, ensuring they align with the music's rhythm and mood.

- **Post-Production Phase**
  - Integration of CGI elements into the live-action footage using advanced compositing techniques.
  - Color grading and visual effects enhancements to ensure a cohesive look.
  - Final review and adjustments based on feedback from the music video production team.

- **Delivery**
  - Provide the final edited music video with integrated CGI elements in the required formats.
  - Documentation of the match moving process and any relevant technical notes for future reference.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1:** Completion of Pre-Production Phase
  - Deliverable: Creative brief and storyboards.

- **Milestone 2:** On-Set Filming and Match Moving
  - Deliverable: Captured camera movement data and initial CGI prototypes.

- **Milestone 3:** Completion of CGI Creation
  - Deliverable: Finalized CGI elements ready for integration.

- **Milestone 4:** Post-Production and Final Review
  - Deliverable: Edited music video with integrated CGI elements.

- **Milestone 5:** Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Final project report and documentation.
