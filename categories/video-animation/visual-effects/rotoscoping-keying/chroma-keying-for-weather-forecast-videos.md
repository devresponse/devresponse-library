
# Project Plan Template: Chroma Keying for Weather Forecast Videos

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Chroma Keying for Weather Forecast Videos" project aims to revolutionize the presentation of weather forecasts by employing advanced chroma keying techniques. The primary objective is to replace traditional green screen backgrounds with visually engaging and contextually relevant weather visuals that accurately depict forecasted conditions. This project will enhance viewer engagement and understanding by integrating high-quality weather footage into existing video content. Key outcomes include the delivery of polished, professional-grade videos that not only inform but also captivate the audience. The selected freelancer will collaborate closely with the client to ensure that the visuals align with specific weather scenarios, while also managing color grading and lighting adjustments for a seamless final product. By the end of this project, we expect to have a series of weather forecast videos that stand out in quality and presentation, ultimately improving audience retention and satisfaction.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to discuss project objectives, specific weather scenarios, and desired visual outcomes.
- **Sourcing Footage**: Research and acquire high-quality weather footage that aligns with the forecasted conditions.
- **Chroma Keying**: Utilize advanced chroma keying techniques to replace green screen backgrounds with sourced weather visuals.
- **Integration**: Seamlessly integrate the new visuals with existing video content, ensuring a cohesive look and feel.
- **Color Grading**: Adjust colors to match the original footage, ensuring consistency in lighting and shadows.
- **Review and Feedback**: Present initial drafts to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the final edited videos in the required formats, ensuring they meet all quality standards.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Project brief document outlining objectives and scenarios.
