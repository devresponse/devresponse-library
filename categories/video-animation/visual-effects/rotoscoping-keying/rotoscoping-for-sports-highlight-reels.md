
# Project Plan: Rotoscoping for Sports Highlight Reels

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Rotoscoping for Sports Highlight Reels" project aims to leverage advanced rotoscoping techniques to create captivating highlight reels that showcase athletes and key moments from sports footage. The primary objective is to meticulously trace the movements of athletes frame by frame, ensuring a seamless integration of visual effects that enhances the overall viewing experience. By focusing on dynamic highlights, the project seeks to capture the excitement and intensity of the sport, ultimately elevating viewer engagement. Key outcomes include a polished highlight reel that emphasizes athletes' skills and delivers a visually stunning narrative. The project will require a high level of attention to detail and proficiency in video editing software, with specific milestones and deadlines established to ensure timely delivery of the final product.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production Planning**
  - Define project requirements and objectives.
  - Gather and review existing sports footage.
  - Develop a storyboard outlining key moments to highlight.

- **Rotoscoping Process**
  - Import footage into video editing software.
  - Frame-by-frame tracing of athletes and key moments.
  - Apply visual effects to enhance the isolated footage.

- **Post-Production**
  - Edit and compile the highlight reel.
  - Incorporate sound effects and background music.
  - Review and refine the final product based on feedback.

- **Delivery**
  - Export the final highlight reel in required formats.
  - Provide a presentation of the highlight reel to stakeholders.
  - Gather feedback for potential revisions.
