
# Project Plan Template: Rotoscoping for Animated Music Videos

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Rotoscoping for Animated Music Videos" project aims to create a visually captivating music video that seamlessly integrates live-action footage with animated elements. The primary objective is to enhance the narrative of the music through meticulous rotoscoping techniques, which involve analyzing the provided footage, identifying key frames, and tracing over them to produce high-quality animated overlays. By employing advanced chroma keying methods, we will effectively remove unwanted backgrounds, allowing for a dynamic and engaging integration of animated components. The key outcomes of this project include a polished final music video that not only showcases the artistic vision of the music but also captivates audiences through stunning visual storytelling. This project will ultimately elevate the viewer's experience, ensuring that the animated elements enhance rather than distract from the live-action footage.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Kick-off meeting with stakeholders to define project goals and expectations.
  - Review of the provided live-action footage and music track.

- **Pre-Production**
  - Develop a storyboard outlining the integration of animated elements.
  - Identify key frames for rotoscoping and animation.

- **Production**
  - Perform rotoscoping on identified key frames, creating animated overlays.
  - Utilize chroma keying techniques to remove unwanted backgrounds from live-action footage.
  - Regular updates and reviews with stakeholders to ensure alignment with the project vision.

- **Post-Production**
  - Finalize the integration of animated elements with live-action footage.
  - Conduct color grading and audio synchronization.
  - Prepare the final output in the required format for distribution.

- **Project Closure**
  - Deliver the final polished music video to stakeholders.
  - Conduct a project review meeting to gather feedback and assess project success.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kick-off**
  - Deliverable: Project initiation report and meeting notes.

- **Milestone 2: Storyboard Completion**
  - Deliverable: Finalized storyboard for the music video.

- **Milestone 3: Rotoscoping Completion**
  - Deliverable: Completed animated overlays for all key frames.

- **Milestone 4: Chroma Keying Completion**
  - Deliverable: Finalized live-action footage with backgrounds removed.

- **Milestone 5: Final Video Delivery**
  - Deliverable: Polished music video ready for distribution.
