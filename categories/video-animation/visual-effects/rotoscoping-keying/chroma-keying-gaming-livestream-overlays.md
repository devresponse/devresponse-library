
# Project Plan Template: Chroma Keying for Gaming Livestream Overlays

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Chroma Keying for Gaming Livestream Overlays" project aims to create custom overlays that enhance the visual experience of gaming livestreams through advanced chroma keying techniques. The primary objective is to seamlessly integrate players into dynamic and visually appealing themed backgrounds that resonate with the specific games being streamed. By collaborating closely with the client, we will capture their vision and translate it into high-quality overlays that not only elevate the aesthetic appeal but also improve viewer engagement. Key outcomes of this project include the delivery of multiple overlay designs tailored for various gaming scenarios, ensuring versatility and a cohesive look across different streams. Ultimately, this project is designed for clients who aspire to enhance their streaming presence with professional-grade visual effects that captivate their audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Client Consultation**: Engage with the client to understand their vision, preferences, and specific requirements for the overlays.
- **Theme Selection**: Identify and select appropriate backgrounds that align with the themes of the games being streamed.
- **Design Development**: Create multiple overlay designs, ensuring each is optimized for different gaming scenarios (e.g., action, adventure, RPG).
- **Chroma Keying Process**: Utilize advanced chroma keying techniques to integrate players into the selected backgrounds seamlessly.
- **Quality Assurance**: Review and refine the overlays to ensure a professional finish, meeting the client’s expectations.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the client with all overlay designs in the required formats, along with usage guidelines.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Client Consultation
  - Deliverable: Summary of client requirements and vision document.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Theme Selection and Background Approval
  - Deliverable: List of selected backgrounds with client approval.

- **Milestone 3**: Overlay Design Development
  - Deliverable: Draft versions of overlay designs for client review.

- **Milestone 4**: Chroma Keying Execution
  - Deliverable: Completed overlays integrated with chroma keying.

- **Milestone 5**: Quality Assurance and Final Revisions
  - Deliverable: Finalized overlays after client feedback.

- **Milestone 6**: Project Closure and Delivery
  - Deliverable: All final overlay designs and usage guidelines delivered to the client.
