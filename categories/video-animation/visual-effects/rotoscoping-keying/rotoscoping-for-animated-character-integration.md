
# Project Plan Template: Rotoscoping for Animated Character Integration

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Rotoscoping for Animated Character Integration" project aims to seamlessly blend animated characters into live-action footage, enhancing the storytelling experience through advanced rotoscoping techniques. The primary objective is to create a visually stunning final product that captivates audiences and elevates the narrative quality of the content. This project will involve a meticulous analysis of the live-action footage, where each frame will be carefully traced to ensure that animated characters interact naturally with their surroundings. Key outcomes include a polished video that maintains consistency in color and lighting, making the animated elements feel organic and engaging. The final deliverable will be suitable for various applications, including films, commercials, and online content, ultimately contributing to a more immersive viewer experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Analysis of Live-Action Footage**: Review and assess the provided live-action footage to identify key scenes for character integration.
- **Rotoscoping Process**: 
  - Frame-by-frame tracing of animated characters to ensure precise outlines.
  - Integration of animated characters into the live-action footage, ensuring natural interaction with the environment.
- **Color Correction**: Adjust colors of the animated characters to match the live-action footage, ensuring visual harmony.
- **Lighting Adjustments**: Modify lighting on animated characters to align with the lighting conditions of the live-action scenes.
- **Final Review and Edits**: Conduct a thorough review of the integrated footage, making necessary adjustments based on feedback.
- **Delivery of Final Product**: Provide the polished video in the required format, ready for distribution.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Live-Action Footage Analysis
  - **Deliverable**: Analysis Report
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Rotoscoping for Key Scenes
  - **Deliverable**: Draft Video with Integrated Characters
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Color Correction and Lighting Adjustments
  - **Deliverable**: Revised Video for Review
- **Milestone 4**: Final Review and Edits
  - **Deliverable**: Feedback Report
- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of Final Polished Video
  - **Deliverable**: Final Video File
