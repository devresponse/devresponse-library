
# Project Plan Template: Green Screen Editing for Product Commercials

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Green Screen Editing for Product Commercials" project aims to elevate the visual quality and professionalism of product advertisements through meticulous green screen editing techniques. The primary objective is to remove existing green screen backgrounds from provided video footage and replace them with high-quality, contextually appropriate backgrounds that enhance the showcased product. Key outcomes include a polished final cut that meets industry standards, ensuring the product stands out in a competitive market. The project will require a keen eye for detail, an understanding of color grading, and the ability to integrate the product seamlessly with the new background. Additionally, the selected freelancer will engage in multiple revisions based on client feedback, emphasizing the importance of clear communication throughout the editing process. This project not only aims to improve the aesthetic appeal of the commercials but also to drive engagement and conversion rates for the products being advertised.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Video Footage Review**: Assess the provided video footage to understand the context and requirements for background replacement.
- **Green Screen Removal**: Utilize advanced rotoscoping techniques to meticulously remove the green screen backgrounds from the footage.
- **Background Selection**: Collaborate with the client to select high-quality backgrounds that are contextually appropriate for the product being showcased.
- **Integration and Color Grading**: Ensure seamless integration of the product with the new background, applying color grading techniques to match lighting and color tones.
- **Revisions**: Implement a structured revision process, allowing for client feedback and adjustments to achieve the desired look and feel.
- **Final Output**: Deliver a polished final cut that adheres to industry standards, ready for distribution across various platforms.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Review and Feedback
  - Deliverable: Assessment report of video footage and proposed background options.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Green Screen Removal Completion
  - Deliverable: First draft of edited footage with green screen removed.

- **Milestone 3**: Background Integration
  - Deliverable: Edited footage with new backgrounds integrated.

- **Milestone 4**: Color Grading and Final Adjustments
  - Deliverable: Color-graded footage ready for client review.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Polished final cut delivered in required formats.
