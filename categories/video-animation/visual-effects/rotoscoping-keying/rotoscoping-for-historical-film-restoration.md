
# Project Plan Template: Rotoscoping for Historical Film Restoration

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Rotoscoping for Historical Film Restoration" project aims to enhance and restore historical film footage through the intricate process of rotoscoping. Our primary objective is to meticulously trace over original frames to isolate and improve specific elements within the footage, thereby increasing clarity and visual appeal. This project will involve the removal of unwanted artifacts, enhancement of colors, and the preservation of historical integrity, ensuring that the restored footage resonates with modern audiences. Key outcomes include a polished version of the original footage that is visually engaging and ready for distribution or archival purposes. This project is tailored for film historians, archivists, and entities dedicated to preserving and presenting historical content in a contemporary context.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Review the historical film footage to identify areas requiring restoration and enhancement.
- **Rotoscoping Process**:
  - Frame-by-frame analysis and tracing of key elements in the footage.
  - Removal of unwanted artifacts and blemishes.
  - Color correction and enhancement to improve visual appeal.
- **Quality Assurance**: Conduct multiple reviews of the restored footage to ensure accuracy and historical integrity.
- **Final Output**: Deliver a high-quality, polished version of the restored footage in the specified format.
- **Documentation**: Provide a detailed report outlining the restoration process, techniques used, and any challenges encountered.
