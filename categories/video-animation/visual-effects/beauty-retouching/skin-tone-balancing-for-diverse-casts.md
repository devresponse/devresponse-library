
# Project Plan Template: Skin Tone Balancing for Diverse Casts

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Skin Tone Balancing for Diverse Casts" project aims to enhance the visual quality of video animations by ensuring that characters of diverse backgrounds are represented with consistent and natural skin tones. This project recognizes the importance of inclusivity in visual media and seeks to correct any discrepancies in lighting and color grading that may affect the appearance of skin tones. The primary objective is to analyze each character's skin tone and make necessary adjustments to achieve a harmonious look across the entire cast. Key outcomes include a visually appealing animation that respects and celebrates diversity, improved audience engagement, and a final product that meets the highest standards of quality. By employing a keen eye for detail and a solid understanding of color theory, the project will deliver results that not only enhance the aesthetic appeal but also promote representation in animation.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Character Analysis**: Review each character's skin tone in the animation to identify discrepancies and areas for improvement.
- **Color Correction**: Utilize color grading techniques to adjust skin tones, ensuring consistency across all characters.
- **Lighting Adjustments**: Analyze and correct lighting effects that may distort the appearance of skin tones.
- **Quality Assurance**: Conduct multiple rounds of review to ensure that the adjustments meet the desired standards of natural representation.
- **Client Feedback**: Present interim results to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments based on their input.
- **Final Output**: Deliver the final animation with balanced skin tones, ensuring it aligns with the project objectives.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Character Analysis
  - Deliverable: Character Analysis Report
- **Milestone 2**: Initial Color Correction
  - Deliverable: Draft Animation with Adjusted Skin Tones
- **Milestone 3**: Lighting Adjustments Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised Animation for Client Review
- **Milestone 4**: Final Quality Assurance Review
  - Deliverable: Final Animation with Balanced Skin Tones
