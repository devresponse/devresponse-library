
# Project Plan Template: Teenage Skin Retouching for Youth-Oriented Ads

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Teenage Skin Retouching for Youth-Oriented Ads" project aims to enhance the visual appeal of teenage models featured in video advertisements while preserving a fresh and natural look that resonates with the target audience. The primary objective is to execute delicate skin retouching techniques that adjust skin tone, texture, and blemishes, ensuring that the final output reflects the youthful essence of the models without compromising authenticity. This project will involve close collaboration with the client to fully understand their vision and preferences, allowing for tailored enhancements that align with the brand's identity. Key outcomes include high-resolution retouched images that meet industry standards for quality and aesthetics, ready for seamless integration into video content. By focusing on subtle and appropriate enhancements, this project will not only elevate the visual representation of the models but also foster a positive connection with the youth demographic.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss project objectives, vision, and specific preferences for retouching.
- **Image Selection**: Collaborate with the client to select the images of teenage models that will undergo retouching.
- **Skin Retouching Process**:
  - Adjust skin tone to ensure a natural and even appearance.
  - Smooth skin texture while preserving natural features.
  - Remove blemishes and imperfections without altering the model's youthful essence.
  - Ensure that the retouching aligns with the brand's aesthetic and target audience.
- **Client Review**: Present the initial retouched images to the client for feedback and adjustments.
- **Final Adjustments**: Implement any necessary changes based on client feedback.
- **Final Deliverables**: Provide the retouched images in high-resolution formats suitable for video integration.
- **Post-Project Review**: Conduct a debrief with the client to gather feedback and discuss potential future collaborations.
