
# Project Plan Template: Beauty Retouching for Wedding Video Animations

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Beauty Retouching for Wedding Video Animations" project aims to enhance the visual allure of wedding videos through specialized beauty retouching services. Our primary objective is to create a flawless appearance by softening skin tones, enhancing smiles, and applying a romantic, dreamy aesthetic that resonates with the emotions of the wedding day. This project will involve meticulous attention to detail, ensuring that each frame reflects the beauty and love shared during the event. Key outcomes include a polished final product that couples will cherish, showcasing advanced techniques in lighting enhancement, color grading, and overall image quality. By the end of this project, we aim to deliver a stunning video that encapsulates the essence of the wedding day, providing couples with a treasured keepsake that evokes the joy and romance of their special occasion.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the couple to understand their vision and preferences for the video retouching.
- **Footage Review**: Analyze the raw wedding video footage to identify areas for enhancement.
- **Beauty Retouching Techniques**:
  - Softening skin tones to achieve a flawless look.
  - Enhancing smiles to capture genuine emotions.
  - Applying a romantic and dreamy aesthetic throughout the video.
- **Color Grading**: Adjust colors to enhance the overall mood and feel of the video.
- **Lighting Enhancement**: Improve lighting in specific scenes to create a more inviting atmosphere.
- **Final Review**: Present the retouched video to the couple for feedback and make necessary adjustments.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final video in the desired format, ensuring high-quality output.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Consultation notes and agreed vision document.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Footage Review Completed
  - Deliverable: Analysis report highlighting areas for enhancement.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Beauty Retouching Completed
  - Deliverable: First draft of the retouched video.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Color Grading and Lighting Enhancement Completed
  - Deliverable: Updated video with color grading and lighting adjustments.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Review and Adjustments Completed
  - Deliverable: Final version of the retouched video.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Final video delivered in the agreed format and project completion report.
