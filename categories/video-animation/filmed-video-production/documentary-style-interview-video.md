
# Project Plan Template: Documentary-Style Interview Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Documentary-Style Interview Video project aims to produce a compelling visual narrative that captures the essence of the subject matter through insightful conversations with key individuals. The primary objective is to create a high-quality video that resonates with the target audience, effectively communicating the intended message. This project will involve a comprehensive pre-production phase, where we will identify interview subjects, develop a script or outline, and schedule filming dates. During the filming phase, we will employ professional videography techniques to capture both interviews and b-roll footage that enhances the narrative. Post-production will focus on editing the footage to create a cohesive story, incorporating graphics, sound design, and color correction to ensure a polished final product. Key outcomes include a visually engaging video that showcases personal stories, corporate values, or community initiatives, ultimately serving as a powerful tool for clients looking to connect with their audience on a deeper level.
