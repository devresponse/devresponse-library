
# Project Plan Template: Wedding Cinematic Film

## 1. Executive Summary

The Wedding Cinematic Film project aims to produce a stunningly crafted video that encapsulates the essence of a couple's wedding day. The primary objective is to create a high-quality cinematic film that not only documents the events of the day but also evokes the emotions felt by the couple and their guests. This project will involve comprehensive pre-production planning, including consultations with {{couple_name}} to understand their vision, preferred styles, and key moments they wish to highlight. On the wedding day, our professional videography team will capture all significant events, from the ceremony to the reception, ensuring that every emotional moment is documented. Post-production will include meticulous editing, color grading, and sound design to create a polished final product. Key outcomes of this project include a beautifully edited wedding film, a highlight reel for social sharing, and a seamless experience for the couple throughout the process. Ultimately, this project is designed to deliver a cherished keepsake that the couple can treasure for a lifetime.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production Planning**
  - Initial consultation with {{couple_name}} to discuss vision and preferences
  - Development of a shot list based on key moments identified by the couple
  - Coordination with other vendors (e.g., wedding planner, photographer) to ensure seamless coverage
  - Site visit to the wedding venue to plan for lighting and angles

- **Production**
  - Full-day coverage of the wedding day, including:
    - Preparation of the couple
    - Ceremony
    - Reception
    - Special moments (toasts, dances, etc.)
  - Use of high-quality cameras and equipment to capture high-definition footage

- **Post-Production**
  - Editing of the footage to create a cinematic narrative
  - Color grading to enhance visual appeal
  - Sound design, including music selection and audio mixing
  - Creation of a highlight reel (3-5 minutes) for social media sharing
  - Delivery of the final wedding film (15-30 minutes) in digital format

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1:** Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Document outlining couple's vision and preferences

- **Milestone 2:** Shot List Finalized
  - Deliverable: Approved shot list and production schedule

- **Milestone 3:** Wedding Day Coverage Completed
  - Deliverable: Raw footage captured on the wedding day

- **Milestone 4:** First Draft of Wedding Film Delivered
  - Deliverable: Initial edit of the wedding film for review

- **Milestone 5:** Final Wedding Film Delivered
  - Deliverable: Polished wedding film and highlight reel in digital format
