
# Project Plan Template: Good Manners and Etiquette

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Good Manners and Etiquette" project aims to create a series of engaging animations designed to teach children the importance of good manners and social etiquette. The primary objective is to produce colorful and lively animations that cover various scenarios, such as greeting others, sharing, saying 'please' and 'thank you', and understanding personal space. By utilizing relatable characters and entertaining storylines, the project seeks to stimulate children's understanding of social interactions and encourage positive behavior in a fun and memorable way. Key outcomes include a collection of multiple short animations, each focusing on a specific aspect of good manners, which will serve as educational tools for parents and educators. Ultimately, the project aspires to foster essential life skills in children, making learning about etiquette enjoyable and impactful.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Development**: Conduct research on effective teaching methods for children regarding good manners and etiquette. Identify key scenarios to be animated.
  
- **Scriptwriting**: Develop engaging scripts for each animation that clearly convey the intended message while being age-appropriate and entertaining.

- **Character Design**: Create relatable characters that will resonate with children. This includes designing their appearance, personality traits, and roles in the animations.

- **Animation Production**: Produce high-quality animations for each scenario. This includes storyboarding, voice-over recording, animation, and post-production editing.

- **Testing and Feedback**: Conduct focus group testing with children and parents to gather feedback on the animations. Make necessary adjustments based on the feedback received.

- **Final Delivery**: Compile all animations into a cohesive series, ensuring they are accessible for various platforms (e.g., online streaming, educational apps).

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Development  
  **Deliverable**: Research report and list of selected scenarios.

- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of Scripts  
  **Deliverable**: Completed scripts for all animations.

- **Milestone 3**: Character Designs Approved  
  **Deliverable**: Character design sheets for all main characters.

- **Milestone 4**: Animation Production Completed  
  **Deliverable**: All animations produced and ready for review.

- **Milestone 5**: Focus Group Testing Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Feedback report and revised animations based on testing.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Product Delivery  
  **Deliverable**: Complete series of animations delivered in required formats.
