
# Project Plan Template: Product Launch Explainer Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Product Launch Explainer Video project is to create a captivating 2D animated video that effectively communicates the features and benefits of the new product, {{Product Name}}. This project aims to simplify complex product details, making them easily digestible for potential customers. By utilizing engaging visuals and a clear narrative, the video will highlight the unique selling points of {{Product Name}}, ensuring that viewers understand its value proposition. The final output will be a polished, professional video that can be utilized across various marketing channels, including social media, websites, and presentations. Key outcomes include increased product awareness, enhanced customer understanding, and improved engagement metrics across digital platforms. The project will follow a structured timeline encompassing script development, storyboarding, animation, and final revisions, ensuring that the end product meets the client's expectations and marketing objectives.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Product Launch Explainer Video includes the following key components:

- **Script Development**: Collaborate with the client to gather information about {{Product Name}} and draft a compelling script that outlines the product's features, benefits, and unique selling points.
  
- **Storyboarding**: Create a visual storyboard that maps out the scenes of the video, illustrating how the script will be translated into animation. This will include key visuals, transitions, and timing for each segment.
  
- **Animation Production**: Develop high-quality 2D animations based on the approved storyboard. This phase will involve designing characters, backgrounds, and other visual elements that align with the brand's identity.
  
- **Voiceover Recording**: Select a professional voiceover artist to record the script, ensuring that the tone and delivery match the intended audience and message.
  
- **Sound Design**: Incorporate background music and sound effects to enhance the overall viewing experience and maintain viewer engagement.
  
- **Final Revisions**: Present the draft video to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions to ensure the final product aligns with the client's vision and objectives.
  
- **Delivery**: Provide the final video in multiple formats suitable for various marketing channels, including social media, websites, and presentations.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Script Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized script for the explainer video.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Storyboard Approval
  - Deliverable: Approved storyboard visualizing the video flow.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Animation Draft
  - Deliverable: First draft of the animated video for client review.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Voiceover and Sound Design
  - Deliverable: Completed video with voiceover and sound effects integrated.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Video Delivery
  - Deliverable: Final polished video delivered in required formats.
