
# Project Plan Template: Employee Onboarding Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Employee Onboarding Video project aims to create a captivating 2D animated explainer video that effectively communicates essential information to new employees. The primary objective of this project is to ensure that new hires feel welcomed and informed from day one by providing them with a comprehensive introduction to the company's policies, culture, and expectations. This video will utilize vibrant visuals and engaging storytelling techniques to simplify complex concepts, making it easier for employees to grasp the company's values and operational procedures. Key outcomes of this project include a polished and professional video that enhances the onboarding experience, fosters a sense of belonging, and aligns new employees with the company's mission. By collaborating with stakeholders to gather necessary content, scriptwriting, storyboarding, animation, and final editing, we will deliver a high-quality product that not only informs but also inspires new employees to integrate seamlessly into the company culture.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Content Gathering**: Collaborate with HR and department heads to gather necessary information regarding company policies, culture, and expectations.
- **Scriptwriting**: Develop a compelling script that conveys the key messages in an engaging manner.
- **Storyboarding**: Create a storyboard that outlines the visual flow of the video, ensuring alignment with the script and key messages.
- **Animation**: Produce high-quality 2D animations that bring the storyboard to life, incorporating vibrant visuals and engaging characters.
- **Voiceover**: Record a professional voiceover that complements the animation and enhances the storytelling.
- **Editing**: Finalize the video through editing, ensuring smooth transitions, appropriate pacing, and alignment with the overall objectives.
- **Feedback and Revisions**: Present the draft video to stakeholders for feedback and make necessary revisions to ensure satisfaction.
- **Final Delivery**: Deliver the completed video in the required formats for distribution to new employees.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Content Gathering Completion
  - Deliverable: Compiled content document from stakeholders.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Script Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized script approved by stakeholders.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Storyboard Approval
  - Deliverable: Approved storyboard outlining the visual flow.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Animation Completion
  - Deliverable: Completed animated video draft.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Voiceover Recording
  - Deliverable: Recorded voiceover integrated into the video.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Final Video Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized video approved by stakeholders.
  
- **Milestone 7**: Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Project closure report and video distribution plan.
