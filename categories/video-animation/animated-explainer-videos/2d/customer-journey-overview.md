
# Project Plan Template: Customer Journey Overview

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Customer Journey Overview" project aims to create a captivating 2D animated explainer video that effectively illustrates the various stages of the customer journey. The primary objective of this project is to provide businesses with a powerful visual tool that communicates how potential customers progress from initial awareness to the final purchase decision. This video will clearly define each stage of the journey, showcasing critical touchpoints that influence customer behavior and decision-making. By utilizing vibrant visuals and concise messaging, the animation will engage viewers and simplify complex concepts, making it easier for businesses to convey their value propositions. Key outcomes include an enhanced understanding of the customer journey, improved marketing efforts, and a polished, professional video ready for distribution across various platforms. Ultimately, this project will empower businesses to connect more effectively with their audience and drive conversions.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct thorough research on customer journey stages relevant to the client's industry. Gather insights on customer behavior and decision-making processes.
  
- **Script Development**: Create a compelling script that outlines the narrative of the customer journey, highlighting key touchpoints and messaging.

- **Storyboarding**: Develop a storyboard that visually represents the script, detailing each scene and animation sequence.

- **Animation Production**: Produce high-quality 2D animations based on the approved storyboard, incorporating engaging visuals and sound effects.

- **Voiceover Recording**: Hire a professional voiceover artist to record the script, ensuring the tone and delivery align with the brand's voice.

- **Editing and Revisions**: Edit the animated video, incorporating feedback from stakeholders. Make necessary revisions to ensure the final product meets quality standards.

- **Final Delivery**: Provide the completed video in various formats suitable for distribution across multiple platforms (e.g., social media, website, presentations).

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Analysis  
  **Deliverable**: Research report summarizing customer journey insights.

- **Milestone 2**: Script Approval  
  **Deliverable**: Finalized script for the animated video.

- **Milestone 3**: Storyboard Approval  
  **Deliverable**: Approved storyboard detailing each scene.

- **Milestone 4**: Animation Production Completion  
  **Deliverable**: Draft version of the animated video.

- **Milestone 5**: Voiceover Recording Completion  
  **Deliverable**: Recorded voiceover audio file.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Video Delivery  
  **Deliverable**: Polished 2D animated explainer video in multiple formats.
