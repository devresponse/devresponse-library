
# Isometric Financial Services Explainer Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Isometric Financial Services Explainer project aims to create a visually stunning and informative animated video tailored for a financial services company. The primary objective of this project is to leverage isometric animation techniques to simplify complex financial concepts, investment strategies, and banking processes into easily digestible segments. This engaging video will feature intricate visuals designed to capture the audience's attention while enhancing their understanding of the subject matter. By utilizing a dynamic perspective, the explainer will guide viewers through various financial scenarios, making it an ideal educational tool for clients and prospects alike. Key outcomes include increased client engagement, improved understanding of financial products, and a cohesive presentation that aligns with the company's branding and messaging. The project will also incorporate multiple revisions based on client feedback to ensure satisfaction with the final output, ultimately positioning the company as a thought leader in the financial services sector.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the financial services industry, including competitor offerings and target audience preferences.
- **Script Development**: Collaborate with the client to create a compelling script that effectively communicates the key financial concepts and strategies.
- **Storyboarding**: Develop a storyboard that outlines the visual flow of the video, ensuring alignment with the script and overall messaging.
- **Isometric Animation**: Create high-quality isometric animations that visually represent the financial concepts, ensuring clarity and engagement.
- **Voiceover Recording**: Hire a professional voiceover artist to narrate the script, ensuring a tone that resonates with the target audience.
- **Sound Design**: Incorporate background music and sound effects that enhance the viewing experience without overshadowing the content.
- **Revisions**: Implement a structured revision process that allows for client feedback at multiple stages, ensuring the final product meets expectations.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the completed animated video in various formats suitable for different platforms (e.g., web, social media, presentations).

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Analysis
  - Deliverable: Research report and audience analysis document
- **Milestone 2**: Finalized Script
  - Deliverable: Approved script document
- **Milestone 3**: Storyboard Approval
  - Deliverable: Storyboard presentation for client review
- **Milestone 4**: Animation Production
  - Deliverable: First draft of the animated video
- **Milestone 5**: Voiceover and Sound Design
  - Deliverable: Integrated video with voiceover and sound
- **Milestone 6**: Client Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised video based on client feedback
- **Milestone 7**: Final Video Delivery
  - Deliverable: Completed animated video in specified formats
