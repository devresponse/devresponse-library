
# Project Plan Template: Isometric Educational Course Overview

## 1. Executive Summary

The Isometric Educational Course Overview project aims to create an engaging and visually stunning isometric explainer video tailored for an online educational platform. The primary objective of this project is to effectively showcase the course content through dynamic 3D visuals, highlighting various learning paths and emphasizing the key benefits for both students and professionals. By collaborating closely with subject matter experts, we will ensure that the video accurately represents the course material while applying isometric design principles to enhance clarity and engagement. The final product will serve as a powerful marketing tool, designed to attract potential learners by providing them with a clear and immersive overview of what the course offers. Key outcomes include a polished and professional video that not only informs but also captivates the audience, ultimately driving enrollment and enhancing the educational platform's reputation.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the course material and learning objectives.
- **Collaboration with Subject Matter Experts (SMEs)**: Engage with SMEs to gather insights and ensure accuracy in content representation.
- **Script Development**: Create a compelling script that outlines the key messages and flow of the video.
- **Storyboarding**: Develop a storyboard that visually represents the script, detailing scenes, transitions, and key visuals.
- **Isometric Design**: Apply isometric design principles to create engaging 3D visuals that align with the course content.
- **Animation**: Animate the storyboard, bringing the visuals to life while ensuring smooth transitions and engaging storytelling.
- **Editing and Finalization**: Edit the video to ensure a polished final product, incorporating feedback from stakeholders.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final video in multiple formats suitable for various online platforms.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Analysis
  - Deliverable: Research Report and Course Overview Document

- **Milestone 2**: Script Development
  - Deliverable: Finalized Script

- **Milestone 3**: Storyboarding
  - Deliverable: Completed Storyboard

- **Milestone 4**: Isometric Design and Animation
  - Deliverable: Draft Animation for Review

- **Milestone 5**: Editing and Finalization
  - Deliverable: Final Video

- **Milestone 6**: Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Project Report and Feedback Summary
