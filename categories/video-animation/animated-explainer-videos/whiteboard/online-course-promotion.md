
# Project Plan Template: Online Course Promotion

## 1. Executive Summary

The Online Course Promotion project aims to create an engaging whiteboard animation that effectively promotes an online course. The primary objective is to visually represent the course's curriculum, highlighting key topics and modules to provide potential students with a clear understanding of what they will learn. The animation will emphasize the benefits of enrolling in the course, including skill development, career advancement, and networking opportunities. By outlining the expected learning outcomes, the animation will ensure that viewers grasp the course's value. The final product will feature hand-drawn illustrations that resonate with the target audience, making complex ideas accessible and appealing. This project will follow a structured timeline and include regular updates to ensure alignment with the client's vision, ultimately driving enrollment and enhancing the course's market presence.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Kick-off meeting with stakeholders to define project objectives and expectations.
  - Gather initial requirements and insights about the target audience.

- **Script Development**
  - Create a compelling script that outlines the course curriculum, benefits, and learning outcomes.
  - Review and revise the script based on client feedback.

- **Storyboarding**
  - Develop a storyboard that visually represents the script, detailing each scene and key illustrations.
  - Present the storyboard to the client for approval.

- **Animation Production**
  - Create hand-drawn illustrations based on the approved storyboard.
  - Produce the whiteboard animation, incorporating voiceover and background music.

- **Review and Revisions**
  - Conduct a review session with the client to gather feedback on the initial animation draft.
  - Implement necessary revisions based on client input.

- **Final Delivery**
  - Deliver the final whiteboard animation in the required formats.
  - Provide a brief guide on how to use the animation for promotional purposes.
