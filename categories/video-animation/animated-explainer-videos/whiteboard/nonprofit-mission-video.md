
# Project Plan Template: Nonprofit Mission Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Nonprofit Mission Video project aims to create a captivating whiteboard animation that effectively communicates the mission, goals, and impact of {{Nonprofit Organization Name}}. The primary objective is to engage potential donors and volunteers by visually illustrating the organization's unique contributions to the community. This project will utilize hand-drawn illustrations to make complex ideas accessible and relatable, ensuring that the narrative resonates with the audience. Key outcomes include a 2-3 minute animation that not only highlights the nonprofit's initiatives but also inspires action and support. The collaborative process will involve gathering insights from the nonprofit to ensure the animation accurately reflects their values and objectives. By combining professional voiceover and background music, we aim to enhance the storytelling experience, ultimately driving engagement and fostering a deeper connection with the audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Kickoff**: Initial meeting with the nonprofit to discuss objectives, gather insights, and establish a project timeline.
  
- **Script Development**: Collaborate with the nonprofit to create a compelling script that outlines the key messages and narrative flow of the animation.

- **Storyboarding**: Develop a storyboard that visually represents the script, detailing the sequence of illustrations and animations.

- **Illustration Creation**: Design hand-drawn illustrations that align with the nonprofit's branding and effectively convey the intended messages.

- **Animation Production**: Animate the illustrations to create a fluid and engaging visual experience.

- **Voiceover Recording**: Hire a professional voiceover artist to record the script, ensuring the tone and delivery match the animation's intent.

- **Background Music Selection**: Choose appropriate background music that complements the animation and enhances the emotional impact.

- **Review and Revisions**: Present the initial draft of the animation to the nonprofit for feedback and make necessary revisions.

- **Final Delivery**: Provide the completed animation in various formats suitable for online sharing and presentations.
