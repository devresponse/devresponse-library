
# Project Plan Template: Product Assembly Guide

## 1. Executive Summary

The Product Assembly Guide project aims to create an engaging whiteboard animation that effectively illustrates the assembly process of {{Product Name}}. The primary objective is to develop a visual aid that simplifies the assembly instructions, making it easier for customers to understand each step of the process. This project will involve scripting detailed assembly instructions, designing hand-drawn illustrations that correspond to each step, and animating these illustrations to create a seamless flow of information. Key outcomes include a polished animation that enhances customer experience, reduces the likelihood of assembly errors, and ultimately leads to higher satisfaction rates. The animation will be tailored to the target audience, ensuring it is both informative and easy to follow. Additionally, the project will incorporate revisions based on client feedback to ensure that the final output meets or exceeds expectations, thereby reinforcing the brand's commitment to customer support and satisfaction.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Scripting**: 
  - Develop a comprehensive script that outlines the assembly instructions for {{Product Name}}.
  - Ensure the language is clear, concise, and tailored to the target audience.

- **Illustration Design**: 
  - Create hand-drawn illustrations for each step of the assembly process.
  - Ensure illustrations are visually appealing and easy to understand.

- **Animation**: 
  - Animate the illustrations to create a fluid and engaging visual experience.
  - Incorporate voiceover narration that aligns with the script.

- **Client Feedback and Revisions**: 
  - Present the initial draft of the animation to the client for feedback.
  - Implement necessary revisions based on client input to ensure satisfaction.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Deliver the final animated video in the required formats (e.g., MP4, MOV).
  - Provide any additional assets, such as the script and illustrations, as requested.
