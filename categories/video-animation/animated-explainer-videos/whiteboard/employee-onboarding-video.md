
# Project Plan Template: Employee Onboarding Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Employee Onboarding Video project aims to create an engaging whiteboard animation that effectively communicates essential information to new employees. The primary objective is to simplify the onboarding process by illustrating key company policies, cultural values, and performance expectations through dynamic storytelling and hand-drawn illustrations. This animation will serve as a visual guide, ensuring that new hires quickly grasp the core elements of the company, fostering a sense of belonging and understanding from day one. Key outcomes include a polished, professional animation that can be seamlessly integrated into the onboarding program, enhancing the overall experience for new employees. By providing a clear and engaging introduction to the organization, this project will contribute to improved employee retention and satisfaction, ultimately supporting the company's long-term success.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: 
  - Gather information on company policies, cultural values, and performance expectations.
  - Conduct interviews with key stakeholders to understand the onboarding process and identify critical content.

- **Script Development**: 
  - Create a compelling script that outlines the narrative flow of the animation.
  - Ensure the script aligns with the company's tone and messaging.

- **Storyboarding**: 
  - Develop a storyboard that visually represents the script, detailing each scene and key illustrations.
  - Review and revise the storyboard with stakeholders for feedback and approval.

- **Animation Production**: 
  - Create hand-drawn illustrations based on the approved storyboard.
  - Animate the illustrations to produce a cohesive and engaging video.

- **Voiceover and Sound Design**: 
  - Record a professional voiceover that complements the animation.
  - Incorporate sound effects and background music to enhance the viewing experience.

- **Review and Revisions**: 
  - Present the initial draft of the video to stakeholders for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on feedback received.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Deliver the final polished animation in the required formats for integration into the onboarding program.
  - Provide any additional materials or documentation as needed.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Analysis
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing key findings and content outline.

- **Milestone 2**: Script Development
  - Deliverable: Finalized script for the animation.

- **Milestone 3**: Storyboarding
  - Deliverable: Approved storyboard with visual representation of the script.

- **Milestone 4**: Animation Production
  - Deliverable: First draft of the animated video.

- **Milestone 5**: Voiceover and Sound Design
  - Deliverable: Completed video with voiceover and sound effects.

- **Milestone 6**: Review and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised video based on stakeholder feedback.

- **Milestone 7**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized animation in required formats, along with supplementary materials.
