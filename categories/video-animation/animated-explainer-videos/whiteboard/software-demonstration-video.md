
# Project Plan Template: Software Demo Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Software Demo Video project is to create an engaging and informative whiteboard animation that effectively showcases the functionality of the software application. This project aims to visually communicate the software's key features, user benefits, and overall user experience, making it easier for potential customers to grasp the software's value proposition. The animation will utilize hand-drawn illustrations to simplify complex concepts, ensuring clarity and engagement for the audience. Key outcomes include a polished video that is ready for distribution across various marketing and educational platforms, increased customer understanding of the software, and enhanced interest in the product. Collaboration with the client will be essential to gather insights about the software's unique selling points and target audience, ensuring the final product resonates with viewers and drives engagement.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Discovery**: 
  - Conduct meetings with the client to gather information about the software, its features, and target audience.
  - Analyze competitors and market trends to identify effective messaging strategies.

- **Script Development**: 
  - Create a compelling script that outlines the narrative of the animation, focusing on key features and benefits.
  - Review and revise the script with client feedback.

- **Storyboarding**: 
  - Develop a storyboard that visually represents the script, detailing each scene and the corresponding illustrations.
  - Present the storyboard to the client for approval.

- **Illustration Creation**: 
  - Design hand-drawn illustrations that align with the storyboard and enhance the storytelling.
  - Ensure illustrations are clear, engaging, and effectively communicate complex concepts.

- **Animation Production**: 
  - Animate the illustrations according to the approved storyboard and script.
  - Incorporate voiceover narration and background music to enhance the viewer's experience.

- **Review and Revisions**: 
  - Present the initial animation draft to the client for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on client input to ensure satisfaction.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Produce the final version of the animation, ensuring high-quality output suitable for various platforms.
  - Deliver the video in multiple formats as requested by the client.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Research and Discovery Completion
  - Deliverable: Research report and initial client meeting notes.

- **Milestone 2**: Script Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized script document.

- **Milestone 3**: Storyboard Approval
  - Deliverable: Approved storyboard visuals.

- **Milestone 4**: Illustration Completion
  - Deliverable: Set of hand-drawn illustrations.

- **Milestone 5**: Animation Draft Presentation
  - Deliverable: Initial animation draft for client review.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Animation Delivery
  - Deliverable: Polished video in requested formats.
