
# Project Plan Template: Product Comparison Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Product Comparison Video project is to create an engaging and informative whiteboard animation that effectively compares two or more products. This project aims to simplify complex information by utilizing hand-drawn illustrations that visually represent the features, benefits, and differences of each product. The final explainer video will serve as a powerful marketing and educational tool, designed to captivate the target audience while delivering clear and concise information. Key outcomes include a well-developed script, detailed storyboards, high-quality animation, and polished final edits that meet the client's specific needs. By the end of this project, the client will have a compelling visual asset that enhances their marketing efforts and aids in customer understanding of their products.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Script Development**: Collaborate with the client to gather information about the products and create a compelling narrative that highlights key features and benefits.
- **Storyboarding**: Develop a storyboard that outlines the visual flow of the animation, including key scenes and transitions.
- **Animation Production**: Create hand-drawn illustrations and animate them to bring the storyboard to life, ensuring that the animation is engaging and aligns with the script.
- **Final Editing**: Review and refine the animation, incorporating client feedback to ensure the final product meets quality standards and effectively communicates the intended message.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final video in the required formats for various platforms (e.g., social media, website, presentations).

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Script Development
  - Deliverable: Finalized script document
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Storyboarding
  - Deliverable: Storyboard presentation for client review
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Animation Production
  - Deliverable: First draft of the animated video
- **Milestone 4**: Final Editing and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Final version of the animated video
- **Milestone 5**: Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Project completion report and feedback session
