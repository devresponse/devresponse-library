
# Project Plan Template: Startup Product Explainer

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Startup Product Explainer project is to create a captivating whiteboard animation that effectively communicates the features and benefits of the new startup product, {{Product Name}}. This project aims to simplify complex ideas through hand-drawn illustrations, making them easily digestible for potential investors and customers. The animation will serve as a powerful marketing tool, designed to highlight the unique selling points of {{Product Name}} while resonating with the target audience. Key outcomes include a visually engaging explainer video that maintains viewer interest and delivers clear, concise information. The final product will be polished and professional, ready for use in marketing campaigns, presentations, and investor pitches. By the end of this project, the client will have a comprehensive video that not only informs but also captivates the audience, driving interest and engagement with {{Product Name}}.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Script Development**: 
  - Collaborate with the client to gather information about {{Product Name}}.
  - Develop a compelling script that outlines the key features and benefits.
  - Ensure the script aligns with the target audience's interests and needs.

- **Storyboarding**: 
  - Create a storyboard that visualizes the script.
  - Outline each scene, including illustrations and text placement.
  - Review and revise the storyboard with the client for approval.

- **Animation Production**: 
  - Produce hand-drawn illustrations based on the approved storyboard.
  - Animate the illustrations to create a fluid and engaging video.
  - Incorporate voiceover and sound effects to enhance the viewing experience.

- **Final Editing**: 
  - Edit the animation for pacing, clarity, and visual appeal.
  - Ensure the final video meets the client's specifications and quality standards.
  - Deliver the final product in the required formats for various platforms.
