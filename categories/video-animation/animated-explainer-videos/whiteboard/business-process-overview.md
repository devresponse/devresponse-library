
# Project Plan Template: Business Process Overview

## 1. Executive Summary

The Business Process Overview project aims to create an engaging whiteboard animation that visually represents the internal processes of {{Client Company Name}}. The primary objective of this project is to educate employees and stakeholders about the various workflows within the organization through a visually appealing and informative medium. By utilizing hand-drawn illustrations, the animation will simplify complex ideas, making them accessible and easy to understand. Key outcomes include a comprehensive understanding of the company's operations, enhanced employee training, and improved stakeholder engagement. The project will involve close collaboration with the client to gather detailed information about the processes to be illustrated, ensuring that the final product accurately reflects the company's unique workflows. The final deliverable will be a high-quality whiteboard animation that can be easily shared and utilized across various platforms, serving as a powerful tool for training and presentations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**: 
  - Kick-off meeting with stakeholders to define project goals and expectations.
  - Identify key processes to be illustrated in the animation.

- **Research and Information Gathering**: 
  - Conduct interviews with subject matter experts to gather detailed information about the internal processes.
  - Review existing documentation and materials related to the workflows.

- **Script Development**: 
  - Create a script that outlines the narrative of the animation, ensuring clarity and engagement.
  - Review and revise the script based on client feedback.

- **Storyboarding**: 
  - Develop a storyboard that visually represents each scene of the animation.
  - Present the storyboard to the client for approval before moving to production.

- **Animation Production**: 
  - Create the whiteboard animation using hand-drawn illustrations and voiceover narration.
  - Incorporate client feedback during the production phase to ensure alignment with expectations.

- **Final Review and Revisions**: 
  - Present the completed animation to the client for final review.
  - Make necessary revisions based on client feedback.

- **Delivery**: 
  - Provide the final high-quality whiteboard animation in the agreed formats.
  - Offer guidance on how to effectively share and utilize the animation across various platforms.
