
# Project Plan Template: Customer Journey Map

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Customer Journey Map project is to create an engaging and informative whiteboard animation that visually encapsulates the customer journey from initial awareness to final purchase. This project aims to enhance the understanding of customer experiences for marketing and sales teams, providing them with a powerful tool to communicate effectively with stakeholders and clients. The animation will simplify complex concepts through a combination of a detailed script, hand-drawn illustrations, and a professional voiceover. Key outcomes include a polished final product that not only meets the specified requirements but also resonates with the target audience, ultimately driving better engagement and understanding of the customer journey. By delivering this animation on time, we will ensure that all project elements align with the overarching goals of improving customer interaction and satisfaction.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Script Development**: Create a comprehensive script that outlines each stage of the customer journey, ensuring clarity and engagement.
- **Illustration Design**: Develop hand-drawn illustrations that visually represent various customer interactions throughout the journey.
- **Voiceover Recording**: Provide a professional voiceover that narrates the script, enhancing the storytelling aspect of the animation.
- **Animation Production**: Combine the script, illustrations, and voiceover into a cohesive whiteboard animation that effectively communicates the customer journey.
- **Revisions and Feedback**: Incorporate feedback from stakeholders during the review process to ensure the final product meets expectations.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the completed animation in the specified format, ready for distribution to marketing and sales teams.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Script Development
  - Deliverable: Finalized script document
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Illustration Design
  - Deliverable: Set of hand-drawn illustrations
- **Milestone 3**: Voiceover Recording
  - Deliverable: Recorded voiceover audio file
- **Milestone 4**: Animation Production
  - Deliverable: Draft version of the whiteboard animation
- **Milestone 5**: Review and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised animation based on stakeholder feedback
- **Milestone 6**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Completed whiteboard animation in the agreed format
