
# Project Plan Template: Historical Event Explainer

## 1. Executive Summary

The Historical Event Explainer project aims to create an engaging whiteboard animation that visually narrates a significant historical event, making it accessible and understandable for educational audiences. The primary objective is to simplify complex narratives through clear visuals and concise storytelling techniques, ensuring that viewers can grasp the importance and impact of the historical event. This project will involve thorough research to ensure accuracy and relevance, followed by the development of hand-drawn illustrations that depict key moments and figures associated with the event. Key outcomes include a polished and professional animation that serves as an educational tool suitable for classrooms, online courses, and educational platforms. By focusing on clarity and engagement, this project will enhance the learning experience and foster a deeper understanding of historical events among diverse audiences.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct in-depth research on the selected historical event, including key figures, dates, and significant moments.
  - Gather credible sources and references to ensure accuracy.

- **Scriptwriting**: 
  - Develop a compelling script that narrates the historical event in an engaging manner.
  - Ensure the script is concise and suitable for the target audience.

- **Storyboarding**: 
  - Create a storyboard that outlines the visual flow of the animation.
  - Include sketches of key scenes and transitions to visualize the narrative.

- **Illustration Development**: 
  - Design hand-drawn illustrations that represent key moments and figures from the historical event.
  - Ensure illustrations are clear, engaging, and aligned with the script.

- **Animation Production**: 
  - Animate the illustrations to create a cohesive and engaging whiteboard animation.
  - Incorporate voiceover narration and background music to enhance the viewing experience.

- **Final Editing**: 
  - Edit the animation for clarity, pacing, and overall quality.
  - Review the final product for accuracy and coherence.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and references.

- **Milestone 2**: Script Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized script for the animation.

- **Milestone 3**: Storyboard Approval
  - Deliverable: Completed storyboard with visual sketches.

- **Milestone 4**: Illustration Development Completion
  - Deliverable: Set of hand-drawn illustrations for the animation.

- **Milestone 5**: Animation Production Completion
  - Deliverable: Draft version of the animated video.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Editing and Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized whiteboard animation ready for distribution.
