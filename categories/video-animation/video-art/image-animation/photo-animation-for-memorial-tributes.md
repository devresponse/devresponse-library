
# Project Plan Template: Photo Animation for Memorial Tributes

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Photo Animation for Memorial Tributes" project aims to create heartfelt and respectful animations from cherished photographs, serving as a meaningful tribute to loved ones who have passed. The primary objective is to transform static images into gentle, flowing animations that evoke emotions and honor the memory of individuals. This project will focus on capturing the essence of the person being commemorated, ensuring that each animation reflects their unique spirit and legacy. Key outcomes include the delivery of high-quality animated videos that can be shared with family and friends, providing a lasting keepsake that resonates deeply. By offering personalized consultations, we will work closely with clients to understand their vision, ensuring that each animation is crafted with care and sensitivity. This service is particularly suitable for commemorative events, online memorials, or personal keepsakes, allowing families to celebrate the lives of their loved ones in a touching and memorable way.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Schedule a personalized consultation with the client to discuss their vision and preferences for the animation.
  - Gather information about the individual being commemorated, including their personality, interests, and any specific themes or elements the client wishes to include.

- **Image Selection**: 
  - Assist the client in selecting cherished photographs that best represent the individual.
  - Ensure that the images are of high quality and suitable for animation.

- **Animation Creation**: 
  - Develop a storyboard for the animation based on the client's input.
  - Utilize animation software to create gentle movements such as soft fades, gentle zooms, and flowing transitions.
  - Incorporate any additional elements requested by the client, such as text overlays or background music.

- **Review and Revisions**: 
  - Present the initial animation draft to the client for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on client input to ensure satisfaction.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Deliver the final high-quality animated video in the desired format.
  - Provide guidance on how to share the animation with family and friends.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Documented client vision and preferences.

- **Milestone 2**: Image Selection
  - Deliverable: Finalized list of photographs to be animated.

- **Milestone 3**: Storyboard Approval
  - Deliverable: Approved storyboard outlining the animation sequence.

- **Milestone 4**: Draft Animation Review
  - Deliverable: Initial draft of the animated video for client feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Animation Delivery
  - Deliverable: High-quality animated video delivered to the client.
