
# Project Plan Template: Animated Event Invitations

## 1. Executive Summary

The Animated Event Invitations project aims to create captivating animated invitations tailored for a variety of events, including weddings, birthdays, and corporate gatherings. The primary objective is to transform static designs into lively animations that not only convey essential event details but also evoke excitement and anticipation among invitees. This project will involve close collaboration with the client to understand their vision, incorporate brand elements, and select appropriate color schemes and typography. Key outcomes include delivering high-quality animated invitations that are suitable for sharing via email and social media platforms, ensuring a wide reach and engagement. By leveraging innovative animation techniques, we aim to create invitations that stand out and leave a lasting impression, making the event memorable right from the start. The successful execution of this project will enhance the client's brand image and create a buzz around their events, ultimately contributing to higher attendance and engagement.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Client Consultation**: Conduct initial meetings with the client to gather requirements, understand their vision, and discuss brand elements.
- **Concept Development**: Create initial design concepts for the animated invitations, including color schemes, typography, and animation styles.
- **Animation Production**: Develop the animations based on the approved concepts, ensuring that they are engaging and visually appealing.
- **Client Review**: Present the animated invitations to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the final animated invitations in formats suitable for email and social media sharing.
- **Post-Delivery Support**: Offer support for any issues related to the sharing of the invitations and gather feedback for future improvements.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Client Consultation
  - Deliverable: Detailed project brief and requirements document.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Approval of Concept Designs
  - Deliverable: Presentation of initial design concepts and client feedback.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Animation Production
  - Deliverable: Draft versions of the animated invitations for client review.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Approval from Client
  - Deliverable: Finalized animated invitations ready for delivery.

- **Milestone 5**: Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Delivery of final files and post-delivery support documentation.
