
# Project Plan: Custom Raid and Host Animations

## 1. Executive Summary

In the competitive world of live streaming, standing out is crucial for attracting and retaining viewers. The objective of the Custom Raid and Host Animations project is to create unique, engaging animations that celebrate the exciting moments when a streamer is raided or hosted by another channel. This project aims to enhance the visual appeal of the stream while fostering a sense of community and excitement among viewers. By designing animations that reflect the streamer's brand and personality, we will ensure a cohesive look and feel that resonates with the audience. Key outcomes include the delivery of multiple animation styles tailored to the streamer's theme, an initial consultation to capture the streamer's vision, and a review phase to guarantee satisfaction with the final product. Ultimately, this project will not only elevate the streamer's brand but also create memorable experiences for their audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the streamer to discuss their vision, brand identity, and preferences for the animations. This will include gathering information on color schemes, themes, and any specific elements they wish to incorporate.
  
- **Concept Development**: Create initial sketches and concepts for the animations based on the consultation. Present these concepts to the streamer for feedback and adjustments.

- **Animation Creation**: Develop the final animations using industry-standard software. This will include:
  - Multiple animation styles (e.g., celebratory graphics, overlays, sound effects)
  - Customization options to align with the streamer's branding

- **Review Phase**: Present the completed animations to the streamer for review. Gather feedback and make necessary revisions to ensure the final product meets their expectations.

- **Final Delivery**: Provide the streamer with the finalized animations in the required formats, along with any necessary documentation for implementation.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Summary document outlining the streamer's vision and preferences.

- **Milestone 2**: Concept Development
  - Deliverable: Presentation of initial sketches and concepts for feedback.

- **Milestone 3**: Animation Creation
  - Deliverable: Draft versions of the animations for review.

- **Milestone 4**: Review Phase
  - Deliverable: Revised animations based on streamer feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized animations in required formats, along with implementation documentation.
