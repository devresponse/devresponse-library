
# Project Plan: Custom Animated Emotes for Streamers

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Custom Animated Emotes for Streamers" project aims to create a unique set of animated emotes that embody the personality, style, and brand of individual streamers. In the competitive landscape of live streaming, these custom emotes will serve as a vital tool for enhancing viewer interaction and fostering community engagement. The project will involve a collaborative process where streamers will provide input on their preferences, ensuring that each emote resonates with their audience. Key outcomes include a collection of visually appealing and emotionally expressive animated emotes optimized for various chat platforms, enabling seamless integration during live broadcasts. By the end of this project, streamers will have a distinctive set of emotes that not only elevate their brand but also enhance the overall viewer experience, making their streams more memorable and engaging.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Discovery**: Conduct interviews with streamers to understand their brand, audience, and desired emotions to convey through the emotes.
- **Concept Development**: Create initial sketches and concepts for the emotes based on the research findings.
- **Design and Animation**: Develop high-quality animated emotes, ensuring they are visually appealing and convey the intended emotions effectively.
- **Feedback and Revisions**: Present the initial designs to the streamer for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Finalization**: Prepare the final versions of the emotes, ensuring they are optimized for various streaming platforms (e.g., Twitch, YouTube).
- **Delivery**: Provide the final animated emotes in the required formats and resolutions for easy integration into the streamer's chat platforms.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Discovery
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing streamer insights and preferences.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Approval of Concept Designs
  - Deliverable: Set of initial sketches and concepts for emotes.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Animated Emotes
  - Deliverable: A collection of animated emotes ready for review.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Revisions and Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized animated emotes based on streamer feedback.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of Final Assets
  - Deliverable: Final emotes delivered in required formats and resolutions.
