
# Project Plan: Custom Animated Alerts for Stream Events

## 1. Executive Summary

In the competitive world of live streaming, standing out is crucial for success. The objective of this project is to create custom animated alerts that will enhance the streamer's broadcast by providing visually appealing notifications for key events such as new followers, subscribers, donations, and raids. This project aims to elevate the streamer's content and foster a more interactive and enjoyable environment for viewers. Each alert will be meticulously designed to align with the streamer's unique theme and branding, ensuring a cohesive viewer experience. The key outcomes include multiple eye-catching alert animations that encourage audience interaction and enhance the overall quality of the stream. By delivering high-quality animations in various formats, we will ensure compatibility with popular streaming platforms, ultimately contributing to the streamer's success and viewer engagement.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Discovery**: 
  - Conduct an initial meeting with the streamer to understand their brand, theme, and specific requirements for alerts.
  - Analyze competitor alerts and current trends in animated notifications.

- **Design Phase**: 
  - Create initial design concepts for each type of alert (follower, subscriber, donation, raid).
  - Present designs to the streamer for feedback and revisions.

- **Animation Development**: 
  - Develop high-quality animations based on approved designs.
  - Ensure animations are engaging and visually appealing.

- **Testing and Quality Assurance**: 
  - Test animations across various streaming platforms to ensure compatibility.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on testing feedback.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Package the final animations in multiple formats (e.g., GIF, MP4, WebM) for easy integration.
  - Provide a user guide for implementation and troubleshooting.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Discovery
  - Deliverable: Research report and initial design brief.

- **Milestone 2**: Approval of Design Concepts
  - Deliverable: Finalized design mockups for all alert types.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Animation Development
  - Deliverable: High-quality animated alerts for each event type.

- **Milestone 4**: Successful Testing and Quality Assurance
  - Deliverable: Testing report and any necessary revisions.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery of All Assets
  - Deliverable: Packaged animations in various formats and user guide.
