
# Custom Vtuber Avatar Design Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Vtuber Avatar Design project aims to create a fully customized Vtuber avatar that embodies the client's unique personality and style. The primary objective is to deliver an engaging virtual presence that captivates audiences and enhances viewer interaction during live streaming sessions. The project will begin with an in-depth consultation to gather insights into the client's vision, preferences, and specific requirements. Following this, the designer will produce initial sketches and concepts, focusing on distinct features such as facial expressions, hairstyles, and outfits that align with the client's brand or character theme. Upon approval of the design, the avatar will be animated to facilitate interactivity, ensuring a dynamic streaming experience. The final deliverable will include the avatar in various formats compatible with different streaming platforms, providing ease of use for the client. Key outcomes include a unique avatar that resonates with the target audience, increased viewer engagement, and a seamless integration into the client's streaming setup.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct an in-depth consultation with the client to understand their vision, preferences, and specific requirements.
  - Gather reference materials and inspirations from the client.

- **Design Phase**: 
  - Create initial sketches and concepts based on the consultation.
  - Present sketches to the client for feedback and revisions.
  - Finalize the avatar design incorporating client feedback.

- **Animation Phase**: 
  - Develop animations for the avatar to enhance interactivity during live streaming.
  - Ensure the avatar can express various emotions and actions as per the client's requirements.

- **Final Deliverables**: 
  - Provide the avatar in multiple formats (e.g., .png, .gif, .webm) suitable for different streaming platforms.
  - Include a user guide for the client on how to implement the avatar in their streaming setup.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the consultation phase
  - Deliverable: Consultation summary document

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of initial sketches and concepts
  - Deliverable: Presentation of sketches for client feedback

- **Milestone 3**: Finalization of avatar design
  - Deliverable: Approved final design files

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of avatar animations
  - Deliverable: Animated avatar files

- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of final avatar in various formats
  - Deliverable: Final avatar package and user guide
