
# Vtuber Avatar Branding Package Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Vtuber Avatar Branding Package aims to create a cohesive and visually appealing identity for your virtual streaming persona. The primary objective of this project is to design a unique logo that encapsulates your brand's personality, alongside custom overlays that enhance the viewer's experience during live streams. This project will also include the development of promotional materials such as banners and social media graphics, which are essential for effectively marketing your Vtuber avatar across various platforms. Key outcomes include a complete branding toolkit that not only captures attention but also resonates with your target audience, fostering deeper connections and engagement during your streams. By the end of this project, you will possess a distinctive and professional branding package that empowers you to stand out in the competitive world of virtual streaming, ultimately driving viewer loyalty and increasing your online presence.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Logo Design**: 
  - Create a unique logo that reflects the personality and theme of the Vtuber avatar.
  - Provide three initial design concepts for review and feedback.
  - Revise the selected concept based on client feedback until final approval.

- **Custom Overlays**: 
  - Design overlays for live streaming that include webcam frames, alerts, and chat boxes.
  - Ensure overlays are compatible with popular streaming platforms (e.g., Twitch, YouTube).
  - Provide a set of overlays in various resolutions for different streaming setups.

- **Promotional Materials**: 
  - Develop social media graphics tailored for platforms such as Twitter, Instagram, and Facebook.
  - Create banners for channel branding and promotional use.
  - Provide templates for future use, allowing for easy updates and modifications.

- **Branding Guidelines**: 
  - Compile a branding guide that outlines color palettes, font choices, and usage guidelines for all branding materials.
  - Ensure consistency across all platforms and materials.
