
# Project Plan Template: Online Survey Creation Guide

## 1. Executive Summary

The Online Survey Creation Guide project aims to produce a high-quality screencasting video that effectively demonstrates the step-by-step process of creating and distributing online surveys using {{survey_tool_name}}. The primary objective of this project is to provide an educational resource for users who are new to online surveys, ensuring they can easily understand and apply the concepts presented. The video will cover essential topics such as setting up surveys, customizing questions, and analyzing responses, making it a valuable tool for businesses, educators, and individuals seeking to gather feedback or data. Key outcomes include a polished and professional final product that incorporates visual aids, such as annotations and highlights, to enhance viewer engagement and understanding. The video will be delivered in a format suitable for online sharing, complete with a brief introduction and conclusion to effectively frame the content. By the end of this project, viewers will be equipped with the knowledge and skills necessary to create and distribute their own online surveys confidently.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Online Survey Creation Guide project includes the following key activities:

- **Research and Planning**: Identify the target audience and their needs, and outline the key topics to be covered in the video.
- **Script Development**: Create a detailed script that outlines the step-by-step process of creating and distributing online surveys, including key points and visual aids.
- **Screencasting**: Record the screencast using {{screen_recording_software}}, ensuring high-quality audio and video.
- **Editing**: Edit the recorded footage to include annotations, highlights, and any additional visual aids to enhance understanding.
- **Review and Feedback**: Share the draft video with stakeholders for feedback and make necessary revisions.
- **Final Production**: Produce the final version of the video, ensuring it meets quality standards and is suitable for online sharing.
- **Distribution**: Upload the video to appropriate platforms and promote it to the target audience.
