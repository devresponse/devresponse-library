
# Project Plan Template: Animated NFT Profile Pictures (PFPs)

## 1. Executive Summary

The Animated NFT Profile Pictures (PFPs) project aims to revolutionize the way individuals express their digital identities through captivating animated NFTs. Our primary objective is to transform static images into dynamic digital art that not only enhances visual appeal but also engages users through subtle animations and interactive elements. This project will cater to artists, collectors, and crypto enthusiasts, providing them with unique, high-quality animated PFPs that stand out in the metaverse. Key outcomes include a streamlined process that begins with initial consultations to capture the client's vision, followed by meticulous design and animation phases. The final product will be a stunning animated NFT that embodies the client's individuality and creativity, ensuring a memorable digital presence.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Conduct a series of meetings with the client to understand their vision, preferences, and specific requirements for the animated PFP.
  
- **Concept Development**: 
  - Create initial sketches and concepts based on the client's input, focusing on the desired style, colors, and themes.
  
- **Design Phase**: 
  - Develop the static image that will serve as the base for the animation, ensuring it aligns with the client's brand and aesthetic.
  
- **Animation Phase**: 
  - Implement subtle movements (e.g., swaying, blinking) and glowing effects to enhance the visual appeal of the PFP.
  - Integrate interactive elements that respond to user actions, creating an engaging experience.
  
- **Client Review**: 
  - Present the animated PFP to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments based on their input.
  
- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Provide the client with the final animated NFT in the required formats, along with any necessary documentation for minting on the blockchain.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client vision and requirements.

- **Milestone 2**: Concept Development Completed
  - Deliverable: Initial sketches and concepts for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Design Phase Completed
  - Deliverable: Finalized static image for animation.

- **Milestone 4**: Animation Phase Completed
  - Deliverable: Animated PFP with all specified effects and interactions.

- **Milestone 5**: Client Review Completed
  - Deliverable: Feedback report and any requested revisions.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Final animated NFT files and minting documentation.
