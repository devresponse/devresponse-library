
# Project Plan Template: Mental Health Awareness Training Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Mental Health Awareness Training Video project aims to create an engaging and informative eLearning video that addresses the critical issue of mental health in workplaces and educational institutions. The primary objective is to raise awareness about mental health challenges and promote strategies for fostering a supportive environment. This project will involve comprehensive research on relevant mental health topics, the development of a compelling script, and the creation of a storyboard that effectively conveys the message. Key outcomes include a high-quality video that incorporates expert insights, real-life testimonials, and practical strategies for mental well-being. The final product will not only inform viewers but also inspire them to take action, making it a valuable resource for organizations seeking to enhance their mental health training programs. By tailoring the content to meet the needs of both workplace training and school curricula, we aim to ensure that the video resonates with diverse audiences and contributes positively to mental health awareness.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: Conduct thorough research on mental health topics relevant to workplaces and educational institutions. This includes reviewing existing literature, interviewing mental health professionals, and gathering testimonials from individuals with lived experiences.
  
- **Scripting**: Develop a detailed script that outlines the key messages, expert insights, and testimonials to be included in the video. The script will be designed to engage viewers and encourage them to reflect on their own mental health and that of others.

- **Storyboarding**: Create a storyboard that visually represents the script, detailing the flow of the video, key visuals, animations, and transitions. This will serve as a blueprint for the production team.

- **Production**: Utilize high-quality visuals and animations to enhance viewer engagement. This phase will include filming, voiceover recording, and the integration of graphics and animations.

- **Post-Production**: Edit the video to ensure a polished final product. This will include sound editing, color correction, and the addition of any necessary subtitles or captions.

- **Feedback and Revisions**: Present the draft video to stakeholders for feedback. Incorporate any necessary revisions based on their input.

- **Final Delivery**: Deliver the final video in multiple formats suitable for various platforms (e.g., online training modules, in-person presentations).

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase

- **Milestone 4**: Production Phase Completion

- **Milestone 5**: Post-Production Completion

- **Milestone 6**: Final Video Delivery  
  **Deliverable**: Finalized video in multiple formats.
