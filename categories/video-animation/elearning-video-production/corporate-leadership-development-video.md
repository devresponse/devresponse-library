
# Project Plan Template: Corporate Leadership Development Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Corporate Leadership Development Video project aims to create an engaging and informative eLearning video tailored for corporate leadership training. The primary objective is to enhance the leadership skills of employees by providing them with practical insights and real-world applications. This project will incorporate real-world case studies to illustrate key leadership concepts, alongside expert interviews that provide valuable insights from industry leaders. Additionally, the video will feature interactive decision-making exercises that allow viewers to apply their knowledge in practical scenarios, enhancing their learning experience. By focusing on interactivity and real-world applications, this eLearning video will serve as a vital resource for organizations looking to develop their leadership talent effectively. The key outcomes of this project include a polished final product that meets the highest standards of quality, increased engagement in leadership training, and improved leadership capabilities among participants.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production**
  - Conduct needs assessment and gather requirements from stakeholders.
  - Develop a project timeline and allocate resources.
  - Scriptwriting: Create a detailed script that outlines the content, including case studies and expert interviews.
  - Storyboarding: Develop visual storyboards to plan the video flow and key visuals.

- **Production**
  - Filming: Schedule and conduct filming sessions with subject matter experts and actors.
  - Capture high-quality audio and video footage.

- **Post-Production**
  - Editing: Edit the footage to create a cohesive narrative that aligns with the script.
  - Incorporate graphics, animations, and interactive elements.
  - Review and revise the video based on feedback from stakeholders.

- **Final Delivery**
  - Produce the final version of the video in multiple formats suitable for various platforms.
  - Provide supplementary materials, such as a facilitator guide and participant handouts.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Project initiation document and stakeholder sign-off.

- **Milestone 2: Script and Storyboard Completion**
  - Deliverable: Finalized script and storyboard for approval.

- **Milestone 3: Filming Completion**
  - Deliverable: All raw footage captured and logged.

- **Milestone 4: First Cut Review**
  - Deliverable: Initial edited version of the video for stakeholder feedback.

- **Milestone 5: Final Video Delivery**
  - Deliverable: Completed eLearning video and supplementary materials.
