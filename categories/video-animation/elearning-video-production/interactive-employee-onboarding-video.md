
# Project Plan Template: Interactive Employee Onboarding Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Interactive Employee Onboarding Video project aims to produce a dynamic and engaging eLearning video tailored for new employees. The primary objective of this project is to create a comprehensive introduction to the company, its culture, and essential policies, ensuring that new hires feel welcomed and informed from day one. The video will incorporate interactive elements such as quizzes and clickable sections, which will not only convey critical information but also actively engage viewers, leading to better retention and understanding. Key outcomes of this project include a polished, high-quality video that aligns with the company's branding and educational goals, providing new hires with a seamless onboarding experience. By enhancing the onboarding process through innovative video content, the company aims to foster a positive work environment and improve employee satisfaction and retention rates.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Phase 1: Script Development**
  - Research and gather information on company culture, policies, and procedures.
  - Collaborate with stakeholders to outline key messages and objectives.
  - Draft the script, incorporating interactive elements and engaging narratives.
  - Review and revise the script based on feedback from stakeholders.

- **Phase 2: Storyboarding**
  - Create a visual storyboard that outlines the flow of the video.
  - Identify key scenes, transitions, and interactive elements.
  - Collaborate with the design team to ensure alignment with branding guidelines.

- **Phase 3: Video Production**
  - Assemble a production team, including videographers, actors, and voiceover artists.
  - Schedule and conduct video shoots, ensuring all necessary footage is captured.
  - Record voiceovers and gather any additional audio elements required.

- **Phase 4: Post-Production Editing**
  - Edit the video to create a cohesive and engaging final product.
  - Incorporate interactive elements such as quizzes and clickable sections.
  - Conduct quality assurance checks to ensure the video meets all technical and content standards.
  - Finalize the video and prepare it for distribution.
