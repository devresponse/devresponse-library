
# Project Plan Template: Cybersecurity Awareness Training Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Cybersecurity Awareness Training Video project aims to create an engaging and informative eLearning video tailored for employees to enhance their understanding of cybersecurity best practices. The primary objective of this project is to equip employees with the knowledge and skills necessary to recognize and respond to potential cybersecurity threats, such as phishing, malware, and social engineering. This project will begin with a thorough needs assessment to identify key topics and learning objectives, ensuring that the content is relevant and impactful. Following the assessment, a detailed storyboard will be developed to outline the flow of the video, integrating captivating animations and real-world scenarios to illustrate the threats effectively. The production phase will involve high-quality animation and professional voiceover work, ensuring a polished final product. A review process will be implemented to incorporate client feedback, resulting in a final deliverable that is ready for deployment on online learning platforms. The key outcomes of this project include increased employee awareness of cybersecurity risks, improved ability to identify threats, and a culture of cybersecurity mindfulness within the organization.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Needs Assessment**: Conduct interviews and surveys with stakeholders to identify key topics and learning objectives related to cybersecurity.
- **Storyboard Development**: Create a detailed storyboard that outlines the video structure, including key messages, animations, and scenarios.
- **Scriptwriting**: Develop a script that aligns with the storyboard and effectively communicates the learning objectives.
- **Animation Production**: Design and produce high-quality animations that visually represent the key concepts and scenarios outlined in the storyboard.
- **Voiceover Recording**: Hire a professional voiceover artist to record the script, ensuring clarity and engagement.
- **Review Process**: Present the draft video to stakeholders for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Final Production**: Finalize the video, incorporating all feedback and ensuring high production quality.
- **Deployment**: Prepare the video for deployment on online learning platforms, including any necessary formatting and technical specifications.
