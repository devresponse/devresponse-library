
# Project Plan Template: Financial Literacy Course Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Financial Literacy Course Video project aims to create a comprehensive eLearning video series that empowers learners with essential financial knowledge. The primary objective of this project is to develop engaging and informative content that covers critical topics such as budgeting, saving, and investing. By providing clear examples and interactive exercises, the course will enhance understanding and retention of financial concepts. Each video will be designed to captivate viewers through dynamic visuals and relatable scenarios, making complex financial ideas accessible to a broad audience. The project will involve thorough research, scriptwriting, storyboarding, and high-quality video production, ensuring that the final product is not only informative but also captivating. Key outcomes include increased financial literacy among participants, improved decision-making skills regarding personal finance, and the establishment of a solid foundation for financial independence. By the end of this project, learners will have the tools they need to make informed financial decisions, setting them on a path to financial literacy and independence.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: Conduct in-depth research on financial literacy topics, including budgeting, saving, and investing. Identify target audience needs and preferences.
- **Scriptwriting**: Develop scripts for each video segment, ensuring clarity, engagement, and educational value. Incorporate feedback from subject matter experts.
- **Storyboarding**: Create detailed storyboards that outline the visual and narrative flow of each video, including key scenes, graphics, and animations.
- **Video Production**: Execute high-quality video production, including filming, editing, and post-production. Utilize professional equipment and software to ensure a polished final product.
- **Interactive Elements**: Design and integrate interactive exercises and quizzes to reinforce learning and assess viewer comprehension.
- **Review and Revisions**: Conduct multiple rounds of review with stakeholders to ensure content accuracy and alignment with project objectives.
- **Final Delivery**: Package the completed video series for distribution on eLearning platforms, ensuring compatibility and accessibility for all users.
