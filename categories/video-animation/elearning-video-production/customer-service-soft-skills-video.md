
# Project Plan Template: Customer Service Soft Skills Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The **Customer Service Soft Skills Video** project aims to develop an engaging and informative eLearning video specifically designed for customer service representatives. The primary objective of this project is to enhance the soft skills essential for success in customer-facing roles, focusing on empathy, effective communication, and conflict resolution techniques. The project will begin with a comprehensive needs assessment to identify key learning objectives and the characteristics of the target audience. Following this, a detailed script will be crafted, incorporating real-world scenarios and interactive elements to foster viewer engagement. The production will utilize high-quality visuals and animations to effectively illustrate the concepts being taught. A thorough review and feedback phase will ensure that the final product aligns with the client's expectations and learning goals. Ultimately, this eLearning video will serve as a vital resource for training programs, empowering customer service representatives to improve their skills and deliver exceptional service.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Needs Assessment**: Conduct interviews and surveys with stakeholders to identify learning objectives and audience characteristics.
- **Script Development**: Create a detailed script that includes:
  - Key soft skills to be covered
  - Real-world scenarios for practical understanding
  - Interactive elements to enhance engagement
- **Video Production**:
  - Storyboarding to visualize the flow of the video
  - Filming and animation creation using high-quality equipment and software
  - Voiceover recording and sound design
- **Review and Feedback**:
  - Present draft video to stakeholders for feedback
  - Make necessary revisions based on feedback received
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the completed video in various formats suitable for different eLearning platforms.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Needs Assessment
  - Deliverable: Needs Assessment Report
- **Milestone 2**: Script Development
  - Deliverable: Finalized Script
- **Milestone 3**: Video Production
  - Deliverable: Draft Video for Review
- **Milestone 4**: Review and Feedback
  - Deliverable: Revised Video
- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Completed eLearning Video in specified formats
