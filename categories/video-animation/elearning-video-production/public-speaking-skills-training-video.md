
# Project Plan Template: Public Speaking Skills Training Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Public Speaking Skills Training Video project aims to create a comprehensive eLearning resource designed to enhance public speaking abilities for learners at all levels. The primary objective is to equip individuals with essential communication skills, techniques for overcoming anxiety, and strategies for engaging an audience effectively. This project will provide a structured learning experience that includes interactive exercises, enabling learners to practice their skills in real-world scenarios. Key outcomes include a polished final video that is informative, engaging, and easily integrable into online learning platforms. By the end of this project, participants will have access to a valuable tool that fosters confidence and competence in public speaking, ultimately contributing to their personal and professional development.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production**
  - Conduct needs assessment and define target audience
  - Develop project timeline and milestones
  - Scriptwriting: Create a detailed script covering key topics such as:
    - Effective communication techniques
    - Overcoming anxiety
    - Audience engagement strategies
  - Storyboarding: Visual representation of the script to outline scenes and transitions

- **Production**
  - Set up filming location and equipment
  - Cast presenters and/or actors as needed
  - Filming: Capture all video segments according to the storyboard
  - Record voiceovers and any additional audio elements

- **Post-Production**
  - Video editing: Compile footage, add graphics, and incorporate interactive elements
  - Review and revisions: Gather feedback from stakeholders and make necessary adjustments
  - Finalize video for quality assurance

- **Delivery**
  - Format the final video for compatibility with online learning platforms
  - Create supplementary materials (e.g., handouts, quizzes)
  - Launch the video and monitor initial feedback

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of needs assessment and project timeline
  - **Deliverable**: Project timeline document

- **Milestone 2**: Finalized script and storyboard
  - **Deliverable**: Approved script and storyboard

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of filming
  - **Deliverable**: Raw video footage

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of video editing and revisions
  - **Deliverable**: Draft version of the training video

- **Milestone 5**: Final video delivery and supplementary materials
  - **Deliverable**: Finalized training video and accompanying resources
