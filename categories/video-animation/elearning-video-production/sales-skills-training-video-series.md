
# Project Plan Template: Sales Skills Training Video Series

## 1. Executive Summary

The Sales Skills Training Video Series aims to empower sales professionals with essential skills and techniques necessary for excelling in their roles. This project will produce a comprehensive series of engaging eLearning videos that incorporate role-playing scenarios to simulate real-world sales situations. Each video will feature interactive exercises designed to encourage active participation, allowing learners to practice and refine their sales techniques in a safe environment. The series will also include expert tips and strategies for improving sales performance, ensuring that participants gain valuable insights that can be applied immediately. By encompassing all stages of video production—from initial concept development and scriptwriting to filming, editing, and final delivery—this project will ensure a polished and professional end product that meets the educational needs of sales professionals. Key outcomes include enhanced sales skills, increased confidence in sales interactions, and measurable improvements in sales performance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Sales Skills Training Video Series includes the following components:

- **Concept Development**: Collaborate with stakeholders to define the objectives, target audience, and key topics for the video series.
- **Scriptwriting**: Develop detailed scripts for each video, incorporating role-playing scenarios and expert insights.
- **Storyboarding**: Create visual storyboards to outline the flow and structure of each video.
- **Filming**: Conduct on-location or studio filming sessions, utilizing professional equipment and talent to capture high-quality footage.
- **Editing**: Edit the raw footage to produce polished videos, incorporating graphics, animations, and interactive elements as needed.
- **Quality Assurance**: Review and revise videos based on feedback from stakeholders to ensure alignment with project objectives.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the completed video series in the required formats for distribution on eLearning platforms.
