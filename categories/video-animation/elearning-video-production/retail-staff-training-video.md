
# Project Plan Template: Retail Staff Training Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Retail Staff Training Video project aims to create an engaging and informative eLearning video tailored specifically for retail employees. The primary objective of this project is to equip retail staff with essential skills and knowledge necessary for effective customer interaction, efficient inventory management, and proven sales techniques. By incorporating role-playing scenarios, the video will provide staff with opportunities to practice and apply their skills in realistic situations, enhancing their learning experience. The project will involve thorough research, scriptwriting, storyboarding, filming, and post-production editing to ensure a polished final product. Key outcomes include improved customer satisfaction, increased sales performance, and a more knowledgeable workforce. This project will be executed with a focus on high-quality visuals and clear, concise messaging to facilitate effective learning, ultimately driving the success of the retail operation.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: Conduct thorough research on retail best practices, customer service techniques, and inventory management strategies.
- **Scriptwriting**: Develop a comprehensive script that covers all essential topics, ensuring clarity and engagement.
- **Storyboarding**: Create a storyboard that outlines the visual elements and flow of the video, including role-playing scenarios.
- **Filming**: Organize and execute the filming process, ensuring high-quality visuals and sound.
- **Post-Production Editing**: Edit the footage to create a cohesive and polished final product, incorporating graphics, animations, and other visual aids as necessary.
- **Review and Feedback**: Present the draft video to stakeholders for feedback and make necessary revisions.
- **Final Delivery**: Deliver the completed eLearning video in the required format for distribution to retail staff.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase  
  **Deliverable**: Research report summarizing findings and best practices.

- **Milestone 2**: Finalized Script  
  **Deliverable**: Approved script ready for storyboarding.

- **Milestone 3**: Completed Storyboard  
  **Deliverable**: Visual storyboard outlining the video sequence.

- **Milestone 4**: Filming Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Raw video footage ready for editing.

- **Milestone 5**: First Draft of Video  
  **Deliverable**: Initial edited version of the video for review.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Video Delivery  
  **Deliverable**: Completed eLearning video delivered in the required format.
