
# Project Plan Template: Corporate Welcome Video

## 1. Executive Summary
The Corporate Welcome Video project aims to create an inviting and informative video that serves as the first point of contact for new employees, clients, or partners. The primary objective is to produce a high-quality video featuring a professional spokesperson who embodies the values and culture of {{Company Name}}. This video will deliver a warm welcome message, an overview of the company's mission, core values, and essential information that new stakeholders need to know. By utilizing high-quality visuals and engaging storytelling techniques, the video will enhance the onboarding experience and strengthen the connection between {{Company Name}} and its audience. Key outcomes include increased engagement from new stakeholders, improved understanding of the company's culture, and a polished representation of the brand that can be shared across various platforms. The final product will be tailored to reflect {{Company Name}}'s unique identity and will be delivered in a format suitable for maximum reach and impact.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work
- **Pre-Production:**
  - Conduct initial meetings with stakeholders to gather requirements and expectations.
  - Develop a script that includes the welcome message, company mission, and core values.
  - Select a professional spokesperson who aligns with the company's culture.
  - Plan the video shoot, including location, props, and visual elements.

- **Production:**
  - Film the video with the selected spokesperson, ensuring high-quality audio and visuals.
  - Capture additional B-roll footage that represents the company environment and culture.

- **Post-Production:**
  - Edit the video to create a cohesive narrative, incorporating graphics, music, and branding elements.
  - Review the video with stakeholders for feedback and make necessary revisions.
  - Finalize the video and prepare it for distribution across various platforms.

- **Delivery:**
  - Provide the final video in multiple formats suitable for web, social media, and internal use.
  - Create a distribution plan to ensure the video reaches the intended audience effectively.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1:** Project Kickoff Meeting
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and project timeline.
  
- **Milestone 2:** Script Development Completion
  - Deliverable: Finalized script for the video.

- **Milestone 3:** Filming Completion
  - Deliverable: Raw video footage.

- **Milestone 4:** Video Editing Completion
  - Deliverable: First draft of the edited video.

- **Milestone 5:** Stakeholder Review and Feedback
  - Deliverable: Feedback document and revised video.

- **Milestone 6:** Final Video Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized video in multiple formats.
