
# Project Plan Template: Snapchat Geofilter Video Ads

## 1. Executive Summary

The Snapchat Geofilter Video Ads project aims to produce vibrant and engaging video advertisements specifically designed for Snapchat's unique platform. The primary objective is to create visually appealing content that resonates with a younger audience, leveraging Snapchat's geofilter features to enhance user interaction and engagement. This project will focus on incorporating dynamic elements such as animations, stickers, and interactive prompts that encourage viewers to participate and share the content. Key outcomes include the delivery of high-quality video ads that align with Snapchat's advertising guidelines and effectively reflect the client's brand identity and messaging. By utilizing innovative storytelling techniques and cutting-edge production methods, the project seeks to drive brand recognition and foster a deeper connection with the target demographic. Ultimately, the success of this project will be measured by the engagement metrics and feedback from the audience, ensuring that the final product not only meets but exceeds expectations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Brainstorming Sessions**: Conduct collaborative meetings with stakeholders to generate creative ideas and concepts for the video ads.
- **Storyboarding**: Develop detailed storyboards that outline the visual flow, key scenes, and messaging of the videos.
- **Video Production**: Execute the filming and animation processes, ensuring that all elements align with the approved storyboard.
- **Post-Production Editing**: Edit the footage, incorporating graphics, sound, and effects to create a polished final product.
- **Client Review and Revisions**: Present the initial drafts to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the completed video ads in the required formats for Snapchat's platform, ensuring compliance with all advertising guidelines.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of brainstorming sessions
  - **Deliverable**: Documented ideas and concepts
- **Milestone 2**: Finalized storyboards
  - **Deliverable**: Approved storyboards for video production
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of video production
  - **Deliverable**: Raw video footage
- **Milestone 4**: Completion of post-production editing
  - **Deliverable**: Edited video ads ready for review
- **Milestone 5**: Client review and feedback
  - **Deliverable**: Client feedback report
- **Milestone 6**: Final delivery of video ads
  - **Deliverable**: Final video files in required formats
