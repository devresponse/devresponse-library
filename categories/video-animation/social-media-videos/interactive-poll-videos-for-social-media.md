
# Project Plan Template: Interactive Poll Videos for Social Media

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Interactive Poll Videos for Social Media" project aims to produce a series of engaging and visually appealing videos designed specifically for various social media platforms. The primary objective is to create content that not only captures the audience's attention but also encourages active participation through embedded polls and questions. By aligning the videos with the client's branding and messaging, we ensure consistency across all social media channels. Key outcomes include increased user engagement, enhanced community interaction, and improved brand visibility. Each video will feature clear calls to action, prompting viewers to engage with the content, thereby fostering a sense of community around the brand. The project will involve close collaboration with the client to understand their target audience and objectives, followed by the creation, editing, and refinement of the video content. Ultimately, this project will deliver a series of interactive videos optimized for maximum reach and engagement across platforms.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Discovery**: 
  - Conduct meetings with the client to understand their brand, target audience, and specific objectives for the interactive poll videos.
  - Analyze competitor content and current trends in social media engagement.

- **Concept Development**: 
  - Develop creative concepts for the interactive poll videos that align with the client's branding.
  - Create storyboards outlining the flow of each video, including key messages and interactive elements.

- **Video Production**: 
  - Scriptwriting for each video, ensuring clarity and engagement.
  - Filming and editing of the videos, incorporating graphics, animations, and interactive elements.
  - Integration of polls and questions within the videos to encourage viewer participation.

- **Review and Feedback**: 
  - Present initial drafts to the client for feedback and revisions.
  - Implement necessary changes based on client input to ensure satisfaction.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Optimize videos for various social media platforms (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, TikTok).
  - Provide the client with final video files and any necessary guidelines for posting.

- **Post-Launch Support**: 
  - Monitor engagement metrics and provide a report on the performance of the videos.
  - Offer recommendations for future content based on audience interaction and feedback.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Discovery Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report and audience analysis document.

- **Milestone 2**: Approval of Video Concepts and Storyboards
  - Deliverable: Finalized storyboards for each video.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Video Production
  - Deliverable: Draft versions of all interactive poll videos.

- **Milestone 4**: Client Review and Feedback
  - Deliverable: Revised videos based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery of Optimized Videos
  - Deliverable: Final video files optimized for each social media platform.

- **Milestone 6**: Post-Launch Performance Report
  - Deliverable: Engagement metrics report and recommendations.
