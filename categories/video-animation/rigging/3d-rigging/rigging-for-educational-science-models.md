
# Project Plan Template: Rigging for Educational Science Models

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Rigging for Educational Science Models" project aims to develop advanced rigging solutions for 3D models tailored specifically for educational animations. The primary objective is to create skeletal structures that facilitate realistic and fluid movements of various scientific models, including anatomical representations of the human body and intricate molecular structures. By collaborating closely with subject matter experts, we will ensure that the rigs are not only functional but also accurate, enhancing the educational value of the animations produced. Key outcomes of this project include the delivery of detailed rigging documentation, a set of fully functional rigs for each model, and a consultation session to guide animators in implementing these rigs within their animation software. This initiative is crucial for producing high-quality educational content that captivates audiences and fosters a deeper understanding of complex scientific concepts.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Development**: Conduct thorough research on the anatomical and molecular structures to be rigged, ensuring accuracy in representation.
- **Collaboration with Experts**: Engage with subject matter experts to gather insights and feedback throughout the rigging process.
- **Rig Creation**: Develop advanced rigs for each selected 3D model, focusing on achieving realistic movements and flexibility.
- **Testing and Iteration**: Perform rigorous testing of the rigs to ensure functionality and make necessary adjustments based on feedback.
- **Documentation**: Create comprehensive rigging documentation that outlines the rigging process, usage instructions, and troubleshooting tips.
- **Consultation Session**: Provide a consultation session for animators to discuss the implementation of the rigs in their animation software, addressing any questions or concerns.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Development
  - Deliverable: Research report and model selection confirmation
  
- **Milestone 2**: Initial Rig Creation
  - Deliverable: First draft of rigs for selected models
  
- **Milestone 3**: Testing and Feedback
  - Deliverable: Feedback report and revised rigs
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Rig Delivery
  - Deliverable: Fully functional rigs for each model
  
- **Milestone 5**: Documentation and Consultation
  - Deliverable: Complete rigging documentation and consultation session
