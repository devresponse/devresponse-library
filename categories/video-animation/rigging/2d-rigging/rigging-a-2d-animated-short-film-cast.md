
# Project Plan Template: Rigging a 2D Animated Short Film Cast

## 1. Executive Summary

The project "Rigging a 2D Animated Short Film Cast" aims to create skeletal rigs for all characters featured in the animated short film, enhancing their movement and expressiveness. The primary objective is to ensure that each character can perform a range of fluid and dynamic movements that align with their personalities and roles within the story. This will involve defining intricate bone structures, setting up control points for animation, and collaborating closely with the animation team to capture unique movements and expressions. Key outcomes include fully functional rigs that are compatible with the animation software used in the project, which will significantly elevate the storytelling aspect of the film. By focusing on the rigging process, we aim to contribute to the overall quality and impact of the animated short film, ensuring that the characters resonate with the audience and effectively convey the narrative.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Character Analysis**: Review character designs and understand their personalities, roles, and specific movement requirements.
- **Bone Structure Definition**: Create detailed bone structures for each character, ensuring they allow for a wide range of motion.
- **Control Point Setup**: Establish control points that facilitate intuitive animation, allowing animators to manipulate characters easily.
- **Collaboration with Animation Team**: Conduct regular meetings with the animation team to gather feedback and adjust rigs according to their needs.
- **Testing and Refinement**: Rig characters and conduct tests to ensure movements are fluid and meet the animation requirements. Make necessary adjustments based on testing feedback.
- **Final Deliverables**: Provide fully functional rigs for each character, along with documentation on how to use them within the animation software.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of character analysis and initial bone structure designs.
  - **Deliverable**: Character analysis report and preliminary bone structure sketches.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Setup of control points for all characters.
  - **Deliverable**: Control point setup documentation and initial rigging prototypes.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Collaboration sessions with the animation team for feedback.
  - **Deliverable**: Meeting notes and feedback implementation plan.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Completion of testing and refinement phase.
  - **Deliverable**: Finalized rigs for each character, along with testing results.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of final rigs and documentation.
  - **Deliverable**: Fully functional character rigs and user guide for animators.
