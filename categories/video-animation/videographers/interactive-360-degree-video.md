
# Project Plan Template: Interactive 360-Degree Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Interactive 360-Degree Video project aims to create a captivating and immersive video experience that allows viewers to explore environments from multiple angles. This project will serve various applications, including virtual tours for real estate properties, museums, and event spaces, as well as enhancing experiential marketing campaigns. The primary objective is to engage audiences effectively by providing them with an interactive platform that encourages exploration and interaction. Key outcomes include a high-quality, interactive video file that meets the client's specifications, seamless navigation capabilities, and a final product that can be easily shared across multiple platforms. By collaborating closely with the client throughout the process, we will ensure that the final deliverable aligns with their vision and objectives, ultimately enhancing their marketing and engagement strategies.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production Planning**
  - Initial consultation with the client to understand project objectives and requirements.
  - Development of a project timeline and milestones.
  - Location scouting and selection of filming sites.
  - Scriptwriting and storyboarding to outline the video flow and interactive elements.
  - Coordination of necessary permits and permissions for filming.

- **On-Site Filming**
  - Setup of 360-degree camera equipment and other necessary filming gear.
  - Filming of the selected locations, ensuring high-quality footage is captured.
  - Direction and management of any talent or participants involved in the video.

- **Post-Production Editing**
  - Editing of the raw footage to create a cohesive and engaging narrative.
  - Integration of interactive elements that allow viewers to navigate through the video.
  - Color correction, sound design, and any necessary visual effects to enhance the final product.

- **Final Review and Delivery**
  - Presentation of the draft video to the client for feedback and revisions.
  - Final adjustments based on client input.
  - Delivery of the high-quality interactive video file in the required formats for various platforms.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Project timeline and initial consultation notes.
  
- **Milestone 2: Pre-Production Completion**
  - Deliverable: Finalized script, storyboard, and location confirmations.

- **Milestone 3: Filming Completion**
  - Deliverable: Raw footage from all filming sessions.

- **Milestone 4: Post-Production Completion**
  - Deliverable: Draft version of the interactive video for client review.

- **Milestone 5: Final Delivery**
  - Deliverable: High-quality interactive video file and documentation for sharing.
