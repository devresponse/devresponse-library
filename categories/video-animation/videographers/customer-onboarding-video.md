
# Project Plan Template: Customer Onboarding Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Customer Onboarding Video project aims to create an engaging and informative video that assists new customers in navigating the onboarding process with ease. The primary objective is to develop a visual guide that highlights the key features and functionalities of the product or service, ensuring that users can maximize their experience from the outset. This project will involve close collaboration between the videographer and the client to identify and emphasize the specific aspects of the product that are most beneficial for new users. Key outcomes include a clear narrative, visually appealing graphics, and step-by-step instructions that resonate with the target audience. By providing a comprehensive onboarding experience, this video is expected to enhance customer satisfaction and significantly reduce the learning curve associated with new products, ultimately leading to increased customer retention and loyalty.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production:**
  - Initial consultation with the client to understand the product and onboarding process.
  - Development of a project timeline and milestones.
  - Scripting the video content based on client input and product features.
  - Creating a storyboard to visualize the flow of the video.

- **Production:**
  - Filming the video, including interviews, demonstrations, and product showcases.
  - Capturing high-quality audio and video footage.

- **Post-Production:**
  - Editing the footage to create a cohesive and engaging narrative.
  - Adding graphics, animations, and text overlays to enhance understanding.
  - Incorporating background music and sound effects to improve viewer engagement.
  - Reviewing the draft video with the client for feedback and revisions.

- **Final Delivery:**
  - Providing the final video in multiple formats suitable for various platforms (e.g., website, social media).
  - Offering a brief training session for the client on how to use and distribute the video effectively.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1:** Project Kickoff
  - Deliverable: Project timeline and initial meeting notes.
  
- **Milestone 2:** Script and Storyboard Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized script and storyboard for client approval.

- **Milestone 3:** Filming Completion
  - Deliverable: All raw footage captured and stored.

- **Milestone 4:** First Draft of Video
  - Deliverable: Initial edited version of the video for client review.

- **Milestone 5:** Final Video Delivery
  - Deliverable: Completed onboarding video in agreed formats.
