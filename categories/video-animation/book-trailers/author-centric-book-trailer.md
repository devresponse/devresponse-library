
# Project Plan Template: Author-Centric Book Trailer

## 1. Executive Summary

The Author-Centric Book Trailer project aims to create a captivating video that highlights the author and their literary work. The primary objective of this project is to produce a high-quality book trailer that effectively communicates the author’s message and entices viewers to explore the book further. This unique trailer will feature the author discussing the inspiration behind their book, the themes explored, and what readers can anticipate from the narrative. By integrating engaging visuals and a carefully selected musical score, the trailer will not only showcase the author's personality but also evoke the emotional essence of the book. Key outcomes include increased visibility for the book in a competitive market, enhanced engagement with potential readers, and a powerful marketing tool that can be shared across various platforms. The project will involve pre-production planning, filming the author’s insights, and post-production editing to ensure a polished and professional final video.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production Planning**
  - Conduct initial meetings with the author to discuss vision and objectives.
  - Develop a script outline based on the author’s insights and themes of the book.
  - Scout locations for filming, considering settings that reflect the book's themes.
  - Schedule filming dates and coordinate logistics.

- **Filming**
  - Capture high-quality video footage of the author discussing their book.
  - Film additional b-roll footage that complements the author’s narrative.
  - Ensure proper lighting and sound quality during filming.

- **Post-Production Editing**
  - Edit the footage to create a cohesive and engaging narrative.
  - Integrate visuals, graphics, and a carefully selected musical score.
  - Review and revise the trailer based on feedback from the author.
  - Finalize the video for distribution.

- **Distribution Strategy**
  - Develop a marketing plan for the trailer’s release.
  - Identify platforms for sharing the trailer (e.g., social media, author’s website).
  - Create promotional materials to accompany the trailer launch.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Initial project meeting notes and confirmed project timeline.

- **Milestone 2: Script and Storyboard Completion**
  - Deliverable: Finalized script and storyboard for the trailer.

- **Milestone 3: Filming Completion**
  - Deliverable: All video footage captured and stored.

- **Milestone 4: First Cut of Trailer**
  - Deliverable: First draft of the edited trailer for review.

- **Milestone 5: Final Trailer Delivery**
  - Deliverable: Polished and finalized book trailer ready for distribution.
