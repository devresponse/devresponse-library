
# Project Plan Template: Nonprofit Website Preview Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Nonprofit Website Preview Video project aims to create a captivating video animation that effectively showcases the nonprofit organization's website. The primary objective is to highlight the organization's mission and visually represent its core values and objectives. This project will deliver a high-quality animation that not only informs potential donors and supporters about the website's features but also inspires them to engage with the nonprofit. Key outcomes include a clear demonstration of the website's design and navigation, with special emphasis on donation functionalities, illustrating how users can contribute seamlessly to the cause. By tailoring the animation to resonate with the target audience, we aim to foster a sense of community and support, ultimately enhancing the nonprofit's online presence and driving user engagement through compelling visual storytelling.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Discovery**: 
  - Conduct interviews with nonprofit stakeholders to understand the organization's mission, values, and objectives.
  - Analyze the current website and identify key features to highlight in the video.

- **Script Development**: 
  - Create a compelling script that outlines the narrative of the video, focusing on the nonprofit's mission and donation process.

- **Storyboarding**: 
  - Develop a storyboard that visually represents the script, detailing each scene and animation.

- **Animation Production**: 
  - Create high-quality animations that align with the storyboard, ensuring that the visuals are engaging and informative.

- **Voiceover and Sound Design**: 
  - Record a professional voiceover that complements the animation.
  - Incorporate background music and sound effects to enhance the viewing experience.

- **Review and Revisions**: 
  - Present the initial draft to stakeholders for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on feedback received.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Deliver the final video in multiple formats suitable for various platforms (e.g., website, social media).

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Discovery
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and insights.

- **Milestone 2**: Script Development
  - Deliverable: Finalized script for the video.

- **Milestone 3**: Storyboarding
  - Deliverable: Completed storyboard with visual representations.

- **Milestone 4**: Animation Production
  - Deliverable: First draft of the animated video.

- **Milestone 5**: Review and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised video based on stakeholder feedback.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Final high-quality video in multiple formats.
