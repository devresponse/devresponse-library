
# Project Plan Template: Subtitles for Legal and Compliance Videos

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Subtitles for Legal and Compliance Videos" project aims to enhance the accessibility and understanding of critical legal and compliance information through high-quality subtitle production. In today's fast-paced digital landscape, organizations must convey important information clearly and effectively to diverse stakeholders. This project will meticulously analyze video content to ensure that every term, phrase, and nuance is accurately captured, adhering to industry-specific terminology and regulatory standards. Key outcomes include the delivery of synchronized subtitles that not only improve viewer comprehension but also comply with legal requirements. By providing a seamless viewing experience, this project will support organizations in maintaining transparency and accessibility in their communications, ultimately fostering better engagement with essential content.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Content Analysis**: Review and analyze the video material to identify key legal and compliance terms and phrases.
- **Transcription**: Create a verbatim transcription of the spoken content in the videos.
- **Subtitle Creation**: Develop high-quality subtitles that accurately reflect the transcription, ensuring adherence to industry-specific terminology.
- **Quality Assurance**: Implement a multi-step review process to ensure accuracy, clarity, and compliance with legal standards.
- **Synchronization**: Align subtitles with the video content for a seamless viewing experience.
- **Final Review**: Conduct a final review with stakeholders to ensure satisfaction with the subtitles.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final subtitle files in the required formats for integration with the video content.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Project Kickoff
  - Deliverable: Project initiation document and timeline.

- **Milestone 2**: Content Analysis Completion
  - Deliverable: Analysis report highlighting key terms and phrases.

- **Milestone 3**: Transcription Completion
  - Deliverable: Complete verbatim transcription of video content.

- **Milestone 4**: Subtitle Draft Submission
  - Deliverable: First draft of subtitles for review.

- **Milestone 5**: Quality Assurance Review
  - Deliverable: QA report with identified issues and corrections.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Subtitle Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized subtitle files in required formats.
