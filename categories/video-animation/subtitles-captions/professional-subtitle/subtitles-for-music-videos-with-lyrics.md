
# Project Plan Template: Subtitles for Music Videos with Lyrics

## 1. Executive Summary

The project "Subtitles for Music Videos with Lyrics" aims to create synchronized subtitles for a selection of music videos, ensuring that the lyrics are accurately timed to align perfectly with the audio. The primary objective is to enhance accessibility and viewer engagement by providing both original lyrics and translations in multiple languages. This will cater to a broader audience, allowing non-native speakers to enjoy the music fully. The project will involve meticulous listening to each track, precise timing adjustments, and the use of professional subtitle software to guarantee high-quality output. Key outcomes include the delivery of subtitles in various formats suitable for different platforms, ensuring compatibility and ease of use. Ultimately, this project is essential for artists and content creators who wish to reach diverse audiences and improve the overall viewer experience through clear and engaging subtitles.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Identify and select music videos for subtitle creation.
  - Gather original lyrics and necessary translations.

- **Subtitle Creation Process**
  - Listen to each music track to understand the flow and timing.
  - Create initial subtitle drafts with original lyrics.
  - Translate lyrics into selected languages.
  - Synchronize subtitles with audio, ensuring accurate timing.

- **Quality Assurance**
  - Review subtitles for accuracy in timing and translation.
  - Conduct testing on various platforms to ensure compatibility.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on feedback.

- **Final Delivery**
  - Export subtitles in multiple formats (e.g., SRT, VTT, ASS).
  - Provide a comprehensive report detailing the subtitle creation process.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Project initiation report, including selected music videos and translation languages.

- **Milestone 2: Initial Subtitle Drafts**
  - Deliverable: Draft subtitles for all selected music videos.

- **Milestone 3: Quality Assurance Review**
  - Deliverable: QA report with feedback and adjustments made.

- **Milestone 4: Final Delivery**
  - Deliverable: Final subtitles in various formats and a comprehensive project report.
