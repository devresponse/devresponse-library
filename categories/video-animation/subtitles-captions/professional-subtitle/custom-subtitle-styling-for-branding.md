
# Project Plan: Custom Subtitle Styling for Branding

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's digital landscape, maintaining a strong brand identity is crucial, even in subtitle presentation. The objective of the "Custom Subtitle Styling for Branding" project is to design and implement custom subtitle styles that reflect your brand's unique visual identity. This project will begin with a comprehensive consultation to understand your brand's aesthetics, including preferred fonts, color schemes, and overall design guidelines. Key outcomes include the creation of a series of subtitle style mockups for your review, ensuring they align seamlessly with your video content. Once the styles are approved, we will implement them across your video assets, ensuring proper synchronization and placement for optimal viewer engagement. The final deliverable will not only enhance your video content but also reinforce your brand identity, making your videos more recognizable and appealing to your audience. By the end of this project, you will have a cohesive subtitle style that enhances your brand's presence in the digital space.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct an initial meeting to discuss brand aesthetics.
  - Gather information on preferred fonts, color schemes, and design guidelines.
  
- **Design Phase**: 
  - Create a series of subtitle style mockups based on the consultation.
  - Present mockups for client feedback and revisions.
  
- **Implementation Phase**: 
  - Finalize the approved subtitle styles.
  - Implement the styles across all designated video assets.
  - Ensure proper synchronization and placement of subtitles for optimal viewer engagement.
  
- **Review Phase**: 
  - Conduct a final review of the implemented subtitles.
  - Make any necessary adjustments based on client feedback.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the consultation phase.
  - **Deliverable**: Summary document of brand aesthetics and preferences.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Delivery of initial subtitle style mockups.
  - **Deliverable**: A set of 3-5 mockups for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Approval of final subtitle styles.
  - **Deliverable**: Documented approval from the client.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Implementation of subtitle styles across video assets.
  - **Deliverable**: All videos updated with new subtitle styles.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final review and adjustments.
  - **Deliverable**: Final report summarizing the project and any adjustments made.
