
# Project Plan Template: Educational Training Explainer

## 1. Executive Summary

The Educational Training Explainer project aims to create an engaging live-action video that simplifies complex educational concepts and training materials into easily digestible content for both students and employees. The primary objective is to enhance understanding and retention of key topics by collaborating with subject matter experts to identify and distill essential information. The video will incorporate real-life scenarios and relatable characters, making the learning experience more engaging and motivational. Key outcomes include a polished final video that serves as both an educational tool and a motivational resource, encouraging viewers to actively engage with the material. By utilizing effective storytelling and visual elements, this project seeks to transform traditional learning methods and foster a more interactive educational environment.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Conduct a kickoff meeting with stakeholders to outline project objectives and expectations.
  - Identify and engage subject matter experts for collaboration.

- **Research and Development**
  - Gather and analyze existing educational materials and training content.
  - Collaborate with subject matter experts to identify key topics for simplification.

- **Script Development**
  - Create a detailed script that outlines the narrative, key messages, and educational content.
  - Review and revise the script based on feedback from stakeholders and subject matter experts.

- **Storyboarding**
  - Develop a storyboard that visually represents the script, including scenes, characters, and transitions.
  - Present the storyboard for approval before proceeding to filming.

- **Filming**
  - Schedule and coordinate filming sessions, including location scouting and talent casting.
  - Capture high-quality video footage in alignment with the approved storyboard.

- **Post-Production Editing**
  - Edit the footage to create a cohesive and engaging final product.
  - Incorporate graphics, animations, and sound design to enhance the educational content.

- **Review and Revisions**
  - Conduct review sessions with stakeholders to gather feedback on the initial cut.
  - Implement necessary revisions to ensure the final video meets client expectations.

- **Final Delivery**
  - Deliver the polished final video in the agreed-upon formats.
  - Provide supplementary materials, such as a behind-the-scenes video or a discussion guide, if applicable.
