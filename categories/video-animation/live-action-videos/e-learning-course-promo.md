
# Project Plan Template: E-Learning Course Promo

## 1. Executive Summary

The E-Learning Course Promo project aims to create a high-quality live-action explainer video that effectively promotes an e-learning course. The primary objective is to showcase the course's content while emphasizing its unique benefits and the specific audience it targets. This project will involve a comprehensive production process, including scripting, storyboarding, filming, and post-production editing, to ensure a polished final product that is both engaging and informative. Key outcomes include a video that utilizes real-life scenarios and testimonials to resonate with potential learners, ultimately inspiring them to enroll in the course. Collaboration with the client will be essential throughout the project to gather insights about the course and its audience, ensuring that the final product aligns with their vision and marketing goals. By delivering a compelling promotional video, we aim to enhance the client's marketing efforts and drive enrollment in the e-learning course.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production**
  - Initial client meetings to gather insights about the course and target audience.
  - Development of a detailed project timeline and milestones.
  - Creation of a script that highlights the course's unique benefits.
  - Storyboarding to visualize the flow of the video and key scenes.

- **Production**
  - Scheduling and organizing filming days, including location scouting.
  - Casting actors or presenters, if necessary, to deliver testimonials and course highlights.
  - Filming the video, ensuring high production quality and engaging visuals.

- **Post-Production**
  - Editing the footage to create a cohesive and engaging narrative.
  - Incorporating graphics, animations, and music to enhance the video.
  - Client review sessions to gather feedback and make necessary revisions.
  - Finalizing the video for delivery in the required formats.

- **Delivery**
  - Providing the final video to the client along with any additional promotional materials.
  - Offering guidance on how to effectively use the video in marketing campaigns.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Project timeline and initial meeting notes.
  
- **Milestone 2: Script and Storyboard Approval**
  - Deliverable: Finalized script and storyboard for client approval.

- **Milestone 3: Filming Completion**
  - Deliverable: All raw footage captured and organized.

- **Milestone 4: First Edit Review**
  - Deliverable: First cut of the video for client feedback.

- **Milestone 5: Final Video Delivery**
  - Deliverable: Polished final video and promotional materials.
