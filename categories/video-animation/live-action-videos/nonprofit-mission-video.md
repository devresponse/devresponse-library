
# Project Plan Template: Nonprofit Mission Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Nonprofit Mission Video project aims to create a compelling live-action explainer video that effectively communicates the essence of {{Nonprofit Organization Name}}. The primary objective is to highlight the organization's mission, core values, and the significant impact it has on the community. This project will utilize real-life footage, testimonials, and engaging storytelling techniques to resonate with potential donors and supporters, fostering a deeper understanding of the nonprofit's goals. Key outcomes include a polished video that aligns with the nonprofit's branding and messaging, increased awareness of the organization's initiatives, and enhanced outreach capabilities to attract funding and support. By showcasing the dedication and tangible results of {{Nonprofit Organization Name}}'s efforts, the final product will serve as a powerful tool for engagement and advocacy, ultimately contributing to the organization's sustainability and growth.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Nonprofit Mission Video project includes the following phases:

- **Pre-Production Planning**: 
  - Initial meetings with stakeholders to gather insights and objectives.
  - Development of a project timeline and budget.
  - Research and selection of filming locations.
  - Casting of individuals for testimonials and interviews.
  - Scriptwriting that encapsulates the nonprofit's mission and values.

- **Production**:
  - Filming of interviews, b-roll footage, and any necessary scenes that illustrate the nonprofit's work.
  - Coordination of filming schedules with all participants.
  - Ensuring all necessary equipment and crew are in place for a smooth shoot.

- **Post-Production**:
  - Editing of the footage to create a cohesive narrative.
  - Incorporation of graphics, music, and sound effects as needed.
  - Review and revisions based on feedback from stakeholders.
  - Finalization of the video for distribution.

- **Delivery**:
  - Providing the final video in multiple formats suitable for various platforms (e.g., social media, website, presentations).
  - Creation of a distribution plan to maximize outreach.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Pre-Production Planning
  - Deliverable: Finalized script and production schedule.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Filming
  - Deliverable: All raw footage captured and organized.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Editing
  - Deliverable: First draft of the video for review.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Review and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Finalized video ready for distribution.

- **Milestone 5**: Video Delivery and Distribution Plan
  - Deliverable: Final video files and distribution strategy document.
