
# Project Plan Template: Employee Onboarding Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Employee Onboarding Video project aims to create an engaging live-action explainer video that effectively communicates the essential aspects of the onboarding process for new employees. The primary objective is to provide newcomers with a clear understanding of the company's culture, values, and operational policies. By utilizing real-life scenarios and relatable characters, the video will simplify complex information, making it accessible and memorable. Key outcomes include increased employee engagement, improved retention rates, and a smoother transition into the company for new hires. This video will serve as a vital resource, ensuring that new employees feel welcomed and informed from day one. The final product will be tailored to reflect the unique identity of the organization, reinforcing its brand and mission. The project encompasses script development, filming, editing, and final delivery of the video in high-quality format, ready for distribution across various platforms.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production:**
  - Conduct stakeholder interviews to gather insights on company culture and onboarding processes.
  - Develop a comprehensive script that outlines the key messages and information to be conveyed.
  - Create a storyboard to visualize the scenes and flow of the video.
  - Identify and secure locations for filming, as well as necessary props and costumes.
  - Cast actors or employees to portray characters in the video.

- **Production:**
  - Schedule and conduct filming sessions, ensuring all scenes are captured according to the storyboard.
  - Utilize professional equipment to ensure high-quality video and audio capture.

- **Post-Production:**
  - Edit the footage to create a cohesive and engaging final product.
  - Incorporate graphics, animations, and music to enhance the viewing experience.
  - Conduct review sessions with stakeholders to gather feedback and make necessary revisions.
  - Finalize the video and prepare it for distribution in various formats.

- **Delivery:**
  - Provide the final video in high-quality format suitable for online platforms and internal use.
  - Offer guidance on how to effectively distribute and utilize the video within the onboarding process.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1:** Completion of script and storyboard
  - **Deliverable:** Finalized script and storyboard document

- **Milestone 2:** Filming completed
  - **Deliverable:** Raw footage from all filming sessions

- **Milestone 3:** First draft of the edited video
  - **Deliverable:** Initial edited version of the video for review

- **Milestone 4:** Final video delivery
  - **Deliverable:** High-quality final video file ready for distribution
