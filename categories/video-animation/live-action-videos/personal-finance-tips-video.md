
# Project Plan Template: Personal Finance Tips Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Personal Finance Tips Video" project aims to create an engaging live-action explainer video that simplifies personal finance concepts for a broad audience. The primary objective is to demystify essential topics such as budgeting, saving, investing, and debt management, making them accessible and relatable. By utilizing real-life scenarios and expert insights, the video will break down complex financial concepts into easily digestible segments, empowering viewers to make informed financial decisions. Key outcomes include a polished and professional video that resonates with viewers, encourages actionable steps towards better financial health, and ultimately contributes to improved financial literacy in the community. This project will encompass scriptwriting, storyboarding, filming, and post-production editing, ensuring a comprehensive approach to delivering high-quality content.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Scriptwriting**: Develop a comprehensive script that outlines the key financial concepts to be covered, ensuring clarity and engagement.
- **Storyboarding**: Create a visual storyboard that maps out each scene, including camera angles, transitions, and key visuals to support the narrative.
- **Filming**: Conduct live-action filming, utilizing actors and real-life scenarios to illustrate financial concepts in a relatable manner.
- **Post-Production Editing**: Edit the footage to create a cohesive and polished final product, incorporating graphics, animations, and sound effects as needed.
- **Feedback and Revisions**: Present the draft video to stakeholders for feedback and make necessary revisions to ensure alignment with project objectives.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the completed video in multiple formats suitable for various platforms, along with a promotional plan for distribution.
