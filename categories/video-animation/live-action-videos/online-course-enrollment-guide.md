
# Project Plan Template: Online Course Enrollment Guide

## 1. Executive Summary

The Online Course Enrollment Guide project aims to create an engaging live-action explainer video that simplifies the enrollment process for prospective students. The primary objective of this project is to demystify the enrollment journey, making it accessible and inviting for potential students. By featuring real-life scenarios and relatable characters, the video will illustrate each step of the enrollment process, from discovering the course to completing registration. Key outcomes include a polished video that not only informs but also motivates viewers to take action. The final product will be designed for versatility, suitable for use on the course website and across various social media platforms, enhancing student engagement and increasing enrollment rates. This project will leverage a clear narrative, visually appealing graphics, and a friendly tone to ensure that the enrollment process is perceived as straightforward and approachable.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production**
  - Conduct stakeholder meetings to gather requirements and expectations.
  - Develop a script that outlines the narrative and key messages.
  - Create storyboards to visualize scenes and transitions.
  - Cast actors who can portray relatable characters effectively.
  - Scout and secure filming locations that reflect the enrollment journey.

- **Production**
  - Schedule and conduct filming sessions.
  - Capture high-quality video footage and audio.
  - Ensure all scenes align with the storyboard and script.

- **Post-Production**
  - Edit the video to create a cohesive narrative.
  - Incorporate graphics and animations to enhance visual appeal.
  - Add background music and sound effects to create an engaging atmosphere.
  - Review and revise the video based on stakeholder feedback.

- **Distribution**
  - Prepare the final video for various platforms (website, social media).
  - Develop a marketing strategy to promote the video to prospective students.
  - Monitor engagement metrics and gather feedback for future improvements.
