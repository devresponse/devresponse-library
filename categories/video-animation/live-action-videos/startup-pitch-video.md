
# Project Plan Template: Startup Pitch Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Startup Pitch Video project aims to create a compelling live-action explainer video that effectively communicates the essence of a startup's business idea to potential investors. The primary objective is to simplify complex concepts and showcase the startup's unique value proposition through engaging visuals and real-life footage. This project will involve several key phases, including scripting, storyboarding, filming, and post-production editing, ensuring a polished final product that resonates with the target audience. Key outcomes include a high-quality video that highlights the core problem the startup addresses, the innovative solution it offers, and the market potential that supports its growth. By delivering a memorable and impactful presentation tool, this project will significantly increase the startup's chances of securing investment and fostering growth.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production Phase**
  - Initial consultation with the startup team to understand the business idea and objectives.
  - Development of a detailed script that outlines the narrative and key messages.
  - Creation of a storyboard to visualize the flow of the video and identify necessary scenes.
  - Selection of locations, actors, and any additional resources required for filming.

- **Production Phase**
  - Filming of the live-action scenes as per the storyboard.
  - Direction and supervision of the filming process to ensure alignment with the script.
  - Collection of supplementary footage and b-roll to enhance the storytelling.

- **Post-Production Phase**
  - Editing of the filmed content to create a cohesive and engaging final video.
  - Incorporation of graphics, animations, and sound effects as needed.
  - Review and revisions based on feedback from the startup team.
  - Finalization of the video and preparation of supplementary materials that outline the key points covered.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Project initiation meeting notes and action items.
  
- **Milestone 2: Script and Storyboard Completion**
  - Deliverable: Finalized script and storyboard for approval.

- **Milestone 3: Filming Completion**
  - Deliverable: All raw footage collected and organized.

- **Milestone 4: First Cut of Video**
  - Deliverable: Initial edit of the video for review.

- **Milestone 5: Final Video Delivery**
  - Deliverable: High-quality final video and supplementary materials ready for presentation.
