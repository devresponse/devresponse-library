
# Project Plan Template: Flash Sale Countdown Ad

## 1. Executive Summary

The Flash Sale Countdown Ad project aims to create a visually striking and engaging short video advertisement that effectively promotes an upcoming flash sale. The primary objective of this project is to develop a compelling video that not only highlights the urgency of the sale through a dynamic countdown timer but also encourages viewers to take immediate action. This ad will encapsulate the essence of the brand while showcasing the products or services on sale, ensuring that the messaging aligns with the client's marketing goals and brand identity. Key outcomes include a polished video ad ready for deployment across various digital platforms, such as social media, websites, and email marketing campaigns. The project timeline is strategically designed to ensure timely delivery ahead of the flash sale event, maximizing its impact and driving customer engagement.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Concept Development**: Collaborate with the client to brainstorm and finalize the creative concept for the video ad, including key messaging and visual style.
- **Scriptwriting**: Develop a concise and engaging script that effectively communicates the urgency of the flash sale and highlights featured products or services.
- **Storyboarding**: Create a storyboard to visualize the flow of the video, including key scenes, transitions, and the countdown timer integration.
- **Video Production**: 
  - **Filming**: Capture high-quality footage as per the approved storyboard.
  - **Editing**: Edit the footage to create a cohesive and engaging video, incorporating graphics, animations, and the countdown timer.
- **Client Review**: Present the draft video to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Final Delivery**: Deliver the final polished video ad in multiple formats suitable for various digital platforms.
- **Post-Launch Support**: Provide guidance on how to effectively use the video across different channels and monitor its performance.
