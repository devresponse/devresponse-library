
# Project Plan Template: Explainer Video for New Service

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the "Explainer Video for New Service" project is to create a high-quality, engaging video that effectively introduces and communicates the unique value proposition of our new service. This project aims to enhance customer understanding of the service's benefits and functionalities, ultimately driving interest and encouraging action. The project will commence with a comprehensive consultation to gather insights about the service, target audience, and key messaging. Following this, a well-crafted script will be developed, focusing on clarity and engagement. Visual elements will be designed to complement the narrative, utilizing animations and graphics that resonate with our brand identity. The final deliverable will be a polished and concise explainer video that serves as a powerful marketing tool, suitable for deployment across various platforms, including websites, social media, and promotional campaigns. Key outcomes include increased customer awareness, improved engagement metrics, and a measurable uptick in service inquiries.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct initial meetings with stakeholders to gather insights about the new service.
  - Identify target audience demographics and preferences.
  - Define key messaging and unique selling points.

- **Script Development**: 
  - Create a draft script that outlines the narrative structure.
  - Ensure the script is clear, engaging, and aligned with the brand voice.
  - Review and revise the script based on feedback from stakeholders.

- **Visual Design**: 
  - Develop storyboards that illustrate the visual flow of the video.
  - Create animations and graphics that align with the script and brand identity.
  - Ensure all visual elements enhance the narrative and maintain audience engagement.

- **Video Production**: 
  - Record voiceovers and any necessary audio elements.
  - Edit the video to combine visuals, audio, and effects seamlessly.
  - Conduct quality assurance checks to ensure the final product meets all standards.

- **Final Review and Delivery**: 
  - Present the final video to stakeholders for approval.
  - Make any necessary adjustments based on feedback.
  - Deliver the final video in multiple formats suitable for various platforms.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Consultation Phase
  - Deliverable: Consultation Summary Document

- **Milestone 2**: Script Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized Script

- **Milestone 3**: Storyboard Approval
  - Deliverable: Approved Storyboards

- **Milestone 4**: Video Production Completion
  - Deliverable: Draft Video for Review

- **Milestone 5**: Final Video Delivery
  - Deliverable: Final Explainer Video in required formats
