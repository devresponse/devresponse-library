
# Project Plan Template: Hand-Drawn Art Canvas for Acoustic Songs

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Hand-Drawn Art Canvas for Acoustic Songs" project aims to create a series of unique, hand-drawn art canvases that resonate with the essence of acoustic music. The primary objective is to produce short, looped video animations that enhance the listening experience on Spotify by visually interpreting the mood and themes of each acoustic or folk track. By utilizing organic and sketch-style visuals, we intend to provide a personal touch that makes the music feel more intimate and relatable to the audience. Key outcomes include the delivery of multiple video loops, each lasting between 5 to 10 seconds, that seamlessly integrate with the audio tracks. This project is designed for musicians and content creators who seek to differentiate their work and engage their audience through visually compelling content. Ultimately, we aim to elevate the overall experience of acoustic music on digital platforms, fostering a deeper connection between the artist and their listeners.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Concept Development**
  - Analyze the acoustic songs provided by the client to understand their themes and moods.
  - Develop initial concepts for the hand-drawn art style that aligns with the music.

- **Art Creation**
  - Create hand-drawn illustrations that reflect the essence of each song.
  - Ensure that the artwork is adaptable for animation.

- **Animation Production**
  - Develop short, looped animations (5 to 10 seconds) from the hand-drawn illustrations.
  - Incorporate smooth transitions and effects that enhance the visual storytelling.

- **Client Review and Revisions**
  - Present initial drafts of the animations to the client for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on client input.

- **Final Delivery**
  - Deliver the final video loops in the required format for Spotify integration.
  - Provide a usage guide for the client on how to implement the videos with their music.
