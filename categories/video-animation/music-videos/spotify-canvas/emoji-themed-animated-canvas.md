
# Project Plan Template: Emoji-Themed Animated Canvas

## 1. Executive Summary

The Emoji-Themed Animated Canvas project aims to create vibrant and engaging Spotify Canvas videos that incorporate animated emojis tailored to the specific mood and tone of each music track. The primary objective of this project is to enhance the auditory experience of listeners by providing visually captivating short loop videos that make the music more memorable. By carefully selecting and animating emojis that reflect the emotions conveyed in the music—whether it be joy, nostalgia, or excitement—we will create a unique visual representation that resonates with the audience. Key outcomes include multiple versions of the animated canvas for each track, allowing artists to choose the best fit for their vision. This project is designed for musicians and content creators who wish to stand out on Spotify and connect with their audience in a fun and innovative way, ultimately driving engagement and increasing listener retention.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Concept Development**: 
  - Analyze the mood and tone of each music track provided by the artist.
  - Develop a concept for the animated emojis that align with the emotional themes of the music.

- **Emoji Selection and Animation**: 
  - Select appropriate emojis that represent the identified emotions.
  - Create animations for each emoji, ensuring they are visually appealing and loop seamlessly.

- **Canvas Creation**: 
  - Design multiple versions of the animated canvas for each track, incorporating the selected emojis.
  - Ensure that the canvases are optimized for Spotify's specifications.

- **Review and Revisions**: 
  - Present the initial versions of the animated canvases to the artist for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on the artist's input to ensure alignment with their vision.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Deliver the final versions of the animated canvases in the required formats.
  - Provide documentation on how to upload and utilize the canvases on Spotify.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Concept Development
  - Deliverable: Concept document outlining emoji selections and animation ideas.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Emoji Selection and Animation
  - Deliverable: Animated emoji files ready for integration into the canvas.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Canvas Creation
  - Deliverable: Multiple versions of the animated canvas for each track.

- **Milestone 4**: Review and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised animated canvases based on artist feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Final animated canvas files and documentation.
