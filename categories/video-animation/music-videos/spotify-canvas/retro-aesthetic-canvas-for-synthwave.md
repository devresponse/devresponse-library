
# Project Plan Template: Retro Aesthetic Canvas for Synthwave

## 1. Executive Summary

The Retro Aesthetic Canvas for Synthwave project aims to create a series of captivating Spotify Canvas videos that embody the vibrant essence of the synthwave genre. This project will focus on producing short loop videos that are visually striking and resonate with the nostalgic 80s-inspired visuals characteristic of synthwave music. The primary objective is to enhance the auditory experience of specific tracks within the synthwave and electronic music spectrum by providing a visually stimulating backdrop that reflects themes of retro futurism and electronic soundscapes. Key outcomes include a set of unique, high-quality Canvas videos that not only engage listeners but also encourage sharing across social media platforms, thereby elevating the artist's presence on Spotify. Close collaboration with the client will ensure that each video aligns perfectly with the mood and style of the music, resulting in a cohesive and immersive experience for the audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Concept Development**: Conduct research on synthwave aesthetics and trends to inform the visual style of the Canvas videos. Develop concepts that align with the specific tracks provided by the client.
  
- **Video Production**: Create a series of short loop videos (approximately 8-10 seconds each) that incorporate vibrant neon lights, retro visuals, and dynamic animations. Each video will be tailored to complement the mood and tempo of the corresponding track.

- **Client Collaboration**: Schedule regular meetings with the client to review concepts, provide updates on progress, and gather feedback to ensure alignment with the artist's vision.

- **Revisions and Finalization**: Implement feedback from the client and make necessary revisions to the videos. Finalize the videos for delivery in the required format for Spotify Canvas.

- **Delivery and Support**: Deliver the final videos to the client along with guidelines for uploading to Spotify. Provide support for any questions or issues that may arise during the upload process.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Concept Development
  - Deliverable: Concept presentation document with visual references and initial ideas.

- **Milestone 2**: First Draft of Videos
  - Deliverable: Initial versions of the Canvas videos for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised videos based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Video Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized Canvas videos ready for upload to Spotify.
