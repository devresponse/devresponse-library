
# Project Plan Template: Dance Video Inspired by Mythology

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Dance Video Inspired by Mythology" project aims to create a captivating dance video that draws inspiration from various mythological stories and characters. The primary objective is to translate ancient tales into a visual spectacle through movement, allowing viewers to experience the rich narratives of mythology in a contemporary format. This project will involve extensive research into selected myths, which will inform the development of original choreography that reflects the themes and emotions inherent in these stories. A professional video production team will be engaged to capture the performance, ensuring that the visuals enhance the storytelling aspect of the dance. The key outcomes of this project include a polished dance video that not only showcases the beauty of dance but also educates and entertains viewers by bringing timeless myths to life. Ultimately, this project seeks to merge the worlds of dance and mythology in a unique and engaging way, appealing to both dance enthusiasts and mythology lovers alike.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Identify and select a range of mythological stories and characters to be featured.
  - Conduct in-depth research on the chosen myths, focusing on themes, emotions, and cultural significance.
  
- **Choreography Development**: 
  - Collaborate with choreographers to create original dance pieces that embody the essence of the selected myths.
  - Rehearse the choreography with dancers, ensuring alignment with the narrative and emotional tone of the stories.

- **Video Production**: 
  - Hire a professional video production team to plan and execute the filming of the dance performance.
  - Scout and secure suitable locations that enhance the visual storytelling.
  - Film the dance performance, utilizing various camera angles and techniques to capture the movement and emotion effectively.

- **Post-Production**: 
  - Edit the footage to create a cohesive and engaging final product.
  - Incorporate sound design, music, and any necessary visual effects to enhance the storytelling.
  
- **Distribution and Promotion**: 
  - Develop a marketing strategy to promote the video upon completion.
  - Identify platforms for distribution, such as social media, dance festivals, or educational institutions.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing selected myths and their themes.

- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of Choreography
  - Deliverable: Video recording of rehearsals and finalized choreography.

- **Milestone 3**: Filming of Dance Performance
  - Deliverable: Raw footage of the dance performance.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Video Editing
  - Deliverable: Final edited dance video ready for distribution.

- **Milestone 5**: Launch and Promotion
  - Deliverable: Marketing materials and distribution strategy.
