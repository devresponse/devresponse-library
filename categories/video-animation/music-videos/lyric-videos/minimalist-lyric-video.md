
# Project Plan: Minimalist Lyric Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Minimalist Lyric Video project aims to create a visually engaging and modern lyric video that encapsulates the essence of the song while maintaining a clean aesthetic. The primary objective is to produce a video that highlights the lyrics through simple yet effective typography, ensuring that they resonate with the song's mood and message. Key outcomes of this project include a polished final product that is suitable for distribution across various platforms, including YouTube, social media, and music streaming services. The project will involve a collaborative approach, allowing the client to provide feedback throughout the design process. This ensures that the final video aligns with the client's vision and meets the expectations of the target audience. By incorporating subtle animations, we aim to enhance the viewing experience without detracting from the lyrical content, ultimately creating a memorable and shareable piece of art.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Concept Development**: Collaborate with the client to understand the song's themes, mood, and target audience.
- **Typography Selection**: Choose fonts that align with the song's essence and ensure readability.
- **Lyric Integration**: Input the lyrics into the video, ensuring proper timing and synchronization with the music.
- **Animation Design**: Create subtle animations that enhance the visual appeal without overwhelming the viewer.
- **Feedback Loops**: Establish checkpoints for client feedback on design elements and overall flow.
- **Final Edits**: Implement client feedback and make necessary adjustments to finalize the video.
- **Exporting and Delivery**: Deliver the final video in multiple formats suitable for various platforms.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Concept Development Completion
  - Deliverable: Concept document outlining the vision and design direction.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Typography and Lyric Integration
  - Deliverable: Draft version of the video with integrated lyrics and selected typography.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Animation Design Completion
  - Deliverable: Video draft with animations incorporated for client review.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Client Feedback Implementation
  - Deliverable: Revised video based on client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Video Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized lyric video in multiple formats.
