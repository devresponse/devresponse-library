
# Project Plan: Watercolor Lyric Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Watercolor Lyric Video project aims to create a visually stunning and immersive experience that harmonizes beautifully with the music. The primary objective is to design and animate lyrics that appear as if they are being painted in real-time on a watercolor canvas. This innovative approach will enhance the emotional impact of the song, captivating viewers and allowing them to connect with the music on a deeper level. Key outcomes of this project include a high-quality lyric video that not only showcases the song's lyrics but also embodies the artistic essence of watercolor art. The project will involve multiple revisions to ensure that the visuals align perfectly with the rhythm and mood of the music, resulting in a polished and professional final product that musicians, bands, and artists can proudly share with their audience. By blending art and music, this project seeks to elevate the viewer's experience and create a lasting impression.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Concept Development**: Collaborate with the client to understand the song's themes, emotions, and desired visual style.
- **Storyboarding**: Create a storyboard that outlines the flow of lyrics and corresponding watercolor animations.
- **Design Elements**: Develop watercolor textures and elements that will be used in the video.
- **Animation**: Animate the lyrics to appear as if they are being painted in real-time, synchronized with the music.
- **Revisions**: Conduct multiple rounds of revisions based on client feedback to ensure alignment with the song's rhythm and mood.
- **Final Edits**: Make final adjustments to the video, ensuring high-quality resolution and audio synchronization.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final lyric video in various formats suitable for different platforms (e.g., YouTube, social media).

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Concept Development Completion
  - Deliverable: Concept document outlining themes and visual style.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Storyboard Approval
  - Deliverable: Approved storyboard for the lyric video.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Design Elements Finalization
  - Deliverable: Collection of watercolor textures and design elements.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Animation Draft
  - Deliverable: First draft of the animated lyric video for review.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Revisions Completion
  - Deliverable: Revised lyric video incorporating client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: High-quality final lyric video in specified formats.
