
# Project Plan Template: Choir Performance Video Production

## 1. Executive Summary

The Choir Performance Video Production project aims to create a high-quality video that captures the artistry and emotional depth of the choir's performance. Our primary objective is to produce a polished final product that not only showcases the choir's talent but also tells a compelling story that resonates with viewers. This project will involve a comprehensive approach, including pre-production planning to understand the specific elements to highlight, on-site filming using high-definition cameras and advanced audio equipment, and meticulous post-production editing to ensure a seamless and engaging final video. Key outcomes include a visually stunning and emotionally impactful video that can be used for promotional purposes, social media sharing, and archival documentation of the choir's performance. By collaborating closely with the choir, we will ensure that the final product encapsulates the essence of their artistry and the atmosphere of the event.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production Planning**
  - Initial consultation with the choir to discuss vision and specific elements to highlight.
  - Development of a shooting script and storyboard to outline the flow of the video.
  - Coordination of logistics, including location scouting and scheduling.

- **On-Site Filming**
  - Setup of high-definition cameras and advanced audio equipment at the performance venue.
  - Capture of the choir's arrangement, individual performances, and audience reactions.
  - Filming of additional b-roll footage to enhance storytelling.

- **Post-Production Editing**
  - Review of all footage and selection of the best takes.
  - Editing of video to create a cohesive narrative, including transitions, color correction, and audio mixing.
  - Incorporation of any requested graphics or titles.
  - Final review and adjustments based on feedback from the choir.

- **Delivery of Final Product**
  - Provision of the final video in multiple formats suitable for various platforms (e.g., social media, website, DVD).
  - Optional: Creation of teaser clips for promotional use.
