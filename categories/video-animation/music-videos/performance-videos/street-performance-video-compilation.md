
# Project Plan Template: Street Performance Video Compilation

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Street Performance Video Compilation" project aims to create a captivating video that showcases the vibrant energy and unique artistry of street performances. Our primary objective is to highlight a diverse range of musicians and performers filmed in various outdoor settings, encapsulating the essence of live music in public spaces. The final product will feature dynamic transitions, engaging visuals, and a carefully curated soundtrack that complements the performances, evoking the atmosphere of the streets where these artists perform. Key outcomes include a high-quality video that not only celebrates the raw talent of street artists but also appeals to a wide audience, promoting the creativity and spontaneity of street art. By adhering to high production standards, including color grading and sound mixing, we aim to deliver a polished final product suitable for sharing across multiple platforms, thereby enhancing the visibility of street performers and fostering a greater appreciation for their art.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production:**
  - Research and identify street performance locations and artists.
  - Obtain necessary permits and permissions for filming in public spaces.
  - Develop a shooting schedule that accommodates the availability of performers and optimal weather conditions.
  - Create a storyboard outlining the flow and key scenes of the video.

- **Production:**
  - Conduct on-site filming of street performances, ensuring a variety of artists and styles are represented.
  - Capture high-quality audio and video footage, utilizing professional equipment to ensure optimal production standards.
  - Engage with performers to capture candid moments and interviews that enhance the narrative of the video.

- **Post-Production:**
  - Edit the footage to create a cohesive compilation that highlights the energy and artistry of the performances.
  - Incorporate dynamic transitions and engaging visuals to maintain viewer interest.
  - Curate a soundtrack that complements the performances and enhances the overall atmosphere of the video.
  - Perform color grading and sound mixing to ensure a polished final product.

- **Distribution:**
  - Develop a marketing strategy for sharing the video across multiple platforms, including social media, video-sharing sites, and local community events.
  - Create promotional materials to accompany the video launch, highlighting featured artists and performances.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1:** Completion of Pre-Production Activities
  - Deliverable: Finalized shooting schedule and storyboard.

- **Milestone 2:** Completion of Filming
  - Deliverable: Raw footage of all street performances.

- **Milestone 3:** Completion of Video Editing
  - Deliverable: First draft of the compilation video for review.

- **Milestone 4:** Finalization of Video
  - Deliverable: Polished final video, including color grading and sound mixing.

- **Milestone 5:** Launch and Distribution
  - Deliverable: Video shared across selected platforms and promotional materials distributed.
