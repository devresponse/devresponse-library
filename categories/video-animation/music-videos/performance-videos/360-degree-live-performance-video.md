
# Project Plan Template: 360-Degree Live Performance Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the 360-Degree Live Performance Video project is to create an immersive and captivating video experience that captures the essence of a live performance. This project will utilize state-of-the-art 360-degree camera technology to record the event from multiple perspectives, allowing viewers to feel as if they are part of the audience. The key outcomes include a high-quality, expertly edited video that enhances both audio and visual elements, showcasing the artist's talent and the energy of the performance. This project aims to produce a unique product that can be shared across various platforms, providing fans with an opportunity to relive the experience through VR headsets or standard devices. By delivering an engaging and innovative viewing experience, we will not only elevate the artist's profile but also expand their reach to a broader audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production Planning**
  - Identify the performance venue and secure necessary permissions.
  - Coordinate with the artist and their management for scheduling.
  - Develop a shot list and production timeline.
  - Assemble a production team, including camera operators and sound engineers.

- **Production**
  - Set up 360-degree camera equipment at strategic locations around the venue.
  - Conduct sound checks and ensure optimal audio capture.
  - Record the live performance, ensuring multiple angles are captured for a comprehensive view.

- **Post-Production**
  - Edit the recorded footage to create a seamless viewing experience.
  - Enhance audio quality and synchronize it with the video.
  - Add visual effects and graphics as needed to elevate the final product.
  - Create different formats of the video for various platforms (VR, standard video).

- **Distribution**
  - Develop a marketing strategy for the video release.
  - Share the final product across social media and streaming platforms.
  - Gather feedback and analytics on viewer engagement.
