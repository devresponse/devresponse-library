
# Project Plan Template: Music Performance Video with Animated Visuals

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the "Music Performance Video with Animated Visuals" project is to create a visually stunning and engaging music video that combines live performance footage with captivating animated visuals. This project aims to elevate the artist's music video by seamlessly integrating high-quality video recordings of the live performance with custom animations that enhance the storytelling aspect of the music. The key outcomes include a fully edited video that captures the energy and emotion of the live event, along with animation assets that complement the performance. Additionally, a consultation session will be provided to discuss the creative direction, ensuring that the final product resonates with the artist's vision and connects deeply with their audience. This project is designed for artists seeking to create memorable experiences for their viewers, ultimately enhancing their brand and artistic expression.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production Phase**
  - Initial consultation with the artist to understand their vision and objectives.
  - Collect high-quality video footage from the live performance.
  - Develop a storyboard that outlines the integration of animated visuals with the live footage.

- **Production Phase**
  - Edit the collected video footage to highlight key moments of the performance.
  - Design and produce custom animations that align with the music and enhance the narrative.

- **Post-Production Phase**
  - Integrate the animations with the edited video footage.
  - Conduct a review session with the artist to gather feedback and make necessary adjustments.
  - Finalize the video and prepare all deliverables, including animation assets.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Initial Consultation**
  - Deliverable: Consultation notes and project brief.
  
- **Milestone 2: Video Footage Collection**
  - Deliverable: High-quality video footage from the live performance.

- **Milestone 3: Storyboard Development**
  - Deliverable: Completed storyboard for the video.

- **Milestone 4: Video Editing**
  - Deliverable: Edited video footage highlighting key performance moments.

- **Milestone 5: Animation Production**
  - Deliverable: Custom animation assets.

- **Milestone 6: Final Review**
  - Deliverable: Feedback session notes and final adjustments.

- **Milestone 7: Final Delivery**
  - Deliverable: Fully edited music performance video and animation assets.
