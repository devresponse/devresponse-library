
# Project Plan Template: Retro 90s Anime Music Video Aesthetic

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Retro 90s Anime Music Video Aesthetic" project aims to create a captivating music video that channels the essence of 90s anime aesthetics. The primary objective is to produce a visually stunning piece that not only serves as a companion to the music but also evokes nostalgia among audiences familiar with retro anime culture. This project will incorporate nostalgic art styles reminiscent of classic anime series, utilizing grainy textures and vibrant color palettes to enhance the emotional depth of the music. Key visual tropes from the era, such as exaggerated facial expressions, dynamic action sequences, and iconic character designs, will be integrated to enrich the storytelling aspect of the music. Close collaboration with the artist will ensure that the visuals align perfectly with the music's themes and emotions, resulting in a cohesive and engaging viewing experience. The key outcomes of this project include a completed music video that resonates with the target audience, increased engagement on digital platforms, and a strengthened connection between the artist and their fanbase.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Concept Development**
  - Analyze popular 90s anime series for visual inspiration.
  - Develop a concept that aligns with the music's themes and emotions.
  
- **Storyboarding**
  - Create a storyboard outlining key scenes and transitions.
  - Include notes on visual styles, character designs, and action sequences.

- **Character Design**
  - Design characters that reflect iconic 90s anime aesthetics.
  - Ensure character designs resonate with the music's narrative.

- **Animation Production**
  - Produce animated sequences using grainy textures and vibrant colors.
  - Incorporate dynamic action sequences and exaggerated facial expressions.

- **Editing and Post-Production**
  - Edit the animated sequences to sync with the music.
  - Apply final touches to enhance visual quality and coherence.

- **Feedback and Revisions**
  - Present the draft video to the artist for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on feedback received.

- **Final Delivery**
  - Deliver the final music video in required formats for distribution.
  - Provide promotional materials and stills for marketing purposes.
