
# Project Plan Template: Interactive Choose-Your-Path Music Video

## 1. Executive Summary

The Interactive Choose-Your-Path Music Video project aims to revolutionize the traditional music video format by integrating interactive storytelling elements that empower viewers to influence the narrative. The primary objective is to create a captivating music video that resonates with the artist's vision while engaging the audience in a unique way. By allowing viewers to make choices that affect the storyline, we will produce multiple possible endings, enhancing replayability and viewer investment. Key outcomes include a fully developed narrative that aligns with the artist's music, seamless technical implementation of interactive features, and a user-friendly interface that ensures accessibility across various platforms. This innovative approach not only aims to elevate viewer engagement but also to create a memorable experience that can be shared widely, ultimately amplifying the artist's reach and impact in the digital landscape.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Kick-off meeting with the artist and creative team to discuss vision and objectives.
  - Research and analysis of existing interactive video formats and technologies.

- **Narrative Development**
  - Collaborate with the artist to create a compelling storyline that aligns with the music.
  - Develop branching narrative paths that allow for viewer choices and multiple endings.

- **Technical Implementation**
  - Select appropriate technology and platforms for interactive video production.
  - Design and develop the interactive elements, ensuring a smooth user experience.
  - Conduct usability testing to refine the interactive features.

- **Production**
  - Plan and execute the video shoot, including casting, location scouting, and set design.
  - Edit the video to incorporate interactive elements and finalize the narrative flow.

- **Launch and Promotion**
  - Develop a marketing strategy to promote the interactive music video.
  - Launch the video across selected platforms and monitor viewer engagement.

- **Post-Launch Evaluation**
  - Collect and analyze viewer feedback and engagement metrics.
  - Prepare a report summarizing the project's success and areas for improvement.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kick-off**
  - Deliverable: Project charter and initial meeting notes.
  
- **Milestone 2: Narrative Development Completion**
  - Deliverable: Finalized script and narrative paths.

- **Milestone 3: Technical Implementation**
  - Deliverable: Prototype of interactive features for review.

- **Milestone 4: Video Production**
  - Deliverable: Completed video ready for editing.

- **Milestone 5: Launch**
  - Deliverable: Live interactive music video across selected platforms.

- **Milestone 6: Post-Launch Report**
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive evaluation report with metrics and feedback.
