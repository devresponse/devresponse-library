## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Underwater Creature Animation project includes the following key components:

- **Character Design**: Develop unique character designs for each underwater creature, including detailed sketches and color palettes.
- **Animation Development**: Create animations that showcase the fluid movements and behaviors of the characters in an aquatic setting.
- **Storyboarding**: Develop storyboards that outline the narrative flow and key scenes for the animations.
- **Draft Reviews**: Provide a series of drafts for client feedback, allowing for adjustments and refinements based on client input.
- **Final Animation Production**: Produce the final animated sequences, ensuring high-quality rendering and visual effects.
- **Delivery Formats**: Prepare the final output in various formats suitable for different applications, including {{MP4}}, {{MOV}}, and {{GIF}}.
- **Post-Production**: Conduct any necessary post-production work, including sound design and editing, to enhance the final product.
