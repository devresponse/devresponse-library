
# Project Plan Template: Character Animation for Horror Games

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Character Animation for Horror Games" project aims to create a series of uniquely designed characters that embody the essence of horror, specifically tailored for the gaming industry. The primary objective is to develop unsettling and creepy characters that evoke fear and intrigue, enhancing the overall gaming experience. The project will commence with the conceptualization phase, where initial sketches and character designs will be crafted to ensure each character's appearance aligns with the horror theme. Following the design phase, the characters will be animated with a focus on eerie movements that amplify the unsettling atmosphere of the game. Key outcomes include fully animated characters that are ready for integration into the game, complete with atmospheric effects such as shadows and lighting to enhance the horror experience. By the end of this project, we aim to deliver characters that not only look terrifying but also move in a way that captivates players and deepens the narrative of the horror game.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Conceptual Design Phase**
  - Research and analysis of horror game aesthetics and character archetypes.
  - Development of initial sketches for each character, focusing on unique features that evoke fear.
  - Iterative feedback sessions with stakeholders to refine character designs.

- **Character Design Phase**
  - Finalization of character designs based on feedback.
  - Creation of detailed character sheets, including front, side, and back views.
  - Development of color palettes and texture maps to enhance visual appeal.

- **Animation Phase**
  - Rigging of characters for animation, ensuring flexibility and realism in movements.
  - Creation of key animations that emphasize eerie and unsettling movements.
  - Integration of atmospheric effects, such as shadows and lighting, to enhance the horror experience.

- **Final Review and Delivery**
  - Conduct final reviews with stakeholders to ensure all characters meet project objectives.
  - Delivery of fully animated characters in the required formats for game integration.
  - Provide documentation and support for integration into the game engine.
