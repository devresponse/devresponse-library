
# Project Plan Template: Family Reunion Highlights

## 1. Executive Summary

The Family Reunion Highlights project aims to create a heartwarming slideshow video that encapsulates the essence of your family gathering. The primary objective is to curate a selection of group photos, candid shots, and memorable moments shared among family members, transforming them into a cohesive and engaging visual narrative. This project will not only serve as a keepsake for attendees but also foster a sense of connection and nostalgia among family members. Key outcomes include a professionally edited video that features sentimental music, enhancing the emotional resonance of the event. Our team will collaborate closely with you to understand your vision and preferences, ensuring that the final product reflects the unique spirit of your family. The high-quality video will be delivered in a format suitable for sharing, preserving these cherished memories for years to come.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to discuss vision, preferences, and specific requirements for the slideshow video.
- **Photo Collection**: Gather a selection of group photos, candid shots, and any other relevant images from family members.
- **Music Selection**: Collaborate with the client to choose sentimental music that resonates with the emotions of the event.
- **Video Editing**: Create a slideshow video that includes transitions, effects, and text overlays as needed to enhance storytelling.
- **Review Process**: Present a draft version of the video to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the completed slideshow video in a high-quality format, suitable for sharing and preserving memories.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client preferences and vision document.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Photo Collection Completed
  - Deliverable: Compiled folder of selected images from family members.

- **Milestone 3**: Music Selection Finalized
  - Deliverable: List of chosen tracks for the slideshow.

- **Milestone 4**: Draft Video Completed
  - Deliverable: First draft of the slideshow video for client review.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Video Delivered
  - Deliverable: High-quality slideshow video in the agreed format.
