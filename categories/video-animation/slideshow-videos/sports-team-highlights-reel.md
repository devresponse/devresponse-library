
# Project Plan Template: Sports Team Highlights Reel

## 1. Executive Summary

The Sports Team Highlights Reel project aims to create an engaging and dynamic slideshow video that encapsulates the essence of the team's season. The primary objective of this project is to celebrate the team's achievements and camaraderie through a curated selection of action shots, team photos, and memorable moments. By collaborating closely with the team, we will ensure that the final product reflects their unique story and spirit. Key outcomes include a polished slideshow video that is set to motivational music, designed to inspire team spirit and serve as a lasting memento of the season. This video will be ideal for end-of-season celebrations, team banquets, or promotional materials, effectively showcasing the hard work and dedication of the team. The project will begin with a consultation to understand the team's narrative and preferences, followed by the collection of necessary media assets. Ultimately, the Sports Team Highlights Reel will not only highlight the season's successes but also foster a sense of pride and unity among team members and supporters.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct an initial meeting with the team to discuss their story, key moments, and preferences for music and visuals.
  - Gather input on specific highlights that should be included in the video.

- **Media Collection**:
  - Collect all necessary media assets from the client, including:
    - Action shots
    - Team photos
    - Video clips
  - Ensure all media is high-quality and relevant to the team's season.

- **Video Production**:
  - Create a storyboard outlining the sequence of images and clips.
  - Select appropriate motivational music that aligns with the team's spirit.
  - Edit the slideshow video, incorporating transitions, effects, and text overlays as needed.
  - Review the video with the team for feedback and make necessary adjustments.

- **Final Delivery**:
  - Deliver the final polished slideshow video in the agreed format.
  - Provide options for additional formats or edits if requested.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and agreed-upon highlights list.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Media Collection
  - Deliverable: All collected media assets organized and ready for editing.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Storyboard Creation
  - Deliverable: Storyboard draft for team review.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Video Editing
  - Deliverable: First draft of the slideshow video for feedback.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Review and Adjustments
  - Deliverable: Final version of the slideshow video.
