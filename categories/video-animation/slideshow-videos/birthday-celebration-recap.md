
# Project Plan Template: Birthday Celebration Recap

## 1. Executive Summary
The Birthday Celebration Recap project aims to create a vibrant and engaging slideshow video that encapsulates the essence of a memorable birthday celebration. The primary objective is to produce a personalized keepsake that not only showcases the joyous moments of the event but also resonates with the birthday individual and their guests. This project will involve curating photos from the celebration, selecting upbeat music that reflects the festive atmosphere, and incorporating playful text to highlight special moments, messages, or quotes. By tailoring the slideshow to the client's preferences, including colors, themes, and overall style, we will ensure that the final product aligns with the spirit of the celebration. Key outcomes include a high-quality video that serves as a delightful reminder of the event, fostering lasting memories for both the birthday person and attendees. Ultimately, this project will deliver a unique and cherished gift that can be enjoyed for years to come.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work
- **Photo Collection**: Gather photos from the birthday celebration, ensuring a comprehensive representation of the event.
- **Music Selection**: Choose upbeat and appropriate background music that enhances the joyful atmosphere of the celebration.
- **Text Integration**: Create and incorporate playful text elements to highlight special moments, messages, and quotes that resonate with the birthday person and guests.
- **Design Customization**: Tailor the slideshow's colors, themes, and overall style to align with the birthday celebration's spirit and the client's preferences.
- **Video Editing**: Edit the slideshow to ensure smooth transitions, appropriate timing of music, and synchronization of text with images.
- **Client Review**: Present the initial draft to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the final slideshow video in the desired format, ensuring it is suitable for sharing and viewing on various platforms.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1**: Photo Collection Completed
  - Deliverable: Compiled folder of all event photos
- **Milestone 2**: Music Selection Finalized
  - Deliverable: List of selected music tracks with licensing information
- **Milestone 3**: Initial Slideshow Draft Created
  - Deliverable: First version of the slideshow video for client review
- **Milestone 4**: Client Feedback Received
  - Deliverable: Document outlining client feedback and requested changes
- **Milestone 5**: Final Slideshow Video Delivered
  - Deliverable: Finalized slideshow video in agreed format
