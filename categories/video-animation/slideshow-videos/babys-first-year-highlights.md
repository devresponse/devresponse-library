
# Project Plan Template: Baby's First Year Highlights

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Baby's First Year Highlights" project aims to create a heartwarming slideshow video that captures the precious moments of a baby's first year. The primary objective is to curate milestone photos provided by the parents, showcasing key events such as the baby's first smile, first steps, and birthday celebrations. This project will not only serve as a visual narrative of the baby's growth but also as a cherished keepsake for families. Each selected photo will be complemented by thoughtful captions that narrate the story behind each moment, ensuring a personal touch that resonates with the family's unique journey. Additionally, soft, soothing music will be selected to enhance the emotional impact of the slideshow, creating a memorable experience for viewers. The final product will be delivered in a high-quality video format, ready for sharing with loved ones or preserving as a family treasure. Key outcomes include a beautifully crafted video that encapsulates the essence of the baby's first year, satisfaction from the clients, and a lasting memory that can be revisited for years to come.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Photo Collection**: Gather milestone photos from parents, including but not limited to:
  - First smile
  - First steps
  - Birthday celebrations
  - Family gatherings
  - Other significant moments

- **Caption Development**: Create thoughtful captions for each photo that narrate the story behind the moment, ensuring a personal touch.

- **Music Selection**: Choose soft, soothing background music that complements the emotional tone of the slideshow.

- **Video Creation**: Compile the photos, captions, and music into a cohesive slideshow video, ensuring smooth transitions and a visually appealing presentation.

- **Review and Revisions**: Present the initial draft to the clients for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.

- **Final Delivery**: Provide the final slideshow video in a high-quality format suitable for sharing and preservation.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Photo Collection Completed
  - Deliverable: Compilation of all milestone photos from parents.

- **Milestone 2**: Caption Development Completed
  - Deliverable: List of captions corresponding to each photo.

- **Milestone 3**: Music Selection Completed
  - Deliverable: Selected background music track.

- **Milestone 4**: Initial Slideshow Draft Completed
  - Deliverable: First draft of the slideshow video for client review.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised slideshow video based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Product Delivered
  - Deliverable: High-quality video file of the completed slideshow.
