
# Project Plan Template: Interview Video Post-Production

## 1. Executive Summary

The Interview Video Post-Production project aims to transform raw interview footage into a polished and engaging final product that captivates viewers. The primary objective is to enhance the quality of interview content, making it more professional and impactful for the audience. This project will involve a comprehensive editing process, which includes the addition of lower-thirds to introduce speakers, ensuring their names and titles are clearly displayed. Furthermore, subtitles will be incorporated to improve accessibility and comprehension, allowing viewers to follow along easily, regardless of their audio settings. The integration of carefully selected background music will complement the tone of the interview, creating an engaging atmosphere that keeps the audience interested. Key outcomes of this project include a fully edited video that meets the client's specifications, elevating the overall viewer experience and providing a product that stands out in the competitive landscape of video content.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Footage Review**: Analyze the raw footage to identify key segments for editing.
- **Lower-Thirds Creation**: Design and implement lower-thirds for each speaker, including their names and titles.
- **Subtitles Integration**: Add accurate subtitles to the video to enhance accessibility.
- **Background Music Selection**: Curate and integrate background music that aligns with the interview's tone and mood.
- **Color Correction**: Apply color correction techniques to ensure visual consistency and appeal.
- **Audio Enhancement**: Improve audio quality through noise reduction and leveling.
- **Final Review and Revisions**: Conduct a thorough review of the edited video and make necessary revisions based on client feedback.
- **Delivery of Final Product**: Provide the client with the final edited video in the desired format.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Footage Review  
  **Deliverable**: Detailed report on key segments identified for editing.

- **Milestone 2**: Lower-Thirds and Subtitles Implementation  
  **Deliverable**: Video draft with lower-thirds and subtitles added.

- **Milestone 3**: Background Music Integration  
  **Deliverable**: Updated video draft with background music.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Review and Revisions  
  **Deliverable**: Revised video based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of Final Edited Video  
  **Deliverable**: Final video file delivered in the specified format.
