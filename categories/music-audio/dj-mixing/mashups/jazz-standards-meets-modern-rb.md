
# Project Plan Template: Jazz Standards Meets Modern R&B

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Jazz Standards Meets Modern R&B" project aims to create a unique and captivating audio mashup that seamlessly blends the elegance of timeless jazz standards with the smooth grooves of modern R&B. The primary objective is to produce a polished audio track that showcases a harmonious fusion of classic melodies and contemporary rhythms, appealing to both jazz aficionados and R&B lovers alike. This project will involve the careful selection of iconic jazz tracks and pairing them with popular R&B hits, ensuring smooth transitions and a cohesive overall sound. Key outcomes include a professionally mixed audio track ready for distribution, along with a detailed explanation of the creative choices made during the production process. By leveraging professional mixing techniques and a deep understanding of both genres, the project aims to deliver a product that not only entertains but also pays homage to the rich musical heritage of jazz while embracing the modern influences of R&B.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Selection of Tracks**: Identify and select a list of iconic jazz standards and popular modern R&B hits that will be used in the mashup. This will involve listening sessions and discussions to ensure the chosen tracks complement each other.
  
- **Arrangement and Composition**: Create a preliminary arrangement of the selected tracks, focusing on how they will be blended together. This includes determining the key, tempo, and structure of the mashup.

- **Recording and Mixing**: Utilize professional audio recording and mixing techniques to produce the final mashup. This will involve layering tracks, adjusting levels, and applying effects to enhance the overall sound quality.

- **Feedback and Revisions**: Present the initial version of the mashup to a select group of listeners for feedback. Make necessary revisions based on the feedback received to ensure the final product meets the project objectives.

- **Final Production**: Complete the final mix of the audio track, ensuring it is polished and ready for distribution. 

- **Documentation**: Prepare a brief explanation of the creative choices made during the production process, detailing the rationale behind track selections and arrangement decisions.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Track Selection  
  - **Deliverable**: List of selected jazz and R&B tracks

- **Milestone 2**: Arrangement Draft

- **Milestone 4**: Final Mix  
  - **Deliverable**: Polished audio track ready for distribution

- **Milestone 5**: Creative Documentation  
  - **Deliverable**: Written explanation of creative choices
