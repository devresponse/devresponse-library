
# Project Plan Template: 80s Synthpop Meets Modern Rap

## 1. Executive Summary

The "80s Synthpop Meets Modern Rap" project aims to create a unique musical mashup that seamlessly blends the vibrant melodies of 80s synthpop with the dynamic and rhythmic verses of modern rap. The primary objective is to evoke nostalgia while introducing a contemporary twist that appeals to both retro music lovers and today's rap enthusiasts. This project will involve the careful selection of iconic synthpop tracks, followed by an in-depth analysis of their melodic structures. We will integrate these melodies with carefully chosen rap verses that complement the original tracks, ensuring a harmonious blend of both genres. The final outcome will be a polished audio track that showcases the best of both worlds, designed for use in parties, playlists, or personal enjoyment. Additionally, a brief consultation will be conducted to discuss the artistic direction and ensure that the final mashup aligns with the client's vision. Key outcomes include a completed audio track, a consultation report, and a marketing strategy for distribution.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Selection**: Identify and select iconic 80s synthpop tracks that resonate with the target audience.
- **Melodic Analysis**: Analyze the selected tracks to understand their melodic structures and key elements.
- **Rap Verse Integration**: Curate and write rap verses that complement the chosen synthpop melodies.
- **Mashup Creation**: Blend the selected synthpop melodies with the rap verses to create a cohesive audio track.
- **Consultation**: Conduct a consultation session with the client to discuss artistic direction and gather feedback.
- **Final Production**: Polish the audio track, ensuring high-quality sound and production standards.
- **Distribution Strategy**: Develop a marketing strategy for the release of the mashup, including potential platforms and promotional activities.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of research and selection of synthpop tracks
  - **Deliverable**: List of selected tracks and rationale for selection
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of melodic analysis
  - **Deliverable**: Analysis report detailing melodic structures
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of rap verse integration
  - **Deliverable**: Draft of rap verses aligned with synthpop melodies
- **Milestone 4**: Completion of the mashup creation
  - **Deliverable**: First draft of the audio mashup
- **Milestone 5**: Completion of consultation session
  - **Deliverable**: Consultation report summarizing feedback and artistic direction
- **Milestone 6**: Final production of the audio track
  - **Deliverable**: Polished audio track ready for distribution
- **Milestone 7**: Development of distribution strategy
  - **Deliverable**: Marketing strategy document
