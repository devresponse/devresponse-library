
# Project Plan Template: K-Pop Hits Meets Western Pop Classics

## 1. Executive Summary

The "K-Pop Hits Meets Western Pop Classics" project aims to create an innovative audio mashup that seamlessly blends popular K-Pop hits with iconic Western pop classics. The primary objective is to produce a unique auditory experience that appeals to fans of both genres, showcasing the strengths of each style while maintaining a cohesive sound. The project will involve the careful selection of 5-7 K-Pop songs and 5-7 Western pop classics, chosen for their compatibility and potential for creative fusion. Key outcomes include a polished audio file ready for distribution and a comprehensive report detailing the creative process and rationale behind track selection. This mashup not only caters to music lovers eager to explore new sounds but also provides DJs with fresh material for their sets, ultimately fostering a greater appreciation for the blending of diverse musical influences.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Identify and analyze popular K-Pop hits and Western pop classics.
  - Evaluate the compatibility of selected tracks for mashup potential.

- **Track Selection**: 
  - Curate a list of 5-7 K-Pop songs and 5-7 Western pop classics.
  - Obtain necessary permissions and licenses for the selected tracks.

- **Editing and Mixing**: 
  - Utilize audio editing software to blend the selected tracks.
  - Ensure high-quality sound through meticulous mixing techniques.

- **Quality Assurance**: 
  - Conduct listening sessions to gather feedback and make necessary adjustments.
  - Finalize the audio file for distribution.

- **Reporting**: 
  - Document the creative process, including challenges and solutions.
  - Provide a rationale for track selection and the overall concept of the mashup.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report on selected K-Pop and Western pop tracks.

- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of Track Selection
  - Deliverable: List of selected tracks with permissions secured.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Editing and Mixing
  - Deliverable: First draft of the audio mashup for review.

- **Milestone 4**: Quality Assurance and Final Adjustments
  - Deliverable: Final audio file ready for distribution.

- **Milestone 5**: Submission of Creative Process Report
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report detailing the creative process and track selection rationale.
