
# Project Plan Template: 90s Pop Meets Modern EDM Mashup

## 1. Executive Summary

The "90s Pop Meets Modern EDM Mashup" project aims to create an exhilarating audio experience that seamlessly blends the catchy melodies and unforgettable hooks of iconic 90s pop songs with the pulsating rhythms and energetic drops of contemporary EDM. The primary objective is to evoke nostalgia while energizing listeners, making the final product ideal for parties, events, and playlists. This project will involve a meticulous selection of key tracks from the 90s that resonate with audiences, followed by a detailed analysis of their structure. The creative integration of these classic elements with modern EDM will ensure smooth transitions, maintaining the essence of both genres while delivering a fresh and innovative sound. The key outcomes include a high-quality audio mashup that captures the spirit of the 90s and the vibrancy of today's electronic music scene, ready for playback in various settings. Ultimately, this project will not only celebrate the past but also create a unique listening experience that appeals to a diverse audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Track Selection**: Identify and select a list of iconic 90s pop songs that will be featured in the mashup. This will involve researching popular tracks and considering audience preferences.
  
- **Analysis of Structure**: Analyze the musical structure of selected tracks, including tempo, key, and arrangement, to determine compatibility with modern EDM elements.
  
- **Creative Integration**: Develop a creative approach to blend the selected 90s pop tracks with contemporary EDM sounds. This includes:
  - Creating new beats and drops that complement the melodies of the 90s songs.
  - Ensuring smooth transitions between different segments of the mashup.
  
- **Production**: Utilize professional audio software to produce the mashup, ensuring high-quality sound and fidelity.
  
- **Feedback and Revisions**: Share initial drafts with a focus group for feedback, and make necessary revisions to enhance the final product.
  
- **Final Deliverable**: Produce a high-quality audio file of the mashup, formatted for playback in various settings (e.g., digital streaming, live events).
