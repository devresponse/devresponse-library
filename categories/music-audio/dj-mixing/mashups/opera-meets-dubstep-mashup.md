
# Project Plan Template: Opera Meets Dubstep Mashup

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Opera Meets Dubstep Mashup" project aims to create a groundbreaking musical piece that seamlessly blends the emotive and powerful elements of opera with the electrifying beats of dubstep. The primary objective is to produce a unique mashup that appeals to a diverse audience, including classical music enthusiasts and electronic dance music fans. This project will involve the careful selection of iconic opera pieces, followed by an in-depth analysis of their structure to identify key melodic and harmonic elements. These elements will then be integrated with meticulously crafted dubstep beats, ensuring a harmonious yet dynamic listening experience. Key outcomes include a polished final product that showcases the juxtaposition of soaring operatic melodies and heavy bass drops, a review phase for client feedback, and a marketing strategy to promote the mashup for various events and personal enjoyment. Ultimately, this innovative fusion of genres is designed to entertain and challenge traditional music boundaries, making it a standout piece in the contemporary music landscape.
