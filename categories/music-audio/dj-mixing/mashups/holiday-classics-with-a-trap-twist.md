
# Project Plan Template: Holiday Classics with a Trap Twist

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Holiday Classics with a Trap Twist" project aims to create an innovative mashup that reimagines beloved holiday classics by infusing them with contemporary trap beats. The primary objective is to craft a unique auditory experience that resonates with both traditional and modern music lovers, thereby bridging the gap between different musical genres. This project will involve selecting key holiday tracks, crafting seamless transitions, and ensuring high-quality sound that captures the essence of both the original songs and the energetic trap genre. Key outcomes include a polished final mix that can be enjoyed at holiday parties, gatherings, and events, as well as a product that appeals to DJs looking to enhance their holiday sets. Ultimately, this project seeks to celebrate the festive spirit while introducing a fresh genre fusion that invites listeners to experience the holidays in a new and exciting way.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Track Selection**: Identify and select a list of popular holiday classics to be included in the mashup. This will involve researching current trends and audience preferences.
  
- **Production**: Create trap beats that complement the selected holiday tracks. This will include:
  - Composing original beats
  - Layering sounds and instruments to enhance the overall auditory experience
  
- **Mashup Creation**: Develop the mashup by:
  - Crafting seamless transitions between tracks
  - Ensuring that the tempo and key of the songs align for a cohesive sound
  
- **Quality Assurance**: Conduct thorough mixing and mastering to ensure high-quality sound. This will involve:
  - Testing the mashup on various sound systems
  - Making necessary adjustments based on feedback
  
- **Marketing and Distribution**: Develop a marketing strategy to promote the final product. This will include:
  - Creating promotional materials
  - Identifying distribution channels (e.g., streaming platforms, social media)
