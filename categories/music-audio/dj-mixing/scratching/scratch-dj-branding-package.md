
# Scratch DJ Branding Package Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Scratch DJ Branding Package aims to elevate the professional image of scratch DJs by providing a comprehensive branding solution tailored to their unique style and musical identity. This project will focus on creating a distinctive logo that encapsulates the essence of the DJ's persona, ensuring it resonates with their target audience. In addition to the logo, we will develop a suite of social media assets, including profile pictures, cover images, and post templates, designed to enhance engagement and promote the DJ's work effectively across various platforms. To further bolster their brand presence, we will produce promotional videos that showcase the DJ's scratching skills, highlighting their talent and creativity in a visually captivating manner. The key outcomes of this project include a cohesive branding package that not only establishes a strong visual identity for the DJ but also facilitates a deeper connection with their audience, ultimately helping them stand out in a competitive market.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Logo Design**
  - Research and conceptualization of logo ideas
  - Creation of initial logo drafts
  - Client feedback and revisions
  - Finalization of logo design in various formats (e.g., PNG, SVG)

- **Social Media Assets**
  - Design of profile pictures tailored to different platforms (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter)
  - Creation of cover images that reflect the DJ's branding
  - Development of post templates for consistent branding across social media
  - Delivery of assets in appropriate dimensions and formats

- **Promotional Videos**
  - Scriptwriting and storyboarding for the promotional video
  - Filming and editing of video content showcasing scratching skills
  - Incorporation of branding elements (logo, colors, etc.) into the video
  - Final review and delivery of the promotional video in HD format

- **Branding Guidelines**
  - Creation of a branding guideline document that outlines logo usage, color palette, typography, and social media best practices
  - Delivery of the branding guidelines to ensure consistency in future branding efforts
