
# Scratch DJ Mentorship Program Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Scratch DJ Mentorship Program aims to empower aspiring scratch DJs by providing them with the essential skills and knowledge necessary to excel in the art of scratching. This program is designed to offer personalized mentorship tailored to each participant's unique style and goals. Over the course of the program, mentees will engage in one-on-one sessions that focus on both basic and advanced scratching techniques, beat matching, and creative transitions. Key outcomes include the development of a refined skill set, constructive feedback on performances, and guidance on navigating the music industry. Participants will also receive valuable career advice on building a personal brand and securing gigs. By the end of the mentorship, participants will not only have a comprehensive understanding of scratching but also the confidence to showcase their talents professionally, setting them on a path toward a successful career in music.
