
# Advanced Scratch Techniques Masterclass Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Advanced Scratch Techniques Masterclass aims to elevate the scratching skills of seasoned DJs by providing an in-depth exploration of advanced techniques such as crab scratching and flare scratching. This project is designed to empower participants with the tools and knowledge necessary to enhance their performances and stand out in the competitive DJing landscape. The masterclass will feature detailed video tutorials, interactive practice sessions, and personalized feedback from industry experts, ensuring that each participant receives tailored guidance. Key outcomes include a comprehensive understanding of advanced scratching techniques, improved hand positioning, timing, and rhythm, and the confidence to apply these skills in live settings. By the end of the masterclass, attendees will not only master the intricacies of advanced scratching but also develop their unique DJ style, setting them apart from their peers.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Course Development**: Create a curriculum that covers advanced scratching techniques, including crab scratching, flare scratching, and other innovative methods.
- **Video Tutorials**: Produce high-quality instructional videos demonstrating each technique, focusing on hand positioning, timing, and rhythm.
- **Interactive Practice Sessions**: Organize live practice sessions where participants can apply techniques in real-time, receiving immediate feedback.
- **Personalized Feedback**: Provide one-on-one feedback sessions with industry experts to address individual challenges and enhance learning.
- **Resource Materials**: Develop supplementary materials, including cheat sheets and practice exercises, to reinforce learning.
- **Marketing and Promotion**: Implement a marketing strategy to attract participants, including social media campaigns, email newsletters, and partnerships with DJ schools.
- **Evaluation and Feedback**: Create a system for participants to provide feedback on the masterclass, allowing for continuous improvement.
