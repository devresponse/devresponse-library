
# Project Plan Template: Personalized Wedding Song

## 1. Executive Summary

The Personalized Wedding Song project aims to create a unique and heartfelt musical piece that encapsulates the love story of the couple. This project will involve a collaborative process where the songwriter engages with the couple to gather insights about their relationship, including key moments, emotions, and themes that define their journey together. The primary objective is to craft original lyrics and melodies that resonate with the couple and their guests, enhancing the overall wedding experience. Key outcomes include a professionally composed song that can be performed live during the wedding ceremony or recorded for future enjoyment. Additionally, the project includes two rounds of revisions to ensure the final product aligns perfectly with the couple's vision, making their special day even more memorable.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the couple to discuss their love story, key moments, and any specific themes or emotions they wish to convey in the song.
  
- **Songwriting Process**: 
  - Develop original lyrics based on the insights gathered during the consultation.
  - Compose a melody that complements the lyrics and reflects the couple's unique style.
  
- **Revisions**: 
  - Present the initial draft of the song to the couple for feedback.
  - Incorporate feedback and provide two rounds of revisions to ensure satisfaction with the final product.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Provide the couple with a professionally recorded version of the song.
  - Offer sheet music and/or a backing track for live performances.

- **Post-Delivery Support**: 
  - Provide guidance on how to perform the song live, if desired.
  - Offer additional support or minor adjustments if needed after the delivery.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of key insights and themes discussed.

- **Milestone 2**: First Draft of the Song Completed
  - Deliverable: Initial lyrics and melody presented to the couple.

- **Milestone 3**: Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Final version of the song after two rounds of revisions.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Delivery of the Song
  - Deliverable: Professionally recorded song, sheet music, and/or backing track provided to the couple.
