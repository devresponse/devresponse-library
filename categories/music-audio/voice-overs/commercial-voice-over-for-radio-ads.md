
# Project Plan Template: Commercial Voice Over for Radio Ads

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Commercial Voice Over for Radio Ads project is to produce high-quality voice over recordings that effectively promote the client's products or services through radio advertisements. This project aims to engage the target audience by delivering clear, persuasive, and captivating narration that aligns with the brand's identity. The selected voice over artist will collaborate closely with the client to ensure a deep understanding of the brand's voice, tone, and key selling points, allowing for a tailored approach to each advertisement. Key outcomes of this project include multiple takes of the script to provide the client with a variety of options, ensuring that the final product resonates with listeners and enhances the overall advertising strategy. By leveraging professional audio content, businesses can stand out in the competitive radio landscape, ultimately driving brand awareness and customer engagement.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Voice Over Artist Selection**: Identify and select a professional voice over artist with experience in radio advertisements.
- **Client Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss brand voice, tone, and key selling points.
- **Script Development**: Collaborate with the client to develop a compelling script that effectively communicates the desired message.
- **Recording Sessions**: Schedule and conduct recording sessions, ensuring a comfortable environment for the voice over artist.
- **Multiple Takes**: Provide multiple takes of the script to offer variety and options for the client.
- **Editing and Post-Production**: Edit the recordings to enhance audio quality, including noise reduction, equalization, and mastering.
- **Client Review**: Present the final recordings to the client for feedback and revisions.
- **Final Delivery**: Deliver the final audio files in the required formats, ensuring compatibility with radio broadcasting standards.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Voice Over Artist Selection
  - Deliverable: Signed contract with selected voice over artist.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Client Consultation
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and agreed-upon brand voice and tone.

- **Milestone 3**: Script Development
  - Deliverable: Finalized script approved by the client.

- **Milestone 4**: Recording Sessions
  - Deliverable: Completed audio recordings of multiple takes.

- **Milestone 5**: Editing and Post-Production
  - Deliverable: Edited audio files ready for client review.

- **Milestone 6**: Client Review
  - Deliverable: Client feedback and any requested revisions.

- **Milestone 7**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Final audio files delivered in specified formats.
