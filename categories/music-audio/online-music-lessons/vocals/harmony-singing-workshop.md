
# Project Plan Template: Harmony Singing Workshop

## 1. Executive Summary

The Harmony Singing Workshop aims to enhance the vocal abilities of singers at all skill levels by focusing on the art of harmonization. This interactive workshop will provide participants with a comprehensive understanding of musical intervals, which are essential for creating harmony, as well as blending techniques that facilitate a cohesive sound in group settings. The project seeks to foster collaboration and teamwork among singers, allowing them to engage in practical exercises such as vocal warm-ups and guided harmonization practices. By utilizing tailored practice tracks, participants will be able to work at their own pace, ensuring that everyone can benefit from the experience. Key outcomes include improved individual vocal skills, increased confidence in ensemble performance, and a deeper appreciation for the collaborative nature of singing. Ultimately, the workshop will serve as an invaluable resource for singers looking to elevate their vocal performance and connect with others through music.
