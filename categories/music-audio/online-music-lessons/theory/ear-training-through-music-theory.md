
# Project Plan Template: Ear Training Through Music Theory

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Ear Training Through Music Theory" project aims to create an innovative course that effectively bridges the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application in music. The primary objective is to enhance participants' aural skills, enabling them to accurately identify musical intervals, chords, and progressions. This course will provide structured lessons that incorporate interactive exercises and real-world examples, allowing students to apply music theory concepts in a manner that significantly improves their listening abilities and overall musicianship. Key outcomes include increased proficiency in interval recognition, chord identification, and harmonic progression analysis. By the end of the course, participants will not only possess a solid understanding of music theory but will also be able to hear and apply these concepts in their musical endeavors. This course is tailored for musicians of all levels, making it an essential resource for anyone looking to deepen their understanding of music theory while honing their ear training skills.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the "Ear Training Through Music Theory" project includes the following components:

- **Course Development**: Design and develop a comprehensive curriculum that covers essential topics such as interval recognition, chord identification, and harmonic progression analysis.
- **Lesson Structure**: Create a series of structured lessons that incorporate interactive exercises, guided practice, and real-world examples.
- **Resource Creation**: Develop supplementary materials, including worksheets, audio examples, and visual aids to enhance learning.
- **Platform Selection**: Identify and set up an online learning platform for course delivery, ensuring it is user-friendly and accessible to all participants.
- **Marketing Strategy**: Develop a marketing plan to promote the course to target audiences, including musicians, music students, and educators.
- **Feedback Mechanism**: Implement a system for collecting participant feedback to continuously improve course content and delivery.
