
# Project Plan: Advanced Harmony Techniques for Composers

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Advanced Harmony Techniques for Composers" project aims to provide an in-depth exploration of advanced harmonic principles tailored for composers seeking to enhance their musical compositions. This meticulously designed course will empower participants to delve into intricate chord progressions, enabling them to create effective tension and resolution within their works. Key objectives include mastering voice leading techniques that facilitate smooth transitions between chords, thereby improving the overall flow of music. Additionally, the curriculum will cover advanced harmonic structures such as modal interchange and extended chords, enriching the compositional palette of each participant. By the conclusion of this course, composers will not only have a firm grasp of these advanced concepts but will also be equipped to apply them creatively in their compositions, resulting in more sophisticated and compelling music. The key outcomes include increased confidence in harmonic decision-making, enhanced creativity in composition, and a deeper understanding of the theoretical underpinnings of harmony.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the "Advanced Harmony Techniques for Composers" project includes the following components:

- **Curriculum Development**: Design a comprehensive curriculum that covers:
  - Intricate chord progressions
  - Techniques for creating tension and resolution
  - Voice leading principles
  - Advanced harmonic structures (modal interchange, extended chords)

- **Course Delivery**: 
  - Develop engaging instructional materials (e.g., presentations, handouts, and exercises)
  - Schedule and conduct live sessions (both in-person and online)
  - Facilitate discussions and Q&A sessions to encourage participant interaction

- **Assessment and Feedback**: 
  - Create assignments and projects for participants to apply learned concepts
  - Provide constructive feedback on compositions submitted by participants
  - Conduct a final assessment to evaluate the understanding and application of advanced harmony techniques

- **Resource Provision**: 
  - Compile a list of recommended readings and resources for further study
  - Provide access to online forums or groups for ongoing support and collaboration among participants
