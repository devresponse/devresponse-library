The scope of work for the "Analyzing Classical Masterpieces" project includes the following key components:

- **Curriculum Development**: Design a comprehensive curriculum that encompasses various modules focusing on different aspects of classical music theory.
- **Module Breakdown**: Each module will feature detailed breakdowns of selected classical pieces, highlighting key techniques and concepts.
- **Interactive Learning**: Incorporate interactive elements such as quizzes, discussion forums, and practical assignments to enhance student engagement.
- **Resource Compilation**: Gather and curate a list of essential resources, including recommended readings, listening examples, and supplementary materials.
- **Assessment Design**: Develop assessment tools to evaluate student understanding and progress throughout the course.
- **Marketing Strategy**: Create a marketing plan to promote the course to the target audience, including social media campaigns and partnerships with music schools.
