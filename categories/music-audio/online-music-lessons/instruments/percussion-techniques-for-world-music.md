
# Project Plan: Percussion Techniques for World Music

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Percussion Techniques for World Music" project aims to create an interactive online course that immerses students in the rich and diverse world of global percussion instruments. The primary objective is to educate participants about the history, cultural significance, and playing techniques of various percussion instruments, including congas, bongos, and djembes. This course will provide an engaging learning experience through video tutorials, hands-on practice sessions, and live Q&A opportunities with experienced instructors. Key outcomes include enhanced musical skills, a deeper appreciation for world music, and the ability to play various percussion instruments confidently. By the end of the course, participants will not only have a solid foundation in percussion techniques but also access to supplementary materials such as sheet music and practice exercises, ensuring a comprehensive and enjoyable learning journey.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Course Development**: 
  - Research and curate content on global percussion instruments.
  - Develop video tutorials covering history, techniques, and cultural contexts.
  - Create supplementary materials, including sheet music and practice exercises.

- **Interactive Components**: 
  - Design and implement hands-on practice sessions.
  - Schedule and facilitate live Q&A sessions with instructors.

- **Platform Setup**: 
  - Select and configure an online learning platform to host the course.
  - Ensure the platform supports video content, interactive sessions, and downloadable materials.

- **Marketing and Outreach**: 
  - Develop a marketing strategy to promote the course.
  - Create promotional materials and utilize social media channels for outreach.

- **Feedback and Improvement**: 
  - Collect participant feedback throughout the course.
  - Analyze feedback to make necessary adjustments and improvements.
