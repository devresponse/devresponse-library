
# Project Plan Template: Accordion Basics for Folk and Polka Music

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Accordion Basics for Folk and Polka Music" project aims to provide an engaging and interactive learning experience for individuals eager to master the accordion, particularly within the folk and polka music genres. The primary objective of this project is to equip students with essential accordion techniques, including hand positioning, bellows control, and basic chord structures. Participants will learn to play traditional folk tunes that showcase the accordion's unique sound and versatility. The course is designed to accommodate both beginners and those with some prior experience, ensuring that all participants can progress at their own pace. Key outcomes include a solid foundation in accordion playing, enabling students to confidently perform simple folk and polka pieces by the end of the course. This project not only fosters musical skills but also promotes a sense of community among participants, encouraging collaboration and shared learning experiences. Join us to embark on this musical journey and unlock the joy of accordion music!
