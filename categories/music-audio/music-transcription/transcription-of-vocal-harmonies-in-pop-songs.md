
# Project Plan Template: Transcription of Vocal Harmonies in Pop Songs

## 1. Executive Summary

The project "Transcription of Vocal Harmonies in Pop Songs" aims to deliver meticulously crafted sheet music that captures the intricate vocal harmonies found in selected pop songs. The primary objective is to provide vocal groups and arrangers with accurate and detailed transcriptions that can be utilized for rehearsals and performances. This project will involve a thorough analysis of each selected piece, focusing on melodic structure, rhythm, and dynamics to ensure that all vocal parts are represented clearly and correctly. Key outcomes include the creation of professional-grade sheet music in both digital and print formats, enhancing the repertoire of musicians and arrangers. By the end of this project, participants will have access to high-quality transcriptions that facilitate better understanding and execution of vocal harmonies in popular music.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Selection of Songs**: Identify and select a list of pop songs that feature notable vocal harmonies. The selection process will involve consultation with stakeholders to ensure relevance and popularity.
  
- **Analysis of Vocal Parts**: For each selected song, analyze the vocal arrangement, focusing on melody, harmony, rhythm, and dynamics. This will involve listening to recordings and possibly consulting original scores if available.

- **Transcription Process**: Transcribe the vocal harmonies into sheet music format. This will include:
  - Notating all vocal parts (soprano, alto, tenor, bass)
  - Indicating dynamics, articulations, and phrasing
  - Ensuring clarity and readability of the sheet music

- **Review and Revisions**: Conduct a review process involving feedback from musicians and arrangers to ensure accuracy and usability of the transcriptions. Revisions will be made based on this feedback.

- **Final Deliverables**: Prepare the final sheet music in both digital (PDF) and print formats. Ensure that the final product meets professional standards for distribution.
