
# Project Plan: Barbershop Quartet Revival

## 1. Executive Summary

The Barbershop Quartet Revival project aims to breathe new life into the classic barbershop quartet format by integrating a male lead singer and a selection of contemporary songs. This initiative seeks to assemble a group of talented male vocalists who can harmonize beautifully while infusing a modern flair into the traditional barbershop style. The lead singer will play a crucial role in shaping the quartet's sound, ensuring that the chosen songs resonate with both nostalgic and contemporary audiences. Key outcomes of this project include a series of rehearsals, the arrangement of selected songs, and a final performance that showcases the unique blend of traditional harmonies and modern music. The overarching goal is to create an engaging and memorable experience that appeals to a wide range of listeners, celebrating the rich history of barbershop music while making it relevant for today's audience. This project not only aims to entertain but also to foster a renewed appreciation for the art of barbershop singing in a modern context.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assemble the Quartet**: Identify and recruit talented male vocalists with experience in harmonization and performance.
- **Select Songs**: Curate a list of contemporary songs that can be arranged for barbershop quartet style, ensuring a mix of genres to appeal to diverse audiences.
- **Arrange Music**: Work with a music arranger to adapt the selected songs into barbershop harmony, focusing on maintaining the essence of the original pieces while enhancing them with vocal harmonies.
- **Rehearsals**: Schedule and conduct regular rehearsals to ensure the quartet is well-prepared for the final performance. This will include vocal warm-ups, song practice, and harmonization exercises.
- **Final Performance**: Organize a live performance that showcases the quartet's work, inviting an audience to experience the blend of traditional and contemporary music.
- **Marketing and Promotion**: Develop a marketing strategy to promote the performance, including social media campaigns, flyers, and community outreach.
- **Feedback and Evaluation**: After the performance, gather feedback from the audience and participants to evaluate the success of the project and identify areas for improvement.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Quartet Assembly
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of quartet members and roles.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Song Selection
  - Deliverable: Finalized list of contemporary songs for arrangement.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Music Arrangement
  - Deliverable: Completed arrangements for all selected songs.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Rehearsals
  - Deliverable: Schedule of rehearsals and completion of practice sessions.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Performance
  - Deliverable: Successful execution of the live performance event.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Feedback Collection
  - Deliverable: Compilation of audience and participant feedback.
