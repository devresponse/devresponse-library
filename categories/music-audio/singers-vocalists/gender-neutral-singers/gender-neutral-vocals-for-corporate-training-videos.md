
# Project Plan: Gender-Neutral Vocals for Corporate Training Videos

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Gender-Neutral Vocals for Corporate Training Videos" project aims to create inclusive and relatable training materials that resonate with a diverse workforce. In today's corporate environment, it is crucial to ensure that all employees feel represented and engaged in their learning experiences. This project will deliver high-quality vocal performances by gender-neutral singers, providing clear and professional voiceovers for corporate training videos. The primary objective is to enhance the learning experience by offering multiple takes for each script, allowing for the selection of the best fit for your corporate identity. By collaborating closely with your team, we will understand the specific content and tone required, ensuring that the final product reflects modern values and fosters a sense of belonging among employees. Key outcomes include improved employee engagement, a more inclusive training environment, and the delivery of professional voiceovers that align with your corporate messaging.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**: 
  - Kick-off meeting to discuss project objectives, timelines, and expectations.
  - Identify key stakeholders and establish communication protocols.

- **Content Review**:
  - Collaborate with your team to review training materials and scripts.
  - Determine the tone, style, and specific requirements for the voiceovers.

- **Vocalist Selection**:
  - Source and select talented gender-neutral vocalists based on project needs.
  - Conduct auditions if necessary to ensure quality and fit.

- **Recording Sessions**:
  - Schedule and conduct recording sessions with selected vocalists.
  - Provide multiple takes for each script to offer variety in selection.

- **Post-Production**:
  - Edit and finalize recordings to ensure high-quality audio output.
  - Incorporate feedback from your team to refine the final product.

- **Delivery**:
  - Deliver the final voiceover files in the required formats.
  - Provide documentation outlining the project and usage guidelines.

- **Feedback and Revisions**:
  - Gather feedback from your team on the delivered voiceovers.
  - Make necessary revisions based on feedback to ensure satisfaction.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 5**: Post-Production Completed

- **Milestone 6**: Final Delivery
