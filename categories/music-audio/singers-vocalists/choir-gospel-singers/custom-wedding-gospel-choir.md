
# Project Plan: Custom Wedding Gospel Choir

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Wedding Gospel Choir project aims to deliver a unique and uplifting musical experience tailored specifically for couples on their special day. Our primary objective is to curate a personalized selection of gospel songs that resonate with the couple's love story, ensuring that the performance is both meaningful and memorable. The project will involve close collaboration with the couple to understand their musical preferences and the themes they wish to convey. Key outcomes include a seamless integration of the choir's performance into the wedding ceremony and reception, a full rehearsal schedule, and effective coordination with the wedding planner. By focusing on these elements, we aim to create an unforgettable atmosphere that enhances the emotional impact of the wedding, leaving a lasting impression on the couple and their guests.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the couple to discuss their vision, preferred gospel songs, and any specific themes they wish to incorporate.
- **Song Selection**: Curate a list of gospel songs based on the couple's preferences, including options for the ceremony and reception.
- **Rehearsal Schedule**: Develop a comprehensive rehearsal schedule that accommodates the choir members and the couple's timeline.
- **Coordination with Wedding Planner**: Collaborate with the wedding planner to ensure all logistical aspects are covered, including sound equipment, venue layout, and timing.
- **Performance Delivery**: Execute the performance during the wedding ceremony and/or reception, ensuring a high-quality musical experience.
- **Post-Event Follow-Up**: Conduct a debrief with the couple to gather feedback and ensure satisfaction.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Documented preferences and song list
- **Milestone 2**: Song Selection Finalized
  - Deliverable: Finalized song list approved by the couple
- **Milestone 3**: Rehearsal Schedule Established
  - Deliverable: Detailed rehearsal schedule shared with all parties
- **Milestone 4**: Coordination with Wedding Planner Completed
  - Deliverable: Logistics plan confirmed with the wedding planner
- **Milestone 5**: Performance Delivered
  - Deliverable: Successful performance at the wedding ceremony and/or reception
- **Milestone 6**: Post-Event Feedback Collected
  - Deliverable: Feedback report summarizing the couple's experience
