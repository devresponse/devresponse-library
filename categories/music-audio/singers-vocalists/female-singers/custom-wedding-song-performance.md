
# Project Plan: Custom Wedding Song Performance

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Wedding Song Performance project aims to create a unique and memorable experience for couples by commissioning a talented female singer to perform a custom song that encapsulates their love story. The primary objective is to collaborate closely with the couple to craft a song that reflects their journey together, from their first meeting to the proposal. This project will involve understanding the couple's preferences, themes, and any special lyrics or messages they wish to include in the song. Key outcomes include a professionally recorded version of the song and a live performance at the couple's wedding or special event. By providing this personalized touch, the project seeks to enhance the emotional atmosphere of the celebration, leaving a lasting impression on the couple and their guests. Ultimately, this project will not only celebrate the couple's love but also create a unique keepsake that they can cherish for years to come.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the couple to discuss their love story, preferences, and any specific themes or messages they want to convey in the song.
- **Songwriting Process**: Collaborate with the couple to write the lyrics and compose the music for the custom song. This may involve multiple drafts and revisions based on the couple's feedback.
- **Recording**: Once the song is finalized, the singer will produce a professional recording of the song, ensuring high-quality audio.
- **Live Performance**: The singer will perform the custom song live at the couple's wedding or special event, creating a memorable moment for the couple and their guests.
- **Post-Event Follow-Up**: Provide the couple with a digital copy of the recorded song and any additional materials, such as lyrics sheets or a video of the live performance.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and agreed-upon themes for the song.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Songwriting Draft Completed
  - Deliverable: First draft of the song lyrics and melody for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Final Song Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized lyrics and melody approved by the couple.

- **Milestone 4**: Professional Recording Completed
  - Deliverable: High-quality audio recording of the custom song.

- **Milestone 5**: Live Performance at Event
  - Deliverable: Live performance of the custom song at the couple's wedding or special event.
