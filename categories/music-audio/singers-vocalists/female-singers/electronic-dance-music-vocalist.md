
# Project Plan Template: Electronic Dance Music Vocalist

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Electronic Dance Music Vocalist project is to enhance EDM tracks with powerful and engaging female vocals that resonate with the genre's vibrant energy. This project aims to identify and collaborate with a talented female vocalist who possesses the versatility to adapt her vocal style across various EDM sub-genres, including progressive house and techno. The project will focus on recording high-quality vocal tracks, providing harmonies, and potentially writing original lyrics that align seamlessly with the producer's vision. Key outcomes include the delivery of professionally recorded vocal tracks that elevate the overall sound of the EDM project, ensuring that the final product captivates listeners and keeps them dancing. By fostering a collaborative environment between the vocalist and the producer, we aim to create a unique and memorable auditory experience that stands out in the competitive EDM landscape.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Vocalist Selection**: Identify and shortlist potential female vocalists with experience in EDM.
- **Audition Process**: Conduct auditions to assess vocal range, style adaptability, and performance energy.
- **Collaboration**: Work closely with the selected vocalist to discuss the project vision, including sub-genre preferences and lyrical themes.
- **Recording Sessions**: Schedule and conduct recording sessions in a professional studio environment to capture high-quality vocal tracks.
- **Harmonies and Layering**: Provide guidance on harmonies and vocal layering to enhance the overall sound.
- **Lyric Writing**: Collaborate with the vocalist on writing original lyrics that fit the track's rhythm and energy.
- **Post-Production**: Work with audio engineers to mix and master the vocal tracks, ensuring they integrate seamlessly with the instrumental components.
- **Feedback and Revisions**: Implement feedback from the producer and make necessary revisions to the vocal tracks.
- **Final Delivery**: Deliver the final vocal tracks in the required formats for distribution and promotion.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Vocalist Selection
  - Deliverable: Shortlist of potential vocalists with demo recordings.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Audition Process Completion
  - Deliverable: Selected vocalist confirmed for the project.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Initial Collaboration Meeting
  - Deliverable: Documented project vision and agreed-upon themes.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Recording Sessions Completed
  - Deliverable: High-quality vocal tracks recorded and ready for mixing.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Lyric Writing Finalized
  - Deliverable: Completed lyrics approved by the producer.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Post-Production Completed
  - Deliverable: Mixed and mastered vocal tracks delivered.
  
- **Milestone 7**: Final Review and Approval
  - Deliverable: Final vocal tracks approved for distribution.
