
# Project Plan Template: Retro 80s Vocal Style Recording

## 1. Executive Summary

The Retro 80s Vocal Style Recording project aims to immerse clients in the vibrant and nostalgic world of 1980s music through a collaboration with a talented female vocalist. This project is designed to produce high-quality vocal tracks that capture the essence of the iconic sounds of the 80s, characterized by rich harmonies, powerful belting, and distinctive vocal styles. Our primary objective is to deliver a professional recording that not only meets but exceeds client expectations, whether for a nostalgic track, a commercial, or a multimedia project. Key outcomes include a pre-recording consultation to align on the client's vision, a dedicated recording session to ensure the delivery of tailored vocal tracks, and the creation of a final product that resonates with both the nostalgia of the past and the freshness of contemporary music. By celebrating the music of the past, we aim to create something exciting and relevant for today's audience, ensuring a memorable and impactful experience for all stakeholders involved.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Recording Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss project goals, vision, and specific requirements.
  - Review reference tracks and discuss the desired vocal style and energy.
  - Finalize the song selection and arrangement details.

- **Recording Session**:
  - Book a professional recording studio equipped with high-quality audio equipment.
  - Conduct a warm-up session with the vocalist to ensure optimal performance.
  - Record multiple takes of the selected tracks, focusing on capturing the essence of the 80s vocal style.
  - Provide the client with options for vocal harmonies and ad-libs.

- **Post-Recording Process**:
  - Edit and mix the recorded tracks to achieve a polished final product.
  - Deliver the final vocal tracks in the required formats (e.g., WAV, MP3).
  - Schedule a follow-up meeting to review the final product and gather client feedback.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Pre-Recording Consultation
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and finalized project brief.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Recording Session
  - Deliverable: Raw vocal tracks recorded during the session.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Editing and Mixing
  - Deliverable: Final mixed vocal tracks delivered to the client.

- **Milestone 4**: Client Feedback and Revisions (if necessary)
  - Deliverable: Revised tracks based on client feedback.
