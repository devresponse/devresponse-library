
# Project Plan Template: Jazz Standards Vocal Rendition

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Jazz Standards Vocal Rendition" project aims to revitalize classic jazz standards through the artistry of an exceptional female jazz vocalist. The primary objective is to collaborate closely with the vocalist to select a curated list of timeless tracks, infusing them with contemporary elements while preserving their original essence. This project will involve initial consultations to align on the artistic vision, followed by a series of rehearsals to refine the arrangements and ensure the highest quality of performance. The key outcomes will include high-quality audio recordings of each track, designed to resonate with both jazz aficionados and new listeners alike. By blending traditional jazz with modern influences, this project seeks to create a unique sound that stands out in today’s music landscape, ultimately contributing to the evolution of jazz music and expanding its audience reach.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Meet with the vocalist to discuss vision, style, and track selection.
  - Identify a list of classic jazz standards to be included in the project.

- **Track Arrangement**:
  - Collaborate with the vocalist and a music arranger to develop contemporary arrangements for selected tracks.
  - Ensure that the essence of the originals is maintained while introducing modern elements.

- **Rehearsals**:
  - Schedule and conduct rehearsals to refine the arrangements and performance.
  - Provide feedback and adjustments as necessary to enhance the final sound.

- **Recording Sessions**:
  - Organize high-quality recording sessions in a professional studio.
  - Capture audio performances of each track, ensuring optimal sound quality.

- **Post-Production**:
  - Edit and mix the recorded tracks to achieve a polished final product.
  - Prepare audio files for distribution and promotion.

- **Final Deliverables**:
  - Deliver high-quality audio recordings of all selected tracks.
  - Provide promotional materials, including cover art and track descriptions.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Documented vision and selected track list.

- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of Track Arrangements
  - Deliverable: Completed arrangements for each selected track.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Rehearsals
  - Deliverable: Rehearsal notes and feedback documentation.

- **Milestone 4**: Recording Sessions Completed
  - Deliverable: Raw audio recordings of all tracks.

- **Milestone 5**: Post-Production Completed
  - Deliverable: Final mixed and mastered audio files.
