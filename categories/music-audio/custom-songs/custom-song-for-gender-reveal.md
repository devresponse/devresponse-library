
# Project Plan Template: Custom Song for Gender Reveals

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the "Custom Song for Gender Reveals" project is to create a unique and engaging musical piece that enhances the celebratory atmosphere of a gender reveal party. This project aims to deliver a custom song that encapsulates the excitement and anticipation surrounding the moment of revealing the baby's gender. The song will be tailored to reflect the theme of the party and will incorporate personal details such as the parents' names, the baby's name, and any specific elements that the clients wish to include. Key outcomes of this project include a professionally composed and recorded track that serves as the highlight of the event, creating lasting memories for the parents and their guests. By collaborating closely with the clients, we will ensure that the final product resonates with their vision, providing a fun and memorable musical touch to their celebration.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the clients to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific details they want included in the song.
- **Theme Development**: Collaborate with the clients to establish the overall theme and mood of the song, ensuring it aligns with the gender reveal party's atmosphere.
- **Lyric Composition**: Write original lyrics that incorporate personal details and reflect the excitement of the gender reveal.
- **Melody Creation**: Compose a catchy and playful melody that complements the lyrics and engages the audience.
- **Recording**: Professionally record the song using high-quality audio equipment and musicians as needed.
- **Revisions**: Provide the clients with a draft of the song for feedback and make necessary revisions to ensure satisfaction.
- **Final Delivery**: Deliver the final mastered track in various formats (e.g., MP3, WAV) suitable for playback at the event.
- **Optional Services**: Offer additional services such as live performance at the gender reveal party or creating a music video.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and agreed-upon vision document.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Theme and Lyric Draft Completed
  - Deliverable: Draft lyrics for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Melody Composition Completed
  - Deliverable: Audio demo of the melody with lyrics.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Recording Session Completed
  - Deliverable: Raw audio files from the recording session.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised song based on client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Final mastered track in requested formats.
