
# Project Plan Template: Big Band Brass for Swing Tracks

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Big Band Brass for Swing Tracks" project aims to deliver a complete brass section specifically designed for swing music compositions. Our primary objective is to provide musicians and producers with a rich and vibrant brass sound that enhances their existing tracks. This project will involve professional session musicians who are experts in playing various brass instruments, including trumpets, trombones, and saxophones. The recording will take place in a high-quality studio environment, ensuring optimal sound clarity and depth. Key outcomes of this project include a meticulously arranged brass section that captures the essence of classic swing, multiple takes for each part to allow flexibility in the final mix, and a collaborative approach that ensures the brass arrangements complement the overall musical experience. By the end of this project, clients can expect a polished and professional brass section that elevates their swing compositions to new heights.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Musician Selection**: Identify and hire professional session musicians skilled in brass instruments (trumpets, trombones, saxophones).
- **Arrangement Development**: Collaborate with clients to develop tailored brass arrangements that fit their specific swing tracks.
- **Recording Sessions**: Schedule and conduct recording sessions in a high-quality studio, ensuring optimal sound capture.
- **Multiple Takes**: Provide multiple takes for each brass part to give clients flexibility in their final mix.
- **Mixing and Mastering**: Offer mixing and mastering services to ensure the brass section integrates seamlessly with the existing tracks.
- **Client Review**: Allow clients to review and provide feedback on the brass arrangements before final delivery.
- **Final Delivery**: Deliver the final brass tracks in the desired format, along with any necessary documentation.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of musician selection and contract signing.
  - **Deliverable**: List of selected musicians and signed contracts.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of brass arrangements.
  - **Deliverable**: Completed arrangement scores for client approval.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of recording sessions.
  - **Deliverable**: Raw audio files of recorded brass parts.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Delivery of multiple takes for each part.
  - **Deliverable**: Organized audio files with multiple takes for client selection.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Completion of mixing and mastering.
  - **Deliverable**: Final mixed and mastered brass tracks.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Client review and feedback.
  - **Deliverable**: Documented client feedback and any requested revisions.
  
- **Milestone 7**: Final delivery of all materials.
  - **Deliverable**: Final brass tracks and documentation delivered to the client.
