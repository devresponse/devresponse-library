
# Project Plan Template: Session Pianist for Pop Song Recording

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Session Pianist for Pop Song Recording" project aims to collaborate with a skilled session pianist to produce high-quality piano tracks that enhance the emotional and musical depth of your pop song. The primary objective is to create captivating piano parts that resonate with listeners and elevate the overall sound of the track. This project will commence with an in-depth consultation to align on your artistic vision, style, and specific requirements. The session pianist will leverage their expertise to record multiple takes, allowing for the selection of the most fitting performance. Key outcomes include the delivery of polished, high-quality audio files in your preferred format, ready for seamless integration into your music production. Ultimately, this collaboration seeks to ensure that your song stands out in the competitive pop music landscape, engaging audiences and enhancing your artistic expression.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Schedule a meeting to discuss the project vision, style, and specific requirements.
  - Gather information on the song's mood, tempo, and any particular piano techniques desired.

- **Recording Phase**: 
  - The session pianist will record multiple takes of the piano parts based on the agreed-upon vision.
  - Ensure a comfortable recording environment to facilitate creativity and performance quality.

- **Review Phase**: 
  - Provide the client with access to the recorded takes for review.
  - Gather feedback and select the best performance that aligns with the song's energy and mood.

- **Finalization Phase**: 
  - Edit and polish the selected piano track to ensure high-quality sound.
  - Deliver the final audio files in the client's preferred format (e.g., WAV, MP3).

- **Post-Delivery Support**: 
  - Offer a brief consultation to discuss the integration of the piano track into the overall production.
  - Provide minor adjustments if necessary based on client feedback.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Documented project vision and requirements.

- **Milestone 2**: Recording Sessions
  - Deliverable: Multiple recorded takes of piano parts.

- **Milestone 3**: Review and Selection
  - Deliverable: Selected piano performance based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Audio Delivery
  - Deliverable: High-quality audio files in the specified format.

- **Milestone 5**: Post-Delivery Consultation
  - Deliverable: Feedback session and minor adjustments if required.
