
# Project Plan: Piano Backing Tracks for Vocalists

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Piano Backing Tracks for Vocalists" project aims to provide vocalists with high-quality, professionally recorded piano accompaniment tailored to their unique vocal ranges and stylistic preferences. This project seeks to enhance vocal performances by offering meticulously crafted backing tracks that not only complement the vocalist's voice but also elevate their overall artistry. Key outcomes include the delivery of multiple versions of each track, allowing vocalists to select the accompaniment that best aligns with their artistic vision. By employing skilled session musicians and utilizing advanced recording techniques, we will ensure that each track is polished and suitable for various applications, including auditions, live performances, and studio recordings. Ultimately, this project will empower vocalists to present their best selves, providing them with the tools necessary to succeed in their musical endeavors.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Track Development**: 
  - Collaborate with vocalists to understand their specific needs, including vocal range and stylistic preferences.
  - Create a detailed outline for each track, including tempo, key signature, and musical style.

- **Recording**:
  - Hire skilled session musicians to record the piano tracks.
  - Utilize high-quality recording equipment and techniques to ensure a polished final product.

- **Editing and Mixing**:
  - Edit the recorded tracks for clarity and quality.
  - Mix the tracks to achieve a balanced sound that complements the vocals.

- **Versioning**:
  - Produce multiple versions of each track, allowing for variations in arrangement and instrumentation.
  - Provide vocalists with the opportunity to select their preferred version.

- **Delivery**:
  - Deliver the final tracks in high-quality audio formats suitable for both live and studio use.
  - Include a brief guide on how to use the tracks effectively in performances.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Project Kickoff
  - Deliverable: Initial meeting with vocalists to gather requirements and preferences.

- **Milestone 2**: Track Outlines Completed
  - Deliverable: Detailed outlines for each track, including arrangements and styles.

- **Milestone 3**: Recording Sessions Completed
  - Deliverable: All piano tracks recorded by session musicians.

- **Milestone 4**: Editing and Mixing Completed
  - Deliverable: Final mixed tracks ready for review.

- **Milestone 5**: Versioning Completed
  - Deliverable: Multiple versions of each track produced.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: High-quality audio files delivered to vocalists.
