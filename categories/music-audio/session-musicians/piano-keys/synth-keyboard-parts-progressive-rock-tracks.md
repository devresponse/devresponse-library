
# Project Plan Template: Synth Keyboards for Progressive Rock Tracks

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Synth Keyboards for Progressive Rock Tracks" project aims to elevate the auditory experience of progressive rock music by integrating intricate synth keyboard parts. The primary objective is to collaborate with a highly skilled session musician specializing in synthesizers and keyboard arrangements to create unique soundscapes that enhance the overall sound and feel of the track. This project will involve an initial consultation to discuss the artist's vision and specific requirements, followed by the musician crafting bespoke synth lines that add depth and complexity to the existing instrumentation. Key outcomes include high-quality audio files of the recorded synth parts and a detailed description of the techniques employed to achieve the desired sound. By pushing the boundaries of musical creativity, this project is designed for artists who aspire to create a rich auditory experience that resonates with listeners and stands out in the progressive rock genre.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the artist to understand their vision, musical style, and specific requirements for the synth parts.
- **Research and Development**: The session musician will research various synth sounds and techniques that align with the progressive rock genre.
- **Composition**: The musician will compose unique synth lines tailored to the track, ensuring they complement the existing instrumentation.
- **Recording**: High-quality recordings of the synth parts will be made in a professional studio environment.
- **Post-Production**: The recorded synth parts will undergo mixing and mastering to ensure they blend seamlessly with the overall track.
- **Documentation**: A detailed description of the techniques used to achieve the desired sound will be provided, including any specific synth settings or effects applied.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - **Deliverable**: Summary of the artist's vision and requirements.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Research and Development Phase Completed
  - **Deliverable**: List of proposed synth sounds and techniques.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Composition of Synth Lines Completed
  - **Deliverable**: Draft versions of the synth parts for review.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Recording Session Completed
  - **Deliverable**: High-quality audio files of the recorded synth parts.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Post-Production Completed
  - **Deliverable**: Final mixed and mastered audio files.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Documentation Delivered
  - **Deliverable**: Detailed description of techniques and settings used.
