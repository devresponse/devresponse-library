
# Project Plan Template: Piano Accompaniment for Choirs and Ensembles

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Piano Accompaniment for Choirs and Ensembles" project aims to provide professional piano accompaniment services tailored to the unique needs of choirs, ensembles, and soloists. The primary objective is to enhance the overall performance quality by delivering expert musical support that fosters a cohesive and harmonious sound. This project will involve hiring a skilled pianist who will collaborate closely with vocalists and instrumentalists to ensure that the selected repertoire is performed to the highest standard. Key outcomes include an initial consultation to assess the specific requirements of the group, meticulous preparation and rehearsal of the chosen pieces, and the delivery of either a recorded performance or live accompaniment. This service is particularly beneficial for choirs preparing for concerts, competitions, or special events, ensuring that each performance is elevated by the artistry of professional piano accompaniment.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the choir or ensemble to discuss their specific needs, including repertoire selection and performance style.
  - Gather information on the group's experience level, preferred genres, and any special requests.

- **Repertoire Preparation**: 
  - Collaborate with the group to finalize the repertoire.
  - Arrange and prepare the selected pieces for piano accompaniment.

- **Rehearsals**: 
  - Conduct rehearsals with the choir or ensemble to ensure a cohesive performance.
  - Provide feedback and adjustments to enhance the musical arrangement.

- **Final Performance**: 
  - Deliver the final performance as either a live accompaniment or a recorded session, based on the client's preference.
  - Ensure all technical requirements for the performance are met.

- **Post-Performance Review**: 
  - Conduct a debrief with the group to gather feedback and discuss the performance experience.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Summary report of the consultation, including repertoire and performance style agreement.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Repertoire Preparation
  - Deliverable: Completed arrangements of selected pieces for piano accompaniment.

- **Milestone 3**: Rehearsals
  - Deliverable: Rehearsal schedule and notes on progress and adjustments made.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Performance
  - Deliverable: Live performance or recorded session as per client requirements.

- **Milestone 5**: Post-Performance Review
  - Deliverable: Feedback report summarizing the performance and suggestions for future collaborations.
