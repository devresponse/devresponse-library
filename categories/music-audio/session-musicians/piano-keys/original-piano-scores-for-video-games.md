
# Project Plan Template: Original Piano Scores for Video Games

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Original Piano Scores for Video Games" project aims to create unique and emotionally resonant piano compositions tailored specifically for video game environments. The primary objective is to collaborate with a skilled composer to develop original scores that enhance the gameplay experience by aligning with the game's themes, characters, and emotional arcs. This project will focus on crafting mood-specific compositions that not only complement the visual elements but also immerse players in the game's narrative. Key outcomes include a series of high-quality audio tracks delivered in formats suitable for seamless integration into the game, a collaborative process involving drafts and revisions to ensure satisfaction, and ultimately, an elevated audio landscape that distinguishes the game in a competitive market. By the end of this project, game developers will have access to a set of original piano scores that enrich the overall gaming experience and resonate with players on a deeper emotional level.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Collaboration with Composer**: Engage a skilled composer with experience in video game music to understand the game's themes, characters, and emotional arcs.
- **Composition Development**: Create original piano scores that reflect the mood and atmosphere of various gameplay scenarios.
- **Drafts and Revisions**: Provide a series of drafts for each composition, allowing for feedback and revisions to ensure alignment with the game's vision.
- **Final Deliverables**: Deliver high-quality audio files in multiple formats (e.g., WAV, MP3) suitable for integration into the game.
- **Integration Support**: Offer guidance on how to implement the scores within the game’s audio framework.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Meeting with Composer
  - Deliverable: Project brief and composer agreement.
  
- **Milestone 2**: First Draft Compositions
  - Deliverable: Submission of first draft scores for review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised compositions based on feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Compositions
  - Deliverable: Finalized piano scores delivered in high-quality audio formats.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Integration Support
  - Deliverable: Documentation and support for integrating scores into the game.
