
# Project Plan: Custom Piano Medleys for Special Occasions

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Custom Piano Medleys for Special Occasions" project aims to create a unique and personalized piano medley that seamlessly blends your favorite songs into a cohesive performance. This project is designed for special events such as birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, and other significant occasions where music plays a crucial role in enhancing the atmosphere. Our primary objective is to collaborate closely with clients to select songs that resonate with their cherished memories and emotions, resulting in a polished, high-quality recording that captures the essence of their chosen pieces. Key outcomes include a tailored medley that reflects the client's personal story, a draft for client review, and the final delivery of a memorable musical experience that will leave a lasting impression on guests. By focusing on individual preferences and emotional connections, we aim to elevate the significance of each occasion through the power of music.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferred songs, and the significance of the occasion.
- **Song Selection**: Collaborate with the client to curate a list of songs that hold personal meaning, ensuring a blend that reflects their story.
- **Arrangement Development**: Our skilled session musicians will create a unique arrangement that harmonizes the selected songs into a cohesive medley.
- **Draft Recording**: Produce a preliminary recording of the medley for client review, allowing for feedback and adjustments.
- **Revisions**: Implement any requested changes based on client feedback to ensure the final product meets their expectations.
- **Final Recording**: Deliver a high-quality, polished recording of the custom piano medley.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final product in the desired format (digital download, CD, etc.) along with any additional materials (e.g., sheet music, notes on the arrangement).

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and song list draft
- **Milestone 2**: Song Selection Finalized
  - Deliverable: Confirmed list of selected songs

- **Milestone 4**: Draft Recording Delivered
  - Deliverable: Preliminary recording for client review
- **Milestone 5**: Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Updated recording based on client feedback
- **Milestone 6**: Final Recording Delivered
  - Deliverable: High-quality final recording in requested format
