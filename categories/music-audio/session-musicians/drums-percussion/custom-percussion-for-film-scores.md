
# Project Plan Template: Custom Percussion for Film Scores

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Custom Percussion for Film Scores" project aims to enhance the auditory experience of film scoring through the creation of bespoke percussion tracks. Our primary objective is to collaborate with filmmakers to develop percussion that reflects the thematic elements and emotional arcs of their films. By leveraging the expertise of professional session musicians, we will provide a diverse range of percussion styles, from traditional orchestral rhythms to innovative, experimental beats. The key outcomes of this project include high-quality audio files that seamlessly integrate into the film score, along with a comprehensive breakdown of the instrumentation used. This initiative promises to elevate the film's emotional impact, ensuring that the audience is deeply engaged and moved by the auditory experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the film director and composer to discuss the film's themes, emotional arcs, and specific percussion needs.
  
- **Research and Development**: Explore various percussion styles and techniques that align with the film's vision. This phase includes listening sessions and brainstorming ideas.

- **Composition**: Create custom percussion tracks tailored to the film's score. This will involve:
  - Writing and arranging percussion parts.
  - Selecting appropriate instruments for each track.

- **Recording Sessions**: Schedule and conduct recording sessions with professional session musicians. This includes:
  - Setting up the studio.
  - Directing musicians during recording to capture the desired sound.

- **Post-Production**: Edit and mix the recorded tracks to ensure high-quality audio output. This includes:
  - Balancing levels.
  - Adding effects as needed.

- **Final Deliverables**: Provide the client with:
  - High-quality audio files in the required formats.
  - A detailed breakdown of the instrumentation used for each track.
  - Recommendations for integrating the percussion into the overall score.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and agreed-upon percussion requirements.

- **Milestone 2**: Research and Development Phase Completed
  - Deliverable: A report outlining selected percussion styles and concepts.

- **Milestone 3**: Composition Phase Completed
  - Deliverable: Draft versions of custom percussion tracks.

- **Milestone 4**: Recording Sessions Completed
  - Deliverable: Raw audio recordings of percussion tracks.

- **Milestone 5**: Post-Production Completed
  - Deliverable: Final mixed audio files.

- **Milestone 6**: Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Final report including audio files and instrumentation breakdown.
