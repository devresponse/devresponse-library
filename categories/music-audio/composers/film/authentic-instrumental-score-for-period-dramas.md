
# Project Plan Template: Period Drama Authentic Instrumental Score

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Period Drama Authentic Instrumental Score" project aims to create a meticulously crafted instrumental score that enhances the narrative of period dramas through historically accurate musical compositions. The primary objective is to evoke the appropriate emotions and transport the audience to the era depicted on screen by utilizing instruments and musical styles that reflect the specific time period represented in the film. The composer will conduct thorough research on the historical context of the film, ensuring that each piece aligns with the cultural and musical characteristics of the time. Key outcomes include the delivery of a high-quality audio score ready for integration into the film's sound design, as well as a collaborative process with the film's director and sound team to ensure that the music complements the visual storytelling effectively. This project not only aims to enhance the viewing experience but also to contribute to the authenticity and artistic integrity of the film.
