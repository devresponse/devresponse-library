
# Project Plan Template: Documentary Emotional Underscore

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Documentary Emotional Underscore" project aims to create a subtle and emotionally resonant musical score specifically designed for documentary films. The primary objective is to enhance the storytelling experience by crafting a soundscape that evokes empathy and complements the visual elements without overshadowing the spoken narrative. The composer will collaborate closely with the client to gain a comprehensive understanding of the documentary's themes, narratives, and emotional arcs. Key outcomes include the delivery of multiple variations of the underscore, ensuring flexibility for different segments of the documentary. By translating the documentary's message into a musical language, the project seeks to create an immersive experience that supports the film's objectives and resonates with viewers on a deeper emotional level.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss the documentary's themes, narratives, and emotional arcs.
- **Research and Analysis**: Review the documentary footage and any existing materials to gain insights into the desired emotional tone.
- **Composition**: Create a series of musical underscore pieces that reflect the identified themes and emotional arcs.
- **Variations**: Develop multiple variations of the underscore to provide flexibility for different scenes and emotional cues.
- **Client Feedback**: Present the initial compositions to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Final Delivery**: Deliver the final versions of the underscore in the required formats, ensuring high-quality audio suitable for film production.
- **Post-Delivery Support**: Offer support for any adjustments or additional variations needed after the initial delivery.
