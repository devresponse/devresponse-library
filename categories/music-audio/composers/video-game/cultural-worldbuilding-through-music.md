
# Project Plan Template: Cultural Worldbuilding Through Music

## 1. Executive Summary

The project "Cultural Worldbuilding Through Music" aims to create a rich and immersive auditory experience for a video game by composing culturally diverse music that reflects various real-world musical traditions. The primary objective is to enhance the game's narrative and environment through authentic soundtracks that resonate with players on an emotional level. By researching and analyzing different cultural music styles, instruments, and rhythms, the selected game composer will ensure that each composition is not only original but also deeply rooted in its cultural context. Key outcomes of this project include multiple original compositions tailored to evoke specific feelings, a collaborative approach with the game design team to align music with gameplay mechanics, and a final soundtrack that enhances the player's connection to the game world. This project will ultimately contribute to a more engaging and immersive gaming experience, allowing players to explore diverse cultures through the medium of music.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct in-depth research on various cultural music traditions, focusing on instruments, rhythms, and styles.
  - Identify key cultural elements that can be integrated into the game's soundtrack.

- **Composition Phase**: 
  - Create multiple original compositions that reflect the researched cultural music styles.
  - Ensure each composition aligns with specific gameplay mechanics and thematic elements of the game.

- **Collaboration**: 
  - Work closely with the game design team to ensure the music enhances the narrative and emotional tone of the game.
  - Participate in regular meetings to discuss progress, receive feedback, and make necessary adjustments.

- **Final Deliverables**: 
  - Provide a complete soundtrack consisting of multiple original compositions.
  - Deliver documentation detailing the cultural influences and creative processes behind each piece.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report on selected cultural music traditions.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Initial Compositions
  - Deliverable: Draft versions of original compositions for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Finalization of Compositions
  - Deliverable: Final versions of all compositions, ready for integration into the game.

- **Milestone 4**: Documentation Submission
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive documentation detailing the cultural influences and creative processes.
