
# Project Plan Template: Choral Arrangement of Folk Tunes

## 1. Executive Summary

The Choral Arrangement of Folk Tunes project aims to create a collection of choral scores that transform traditional folk melodies into rich, harmonious pieces suitable for vocal ensembles. The primary objective is to preserve the essence of the original tunes while enhancing them with intricate vocal harmonies and textures that evoke a sense of community and emotional connection among performers and audiences alike. This project will involve a meticulous arrangement process, taking into account various vocal ranges, dynamics, and the interplay between different voice parts to ensure a balanced and engaging performance. Key outcomes include a set of easily performable choral scores that not only celebrate the cultural heritage of the folk tunes but also provide contemporary interpretations that resonate with today's audiences. Ultimately, this project seeks to foster a deeper appreciation for folk music within choral settings, encouraging singers to connect with their roots while delivering a fresh and vibrant performance experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Selection of Folk Tunes**: Identify and select a diverse range of traditional folk tunes that represent various cultures and regions.
- **Arrangement Development**: Create choral arrangements for each selected folk tune, focusing on:
  - Vocal ranges and parts (Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass)
  - Dynamics and expression
  - Interplay between voice parts to enhance harmony and texture
- **Score Preparation**: Prepare and format the final choral scores, ensuring they are clear and accessible for choirs.
- **Rehearsal and Feedback**: Conduct rehearsals with a selected vocal ensemble to test the arrangements, gather feedback, and make necessary adjustments.
- **Finalization of Scores**: Incorporate feedback and finalize the choral scores for publication.
- **Distribution**: Develop a plan for distributing the final scores to choirs and music educators.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of folk tune research and selection
  - Deliverable: List of selected folk tunes
- **Milestone 2**: First draft of choral arrangements
  - Deliverable: Draft scores for review
- **Milestone 3**: Rehearsal sessions with vocal ensemble
  - Deliverable: Feedback report from rehearsals
- **Milestone 4**: Finalized choral scores
  - Deliverable: Complete set of choral scores ready for distribution
- **Milestone 5**: Distribution plan implementation
  - Deliverable: Distribution of scores to choirs and music educators
