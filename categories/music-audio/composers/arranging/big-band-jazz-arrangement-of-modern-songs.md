
# Project Plan Template: Big Band Jazz Arrangement of Modern Songs

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Big Band Jazz Arrangement of Modern Songs" project aims to creatively transform contemporary popular tracks into vibrant arrangements suitable for a full jazz ensemble. The primary objective is to reimagine these modern songs while preserving their essence, allowing them to resonate with both jazz enthusiasts and general audiences. This project will involve a meticulous analysis of each song to identify key elements that can be enhanced through rich harmonies and intricate textures typical of big band music. Key outcomes include a series of polished arrangements that emphasize swing rhythms, making them ideal for live performances and adaptable for recordings. By the end of the project, we will deliver a catalog of arrangements that not only celebrate the original compositions but also showcase the unique character of big band jazz, providing musicians with engaging material that can elevate their performances.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Song Selection**: Identify and select a list of contemporary songs for arrangement. This will involve collaboration with stakeholders to ensure popular and relevant choices.
  
- **Arrangement Development**: Create detailed arrangements for each selected song, focusing on:
  - Instrumentation: Brass (trumpets, trombones), woodwinds, rhythm section, and optional vocals.
  - Harmonic Structure: Enhance the original song with rich harmonies.
  - Rhythm: Emphasize swing rhythms to create an engaging performance atmosphere.
  
- **Rehearsal Preparation**: Provide rehearsal notes and guidelines for musicians to effectively interpret the arrangements.
  
- **Recording Preparation**: Ensure that arrangements are adaptable for studio recordings, including necessary parts for each instrument.
  
- **Feedback and Revisions**: Incorporate feedback from musicians and stakeholders to refine arrangements as needed.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of song selection
  - **Deliverable**: Finalized list of selected songs
  
- **Milestone 2**: First draft of arrangements for all selected songs
  - **Deliverable**: Draft arrangements for review
  
- **Milestone 3**: Revisions based on feedback
  - **Deliverable**: Final arrangements ready for rehearsal
  
- **Milestone 4**: Preparation of rehearsal materials
  - **Deliverable**: Comprehensive rehearsal notes and musician parts
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final review and approval of arrangements
  - **Deliverable**: Approved arrangements for live performance and recording
