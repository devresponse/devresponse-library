
# Project Plan Template: Children’s Choir Arrangement of Nursery Rhymes

## 1. Executive Summary

The Children’s Choir Arrangement of Nursery Rhymes project aims to create a vibrant and engaging musical experience for young singers and their audiences. The primary objective is to reimagine popular nursery rhymes by incorporating playful harmonies and lively rhythms that resonate with children. This project will focus on developing vocal arrangements that are accessible and enjoyable for children, ensuring that they can easily learn and perform the pieces. Key outcomes include a collection of nursery rhyme arrangements that not only entertain but also educate young listeners about music and rhythm. The final deliverables will consist of professionally arranged sheet music and performance notes, designed to facilitate rehearsals and performances in a choir setting. By emphasizing the dynamics of group singing, this project will enhance the overall experience for both the performers and their audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Selection of Nursery Rhymes**: Identify a list of popular nursery rhymes that are suitable for children's choir arrangements. This will involve considering the age group of the choir and the educational value of each rhyme.
  
- **Arrangement Development**: Create vocal arrangements that include melody lines, harmonies, and rhythmic patterns. The arrangements will be designed to be easy for children to sing while also providing opportunities for vocal development.

- **Incorporation of Performance Notes**: Develop performance notes that guide choir directors on how to effectively teach and present the arrangements. This will include suggestions for dynamics, expression, and staging.

- **Rehearsal Materials**: Prepare rehearsal materials, including practice tracks and simplified sheet music for individual parts, to aid in the learning process.

- **Final Delivery**: Compile all arrangements, performance notes, and rehearsal materials into a comprehensive package that is easy to distribute and use in a choir setting.
