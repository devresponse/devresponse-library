
# Project Plan Template: World Music Arrangement of Pop Hits

## 1. Executive Summary

The "World Music Arrangement of Pop Hits" project aims to creatively reimagine popular music hits by infusing them with rich world music influences. The primary objective is to maintain the essence of the original songs while introducing new melodies, harmonies, and rhythms that reflect diverse cultural backgrounds. By incorporating traditional instruments such as the {{sitar}}, {{djembe}}, and {{bamboo flute}}, alongside contemporary pop elements, the project seeks to create a unique fusion that appeals to both pop music fans and those interested in global sounds. Key outcomes include a fully arranged score, audio samples, and a detailed explanation of the cultural influences integrated into each piece. This project will require collaboration with skilled musicians familiar with various world music styles to ensure authenticity and creativity in the arrangements. Ultimately, the project aims to broaden the audience's musical horizons and celebrate the beauty of diverse musical traditions.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Selection of Pop Hits**: Identify and select a list of popular music hits to be arranged. This will involve analyzing current trends and audience preferences.
  
- **Collaboration with Musicians**: Engage skilled musicians proficient in world music styles to collaborate on arrangements. This includes sourcing musicians for traditional instruments and contemporary elements.

- **Arrangement Development**: Create arrangements that blend the selected pop hits with world music influences. This will involve composing new melodies, harmonies, and rhythms that reflect the cultural backgrounds of the instruments used.

- **Recording Sessions**: Schedule and conduct recording sessions with the musicians to capture high-quality audio samples of the arrangements.

- **Documentation**: Prepare a detailed explanation of the cultural influences integrated into each piece, highlighting the significance of the chosen instruments and styles.

- **Final Deliverables**: Compile and deliver the fully arranged score, audio samples, and documentation to stakeholders.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Selection of Pop Hits
  - Deliverable: List of selected pop hits for arrangement

- **Milestone 2**: Collaboration with Musicians Established
  - Deliverable: Contracts and agreements with musicians

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Arrangement Development
  - Deliverable: Draft arrangements for review

- **Milestone 4**: Recording Sessions Completed
  - Deliverable: High-quality audio samples of arrangements

- **Milestone 5**: Documentation Finalized
  - Deliverable: Detailed explanation of cultural influences

- **Milestone 6**: Final Deliverables Submitted
  - Deliverable: Fully arranged score, audio samples, and documentation
