
# Project Plan Template: Reorchestrating Existing Scores for Smaller Ensembles

## 1. Executive Summary
The project "Reorchestrating Existing Scores for Smaller Ensembles" aims to adapt and transform existing musical theater scores to be performed by smaller ensembles or solo artists while maintaining the integrity and emotional depth of the original compositions. The primary objective is to analyze the selected scores, identifying essential musical themes and motifs that characterize the work. Through a collaborative process with the client, the composer will tailor these elements to suit the specific instrumentation and performance context, ensuring that the reorchestrated pieces resonate with both performers and audiences. Key outcomes include the delivery of high-quality sheet music for the reorchestrated works and a comprehensive guide detailing performance notes and instrumentation. This project seeks to introduce beloved musical theater pieces to new audiences in more intimate settings, thereby expanding their reach and appreciation.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work
- **Analysis of Existing Scores**: The composer will conduct a thorough analysis of the selected musical theater scores, identifying key themes, motifs, and instrumentation.
- **Collaboration with Client**: Regular meetings will be scheduled with the client to discuss specific requirements, including preferred instrumentation and performance context.
- **Reorchestration Process**: The composer will creatively adapt the identified musical elements for smaller ensembles or solo performances, ensuring the emotional and narrative integrity of the original work is preserved.
- **Creation of Sheet Music**: Final deliverables will include professionally formatted sheet music for each reorchestrated piece.
- **Performance Guide**: A detailed guide will accompany the sheet music, providing performance notes, instrumentation details, and any additional insights necessary for effective interpretation.
- **Feedback and Revisions**: The composer will incorporate feedback from the client and performers, making necessary revisions to ensure satisfaction with the final product.
