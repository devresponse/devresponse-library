
# Project Plan Template: Musical Medleys for Curtain Calls

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Musical Medleys for Curtain Calls" project aims to create a series of engaging musical medleys that encapsulate the essence of the production's key themes, designed specifically for curtain calls. The primary objective is to collaborate closely with the production team to identify impactful musical segments that resonate with the audience, ensuring that the medleys reflect the emotional highs of the performance. Key outcomes include polished compositions that enhance the overall theatrical experience and leave a lasting impression on the viewers. The selected composer will be responsible for multiple revisions based on feedback from the production team, ensuring alignment with the show's vision. Additionally, the composer will provide guidance on arrangement and orchestration tailored to the performance's specific needs, facilitating a seamless integration into the curtain call. This project not only aims to elevate the audience's farewell experience but also to reinforce the thematic elements of the production, creating a memorable conclusion to the performance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Collaboration with Production Team**: 
  - Initial meetings to discuss the production's themes and emotional arcs.
  - Identification of key musical segments for inclusion in the medleys.

- **Composition Development**: 
  - Creation of multiple medleys that encapsulate the identified themes.
  - Focus on emotional highs and audience engagement.

- **Revisions and Feedback**: 
  - Present initial compositions to the production team for feedback.
  - Implement revisions based on constructive feedback to ensure alignment with the show's vision.

- **Finalization of Compositions**: 
  - Polish the final medleys for live performance.
  - Ensure compositions are suitable for the specific arrangement and orchestration needs of the production.

- **Guidance on Performance Integration**: 
  - Provide recommendations for orchestration and arrangement.
  - Collaborate with musicians and conductors to ensure seamless integration into the curtain call.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Meeting with Production Team
  - **Deliverable**: Meeting notes and agreed-upon themes and segments.

- **Milestone 2**: First Draft of Medleys
  - **Deliverable**: Submission of initial medley compositions for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Feedback Session
  - **Deliverable**: Documented feedback from the production team.

- **Milestone 4**: Revised Medleys
  - **Deliverable**: Submission of revised medleys incorporating feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Compositions
  - **Deliverable**: Polished medleys ready for live performance.

- **Milestone 6**: Performance Integration Guidance
  - **Deliverable**: Recommendations and arrangements for orchestration.
