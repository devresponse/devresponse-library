
# Podcast Guest Management System Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Podcast Guest Management System project aims to revolutionize the way podcast producers manage their guest interactions. The primary objective is to develop an efficient and user-friendly platform that streamlines the scheduling of interviews, making it easier for hosts to coordinate with guests. This system will not only facilitate scheduling but will also provide comprehensive guest briefing documents that outline expectations, discussion topics, and logistical details. Key outcomes include improved communication through automated reminders and follow-up messages, which will enhance the overall experience for both hosts and guests. By implementing this system, podcast producers will be able to focus more on content creation, ensuring that their guest management process is organized, professional, and ultimately more productive. The project will deliver a robust solution that meets the needs of modern podcasting, fostering better relationships between hosts and their guests.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **User Research and Requirements Gathering**
  - Conduct interviews and surveys with podcast hosts and guests to identify pain points in the current guest management process.
  - Define user personas and use cases to guide system design.

- **System Design and Architecture**
  - Develop wireframes and prototypes for the user interface.
  - Design the database schema to support guest information, scheduling, and communication features.

- **Development**
  - Build the front-end interface for hosts and guests.
  - Implement back-end functionalities for scheduling, reminders, and document generation.
  - Integrate email and notification systems for automated communication.

- **Testing**
  - Conduct unit testing, integration testing, and user acceptance testing to ensure system functionality and usability.
  - Gather feedback from beta users and make necessary adjustments.

- **Deployment**
  - Launch the Podcast Guest Management System on a suitable platform.
  - Provide training and support materials for users.

- **Post-Launch Support**
  - Monitor system performance and user feedback.
  - Implement updates and improvements based on user needs.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
