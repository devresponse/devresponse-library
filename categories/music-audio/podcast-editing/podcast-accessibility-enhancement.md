
# Podcast Accessibility Enhancement Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Podcast Accessibility Enhancement project aims to ensure that podcast content is accessible to all audiences, including those with visual or hearing impairments. This initiative will focus on creating accurate transcripts of audio episodes, allowing listeners to read along or reference content at their convenience. Additionally, we will implement captions for video podcasts, ensuring that viewers can follow along regardless of their hearing ability. Our team will also assess and enhance the podcast's compatibility with various assistive technologies, such as screen readers and hearing aids, to provide a seamless experience for all users. By promoting inclusivity, we aim to break down accessibility barriers and expand the podcast's reach to a wider audience. Key outcomes include improved audience engagement, compliance with accessibility standards, and a commitment to social responsibility in content creation.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Transcription Services**: 
  - Create accurate transcripts for all existing audio episodes of the podcast.
  - Ensure transcripts are formatted for readability and accessibility.

- **Captioning for Video Podcasts**: 
  - Develop and integrate captions for all video podcast episodes.
  - Ensure captions are synchronized with audio and are easy to read.

- **Accessibility Assessment**: 
  - Conduct a thorough assessment of the podcast's current accessibility features.
  - Identify areas for improvement in compatibility with assistive technologies.

- **Implementation of Enhancements**: 
  - Implement necessary changes to improve accessibility based on the assessment findings.
  - Test the podcast with various assistive technologies to ensure seamless user experience.

- **Training and Documentation**: 
  - Provide training for the podcast team on maintaining accessibility standards.
  - Create documentation outlining best practices for future episodes.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Transcription Services
  - Deliverable: Accurate transcripts for all existing audio episodes.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Captioning of Video Podcasts
  - Deliverable: Captions for all video episodes integrated and tested.

- **Milestone 3**: Accessibility Assessment Report
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report detailing current accessibility features and recommendations for improvement.

- **Milestone 4**: Implementation of Accessibility Enhancements
  - Deliverable: Updated podcast episodes with enhanced accessibility features.

- **Milestone 5**: Training and Documentation Completion
  - Deliverable: Training session conducted and documentation provided to the podcast team.
