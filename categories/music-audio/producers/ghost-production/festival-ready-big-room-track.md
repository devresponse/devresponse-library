
# Project Plan Template: Festival-Ready Big Room Track

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Festival-Ready Big Room Track" project aims to create a high-energy electronic dance music track that embodies the vibrant spirit of festival culture. The primary objective is to produce a track that features powerful drops, uplifting melodies, and an infectious rhythm, making it ideal for large-scale events. This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of current trends in the big room genre, ensuring that the final product not only meets but exceeds industry standards. Key outcomes include a professionally produced track that resonates with festival-goers and enhances the client's performance. Throughout the production process, the client will receive regular updates and opportunities for feedback, fostering a collaborative environment that aligns the final product with their artistic vision. By the end of this project, the client will have a polished, festival-ready track that stands out in the competitive electronic dance music landscape.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct thorough research on current trends in big room electronic dance music, including popular tracks, artist styles, and festival preferences.
  
- **Composition**: Create an original composition that includes:
  - Intro
  - Build-up
  - Drop
  - Breakdown
  - Outro

- **Production**: 
  - Record and arrange all musical elements, including synths, drums, and basslines.
  - Implement sound design techniques to enhance the track's energy and appeal.

- **Mixing and Mastering**: 
  - Mix the track to ensure clarity and balance among all elements.
  - Master the final mix to meet industry standards for loudness and quality.

- **Client Feedback**: Schedule regular check-ins with the client to present drafts and gather feedback, ensuring the track aligns with their vision.

- **Final Delivery**: Provide the final track in multiple formats (WAV, MP3) along with a project file for potential future edits.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Analysis
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and insights.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Composition
  - Deliverable: Draft version of the track for initial review.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Production
  - Deliverable: Full arrangement of the track ready for mixing.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Mixing
  - Deliverable: Mixed version of the track for client feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Completion of Mastering
  - Deliverable: Final mastered track delivered in specified formats.
