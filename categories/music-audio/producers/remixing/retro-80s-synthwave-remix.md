
# Project Plan Template: Retro 80s Synthwave Remix

## 1. Executive Summary

The Retro 80s Synthwave Remix project aims to transform a contemporary music track into a captivating remix that embodies the nostalgic sounds and aesthetics of the 1980s. The primary objective is to create a fresh auditory experience that resonates with both modern listeners and fans of retro music. By utilizing vintage synthesizers, drum machines, and melodic elements characteristic of the synthwave genre, the project will enhance the original track while maintaining its core essence. Key outcomes include a high-quality audio file of the remix, a detailed report outlining the creative process, and an optional consultation session to discuss the remixing approach and any specific client requests. This project not only breathes new life into the original music but also pays homage to the iconic sounds of the past, making it an ideal choice for artists looking to connect with a broader audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific elements they want to incorporate into the remix.
- **Track Analysis**: Analyze the original music track to identify key components such as melody, rhythm, and instrumentation that can be enhanced or altered.
- **Remix Production**:
  - Select and utilize vintage synthesizers and drum machines to create the synthwave sound.
  - Develop new melodic lines and harmonies that align with the retro aesthetic.
  - Incorporate sound design techniques to evoke the 80s vibe.
- **Mixing and Mastering**: Ensure the final remix is polished and meets industry standards for audio quality.
- **Deliverables**:
  - A high-quality audio file of the remix in the desired format (e.g., WAV, MP3).
  - A brief report detailing the creative process, including insights on the choices made during the remixing.
  - An optional consultation session to review the remix and discuss any feedback or adjustments.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Client feedback and project direction confirmed.

- **Milestone 4**: Mixing and Mastering Completed

- **Milestone 5**: Project Closure
