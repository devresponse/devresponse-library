
# Project Plan Template: Corporate Team Portraits

## 1. Executive Summary
The Corporate Team Portraits project aims to deliver high-quality group portraits that encapsulate the professionalism and unity of your corporate team. In today's competitive business environment, a strong visual representation is crucial for establishing brand identity and fostering team spirit. This project will involve a collaborative approach, where our experienced portrait photographers will work closely with your team to understand your brand's essence and the message you wish to convey through the portraits. The photoshoot will be conducted at a location of your choice, utilizing various lighting techniques and settings to create an inviting and professional atmosphere. Key outcomes will include a series of professionally edited, high-resolution images that are suitable for both digital and print use, ensuring that your corporate team is presented in the best light possible. Ultimately, this project will enhance your brand's visual identity and strengthen internal and external perceptions of your team.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work
- **Pre-Production Planning**: 
  - Initial consultation to understand brand identity and desired outcomes.
  - Location scouting and selection based on client preferences.
  - Development of a shot list and discussion of poses and group arrangements.

- **Photoshoot Execution**: 
  - On-site setup of lighting and equipment.
  - Direction of team members to achieve natural and engaging poses.
  - Capture of multiple shots to ensure a variety of options.

- **Post-Production**: 
  - Selection of the best images from the shoot.
  - Professional editing of selected images to enhance quality and consistency.
  - Delivery of high-resolution images in both digital and print formats.

- **Final Review and Delivery**: 
  - Presentation of edited images for client review.
  - Final adjustments based on client feedback.
  - Delivery of final images via a secure online gallery or USB drive.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Document outlining brand identity and project goals.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Location and Shot List Confirmation
  - Deliverable: Finalized location and shot list approved by the client.

- **Milestone 3**: Photoshoot Day
  - Deliverable: Completion of the photoshoot with a variety of images captured.

- **Milestone 4**: Image Selection and Editing
  - Deliverable: Edited images ready for client review.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: High-resolution images delivered in agreed formats.
