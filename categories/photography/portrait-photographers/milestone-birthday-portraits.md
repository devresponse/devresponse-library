
# Project Plan Template: Milestone Birthday Portraits

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Milestone Birthday Portraits" project aims to create a unique and personalized portrait experience for individuals celebrating significant birthdays. Recognizing that milestone birthdays, such as 1st, 18th, 30th, and 50th, are pivotal moments in a person's life, this project is designed to capture the essence and personality of the birthday honoree through high-quality photography. The primary objective is to collaborate closely with clients to curate a portrait session that reflects their interests, style, and the significance of the occasion. Key outcomes include a collection of professionally edited digital images that not only serve as beautiful keepsakes but also as a means to share joyous memories with family and friends. By employing various lighting techniques and selecting ideal locations and props, the project will ensure that each portrait session is a memorable and meaningful experience, resulting in images that can be cherished for years to come.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific themes for the portrait session.
- **Location Selection**: Collaborate with the client to choose a suitable location that resonates with the celebrant's personality and interests.
- **Backdrop and Props**: Assist the client in selecting appropriate backdrops and props that enhance the portrait session.
- **Photography Session**: Conduct the portrait session, utilizing various lighting techniques to capture the mood and ambiance.
- **Post-Production**: Edit the selected images to ensure high-quality results, focusing on color correction, retouching, and overall enhancement.
- **Final Deliverables**: Provide the client with a selection of professionally edited digital images, formatted for both printing and online sharing.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Client brief and session plan document
- **Milestone 2**: Location and Props Finalized
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of location and props list
- **Milestone 3**: Photography Session Conducted
  - Deliverable: Raw images captured during the session
- **Milestone 4**: Image Editing Completed
  - Deliverable: Edited images ready for review
- **Milestone 5**: Final Images Delivered
  - Deliverable: Digital gallery of final images provided to the client
