
# Project Plan Template: Senior Graduation Portraits

## 1. Executive Summary

The Senior Graduation Portraits project aims to create stunning and personalized graduation portraits for high school and college seniors, capturing the essence of their academic journey and individual style. The primary objective is to provide each graduate with a memorable experience that celebrates their hard work and dedication. Through collaborative sessions, the photographer will work closely with each senior to select locations, outfits, and props that resonate with their unique stories and interests. Key outcomes include a collection of beautifully edited digital images that reflect the personality and achievements of each graduate, suitable for sharing with family and friends or for use in graduation announcements. This project not only focuses on delivering high-quality images but also emphasizes the importance of creating a comfortable and enjoyable atmosphere during the portrait sessions, ensuring that each graduate feels valued and celebrated.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with each senior to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific themes or ideas they wish to incorporate into their portraits.
- **Location Scouting**: Collaborate with the senior to choose suitable locations that reflect their personality and interests, ensuring a variety of backdrops for the portrait session.
- **Outfit and Prop Selection**: Assist the senior in selecting outfits and props that highlight their achievements and personal style, including items such as sports gear, musical instruments, or academic awards.
- **Photography Session**: Conduct the portrait session using professional lighting and composition techniques, ensuring a comfortable environment for the senior to express themselves.
- **Image Editing**: Edit the selected images to enhance quality, ensuring they are polished and ready for sharing.
- **Final Deliverables**: Provide a selection of digital images in high resolution, suitable for printing and sharing, along with any requested prints or graduation announcements.
