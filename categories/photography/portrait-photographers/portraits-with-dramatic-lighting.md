
# Project Plan: Portraits with Dramatic Lighting

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Portraits with Dramatic Lighting" project aims to create visually striking portraits that leverage dramatic lighting techniques to evoke deep emotions and enhance the overall impact of the images. This project will involve a collaborative approach, where the photographer will engage closely with the client to understand their unique vision, preferences, and the specific mood they wish to convey through their portraits. By employing various lighting setups, including chiaroscuro and high-contrast styles, the photographer will skillfully highlight the subject's features, creating a cinematic atmosphere that captivates viewers. The sessions can be conducted in a controlled studio environment or on location, depending on the desired effect and the client's preferences. Additionally, comprehensive post-production editing will be included to refine the images, ensuring they meet the highest standards of quality. The final deliverables will consist of high-resolution portraits suitable for both print and digital use, empowering clients to showcase their images across various mediums. Key outcomes of this project include enhanced client satisfaction, a portfolio of stunning portraits, and the establishment of a strong professional relationship between the photographer and the client.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and desired mood for the portraits.
- **Concept Development**: Collaborate with the client to develop a concept that aligns with their vision, including selecting appropriate locations and themes.
- **Lighting Setup**: Plan and execute various lighting techniques, such as chiaroscuro and high-contrast styles, to achieve the desired dramatic effect.
- **Photography Sessions**: Conduct portrait sessions either in a studio or on location, ensuring a comfortable environment for the subject.
- **Post-Production Editing**: Edit the images to enhance quality, adjust lighting, and refine details, ensuring the final portraits meet professional standards.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the client with high-resolution images in both print and digital formats, along with any requested prints or products.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Client brief document outlining vision and preferences.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Concept Development Finalized
  - Deliverable: Concept proposal including location, theme, and lighting setup.

- **Milestone 3**: Photography Sessions Completed
  - Deliverable: Raw images captured during the sessions.

- **Milestone 4**: Post-Production Editing Completed
  - Deliverable: Edited high-resolution images ready for delivery.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery to Client
  - Deliverable: High-resolution images provided in digital format and any requested prints.
