
# Project Plan Template: Architectural Detail Photography

## 1. Executive Summary

The Architectural Detail Photography project aims to showcase the unique architectural features of properties through high-quality photography. This project will focus on capturing intricate details such as moldings, staircases, and other distinctive design elements that set a property apart. The primary objective is to create a visually compelling portfolio that appeals to niche buyers who appreciate craftsmanship and design. By conducting a pre-shoot consultation, the photographer will identify specific areas of focus, ensuring that the shoot aligns with the client's vision. The on-site photography session will utilize professional equipment and techniques to highlight the property's best features. Following the shoot, the photographer will provide edited images that enhance the architectural details, making them suitable for marketing materials and online listings. The key outcomes include a refined portfolio that effectively attracts discerning buyers and supports real estate agents and property sellers in showcasing their properties' unique characteristics.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Shoot Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss specific architectural features to highlight.
  - Review the property layout and identify key areas of focus.
  - Establish a timeline and logistics for the on-site photography session.

- **On-Site Photography Session**: 
  - Conduct the photography session at the property, utilizing professional-grade equipment.
  - Capture a variety of angles and perspectives to showcase intricate details.
  - Ensure optimal lighting conditions to enhance the architectural features.

- **Post-Shoot Editing**: 
  - Edit the captured images to enhance clarity, color, and detail.
  - Provide a selection of images that best represent the property’s unique characteristics.
  - Prepare images for various formats suitable for online listings and marketing materials.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Deliver the edited images to the client in the agreed-upon format.
  - Provide guidance on how to best utilize the images for marketing purposes.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Pre-Shoot Consultation
  - Deliverable: Consultation notes and agreed-upon focus areas.

- **Milestone 2**: On-Site Photography Session
  - Deliverable: Raw images captured during the session.

- **Milestone 3**: Image Editing
  - Deliverable: Edited images ready for client review.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Image Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized images delivered to the client.
