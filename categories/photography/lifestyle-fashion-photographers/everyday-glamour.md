
# Project Plan Template: Everyday Glamour

## 1. Executive Summary

The 'Everyday Glamour' project aims to explore and capture the seamless integration of glamour into everyday life. The primary objective is to showcase individuals who effortlessly blend casual and high-fashion styles in their daily routines, creating a visual narrative that resonates with a broad audience. This project will involve close collaboration between the photographer and subjects to produce visually stunning images that reflect their unique personalities while incorporating elements of glamour. The shoot will take place in relatable settings, such as urban environments, cozy homes, or vibrant cafes, ensuring that the final images not only highlight current fashion trends but also tell a compelling story about the subjects' lifestyles. Key outcomes include a series of high-quality photographs suitable for brands looking to connect with consumers on a personal level, as well as for magazines seeking fresh and engaging content that captures the essence of modern living. Ultimately, 'Everyday Glamour' will bridge the gap between high fashion and everyday life, making glamour accessible and relatable.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production Phase:**
  - Identify and select subjects who embody the 'Everyday Glamour' theme.
  - Conduct interviews with subjects to understand their personal style and lifestyle.
  - Scout and finalize shooting locations that reflect relatable settings.
  - Develop a mood board to establish the visual direction of the project.
  - Create a detailed shooting schedule, including dates, times, and locations.

- **Production Phase:**
  - Conduct photo shoots with selected subjects in chosen locations.
  - Utilize professional lighting and equipment to enhance the glamour aspect.
  - Direct subjects during shoots to capture authentic expressions and poses.
  - Ensure a variety of shots, including close-ups, full-body, and candid moments.

- **Post-Production Phase:**
  - Edit and retouch photographs to enhance quality and aesthetic appeal.
  - Curate a selection of images that best represent the project's objectives.
  - Prepare images for delivery in various formats suitable for print and digital use.

- **Delivery Phase:**
  - Present the final collection of photographs to stakeholders.
  - Provide a detailed report on the project outcomes and insights gained.
  - Discuss potential for future collaborations or projects based on the success of 'Everyday Glamour.'

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1:** Completion of subject selection and interviews
  - **Deliverable:** List of selected subjects and interview summaries

- **Milestone 2:** Finalization of shooting locations and mood board
  - **Deliverable:** Approved mood board and location list

- **Milestone 3:** Completion of photo shoots
  - **Deliverable:** Raw images from all scheduled shoots

- **Milestone 4:** Final editing and selection of photographs
  - **Deliverable:** Curated collection of edited photographs

- **Milestone 5:** Presentation of final images and project report
  - **Deliverable:** Final presentation and project insights report
