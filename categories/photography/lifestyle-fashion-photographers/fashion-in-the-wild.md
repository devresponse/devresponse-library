
# Project Plan Template: Fashion in the Wild

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Fashion in the Wild" project aims to create a captivating visual narrative that merges high fashion with the raw beauty of nature. The primary objective is to showcase models in stunning outdoor locations, such as forests, mountains, and beaches, while they don high-fashion outfits that reflect elegance and sophistication. This project will not only highlight the contrast between the polished world of fashion and the untamed elements of the environment but also serve as a powerful marketing tool for brands seeking to differentiate themselves in a competitive market. Key outcomes include a series of high-resolution images that can be utilized for both print and digital platforms, as well as a behind-the-scenes video that documents the creative process. By collaborating closely with stylists and selecting diverse locations, this project will deliver a unique portfolio piece that captures the essence of both fashion and nature.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Location Scouting**: Identify and select diverse outdoor locations that embody the rawness of nature, including but not limited to forests, mountains, and beaches.
- **Model Selection**: Collaborate with agencies to select models that align with the vision of the project.
- **Styling**: Work closely with stylists to curate high-fashion outfits that complement the natural surroundings while maintaining elegance.
- **Photography**: Conduct multiple photo shoots in the selected locations, ensuring a variety of angles and compositions to capture the essence of both fashion and nature.
- **Post-Production**: Edit the final images to enhance the contrast between the sophistication of the fashion and the beauty of the environment, ensuring high-resolution quality for print and digital use.
- **Behind-the-Scenes Video**: Capture the creative process through video footage, showcasing the collaboration between the photographer, models, and stylists.
- **Final Deliverables**: Provide a comprehensive package of high-resolution images and the behind-the-scenes video to the client.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of location scouting and selection
  - **Deliverable**: List of selected locations with images and descriptions
- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of model and stylist selection
  - **Deliverable**: Contracts and agreements with models and stylists
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of photo shoots
  - **Deliverable**: Raw images from each location
- **Milestone 4**: Final editing of images
  - **Deliverable**: High-resolution edited images ready for distribution
- **Milestone 5**: Completion of behind-the-scenes video
  - **Deliverable**: Edited video showcasing the creative process
- **Milestone 6**: Delivery of final project package to the client
  - **Deliverable**: Comprehensive package including images and video
