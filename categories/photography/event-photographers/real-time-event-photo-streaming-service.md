
# Project Plan Template: Real-Time Event Photo Streaming

## 1. Executive Summary

The Real-Time Event Photo Streaming project aims to revolutionize the way attendees experience significant occasions by providing a seamless live streaming service for event photographs. This innovative service will allow guests, both on-site and remote, to view high-quality images as they are captured, enhancing their connection to the event. The project objectives include setting up advanced photographic equipment, managing a live feed, and curating images for display on large screens and online platforms. Key outcomes will involve increased attendee engagement, improved event satisfaction, and the creation of lasting memories through real-time sharing. By leveraging professional event photographers and cutting-edge technology, we will ensure that every moment is captured and shared promptly, making this service ideal for weddings, corporate events, parties, and other significant gatherings.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Event Planning**
  - Consultation with the client to understand specific needs and expectations.
  - Site visit to assess venue layout and determine optimal equipment placement.
  - Selection of appropriate technology and equipment for live streaming and photography.

- **Equipment Setup**
  - Installation of cameras, lighting, and streaming devices at the venue.
  - Testing of all equipment to ensure functionality and quality.

- **Live Streaming Management**
  - Coordination of the live feed during the event.
  - Continuous monitoring of image quality and streaming performance.
  - On-site support from professional photographers to capture key moments.

- **Image Curation and Display**
  - Real-time editing and selection of images for display.
  - Management of large screens at the venue and online platforms for remote viewers.
  - Post-event delivery of all captured images to the client.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Initial project plan and timeline approved by the client.

- **Milestone 2: Equipment Setup Completion**
  - Deliverable: All equipment installed and tested one day before the event.

- **Milestone 3: Event Day Execution**
  - Deliverable: Successful live streaming of event photographs throughout the occasion.

- **Milestone 4: Post-Event Review**
  - Deliverable: Delivery of all images and a summary report of the event performance within one week after the event.
