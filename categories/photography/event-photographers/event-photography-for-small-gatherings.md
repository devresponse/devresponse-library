
# Project Plan: Event Photography for Small Gatherings

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Event Photography for Small Gatherings" project aims to provide exceptional photography services tailored specifically for intimate events such as family reunions, private dinners, and small celebrations. The primary objective is to capture the essence of these gatherings through a personalized approach, ensuring that every significant moment is documented beautifully. This project will involve close collaboration with clients to understand their vision, preferences, and any specific requests they may have. Key outcomes include a collection of high-resolution images that reflect the atmosphere and emotions of the event, professionally edited to enhance their quality. By offering pre-event consultations, on-site photography, and post-event image delivery, this project guarantees that clients will have lasting memories of their special occasions, fostering a sense of satisfaction and connection with the captured moments.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Event Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss the event's theme, desired shots, and any specific requests.
  - Create a shot list based on the client's preferences.
  
- **Event Coverage**: 
  - Arrive at the venue ahead of time to set up equipment.
  - Capture candid moments, group photos, and special activities throughout the event.
  - Ensure a variety of shots that reflect the gathering's atmosphere.

- **Post-Event Deliverables**: 
  - Curate a selection of high-resolution images.
  - Professionally edit images to enhance quality and presentation.
  - Deliver the final images to the client via an online gallery or USB drive.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Completed shot list and event plan.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Event Day Coverage
  - Deliverable: Captured images during the event.

- **Milestone 3**: Image Editing and Curation
  - Deliverable: Edited high-resolution images.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Online gallery or USB drive containing the final images.
