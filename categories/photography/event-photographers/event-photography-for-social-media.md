
# Project Plan Template: Event Photography for Social Media

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Event Photography for Social Media" project aims to deliver high-quality photography services tailored specifically for enhancing clients' online presence during events. The primary objective is to capture key moments that resonate with the audience, thereby increasing engagement on social media platforms. This project will involve real-time editing of images to ensure they are visually appealing and optimized for various formats, allowing for immediate posting during the event. Key outcomes include a curated selection of images that not only highlight the event's essence but also align with current social media trends. By providing this service, we aim to help clients boost their visibility and create a buzz around their events, ultimately leading to increased follower engagement and brand awareness.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Event Consultation**: Discuss the client's vision, key moments to capture, and specific social media platforms to focus on.
- **Event Coverage**: Attend the event and capture high-quality images, focusing on candid moments, key speakers, and audience engagement.
- **Real-Time Editing**: Perform on-site editing to ensure images are ready for immediate posting, tailored to the specifications of each social media platform.
- **Social Media Posting**: Select and post images during the event to maximize engagement and interaction with followers.
- **Post-Event Deliverables**: Provide a curated selection of edited images, optimized for clarity and impact, delivered in various formats suitable for different social media platforms.
- **Feedback Session**: Conduct a follow-up meeting with the client to review the images and gather feedback for future projects.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Pre-Event Consultation Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Document outlining the client's vision and key moments to capture.

- **Milestone 2**: Event Coverage Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Raw images captured during the event.

- **Milestone 3**: Real-Time Editing Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Edited images ready for social media posting.

- **Milestone 4**: Social Media Posting Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Live posts made during the event.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Image Delivery Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Curated selection of edited images delivered to the client.
