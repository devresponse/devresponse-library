
# Project Plan Template: Event Photography for Nonprofits

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Event Photography for Nonprofits" project aims to provide professional photography services specifically designed for nonprofit organizations. The primary objective is to capture the essence of nonprofit events, highlighting their impact on the community and the causes they champion. By attending various events such as fundraisers, community outreach programs, and awareness campaigns, the photographer will document key moments and interactions that reflect the organization's mission. The high-quality images produced will be made available to the nonprofit for use in promotional materials, social media, and reports, thereby enhancing their visibility and storytelling capabilities. This initiative not only offers nonprofits a cost-effective solution for professional photography but also supports their efforts in raising awareness and funds for their causes. By collaborating closely with each nonprofit to understand their objectives, the photographer will ensure that the captured images align with their messaging and branding, ultimately contributing to the organization's success.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Event Consultation**: Meet with the nonprofit organization to discuss event details, objectives, and specific photography needs.
- **Event Coverage**: Attend and photograph the nonprofit events, capturing key moments, interactions, and the overall atmosphere.
- **Post-Event Editing**: Edit and enhance the images to ensure high quality and alignment with the nonprofit's branding.
- **Image Delivery**: Provide a specified number of high-resolution images to the nonprofit organization within a predetermined timeframe.
- **Usage Rights**: Grant the nonprofit full rights to use the images for promotional purposes, including social media, newsletters, and reports.
- **Feedback Session**: Conduct a follow-up meeting with the nonprofit to gather feedback and discuss the impact of the photography on their initiatives.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and agreed-upon photography objectives.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Event Coverage Completed
  - Deliverable: Captured images from the event.

- **Milestone 3**: Image Editing Completed
  - Deliverable: Edited images ready for delivery.

- **Milestone 4**: Image Delivery
  - Deliverable: High-resolution images delivered to the nonprofit.

- **Milestone 5**: Feedback Session Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of feedback and potential areas for improvement.
