
# Project Plan Template: High-Contrast Sports Presets

## 1. Executive Summary

The High-Contrast Sports Presets project aims to create a unique set of custom photo presets specifically tailored for sports photography. The primary objective is to enhance the visual impact of action shots by emphasizing sharpness and high contrast, ensuring that every moment is captured with clarity and vibrancy. This project will involve a thorough analysis of various sports photography styles, identifying key elements that contribute to dynamic imagery. The key outcomes will include a collection of presets that highlight the intensity and energy of sports, making them suitable for photographers who wish to elevate their work. Additionally, the project will deliver a comprehensive guide on how to effectively apply these presets, along with the presets themselves in formats compatible with popular editing software. Ultimately, this project is designed to streamline the editing process for photographers, enabling them to achieve professional results with ease.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: 
  - Conduct a review of existing sports photography styles.
  - Identify key elements that enhance the visual appeal of sports images.
  
- **Preset Development**: 
  - Create a series of high-contrast presets tailored for different sports.
  - Test and refine presets based on feedback from sports photographers.

- **Documentation**: 
  - Develop a comprehensive guide detailing how to apply the presets.
  - Include tips and tricks for maximizing the impact of the presets in various sports contexts.

- **Final Deliverables**: 
  - A set of high-contrast sports presets in compatible formats (e.g., .xmp, .lrtemplate).
  - A user-friendly guide that accompanies the presets.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Analysis
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings on sports photography styles.

- **Milestone 2**: Development of Initial Presets
  - Deliverable: First draft of high-contrast presets for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Testing and Refinement of Presets
  - Deliverable: Finalized set of presets based on feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Documentation Completion
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive user guide for applying the presets.

- **Milestone 5**: Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Final project report and delivery of all materials to stakeholders.
