
# Project Plan Template: Underwater Photography Presets

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Underwater Photography Presets" project aims to create a specialized set of photo presets designed specifically for underwater photography. The primary objective is to address the common challenges faced by photographers, such as color distortion and loss of vibrancy in underwater images. By developing a series of custom filters, we will enhance the natural blues and aquatic tones, transforming ordinary underwater photos into stunning visual masterpieces. Key outcomes of this project include the delivery of user-friendly presets compatible with popular editing software, along with comprehensive instructions for effective application. This project is tailored for photographers of all skill levels, providing them with the tools to elevate their underwater photography and capture the true beauty of the aquatic world. Ultimately, we aim to empower photographers to create captivating images that resonate with viewers and showcase the vibrant life beneath the surface.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Development**: 
  - Conduct a thorough analysis of common color distortions in underwater photography.
  - Identify key color correction techniques and styles that enhance underwater images.

- **Preset Creation**:
  - Develop a series of presets that correct color distortions, emphasizing blues and aquatic tones.
  - Ensure presets are compatible with popular editing software such as Adobe Lightroom and Photoshop.

- **User Testing**:
  - Engage a group of underwater photographers to test the presets.
  - Gather feedback on usability and effectiveness, making adjustments as necessary.

- **Documentation**:
  - Create detailed instructions on how to install and use the presets.
  - Include tips for achieving the best results with underwater photography.

- **Marketing and Distribution**:
  - Develop a marketing strategy to promote the presets to the target audience.
  - Set up a distribution platform for easy access and purchase.
