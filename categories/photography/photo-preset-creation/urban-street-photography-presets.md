
# Project Plan Template: Urban Street Photography Presets

## 1. Executive Summary

The Urban Street Photography Presets project aims to create a unique collection of custom photo presets tailored specifically for urban street photography. The primary objective is to develop presets that enhance the distinctive features of urban environments, emphasizing the raw, gritty essence of street scenes. By amplifying contrast, boosting vibrant colors, and adding depth to images, these presets will transform ordinary photographs into visually striking works of art. The project will involve thorough research into current trends in urban photography, testing various settings, and fine-tuning the presets to achieve the desired aesthetic. Key outcomes include a series of professional-grade filters that cater to photographers and digital artists, along with a comprehensive guide on how to effectively apply the presets. This project not only aims to elevate the quality of urban photography but also to provide users with the tools they need to capture the dynamic nature of city life.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Analyze current trends in urban street photography.
  - Identify popular editing styles and techniques used by professional photographers.
  
- **Preset Development**:
  - Create a series of presets focusing on enhancing contrast, color vibrancy, and depth.
  - Test various settings to ensure compatibility with popular editing software (e.g., Adobe Lightroom, Photoshop).
  
- **Testing and Feedback**:
  - Conduct user testing with a select group of photographers to gather feedback on the presets.
  - Fine-tune presets based on feedback to meet user expectations and aesthetic goals.
  
- **Documentation**:
  - Develop a comprehensive guide detailing how to apply the presets effectively.
  - Include tips and tricks for maximizing the impact of the presets in urban photography.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and trends in urban photography.

- **Milestone 2**: Development of Initial Presets
  - Deliverable: First draft of presets ready for testing.

- **Milestone 3**: User Testing and Feedback Collection
  - Deliverable: Feedback report summarizing user experiences and suggestions.

- **Milestone 4**: Finalization of Presets
  - Deliverable: Finalized presets ready for distribution.

- **Milestone 5**: Documentation Completion
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive guide on applying the presets.
