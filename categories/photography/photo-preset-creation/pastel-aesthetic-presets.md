
# Project Plan Template: Pastel Aesthetic Presets

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Pastel Aesthetic Presets" project aims to create a unique collection of custom photo presets that embody soft pastel tones, catering specifically to photographers and digital artists. The primary objective is to evoke a dreamy and whimsical aesthetic in images, transforming ordinary scenes into enchanting works of art. This project will begin with an initial consultation to understand the client's vision and specific requirements, ensuring that the presets align with their artistic goals. Key outcomes include a series of meticulously crafted presets that enhance the visual appeal of various types of images, allowing for seamless application and consistent results. Additionally, a comprehensive guide will be provided to clients, detailing how to effectively use the presets in their workflow. By the end of this project, clients will have access to a professional and unique set of tools that elevate their photography and artistic expression.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and specific requirements for the pastel presets.
  - Gather reference images and examples that resonate with the desired aesthetic.

- **Preset Development**:
  - Create a series of custom presets that embody soft pastel tones.
  - Ensure versatility across various types of images, including portraits, landscapes, and still life.
  - Test each preset on different images to ensure consistent results and ease of use.

- **Documentation**:
  - Develop a comprehensive user guide that includes:
    - Instructions on how to install and apply the presets.
    - Tips for achieving the best results with different types of images.
    - Troubleshooting common issues.

- **Client Review**:
  - Present the presets and user guide to the client for feedback.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on client input.

- **Final Delivery**:
  - Deliver the final set of presets and the user guide in a digital format.
  - Provide ongoing support for any questions or issues that may arise post-delivery.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client requirements and vision.

- **Milestone 2**: Preset Development Completed
  - Deliverable: A series of custom pastel presets.

- **Milestone 3**: User Guide Completed
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive guide for using the presets.

- **Milestone 4**: Client Review and Feedback
  - Deliverable: Adjusted presets based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized presets and user guide delivered to the client.
