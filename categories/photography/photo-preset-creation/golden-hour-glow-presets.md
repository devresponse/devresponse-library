
# Project Plan Template: Golden Hour Glow Presets

## 1. Executive Summary

The Golden Hour Glow Presets project aims to create a series of custom photo presets that encapsulate the enchanting warmth and beauty of sunset lighting. The primary objective is to develop a versatile set of presets tailored for outdoor portraits and landscapes, enhancing the visual appeal of photographs. This project will begin with an initial consultation to gather insights into the client's vision and specific requirements, ensuring that the final products align with their artistic goals. Key outcomes include a comprehensive range of presets that can be easily applied to various images, along with a user guide that provides detailed instructions on installation and usage. By the end of this project, photographers and digital artists will have access to professional-grade tools that elevate their work, making it an ideal solution for both seasoned professionals and enthusiastic amateurs seeking to enhance their photography.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and specific requirements for the presets.
- **Research and Development**: Analyze existing presets and sunset photography techniques to inform the design process.
- **Preset Creation**: Develop a range of presets that capture the essence of golden hour lighting, focusing on enhancing warmth, contrast, and color saturation.
- **Testing**: Apply the presets to various types of photographs (e.g., portraits, landscapes) to ensure versatility and effectiveness across different styles.
- **User Guide Creation**: Write a comprehensive user guide that includes installation instructions, usage tips, and examples of before-and-after images.
- **Feedback and Revisions**: Present the presets to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments based on their input.
- **Final Delivery**: Package the presets and user guide for delivery to the client in a user-friendly format.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
