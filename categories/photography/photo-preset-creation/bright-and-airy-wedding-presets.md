
# Project Plan Template: Bright and Airy Wedding Presets

## 1. Executive Summary

The Bright and Airy Wedding Presets project aims to create a series of custom photo presets tailored specifically for wedding photographers. The primary objective is to enhance the natural beauty of wedding photos by emphasizing soft, light, and romantic tones. This project will begin with an initial consultation to understand the client's vision and specific requirements, ensuring that the presets align with their artistic style. Key outcomes include the development of multiple presets that can be easily applied to various images, providing consistency and a professional finish. The final product will be delivered in a format compatible with popular editing software, enabling photographers to effortlessly elevate their work. Ultimately, this project seeks to add a unique touch to wedding photography portfolios, making each image feel more enchanting and memorable.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and specific requirements for the presets.
- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather inspiration from existing wedding photography styles and trends to inform the preset creation process.
- **Preset Development**: Create a series of {number_of_presets} presets that emphasize bright and airy tones, ensuring they are versatile for various lighting conditions and settings.
- **Testing and Refinement**: Apply the presets to a selection of sample images to test their effectiveness and make necessary adjustments based on feedback.
- **Finalization**: Prepare the presets for delivery, ensuring compatibility with popular editing software such as Adobe Lightroom and Photoshop.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final presets to the client along with a user guide on how to install and use them effectively.
