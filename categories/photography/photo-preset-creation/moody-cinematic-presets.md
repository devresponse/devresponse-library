
# Project Plan: Moody Cinematic Presets

## 1. Executive Summary

The Moody Cinematic Presets project aims to develop a unique collection of photo presets that embody a cinematic aesthetic characterized by deep shadows and rich, vibrant tones. The primary objective of this project is to create a versatile set of presets that enhance the emotional impact of photographs, transforming ordinary images into captivating visual narratives. This project will focus on crafting presets suitable for various lighting conditions and subjects, ensuring that photographers and digital artists can utilize them effectively across different scenarios. Key outcomes will include a polished collection of presets that evoke a sense of drama and storytelling, making them ideal for portrait, landscape, and street photography. Additionally, the project will involve thorough testing and adjustments to ensure compatibility with popular editing software, providing users with an effortless application process. By the end of this project, clients will receive a comprehensive set of presets that elevate their photography to a professional level, ultimately enhancing their creative expression and visual storytelling capabilities.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Inspiration Gathering**: 
  - Analyze existing cinematic presets and trends in photography.
  - Collect visual references and mood boards to guide the design process.

- **Preset Development**:
  - Create a diverse range of presets tailored for different lighting conditions (e.g., low light, golden hour, harsh sunlight).
  - Design presets for various subjects, including portraits, landscapes, and street photography.

- **Testing and Adjustments**:
  - Conduct thorough testing of each preset across multiple editing software platforms (e.g., Adobe Lightroom, Photoshop).
  - Gather feedback from a select group of photographers to refine presets.

- **Documentation and User Guide Creation**:
  - Develop a comprehensive user guide detailing installation, usage tips, and examples of each preset in action.
  - Create promotional materials showcasing the presets and their applications.

- **Final Review and Packaging**:
  - Perform a final review of all presets and documentation.
  - Package the presets for distribution, ensuring ease of access for users.
