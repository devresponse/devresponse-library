
# Project Plan Template: Retro 80s Vibe Presets

## 1. Executive Summary

The Retro 80s Vibe Presets project aims to create a unique collection of custom photo presets that capture the vibrant and bold color palette characteristic of the 1980s. The primary objective is to enhance digital photographs, allowing users to achieve a nostalgic retro aesthetic reminiscent of classic films and iconic photography from that era. This project will involve comprehensive research into the visual trends of the 1980s, focusing on color theory, composition styles, and the overall mood that defined the decade. Key outcomes include a set of high-quality presets compatible with popular photo editing software, along with detailed instructions for application and customization. By the end of this project, photographers, digital artists, and enthusiasts will have access to a versatile toolkit that infuses their work with a unique retro flair, enabling them to evoke the spirit of the 80s in their creative endeavors.
