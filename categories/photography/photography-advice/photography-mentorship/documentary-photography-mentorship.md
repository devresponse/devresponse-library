
# Documentary Photography Mentorship Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Documentary Photography Mentorship program aims to empower aspiring photographers to master the art of storytelling through their lens. This project is designed to provide participants with a comprehensive understanding of documentary photography, focusing on narrative construction, composition techniques, and emotional engagement with the audience. Throughout the mentorship, participants will engage in hands-on assignments that challenge their skills and foster creativity, while also receiving personalized feedback on their portfolios. Ethical considerations in documentary work will be a key topic of discussion, ensuring that participants are well-equipped to navigate the complexities of their craft. By the end of the program, participants will have developed a unique photographic style and a profound understanding of how to convey powerful stories through their images. Key outcomes include enhanced technical skills, a refined artistic voice, and a portfolio that reflects their growth as documentary photographers.
