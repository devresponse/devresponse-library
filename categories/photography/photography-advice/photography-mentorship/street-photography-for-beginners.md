
# Project Plan Template: Street Photography for Beginners

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Street Photography for Beginners" mentorship program aims to empower novice and aspiring photographers to explore the dynamic realm of street photography. This program is designed to provide participants with a comprehensive understanding of the fundamental principles of capturing candid moments and telling compelling stories through their images. Over the course of the mentorship, participants will engage in hands-on learning experiences that cover essential topics such as understanding light and composition, developing a unique photographic style, and navigating the ethical considerations of photographing people in public spaces. Key outcomes include enhanced technical skills, a portfolio of narrative-driven images, and increased confidence in their photographic abilities. By the end of the program, participants will not only appreciate the art of street photography but will also be equipped to create stunning images that resonate with viewers.

The scope of the "Street Photography for Beginners" mentorship program includes the following components:

- **Program Duration**: {{Program Duration}} weeks, with weekly sessions.
- **Target Audience**: Novice and aspiring photographers.

  - Developing a Unique Photographic Style

  - One-on-one feedback sessions
