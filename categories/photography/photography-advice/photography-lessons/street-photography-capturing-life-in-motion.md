
# Project Plan Template: Street Photography: Capturing Life in Motion

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Street Photography: Capturing Life in Motion" project aims to enhance participants' street photography skills through a series of expert-led lessons. The primary objective is to equip photographers with the techniques and insights necessary to effectively capture candid moments that reflect the vibrancy of urban life. This course will cover essential skills such as utilizing natural light, composing shots that tell compelling stories, and engaging in practical exercises in various city environments. Key outcomes include improved confidence in street photography, the ability to create stunning images, and a deeper understanding of the art of capturing life in motion. By the end of the course, participants will have developed a portfolio of work that showcases their growth and newfound skills, making this project a valuable opportunity for both beginners and experienced photographers alike.
