
# Project Plan Template: Luxury Resort Scenic Photography

## 1. Executive Summary

The Luxury Resort Scenic Photography project aims to capture the essence of luxury resorts through high-quality, breathtaking imagery that highlights both their architectural beauty and the surrounding landscapes. The primary objective is to create a visually stunning portfolio that can be utilized across various marketing channels, including websites, brochures, and social media platforms. This project will involve the selection of a skilled scenic photographer with extensive experience in travel and resort photography, who possesses a keen eye for detail and an ability to emphasize the luxurious aspects of the property. Key outcomes include a comprehensive collection of edited high-resolution images that effectively communicate the resort's aesthetic and appeal to high-end clientele. By showcasing the resort's gardens, pools, beaches, and nearby natural attractions, this project will enhance the resort's marketing efforts and ultimately drive increased bookings and brand recognition.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Photography Sessions**: Conduct multiple photography sessions at various times of the day to capture the resort in different lighting conditions.
- **Locations**: Focus on key areas including gardens, pools, beaches, and any unique architectural features of the resort.
- **Image Editing**: Provide professional editing of all selected images to ensure they meet high-quality standards for both print and digital use.
- **Portfolio Creation**: Develop a cohesive portfolio that showcases the resort's luxury and appeal, tailored for marketing purposes.
- **Usage Rights**: Ensure that the resort has full usage rights for all images captured, allowing for flexibility in marketing strategies.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation and Planning
  - Deliverable: Project plan and timeline finalized
- **Milestone 2**: Photography Sessions Completed
  - Deliverable: Raw images captured from all designated locations
- **Milestone 3**: Image Selection and Editing
  - Deliverable: Edited high-resolution images ready for review
- **Milestone 4**: Final Portfolio Submission
  - Deliverable: Complete portfolio delivered in both digital and print-ready formats
