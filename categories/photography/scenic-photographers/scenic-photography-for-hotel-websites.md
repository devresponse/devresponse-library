
# Project Plan Template: Scenic Photography for Hotel Websites

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Scenic Photography for Hotel Websites" project aims to enhance the online presence of hotels and resorts by capturing stunning scenic photographs of their surrounding landscapes. The primary objective is to showcase the natural beauty of each location, highlighting unique features such as mountains, beaches, gardens, and other picturesque views that can entice potential guests. By conducting thorough site visits, the selected scenic photographer will identify the best angles and times for shooting, ensuring optimal lighting and composition. The final images will be tailored for web use, optimized for fast loading times while maintaining high quality. Key outcomes include a collection of high-resolution images that reflect the essence of each hotel or resort, creating a compelling visual narrative that makes their websites more appealing and inviting to travelers. Ultimately, this project seeks to drive increased interest and bookings through enhanced visual storytelling.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Site Visits**: Conduct site visits to each hotel and resort to assess the landscape and identify optimal shooting locations.
- **Photography Sessions**: Schedule and execute photography sessions during the best times of day for lighting, capturing a variety of angles and compositions.
- **Image Selection**: Review and select the best images that represent the unique features of each location.
- **Image Editing**: Edit and optimize the selected images for web use, ensuring high quality while maintaining fast loading times.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide a final collection of images in the required formats and resolutions, along with usage guidelines for the hotel websites.
- **Feedback and Revisions**: Allow for client feedback and make necessary revisions to ensure satisfaction with the final product.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of site visits for all locations
  - **Deliverable**: Site visit reports with identified shooting locations and times

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of photography sessions
  - **Deliverable**: Raw image files from all sessions

- **Milestone 3**: Selection of final images
  - **Deliverable**: A curated selection of images for client review

- **Milestone 4**: Final image editing and optimization
  - **Deliverable**: High-resolution images optimized for web use

- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of final images and usage guidelines
  - **Deliverable**: Final image package and documentation
