
# Travel Destination Photo Library Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Travel Destination Photo Library project aims to compile a stunning collection of high-quality scenic photographs that highlight the beauty of popular travel destinations across the globe. The primary objective of this project is to create a curated digital library that serves as a valuable resource for travel agencies, tourism boards, and bloggers. By showcasing a diverse range of landscapes—including mountains, beaches, forests, and urban settings—this library will ensure a comprehensive representation of the world's most picturesque locations. Key outcomes include the selection of visually captivating images that evoke a sense of wanderlust, organized by destination for easy access. Each photograph will be accompanied by usage rights and metadata, facilitating seamless integration into various marketing platforms. Ultimately, this project will enhance promotional materials and inspire potential travelers, making it an essential tool for the travel industry.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Selection**: Identify and research popular travel destinations worldwide. 
- **Photography Acquisition**: Collaborate with scenic photographers to acquire high-quality images that represent each destination.
- **Image Curation**: Select images based on aesthetic appeal, diversity of landscapes, and ability to evoke wanderlust.
- **Metadata Creation**: Develop metadata for each image, including location, photographer credits, and usage rights.
- **Digital Library Development**: Create a user-friendly digital library that organizes images by destination.
- **Quality Assurance**: Perform quality checks to ensure all images meet the required standards.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the completed digital library along with documentation of usage rights and metadata.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of research and selection of destinations
  - **Deliverable**: List of selected destinations and corresponding photographers
- **Milestone 2**: Acquisition of photographs
  - **Deliverable**: Collection of high-quality images from selected photographers
- **Milestone 3**: Image curation and metadata creation
  - **Deliverable**: Curated selection of images with complete metadata
- **Milestone 4**: Development of the digital library
  - **Deliverable**: Fully functional digital library organized by destination
- **Milestone 5**: Quality assurance and final review
  - **Deliverable**: Finalized digital library ready for distribution
