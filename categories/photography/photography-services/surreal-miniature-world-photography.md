
# Project Plan: Surreal Miniature World Photography

## 1. Executive Summary

The Surreal Miniature World Photography project aims to create a captivating series of high-resolution photographs that blend miniature figures with real-world environments, challenging perceptions of reality. The primary objective is to explore the boundaries of traditional photography by employing creative techniques such as depth of field manipulation, lighting adjustments, and post-processing. This project will involve selecting diverse locations, positioning miniature figures in imaginative ways, and capturing striking images that evoke wonder and creativity. Key outcomes include a collection of visually stunning photographs that tell unique visual narratives, engaging viewers on multiple levels and inviting them to experience a surreal world where scale and imagination intertwine. Ultimately, this project seeks to push the limits of artistic expression in photography and inspire others to see the world through a different lens.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Location Selection**: Identify and scout various real-world locations that provide a suitable backdrop for surreal scenes.
- **Miniature Figure Selection**: Curate a diverse range of miniature figures that can be creatively integrated into the chosen environments.
- **Scene Composition**: Plan and design each scene, considering the placement of figures, background elements, and overall composition.
- **Photography Techniques**: Utilize advanced photography techniques, including:
  - Depth of field manipulation to create a sense of scale.
  - Strategic lighting adjustments to enhance mood and atmosphere.
  - Post-processing to refine images and amplify their surreal qualities.
- **Final Output**: Produce a series of high-resolution photographs, ensuring each image captures the intended surreal effect and narrative.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of location scouting and selection
  - **Deliverable**: List of selected locations with photographs and notes on potential scenes.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of miniature figures and scene compositions
  - **Deliverable**: Document detailing selected figures and planned compositions for each location.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Photography sessions completed
  - **Deliverable**: Raw image files from all photography sessions.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Post-processing and final edits completed
  - **Deliverable**: Final high-resolution photographs ready for presentation.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Project review and feedback session
  - **Deliverable**: Summary report of project outcomes and viewer engagement metrics.
