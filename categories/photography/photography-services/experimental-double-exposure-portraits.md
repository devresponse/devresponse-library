
# Project Plan Template: Experimental Double Exposure Portraits

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Experimental Double Exposure Portraits" project aims to create unique and artistic double exposure portraits that blend two distinct images into one cohesive composition. The primary objective is to capture the essence of the subject while telling a story or evoking emotion through the innovative use of photography techniques. This project will involve a comprehensive consultation phase to understand the client's vision, preferences, and any specific themes they wish to explore. By utilizing advanced editing software, the photographer will ensure that the final product maintains clarity and artistic integrity, resulting in high-resolution digital files suitable for both print and online use. Key outcomes include a collection of striking portraits that not only serve as personal branding tools but also as memorable gifts or social media content. Ultimately, this project seeks to push the boundaries of traditional portrait photography, offering clients a novel way to express their identity and creativity.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Initial meeting with the client to discuss their vision and preferences.
  - Exploration of themes, concepts, and potential secondary images.
  - Development of a creative brief outlining the direction of the project.

- **Photography Session**:
  - Scheduling and conducting a portrait session with the client.
  - Capturing high-quality images that will serve as the primary portrait.

- **Image Selection**:
  - Collaborating with the client to select the secondary image that complements the primary portrait.
  - Ensuring that the chosen images align with the client's vision.

- **Editing Process**:
  - Utilizing advanced editing software to blend the primary and secondary images.
  - Ensuring clarity and artistic integrity in the final composition.
  - Providing the client with updates and drafts during the editing process for feedback.

- **Final Deliverables**:
  - High-resolution digital files of the completed double exposure portraits.
  - A brief explanation of the creative process behind each portrait.
  - Optional: Printed versions of the portraits upon request.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the consultation phase
  - Deliverable: Creative brief document

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of the photography session
  - Deliverable: Raw images from the session

- **Milestone 3**: Selection of images for editing
  - Deliverable: Confirmed primary and secondary images

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of the editing process
  - Deliverable: Draft versions of the double exposure portraits for client review

- **Milestone 5**: Final delivery of high-resolution digital files
  - Deliverable: Finalized digital portraits and creative process explanations
