
# Project Plan Template: Refraction Art Photography

## 1. Executive Summary

The Refraction Art Photography project aims to explore the captivating interplay of light, glass, and liquid to create mesmerizing visual art. This innovative endeavor will involve the meticulous selection of various glass objects, such as prisms, bottles, and lenses, combined with different liquids to produce unique refraction effects. The primary objective is to transform ordinary items into extraordinary pieces of art that evoke emotion and intrigue. The project will document the entire creative process, providing insights into the techniques employed and the artistic decisions made. Key outcomes include a collection of stunning photographs that challenge traditional photography norms and an accompanying narrative that details the artistic journey. This project is designed for those who wish to push the boundaries of conventional photography and embrace a more avant-garde approach, ultimately contributing to the evolution of visual art.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Concept Development**: 
  - Investigate various glass objects and liquids that can create interesting refraction effects.
  - Develop a conceptual framework for the artistic direction of the project.

- **Material Selection**: 
  - Source and select a variety of glass objects (prisms, bottles, lenses) and liquids (water, colored liquids, oils).
  - Ensure all materials are safe and suitable for photography.

- **Photography Sessions**: 
  - Schedule multiple photography sessions to experiment with different angles, lighting, and backgrounds.
  - Capture a wide range of images focusing on the interplay of light and glass.

- **Post-Processing**: 
  - Edit and enhance the captured images using photography software to achieve the desired artistic effect.
  - Prepare images for presentation and potential exhibition.

- **Documentation**: 
  - Keep a detailed journal of the creative process, including techniques used and decisions made.
  - Create a narrative that accompanies the final collection of photographs.

- **Final Presentation**: 
  - Curate a selection of the best images for a final exhibition or digital showcase.
  - Prepare a presentation that includes the narrative and insights from the project.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research and Concept Development
  - Deliverable: Concept document outlining the artistic direction and selected materials.

- **Milestone 2**: Material Selection Finalized
  - Deliverable: List of selected glass objects and liquids with sourcing details.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Photography Sessions
  - Deliverable: Raw image files from all photography sessions.

- **Milestone 4**: Post-Processing Completed
  - Deliverable: Edited images ready for presentation.

- **Milestone 5**: Documentation and Narrative Finalized
  - Deliverable: Completed journal and narrative document.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Presentation Prepared
  - Deliverable: Curated collection of images and presentation materials for exhibition.
