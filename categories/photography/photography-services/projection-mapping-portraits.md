
# Project Plan Template: Projection Mapping Portraits

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Projection Mapping Portraits" project aims to revolutionize the art of portrait photography through the innovative technique of projection mapping. Our primary objective is to create stunning and interactive portraits that not only capture the essence of the subject but also incorporate dynamic visuals that resonate with the client's vision. The project will begin with a comprehensive consultation to understand the specific themes and visuals desired by the client. Following this, we will identify suitable locations and subjects for the shoot, ensuring that each portrait is unique and tailored to the client's preferences. During the photography session, we will employ projection mapping to cast dynamic visuals onto the subjects, creating an engaging interplay of light and shadow that enhances the overall aesthetic. The post-production phase will focus on seamlessly integrating these projections into high-resolution images, resulting in captivating portraits that stand out in both print and digital formats. Key outcomes include a portfolio of unique portraits that exemplify creativity and innovation, as well as a satisfied client who feels their vision has been realized.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct an initial meeting with the client to discuss their vision, themes, and desired outcomes.
  - Develop a concept proposal based on the client's input.

- **Pre-Production**:
  - Identify and secure suitable locations for the photography sessions.
  - Select subjects that align with the client's vision.
  - Plan the projection visuals that will be used during the shoot.

- **Photography Session**:
  - Set up the necessary equipment for projection mapping.
  - Execute the photography session, projecting visuals onto the subjects in real-time.
  - Capture a variety of shots to ensure a diverse selection of portraits.

- **Post-Production**:
  - Edit the images to integrate the projections seamlessly.
  - Enhance the overall aesthetic through color correction and retouching.
  - Prepare high-resolution images for both print and digital use.

- **Final Deliverables**:
  - Provide the client with a selection of high-resolution portraits.
  - Deliver digital files in various formats suitable for different uses.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the initial consultation and concept proposal.
  - **Deliverable**: Concept proposal document.

- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of locations and subjects.
  - **Deliverable**: Location and subject list.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of the photography session.
  - **Deliverable**: Raw images captured during the session.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of post-production editing.
  - **Deliverable**: High-resolution edited portraits.

- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of final images to the client.
  - **Deliverable**: Digital files of the final portraits.
