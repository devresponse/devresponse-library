
# Project Plan: Interactive Projection Portraits

## 1. Executive Summary

The Interactive Projection Portraits project aims to revolutionize traditional portrait photography by integrating innovative interactive projection techniques. The primary objective is to capture high-quality portraits that not only highlight the subject's personality but also create an immersive experience through dynamic visuals that respond to the subject's movements and expressions. This project will leverage cutting-edge technology to produce a series of stunning images that stand out from conventional offerings, appealing to clients seeking distinctive and memorable photographic solutions. Key outcomes include a selection of edited portraits featuring the interactive projections, a behind-the-scenes video documenting the creative process, and a consultation session to discuss the vision and execution of the project. By merging art and technology, this project seeks to redefine the boundaries of portrait photography and engage viewers in a unique way.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Production Planning**
  - Define project objectives and vision with the client.
  - Develop a creative brief outlining the desired interactive elements and themes.
  - Scout and select suitable locations for the photo shoot.
  - Assemble a team of photographers, projection artists, and technical support.

- **Production Phase**
  - Conduct the photo shoot, capturing high-quality portraits of the subject.
  - Implement interactive projection techniques during the shoot, ensuring visuals align with the subject's movements and expressions.
  - Document the process through video recording for behind-the-scenes content.

- **Post-Production**
  - Edit the captured portraits to enhance quality and integrate interactive projections.
  - Produce a behind-the-scenes video showcasing the creative process and technology used.
  - Prepare a consultation session to review the final deliverables with the client.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Creative brief and project plan approval.
  
- **Milestone 2: Location Scouting and Team Assembly**
  - Deliverable: Confirmed locations and team roles.

- **Milestone 3: Photo Shoot Completion**
  - Deliverable: Raw images and behind-the-scenes footage.

- **Milestone 4: Post-Production Completion**
  - Deliverable: Edited portraits and finalized behind-the-scenes video.

- **Milestone 5: Final Review and Consultation**
  - Deliverable: Client feedback and final adjustments.
