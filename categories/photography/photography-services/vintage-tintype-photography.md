
# Project Plan Template: Vintage Tintype Photography

## 1. Executive Summary

The Vintage Tintype Photography project aims to revive the classic tintype process, offering clients a unique and nostalgic way to capture their most cherished moments. This project will provide a distinctive service that utilizes the traditional wet plate collodion technique, which has been celebrated since the 1850s for its ability to create rich textures and a timeless aesthetic that digital photography cannot replicate. The primary objective is to deliver one-of-a-kind images that resonate with the emotional significance of the moments they represent. Key outcomes include personalized consultations with clients to understand their vision, hands-on photography sessions that demonstrate the tintype process, and the delivery of beautifully finished tintypes alongside a digital gallery of the captured images. This service is particularly suited for individuals seeking to commemorate special occasions or create unique art pieces that stand out in a world dominated by digital imagery.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct initial meetings with clients to discuss their vision and preferences.
  - Provide information about the tintype process and what to expect during the session.

- **Photography Session**: 
  - Set up the necessary equipment and materials for the wet plate collodion process.
  - Guide clients through the photography session, ensuring a comfortable and engaging experience.
  - Capture images using the tintype technique, focusing on the unique aspects of each client’s vision.

- **Post-Production**: 
  - Develop the captured tintypes using traditional methods.
  - Create a digital gallery showcasing the images taken during the session.

- **Delivery**: 
  - Present clients with their finished tintypes.
  - Provide access to the digital gallery for sharing and archiving purposes.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Client Consultations
  - Deliverable: Client vision document outlining preferences and expectations.

- **Milestone 2**: Photography Session Execution
  - Deliverable: Captured tintypes and behind-the-scenes images.

- **Milestone 3**: Development of Tintypes
  - Deliverable: Finished tintypes ready for presentation.

- **Milestone 4**: Digital Gallery Creation
  - Deliverable: Online gallery link for clients to view and share their images.
