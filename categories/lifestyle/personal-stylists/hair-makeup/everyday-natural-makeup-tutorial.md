
# Project Plan Template: Everyday Natural Makeup Tutorial

## 1. Executive Summary

The Everyday Natural Makeup Tutorial project aims to empower clients with the skills and knowledge necessary to achieve a flawless, natural makeup look that is suitable for everyday wear. This comprehensive tutorial will provide a step-by-step guide tailored to individual preferences and skin types, ensuring personalized guidance for each participant. The project will cover essential techniques, including skin preparation, foundation application, subtle eye makeup, and natural lip color. Key outcomes include increased client confidence in makeup application, enhanced understanding of product selection, and the ability to adapt makeup routines for various occasions. By the end of the tutorial, clients will not only master the art of natural makeup but also feel equipped to enhance their natural beauty effortlessly.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Client Consultation**: Conduct an initial consultation to understand each client's preferences, skin type, and specific needs.
- **Tutorial Development**: Create a customized tutorial plan that includes:
  - Skin preparation techniques
  - Foundation application methods tailored to skin types
  - Subtle eye makeup techniques
  - Recommendations for natural lip colors
- **Product Recommendations**: Provide a list of recommended products based on individual client needs, including alternatives for different budgets.
- **Hands-On Instruction**: Conduct a live tutorial session where clients will practice techniques with guidance.
- **Follow-Up Support**: Offer a follow-up session or Q&A to address any questions and reinforce learning.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Client Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Customized tutorial plan for each client
- **Milestone 2**: Tutorial Development Finalized
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive tutorial materials, including product lists and technique guides
- **Milestone 3**: Live Tutorial Session Conducted
  - Deliverable: Successful completion of the tutorial with client participation
- **Milestone 4**: Follow-Up Support Provided
  - Deliverable: Q&A session or feedback collection from clients
