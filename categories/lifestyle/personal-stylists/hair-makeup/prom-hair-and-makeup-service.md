
# Project Plan: Prom Hair and Makeup Service

## 1. Executive Summary

The Prom Hair and Makeup Service project aims to provide a comprehensive and personalized beauty experience for clients on their prom night. Our primary objective is to create a stunning and youthful appearance that enhances the client's natural beauty while aligning with their individual style and preferences. The project encompasses a pre-event consultation to discuss desired styles, colors, and specific requests, ensuring that each client feels heard and valued. On the day of the event, our skilled hair stylists and makeup artists will deliver a full range of services, including hair styling options such as updos, curls, or sleek looks, paired with professional makeup application. Key outcomes of this project include client satisfaction, a polished and radiant appearance for each participant, and the establishment of a reputable service that clients can trust for their special occasions. Ultimately, our goal is to make prom night an unforgettable experience, filled with confidence and joy.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Event Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific requests.
  - Provide style and color recommendations based on the client's outfit and personal style.
  
- **Hair Styling Services**:
  - Offer a variety of hair styling options, including:
    - Updos
    - Loose curls
    - Sleek straight styles
  - Ensure all hair products used are suitable for the client's hair type and preferences.

- **Makeup Application**:
  - Provide a professional makeup application that enhances the client's natural features.
  - Discuss and apply makeup styles that align with the client's vision, including options for:
    - Foundation and concealer
    - Eye makeup (eyeshadow, eyeliner, mascara)
    - Lip color
    - Blush and highlighter

- **Day of Event Execution**:
  - Arrive at the designated location on time to set up and prepare for services.
  - Provide a relaxing and enjoyable experience for the client, ensuring they feel pampered and special.

- **Post-Service Follow-Up**:
  - Gather feedback from the client regarding their experience and satisfaction with the services provided.
  - Offer recommendations for future events or services.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Scheduled
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of consultation date and time with the client.

- **Milestone 2**: Style and Color Plan Finalized
  - Deliverable: Document outlining agreed-upon hair and makeup styles.

- **Milestone 3**: Service Date Confirmation
  - Deliverable: Final confirmation of service date, time, and location.

- **Milestone 4**: Hair and Makeup Services Delivered
  - Deliverable: Completed hair and makeup services on the day of the event.

- **Milestone 5**: Client Feedback Collected
  - Deliverable: Feedback form completed by the client post-event.
