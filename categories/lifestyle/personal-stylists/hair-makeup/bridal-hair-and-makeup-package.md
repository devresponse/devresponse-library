
# Bridal Hair and Makeup Package Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Bridal Hair and Makeup Package aims to provide brides with a seamless and personalized experience on one of the most significant days of their lives. This project is designed to enhance the bride's natural beauty while ensuring her look aligns with her personal style and the overall theme of the wedding. The project will begin with an initial consultation to discuss the bride's vision, preferences, and specific styles she wishes to explore. Following this, a trial session will be conducted, allowing the bride to experience her chosen hair and makeup looks, with opportunities for adjustments to ensure complete satisfaction. On the wedding day, our professional stylists will arrive on-site to execute the final look, ensuring the bride feels confident and radiant. Additionally, we will provide touch-up services throughout the event to maintain the perfect appearance. Key outcomes include high client satisfaction, a flawless execution of hair and makeup, and a memorable experience for the bride and her party.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the bride to discuss her vision, preferences, and any specific styles she wishes to explore.
  - Document the bride's ideas and inspirations.

- **Trial Session**: 
  - Conduct a trial session to apply the chosen hair and makeup looks.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on the bride's feedback.
  - Finalize the look and document all products and techniques used.

- **Wedding Day Services**: 
  - Arrive on-site at the agreed time to execute the final hair and makeup look.
  - Ensure all tools and products are prepared and organized for efficiency.
  - Provide touch-up services throughout the event as needed.

- **Post-Wedding Follow-Up**: 
  - Conduct a follow-up with the bride to gather feedback on the services provided.
  - Address any concerns and document testimonials for future marketing.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Documented vision and preferences of the bride.

- **Milestone 2**: Trial Session Completed
  - Deliverable: Finalized hair and makeup look with documented products and techniques.

- **Milestone 3**: Wedding Day Services Executed
  - Deliverable: Bride's hair and makeup completed to satisfaction, with touch-up services provided.

- **Milestone 4**: Post-Wedding Follow-Up Completed
  - Deliverable: Feedback and testimonials collected from the bride.
