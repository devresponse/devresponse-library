
# Project Plan: Hair and Makeup for Fitness Competitions

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Hair and Makeup for Fitness Competitions" project aims to provide specialized hair and makeup services tailored specifically for fitness competitors preparing for competitions. Our primary objective is to enhance the stage presence of each competitor, ensuring they look their absolute best under bright lights. This project encompasses a comprehensive approach, including a pre-competition consultation to discuss desired styles, a trial session to finalize the look, and on-site application on the day of the event. Key outcomes include the delivery of customized looks that enhance physique and personal style, the use of high-quality, long-lasting products, and the assurance that clients feel confident and radiant throughout their competition. By collaborating closely with clients, we aim to celebrate their hard work and dedication, providing exceptional results for bodybuilding competitions, fitness model showcases, and other fitness-related events.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Competition Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss their vision and preferences.
  - Assess the competitor's physique and personal style to tailor the hair and makeup accordingly.
  
- **Trial Session**: 
  - Conduct a trial session to finalize the look.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on client feedback.
  - Document the finalized look for reference on the competition day.

- **On-Site Application**: 
  - Provide hair and makeup services on the day of the event.
  - Ensure timely arrival at the venue to allow for any last-minute adjustments.
  - Use high-quality, long-lasting products designed for stage performance.

- **Post-Event Follow-Up**: 
  - Gather feedback from the client regarding their experience.
  - Discuss any potential improvements for future services.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Pre-Competition Consultation
  - Deliverable: Client's vision and preferences documented.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Trial Session
  - Deliverable: Finalized hair and makeup look documented and approved by the client.

- **Milestone 3**: On-Site Application on Competition Day
  - Deliverable: Successfully applied hair and makeup for the competitor, ensuring they are competition-ready.

- **Milestone 4**: Post-Event Follow-Up
  - Deliverable: Feedback report summarizing client satisfaction and areas for improvement.
