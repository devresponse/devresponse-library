
# Project Plan Template: Corporate Headshot Hair and Makeup

## 1. Executive Summary

The Corporate Headshot Hair and Makeup project aims to provide clients with a polished and sophisticated appearance that enhances their professional image through high-quality headshots. In today's competitive corporate environment, first impressions are paramount, and our service is designed to help clients project confidence and professionalism. The project will involve a comprehensive consultation to understand each client's personal style and preferences, ensuring that the final look aligns with their professional goals. Key outcomes include a tailored hair and makeup application that enhances natural features while maintaining a professional appearance, resulting in headshots that effectively elevate the client's personal brand. Our experienced stylists will work diligently to ensure that every client feels comfortable and confident, ultimately leading to a successful and impactful headshot session.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Schedule a comprehensive consultation with the client to discuss their preferences, desired look, and specific requirements for the headshot.
  - Assess the client's personal style and the image they wish to project.

- **Preparation Phase**: 
  - Develop a customized hair and makeup plan based on the consultation.
  - Gather all necessary products and tools to achieve the desired look.

- **Execution Phase**: 
  - On the day of the shoot, arrive at the location with ample time to set up.
  - Style the client's hair according to the agreed-upon plan, ensuring it complements their features and attire.
  - Apply makeup that enhances the client's natural beauty while maintaining a professional appearance.

- **Post-Session Review**: 
  - Provide the client with a brief review of the headshot session and gather feedback on their experience.
  - Offer recommendations for future styling or makeup needs.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Completed consultation notes and personalized styling plan.

- **Milestone 2**: Preparation for Headshot Session
  - Deliverable: All necessary products and tools prepared and confirmed.

- **Milestone 3**: Headshot Session Day
  - Deliverable: Successfully styled hair and applied makeup for the client.

- **Milestone 4**: Post-Session Feedback
  - Deliverable: Client feedback collected and documented.
