
# Project Plan Template: Teen Makeup Workshop

## 1. Executive Summary

The Teen Makeup Workshop aims to empower young individuals by providing them with essential knowledge and skills to enhance their natural beauty through age-appropriate makeup techniques and skincare practices. This workshop will offer a comprehensive overview of various skin types, proper skincare routines, and the fundamentals of makeup application, including foundation, blush, eye makeup, and lip care. Participants will engage in hands-on activities, allowing them to practice techniques under the guidance of experienced makeup artists. The workshop will also focus on fostering self-confidence and personal expression through makeup, ensuring that each teen leaves feeling inspired and knowledgeable. By the end of the session, attendees will receive a personalized makeup kit and a comprehensive guide to continue their beauty journey at home. The key outcomes include improved makeup skills, enhanced self-esteem, and a deeper understanding of skincare, ultimately equipping participants with the tools they need to express their individuality.
