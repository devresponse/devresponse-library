
# Project Plan: Hair and Makeup for Theater Performances

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Hair and Makeup for Theater Performances" project aims to deliver specialized hair and makeup services tailored to the unique needs of theater performers. The primary objective is to enhance the visual impact of each character through a collaborative process involving the stylist, performers, and directors. By understanding the character's persona and the overall production theme, the stylist will create looks that not only align with the narrative but also withstand the rigors of stage lighting and photography. Key outcomes include a pre-performance consultation to assess individual requirements, the application of makeup that ensures visibility from the audience's perspective, and hair styling that reflects the character's traits. Ultimately, this project will contribute to a captivating theatrical experience, ensuring that each performer is stage-ready and able to deliver a powerful performance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Performance Consultation**: 
  - Conduct individual meetings with each performer and the director to discuss character requirements, including personality traits, skin tones, and hair types.
  - Develop a comprehensive understanding of the production theme to ensure cohesive styling across all performers.

- **Makeup Application**: 
  - Apply stage makeup that is durable and designed to withstand bright stage lighting and photography.
  - Utilize products that enhance visibility and impact, ensuring that performers are effectively seen by the audience.

- **Hair Styling**: 
  - Provide hair styling services that align with the character's traits and the overall aesthetic of the production.
  - Offer guidance on hair maintenance and styling techniques for performers to use during the run of the show.

- **Final Touches and Adjustments**: 
  - Conduct final checks and adjustments to ensure each performer is stage-ready before each performance.
  - Be available for touch-ups during performances as needed.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Pre-Performance Consultations
  - Deliverable: Documented character requirements and styling plans for each performer.

- **Milestone 2**: Makeup and Hair Styling Application
  - Deliverable: Fully styled performers ready for the first dress rehearsal.

- **Milestone 3**: Final Adjustments and Touch-Ups
  - Deliverable: Performers are stage-ready for the opening night performance.

- **Milestone 4**: Post-Performance Review
  - Deliverable: Feedback report from performers and directors regarding the effectiveness of the hair and makeup services.
