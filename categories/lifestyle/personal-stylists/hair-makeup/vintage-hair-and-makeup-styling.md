
# Project Plan Template: Vintage Hair and Makeup Styling

## 1. Executive Summary

The Vintage Hair and Makeup Styling project aims to deliver exquisite styling services that transport clients back to their favorite eras, including the glamorous 1920s, the elegant 1950s, and the bold 1980s. Our primary objective is to collaborate closely with clients to understand their vision and the specific theme of their event or photoshoot. This project will begin with a detailed consultation to discuss preferences, styles, and any specific requirements. The key outcomes include a tailored hair and makeup application that enhances the client's natural beauty while embodying the essence of the chosen vintage style. Each session will ensure that the final look is meticulously crafted for visual impact and longevity, allowing clients to shine throughout their event. Ultimately, our goal is to create a memorable experience that leaves clients feeling confident and beautiful, perfectly aligned with the vintage theme they desire. 

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Schedule an initial consultation with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and specific requirements.
  - Gather information on the event theme, desired vintage era, and any reference images or inspirations.

- **Styling Preparation**:
  - Research and select appropriate hair and makeup techniques that align with the chosen vintage era.
  - Prepare a list of necessary products and tools required for the styling session.

- **Application Phase**:
  - Execute the hair and makeup application during the scheduled session, ensuring a comfortable and enjoyable experience for the client.
  - Provide guidance and tips for maintaining the look throughout the event or photoshoot.

- **Follow-Up**:
  - Conduct a follow-up with the client post-event to gather feedback and assess satisfaction with the services provided.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Client's vision and preferences documented.

- **Milestone 2**: Styling Preparation Completed
  - Deliverable: List of products and tools prepared and confirmed.

- **Milestone 3**: Hair and Makeup Application Completed
  - Deliverable: Final look achieved and client satisfaction confirmed.

- **Milestone 4**: Follow-Up Completed
  - Deliverable: Client feedback collected and documented.
