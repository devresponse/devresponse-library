
# Project Plan: Glamorous Red Carpet Look

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Glamorous Red Carpet Look" project aims to deliver an exceptional hair and makeup experience tailored specifically for clients attending formal events or galas. Our primary objective is to enhance the client's natural beauty through a customized look that aligns with their vision and preferences. This project will involve a comprehensive pre-event consultation to gather insights into the client's desired style, ensuring that every detail is meticulously planned. Key outcomes include a luxurious hair styling session, which may feature intricate updos or flowing waves, alongside a professional makeup application using high-quality products for a flawless finish. By focusing on quality and client satisfaction, we aim to provide a seamless experience from start to finish, leaving our clients feeling confident, glamorous, and ready to turn heads on the red carpet.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Event Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a one-on-one consultation with the client to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific requirements.
  - Review inspiration images and discuss color palettes, styles, and desired looks.

- **Hair Styling**: 
  - Provide a full hair styling session, which may include:
    - Intricate updos
    - Flowing waves
    - Hair accessories if requested
  - Ensure the hairstyle complements the client's overall look and outfit.

- **Makeup Application**: 
  - Conduct a professional makeup application that includes:
    - Flawless foundation matching
    - Eye makeup tailored to enhance the client's features
    - Lip color selection that aligns with the overall look
    - Use of high-quality, long-lasting products to ensure durability throughout the event.

- **Final Touches**: 
  - Conduct a final review with the client to ensure satisfaction with the overall look.
  - Make any necessary adjustments based on client feedback.

- **Post-Event Follow-Up**: 
  - Send a follow-up message to gather feedback on the experience and the longevity of the hair and makeup.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Pre-Event Consultation
  - Deliverable: Completed consultation notes and agreed-upon look.

- **Milestone 2**: Hair Styling Session
  - Deliverable: Styled hair ready for the event.

- **Milestone 3**: Makeup Application
  - Deliverable: Completed makeup application.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Review
  - Deliverable: Client approval of the final look.

- **Milestone 5**: Post-Event Follow-Up
  - Deliverable: Feedback report from the client.
