
# Project Plan: Hair and Makeup for Senior Portraits

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Hair and Makeup for Senior Portraits" project aims to provide high school seniors with specialized hair and makeup services tailored to enhance their natural beauty during their portrait sessions. Our primary objective is to create a personalized experience that reflects each senior's unique style and preferences, ensuring they feel confident and look stunning in their photographs. The project will include a pre-appointment consultation to discuss desired styles, skin types, and specific requirements, allowing our expert stylists to craft a customized look. On the day of the portrait session, our team will deliver professional hair styling and makeup application, ensuring a seamless and enjoyable experience. Key outcomes of this project include increased client satisfaction, memorable portrait sessions, and a lasting impression that captures this significant milestone in the lives of high school seniors. By focusing on individual needs and preferences, we aim to elevate the standard of beauty services provided for senior portraits.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Pre-Appointment Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a consultation with each senior to discuss their desired hair and makeup styles.
  - Assess skin types and any specific requirements or allergies.
  - Create a personalized style guide based on the consultation.

- **Hair and Makeup Services**:
  - Provide professional hair styling on the day of the portrait session, including options for updos, curls, or straight styles.
  - Offer makeup application services that enhance the senior's features while ensuring a natural look.
  - Use high-quality products suitable for photography to ensure longevity and vibrancy.

- **Post-Session Follow-Up**:
  - Gather feedback from the seniors and their families regarding the services provided.
  - Offer a digital portfolio of the styled looks for personal use and sharing.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Scheduled
  - Deliverable: Completed consultation notes and personalized style guide.

- **Milestone 2**: Hair and Makeup Services Delivered
  - Deliverable: Styled hair and makeup on the day of the portrait session.

- **Milestone 3**: Feedback Collection
  - Deliverable: Compiled feedback from clients and families.

- **Milestone 4**: Digital Portfolio Delivery
  - Deliverable: Digital portfolio of styled looks sent to clients.
