
# Project Plan Template: Teen Skincare Guidance Session

## 1. Executive Summary

The Teen Skincare Guidance Session aims to provide teenagers with personalized skincare advice to effectively manage the challenges associated with hormonal skin changes. This project is designed to educate adolescents about their unique skin needs, addressing common concerns such as acne, oiliness, and sensitivity. By offering tailored recommendations for suitable skincare products and developing a straightforward skincare routine, we empower teenagers to take control of their skincare journey. Key outcomes include increased awareness of proper skincare practices, enhanced confidence in managing skin issues, and the promotion of a positive self-image among participants. Ultimately, this session seeks to equip teenagers with the knowledge and tools necessary for maintaining healthy skin, fostering a sense of ownership over their skincare regimen.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Target Audience**: Teenagers aged {{age range}} who are experiencing hormonal skin changes.
- **Consultation Format**: One-on-one or group sessions lasting approximately {{duration}} minutes.
- **Content Development**:
  - Assessment of individual skin types and concerns.
  - Education on common adolescent skin issues (e.g., acne, oiliness, sensitivity).
  - Recommendations for suitable skincare products tailored to each participant's needs.
  - Creation of a simple, effective skincare routine that includes essential steps: cleansing, moisturizing, and sun protection.
- **Materials Provided**:
  - Skincare product samples for testing.
  - Informational handouts summarizing key points discussed during the session.
  - A follow-up guide for maintaining a skincare routine.
- **Follow-Up Support**: Optional follow-up consultations to assess progress and make adjustments to skincare routines as needed.
