
# Project Plan Template: Vintage Wardrobe Curation

## 1. Executive Summary

The Vintage Wardrobe Curation project aims to empower clients by guiding them through the exciting journey of building a vintage wardrobe that reflects their unique personal style and enhances their confidence. This project will begin with a comprehensive assessment of the client's current wardrobe, fashion preferences, lifestyle, and body type. Our experienced fashion stylist will then curate a selection of timeless vintage pieces that align with the client's aesthetic. Key outcomes of this project include not only a beautifully curated wardrobe but also personalized consultations that teach clients how to effectively mix and match vintage items with modern clothing. Clients will gain valuable insights into styling techniques, color coordination, and accessorizing, ensuring they can create distinctive outfits that stand out. By the end of this project, clients will possess a curated vintage wardrobe and the knowledge to express their style confidently through a blend of vintage and contemporary fashion.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a one-on-one meeting with the client to assess their current wardrobe, fashion preferences, lifestyle, and body type.
- **Wardrobe Assessment**: Analyze the client's existing clothing items to identify what can be retained, altered, or discarded.
- **Sourcing Vintage Pieces**: Research and source unique vintage items that align with the client's aesthetic and personal style.
- **Personalized Styling Sessions**: Schedule multiple sessions to provide guidance on how to mix and match vintage pieces with modern clothing.
- **Styling Techniques**: Offer expert advice on styling techniques, color coordination, and accessorizing to create cohesive looks.
- **Final Review**: Conduct a final review session to evaluate the curated wardrobe and provide additional tips for future styling.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Client Wardrobe Assessment Report
- **Milestone 2**: Vintage Pieces Sourced
  - Deliverable: Curated Vintage Selection List
- **Milestone 3**: First Styling Session Completed
  - Deliverable: Personalized Styling Guide
- **Milestone 4**: Final Review Session Completed
  - Deliverable: Final Wardrobe Curation Report and Styling Tips
