
# Project Plan Template: Corporate Style Makeover

## 1. Executive Summary

The Corporate Style Makeover project is designed to empower professionals to enhance their workplace appearance and make a lasting impression. The primary objective of this project is to curate a personalized wardrobe strategy that aligns with the client's career aspirations, personal style, and workplace culture. Through an initial consultation, our experienced fashion stylist will gather insights into the client's preferences and professional environment. The key outcomes of this project include a tailored selection of versatile clothing pieces that can be mixed and matched for various professional settings, personalized shopping assistance, and a comprehensive lookbook featuring outfit combinations and styling tips. Ultimately, this project aims to instill confidence in clients, enabling them to express their professional identity through fashion while ensuring they feel polished and ready to excel in their careers.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a one-on-one meeting with the client to discuss career aspirations, personal style, and workplace culture.
- **Wardrobe Strategy Development**: Create a tailored wardrobe strategy that includes:
  - Selection of versatile clothing pieces suitable for various professional settings.
  - Recommendations for colors, fabrics, and styles that complement the client's body type and personal aesthetic.
- **Personalized Shopping Assistance**: Accompany the client on shopping trips or provide curated online shopping lists to facilitate the selection of clothing.
- **Lookbook Creation**: Develop a comprehensive lookbook that includes:
  - Outfit combinations tailored to the client's wardrobe.
  - Styling tips and tricks to enhance the client's overall appearance.
- **Follow-Up Consultation**: Schedule a follow-up meeting to review the purchased items and provide additional styling advice as needed.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Client profile document summarizing aspirations, style preferences, and workplace culture.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Wardrobe Strategy Developed
  - Deliverable: Tailored wardrobe strategy document outlining selected clothing pieces and styling recommendations.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Personalized Shopping Assistance Provided
  - Deliverable: List of purchased items and receipts, along with notes on how to style each piece.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Lookbook Delivered
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive lookbook featuring outfit combinations and styling tips.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Follow-Up Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of follow-up meeting and any additional recommendations.
