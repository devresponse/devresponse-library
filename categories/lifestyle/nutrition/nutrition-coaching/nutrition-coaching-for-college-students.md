
# Project Plan Template: Nutrition Coaching for College Students

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Nutrition Coaching for College Students" project aims to provide tailored nutrition coaching services that address the unique challenges faced by college students in maintaining a healthy diet on a limited budget. The primary objective is to empower students with the knowledge and tools necessary to make informed dietary choices that enhance their energy levels and overall well-being. This project will begin with an initial consultation to assess each student's dietary preferences, restrictions, and lifestyle factors. Following this assessment, a comprehensive meal plan will be developed, featuring affordable ingredients and easy-to-prepare recipes. Key outcomes include improved nutritional knowledge, enhanced meal preparation skills, and strategies for making healthier choices in dining facilities. By the end of the project, students will possess a clear, actionable plan that aligns with their academic and personal goals, ultimately fostering a healthier campus community.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct one-on-one meetings with each participant to evaluate their dietary preferences, restrictions, and lifestyle factors.
- **Meal Plan Development**: Create personalized meal plans that incorporate:
  - Affordable ingredients
  - Easy-to-prepare recipes
  - Nutritional guidance tailored to enhance energy levels and overall well-being
- **Grocery Shopping Tips**: Provide students with strategies for budget-friendly grocery shopping, including how to read labels and identify healthy options.
- **Meal Prepping Guidance**: Offer practical advice on meal prepping techniques to save time and reduce food waste.
- **Dining Facility Navigation**: Equip students with tips on making healthier choices when eating at campus dining facilities.
- **Follow-Up Sessions**: Schedule follow-up sessions to assess progress, address challenges, and make necessary adjustments to meal plans.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of initial consultations for all participants
  - **Deliverable**: Summary report of individual assessments
- **Milestone 2**: Development of personalized meal plans
  - **Deliverable**: Customized meal plans for each participant
- **Milestone 3**: Distribution of grocery shopping and meal prepping resources
  - **Deliverable**: Resource guide for budget-friendly shopping and meal prep
- **Milestone 4**: Conduct follow-up sessions
  - **Deliverable**: Progress reports and updated meal plans as needed
