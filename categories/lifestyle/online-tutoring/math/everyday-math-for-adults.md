
# Project Plan Template: Everyday Math for Adults

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Everyday Math for Adults" project aims to empower adult learners with essential mathematical skills that are directly applicable to their daily lives. The primary objective of this course is to provide participants with a comprehensive understanding of practical math concepts, including budgeting, measurements, and basic arithmetic operations. Through interactive online sessions, learners will engage in real-world scenarios that enhance their ability to calculate expenses, understand interest rates, and convert units of measurement. Key outcomes of the project include increased confidence in tackling everyday math challenges, improved financial decision-making skills, and an overall enhancement in the quality of life for participants. By the end of the course, participants will have developed a solid foundation in essential math skills, enabling them to navigate daily tasks with greater ease and assurance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the "Everyday Math for Adults" project includes the following components:

- **Course Development**: Create a curriculum that covers essential math topics relevant to adults, including budgeting, measurements, and basic arithmetic.
- **Interactive Online Sessions**: Conduct a series of online classes that promote participation and hands-on practice.
- **Real-World Scenarios**: Design exercises and activities that simulate everyday math challenges, such as calculating expenses and understanding interest rates.
- **Assessment and Feedback**: Implement assessments to gauge participant understanding and provide constructive feedback to enhance learning outcomes.
- **Support Materials**: Develop supplementary materials, such as worksheets and guides, to reinforce learning and provide additional resources for participants.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Course Curriculum Development
  - Deliverable: Finalized curriculum document outlining course topics and structure.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Launch of Online Sessions
  - Deliverable: Schedule of online sessions and access links for participants.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Mid-Course Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment results and feedback report for participants.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Completion of All Online Sessions
  - Deliverable: Recordings of sessions and participant engagement metrics.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Course Evaluation
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive evaluation report summarizing participant feedback and learning outcomes.
