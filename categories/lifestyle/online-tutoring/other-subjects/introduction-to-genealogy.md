
# Project Plan Template: Introduction to Genealogy

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Introduction to Genealogy" project aims to empower individuals with the skills and knowledge necessary to explore their family history and construct a detailed family tree. This course will provide participants with a comprehensive overview of genealogy, focusing on the use of various online tools and resources, including genealogy websites, databases, and historical records. The primary objective is to equip learners with the ability to gather information, verify facts, and organize their findings into a coherent family tree. Key outcomes include the development of research skills, best practices for documenting findings, and strategies for preserving family history for future generations. By the end of the project, participants will be able to confidently trace their ancestry and share their discoveries with family and friends, fostering a deeper connection to their heritage.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Course Development**: Create a structured curriculum that covers the fundamentals of genealogy, including:
  - Introduction to genealogy concepts
  - Overview of online genealogy tools and resources
  - Techniques for gathering and verifying information
  - Best practices for documenting research
  - Methods for preserving family history
  
- **Instructional Materials**: Develop instructional materials such as:
  - Video tutorials
  - Written guides and handouts
  - Interactive exercises and quizzes

- **Expert Tutoring**: Engage experienced genealogists to provide guidance and support throughout the course, including:
  - Live Q&A sessions
  - One-on-one consultations

- **Participant Engagement**: Foster a collaborative learning environment through:
  - Group discussions
  - Peer feedback sessions

- **Final Project**: Participants will complete a final project that involves creating a detailed family tree and presenting their findings.
