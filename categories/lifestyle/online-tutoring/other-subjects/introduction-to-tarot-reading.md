
# Project Plan: Introduction to Tarot Reading

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Introduction to Tarot Reading" project aims to provide participants with a comprehensive understanding of tarot as a tool for self-discovery and personal growth. This course is designed for individuals who are either complete beginners or those seeking to deepen their existing knowledge of tarot. The project will cover the fundamental meanings of each tarot card, various interpretation techniques, and the significance of different tarot spreads. Key outcomes include enhanced intuitive skills, the ability to conduct personal readings, and a deeper understanding of how tarot can serve as a guide in decision-making. By the end of the course, participants will not only have a solid foundation in tarot reading but also the confidence to apply these skills in their daily lives. This project promises to illuminate the path for individuals seeking clarity and insight through the mystical art of tarot.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the "Introduction to Tarot Reading" project includes the following key components:

- **Course Development**: Create a structured curriculum that covers the basics of tarot reading, including card meanings, spreads, and interpretation techniques.
- **Materials Preparation**: Develop instructional materials such as handouts, visual aids, and practice exercises to support learning.
- **Expert Tutor Engagement**: Hire an experienced tarot reader to facilitate the course, ensuring participants receive expert guidance and support.
- **Practical Exercises**: Design interactive sessions where participants can practice reading tarot cards and interpreting spreads in real-time.
- **Feedback Mechanism**: Implement a system for participants to provide feedback on the course content and delivery, allowing for continuous improvement.
- **Final Assessment**: Create a final assessment to evaluate participants' understanding and skills acquired throughout the course.
