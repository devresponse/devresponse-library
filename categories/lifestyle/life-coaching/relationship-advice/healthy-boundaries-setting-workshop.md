
# Healthy Boundaries Setting Workshop Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Healthy Boundaries Setting Workshop aims to empower individuals with the essential knowledge and skills required to establish and maintain healthy boundaries in both personal and professional relationships. This interactive workshop will provide participants with a comprehensive understanding of the significance of boundaries, enabling them to identify their personal limits and communicate their needs effectively. Key outcomes include enhanced self-awareness, improved communication skills, and the development of actionable strategies for boundary-setting. Participants will engage in practical exercises, including self-reflection and role-playing scenarios, to practice these skills in a supportive environment. By the end of the workshop, attendees will leave with a personalized boundary-setting plan that can be implemented in their daily lives, ultimately fostering healthier and more fulfilling relationships.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Healthy Boundaries Setting Workshop includes the following components:

- **Workshop Design and Development**: Create a curriculum that covers the importance of boundaries, personal limits, and effective communication techniques.
- **Interactive Activities**: Develop practical exercises that encourage self-reflection and role-playing scenarios for participants to practice boundary-setting.
- **Materials Preparation**: Prepare handouts, worksheets, and resources that participants can use during and after the workshop.
- **Facilitation**: Conduct the workshop in a conducive environment that encourages open discussion and interaction among participants.
- **Feedback and Evaluation**: Collect feedback from participants to assess the effectiveness of the workshop and identify areas for improvement.
