
# Indie Game Spotlight Playthroughs Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Indie Game Spotlight Playthroughs project aims to create engaging and informative recorded playthroughs of selected indie games. The primary objective is to highlight the unique gameplay mechanics and storytelling elements that distinguish these games from mainstream titles. Each playthrough will not only showcase the gameplay but will also delve into the creative processes behind the development of these games, providing viewers with a deeper understanding of the artistry involved. Through thorough research on each game, we will capture high-quality video footage and provide insightful commentary that reflects the design choices made by the developers. The final product will serve as a valuable resource for gamers eager to explore indie titles and for developers seeking inspiration from their peers. By showcasing the innovation and creativity within the indie gaming community, this project will foster a greater appreciation for these unique games and encourage players to support indie developers. Key outcomes include increased visibility for indie games, enhanced community engagement, and a comprehensive catalog of playthroughs that can be utilized for educational and promotional purposes.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Game Selection**: Identify and select a diverse range of indie games that exemplify unique gameplay and storytelling.
- **Research**: Conduct in-depth research on each selected game, including gameplay mechanics, development history, and developer interviews.
- **Video Capture**: Record high-quality video footage of gameplay, ensuring to capture key moments and features.
- **Commentary**: Provide engaging and informative commentary during the playthroughs, discussing design choices, gameplay strategies, and narrative elements.
- **Editing**: Edit the recorded footage to create polished final videos, incorporating graphics, subtitles, and additional insights where necessary.
- **Distribution**: Develop a strategy for distributing the playthrough videos across various platforms, including YouTube, Twitch, and social media.
- **Feedback and Iteration**: Gather feedback from viewers and developers to refine future playthroughs and improve content quality.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Game Selection Completed
  - Deliverable: List of selected indie games with rationale for selection.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Research Phase Completed
  - Deliverable: Research documentation for each game, including gameplay mechanics and developer insights.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Video Capture Completed
  - Deliverable: Raw video footage for each playthrough.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Commentary Recorded
  - Deliverable: Audio commentary files for each playthrough.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Video Editing Completed
  - Deliverable: Polished playthrough videos ready for distribution.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Distribution Strategy Implemented
  - Deliverable: Playthrough videos published on selected platforms with promotional materials.
