
# Project Plan Template: Beginner's Guide to Gaming Genres

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Beginner's Guide to Gaming Genres" project aims to create a comprehensive and accessible resource for new gamers who wish to explore the diverse world of video games. The primary objective is to demystify various gaming genres, including action, adventure, role-playing, simulation, strategy, and more, by providing clear explanations of their core mechanics and gameplay styles. This guide will not only introduce the fundamental aspects of each genre but also recommend beginner-friendly games that align with different interests and skill levels. Key outcomes include an engaging and informative guide that empowers new gamers to make informed choices, enhances their gaming experience, and fosters a deeper appreciation for the medium. By the end of this project, we aim to equip new players with the knowledge and confidence to embark on their gaming journey, ensuring they have the tools to select games that resonate with their preferences.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct thorough research on various gaming genres.
  - Identify key mechanics, gameplay styles, and player experiences associated with each genre.
  - Gather insights from gaming communities and expert reviews.

- **Content Development**:
  - Write engaging and informative content for each genre.
  - Include sections on core mechanics, gameplay styles, and player experiences.
  - Curate a list of beginner-friendly games for each genre, providing brief descriptions and reasons for recommendations.

- **Design and Layout**:
  - Create a visually appealing layout for the guide.
  - Incorporate graphics, images, and infographics to enhance understanding.
  - Ensure the guide is easy to navigate and user-friendly.

- **Review and Editing**:
  - Conduct multiple rounds of editing to ensure clarity and accuracy.
  - Gather feedback from beta readers, particularly from new gamers, to refine the content.

- **Publication and Distribution**:
  - Format the guide for various platforms (e.g., PDF, eBook).
  - Develop a marketing strategy to promote the guide to target audiences.
  - Distribute the guide through appropriate channels (e.g., gaming forums, social media, gaming websites).

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings on gaming genres.

- **Milestone 2**: Draft Content Development
  - Deliverable: First draft of the guide covering all gaming genres.

- **Milestone 3**: Design and Layout Finalization
  - Deliverable: Finalized design mockup of the guide.

- **Milestone 4**: Review and Editing Completion
  - Deliverable: Edited and polished version of the guide ready for publication.

- **Milestone 5**: Publication and Distribution
  - Deliverable: Published guide available in selected formats and distributed to target audiences.
