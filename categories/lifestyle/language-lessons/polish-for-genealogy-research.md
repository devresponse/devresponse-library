
# Project Plan Template: Polish for Genealogy Research

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Polish for Genealogy Research" project aims to empower individuals exploring their Polish heritage by providing them with essential language skills tailored specifically for genealogical research. This specialized course will equip participants with the vocabulary and phrases necessary to comprehend historical documents, including birth, marriage, and death certificates, as well as census records. The project will focus on teaching the nuances of Polish grammar and pronunciation, enabling effective communication with local archives and genealogical societies. Key outcomes include enhanced confidence in navigating Polish records, improved understanding of genealogical terminology, and the ability to conduct thorough research into one’s ancestry. Ultimately, this course will foster a deeper connection to participants' Polish roots, enriching their genealogical journey.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the "Polish for Genealogy Research" project includes the following components:

- **Curriculum Development**: Create a comprehensive curriculum that covers essential vocabulary, phrases, and grammar rules relevant to genealogical research in Polish.
- **Lesson Structure**: Design interactive lessons that incorporate practical exercises, including reading and interpreting historical documents.
- **Resource Materials**: Develop supplementary materials such as glossaries, pronunciation guides, and sample documents for practice.
- **Delivery Method**: Determine the format of course delivery, whether in-person, online, or hybrid, ensuring accessibility for all participants.
- **Assessment**: Implement assessment methods to evaluate participant progress and understanding throughout the course.
- **Feedback Mechanism**: Establish a system for collecting participant feedback to continuously improve course content and delivery.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Curriculum Development
  - Deliverable: Finalized curriculum document outlining lesson plans and learning objectives.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Creation of Resource Materials
  - Deliverable: Glossaries, pronunciation guides, and sample documents ready for distribution.

- **Milestone 3**: Course Delivery Setup
  - Deliverable: Established platform for course delivery (e.g., online learning management system or physical classroom setup).

- **Milestone 4**: Course Launch
  - Deliverable: Successful initiation of the first course session with enrolled participants.

- **Milestone 5**: Participant Assessment and Feedback Collection
  - Deliverable: Completed assessments and feedback forms from participants to evaluate course effectiveness.
