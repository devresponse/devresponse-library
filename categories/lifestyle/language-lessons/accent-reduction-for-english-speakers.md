
# Project Plan: Accent Reduction for English Speakers

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Accent Reduction for English Speakers" project aims to provide a comprehensive and interactive course specifically designed for non-native English speakers seeking to improve their pronunciation and spoken clarity. The primary objective is to identify common pronunciation challenges and equip participants with the necessary tools to address these issues effectively. Through a series of targeted lessons focusing on phonetic sounds, intonation, and rhythm, learners will engage in practical exercises that enhance their communication skills. Key outcomes include noticeable improvements in accent, increased confidence in speaking English, and enhanced career prospects for professionals. By incorporating personalized feedback sessions, participants will receive real-time guidance, ensuring that they can practice and refine their skills effectively. This project ultimately seeks to foster more effective communication in both personal and professional settings, empowering individuals to express themselves clearly and confidently.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the "Accent Reduction for English Speakers" project includes the following components:

- **Course Development**: Design and develop a structured curriculum that includes interactive lessons focusing on phonetic sounds, intonation, and rhythm.
- **Lesson Delivery**: Conduct a series of live online sessions, utilizing video conferencing tools to facilitate real-time interaction between instructors and participants.
- **Targeted Exercises**: Create and implement exercises that address specific pronunciation challenges faced by participants, allowing for hands-on practice.
- **Personalized Feedback**: Schedule one-on-one feedback sessions for each participant to assess their progress and provide tailored guidance.
- **Assessment and Evaluation**: Develop assessment tools to measure participants' improvement throughout the course and gather feedback for future iterations.
- **Resource Materials**: Provide supplementary materials, including recordings, worksheets, and reference guides, to support participants' learning outside of live sessions.
