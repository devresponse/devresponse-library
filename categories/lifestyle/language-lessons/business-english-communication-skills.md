
# Project Plan Template: Business English Communication Skills

## 1. Executive Summary

The 'Business English Communication Skills' project aims to empower non-native English speakers with the essential communication skills required in a professional environment. The primary objective is to enhance participants' proficiency in business communication through a comprehensive curriculum that includes effective email writing, impactful presentation delivery, and negotiation techniques. This course will provide an interactive learning experience, incorporating real-world scenarios that allow participants to practice and refine their skills in a supportive environment. Key outcomes include increased confidence in professional communication, personalized feedback from experienced instructors, and the identification of individual strengths and areas for improvement. By the end of the course, participants will be better equipped to navigate business interactions, leading to personal growth and career advancement.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the 'Business English Communication Skills' project includes the following key components:

- **Curriculum Development**: Design and develop a comprehensive curriculum that covers essential business communication skills, including:
  - Effective email writing
  - Delivering impactful presentations
  - Mastering negotiation techniques

- **Interactive Lessons**: Create engaging and interactive lessons that incorporate real-world scenarios, allowing participants to practice their skills in a realistic context.

- **Instructor Support**: Provide personalized feedback and support from experienced instructors throughout the course to help participants identify their strengths and areas for improvement.

- **Assessment and Evaluation**: Implement assessments to evaluate participants' progress and understanding of the material, ensuring that learning objectives are met.

- **Course Delivery**: Facilitate the course through a combination of live sessions, recorded materials, and supplementary resources to enhance the learning experience.
