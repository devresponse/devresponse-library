
# Project Plan Template: Steampunk Airship World

## 1. Executive Summary

The Steampunk Airship World project aims to create a captivating and immersive environment that embodies the essence of steampunk aesthetics. This project will develop a network of floating airships that traverse the skies, connecting various mechanical cities filled with intricate designs and advanced technology inspired by the Victorian era. The primary objective is to design a richly detailed world that enhances the narrative and visual appeal for game developers. Key outcomes will include a comprehensive design document that outlines the visual and thematic elements of the world, along with concept art and environmental sketches. The project will also explore diverse sky-based ecosystems, including floating islands and atmospheric flora and fauna, enriching the gameplay experience. By the end of this project, we aim to deliver a unique and engaging setting that captivates players and inspires creativity in game development.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Inspiration Gathering**
  - Conduct research on steampunk aesthetics, Victorian architecture, and mechanical innovations.
  - Compile a list of references, including books, films, and artworks that embody the steampunk genre.

- **World Design**
  - Develop a conceptual framework for the airship network and mechanical cities.
  - Create detailed descriptions of each city, including architectural styles, cultural elements, and marketplace dynamics.

- **Ecosystem Development**
  - Design floating islands and atmospheric ecosystems, including flora and fauna that thrive in the steampunk world.
  - Create ecological interactions and relationships between different species and environments.

- **Visual Development**
  - Produce concept art and environmental sketches that illustrate the world, cities, and ecosystems.
  - Develop a color palette and design guidelines that reflect the steampunk aesthetic.

- **Documentation**
  - Compile all research, designs, and visual elements into a comprehensive design document.
  - Ensure the document is accessible and usable for game developers, including guidelines for implementation.
