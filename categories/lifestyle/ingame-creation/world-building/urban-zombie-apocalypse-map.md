
# Project Plan Template: Urban Zombie Apocalypse Map

## 1. Executive Summary

The Urban Zombie Apocalypse Map project aims to create a detailed and immersive urban environment that encapsulates the chaos and survival elements inherent in a zombie apocalypse scenario. The primary objective is to design a sprawling cityscape that features abandoned buildings, overgrown streets, and lurking dangers, all of which contribute to a thrilling gameplay experience. Key outcomes include the development of strategically placed safe zones that provide players with refuge and resources, as well as the integration of barricades to enhance gameplay dynamics. Furthermore, the map will incorporate interactive survival elements such as scavenging points, traps, and hidden resources, enriching the overall gaming experience. The final deliverable will be a fully functional map that not only serves as a backdrop for gameplay but also enhances the narrative and engagement of the game. This project is particularly suited for game developers seeking to elevate their zombie-themed games with a unique and captivating environment that encourages exploration and strategic planning.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Concept Development**
  - Conduct research on zombie apocalypse themes and urban environments.
  - Develop initial concepts and sketches for the map layout.

- **Map Design**
  - Create a detailed cityscape layout, including key areas such as:
    - Abandoned buildings
    - Overgrown streets
    - Safe zones
    - Barricades
  - Design interactive elements:
    - Scavenging points
    - Traps
    - Hidden resources

- **3D Modeling and Texturing**
  - Develop 3D models for buildings, streets, and interactive elements.
  - Apply textures to enhance realism and immersion.

- **Gameplay Integration**
  - Collaborate with game developers to ensure the map integrates seamlessly with gameplay mechanics.
  - Test the map for functionality and balance.

- **Final Review and Adjustments**
  - Conduct a final review with stakeholders.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on feedback.
