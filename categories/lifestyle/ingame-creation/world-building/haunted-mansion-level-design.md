
# Project Plan Template: Haunted Mansion Level Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Haunted Mansion Level Design project aims to create an immersive and chilling experience for players in a video game setting. The primary objective is to design a haunted mansion that captivates players through its unique atmosphere, interactive elements, and engaging narrative. This project will involve the development of various rooms within the mansion, each designed to evoke a sense of fear and curiosity, featuring eerie sounds, ghostly apparitions, and hidden secrets. Key outcomes include a fully designed level that integrates seamlessly with the game's mechanics and aesthetic, ensuring players are drawn into the haunted history of the mansion. By incorporating detailed landscapes, such as overgrown gardens and dark basements, the project will enhance the overall gameplay experience, making it both thrilling and memorable. Ultimately, the goal is to deliver a level that not only meets but exceeds player expectations, providing a captivating journey through the haunted mansion.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Concept Development**: 
  - Brainstorm and outline the overall theme and narrative of the haunted mansion.
  - Create mood boards and sketches for visual inspiration.

- **Room Design**:
  - Design individual rooms with unique atmospheres (e.g., library, ballroom, attic).
  - Incorporate interactive elements (e.g., hidden doors, puzzles).

- **Environmental Design**:
  - Develop detailed landscapes, including gardens, basements, and exterior views.
  - Use lighting and sound design to enhance the eerie atmosphere.

- **Narrative Integration**:
  - Create a storyline that unfolds as players explore the mansion.
  - Develop character backstories and ghostly apparitions that players may encounter.

- **Gameplay Mechanics**:
  - Ensure that the level design aligns with the game's mechanics and controls.
  - Test interactive elements for functionality and player engagement.

- **Testing and Feedback**:
  - Conduct playtesting sessions to gather feedback on the level design.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on player experiences and suggestions.

- **Final Deliverables**:
  - A fully designed haunted mansion level ready for integration into the game.
  - Documentation detailing design choices, narrative elements, and gameplay mechanics.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Concept Development Completion
  - Deliverable: Concept sketches and mood boards.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Room Design Completion
  - Deliverable: Detailed designs for each room.

- **Milestone 3**: Environmental Design Completion
  - Deliverable: Finalized landscapes and atmospheric elements.

- **Milestone 4**: Narrative Integration Completion
  - Deliverable: Documented storyline and character backstories.

- **Milestone 5**: Gameplay Mechanics Testing
  - Deliverable: Feedback report from playtesting sessions.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Level Delivery
  - Deliverable: Fully designed level and accompanying documentation.
