
# Project Plan Template: Urban Rooftop Park

## 1. Executive Summary

The Urban Rooftop Park project aims to create an innovative and visually appealing rooftop park that enhances the urban landscape within a city-building or simulation game. The primary objective is to design a vibrant green space that contrasts with the surrounding concrete structures, providing players with a refreshing environment to explore and interact with. Key outcomes include the integration of lush greenery, such as trees, shrubs, and flower beds, alongside recreational areas designed for various activities, including picnic spots, walking paths, and seating areas. The project will also incorporate interactive elements like playgrounds, art installations, and community gardens, fostering player engagement and enhancing gameplay. By prioritizing accessibility and aesthetic appeal, the rooftop park will serve as a focal point in the game, enriching the player experience and contributing to the overarching narrative of urban development and sustainability. Ultimately, this project seeks to inspire players to appreciate the importance of green spaces in urban settings and promote a sense of community within the game.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Design Phase**
  - Research and gather inspiration for rooftop park designs.
  - Create initial design concepts and sketches.
  - Develop a detailed design plan, including layout, plant selection, and materials.

- **Development Phase**
  - Build the 3D model of the rooftop park within the game engine.
  - Implement landscaping features, including trees, shrubs, and flower beds.
  - Design and integrate recreational areas, such as picnic spots and walking paths.
  - Create interactive elements, including playgrounds, art installations, and community gardens.

- **Testing Phase**
  - Conduct playtesting sessions to gather feedback on park usability and aesthetics.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on player feedback and testing results.

- **Finalization Phase**
  - Polish the final design and ensure all elements are functioning correctly.
  - Prepare marketing materials and in-game promotional content for the rooftop park.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1:** Completion of Design Phase
  - Deliverable: Detailed design plan and initial concept sketches.

- **Milestone 2:** Completion of Development Phase
  - Deliverable: Fully integrated 3D model of the rooftop park in the game.

- **Milestone 3:** Completion of Testing Phase
  - Deliverable: Playtesting report with feedback and adjustments made.

- **Milestone 4:** Finalization and Launch
  - Deliverable: Polished rooftop park ready for in-game launch and marketing materials.
