
# DIY Candle Making Kit Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The DIY Candle Making Kit project aims to provide individuals and families with an engaging and creative outlet through the art of candle making. The primary objective is to empower participants to explore their artistic flair by crafting personalized scented candles in the comfort of their homes. This project overview encompasses the provision of a comprehensive kit that includes high-quality wax, a variety of delightful fragrances, vibrant color dyes, and molds in different shapes. Key outcomes of this initiative include enhancing participants' creativity, fostering a sense of accomplishment through hands-on crafting, and producing unique, beautifully crafted candles that can serve as decorative pieces or thoughtful gifts. By the end of the project, participants will have gained valuable skills in candle making, from melting wax to customizing scents, ultimately filling their spaces with delightful aromas and a sense of personal achievement.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the DIY Candle Making Kit project includes the following components:

- **Kit Development**: Design and assemble a comprehensive candle making kit that includes:
  - High-quality wax (soy, paraffin, or beeswax)
  - A selection of fragrances (lavender, citrus, vanilla, etc.)
  - Color dyes in various shades
  - Molds in different shapes (jar, pillar, tealight, etc.)
  - Essential tools (thermometer, pouring pot, stirring sticks, etc.)
  
- **Instructional Materials**: Create detailed instructional guides and video tutorials that cover:
  - Safety precautions
  - Step-by-step candle making process
  - Tips for customizing scents and colors

- **Marketing and Promotion**: Develop a marketing strategy to promote the DIY Candle Making Kit through:
  - Social media campaigns
  - Collaborations with influencers in the arts and crafts niche
  - Online workshops or webinars to demonstrate the candle making process

- **Distribution**: Establish a distribution plan to ensure timely delivery of kits to customers, including:
  - Packaging design that is eco-friendly and visually appealing
  - Partnerships with reliable shipping services
