
# Project Plan: Handwoven Coasters

## 1. Executive Summary

The Handwoven Coasters project aims to immerse participants in the art of weaving, providing a comprehensive learning experience for both beginners and experienced crafters. The primary objective is to equip participants with essential weaving techniques, enabling them to create beautiful and functional coasters that enhance home decor or serve as thoughtful gifts. This project will cover the selection of appropriate materials, an introduction to basic weaving patterns, and the mastery of color coordination. Key outcomes include the completion of a unique set of handwoven coasters, fostering creativity and personal expression. By the end of the project, participants will not only have developed practical skills but also gained confidence in their crafting abilities, making this an enriching experience for all involved.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The Handwoven Coasters project will include the following components:

- **Material Selection**: Participants will learn how to choose the right yarns and weaving tools, including looms and shuttle options.
- **Weaving Techniques**: Instruction on basic weaving techniques such as plain weave, twill, and more advanced patterns for those seeking a challenge.
- **Color Coordination**: Guidance on selecting color palettes that complement each other and enhance the overall design of the coasters.
- **Project Execution**: Step-by-step instructions on how to set up the loom, weave the coasters, and finish them properly.
- **Final Presentation**: Participants will showcase their completed coasters, sharing their creative process and the inspiration behind their designs.
