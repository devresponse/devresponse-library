
# Project Plan: Upcycled Glass Jar Lanterns

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Upcycled Glass Jar Lanterns" project aims to transform ordinary glass jars into beautiful, functional lanterns that enhance any space. The primary objective is to teach participants, regardless of their crafting experience, how to creatively repurpose materials while developing their artistic skills. This project will provide a hands-on experience in using simple techniques such as painting, wrapping, and lighting to create unique lanterns. Key outcomes include the completion of personalized lanterns that can serve as home decor, gifts, or decorative elements for events. Participants will also gain valuable insights into upcycling and sustainable crafting practices, fostering a sense of creativity and environmental responsibility. By the end of the project, each participant will leave with not only their crafted lanterns but also the knowledge and inspiration to explore further upcycled crafts.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the "Upcycled Glass Jar Lanterns" project includes the following key activities:

- **Material Gathering**: Participants will collect necessary materials, including glass jars, acrylic paint, twine, and LED lights.
- **Design Planning**: Each participant will sketch their design ideas for their lanterns, considering color schemes and patterns.
- **Painting**: Participants will paint their glass jars using acrylic paint, applying techniques to achieve desired effects.
- **Twine Wrapping**: After the paint dries, participants will wrap twine around the jars to add a rustic touch.
- **Lighting Installation**: LED lights will be inserted into the jars to create a warm glow, completing the lanterns.
- **Showcase and Feedback**: A final showcase will allow participants to display their lanterns and share their experiences and techniques learned during the project.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Material Gathering
  - Deliverable: List of materials collected by each participant.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Design Planning
  - Deliverable: Completed design sketches for each lantern.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Painting Phase
  - Deliverable: Painted glass jars ready for twine wrapping.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Twine Wrapping
  - Deliverable: Twine-wrapped jars prepared for lighting installation.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Lighting Installation
  - Deliverable: Completed lanterns with LED lights installed.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Showcase Event
  - Deliverable: Display of all completed lanterns and participant feedback.
