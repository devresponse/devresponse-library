
# Project Plan: Watercolor Greeting Cards

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Watercolor Greeting Cards" project aims to create a series of personalized, handcrafted greeting cards that cater to various occasions, including birthdays, anniversaries, holidays, and everyday greetings. The primary objective is to deliver unique, artistic cards that reflect the client's preferences and sentiments, enhancing the personal touch of gifting. The project will commence with a detailed consultation to understand the client's desired themes, color schemes, and styles. Following this, initial sketches will be developed and presented for client feedback, ensuring alignment with their vision. Upon approval, high-quality watercolor techniques will be employed to bring the designs to life, resulting in vibrant and professional-grade cards. Key outcomes include a set of beautifully crafted greeting cards, each paired with an envelope, ready for gifting. This project not only showcases artistic creativity but also fulfills the client's need for heartfelt, handmade cards that convey meaningful messages.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss preferences, themes, and color schemes.
  - Document client requirements and expectations.

- **Design Phase**:
  - Create initial sketches based on the consultation feedback.
  - Present sketches to the client for review and feedback.
  - Revise designs as necessary until final approval is obtained.

- **Production Phase**:
  - Select high-quality watercolor materials and tools.
  - Paint the approved designs using professional watercolor techniques.
  - Ensure each card is crafted with attention to detail and vibrant colors.

- **Finalization Phase**:
  - Prepare the cards for delivery, including pairing each card with an appropriate envelope.
  - Conduct a quality check to ensure all cards meet professional standards.

- **Delivery**:
  - Package the cards securely for shipping or hand delivery.
  - Provide the client with care instructions for the cards.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the consultation phase.
  - **Deliverable**: Documented client preferences and requirements.

- **Milestone 2**: Submission of initial sketches for client feedback.
  - **Deliverable**: Set of sketches representing proposed designs.

- **Milestone 3**: Final approval of card designs.
  - **Deliverable**: Approved design files ready for production.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of the painting phase.
  - **Deliverable**: Finished watercolor greeting cards.

- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of final products to the client.
  - **Deliverable**: Set of personalized greeting cards with envelopes.
