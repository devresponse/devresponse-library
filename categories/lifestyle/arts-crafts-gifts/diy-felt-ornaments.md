
# DIY Felt Ornaments Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The DIY Felt Ornaments project aims to immerse participants in the enchanting world of crafting, where creativity and craftsmanship converge. This project invites individuals to create charming felt ornaments that not only enhance seasonal decorations but also serve as heartfelt keepsakes for loved ones. The primary objective is to equip participants with the skills to select appropriate materials, including vibrant felt, threads, and embellishments, to bring their unique designs to life. Through a comprehensive step-by-step process, participants will learn to cut, stitch, and assemble their ornaments, ensuring accessibility for beginners. Key outcomes include the completion of a collection of beautiful, handmade ornaments that reflect personal style and creativity. By the end of the project, participants will have gained valuable crafting skills and produced treasured items perfect for holiday celebrations, gifts, or simply adding a touch of handmade charm to their homes.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Define project goals and objectives.
  - Identify target audience and participant demographics.
  
- **Material Selection**
  - Research and select high-quality felt, threads, and embellishments.
  - Create a materials list and sourcing plan.

- **Design Development**
  - Develop a range of ornament designs suitable for various skill levels.
  - Create templates for cutting and stitching.

- **Instructional Content Creation**
  - Write detailed step-by-step instructions for each ornament design.
  - Produce visual aids, including diagrams and photographs.

- **Workshop Planning**
  - Schedule workshops or online sessions for participants.
  - Prepare necessary tools and equipment for hands-on crafting.

- **Execution**
  - Conduct workshops, guiding participants through the crafting process.
  - Provide support and feedback during the creation of ornaments.

- **Project Closure**
  - Collect feedback from participants on their experience.
  - Document lessons learned and best practices for future projects.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Project charter and initial planning documents.
  
- **Milestone 2: Material Selection Completed**
  - Deliverable: Finalized materials list and sourcing plan.

- **Milestone 3: Design Templates Developed**
  - Deliverable: Completed ornament design templates and instructions.

- **Milestone 4: Workshop Sessions Conducted**
  - Deliverable: Successful completion of all scheduled workshops.

- **Milestone 5: Participant Feedback Collected**
  - Deliverable: Feedback report summarizing participant experiences.
