
# Project Plan: DIY Polymer Clay Keychains

## 1. Executive Summary

The DIY Polymer Clay Keychains project aims to provide participants with an engaging and creative experience in crafting unique keychains using polymer clay. The primary objective is to equip individuals with essential techniques such as conditioning, shaping, and baking polymer clay, enabling them to create durable and vibrant keychains that reflect their personal style. This project is designed to cater to all skill levels, making it an excellent opportunity for beginners to explore their artistic side while also offering seasoned crafters a new challenge. Key outcomes include the completion of personalized keychains, enhanced crafting skills, and increased confidence in artistic abilities. Participants will leave with not only their handmade creations but also a sense of accomplishment and inspiration to continue their crafting journey. Join us in this delightful exploration of creativity and self-expression!

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Overview**: The DIY Polymer Clay Keychains project will involve a series of workshops where participants will learn to create keychains from polymer clay. Each session will cover different techniques and design ideas.

- **Materials Required**: 
  - Polymer clay in various colors
  - Keychain hardware (rings, chains, etc.)
  - Tools (sculpting tools, rolling pins, baking sheets)
  - Paints and sealants (optional for decoration)

- **Workshop Structure**:
  - Introduction to polymer clay and safety guidelines
  - Hands-on demonstrations of conditioning and shaping clay
  - Guided sessions for creating various keychain designs
  - Baking and finishing techniques to ensure durability
  - Final showcase where participants present their creations

- **Target Audience**: Individuals of all ages and skill levels interested in arts and crafts.
