
# Project Plan: Minimalist Greeting Cards for Any Occasion

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Minimalist Greeting Cards for Any Occasion" project aims to create a series of digital greeting cards that embody a minimalist aesthetic, allowing users to convey heartfelt messages in a simple yet elegant manner. The project will focus on designing cards suitable for various occasions, including birthdays, anniversaries, holidays, and everyday greetings. By utilizing a clean layout, limited color palettes, and thoughtful typography, each card will resonate with the intended sentiment while maintaining a modern look. Key outcomes include a set of customizable templates that clients can easily adapt for personal use, along with high-resolution digital files optimized for online sharing and printing. This project not only provides a unique and stylish way for individuals to express their feelings but also enhances the overall experience of celebrating special moments in life.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Inspiration**: Conduct research on current design trends in minimalist greeting cards and gather inspiration from various sources.
- **Design Development**: Create initial sketches and digital mockups for a series of greeting cards tailored to different occasions.
- **Client Review**: Present the initial designs to the client for feedback and revisions.
- **Final Design Creation**: Develop the final versions of the greeting cards, ensuring they meet the aesthetic and functional requirements.
- **Template Creation**: Design customizable templates that clients can easily modify for personal use.
- **File Preparation**: Prepare high-resolution digital files in various formats (e.g., PDF, PNG) optimized for both online sharing and printing.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final deliverables to the client, including instructions for customization and usage.
