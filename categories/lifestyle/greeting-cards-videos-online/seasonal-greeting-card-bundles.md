
# Project Plan Template: Seasonal Greeting Card Bundles

## 1. Executive Summary

The Seasonal Greeting Card Bundles project aims to create four distinct themed bundles of digital greeting cards, each representing one of the four seasons: spring, summer, fall, and winter. The primary objective is to design a minimum of five unique greeting card designs for each season that encapsulate its essence and spirit. These designs will be vibrant, engaging, and suitable for various occasions, including birthdays, holidays, and special celebrations. The key outcomes of this project include delivering high-resolution digital formats of the greeting cards, ensuring they are ready for online sharing or printing. By collaborating closely with the client, we will ensure that the designs align with their vision and branding, resulting in a personalized and creative collection. This comprehensive seasonal offering will enable clients to connect with their loved ones throughout the year, enhancing their ability to celebrate special moments with thoughtful and beautifully crafted cards.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Concept Development**: 
  - Conduct research on seasonal themes and trends.
  - Develop initial concepts for each seasonal bundle.

- **Design Creation**: 
  - Create five unique greeting card designs for each season (spring, summer, fall, winter).
  - Ensure designs are vibrant and suitable for various occasions.

- **Client Collaboration**: 
  - Schedule regular meetings with the client to discuss concepts and gather feedback.
  - Revise designs based on client input to ensure alignment with their vision.

- **Finalization and Delivery**: 
  - Prepare high-resolution digital files of the final designs.
  - Ensure all files are formatted for online sharing and printing.

- **Marketing Support (Optional)**: 
  - Provide suggestions for marketing the greeting card bundles if requested by the client.
