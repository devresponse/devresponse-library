
# Project Plan Template: Custom Animated Birthday Greetings

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Custom Animated Birthday Greetings" project aims to create unique and personalized animated birthday greeting videos that elevate the celebration of birthdays into a memorable experience. The primary objective is to design high-quality videos that incorporate custom messages, animated characters, and themes tailored to the recipient's personality and interests. By engaging clients in the creative process, we will gather essential details such as favorite colors, hobbies, and cherished memories to ensure the final product resonates deeply with the recipient. Key outcomes include delivering a heartfelt video that can be easily shared via social media or directly with the birthday person, fostering a sense of connection and celebration. This project not only seeks to provide a visual gift but also aims to evoke emotions and create lasting memories, making each birthday truly special.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to gather information about the recipient, including preferences, interests, and any specific messages or themes they wish to include.
  
- **Storyboarding**: Develop a storyboard that outlines the flow of the video, including key scenes, animations, and transitions based on the information collected during the consultation.

- **Animation Production**: Create the animated video using professional animation software, incorporating custom characters, backgrounds, and effects that align with the recipient's personality.

- **Client Review**: Present the draft version of the video to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.

- **Final Delivery**: Produce the final version of the video in a high-quality format suitable for sharing on social media platforms and direct messaging.

- **Post-Delivery Support**: Provide clients with guidance on how to share the video and offer support for any technical issues that may arise.
