
# Project Plan: Reiki for Stress Relief Workshop

## 1. Executive Summary

The **Reiki for Stress Relief Workshop** aims to provide participants with an immersive experience in the art of Reiki, a holistic healing practice that channels energy to promote relaxation and alleviate stress. This workshop is designed to educate attendees on the fundamental principles of Reiki, including its history, techniques, and benefits. Participants will engage in guided sessions where they will learn to harness their own energy and apply Reiki techniques to themselves and others. Key outcomes of the workshop include enhanced understanding of energy balance, practical skills for stress management, and the ability to integrate Reiki practices into daily life. By the end of the workshop, attendees will leave equipped with valuable tools and techniques to achieve a state of balance and tranquility, ultimately fostering a healthier lifestyle.
