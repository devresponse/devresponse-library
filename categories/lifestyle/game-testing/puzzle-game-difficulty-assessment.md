
# Project Plan Template: Puzzle Game Difficulty Assessment

## 1. Executive Summary

The Puzzle Game Difficulty Assessment project aims to evaluate the difficulty curve of selected puzzle games, focusing on the engagement level and appropriateness of challenges for players. The primary objective is to analyze how well the difficulty scales as players progress through various levels of the game. This will involve a thorough examination of game mechanics, player feedback, and the overall gameplay experience. Key outcomes will include a comprehensive report detailing strengths and areas for improvement, which will provide valuable insights for game developers. By assessing elements such as the clarity of instructions, intuitiveness of controls, and overall enjoyment, the project seeks to ensure that challenges are engaging yet accessible to a wide range of players, from novices to experts. Ultimately, this assessment will empower developers to refine their products, enhance player satisfaction, and create a more enjoyable gaming experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Game Selection**: Identify and select a range of puzzle games for assessment, ensuring a mix of difficulty levels and styles.
- **Gameplay Sessions**: Conduct multiple gameplay sessions for each selected game, documenting player experiences and challenges faced.
- **Mechanics Analysis**: Analyze game mechanics, including level design, challenge progression, and player feedback mechanisms.
- **Difficulty Curve Evaluation**: Assess the difficulty curve by tracking player performance and engagement levels throughout the game.
- **Feedback Collection**: Gather qualitative and quantitative feedback from players regarding their experiences, focusing on clarity of instructions, control intuitiveness, and overall enjoyment.
- **Report Compilation**: Compile findings into a comprehensive report that highlights strengths, weaknesses, and actionable recommendations for game developers.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Game Selection Completed
  - Deliverable: List of selected puzzle games for assessment.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Gameplay Sessions Conducted
  - Deliverable: Documentation of gameplay sessions, including player feedback and observations.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Mechanics Analysis Completed
  - Deliverable: Analysis report detailing game mechanics and difficulty scaling.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Feedback Collection Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of player feedback and engagement metrics.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Report Delivered
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report with findings, strengths, weaknesses, and recommendations.
