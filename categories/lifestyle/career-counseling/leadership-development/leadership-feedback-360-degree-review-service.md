
# Leadership Feedback and 360-Degree Review Service Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Leadership Feedback and 360-Degree Review Service aims to empower leaders within {{Organization Name}} by providing comprehensive feedback from multiple perspectives. The primary objective of this project is to enhance leadership effectiveness through a structured feedback process that includes insights from peers, subordinates, and supervisors. This holistic approach allows leaders to gain a deeper understanding of their performance and impact on the organization. The project will begin with the development of a tailored feedback questionnaire that aligns with the organization's leadership competencies. Following the collection of feedback, our team will conduct a thorough analysis to identify strengths and areas for improvement. The key outcomes of this service include a detailed report that outlines key findings, trends, and actionable recommendations for personal and professional growth. Ultimately, this service not only fosters individual development but also contributes to improved team dynamics and overall organizational success.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**: 
  - Conduct a kickoff meeting with stakeholders to define project objectives and expectations.
  - Identify key leadership competencies relevant to the organization.

- **Questionnaire Development**: 
  - Collaborate with stakeholders to design a tailored feedback questionnaire.
  - Ensure alignment with the organization's leadership competencies and values.

- **Feedback Collection**: 
  - Distribute the questionnaire to selected participants, including peers, subordinates, and supervisors.
  - Ensure confidentiality and anonymity to encourage honest feedback.

- **Data Analysis**: 
  - Collect and analyze the feedback data to identify patterns, strengths, and areas for improvement.
  - Utilize qualitative and quantitative methods to ensure a comprehensive analysis.

- **Report Preparation**: 
  - Develop a detailed report summarizing key findings, trends, and actionable recommendations.
  - Include visual aids such as charts and graphs to enhance understanding.

- **Feedback Session**: 
  - Schedule a feedback session with the leader to discuss the report findings.
  - Provide guidance on creating a personal development plan based on the feedback.

- **Follow-Up**: 
  - Conduct a follow-up session after a predetermined period to assess progress and adjust the development plan as necessary.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Project Kickoff Meeting
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and project charter.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Questionnaire Development
  - Deliverable: Finalized feedback questionnaire.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Feedback Collection
  - Deliverable: Collected feedback data.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Data Analysis
  - Deliverable: Analysis report summarizing findings.

- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of Final Report
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report with actionable recommendations.

- **Milestone 6**: Feedback Session with Leader
  - Deliverable: Session notes and personal development plan.

- **Milestone 7**: Follow-Up Session
  - Deliverable: Follow-up report assessing progress.
