
# Project Plan Template: Retirement Technology Training

## 1. Executive Summary

The Retirement Technology Training project is designed to empower retirees by enhancing their technological skills, enabling them to navigate the digital landscape confidently. The primary objective of this project is to provide a comprehensive training program that covers essential topics such as smartphone usage, social media engagement, and online banking practices. By tailoring each session to meet the unique needs of retirees, we aim to create a comfortable and supportive learning environment. Key outcomes include increased proficiency in technology use, improved connectivity with families and communities, and a more fulfilling retirement experience. Participants will benefit from hands-on training, practical exercises, and resources that reinforce their learning. Ultimately, this project seeks to bridge the digital divide for retirees, fostering independence and confidence in their ability to engage with the modern world.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Retirement Technology Training project includes the following key components:

- **Needs Assessment**: Conduct surveys and interviews with potential participants to identify specific technological skills they wish to learn.
- **Curriculum Development**: Create a tailored curriculum that addresses the identified needs, covering topics such as:
  - Smartphone usage (basic functions, apps, and settings)
  - Social media engagement (creating accounts, privacy settings, and communication)
  - Online banking practices (setting up accounts, security measures, and transactions)
- **Training Sessions**: Organize and deliver a series of interactive training sessions, ensuring a hands-on approach to learning.
- **Resource Materials**: Develop and distribute user-friendly guides, cheat sheets, and video tutorials for participants to reference after training.
- **Feedback and Evaluation**: Implement a feedback mechanism to assess participant satisfaction and learning outcomes, allowing for continuous improvement of the program.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Needs Assessment
  - Deliverable: Summary report of participant needs and preferences
- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of Curriculum
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive training curriculum document
- **Milestone 3**: Delivery of Training Sessions
  - Deliverable: Completion of all scheduled training sessions
- **Milestone 4**: Distribution of Resource Materials
  - Deliverable: Printed and digital resource materials provided to participants
- **Milestone 5**: Collection of Feedback
  - Deliverable: Feedback report summarizing participant evaluations and suggestions
