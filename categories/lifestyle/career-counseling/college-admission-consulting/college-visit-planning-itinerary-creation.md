
# Project Plan Template: College Visit Planning and Itinerary Creation

## 1. Executive Summary

The College Visit Planning and Itinerary Creation project aims to provide students with a structured and supportive approach to organizing their college visits. The primary objective is to alleviate the stress associated with planning these visits by offering tailored consultations that consider each student's unique preferences, target colleges, and specific interests. Through a comprehensive understanding of the student's needs, we will develop a detailed itinerary that includes campus tours, information sessions, and meetings with admissions officers. Key outcomes of this project include a well-organized schedule for each visit, a curated list of insightful questions for students to ask during their tours, and an overall enhanced experience that contributes positively to the college admission process. By ensuring that students are well-prepared and informed, this project will empower them to make educated decisions regarding their future educational paths.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a one-on-one consultation with the student to gather information about their preferences, target colleges, and specific interests.
  
- **Research and Selection**: Identify potential colleges for visits based on the student's criteria, including location, programs offered, campus culture, and admission requirements.

- **Itinerary Development**: Create a detailed itinerary for each college visit, including:
  - Dates and times for campus tours
  - Scheduled information sessions
  - Meetings with admissions officers or faculty members
  
- **Preparation of Questions**: Develop a tailored list of insightful questions for the student to ask during their campus tours, focusing on aspects such as academic programs, campus life, and financial aid.

- **Follow-Up Support**: Provide follow-up support after the visits to discuss impressions and help the student reflect on their experiences.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Summary report of student preferences and target colleges.

- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of College List
  - Deliverable: List of selected colleges for visits.

- **Milestone 3**: Itinerary Creation
  - Deliverable: Detailed itinerary for each college visit.

- **Milestone 4**: Preparation of Questions
  - Deliverable: Customized list of questions for campus tours.

- **Milestone 5**: Follow-Up Discussion
  - Deliverable: Summary of follow-up discussion and reflections.
