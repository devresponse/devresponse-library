
# Project Plan Template: University Shortlisting Based on Student Profile

## 1. Executive Summary

The University Shortlisting project aims to provide a tailored university selection service that aligns with the unique profile of each student. The primary objective is to conduct a comprehensive assessment of the student's academic achievements, career aspirations, and personal preferences. This includes evaluating key metrics such as GPA, standardized test scores, extracurricular activities, and intended major. By leveraging this information, the consultant will curate a customized list of universities that offer a balanced mix of reach, match, and safety schools. Key outcomes of this project include a detailed report on each shortlisted university, outlining admission requirements, campus culture, and available programs. This service is crucial for students seeking to maximize their chances of acceptance into institutions that resonate with their goals and aspirations, ultimately empowering them to make informed decisions regarding their college applications.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a one-on-one meeting with the student to gather information about their academic background, career goals, and personal preferences.
  
- **Profile Assessment**: Evaluate the student's GPA, standardized test scores (e.g., SAT, ACT), extracurricular activities, and intended major to create a comprehensive student profile.

- **Research Phase**: Investigate potential universities that align with the student's profile, focusing on factors such as academic programs, campus culture, location, and admission requirements.

- **Shortlisting Process**: Develop a curated list of universities categorized into reach, match, and safety schools, ensuring a balanced selection.

- **Final Report Preparation**: Compile a detailed report for the student that includes:
  - A summary of each shortlisted university
  - Admission requirements and deadlines
  - Insights into campus culture and student life
  - Available programs and majors

- **Follow-Up Consultation**: Schedule a follow-up meeting to discuss the report, answer any questions, and provide guidance on the next steps in the application process.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed

- **Milestone 5**: Final Report Prepared
