
# Project Plan Template: Athletic Scholarship Application Consulting

## 1. Executive Summary

The Athletic Scholarship Application Consulting project aims to provide comprehensive support to student-athletes seeking financial assistance through athletic scholarships. The primary objective is to guide clients through the entire application process, ensuring they present their athletic skills and academic achievements in the best possible light. This project will include an initial assessment of each student's strengths, followed by personalized assistance in crafting compelling personal statements and resumes. Additionally, we will offer expert advice on creating recruitment videos that effectively showcase their athletic abilities. Communication strategies with college coaches will also be a key focus, equipping student-athletes with the skills to present themselves professionally and confidently. Ultimately, our goal is to empower student-athletes with the tools and knowledge necessary to enhance their chances of securing athletic scholarships and gaining admission to their desired colleges, leading to successful academic and athletic careers.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a thorough evaluation of the student-athlete's athletic skills, academic achievements, and scholarship eligibility.
  
- **Personal Statement Development**: Assist in drafting and refining personal statements that highlight the student-athlete's unique qualities, experiences, and aspirations.

- **Resume Crafting**: Create a professional resume that effectively showcases the student-athlete's accomplishments, skills, and relevant experiences.

- **Recruitment Video Guidance**: Provide expert advice on how to create impactful recruitment videos, including tips on filming, editing, and content to include.

- **Communication Strategies**: Develop effective communication strategies for reaching out to college coaches, including email templates and conversation tips.

- **Mock Interviews**: Conduct mock interviews to prepare student-athletes for interactions with college coaches and admissions committees.

- **Ongoing Support**: Offer continuous support throughout the application process, including answering questions and providing feedback on submitted materials.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Assessment Completion
  - Deliverable: Assessment report detailing strengths and areas for improvement.

- **Milestone 2**: Personal Statement Draft
  - Deliverable: First draft of the personal statement for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Resume Draft
  - Deliverable: First draft of the resume for review.

- **Milestone 4**: Recruitment Video Outline
  - Deliverable: Outline and content suggestions for the recruitment video.

- **Milestone 5**: Communication Strategy Plan
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive communication strategy document.

- **Milestone 6**: Mock Interview Session
  - Deliverable: Feedback report from the mock interview session.

- **Milestone 7**: Final Review and Submission
  - Deliverable: Finalized personal statement, resume, and recruitment video ready for submission.
