
# Project Plan Template: Letters of Recommendation Strategy Session

## 1. Executive Summary

The Letters of Recommendation Strategy Session aims to empower students in their college admission journey by providing expert guidance on selecting the most suitable recommenders. This project focuses on helping students understand the critical role that letters of recommendation play in the college application process. By emphasizing the importance of choosing individuals who can effectively articulate the student's strengths, achievements, and character, we aim to enhance the quality of recommendations received. The session will also cover practical strategies for approaching potential recommenders, including crafting a compelling request and providing context to aid in writing impactful letters. Key outcomes of this project include a personalized strategy for each student to secure strong letters of recommendation, increased confidence in approaching recommenders, and ultimately, a more competitive college application that improves the chances of acceptance into desired institutions.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a one-on-one meeting with each student to assess their academic background, extracurricular activities, and personal achievements.
  
- **Recommender Selection Guidance**: Provide criteria for selecting recommenders, including teachers, mentors, and community leaders who can best represent the student’s strengths.

- **Approach Strategy Development**: Guide students on how to approach potential recommenders, including:
  - Crafting a compelling request for a recommendation.
  - Providing context about the college programs they are applying to.
  - Sharing personal achievements and experiences that the recommender can highlight.

- **Mock Request Practice**: Conduct role-playing exercises to help students practice their approach and refine their communication skills.

- **Follow-Up Strategies**: Discuss how to follow up with recommenders after the initial request, including expressing gratitude and providing updates on the application process.

- **Resource Provision**: Supply students with templates and examples of effective recommendation requests and follow-up emails.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Student profile assessment report.

- **Milestone 2**: Recommender Selection Criteria Provided
  - Deliverable: Customized list of potential recommenders for each student.

- **Milestone 3**: Approach Strategy Developed
  - Deliverable: Personalized approach strategy document.

- **Milestone 4**: Mock Request Practice Session Conducted
  - Deliverable: Feedback report on practice sessions.

- **Milestone 5**: Follow-Up Strategies Discussed
  - Deliverable: Follow-up strategy guide.

- **Milestone 6**: Resource Materials Distributed
  - Deliverable: Templates and examples for recommendation requests.
