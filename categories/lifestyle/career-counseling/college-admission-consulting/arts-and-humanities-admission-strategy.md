
# Project Plan Template: Arts and Humanities Admission Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary

The Arts and Humanities Admission Strategy project aims to empower students in their pursuit of admission to competitive college programs in the arts and humanities. The primary objective is to provide personalized consulting services that cater to each student's unique background, interests, and aspirations. Through a collaborative approach, the consultant will assist students in crafting compelling narratives that effectively showcase their individual experiences, creativity, and relevant skills. Key outcomes include the development of impactful personal statements, the selection of appropriate portfolio pieces, and comprehensive interview preparation. By offering insights into the specific requirements and expectations of various institutions, this project will help students align their applications with the values and goals of their target schools. Ultimately, the goal is to enhance the likelihood of acceptance into preferred programs, ensuring that each application stands out in a competitive landscape.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct an in-depth assessment of the student's background, interests, and aspirations to tailor the consulting approach.
- **Personal Statement Development**: Guide the student in crafting a personal statement that reflects their unique voice and experiences, emphasizing creativity and individuality.
- **Portfolio Selection**: Assist in selecting and curating portfolio pieces that best represent the student's skills and artistic vision.
- **Interview Preparation**: Provide mock interview sessions and feedback to prepare the student for potential admissions interviews.
- **Institutional Research**: Offer insights into the specific requirements and expectations of targeted institutions, including program values and application nuances.
- **Application Review**: Review completed applications to ensure alignment with institutional expectations and provide final feedback.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive assessment report outlining the student's strengths and areas for development.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Personal Statement Draft Completed
  - Deliverable: First draft of the personal statement for review and feedback.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Portfolio Pieces Selected
  - Deliverable: Curated list of portfolio pieces with rationale for selection.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Mock Interview Conducted
  - Deliverable: Feedback report on interview performance and areas for improvement.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Application Review Completed
  - Deliverable: Finalized application package with all components reviewed and approved.
