
# Interview Follow-Up Strategy Coaching Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Interview Follow-Up Strategy Coaching project aims to equip candidates with essential skills to effectively communicate post-interview. In today's competitive job market, a well-crafted follow-up can significantly influence hiring decisions. This project will provide personalized coaching sessions that focus on creating impactful follow-up emails and thank-you notes. The objectives include teaching candidates how to express gratitude, reinforce their interest in the position, and highlight key discussion points from the interview. Key outcomes will include the development of tailored follow-up communications that resonate with hiring managers, enhancing the candidate's chances of securing their desired role. By the end of the coaching, participants will feel confident in their ability to articulate their appreciation and maintain a positive presence in the minds of potential employers.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Interview Follow-Up Strategy Coaching includes the following components:

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct an initial assessment of the candidate's interview experience and their current follow-up practices.
- **Coaching Sessions**: Provide a series of coaching sessions ({{number_of_sessions}}) focused on:
  - Crafting personalized thank-you notes.
  - Developing follow-up emails that reinforce interest in the position.
  - Discussing best practices for timing and tone in follow-up communications.
- **Resource Materials**: Provide templates and examples of effective follow-up communications.
- **Feedback and Revision**: Offer feedback on drafted follow-up messages and assist in revisions to ensure clarity and impact.
- **Final Review**: Conduct a final review session to ensure candidates are confident in their follow-up strategy.
