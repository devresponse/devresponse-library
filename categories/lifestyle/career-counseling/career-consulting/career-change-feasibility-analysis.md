
# Career Change Feasibility Analysis Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Career Change Feasibility Analysis project aims to provide individuals with a comprehensive assessment of their potential career transitions. The primary objective is to evaluate the client's current skill set and identify transferable skills that can be leveraged in a new professional environment. This project will also involve extensive market research to assess the demand for potential new roles, including salary expectations and job availability. By identifying potential challenges and barriers to entry in the new field, we will equip clients with a clear understanding of what to expect during their transition. Key outcomes include a detailed report outlining actionable insights and a strategic plan tailored to the client's unique situation. Ultimately, this project empowers clients to make informed decisions about their career change, enhancing their confidence and readiness for the next steps in their professional journey.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a one-on-one meeting with the client to understand their current career situation, aspirations, and motivations for change.
- **Skill Set Evaluation**: Assess the client's existing skills, qualifications, and experiences to identify transferable skills relevant to potential new careers.
- **Market Research**: Perform thorough research on the job market, focusing on:
  - Demand for identified potential roles
  - Salary expectations for those roles
  - Job availability and growth trends in the target industry
- **Challenges Assessment**: Identify potential challenges and barriers to entry in the new field, including required qualifications, certifications, and industry-specific skills.
- **Strategic Plan Development**: Create a tailored strategic plan that includes:
  - Recommended steps for skill enhancement or retraining
  - Networking strategies and resources
  - Timeline for the transition
- **Final Report**: Deliver a comprehensive report summarizing findings, insights, and the strategic plan, along with recommendations for the client's next steps.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and preliminary assessment report
- **Milestone 2**: Skill Set Evaluation Completed
  - Deliverable: Skill assessment report highlighting transferable skills
- **Milestone 3**: Market Research Completed
  - Deliverable: Market research report detailing job demand, salary expectations, and growth trends
- **Milestone 4**: Challenges Assessment Completed
  - Deliverable: Challenges report outlining barriers to entry and required qualifications
- **Milestone 5**: Strategic Plan Developed
  - Deliverable: Tailored strategic plan document
- **Milestone 6**: Final Report Delivered
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive final report summarizing all findings and recommendations
