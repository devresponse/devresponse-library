
# Career Goal Setting Workshop Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Career Goal Setting Workshop aims to empower individuals by equipping them with essential tools and strategies to clearly define their career aspirations. This interactive workshop will guide participants through a structured process of self-assessment, goal identification, and action planning. The primary objective is to help attendees gain introspection and clarity regarding their professional ambitions, enabling them to make informed decisions about their career paths. Key outcomes include a personalized career action plan for each participant, which outlines actionable steps toward achieving their goals, as well as resources for ongoing support. By focusing on understanding personal strengths and weaknesses, exploring potential career paths, and developing SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) goals, the workshop will cater to individuals at various stages of their career journey—whether they are just starting out, seeking advancement, or contemplating a career change. Ultimately, this workshop will foster a supportive environment that encourages personal growth and professional development.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The Career Goal Setting Workshop will include the following components:

- **Pre-Workshop Assessment**: Participants will complete a self-assessment questionnaire to identify their strengths, weaknesses, and career interests.
- **Interactive Sessions**: The workshop will consist of several interactive sessions focusing on:
  - Understanding personal strengths and weaknesses
  - Exploring potential career paths
  - Developing SMART goals
- **Group Activities**: Participants will engage in group discussions and activities to share insights and experiences.
- **Personalized Action Planning**: Each participant will create a personalized career action plan that outlines specific steps to achieve their career goals.
- **Resource Provision**: Attendees will receive a resource pack that includes templates, reading materials, and links to additional support services.
- **Post-Workshop Follow-Up**: A follow-up session will be scheduled to review progress and provide additional guidance.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Pre-Workshop Assessments
  - **Deliverable**: Summary report of participant assessments
- **Milestone 2**: Conducting the Workshop
  - **Deliverable**: Workshop materials and participant feedback forms
- **Milestone 3**: Distribution of Personalized Action Plans
  - **Deliverable**: Individual action plans for each participant
- **Milestone 4**: Post-Workshop Follow-Up Session
  - **Deliverable**: Summary of follow-up discussions and additional resources provided
