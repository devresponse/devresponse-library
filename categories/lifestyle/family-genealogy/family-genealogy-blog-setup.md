
# Project Plan Template: Family Genealogy Blog Setup

## 1. Executive Summary

The Family Genealogy Blog Setup project aims to create a personalized digital platform dedicated to documenting and sharing the rich history and stories of the {{family_name}} family. The primary objective is to establish a blog that serves as a comprehensive repository for genealogical findings, family narratives, and ancestral research. This blog will not only showcase the family's journey but also foster connections with relatives and fellow genealogy enthusiasts. Key outcomes of this project include the selection of an appropriate blogging platform, customization of the blog's design to reflect the family's heritage, and the implementation of essential features such as categorized family branches, a search function for easy navigation, and multimedia content options. Furthermore, the project will provide guidance on best practices for writing engaging posts and strategies for promoting the blog within genealogy communities. Ultimately, this initiative aims to create a lasting digital legacy that preserves the {{family_name}} family's stories for future generations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Platform Selection**: Research and select a suitable blogging platform (e.g., WordPress, Blogger, Wix) that meets the project's requirements.
- **Design Customization**: Develop a blog design that reflects the {{family_name}} heritage, including color schemes, fonts, and layout.
- **Feature Implementation**:
  - Create categories for different branches of the family tree.
  - Implement a search function for easy navigation.
  - Enable multimedia content options for photos, videos, and documents.
- **Content Strategy**: Provide guidance on writing engaging blog posts, including tips on storytelling and structuring content.
- **Promotion Strategy**: Develop a plan for promoting the blog within genealogy communities and social media platforms.
- **Training and Support**: Offer training sessions on how to manage and update the blog, including troubleshooting common issues.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of platform selection
  - Deliverable: Selected blogging platform and rationale document
- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of blog design
  - Deliverable: Mockup of the blog design for review and approval
- **Milestone 3**: Implementation of essential features
  - Deliverable: Fully functional blog with categories, search function, and multimedia options
- **Milestone 4**: Content and promotion strategy development
  - Deliverable: Document outlining content strategy and promotion plan
- **Milestone 5**: Training and support session
  - Deliverable: Training materials and session completion report
