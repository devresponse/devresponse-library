
# Project Plan Template: Family Tree Wall Art Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Family Tree Wall Art Design project aims to create a unique and personalized piece of wall art that visually represents the client's family lineage. The primary objective is to celebrate and showcase the client's family heritage through a beautifully designed artwork that incorporates key elements such as names, dates, and significant milestones in the family's history. This project will begin with a comprehensive consultation to gather detailed information about the client's ancestry, ensuring that the final design is both meaningful and representative of their family story. Key outcomes include a draft design for client approval, high-quality printing of the artwork, and the delivery of a stunning piece that serves as both a decorative element and a conversation starter. By the end of this project, clients will have a tangible representation of their family legacy, fostering a deeper connection to their roots and providing a lasting tribute to their ancestry.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Schedule an initial meeting with the client to discuss their family history.
  - Gather information including names, dates of birth, marriage, and death, as well as any significant family milestones or stories.
  
- **Design Phase**: 
  - Create a draft design based on the information collected during the consultation.
  - Present the draft to the client for feedback and revisions.
  - Finalize the design after incorporating client feedback.

- **Production Phase**: 
  - Select high-quality materials for printing the artwork.
  - Print the final design ensuring durability and aesthetic appeal.
  
- **Delivery Phase**: 
  - Package the artwork securely for delivery.
  - Arrange for shipping or local pickup, ensuring the client receives the artwork in pristine condition.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the consultation phase
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive family history document
  
- **Milestone 2**: Draft design presentation
  - Deliverable: Initial draft of the family tree wall art
  
- **Milestone 3**: Final design approval
  - Deliverable: Approved final design ready for printing
  
- **Milestone 4**: Artwork production
  - Deliverable: Printed wall art on high-quality materials
  
- **Milestone 5**: Artwork delivery
  - Deliverable: Final product delivered to the client
