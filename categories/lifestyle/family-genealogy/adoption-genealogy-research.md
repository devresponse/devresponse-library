
# Project Plan Template: Adoption Genealogy Research

## 1. Executive Summary

The Adoption Genealogy Research project aims to assist adoptees in uncovering their biological family history and ancestry. The primary objective of this project is to provide a comprehensive service that involves meticulous research into public records, birth certificates, and other relevant documents to trace lineage and establish connections with biological relatives. This project will begin with an initial consultation to understand the adoptee's background and specific goals, ensuring that the research is tailored to their unique needs. Following this, a detailed research plan will be developed, focusing on key areas such as birth family history, potential relatives, and any available genetic information. The key outcomes of this project include a well-organized report summarizing findings, along with recommendations for further exploration if desired. Ultimately, this project aims to provide adoptees with a clearer understanding of their roots and familial connections, fostering a sense of identity and belonging.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the adoptee to gather background information, understand their goals, and discuss any known details about their adoption.
  
- **Research Plan Development**: Create a detailed research plan that outlines the specific areas of focus, including:
  - Birth family history
  - Potential relatives
  - Genetic information (if applicable)
  
- **Data Collection**: Perform thorough research using various sources, including:
  - Public records
  - Birth certificates
  - Adoption records
  - Online genealogy databases
  
- **Analysis of Findings**: Analyze the collected data to identify connections and construct a family tree or lineage chart.

- **Final Report Preparation**: Compile a comprehensive report that includes:
  - Summary of findings
  - Family tree or lineage chart
  - Recommendations for further exploration
  
- **Follow-Up Consultation**: Schedule a follow-up meeting to discuss the findings and provide guidance on next steps.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and agreed-upon research objectives.

- **Milestone 2**: Research Plan Developed
  - Deliverable: Detailed research plan document.

- **Milestone 3**: Data Collection Completed
  - Deliverable: Compiled data from various sources.

- **Milestone 4**: Analysis of Findings Completed
  - Deliverable: Draft of family tree or lineage chart.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Report Delivered
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report summarizing findings and recommendations.

- **Milestone 6**: Follow-Up Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and feedback on the report.
