
# Project Plan Template: Historical Family Records Research

## 1. Executive Summary

The Historical Family Records Research project aims to provide individuals with a comprehensive understanding of their ancestral lineage through meticulous investigation of historical documents. The primary objective is to uncover vital information about family members, including birth, marriage, and death records, as well as census data, immigration papers, and land deeds. By leveraging both online databases and physical archives, our experienced genealogists will ensure the accuracy and depth of the research. Key outcomes include a detailed report summarizing the findings, along with copies of original documents when available. This project is designed for those seeking to deepen their connection to their heritage and create a lasting family legacy. Ultimately, participants will gain a clearer picture of their family's history, the connections that bind them, and the rich stories that have shaped their lineage.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Identify and gather relevant historical documents, including but not limited to:
    - Birth certificates
    - Marriage licenses
    - Death certificates
    - Census records
    - Immigration papers
    - Land deeds
  - Utilize both online databases (e.g., Ancestry.com, FamilySearch) and physical archives (e.g., local courthouses, libraries, historical societies).

- **Analysis Phase**:
  - Analyze collected data to establish connections between family members.
  - Verify the accuracy of the information through cross-referencing multiple sources.

- **Reporting Phase**:
  - Compile a comprehensive report detailing the findings.
  - Include narratives that provide context to the historical data.
  - Provide copies of original documents whenever possible.

- **Presentation Phase**:
  - Deliver the final report to the client in both digital and printed formats.
  - Offer a consultation session to discuss findings and answer any questions.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Project Kickoff
  - Deliverable: Initial meeting with the client to outline objectives and gather preliminary information.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Document Research
  - Deliverable: A list of documents identified and accessed, along with preliminary findings.

- **Milestone 3**: Data Analysis Completion
  - Deliverable: A draft report summarizing the connections and insights derived from the research.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Report Submission
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report including findings, narratives, and copies of original documents.

- **Milestone 5**: Client Consultation
  - Deliverable: A meeting to present findings and discuss the implications of the research.
