
# Project Plan Template: Genealogical Brick Wall Problem Solving

## 1. Executive Summary

The Genealogical Brick Wall Problem Solving project aims to provide specialized assistance to clients who are encountering obstacles in their family history research. The primary objective is to identify and analyze the specific challenges faced by clients in tracing their ancestry. Our team of expert genealogists will conduct a comprehensive review of the existing research, evaluating the information gathered to offer strategic recommendations that will help break through these barriers. By utilizing a variety of genealogical resources, databases, and methodologies, we will uncover new leads and insights that empower clients to continue their genealogical journey. The key outcomes of this project include actionable steps and resources tailored to each client's unique situation, ultimately enabling them to connect more deeply with their heritage and family history. This service is particularly beneficial for individuals who have reached a dead end in their research and require expert guidance to move forward.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a detailed discussion with the client to understand their research history, challenges faced, and specific goals.
  
- **Research Review**: Analyze the existing research documentation provided by the client, including family trees, records, and any previous findings.

- **Barrier Identification**: Identify specific genealogical "brick walls" that are hindering progress in the client's research.

- **Resource Utilization**: Leverage various genealogical databases, archives, and resources to gather new information and leads relevant to the client's ancestry.

- **Strategic Recommendations**: Develop a personalized action plan that includes recommendations for further research, potential resources, and methodologies to overcome identified challenges.

- **Follow-Up Support**: Provide ongoing support through follow-up consultations to address any additional questions or challenges that may arise as the client implements the recommendations.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Summary report of client’s research history and identified challenges.

- **Milestone 2**: Research Review Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Detailed analysis report of existing research documentation.

- **Milestone 3**: Barrier Identification Completed  
  **Deliverable**: List of specific genealogical challenges identified.

- **Milestone 4**: Resource Gathering Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Compiled list of new leads and resources discovered.

- **Milestone 5**: Strategic Recommendations Delivered  
  **Deliverable**: Personalized action plan document with recommendations.

- **Milestone 6**: Follow-Up Support Session Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Summary of follow-up session and any additional guidance provided.
