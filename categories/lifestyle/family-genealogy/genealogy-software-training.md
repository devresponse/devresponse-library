
# Genealogy Software Training Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Genealogy Software Training project aims to provide comprehensive training sessions that empower clients with the necessary skills to effectively utilize genealogy software and tools. This project will focus on equipping participants with the knowledge to navigate various platforms, input data accurately, and leverage advanced features for research and organization of their family history. Key objectives include enhancing participants' ability to build family trees, conduct searches for historical records, and manage genealogical information efficiently. By the end of the training, clients will possess a solid understanding of how to utilize these tools to uncover their ancestry and preserve their family stories. The key outcomes of this project will include increased confidence in using technology for genealogical research, improved data management skills, and a deeper appreciation for their heritage. This training is ideal for individuals or groups seeking to enhance their genealogical research capabilities.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Genealogy Software Training project includes the following components:

- **Training Sessions**: Conduct a series of interactive training sessions, both in-person and online, tailored to the needs of participants.
- **Curriculum Development**: Create a comprehensive curriculum that covers essential topics such as:
  - Introduction to genealogy software
  - Building and managing family trees
  - Conducting searches for historical records
  - Utilizing advanced features for research
  - Data entry best practices
  - Organizing and preserving family stories
- **Materials and Resources**: Provide participants with training materials, including handouts, guides, and access to online resources.
- **Support and Follow-up**: Offer ongoing support through Q&A sessions and access to a dedicated online forum for participants to ask questions and share experiences.
- **Feedback and Evaluation**: Collect feedback from participants to evaluate the effectiveness of the training and make necessary adjustments for future sessions.
