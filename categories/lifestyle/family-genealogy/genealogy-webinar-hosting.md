
# Genealogy Webinar Hosting Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Genealogy Webinar Hosting project aims to deliver a series of informative and engaging webinars designed to educate participants on genealogical research methodologies, tools, and resources. The primary objective is to empower individuals to confidently trace their family history by providing them with actionable knowledge and insights. Each session will focus on distinct topics, including how to initiate a genealogy journey, leveraging online databases, understanding DNA testing, and interpreting historical records. Key outcomes include increased participant engagement, enhanced understanding of genealogical research, and the provision of follow-up resources to reinforce learning. By facilitating interactive Q&A sessions and discussions, the project seeks to create a supportive community where participants can share experiences and gain clarity in their genealogical pursuits.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Genealogy Webinar Hosting project includes the following key components:

- **Webinar Series Development**: Design and schedule a series of webinars, each focusing on a specific topic related to genealogical research.
- **Content Creation**: Develop comprehensive presentation materials, including slides, handouts, and supplementary resources for each webinar.
- **Platform Setup**: Select and configure a suitable online platform for hosting the webinars, ensuring it supports interactive features such as Q&A and polls.
- **Marketing and Promotion**: Create a marketing plan to promote the webinars through social media, email newsletters, and genealogy forums to attract participants.
- **Facilitation**: Host the webinars, providing expert insights, answering participant questions, and facilitating discussions.
- **Follow-Up Resources**: Develop and distribute follow-up materials, including recorded sessions, additional reading resources, and links to relevant tools and databases.
- **Feedback Collection**: Implement a feedback mechanism to gather participant insights and improve future webinars.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Project Kickoff
  - Deliverable: Project initiation meeting and finalized project plan.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Content Development Completion
  - Deliverable: Completed presentation materials for all webinars.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Marketing Campaign Launch
  - Deliverable: Marketing materials distributed and promotional activities initiated.
  
- **Milestone 4**: First Webinar Execution
  - Deliverable: Successful hosting of the first webinar, including participant engagement metrics.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Series Completion
  - Deliverable: Hosting of all scheduled webinars and distribution of follow-up resources.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Project Evaluation
  - Deliverable: Collection and analysis of participant feedback, along with a final project report.
