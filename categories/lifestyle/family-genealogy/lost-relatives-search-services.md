
# Project Plan Template: Lost Relatives Search Services

## 1. Executive Summary

The Lost Relatives Search Services project aims to assist individuals in reconnecting with family members who have been lost over time. The primary objective is to utilize comprehensive genealogical research techniques to trace lineage and identify potential relatives. This project will begin with an initial consultation to gather pertinent information about the client's family history and any known relatives. Following this, our team will conduct thorough research, delving into public records, family trees, and historical documents. Key outcomes of this project include a detailed report outlining the connections made, insights into the client's heritage, and, where applicable, contact information for lost relatives. By the end of the project, clients will not only have the opportunity to reconnect with family members but also gain a deeper understanding of their family history, thereby strengthening familial ties and bridging the gap with their past.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss their family history and gather relevant information.
  - Document known relatives, significant dates, and any existing family trees.

- **Research Phase**:
  - Conduct comprehensive genealogical research using public records, historical documents, and online databases.
  - Identify potential lost relatives based on the information provided by the client.

- **Analysis and Reporting**:
  - Analyze the data collected to establish connections and relationships.
  - Prepare a detailed report that includes:
    - A summary of findings
    - Connections made
    - Contact information for identified relatives (where applicable)
    - Insights into the client's family history and heritage

- **Final Review**:
  - Schedule a follow-up meeting with the client to present the findings and discuss the report.
  - Provide additional resources or recommendations for further exploration of family history if desired.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Client information document

- **Milestone 2**: Research Phase Completed
  - Deliverable: Preliminary findings report

- **Milestone 3**: Analysis and Reporting Completed
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report detailing connections and insights

- **Milestone 4**: Final Review Meeting Conducted
  - Deliverable: Client feedback and additional recommendations
