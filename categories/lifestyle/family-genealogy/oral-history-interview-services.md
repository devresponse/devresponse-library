
# Project Plan Template: Oral History Interview Services

## 1. Executive Summary

The Oral History Interview Services project aims to capture and preserve the rich tapestry of family stories and experiences through in-depth, one-on-one interviews with selected family members. The primary objective is to document personal histories, memories, and anecdotes that reflect the unique heritage of each family. By conducting professionally recorded sessions, we ensure that these invaluable narratives are preserved for future generations. Key outcomes of this project include high-quality audio recordings and written transcripts of each interview, enabling families to relive and share cherished memories. This service not only serves as a means of documentation but also fosters connection and reflection among family members, creating a lasting tribute to their legacy. Ultimately, this project seeks to empower families to honor their past while enriching their present and future.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Oral History Interview Services project includes the following key activities:

- **Pre-Interview Preparation**: 
  - Identify and select family members for interviews.
  - Develop a customized interview guide with thoughtful questions tailored to each interviewee.
  - Schedule interview sessions at convenient times for participants.

- **Conducting Interviews**: 
  - Facilitate one-on-one interviews in a comfortable setting, either in-person or virtually.
  - Record each session using professional audio equipment to ensure high-quality sound.
  - Encourage storytelling and reflection through guided questions.

- **Post-Interview Processing**: 
  - Transcribe the recorded interviews into written format.
  - Edit audio recordings for clarity and quality.
  - Compile and package the final deliverables, including audio files and transcripts.

- **Delivery of Final Products**: 
  - Provide families with digital copies of audio recordings and transcripts.
  - Offer options for physical copies or additional formats as requested.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Project Kickoff
  - Deliverable: Project initiation meeting with stakeholders to outline objectives and expectations.

- **Milestone 2**: Interview Guide Development
  - Deliverable: Customized interview guides for each selected family member.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Interviews
  - Deliverable: All interviews conducted and recorded.

- **Milestone 4**: Transcription and Editing
  - Deliverable: Completed transcripts and edited audio recordings.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Digital copies of audio recordings and transcripts delivered to families.
