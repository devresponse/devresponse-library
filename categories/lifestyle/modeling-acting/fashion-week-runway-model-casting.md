
# Project Plan Template: Fashion Week Runway Model Casting

## 1. Executive Summary
The Fashion Week Runway Model Casting project aims to identify and select talented runway models for a prestigious fashion event. The primary objective is to evaluate models based on specific criteria, including height, size, runway experience, versatility, and their ability to embody the designer's vision. This project provides an exciting opportunity for aspiring models to gain exposure in the fashion industry and work alongside renowned designers. Key outcomes include the successful selection of models who will participate in the fashion week, ensuring a diverse representation of talent, and facilitating a professional environment for models to showcase their skills. By the end of this project, selected models will receive comprehensive instructions regarding fittings, rehearsals, and the show schedule, ensuring they are well-prepared for the event.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work
- **Model Application Process**: Develop and implement an online application system for aspiring models to submit their portfolios and personal information.
- **Casting Call**: Organize a casting call event where models will be evaluated based on their runway skills and overall presence.
- **Evaluation Criteria**: Establish clear evaluation criteria that include height, size, experience, and versatility.
- **Selection Process**: Form a panel of judges comprising industry professionals to review applications and conduct live evaluations during the casting call.
- **Notification of Selected Models**: Communicate with selected models regarding their participation, providing them with all necessary details.
- **Preparation for Fashion Week**: Coordinate fittings, rehearsals, and provide a detailed schedule for the event to ensure models are adequately prepared.
