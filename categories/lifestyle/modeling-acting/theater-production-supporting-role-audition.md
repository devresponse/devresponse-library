
# Project Plan Template: Theater Production - Supporting Role Audition

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Theater Production: Supporting Role Audition" project aims to identify and select talented actors for supporting roles in a local theater's production of a classic play. The primary objective is to provide a platform for stage actors to showcase their skills, gain valuable experience, and contribute to a collaborative artistic endeavor. This project will involve a structured audition process where participants will prepare a monologue and engage in group auditions, allowing them to demonstrate their individual talents as well as their ability to work within a team. Key outcomes include the selection of passionate and skilled actors who can embody their characters, the successful execution of rehearsals, and ultimately, a live performance that captivates the audience. By fostering a creative environment, this project not only enhances the actors' visibility in the theater community but also enriches the cultural landscape of the local area.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Audition Preparation**: 
  - Actors will be required to select and prepare a monologue from the classic play.
  - Provide guidelines and resources for monologue selection and preparation.

- **Audition Process**:
  - Schedule audition dates and times.
  - Organize and conduct individual monologue auditions.
  - Facilitate group auditions to assess teamwork and collaboration skills.

- **Selection Criteria**:
  - Develop criteria for evaluating audition performances.
  - Assemble a panel of judges to review auditions and select actors.

- **Rehearsals**:
  - Schedule regular rehearsal sessions for selected actors.
  - Provide direction and feedback during rehearsals to enhance performance quality.

- **Performance**:
  - Organize a final performance for the selected actors in front of a live audience.
  - Ensure all logistical aspects of the performance are managed, including venue setup and audience engagement.
