
# Project Plan Template: Short Film Casting: Lead Actor Needed

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Short Film Casting: Lead Actor Needed" project aims to identify and cast a talented lead actor for an indie short film that explores the profound themes of identity and resilience. This film will narrate a compelling journey of self-discovery, highlighting the strength found in overcoming personal challenges. The primary objective is to find an actor who not only possesses the necessary skills but also has a passion for storytelling and the ability to convey deep emotions through their performance. Key outcomes include successfully casting the lead role, conducting rehearsals, and executing filming and promotional activities that will enhance the visibility of the film. This project presents a unique opportunity for aspiring actors to showcase their talents and gain invaluable experience in the film industry. We are committed to fostering diversity and inclusivity in our casting process, as we believe that varied perspectives significantly enrich the storytelling experience. Join us in bringing this powerful narrative to life and take a significant step forward in your acting career.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Casting Call Announcement**: Create and distribute a casting call to attract potential candidates through various platforms, including social media, acting schools, and industry networks.
  
- **Audition Process**: Organize auditions where selected actors will perform monologues and scenes relevant to the character. This will include:
  - Initial video submissions
  - In-person auditions for shortlisted candidates
  
- **Selection Criteria**: Establish criteria for evaluating actors based on their performance, emotional range, and fit for the character.
  
- **Rehearsals**: Schedule and conduct rehearsals with the selected actor to develop character depth and chemistry with other cast members.
  
- **Filming**: Coordinate filming schedules and locations, ensuring the lead actor is available and prepared for all shooting days.
  
- **Promotional Activities**: Involve the lead actor in promotional activities, including interviews, social media campaigns, and film festivals to enhance the film's visibility.
