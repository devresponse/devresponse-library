
# Project Plan Template: Digital Ad Campaign: Family Role Casting

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Digital Ad Campaign: Family Role Casting" project aims to cast a family unit, either real or assembled, to represent a lifestyle brand that emphasizes the importance of family connections. The primary objective is to create engaging digital content that resonates with the target audience by showcasing relatable moments that highlight the essence of family life. The selected family will embody the brand's values, demonstrating natural chemistry and comfort in front of the camera. Key outcomes of this project include the production of a series of short video clips and still images that capture authentic interactions and joyful moments. These deliverables will be utilized across various digital platforms, including social media and the brand's website, to enhance visibility and engagement. This campaign not only provides a unique opportunity for families to be featured in a professional setting but also promotes a brand that celebrates diverse family experiences. 

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Casting Call**: Develop and distribute a casting call to attract families of diverse backgrounds and sizes. 
- **Submission Process**: Create an online submission platform where families can submit their applications, including photos and a short video showcasing their family dynamics.
- **Selection Process**: Review submissions and select families based on chemistry, relatability, and alignment with brand values.
- **Pre-Production**: Conduct meetings with selected families to discuss the campaign vision, schedule, and logistics.
- **Production**: Organize and execute video shoots and photo sessions, capturing authentic family interactions in various settings.
- **Post-Production**: Edit video clips and images, ensuring they align with the brand's aesthetic and messaging.
- **Distribution**: Develop a distribution plan for the final deliverables across digital platforms, including social media and the brand's website.
- **Feedback and Evaluation**: Collect feedback from the brand and audience to evaluate the campaign's effectiveness and impact.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Launch Casting Call
  - Deliverable: Casting call announcement and submission platform live date.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Submission Deadline
  - Deliverable: Final collection of family submissions.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Family Selection
  - Deliverable: Announcement of selected families.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Pre-Production Meeting
  - Deliverable: Completed pre-production meeting with selected families.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Production Phase
  - Deliverable: Completion of all video shoots and photo sessions.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Post-Production Completion
  - Deliverable: Final edited video clips and images ready for distribution.
  
- **Milestone 7**: Campaign Launch
  - Deliverable: Launch of the digital ad campaign across all platforms.
