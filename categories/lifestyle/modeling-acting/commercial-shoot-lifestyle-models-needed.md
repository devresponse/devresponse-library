
# Project Plan Template: Commercial Shoot: Lifestyle Models Needed

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Commercial Shoot: Lifestyle Models Needed" project aims to create a visually compelling advertisement that showcases our innovative line of home and kitchen products. The primary objective is to capture the essence of modern living, demonstrating how our products enhance everyday life through relatable and diverse representations. We are committed to inclusivity, encouraging models of all ages and backgrounds to participate, as we believe that representation is crucial in our marketing efforts. The shoot will take place in a vibrant studio setting, featuring various scenes that depict real-life scenarios involving our products. Key outcomes include a high-quality commercial that resonates with our target audience, a diverse portfolio of lifestyle imagery for marketing purposes, and a positive experience for all participants. This project not only aims to elevate our brand's image but also provides an excellent opportunity for models to gain exposure and expand their portfolios in the lifestyle sector.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Casting Call**: 
  - Announce a casting call for diverse lifestyle models through various channels (social media, modeling agencies, etc.).
  - Review applications and select models based on diversity, age, and suitability for the brand image.

- **Pre-Production**:
  - Develop a detailed shooting schedule, including scenes, activities, and model assignments.
  - Coordinate with the creative team to finalize the concept, wardrobe, and props needed for the shoot.
  - Ensure all necessary permits and studio bookings are secured.

- **Production**:
  - Conduct a full-day commercial shoot in a well-lit studio setting.
  - Capture various scenes that showcase models engaging with the kitchenware and home essentials.
  - Ensure a positive and inclusive environment, providing support and guidance to all models throughout the shoot.

- **Post-Production**:
  - Edit and finalize the footage to create a polished commercial.
  - Develop a selection of lifestyle images for marketing collateral.
  - Gather feedback from the team and make necessary adjustments.
