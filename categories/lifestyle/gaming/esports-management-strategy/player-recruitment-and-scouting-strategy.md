
# Project Plan Template: Player Recruitment and Scouting Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary

The Player Recruitment and Scouting Strategy project aims to establish a comprehensive framework for identifying and recruiting skilled players for eSports teams. The primary objective is to develop clear evaluation criteria that align with the team's competitive goals and culture, ensuring that the recruitment process is both effective and efficient. This project will involve identifying various talent pools, including online platforms, local gaming communities, and tournaments, to ensure a diverse range of candidates. By implementing a structured trial process, we will assess player skills, teamwork, and adaptability in a competitive environment. Key outcomes of this project include a detailed recruitment strategy that enhances team performance, fosters a strong gaming community, and provides a sustainable pipeline of talent for future competitions. Ultimately, this project will empower eSports organizations to build a competitive edge through strategic player recruitment.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**
  - Conduct a market analysis of current player recruitment trends in eSports.
  - Identify key performance indicators (KPIs) for player evaluation.
  
- **Development of Evaluation Criteria**
  - Collaborate with team coaches and management to define specific skills and attributes desired in players.
  - Create a scoring system to objectively assess player capabilities.

- **Talent Pool Identification**
  - Explore various platforms for talent scouting, including:
    - Online gaming platforms (e.g., Twitch, Discord)
    - Local gaming communities and LAN events
    - Major eSports tournaments and leagues
  - Develop partnerships with local gaming organizations and schools.

- **Trial Process Design**
  - Outline a structured trial process that includes:
    - Initial screening of candidates based on evaluation criteria.
    - Organized scrimmages and practice sessions to assess teamwork and adaptability.
    - Feedback mechanisms for continuous improvement.

- **Documentation and Reporting**
  - Compile findings and strategies into a comprehensive recruitment strategy document.
  - Provide recommendations for ongoing player development and community engagement.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Market Analysis Completion**
  - Deliverable: Market analysis report detailing current trends and best practices in player recruitment.

- **Milestone 2: Evaluation Criteria Finalization**
  - Deliverable: Document outlining the evaluation criteria and scoring system.

- **Milestone 3: Talent Pool Identification**
  - Deliverable: List of identified talent pools and potential partnerships.

- **Milestone 4: Trial Process Outline**
  - Deliverable: Detailed outline of the structured trial process.

- **Milestone 5: Final Recruitment Strategy Document**
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive recruitment strategy document, including recommendations for future player development.
