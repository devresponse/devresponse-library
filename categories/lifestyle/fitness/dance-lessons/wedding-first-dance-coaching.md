
# Project Plan Template: Wedding First Dance Coaching

## 1. Executive Summary

The Wedding First Dance Coaching project aims to provide couples with a personalized and memorable dance experience for their wedding day. Our primary objective is to ensure that each couple feels confident and joyful while performing their first dance, creating a moment that reflects their unique love story. The project will encompass tailored dance lessons, song selection, and choreography, catering to both beginners and those with prior dance experience. Key outcomes include a polished dance routine, enhanced couple confidence, and a memorable performance that will leave a lasting impression on their guests. By focusing on technique, rhythm, and expression, we will guide couples through a transformative experience that not only prepares them for their first dance but also strengthens their bond as partners.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the couple to discuss their vision, preferences, and any prior dance experience. This will help tailor the lessons to their specific needs.
  
- **Song Selection**: Assist the couple in selecting the perfect song that resonates with their relationship and fits the style of their wedding.

- **Choreography Development**: Create a customized dance routine that incorporates the couple's preferred dance style, ensuring it is manageable and enjoyable.

- **Lesson Sessions**: Conduct a series of dance lessons, focusing on:
  - Basic dance techniques
  - Rhythm and timing
  - Expression and connection between partners
  - Practice of the choreographed routine
  
- **Final Rehearsal**: Schedule a final rehearsal to refine the performance and build confidence before the wedding day.

- **Post-Performance Feedback**: Offer a follow-up session to discuss the couple's experience and gather feedback for future improvements.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Customized lesson plan based on couple's preferences.

- **Milestone 2**: Song Selected
  - Deliverable: Finalized song choice for the first dance.

- **Milestone 3**: Choreography Developed
  - Deliverable: Complete dance routine tailored to the couple.

- **Milestone 4**: Dance Lessons Completed
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of lesson completion and couple's readiness.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Rehearsal Conducted
  - Deliverable: Polished performance ready for the wedding day.
