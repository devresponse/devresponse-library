
# Project Plan: Jazz Dance Fundamentals

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Jazz Dance Fundamentals" project aims to introduce beginners to the dynamic and expressive world of jazz dance. The primary objective is to equip participants with essential techniques such as body alignment, rhythm, and improvisation, fostering their ability to develop a unique dance style. This course will provide a comprehensive understanding of various jazz dance styles, ensuring that students not only learn the foundational movements but also gain the confidence to express themselves through dance. Key outcomes include improved fitness levels, enhanced musicality, and the ability to perform basic jazz dance routines. By the end of the program, participants will have a solid foundation in jazz dance, preparing them for more advanced techniques and styles. The course will consist of engaging video lessons, practice exercises, and personalized feedback sessions, creating an interactive and supportive learning environment.
