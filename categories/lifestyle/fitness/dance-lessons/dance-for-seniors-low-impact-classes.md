
# Project Plan Template: Dance for Seniors: Low-Impact Classes

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Dance for Seniors: Low-Impact Classes" project aims to provide gentle and engaging dance lessons specifically tailored for seniors. The primary objective is to enhance the physical well-being and social interaction of participants through low-impact movements that promote mobility and balance. This project will create a supportive and enjoyable environment where seniors can express themselves through dance, fostering a sense of community and belonging. Key outcomes include improved physical fitness, increased social engagement, and enhanced mental health among participants. The curriculum will feature a variety of easy-to-follow dance styles, ensuring accessibility for individuals of varying fitness levels. By executing this project over a fixed timeline, we will establish clear objectives and measurable outcomes to guarantee the highest quality of instruction and participant satisfaction.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Target Audience**: Seniors aged {{target_age_range}} who are looking for low-impact physical activities.
- **Class Structure**: 
  - Duration: Each class will last for {{class_duration}} minutes.
  - Frequency: Classes will be held {{class_frequency}}.
  - Class Size: Maximum of {{max_class_size}} participants per session to ensure personalized attention.
- **Curriculum Development**: 
  - Incorporate various dance styles such as ballroom, line dancing, and gentle jazz.
  - Develop lesson plans that focus on mobility, balance, and coordination.
  - Include warm-up and cool-down exercises to prevent injuries.
- **Instructor Qualifications**: All instructors will be certified in senior fitness and have experience in dance instruction.
- **Venue Requirements**: 
  - Accessible location with adequate space for movement.
  - Facilities for seating and social interaction before and after classes.
- **Marketing and Outreach**: 
  - Develop promotional materials targeting local senior communities.
  - Collaborate with senior centers and healthcare providers for outreach.
- **Evaluation and Feedback**: 
  - Implement participant surveys to assess satisfaction and areas for improvement.
  - Monitor physical and social outcomes through pre- and post-class assessments.
