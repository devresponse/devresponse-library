
# Project Plan Template: Board Game and Tabletop RPG Rentals

## 1. Executive Summary

The Board Game and Tabletop RPG Rentals project aims to create a comprehensive rental service that offers a diverse selection of board games and tabletop role-playing games (RPGs) to cater to both casual players and dedicated enthusiasts. Our primary objective is to provide a hassle-free and enjoyable rental experience, allowing customers to explore new games without the commitment of purchase. The project will focus on curating an extensive collection of well-maintained and sanitized games, ensuring a safe environment for our customers. Key outcomes include fostering a vibrant gaming community, enhancing recreational gaming experiences, and providing personalized recommendations to help customers discover new titles. By streamlining the rental process and offering exceptional customer service, we aim to position ourselves as a go-to destination for board game and tabletop RPG enthusiasts.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Game Collection Development**: Curate a diverse selection of board games and tabletop RPGs, ensuring a mix of popular titles and hidden gems.
- **Rental Process Design**: Develop a user-friendly rental process that allows customers to browse, select, and rent games easily.
- **Maintenance and Sanitization**: Establish protocols for the regular maintenance and sanitization of all games to ensure safety and quality.
- **Customer Support**: Implement a customer support system to assist with recommendations, inquiries, and rental issues.
- **Marketing Strategy**: Create a marketing plan to promote the rental service, targeting local gaming communities and social media platforms.
- **Feedback Mechanism**: Develop a system for collecting customer feedback to continuously improve the service and game selection.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of game collection curation
  - Deliverable: Catalog of available games with descriptions and rental terms
- **Milestone 2**: Development of the rental process and website
  - Deliverable: Fully functional rental platform with user interface
- **Milestone 3**: Implementation of maintenance and sanitization protocols
  - Deliverable: Documented procedures for game care
- **Milestone 4**: Launch of marketing campaign
  - Deliverable: Marketing materials and social media posts
- **Milestone 5**: Launch of rental service
  - Deliverable: Official opening of the rental service to customers
- **Milestone 6**: Collection of customer feedback post-launch
  - Deliverable: Summary report of customer feedback and suggestions
