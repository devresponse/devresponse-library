## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**:

- **Trend Analysis**:

- **Forecasting**:
  - Apply advanced forecasting techniques (e.g., time series analysis, regression analysis) to predict future market movements.

- **Report Development**:
  - Compile findings into a comprehensive report that includes:

- **Presentation**:
