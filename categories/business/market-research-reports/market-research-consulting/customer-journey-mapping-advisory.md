
# Project Plan Template: Customer Journey Mapping Advisory

## 1. Executive Summary

The Customer Journey Mapping Advisory project aims to empower businesses with a deep understanding of their customer interactions, ultimately enhancing user engagement and satisfaction. The primary objective is to conduct a thorough analysis of customer touchpoints, identifying key stages in the customer journey from initial awareness through to post-purchase engagement. By facilitating workshops with stakeholders and customers, our expert consultants will gather invaluable insights that provide a holistic view of the customer experience. The key outcomes of this project include detailed journey maps that pinpoint pain points and opportunities for improvement, alongside actionable recommendations tailored to the specific needs of the client. This initiative is designed not only to drive customer loyalty but also to foster business growth by equipping the client with the necessary tools to implement effective changes. The project will culminate in a comprehensive presentation of findings and strategies, ensuring that the client is well-prepared to enhance their customer journey.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Kick-off meeting with stakeholders to outline project objectives and expectations.
  - Define project timeline and key roles.

- **Research and Analysis**
  - Conduct stakeholder interviews to gather insights on current customer interactions.
  - Perform customer surveys to understand pain points and expectations.
  - Analyze existing customer data to identify trends and behaviors.

- **Workshop Facilitation**
  - Organize and facilitate workshops with stakeholders and customers to brainstorm and discuss customer journey stages.
  - Document insights and feedback from workshops.

- **Journey Mapping**
  - Develop detailed customer journey maps that illustrate key stages, touchpoints, and interactions.
  - Highlight pain points and opportunities for improvement within the journey maps.

- **Recommendations and Strategy Development**
  - Provide actionable recommendations based on the analysis and journey maps.
  - Develop a tailored strategy for enhancing customer engagement and satisfaction.

- **Final Presentation**
  - Prepare a comprehensive presentation of findings, journey maps, and recommendations.
  - Present to stakeholders and gather feedback for final adjustments.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kick-off**

- **Milestone 5: Recommendations Report**

- **Milestone 6: Final Presentation**
  - Deliverable: Presentation slides and final report.
