
# Project Plan Template: Retail Store Layout Feedback Focus Groups

## 1. Executive Summary
The Retail Store Layout Feedback Focus Groups project aims to gather qualitative insights from customers regarding their experiences and preferences related to retail store layouts. The primary objective is to organize and facilitate multiple focus group sessions that will allow participants to discuss their shopping experiences, preferences for store layout, and suggestions for improvement. By engaging directly with customers, we seek to uncover valuable insights that can inform retailers on how to optimize their store environments. Key outcomes of this project will include a comprehensive analysis of customer feedback, identification of strengths and weaknesses in current retail layouts, and actionable recommendations for enhancing customer satisfaction and driving sales. Ultimately, this project is essential for businesses that aspire to create a customer-centric shopping experience, ensuring that their store layouts align with customer needs and preferences.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work
- **Participant Recruitment**: Identify and recruit a diverse group of participants representing various demographics and shopping behaviors to ensure a comprehensive understanding of customer perspectives.
- **Focus Group Sessions**: Organize and facilitate a series of focus group sessions, each lasting approximately 1.5 to 2 hours. Sessions will be held in a neutral location to encourage open discussion.
- **Moderation**: Employ skilled moderators to guide discussions, ensuring that all participants have the opportunity to share their thoughts and experiences.
- **Recording and Analysis**: Record each session (with participant consent) and transcribe discussions for analysis. Key themes and insights will be extracted to inform the final report.
- **Final Report**: Compile a comprehensive report detailing customer feedback, including strengths and areas for improvement in current retail layouts, along with actionable recommendations for retailers.
