
# Project Plan Template: Subscription Service Feedback Interviews

## 1. Executive Summary

The Subscription Service Feedback Interviews project aims to gather valuable insights from current subscribers regarding their experiences with the service. The primary objective is to conduct structured one-on-one interviews that delve into various aspects of subscriber satisfaction, including service quality, content relevance, pricing, and customer support. By engaging directly with subscribers, we seek to identify both strengths and weaknesses in the current offering, as well as potential areas for improvement. The insights gained from these interviews will be instrumental in compiling a comprehensive report that highlights key themes and actionable recommendations. Ultimately, this project is essential for businesses looking to enhance their subscription services and better meet the needs of their customers. The final deliverable will include a summary of findings and suggested strategies for improvement, ensuring that the organization can make informed decisions based on subscriber feedback.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Objective**: Conduct structured one-on-one interviews with current subscribers to gather feedback on their experiences with the subscription service.
  
- **Target Audience**: Current subscribers of the subscription service, segmented by demographics such as age, subscription duration, and usage patterns.

- **Methodology**:
  - Develop an interview guide with open-ended questions to facilitate in-depth discussions.
  - Schedule and conduct interviews, ensuring a comfortable environment for participants.
  - Record interviews (with consent) for accurate data collection and analysis.

- **Data Analysis**:
  - Transcribe interviews and analyze responses to identify key themes and patterns.
  - Compile findings into a comprehensive report that includes both qualitative and quantitative insights.

- **Deliverables**:
  - Interview guide
  - Transcribed interview data
  - Comprehensive report summarizing findings and recommendations
