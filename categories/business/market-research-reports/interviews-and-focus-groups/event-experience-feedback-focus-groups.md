
# Event Experience Feedback Focus Groups Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Event Experience Feedback Focus Groups project aims to gather qualitative insights from event attendees to assess their experiences and identify areas for enhancement. This initiative will involve organizing and conducting multiple focus group sessions with a diverse range of participants who have attended recent events. The primary objective is to create an open dialogue environment where attendees can freely share their thoughts on various aspects of the event, including venue, content, networking opportunities, and overall satisfaction. By employing skilled facilitators to guide discussions, we will ensure that all voices are heard and that key themes emerge. The key outcomes of this project will include a comprehensive report summarizing the findings and providing actionable recommendations for future events. This project is essential for organizations looking to refine their event strategies and improve attendee engagement, ultimately leading to more successful and impactful events.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Participant Recruitment**: Identify and recruit a diverse group of participants who have attended recent events. This will include a mix of demographics, event types, and engagement levels.
  
- **Focus Group Sessions**: Organize and conduct a series of focus group sessions, each lasting approximately 1.5 to 2 hours. Sessions will be scheduled at convenient times for participants to maximize attendance.

- **Facilitation**: Engage experienced facilitators to guide the discussions, ensuring that all participants have the opportunity to contribute. Facilitators will use a structured discussion guide to cover key topics while allowing for organic conversation.

- **Data Collection**: Record and transcribe focus group discussions for accurate data collection. Ensure that participant confidentiality is maintained throughout the process.

- **Analysis and Reporting**: Analyze the qualitative data collected from the focus groups to identify key themes, insights, and areas for improvement. Compile a comprehensive report that includes findings and actionable recommendations.

- **Presentation of Findings**: Present the findings to stakeholders through a formal presentation, highlighting key insights and suggested strategies for enhancing future events.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of participant recruitment
  - **Deliverable**: List of recruited participants and confirmation of attendance

- **Milestone 2**: Conducting focus group sessions
  - **Deliverable**: Recorded and transcribed sessions for each focus group

- **Milestone 3**: Data analysis and report compilation
  - **Deliverable**: Comprehensive report summarizing findings and recommendations

- **Milestone 4**: Presentation of findings to stakeholders
  - **Deliverable**: Presentation slides and summary of key insights
