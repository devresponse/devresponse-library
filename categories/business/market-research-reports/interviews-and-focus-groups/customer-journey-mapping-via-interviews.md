
# Project Plan Template: Customer Journey Mapping via Interviews

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Customer Journey Mapping via Interviews" project aims to conduct in-depth interviews with customers to map their journey and identify critical touchpoints that influence their experience with our brand. The primary objective is to gather qualitative insights that reveal customer motivations, pain points, and preferences throughout their interaction with our products or services. By engaging directly with users, we will cover various stages of the customer journey, from awareness to post-purchase evaluation. The key outcomes of this project will include a comprehensive report synthesizing the findings, highlighting key insights, and providing actionable recommendations for enhancing user experience. This project is essential for businesses seeking to refine their customer engagement strategies and improve overall satisfaction, ultimately leading to increased loyalty and retention.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Preparation Phase:**
  - Define target customer segments for interviews.
  - Develop interview guide with structured questions covering all stages of the customer journey.
  - Recruit participants through various channels (e.g., email, social media, existing customer database).

- **Interview Phase:**
  - Conduct in-depth interviews (approximately {{number}} interviews) with selected customers.
  - Ensure interviews are recorded (with consent) for accurate data collection.
  - Utilize a mix of open-ended and closed questions to gather qualitative and quantitative data.

- **Analysis Phase:**
  - Transcribe interviews and analyze responses to identify common themes and insights.
  - Map customer journey stages and critical touchpoints based on interview findings.
  - Highlight pain points, motivations, and preferences expressed by customers.

- **Reporting Phase:**
  - Synthesize findings into a comprehensive report.
  - Include visual aids (e.g., journey maps, charts) to illustrate key insights.
  - Provide actionable recommendations for improving customer engagement strategies.

- **Presentation Phase:**
  - Present findings to stakeholders through a dedicated presentation session.
  - Facilitate a discussion on the implications of the findings and next steps.

- **Milestone 6:** Final Report
