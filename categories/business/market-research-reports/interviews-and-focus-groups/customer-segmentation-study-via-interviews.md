
# Project Plan Template: Customer Segmentation Study via Interviews

## 1. Executive Summary

The Customer Segmentation Study via Interviews aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of our customer base through qualitative research. The primary objective of this project is to conduct structured one-on-one interviews that delve into the diverse needs, preferences, and behaviors of our customers. By employing this method, we will gather rich insights that quantitative data alone cannot capture, enabling us to segment our customers effectively. The project will encompass the design of an interview guide, recruitment of participants, execution of interviews, and thorough data analysis. Key outcomes will include a detailed segmentation framework that informs marketing strategies and product offerings, ultimately enhancing customer engagement and satisfaction. This study will empower our organization to tailor its approach to meet the specific demands of different customer segments, driving business growth and improving overall performance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Define project objectives and success criteria.
  - Assemble project team and assign roles.

- **Design Phase**
  - Develop a comprehensive interview guide that includes key questions and topics to explore.
  - Identify and define customer segments for targeted interviews.

- **Recruitment Phase**
  - Create a recruitment plan to identify and select participants from various customer segments.
  - Utilize multiple channels for participant recruitment (e.g., email, social media, customer database).

- **Interview Phase**
  - Schedule and conduct one-on-one interviews with selected participants.
  - Ensure interviews are recorded (with consent) for accurate data collection.

- **Analysis Phase**
  - Transcribe and analyze interview data to identify key themes and insights.
  - Develop a segmentation framework based on findings.

- **Reporting Phase**
  - Prepare a comprehensive report detailing insights, segmentation framework, and actionable recommendations.
  - Present findings to stakeholders and gather feedback.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Project charter and team roles defined.
  
- **Milestone 2: Interview Guide Completion**
  - Deliverable: Finalized interview guide ready for use.

- **Milestone 3: Participant Recruitment**
  - Deliverable: List of recruited participants confirmed.

- **Milestone 4: Completion of Interviews**
  - Deliverable: All interviews conducted and recorded.

- **Milestone 5: Data Analysis**
  - Deliverable: Transcribed data and initial analysis completed.

- **Milestone 6: Final Report Submission**
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report with segmentation framework and recommendations delivered to stakeholders.
