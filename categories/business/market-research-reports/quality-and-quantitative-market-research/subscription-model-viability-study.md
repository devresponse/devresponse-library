
# Project Plan Template: Subscription Model Viability Study

## 1. Executive Summary

The Subscription Model Viability Study aims to provide a comprehensive evaluation of the potential success of subscription-based business models for {{Organization Name}}. The primary objective of this project is to leverage both qualitative and quantitative research methodologies to gain a deep understanding of consumer preferences, pain points, and willingness to pay. Through in-depth interviews and surveys, we will gather qualitative data that captures customer sentiments and expectations. This will be complemented by quantitative revenue forecasting, which will analyze market trends and financial metrics to project potential financial outcomes. The key outcomes of this study will include a detailed report synthesizing qualitative insights and quantitative forecasts into actionable recommendations. This will enable {{Organization Name}} to make informed decisions regarding the adoption of a subscription model, ensuring that the strategy is data-driven and aligned with market opportunities. Ultimately, this study is designed to equip your business with a clear understanding of market dynamics and customer expectations, paving the way for successful implementation of a subscription-based approach.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Subscription Model Viability Study includes the following key activities:

- **Qualitative Research**: Conduct in-depth interviews with a diverse group of customers to gather insights on their preferences, pain points, and willingness to pay for subscription services.
- **Quantitative Research**: Design and distribute surveys to a broader audience to collect quantitative data on consumer behavior and market trends.
- **Data Analysis**: Perform statistical analysis on the qualitative and quantitative data collected to identify patterns, trends, and correlations.
- **Market Assessment**: Evaluate current market conditions and competitor offerings to understand the landscape for subscription models.
- **Financial Forecasting**: Develop revenue forecasts based on the data analysis to project potential financial outcomes of implementing a subscription model.
- **Final Report**: Compile findings into a comprehensive report that includes actionable recommendations for {{Organization Name}} regarding the adoption of a subscription model.
