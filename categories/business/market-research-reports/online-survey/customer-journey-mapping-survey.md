
# Project Plan Template: Customer Journey Mapping Survey

## 1. Executive Summary

The Customer Journey Mapping Survey project aims to create a comprehensive online survey that captures the entire customer journey, from initial awareness through to the final purchase decision. The primary objective is to uncover critical touchpoints where customers interact with the brand, as well as identify any pain points that may hinder their experience. By employing a mix of quantitative and qualitative questions, we will gather valuable insights that can inform strategic business decisions. Key outcomes of this project include a detailed analysis of customer interactions, identification of opportunities for enhancing the customer experience, and actionable recommendations for improving customer satisfaction and loyalty. Collaboration with the client will ensure that the survey aligns with their specific objectives and target audience, leading to a thorough review of the findings and a clear path forward for strategic enhancements.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Conduct a kickoff meeting with stakeholders to define project goals and objectives.
  - Identify key stakeholders and establish communication channels.

- **Survey Design**
  - Develop a comprehensive survey framework that includes both quantitative and qualitative questions.
  - Collaborate with the client to ensure alignment with their brand and objectives.
  - Create a draft of the survey for client review and feedback.

- **Survey Implementation**
  - Deploy the online survey using a suitable platform.
  - Ensure accessibility and user-friendliness of the survey for the target audience.
  - Monitor survey responses and engagement throughout the data collection period.

- **Data Analysis**
  - Analyze survey results to identify trends, touchpoints, and pain points in the customer journey.
  - Prepare a detailed report summarizing key findings and insights.

- **Recommendations and Reporting**
  - Develop actionable recommendations based on survey analysis.
  - Present findings and recommendations to stakeholders in a final presentation.

- **Project Closure**
  - Conduct a project closure meeting to review outcomes and gather feedback.
  - Provide all project documentation and deliverables to the client.

- **Milestone 5: Final Presentation**
