
# Project Plan Template: Subscription Service Feedback Survey

## 1. Executive Summary

The Subscription Service Feedback Survey project aims to develop a comprehensive online survey tailored specifically for subscribers of the {{Subscription Service Name}}. The primary objective of this project is to gather valuable insights regarding overall satisfaction with the service, preferences for specific features, and the reasons behind subscription cancellations. By engaging subscribers through a user-friendly survey, we intend to achieve high response rates that will yield actionable feedback. The key outcomes of this project will include a well-structured survey design, an effective implementation plan, and a detailed report analyzing the collected data. This report will highlight trends, identify areas for improvement, and provide strategic recommendations that can enhance service offerings, improve customer retention, and ultimately drive business growth. The insights gained from this survey will empower the client to make informed decisions that align with subscriber needs and expectations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Survey Design**: Create a user-friendly online survey that includes questions focusing on:
  - Overall satisfaction with the service
  - Preferences for specific features
  - Insights into reasons for subscription cancellations
  - Demographic information for segmentation analysis

- **Implementation Plan**: Develop a step-by-step plan for deploying the survey, including:
  - Target audience identification
  - Survey distribution channels (email, social media, etc.)
  - Timeline for survey launch and response collection

- **Data Analysis**: Analyze the survey responses to identify:
  - Key trends in subscriber satisfaction
  - Areas for improvement in service offerings
  - Factors influencing subscription cancellations

- **Reporting**: Prepare a comprehensive report that includes:
  - Summary of findings
  - Strategic recommendations based on data analysis
  - Visual representations of data (charts, graphs)
