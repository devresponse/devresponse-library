
# Project Plan Template: Content Gap Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary

The Content Gap Analysis project aims to provide a comprehensive evaluation of your website's existing content to identify areas that are missing or underperforming. This project will involve a detailed review of your current content against industry standards, competitor offerings, and audience expectations. Our team will utilize advanced online research techniques to uncover gaps in your content strategy, highlighting topics that are relevant but not adequately covered. Additionally, we will assess the performance of your existing content to pinpoint areas that may require enhancement or reworking. The final report will include actionable insights and recommendations for creating targeted material that resonates with your audience, ultimately driving engagement and conversions. This project is designed to empower your content strategy with data-driven decisions, ensuring that your website remains competitive and relevant in your industry. Key outcomes will include a prioritized list of content opportunities, performance metrics for existing content, and a strategic roadmap for future content development.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Content Inventory**: Conduct a thorough inventory of existing website content, categorizing it by type, topic, and performance metrics.
- **Competitor Analysis**: Analyze competitor websites to identify content strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities.
- **Audience Research**: Gather insights on audience preferences and expectations through surveys, social media analysis, and keyword research.
- **Gap Identification**: Identify content gaps by comparing existing content against industry standards and audience needs.
- **Performance Assessment**: Evaluate the performance of current content using analytics tools to identify underperforming pages and topics.
- **Recommendations Report**: Compile findings into a comprehensive report that includes actionable insights, prioritized content opportunities, and strategic recommendations for content enhancement.
- **Presentation**: Present findings and recommendations to stakeholders, ensuring clarity and understanding of the proposed content strategy.
