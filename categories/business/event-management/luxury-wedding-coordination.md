
# Project Plan Template: Luxury Wedding Coordination

## 1. Executive Summary

The Luxury Wedding Coordination project aims to provide a comprehensive and personalized wedding planning experience for couples seeking to create their dream wedding. Our primary objective is to transform each couple's unique vision into reality by managing every aspect of the wedding planning process. This includes personalized venue selection, meticulous vendor management, and guest list coordination, all tailored to align with the couple's style and budget. Key outcomes of this project include a seamless wedding day experience, a cohesive aesthetic that reflects the couple's personality, and the assurance that all details are handled with precision and care. By the end of this project, couples can expect an unforgettable wedding experience, free from stress, allowing them to fully enjoy their special day.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a detailed meeting with the couple to understand their vision, preferences, and budget.
- **Venue Selection**: Research and present a list of potential venues that match the couple's style and budget. Schedule site visits and assist in final selection.
- **Vendor Management**: Identify, negotiate, and manage contracts with top-quality vendors, including catering, photography, floral design, and entertainment.
- **Theme Design**: Collaborate with the couple to create a cohesive wedding theme that reflects their personality, including color schemes, decor, and overall aesthetic.
- **Guest List Coordination**: Assist in compiling and managing the guest list, including sending invitations and tracking RSVPs.
- **Day-of Coordination**: Provide on-site coordination on the wedding day, overseeing all activities to ensure everything runs smoothly and according to plan.
- **Post-Wedding Follow-Up**: Conduct a debrief with the couple to gather feedback and ensure all final payments and vendor contracts are settled.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Detailed wedding vision document
- **Milestone 2**: Venue Selection Finalized
  - Deliverable: Signed venue contract
- **Milestone 3**: Vendor Contracts Secured
  - Deliverable: List of confirmed vendors with contracts
- **Milestone 4**: Theme Design Approved
  - Deliverable: Theme design board and checklist
- **Milestone 5**: Guest List Finalized
  - Deliverable: Complete guest list with RSVP tracking
- **Milestone 6**: Day-of Coordination Executed
  - Deliverable: Successful wedding day with all activities managed
- **Milestone 7**: Post-Wedding Follow-Up Completed
  - Deliverable: Feedback report and final payments settled
