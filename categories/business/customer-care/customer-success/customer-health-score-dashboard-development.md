
# Customer Health Score Dashboard Creation Project Plan

## Executive Summary
The Customer Health Score Dashboard Development project aims to create a comprehensive and user-friendly dashboard that allows businesses to monitor and analyze customer health scores effectively. The primary objective of this project is to integrate key metrics such as customer engagement, satisfaction levels, and product usage statistics into a single platform, providing a holistic view of customer well-being. By leveraging these metrics, the dashboard will enable businesses to identify trends, spot potential issues, and take proactive measures to enhance customer satisfaction and retention. The final product will feature an intuitive interface, ensuring that stakeholders can easily interpret the data and make informed decisions. Key outcomes of this project include improved visibility into customer health, enhanced decision-making capabilities, and strengthened customer success initiatives, ultimately fostering long-term relationships with clients.

## Detailed Scope of Work
1. **Requirements Gathering**
   - Conduct stakeholder interviews to identify key metrics and desired features.
   - Document functional and non-functional requirements.

2. **Design Phase**
   - Create wireframes and mockups of the dashboard interface.
   - Review designs with stakeholders for feedback and approval.

3. **Development Phase**
   - Develop the dashboard using appropriate technologies and frameworks.
   - Integrate data sources for customer engagement, satisfaction, and product usage.

4. **Testing Phase**
   - Conduct unit testing, integration testing, and user acceptance testing (UAT).
   - Address any identified issues and ensure the dashboard meets all requirements.

5. **Deployment**
   - Deploy the dashboard to the production environment.
   - Provide training sessions for stakeholders on how to use the dashboard effectively.

6. **Post-Deployment Support**
   - Offer ongoing support and maintenance for a specified period.
   - Gather feedback for future enhancements.

## Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1:** Completion of Requirements Gathering

- **Milestone 6:** Completion of Training Sessions
