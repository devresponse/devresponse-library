
# Project Plan Template: Customer Journey Mapping Workshop

## 1. Executive Summary

The Customer Journey Mapping Workshop aims to empower businesses with a comprehensive understanding of their customers' experiences throughout their journey. This interactive workshop will facilitate collaborative exercises that allow participants to visualize the entire customer journey, from initial awareness to post-purchase interactions. The primary objective is to identify key pain points that customers encounter and explore innovative strategies to optimize each touchpoint, ultimately enhancing customer satisfaction and loyalty. By the conclusion of the workshop, participants will have developed a clear and actionable roadmap that outlines steps to improve customer engagement and drive overall business performance. This workshop is particularly beneficial for teams seeking to deepen their customer insights and implement effective customer care strategies, ensuring that they are well-equipped to meet and exceed customer expectations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Customer Journey Mapping Workshop includes the following key components:

- **Pre-Workshop Preparation**: 
  - Conduct a needs assessment with stakeholders to understand current customer engagement challenges.
  - Gather relevant customer data and feedback to inform the workshop discussions.
  
- **Workshop Structure**:
  - Introduction to Customer Journey Mapping concepts and methodologies.
  - Interactive sessions where participants will map out the customer journey using provided templates.
  - Identification of key customer touchpoints and pain points through group discussions and brainstorming sessions.
  - Development of strategies to enhance customer interactions at each touchpoint.
  
- **Post-Workshop Deliverables**:
  - A comprehensive Customer Journey Map that visualizes the entire customer experience.
  - A report summarizing key findings, pain points, and recommended strategies for improvement.
  - An actionable roadmap outlining steps for implementation of the strategies discussed.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Pre-Workshop Preparation
  - Deliverable: Needs assessment report and customer data compilation.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Conducting the Workshop
  - Deliverable: Completed Customer Journey Map and participant feedback forms.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Post-Workshop Analysis and Reporting
  - Deliverable: Final report including the Customer Journey Map, key findings, and actionable roadmap.
