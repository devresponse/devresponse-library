
# Project Plan Template: Press Release Fact-Checking

## 1. Executive Summary

The Press Release Fact-Checking project aims to ensure the utmost accuracy and reliability of press releases that represent our brand to the public and media. In today's fast-paced business environment, misinformation can severely damage a company's reputation and credibility. Our primary objective is to provide a comprehensive review of press releases, meticulously verifying every fact, figure, and claim against credible sources. This project will involve a thorough examination of statistics, quotes, and referenced data by our experienced fact-checkers. The key outcomes of this project include enhanced credibility of press releases, prevention of potential misinformation, and the establishment of trust with our audience and media outlets. By implementing this fact-checking service, we aim to uphold our business's integrity and reputation, allowing us to confidently publish accurate and reliable press releases.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Press Release Fact-Checking project includes the following tasks:

- **Initial Review**: Conduct a preliminary assessment of the press release to identify key claims and data points that require verification.
- **Source Verification**: Cross-check all statistics, quotes, and referenced data against credible sources, including academic journals, reputable news outlets, and official reports.
- **Fact-Checking Report**: Compile a detailed report outlining the findings of the fact-checking process, including any discrepancies or inaccuracies identified.
- **Revisions and Recommendations**: Provide recommendations for revisions to ensure the press release meets accuracy standards and suggest alternative sources if necessary.
- **Final Approval**: Collaborate with the client to finalize the press release after all necessary adjustments have been made based on the fact-checking report.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Review Completion
  - Deliverable: Summary of key claims and data points identified for verification.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Source Verification Completion
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive fact-checking report detailing findings and discrepancies.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Recommendations and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Suggested revisions and alternative sources for any inaccurate claims.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized press release ready for publication.
