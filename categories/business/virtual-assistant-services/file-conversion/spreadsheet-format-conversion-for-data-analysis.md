
# Project Plan Template: Spreadsheet Format Conversion for Data Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Spreadsheet Format Conversion for Data Analysis" project aims to facilitate the seamless transition of data from Excel files to Google Sheets, ensuring that all information remains intact and accessible for effective analysis. In today's data-driven environment, the ability to convert spreadsheets between different formats is essential for businesses seeking to enhance their analytical capabilities and streamline data management processes. This project will involve a comprehensive review of the original Excel files to identify potential issues, including formulas, formatting, and embedded objects that may not translate directly to Google Sheets. The key outcome will be a fully functional Google Sheets document that mirrors the original Excel file, enabling easy collaboration and sharing among team members. By focusing on data integrity and usability, this project will meet all client specifications and support their data analysis needs effectively.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to understand their specific needs and expectations regarding the conversion process.
- **File Review**: Conduct a thorough review of the original Excel files to identify:
  - Formulas that may not convert directly
  - Formatting issues that need to be addressed
  - Embedded objects that require special handling
- **Conversion Process**: 
  - Convert the identified Excel files to Google Sheets format.
  - Ensure that all data, formulas, and formatting are preserved as much as possible.
- **Quality Assurance**: 
  - Perform a detailed comparison between the original Excel file and the converted Google Sheets document to ensure accuracy.
  - Test all formulas and functionalities in the Google Sheets document.
- **Client Review**: Present the converted document to the client for feedback and make any necessary adjustments.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the final Google Sheets document along with a summary report detailing the conversion process and any issues encountered.
