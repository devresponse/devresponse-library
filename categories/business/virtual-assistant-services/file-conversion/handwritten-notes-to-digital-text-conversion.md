
# Project Plan Template: Handwritten Notes to Digital Text Conversion

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Handwritten Notes to Digital Text Conversion" project aims to transform scanned handwritten documents into editable digital text using advanced Optical Character Recognition (OCR) technology. The primary objective is to provide a seamless transition from physical notes to a digital format that enhances accessibility, editing, and sharing capabilities for students, researchers, and professionals. The project will begin with the collection of high-quality scans of handwritten notes, followed by the application of state-of-the-art OCR software to accurately recognize and convert the handwritten content. Key outcomes include a fully editable digital document that meets the client's specifications, ensuring accuracy and proper formatting. This project not only enhances productivity by making handwritten notes more accessible but also preserves valuable information in a digital format for future use. By the end of the project, clients will receive a comprehensive digital archive of their handwritten notes, facilitating easier management and retrieval of information.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Collection of Handwritten Notes**: Gather high-quality scans of handwritten documents from the client.
- **OCR Processing**: Utilize advanced OCR software to convert scanned images into editable text.
- **Quality Assurance**: Conduct thorough reviews of the converted text for accuracy and formatting.
- **Client Review**: Present the converted documents to the client for feedback and necessary revisions.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the final editable digital documents in the requested format (e.g., Word, PDF).
- **Post-Project Support**: Offer a limited period of support for any issues or additional requests related to the converted documents.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Collection of Handwritten Notes
  - Deliverable: High-quality scans received from the client.
  
- **Milestone 2**: OCR Processing Completed
  - Deliverable: Initial conversion of handwritten notes into editable text.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Quality Assurance Review
  - Deliverable: Reviewed and corrected text documents ready for client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Client Review and Feedback
  - Deliverable: Client feedback received and any necessary revisions identified.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery of Documents
  - Deliverable: Final editable digital documents delivered to the client.
