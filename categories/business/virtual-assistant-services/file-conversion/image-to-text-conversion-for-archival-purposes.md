
# Project Plan Template: Image to Text Conversion for Archival Purposes

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Image to Text Conversion for Archival Purposes" project aims to transform scanned images of documents into editable text files, thereby facilitating efficient digital archiving. The primary objective is to ensure accurate extraction of text from images while preserving the integrity of the original content. Utilizing advanced Optical Character Recognition (OCR) technology, the project will convert images into widely-used text formats such as Word and PDF. Key outcomes include the delivery of high-quality, searchable digital documents that enhance accessibility and usability for businesses seeking to digitize their records. The project will encompass a thorough preliminary assessment of the scanned images, a meticulous conversion process, and a comprehensive final quality check to ensure that all deliverables align with the client's specifications. This initiative is essential for organizations aiming to streamline their document management processes and improve operational efficiency.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for this project includes the following key activities:

- **Preliminary Assessment**: Evaluate the quality and condition of the scanned images to determine the best approach for conversion.
- **OCR Conversion**: Utilize OCR technology to convert scanned images into editable text formats (Word, PDF).
- **Quality Assurance**: Conduct a thorough review of the converted text to ensure accuracy, formatting consistency, and adherence to the original document.
- **Client Review**: Present the converted documents to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments based on their input.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the final output in the agreed-upon formats, ensuring all files are organized and easily accessible.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Preliminary Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing the condition of scanned images and recommended conversion approach.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of OCR Conversion
  - Deliverable: Initial batch of converted text files in Word and PDF formats.

- **Milestone 3**: Quality Assurance Review
  - Deliverable: Quality Assurance Report highlighting any discrepancies and corrections made.

- **Milestone 4**: Client Review and Feedback
  - Deliverable: Documented feedback from the client and any requested revisions.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery of Converted Documents
  - Deliverable: Final set of converted documents organized and delivered to the client.
