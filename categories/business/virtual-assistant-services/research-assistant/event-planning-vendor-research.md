
# Project Plan Template: Event Planning Vendor Research

## 1. Executive Summary

The Event Planning Vendor Research project aims to conduct comprehensive research to identify and evaluate potential vendors and venues for upcoming corporate or personal events. The primary objective is to gather detailed data on various options, including catering services, event spaces, audiovisual equipment providers, and entertainment options. This research will encompass critical factors such as pricing, availability, client reviews, and service offerings. The final output will be a comprehensive report that highlights the top choices based on the client's specific requirements and preferences. Key outcomes include providing the client with informed recommendations that align with their event goals, budget, and timeline. By ensuring a thorough vetting process, the Research Assistant will facilitate a seamless event planning experience, ultimately contributing to the success of the client’s event.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Identify potential vendors and venues for corporate and personal events.
  - Gather data on catering services, event spaces, audiovisual equipment providers, and entertainment options.
  - Evaluate vendors based on pricing, availability, client reviews, and service offerings.

- **Analysis Phase**: 
  - Analyze gathered data to determine the best options that meet the client's specific requirements.
  - Create a comparison matrix to visualize differences among vendors.

- **Reporting Phase**: 
  - Compile findings into a comprehensive report.
  - Highlight top choices and provide detailed descriptions of each vendor, including pros and cons.

- **Recommendations**: 
  - Offer insights and recommendations to assist the client in the selection process.
  - Suggest next steps for contacting and negotiating with selected vendors.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of initial vendor identification
  - Deliverable: List of potential vendors and venues

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of data gathering
  - Deliverable: Data compilation document with pricing, availability, and reviews

- **Milestone 3**: Analysis and comparison of vendors
  - Deliverable: Comparison matrix of vendor options

- **Milestone 4**: Final report preparation
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report with top choices and recommendations

- **Milestone 5**: Presentation of findings to the client
  - Deliverable: Presentation summarizing key findings and recommendations
