
# Project Plan Template: Daily Email Management

## 1. Executive Summary

The Daily Email Management project aims to provide comprehensive support in managing email communications effectively. The primary objective is to organize the client's inbox by categorizing emails into actionable items and prioritizing them based on urgency and importance. This service will include responding to routine inquiries on behalf of the client, ensuring that critical communications are never missed while maintaining a clutter-free inbox. Key outcomes of this project include enhanced productivity, improved workflow, and the implementation of a flagging system for emails that require the client's personal attention. By executing this project on a daily basis, we will ensure consistent and efficient email management, allowing the client to focus on more strategic tasks without the distraction of overwhelming email volumes.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Email Categorization**: Organize incoming emails into predefined categories such as Action Required, Follow-Up, and Information Only.
- **Prioritization**: Assess the urgency and importance of each email and prioritize them accordingly.
- **Routine Responses**: Develop and implement standard responses for frequently asked questions to streamline communication.
- **Flagging System**: Create a system to flag emails that require the client's personal attention, ensuring they are easily identifiable.
- **Daily Review**: Conduct a daily review of the inbox to ensure all emails are addressed and categorized appropriately.
- **Reporting**: Provide weekly reports summarizing email management activities, including the number of emails processed and outstanding items.

  - Deliverable: Fully functional flagging system for critical emails.
