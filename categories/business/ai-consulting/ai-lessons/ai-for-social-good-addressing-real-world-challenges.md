
# Project Plan Template: AI for Social Good: Solving Real-World Problems

## 1. Executive Summary

The project "AI for Social Good: Addressing Real-World Challenges" aims to leverage artificial intelligence to confront and resolve critical global issues, including climate change, poverty, and educational disparities. The primary objective is to engage participants in a series of in-depth case studies that not only highlight these pressing problems but also explore innovative AI-driven solutions. Participants will work collaboratively in teams to analyze relevant data, develop algorithms, and formulate actionable strategies that can be implemented to create a positive social impact. Key outcomes of this project include enhanced AI competencies among participants, the development of practical solutions to real-world challenges, and a comprehensive presentation of findings that showcases the potential of AI technologies for societal benefit. This initiative is designed to instill a sense of responsibility and commitment towards addressing societal challenges, making it a unique and impactful learning experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the "AI for Social Good" project includes the following key components:

- **Research and Analysis**: Participants will conduct thorough research on selected global issues, gathering data and insights to understand the complexities involved.
- **Case Studies**: Development of comprehensive case studies that illustrate real-world challenges and the potential role of AI in addressing these issues.
- **Team Collaboration**: Formation of diverse teams to encourage collaboration, idea sharing, and collective problem-solving.
- **Algorithm Development**: Participants will design and develop algorithms tailored to the specific challenges identified in their case studies.
- **Strategy Formulation**: Creation of actionable strategies that outline how the proposed AI solutions can be implemented effectively in real-world scenarios.
- **Presentation of Findings**: A final presentation where teams will showcase their research, solutions, and the potential impact of their work on society.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Project Kick-off Meeting
  - Deliverable: Project charter and team assignments
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research reports on selected global issues
- **Milestone 3**: Case Study Development
  - Deliverable: Completed case studies for each team
- **Milestone 4**: Algorithm Development
  - Deliverable: Functional algorithms addressing the identified challenges
- **Milestone 5**: Strategy Formulation
  - Deliverable: Actionable strategy documents
- **Milestone 6**: Final Presentation
  - Deliverable: Presentation slides and a summary report of findings
