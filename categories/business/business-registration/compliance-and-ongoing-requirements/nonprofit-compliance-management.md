
# Project Plan Template: Nonprofit Compliance Management

## 1. Executive Summary

The Nonprofit Compliance Management project aims to provide tailored compliance management services specifically designed for nonprofit organizations. The primary objective is to navigate the complexities of compliance, ensuring that nonprofits can focus on their mission while we manage the intricacies of regulatory requirements. This project will encompass the preparation of IRS Form 990, ensuring that all financial information is accurately reported and compliant with federal regulations. Additionally, we will assist with necessary state filings to meet local compliance requirements. Key outcomes include a thorough review of operational practices, guidance on maintaining compliance with ongoing regulatory changes, and the establishment of internal processes for continuous adherence to legal standards. By partnering with us, nonprofits will benefit from our expertise, allowing them to operate confidently within the legal framework and avoid potential penalties.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **IRS Form 990 Preparation**: 
  - Gather financial data and documentation from the nonprofit.
  - Prepare and review IRS Form 990 to ensure accuracy and compliance.
  - Submit the completed form to the IRS by the required deadline.

- **State Filings**: 
  - Identify necessary state compliance requirements based on the nonprofit's location.
  - Prepare and submit all required state filings to ensure local compliance.

- **Operational Review**: 
  - Conduct a comprehensive review of the nonprofit's operational practices.
  - Identify areas of improvement and provide recommendations for compliance enhancement.

- **Ongoing Compliance Guidance**: 
  - Monitor changes in federal and state regulations affecting nonprofits.
  - Provide updates and guidance to the nonprofit on maintaining compliance.

- **Internal Process Establishment**: 
  - Assist in developing internal processes and policies to ensure ongoing compliance.
  - Provide training and resources for staff to understand compliance requirements.
