
# Project Plan Template: Employee Classification Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

The Employee Classification Audit is a critical initiative aimed at ensuring that all employees within {{Organization Name}} are accurately classified in accordance with applicable labor laws. The primary objective of this project is to conduct a comprehensive review of current employee roles, responsibilities, and compensation structures to identify any potential misclassifications that may expose the organization to legal risks and penalties. This audit will involve a meticulous examination of job descriptions, employment contracts, and payroll records, ensuring compliance with both federal and state regulations. Key outcomes of the project will include a detailed report outlining any discrepancies found, along with actionable recommendations for rectifying these issues. By the conclusion of this audit, {{Organization Name}} will possess a clear understanding of its employee classifications and the necessary steps to maintain ongoing compliance, thereby mitigating risks associated with labor law violations and enhancing operational integrity.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Employee Classification Audit includes the following key activities:

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a preliminary review of existing employee classifications and gather relevant documentation, including job descriptions, employment contracts, and payroll records.
  
- **Data Analysis**: Analyze the collected data to identify potential misclassifications based on federal and state labor laws. This will involve comparing job responsibilities and compensation structures against legal classification criteria.
  
- **Interviews and Surveys**: Engage with department heads and employees to gather insights on job functions and responsibilities, ensuring a comprehensive understanding of each role.
  
- **Compliance Review**: Evaluate the organization’s current classification practices against legal standards to identify gaps and areas for improvement.
  
- **Reporting**: Prepare a detailed audit report that outlines findings, identifies misclassifications, and provides actionable recommendations for compliance.
  
- **Follow-Up Consultation**: Offer a follow-up consultation to discuss the audit findings and assist in implementing recommended changes.
