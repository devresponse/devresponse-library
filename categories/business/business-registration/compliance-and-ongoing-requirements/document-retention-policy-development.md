
# Project Plan Template: Document Retention Policy Setup

## 1. Executive Summary

The Document Retention Policy Setup project aims to establish a comprehensive framework for managing organizational documents in compliance with legal and regulatory requirements. In today's complex regulatory landscape, businesses must ensure that they retain necessary documents while securely disposing of those that are no longer needed. This project will involve an initial consultation to assess the specific needs and operations of {{Organization Name}}, followed by the development of a tailored document retention policy. Key outcomes include the identification of document types, retention durations, and secure disposal procedures. Furthermore, we will provide implementation guidance and staff training to foster adherence to the policy. By mitigating legal risks and enhancing operational efficiency, this project will empower {{Organization Name}} to manage its documentation effectively, ensuring compliance and promoting best practices in document management.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a thorough assessment of {{Organization Name}}'s current document management practices, regulatory obligations, and business operations.
  
- **Policy Development**: Create a detailed document retention policy that includes:
  - Identification of document types to be retained (e.g., financial records, employee files, contracts).
  - Specification of retention periods for each document type based on legal requirements and best practices.
  - Procedures for secure disposal of documents that are no longer needed, including methods for shredding or digital deletion.
  
- **Implementation Guidance**: Provide a step-by-step plan for implementing the document retention policy within {{Organization Name}}.
  
- **Training Sessions**: Develop and conduct training sessions for staff to ensure understanding and adherence to the new policy.

- **Review and Feedback**: Establish a mechanism for periodic review of the policy and gather feedback from staff to ensure continuous improvement.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Summary report of current document management practices and regulatory obligations.

- **Milestone 2**: Draft Document Retention Policy
  - Deliverable: Draft version of the document retention policy for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Final Document Retention Policy
  - Deliverable: Finalized document retention policy document.

- **Milestone 4**: Implementation Plan
  - Deliverable: Detailed implementation plan for the policy.

- **Milestone 5**: Staff Training Completion
  - Deliverable: Training materials and confirmation of training sessions conducted.

- **Milestone 6**: Policy Review Mechanism Established
  - Deliverable: Document outlining the review process and feedback collection.
