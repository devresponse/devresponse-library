
# Project Plan Template: S Corporation Election Filing

## 1. Executive Summary

The S Corporation Election Filing project aims to assist businesses in successfully navigating the complex process of electing S Corporation status. The primary objective is to ensure that clients understand the eligibility requirements set forth by the IRS and to facilitate the accurate completion and timely submission of Form 2553. Our expert team will provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide, addressing all necessary information and potential issues that may arise during the process. Key outcomes of this project include a thorough preliminary assessment of the business's eligibility for S Corporation status, ensuring compliance with all legal requirements, and enhancing operational efficiency. By the conclusion of this project, clients will possess a clear understanding of the S Corporation election process, empowering them to make informed decisions regarding their business structure.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Preliminary Assessment**: Conduct an initial evaluation of the client's business to determine eligibility for S Corporation status based on IRS guidelines.
- **Client Consultation**: Schedule meetings with clients to discuss their business structure, goals, and any concerns regarding the S Corporation election process.
- **Form 2553 Preparation**: Assist in the accurate completion of IRS Form 2553, ensuring all required information is included and correctly filled out.
- **Submission Process**: Guide clients through the submission process, including determining the appropriate filing method (mail or electronic) and deadlines.
- **Follow-Up**: Provide follow-up support to address any questions or issues that arise after submission, including communication with the IRS if necessary.
- **Documentation**: Supply clients with a comprehensive guide outlining the S Corporation election process, eligibility requirements, and ongoing compliance obligations.

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Preliminary Assessment
