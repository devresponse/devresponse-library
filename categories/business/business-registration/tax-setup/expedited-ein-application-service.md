
# Expedited EIN Application Service Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Expedited EIN Application Service aims to provide businesses, particularly startups and small enterprises, with a swift and efficient process for obtaining their Employer Identification Number (EIN). The primary objective of this project is to streamline the EIN application process, ensuring that clients receive their EIN on the same day of application submission. This service is crucial for businesses that need to establish their tax structure promptly to comply with federal regulations. Key outcomes include a thorough review of all necessary documentation, timely submission of the application, and a high success rate in obtaining EINs without delays. By leveraging our expertise, clients can focus on their core business activities while we handle the complexities of the EIN application process, ultimately providing them with peace of mind and a faster path to compliance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a detailed consultation with the client to gather necessary information for the EIN application.
- **Documentation Review**: Review all required documentation to ensure completeness and accuracy.
- **Application Preparation**: Prepare the EIN application form (Form SS-4) with the information provided by the client.
- **Same-Day Submission**: Submit the EIN application on the same day as the consultation.
- **Follow-Up**: Provide follow-up communication to the client regarding the status of their application.
- **EIN Delivery**: Ensure the client receives their EIN promptly, typically within 24 hours of submission.
- **Customer Support**: Offer ongoing support for any questions or issues that may arise post-application.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Completed client information form and documentation checklist.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Documentation Review Completed
  - Deliverable: Reviewed and approved documentation ready for application preparation.
  
- **Milestone 3**: EIN Application Prepared
  - Deliverable: Completed Form SS-4 ready for submission.
  
- **Milestone 4**: EIN Application Submitted
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of application submission to the IRS.
  
- **Milestone 5**: EIN Received by Client
  - Deliverable: EIN provided to the client along with a summary of the application process.
