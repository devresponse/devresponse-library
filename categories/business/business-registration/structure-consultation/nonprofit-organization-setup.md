
# Project Plan Template: Nonprofit Organization Setup

## 1. Executive Summary

The Nonprofit Organization Setup project aims to provide comprehensive support for clients seeking to establish a nonprofit entity. The primary objective is to guide clients through the complex process of nonprofit formation, ensuring they have the necessary knowledge and resources to operate effectively within legal frameworks. This project will begin with an initial consultation to understand the client's mission and vision, allowing us to tailor our advice to their specific needs. Key outcomes include the selection of an appropriate legal structure, preparation and submission of registration documents, and assistance in applying for tax-exempt status. By the end of this project, clients will be equipped to successfully launch their nonprofit organization, contributing to their mission-driven goals while remaining compliant with state and federal regulations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their mission, vision, and goals for the nonprofit organization.
- **Legal Structure Selection**: Provide detailed advice on the various legal structures available for nonprofits, including 501(c)(3) status, and help the client select the most suitable option.
- **Document Preparation**: Assist in the preparation of all necessary registration documents, including Articles of Incorporation and Bylaws.
- **Submission of Documents**: Guide the client through the submission process for state and federal registration, ensuring all forms are completed accurately and submitted on time.
- **Tax-Exempt Status Application**: Provide support in completing the IRS Form 1023 or 1023-EZ for tax-exempt status, including a thorough review of the application to ensure compliance with IRS requirements.
- **Ongoing Support**: Offer additional resources and support for the client as they navigate the early stages of their nonprofit operation, including compliance with ongoing reporting requirements.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary report of the consultation, including client’s mission and vision.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Legal Structure Selected
  - Deliverable: Document outlining the recommended legal structure and rationale.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Registration Documents Prepared
  - Deliverable: Completed Articles of Incorporation and Bylaws.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Submission of Registration Documents
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of submission and any correspondence from state or federal agencies.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Tax-Exempt Status Application Submitted
  - Deliverable: Copy of the submitted IRS Form 1023 or 1023-EZ and confirmation of receipt from the IRS.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Ongoing Support Initiated
  - Deliverable: Resource guide for compliance and operational best practices.
