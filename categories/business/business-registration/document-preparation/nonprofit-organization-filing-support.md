
# Project Plan Template: Nonprofit Organization Filing Support

## 1. Executive Summary

The Nonprofit Organization Filing Support project aims to provide comprehensive assistance to individuals and groups seeking to establish a nonprofit organization. Our primary objective is to ensure that all necessary documentation is accurately prepared and filed, facilitating a smooth registration process. This includes drafting the Articles of Incorporation, which define the organization's purpose and structure, and completing IRS Form 1023, crucial for obtaining tax-exempt status. By leveraging our expertise, clients will minimize the risk of errors that could delay their registration, allowing them to focus on their mission and community impact. Key outcomes of this project include timely submission of all required documents, enhanced understanding of nonprofit compliance, and a streamlined process that empowers clients to launch their organizations effectively.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a thorough consultation with the client to understand their vision, mission, and specific needs for their nonprofit organization.
  
- **Document Preparation**:
  - Draft the Articles of Incorporation, ensuring alignment with state requirements and the organization's mission.
  - Complete IRS Form 1023, providing guidance on the necessary supporting documentation and financial information.
  
- **Review and Revisions**: Collaborate with the client to review all documents, making necessary revisions based on feedback to ensure accuracy and compliance.

- **Filing Assistance**: Submit the completed Articles of Incorporation to the appropriate state agency and IRS Form 1023 to the IRS, ensuring all deadlines are met.

- **Post-Filing Support**: Provide follow-up assistance to address any inquiries from state or federal agencies regarding the submitted documents.

- **Educational Resources**: Offer resources and materials to educate the client about ongoing compliance requirements and best practices for nonprofit management.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary report of client’s needs and objectives.

- **Milestone 2**: Draft Articles of Incorporation
  - Deliverable: Draft document for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Complete IRS Form 1023
  - Deliverable: Completed form ready for submission.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Review and Approval
  - Deliverable: Finalized documents approved by the client.

- **Milestone 5**: Submission of Documents
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of submission to state agency and IRS.

- **Milestone 6**: Post-Filing Support
  - Deliverable: Follow-up report addressing any inquiries from agencies.
