
# Project Plan Template: DBA (Doing Business As) Filing Assistance

## 1. Executive Summary

The DBA (Doing Business As) Filing Assistance project aims to provide comprehensive support for businesses seeking to register a DBA name. The primary objective is to streamline the registration process, allowing clients to focus on their core business activities while we handle the necessary paperwork efficiently and accurately. This project will include a thorough consultation to understand the client's unique business needs and the specific name they wish to register. Our experienced professionals will prepare all required documentation, ensuring compliance with state and local regulations. Key outcomes of this project include successful DBA name registration, a seamless filing process, and the assurance that clients' desired names are available and compliant with legal standards. By choosing our service, clients can expect timely completion of their registration without unnecessary delays or complications, ultimately enhancing their business operations and branding efforts.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Services**: Conduct an initial consultation to gather information about the client's business and desired DBA name.
- **Name Availability Search**: Perform a thorough search to confirm that the desired DBA name is not already in use or registered.
- **Documentation Preparation**: Prepare all necessary documentation required for DBA registration, ensuring compliance with state and local regulations.
- **Filing Assistance**: Guide clients through the filing process, including submission of documents to the appropriate authorities.
- **Ongoing Support**: Provide ongoing support to address any questions or challenges that may arise during the registration process.
- **Final Confirmation**: Confirm successful registration of the DBA name and provide clients with all relevant documentation.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Consultation summary report
- **Milestone 2**: Name Availability Search Completed
  - Deliverable: Name availability report
- **Milestone 3**: Documentation Prepared
  - Deliverable: Completed DBA registration forms
- **Milestone 4**: Filing Submitted
  - Deliverable: Confirmation of filing submission
- **Milestone 5**: Registration Confirmation Received
  - Deliverable: Official DBA registration certificate
