
# Impact Measurement Business Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Impact Measurement Business Plan project aims to develop a comprehensive business plan tailored specifically for non-profit organizations focused on measuring and reporting their social impact. The primary objective of this project is to provide a structured framework that enables non-profits to effectively assess and communicate their social contributions. This will involve a thorough analysis of the organization's mission, objectives, and existing initiatives, ensuring alignment with best practices in impact measurement. Key outcomes will include the establishment of clear metrics for evaluating success, the integration of established frameworks such as the Logic Model, Theory of Change, and Social Return on Investment (SROI), and the creation of a guiding document that enhances operational strategies. Ultimately, this project will empower non-profits to track their progress, secure funding, and demonstrate their value to the community they serve, fostering greater transparency and accountability.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Conduct a kickoff meeting with stakeholders to outline project objectives and expectations.
  - Gather initial documentation related to the organization's mission and current impact measurement practices.

- **Needs Assessment**
  - Perform interviews and surveys with key stakeholders to identify current challenges in measuring impact.
  - Analyze existing data and reports to understand the organization's current impact measurement framework.

- **Framework Development**
  - Research and select appropriate frameworks (Logic Model, Theory of Change, SROI) that align with the organization's goals.
  - Develop a customized impact measurement framework that incorporates selected methodologies.

- **Metrics Identification**
  - Collaborate with stakeholders to define key performance indicators (KPIs) for measuring social impact.
  - Establish baseline data for each KPI to facilitate future evaluations.

- **Drafting the Business Plan**
  - Compile findings and recommendations into a comprehensive business plan document.
  - Include sections on strategic direction, operational strategies, and funding opportunities.

- **Review and Feedback**
  - Present the draft business plan to stakeholders for feedback and revisions.
  - Incorporate feedback and finalize the business plan.

- **Final Deliverable**
  - Deliver the completed Impact Measurement Business Plan to the organization.
  - Provide a presentation to stakeholders summarizing key components of the plan.
