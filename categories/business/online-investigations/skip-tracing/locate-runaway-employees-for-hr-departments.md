
# Project Plan Template: Locate Runaway Employees for HR Departments

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's competitive business environment, the sudden disappearance of employees can pose significant challenges for HR departments, particularly when it involves potential theft or breach of contract. The objective of this project is to utilize advanced skip tracing techniques to locate runaway employees who have left their positions under questionable circumstances. Our project will employ a combination of digital tools, public records, and investigative methodologies to gather pertinent information about the individual's whereabouts. Key outcomes include a comprehensive report detailing our findings, which will encompass last known addresses, social media activity, and any other relevant data that can assist in resolving the situation. By recovering company property and ensuring that contractual obligations are upheld, this project aims to protect the interests of the organization and mitigate risks associated with employee misconduct. 

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the HR department to understand the specific circumstances surrounding the employee's departure and gather any available information.
- **Data Collection**: Utilize digital tools and public records to collect data on the employee, including but not limited to:
  - Last known addresses
  - Employment history
  - Social media profiles
  - Financial records (if applicable)
- **Investigation**: Conduct a thorough investigation using skip tracing techniques to locate the employee. This may involve:
  - Contacting known associates
  - Reviewing public records
  - Utilizing specialized databases
- **Reporting**: Compile a comprehensive report that includes:
  - Summary of findings
  - Last known addresses
  - Social media activity
  - Recommendations for next steps
- **Follow-Up**: Provide a follow-up consultation with the HR department to discuss the findings and potential actions to take.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and initial information gathered
- **Milestone 2**: Data Collection Phase Completed
  - Deliverable: List of collected data points and sources
- **Milestone 3**: Investigation Phase Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of investigative actions taken
- **Milestone 4**: Comprehensive Report Delivered
  - Deliverable: Final report detailing findings and recommendations
- **Milestone 5**: Follow-Up Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and action plan based on findings
