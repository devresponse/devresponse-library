
# Project Plan Template: Locate Former Clients for Financial Advisors

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Locate Former Clients for Financial Advisors" project aims to enhance client engagement strategies for financial advisors by providing a specialized skip tracing service. In the competitive landscape of financial advising, maintaining relationships with former clients is crucial for ongoing success. This project will utilize advanced investigative techniques and digital tools to locate individuals who may have changed their contact information or relocated. Our objectives include delivering accurate and comprehensive contact details, such as updated phone numbers, email addresses, and physical addresses. By reconnecting financial advisors with their past clients, we aim to facilitate portfolio updates and new investment opportunities, ultimately driving business growth. Key outcomes will include increased client retention, improved communication, and enhanced overall client satisfaction.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Methodology**: Utilize a combination of public records, social media platforms, and proprietary databases to gather information on former clients.
- **Data Collection**: Compile updated contact information, including phone numbers, email addresses, and physical addresses.
- **Verification Process**: Implement a verification process to ensure the accuracy of the gathered data through multiple sources.
- **Reporting**: Provide a comprehensive report detailing the findings, including a summary of the methods used and the accuracy of the information obtained.
- **Client Consultation**: Offer a consultation session with financial advisors to discuss the findings and strategize on re-engagement with former clients.
- **Follow-up Support**: Provide follow-up support for a specified period to assist financial advisors in their outreach efforts.
