
# Feature Prioritization Workshop Project Plan

## Executive Summary
The Feature Prioritization Workshop aims to unite key stakeholders from {{Organization Name}} to collaboratively assess and prioritize product features that align with customer needs and business objectives. This interactive session will leverage established prioritization frameworks, including the MoSCoW method and the RICE scoring model, to evaluate potential features based on their impact, feasibility, and alignment with strategic goals. Participants will engage in meaningful discussions that incorporate market trends and customer feedback, ensuring that the prioritization process is comprehensive and reflective of user expectations. The key outcomes of this workshop will include a clear roadmap of prioritized features, which will guide product development efforts and ensure that resources are allocated effectively to high-impact areas. Ultimately, this workshop will enhance product value and drive growth by focusing on features that matter most to our users and the business.

## Detailed Scope of Work
The scope of the Feature Prioritization Workshop includes the following key activities:
1. **Preparation Phase**:
   - Identify and invite key stakeholders from various departments, including product management, marketing, sales, and customer support.
   - Gather preliminary data on customer feedback, market trends, and existing product features.
   - Develop a workshop agenda that outlines the structure and flow of the session.

2. **Workshop Execution**:
   - Facilitate the workshop, guiding participants through the prioritization frameworks (MoSCoW and RICE).
   - Lead discussions to evaluate features based on customer needs, business objectives, and technical feasibility.
   - Document insights and decisions made during the workshop.

3. **Post-Workshop Activities**:
   - Compile and analyze the results of the prioritization exercise.
   - Create a prioritized feature roadmap that reflects the outcomes of the workshop.
   - Present the final roadmap to stakeholders for feedback and approval.
