
# Project Plan Template: Customer Journey Mapping

## 1. Executive Summary

The Customer Journey Mapping project aims to provide a comprehensive visualization of how customers engage with {{Product Name}} at every touchpoint. The primary objective is to collect and analyze data on customer interactions, behaviors, and feedback to create a detailed map that highlights key stages in the customer journey. This project will involve conducting stakeholder interviews, user research, and competitive analysis to ensure that the journey map accurately reflects real-world interactions. By identifying pain points and opportunities for improvement, we aim to enhance the overall user experience and drive customer satisfaction. Key outcomes will include a visually engaging and actionable customer journey map, along with tailored recommendations for optimizing each stage of the journey to better meet customer needs and expectations. Ultimately, this project seeks to empower stakeholders with insights that can lead to strategic enhancements in product offerings and customer engagement strategies.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Stakeholder Interviews**: Conduct interviews with key stakeholders to gather insights on customer interactions and expectations.
- **User Research**: Perform qualitative and quantitative research to understand customer behaviors and preferences.
- **Competitive Analysis**: Analyze competitors’ customer journey strategies to identify best practices and areas for differentiation.
- **Data Collection**: Gather data from various sources, including surveys, analytics, and customer feedback.
- **Journey Mapping**: Create a detailed customer journey map that outlines each stage of the customer experience, including touchpoints, emotions, and pain points.
- **Recommendations**: Develop actionable recommendations for optimizing each stage of the journey based on findings.
- **Final Presentation**: Present the customer journey map and recommendations to stakeholders in a visually engaging format.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of stakeholder interviews
  - **Deliverable**: Summary report of insights gathered from interviews
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of user research
  - **Deliverable**: User research findings report
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of competitive analysis
  - **Deliverable**: Competitive analysis report
- **Milestone 4**: Data collection and analysis
  - **Deliverable**: Comprehensive data analysis report
- **Milestone 5**: Development of the customer journey map
  - **Deliverable**: Visual customer journey map
- **Milestone 6**: Final recommendations and presentation
  - **Deliverable**: Presentation of the customer journey map and recommendations to stakeholders
