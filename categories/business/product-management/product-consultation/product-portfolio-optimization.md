
# Project Plan Template: Product Portfolio Optimization

## 1. Executive Summary

The Product Portfolio Optimization project aims to deliver a comprehensive analysis of your current product offerings, ensuring they are strategically aligned with your overarching business goals. This project will involve a detailed review of your product portfolio, identifying underperforming products that may be limiting your growth potential. Our expert consultants will leverage market trends, customer feedback, and competitive positioning to uncover opportunities for enhancement and innovation. The key outcomes of this project will include a strategic roadmap that outlines actionable recommendations for product optimization, such as potential product discontinuations, enhancements, or new product introductions. Ultimately, this project seeks to empower your business with data-driven insights, enabling informed decision-making that enhances product value and drives sustainable growth.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a kickoff meeting with stakeholders to understand business goals and expectations.
  
- **Data Collection**: Gather relevant data on current product offerings, including sales performance, customer feedback, and market trends.

- **Market Analysis**: Analyze industry trends and competitive positioning to identify gaps and opportunities in the product portfolio.

- **Product Performance Review**: Evaluate each product's performance against key metrics to identify underperformers.

- **Stakeholder Interviews**: Conduct interviews with key stakeholders to gather qualitative insights on product perceptions and potential improvements.

- **Recommendations Development**: Develop actionable recommendations for product optimization, including:
  - Product discontinuations
  - Product enhancements
  - New product introductions

- **Strategic Roadmap Creation**: Create a detailed strategic roadmap that outlines the recommended actions, timelines, and responsible parties.

- **Final Presentation**: Present findings and recommendations to stakeholders, ensuring clarity and alignment on the next steps.
