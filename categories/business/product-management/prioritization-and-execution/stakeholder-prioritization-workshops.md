
# Project Plan Template: Stakeholder Prioritization Workshops

## 1. Executive Summary

The Stakeholder Prioritization Workshops aim to create a structured and collaborative environment for key stakeholders to engage in meaningful discussions around product initiatives. The primary objective of this project is to facilitate a series of workshops that will allow participants to share their insights, concerns, and expectations regarding product development. By employing guided discussions and interactive activities, we will work towards identifying critical product features and initiatives that align with the overarching business objectives. The key outcomes of this project will include a prioritized list of product initiatives, bolstered by stakeholder feedback and consensus. This process not only enhances stakeholder engagement but also ensures that resources are allocated effectively to initiatives that deliver the highest value to the organization. Ultimately, the workshops will culminate in a comprehensive report that summarizes the prioritized initiatives and the rationale behind them, providing a clear roadmap for the product development team to follow.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Stakeholder Prioritization Workshops includes the following key activities:

- **Workshop Planning**: Define the objectives, agenda, and participant list for each workshop session.
- **Stakeholder Identification**: Collaborate with project sponsors to identify key stakeholders who will participate in the workshops.
- **Facilitation of Workshops**: Conduct a series of interactive workshops, utilizing techniques such as brainstorming, dot voting, and group discussions to gather insights and prioritize initiatives.
- **Documentation**: Capture discussions, decisions, and feedback during each workshop session.
- **Analysis and Reporting**: Analyze the collected data to create a prioritized list of product initiatives, along with a rationale for each priority.
- **Final Presentation**: Present the findings and the comprehensive report to stakeholders and the product development team.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Workshop Planning
  - Deliverable: Workshop agenda and participant list
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Stakeholder Identification
  - Deliverable: Finalized list of stakeholders
- **Milestone 3**: Conducting Workshops
  - Deliverable: Workshop session notes and feedback
- **Milestone 4**: Analysis of Workshop Outcomes
  - Deliverable: Draft prioritized list of product initiatives
- **Milestone 5**: Final Report and Presentation
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report summarizing prioritized initiatives and rationale
