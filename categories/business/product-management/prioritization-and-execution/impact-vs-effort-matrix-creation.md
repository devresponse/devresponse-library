
# Project Plan Template: Impact vs. Effort Matrix Creation

## 1. Executive Summary

The Impact vs. Effort Matrix Creation project aims to equip product teams with a strategic visual tool that enhances their ability to prioritize tasks based on potential impact and required effort. The primary objective is to create a matrix that allows teams to identify high-impact, low-effort tasks that can significantly drive business value. This project will involve comprehensive stakeholder engagement to gather insights on priorities, followed by the design of an intuitive matrix layout. Key outcomes include a finalized matrix that serves as a decision-making framework, along with guidance on its effective use in product management meetings. By the conclusion of this project, teams will possess a clear and actionable matrix that streamlines ongoing product development discussions, ensuring efficient resource allocation and timely execution of critical tasks. This initiative is expected to enhance productivity and focus within teams, ultimately leading to improved product outcomes.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Stakeholder Engagement**: Conduct interviews and surveys with key stakeholders to gather insights on their priorities and expectations for the matrix.
- **Matrix Design**: Develop a visually appealing and functional layout for the Impact vs. Effort Matrix that is easy to understand and use.
- **Guidance Documentation**: Create a comprehensive guide on how to utilize the matrix effectively in product management meetings, including best practices and examples.
- **Feedback Loop**: Implement a feedback mechanism to refine the matrix based on initial usage and stakeholder input.
- **Training Session**: Organize a training session for teams to ensure they understand how to leverage the matrix in their workflows.
- **Final Review and Adjustments**: Conduct a final review of the matrix and documentation, making necessary adjustments based on feedback received.
