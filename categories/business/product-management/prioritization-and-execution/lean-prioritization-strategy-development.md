
# Lean Prioritization Strategy Development Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Lean Prioritization Strategy Development project aims to create a comprehensive framework that enables organizations to maximize product impact while minimizing waste. In today's fast-paced business environment, it is crucial for companies to focus on high-impact product features that align with their strategic objectives. This project will involve a thorough analysis of current product offerings, gathering input from key stakeholders, and employing lean methodologies to prioritize features based on their potential value. The key outcomes of this project will include a prioritized feature list, a detailed roadmap for implementation, and guidelines for ongoing prioritization processes. By the end of this project, clients will have a clear and actionable strategy that ensures their product development efforts are efficient, effective, and aligned with their overarching business goals.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Lean Prioritization Strategy Development project includes the following key activities:

- **Current Product Analysis**: Review and analyze existing product offerings to identify strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities for improvement.
- **Stakeholder Engagement**: Conduct interviews and workshops with key stakeholders to gather insights and input on product features and priorities.
- **Lean Methodology Application**: Utilize lean methodologies to assess and prioritize product features based on their potential value and alignment with business objectives.
- **Feature Prioritization**: Develop a prioritized list of product features that will deliver the highest impact.
- **Roadmap Development**: Create a detailed roadmap for the implementation of prioritized features, including timelines and resource allocation.
- **Guidelines Creation**: Develop guidelines for ongoing prioritization processes to ensure continuous alignment with business goals.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current Product Analysis  
  **Deliverable**: Analysis report outlining strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities.

- **Milestone 2**: Stakeholder Engagement Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Summary report of stakeholder insights and input.

- **Milestone 3**: Lean Methodology Assessment  
  **Deliverable**: Document detailing the application of lean methodologies and resulting prioritization criteria.

- **Milestone 4**: Prioritized Feature List Developed  
  **Deliverable**: A comprehensive list of prioritized product features.

- **Milestone 5**: Roadmap for Implementation Created  
  **Deliverable**: A detailed roadmap outlining timelines and resource allocation for feature implementation.

- **Milestone 6**: Guidelines for Ongoing Prioritization Established  
  **Deliverable**: A set of guidelines for maintaining alignment with business goals in future prioritization efforts.
