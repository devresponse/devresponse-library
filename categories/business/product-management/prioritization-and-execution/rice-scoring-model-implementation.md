
# Project Plan Template: RICE Scoring Implementation

## 1. Executive Summary

The RICE Scoring Implementation project aims to enhance our product prioritization process by integrating the RICE scoring model into our organizational framework. The primary objective is to systematically evaluate potential product initiatives based on four key factors: Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort. This project will begin with a comprehensive analysis of our current product initiatives, gathering relevant data to inform our scoring system. By quantifying these factors, we will create a clear and objective comparison of initiatives, enabling informed decision-making. The key outcomes of this project include a detailed report that prioritizes initiatives based on their RICE scores and actionable recommendations for execution. Ultimately, this structured approach will ensure that resources are allocated effectively, maximizing the potential for successful product outcomes and aligning our product strategy with organizational goals.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Phase 1: Current State Analysis**
  - Conduct a thorough review of existing product initiatives.
  - Gather relevant data on Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort for each initiative.
  - Identify gaps in data and areas for improvement.

- **Phase 2: Development of RICE Scoring System**
  - Create a scoring system that quantifies Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort.
  - Develop guidelines for scoring each initiative consistently.
  - Pilot the scoring system with a select group of initiatives to validate its effectiveness.

- **Phase 3: Implementation and Training**
  - Roll out the RICE scoring model across the organization.
  - Conduct training sessions for product managers and relevant stakeholders.
  - Provide ongoing support and resources for effective implementation.

- **Phase 4: Reporting and Recommendations**
  - Compile a comprehensive report detailing the prioritized initiatives based on RICE scores.
  - Include actionable recommendations for execution.
  - Present findings to key stakeholders and gather feedback.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Completion of Current State Analysis**
  - Deliverable: Analysis report outlining existing initiatives and data gaps.

- **Milestone 2: Development of RICE Scoring System**
  - Deliverable: Document detailing the scoring system and guidelines.

- **Milestone 3: Training and Implementation**
  - Deliverable: Training materials and session completion report.

- **Milestone 4: Final Report and Recommendations**
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report with prioritized initiatives and execution recommendations.
