
# Project Plan Template: Competitor Analysis for Feature Prioritization

## 1. Executive Summary
The "Competitor Analysis for Feature Prioritization" project aims to equip our product management team with critical insights into the competitive landscape. In today's fast-paced market, understanding competitors is essential for effective product management and strategic decision-making. This project will conduct a comprehensive analysis of key competitors, focusing on their product features, pricing strategies, and customer feedback. By identifying gaps in the current market offerings, we will prioritize product features that not only address these gaps but also provide a competitive advantage. The key outcomes of this project will include a detailed report outlining our findings, strategic recommendations for feature prioritization, and actionable insights for execution. Ultimately, this project will enable our team to make informed decisions that align with our business goals and enhance our product's market position.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work
- **Competitor Identification**: Identify and select key competitors in the market relevant to our product category.
- **Data Collection**: Gather data on competitors’ product features, pricing strategies, and customer feedback through various methods such as surveys, reviews, and market research reports.
- **Gap Analysis**: Analyze the collected data to identify gaps in the current market offerings and areas where our product can differentiate itself.
- **Feature Prioritization**: Develop a prioritization framework to rank potential product features based on market demand, competitive advantage, and alignment with business goals.
- **Report Compilation**: Synthesize findings into a comprehensive report that includes:
  - Executive summary of the analysis
  - Detailed findings on competitor features and strategies
  - Strategic recommendations for feature prioritization
  - Actionable insights for execution
- **Presentation**: Present the findings and recommendations to key stakeholders for feedback and alignment.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1**: Completion of competitor identification
  - Deliverable: List of selected competitors
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of data collection
  - Deliverable: Compiled data set on competitors’ features, pricing, and feedback
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of gap analysis
  - Deliverable: Gap analysis report
- **Milestone 4**: Development of feature prioritization framework
  - Deliverable: Prioritization framework document
- **Milestone 5**: Compilation of the final report
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive competitor analysis report
- **Milestone 6**: Presentation to stakeholders
  - Deliverable: Presentation slides and feedback summary
