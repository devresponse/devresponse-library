
# Project Plan Template: Weighted Scoring Model Implementation

## 1. Executive Summary

The Weighted Scoring Model Implementation project aims to provide a structured approach for evaluating and prioritizing product initiatives within {{Organization Name}}. The primary objective of this project is to design a customized weighted scoring model that aligns with the specific business objectives and product portfolio of the organization. This initiative will begin with a comprehensive consultation process to identify key criteria that influence product success, such as market demand, revenue potential, and alignment with strategic goals. By developing a scoring framework that assigns appropriate weights to each criterion based on its significance, the project will facilitate informed decision-making. The key outcomes of this project include a detailed report outlining the scoring model and a user-friendly tool that enables ongoing prioritization of product initiatives. Ultimately, this model will empower {{Organization Name}} to allocate resources efficiently and ensure that high-impact initiatives are executed in a timely manner, driving overall business success.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: Conduct meetings with stakeholders to identify and define key criteria for product evaluation.
- **Criteria Development**: Collaborate with the team to establish a comprehensive list of criteria, including but not limited to:
  - Market Demand
  - Revenue Potential
  - Strategic Alignment
  - Competitive Advantage
  - Resource Availability
- **Weight Assignment**: Develop a scoring framework that assigns weights to each criterion based on its importance to the organization.
- **Model Design**: Create a customized weighted scoring model that integrates the identified criteria and their respective weights.
- **Tool Development**: Design a user-friendly tool (e.g., spreadsheet or software application) that allows team members to input data and utilize the scoring model for ongoing product prioritization.
- **Documentation**: Prepare a comprehensive report that details the scoring model, its application, and guidelines for use.
- **Training Session**: Conduct a training session for stakeholders to ensure proper understanding and utilization of the scoring model and tool.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of stakeholder consultation
  - **Deliverable**: Summary report of identified criteria
- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of criteria and weight assignment
  - **Deliverable**: Document outlining criteria and assigned weights
- **Milestone 3**: Development of the weighted scoring model
  - **Deliverable**: Draft of the scoring model
- **Milestone 4**: Creation of the user-friendly tool
  - **Deliverable**: Functional prototype of the prioritization tool
- **Milestone 5**: Final report and training session
  - **Deliverable**: Comprehensive report and training materials
