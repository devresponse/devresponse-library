
# Project Plan Template: Customer Journey Mapping for Prioritization

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Customer Journey Mapping for Prioritization" project aims to enhance product management effectiveness by providing a detailed understanding of the customer journey. In today's competitive market, it is essential to identify critical touchpoints and pain points experienced by customers to prioritize product features that meet their needs. This project will involve analyzing customer interactions and feedback to create a comprehensive customer journey map. The key outcomes include a visual representation of the customer journey, thorough documentation of identified pain points, and a prioritized list of product features. By aligning product development efforts with customer needs, we will ensure that resources are allocated efficiently, ultimately leading to improved customer satisfaction and successful project execution. This structured approach will not only facilitate better decision-making but also enhance the overall alignment of product strategies with business objectives.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Analysis**: Conduct qualitative and quantitative research to gather customer feedback and interaction data.
- **Customer Journey Mapping**: Create a visual representation of the customer journey, highlighting key touchpoints and interactions.
- **Pain Point Identification**: Document and analyze pain points experienced by customers at various stages of their journey.
- **Feature Prioritization**: Develop a prioritized list of product features based on the identified pain points and customer feedback.
- **Stakeholder Engagement**: Collaborate with key stakeholders to validate findings and ensure alignment with business objectives.
- **Final Report**: Compile all findings, visual maps, and prioritized features into a comprehensive report for presentation to stakeholders.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of customer research and data collection
  - Deliverable: Research Summary Report
- **Milestone 2**: Development of the customer journey map
  - Deliverable: Visual Customer Journey Map
- **Milestone 3**: Identification and documentation of pain points
  - Deliverable: Pain Points Analysis Document
- **Milestone 4**: Prioritization of product features
  - Deliverable: Prioritized Product Features List
- **Milestone 5**: Final presentation and report delivery
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Project Report
