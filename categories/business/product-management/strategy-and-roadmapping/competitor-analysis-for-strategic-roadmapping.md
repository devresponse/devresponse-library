
# Project Plan Template: Competitor Analysis for Strategic Roadmapping

## 1. Executive Summary

The primary objective of the "Competitor Analysis for Strategic Roadmapping" project is to conduct a thorough examination of key competitors within the {{industry}}. This analysis aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of their strengths, weaknesses, product offerings, market positioning, and customer feedback. By identifying gaps in the market and uncovering opportunities for differentiation, we will equip your organization with actionable insights that will inform the development of a robust and innovative product roadmap. The project will culminate in a detailed report that synthesizes our findings and recommendations, ensuring that your product strategy is aligned with both business objectives and prevailing market trends. Key outcomes will include a clear identification of competitive advantages, potential threats, and strategic opportunities that will guide the product's lifecycle and enhance its market positioning.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Competitor Identification**: Identify and list the top {{number}} competitors in the {{industry}} based on market share, relevance, and product offerings.
  
- **SWOT Analysis**: Conduct a SWOT analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) for each identified competitor to understand their market positioning.
  
- **Product Offering Evaluation**: Analyze the product offerings of each competitor, focusing on features, pricing, and customer feedback.
  
- **Market Positioning Assessment**: Evaluate how competitors position themselves in the market, including their branding strategies and target demographics.
  
- **Customer Feedback Analysis**: Gather and analyze customer reviews and feedback from various platforms to gauge customer satisfaction and areas for improvement.
  
- **Gap Identification**: Identify gaps in the market that your product can exploit, based on the analysis conducted.
  
- **Opportunity Recommendations**: Provide actionable recommendations for product differentiation and strategic positioning based on the findings.
  
- **Final Report Preparation**: Compile all findings and recommendations into a comprehensive report, including visual aids such as charts and graphs to illustrate key points.
