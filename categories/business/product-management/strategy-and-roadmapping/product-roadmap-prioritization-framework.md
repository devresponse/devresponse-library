
# Project Plan Template: Product Roadmap Prioritization Framework

## 1. Executive Summary

The Product Roadmap Prioritization Framework project aims to establish a systematic approach for evaluating and prioritizing features and initiatives within a product roadmap. The primary objective is to ensure that the most critical features are prioritized effectively, thereby enhancing product success and aligning with strategic goals. This project will involve a thorough analysis of key criteria such as impact, effort, and business value, which will serve as the foundation for prioritization decisions. To achieve this, we will conduct stakeholder interviews to gather insights on business objectives, market trends, and user needs. Additionally, workshops will be organized to align the team on prioritization criteria and scoring methods. The key outcomes of this project will include a comprehensive document that outlines the prioritization framework, along with templates and guidelines for ongoing use. By implementing this framework, the product team will be empowered to make informed decisions and optimize resource allocation, ultimately driving greater success for the product.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Stakeholder Interviews**: Conduct interviews with key stakeholders to gather insights on business objectives, market trends, and user needs.
- **Workshops**: Facilitate workshops to align the team on prioritization criteria and scoring methods.
- **Framework Development**: Develop a structured prioritization framework that includes:
  - Key criteria for evaluation (impact, effort, business value)
  - Scoring methods and templates
  - Guidelines for ongoing use of the framework
- **Documentation**: Create a comprehensive document that outlines the prioritization framework, including all templates and guidelines.
- **Training**: Provide training sessions for the product team on how to effectively utilize the prioritization framework.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of stakeholder interviews
  - Deliverable: Summary report of insights gathered from interviews
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of workshops
  - Deliverable: Workshop summary and agreed-upon prioritization criteria
- **Milestone 3**: Development of the prioritization framework
  - Deliverable: Draft of the prioritization framework document
- **Milestone 4**: Finalization of the framework document
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive prioritization framework document with templates and guidelines
- **Milestone 5**: Training sessions conducted
  - Deliverable: Training materials and feedback report
