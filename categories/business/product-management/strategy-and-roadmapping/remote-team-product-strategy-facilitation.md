
# Project Plan Template: Remote Team Product Strategy Facilitation

## 1. Executive Summary

The Remote Team Product Strategy Facilitation project aims to align distributed teams on a cohesive product strategy and roadmap through a series of interactive remote workshops. In today's fast-paced digital landscape, it is crucial for cross-functional teams to collaborate effectively, despite geographical barriers. This project will leverage collaborative tools and techniques to foster engagement and communication among participants. The workshops will cover essential topics, including market analysis, user needs, competitive positioning, and feature prioritization. By the conclusion of these sessions, teams will develop a clear and actionable product strategy and roadmap that aligns with the overarching business objectives. Key outcomes include enhanced team collaboration, a shared understanding of product direction, and empowered teams that take ownership of their contributions to the product's success.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Workshop Planning**: 
  - Identify key stakeholders and participants from various teams.
  - Schedule workshop sessions and allocate time for each topic.
  - Prepare workshop materials, including agendas, presentations, and collaborative tools.

- **Workshop Facilitation**:
  - Conduct a series of remote workshops focused on:
    - Market Analysis
    - User Needs Assessment
    - Competitive Positioning
    - Feature Prioritization
  - Utilize collaborative tools (e.g., Miro, Zoom) to facilitate discussions and capture insights.

- **Post-Workshop Activities**:
  - Compile workshop outputs into a comprehensive product strategy document.
  - Develop a visual product roadmap that outlines key milestones and deliverables.
  - Schedule follow-up sessions to review and refine the strategy as needed.
