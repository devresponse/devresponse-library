
# Project Plan Template: Diary Study for Long-Term User Insights

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Diary Study for Long-Term User Insights" project aims to conduct an in-depth diary study that captures the nuanced interactions, experiences, and emotions of users as they engage with a specific product over an extended period. The primary objective is to gather qualitative data that reveals patterns in user behavior, preferences, and pain points. By encouraging participants to document their experiences, we will gain valuable insights into how users incorporate the product into their daily lives. This research method is particularly effective in uncovering long-term user insights that can inform product development and enhancement. The key outcomes of this project will include a comprehensive final report that synthesizes the findings, highlights key themes, and provides actionable recommendations for improving user experience. Ultimately, this project is essential for businesses seeking to adopt a user-centric approach in their product management strategies, ensuring that their offerings align closely with user needs and expectations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Participant Recruitment**: Identify and recruit a diverse group of participants who regularly use the product. This will involve creating recruitment materials and screening criteria to ensure a representative sample.
  
- **Diary Study Design**: Develop a structured diary study framework, including guidelines for participants on how to document their experiences, frequency of entries, and specific prompts to guide their reflections.

- **Data Collection**: Over a predetermined period, participants will document their interactions with the product through diary entries. This phase will include regular check-ins to encourage participation and address any questions.

- **Data Analysis**: Analyze the collected diary entries to identify recurring themes, patterns, and insights. This will involve qualitative analysis methods to extract meaningful data from the participants' narratives.

- **Reporting**: Compile the findings into a comprehensive report that includes an executive summary, detailed analysis, key themes, and actionable recommendations for product improvement.

- **Presentation of Findings**: Present the final report to stakeholders, highlighting critical insights and recommendations for enhancing user experience and product alignment.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Participant Recruitment Completed  
  **Deliverable**: List of recruited participants and consent forms.

- **Milestone 2**: Diary Study Framework Finalized  
  **Deliverable**: Document outlining the diary study guidelines and prompts.

- **Milestone 3**: Data Collection Phase Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Collection of all diary entries from participants.

- **Milestone 4**: Data Analysis Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Summary of key themes and insights derived from the analysis.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Report Draft Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Comprehensive report synthesizing findings and recommendations.

- **Milestone 6**: Presentation to Stakeholders Completed  
  **Deliverable**: Presentation slides and feedback from stakeholders.
