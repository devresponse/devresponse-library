
# Project Plan Template: Customer Journey Mapping Workshop

## 1. Executive Summary

The Customer Journey Mapping Workshop aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of the customer experience, from initial awareness through to post-purchase interactions. This interactive workshop will engage participants in collaborative activities designed to visualize the customer journey, identify key touchpoints, and uncover pain points that may hinder user satisfaction. By employing techniques such as persona development, empathy mapping, and journey mapping exercises, the workshop will ensure a holistic view of the customer experience. Key outcomes include a detailed customer journey map, actionable insights, and recommendations for enhancing user engagement and satisfaction. This workshop is essential for businesses seeking to refine their product offerings and improve overall customer experience, ultimately leading to increased customer loyalty and business success.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Workshop Preparation**
  - Define objectives and outcomes with stakeholders.
  - Develop workshop materials, including templates for journey mapping and persona development.
  - Identify and invite participants from relevant departments (e.g., marketing, product development, customer service).

- **Workshop Execution**
  - Facilitate the workshop, guiding participants through each activity.
  - Conduct persona development sessions to create representative customer profiles.
  - Lead empathy mapping exercises to understand customer emotions and motivations.
  - Facilitate journey mapping sessions to visualize the customer journey, identifying key touchpoints and pain points.

- **Post-Workshop Activities**
  - Compile findings and insights from the workshop.
  - Create a comprehensive report detailing the customer journey map, identified pain points, and actionable recommendations.
  - Schedule a follow-up meeting to discuss the report and next steps.
