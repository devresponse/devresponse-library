
# ERP Training and Onboarding Program Project Plan Template

## 1. Executive Summary

The ERP Training and Onboarding Program aims to facilitate a seamless transition for employees as they adopt the new ERP system. The primary objective of this project is to empower staff with the knowledge and skills necessary to leverage the ERP system effectively, thereby improving productivity and operational efficiency. The program will begin with an initial assessment of the organization's specific needs and the current skill levels of employees, ensuring that the training is tailored to address the unique challenges faced by the team. Customized training materials will be developed to cover essential ERP functionalities, best practices, and troubleshooting techniques. The training will be delivered through a combination of interactive workshops, hands-on sessions, and digital resources, accommodating various learning styles. Ongoing support and resources will be provided to ensure that employees feel confident in using the ERP system. Key outcomes include enhanced employee competency, increased system utilization, and a smoother transition to the new ERP environment.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a comprehensive analysis of the organization's needs and current employee skill levels related to the ERP system.
  
- **Development of Training Materials**: Create customized training materials that include:
  - User manuals
  - Quick reference guides
  - Video tutorials
  - Interactive e-learning modules
  
- **Training Delivery**:
  - Organize interactive workshops to engage employees in hands-on learning.
  - Conduct hands-on sessions to practice real-world scenarios using the ERP system.
  - Provide digital resources for self-paced learning.
  
- **Ongoing Support**:
  - Establish a helpdesk for immediate assistance.
  - Schedule follow-up sessions to address any challenges faced by employees post-training.
  - Create a repository of FAQs and troubleshooting tips accessible to all employees.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing employee skill levels and training needs.

- **Milestone 2**: Development of Training Materials
  - Deliverable: Customized training materials ready for distribution.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Training Sessions
  - Deliverable: Attendance records and feedback forms from training participants.

- **Milestone 4**: Implementation of Ongoing Support
  - Deliverable: Helpdesk operational and resource repository established.
