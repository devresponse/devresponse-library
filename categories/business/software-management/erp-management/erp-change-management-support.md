
# ERP Change Management Support Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The ERP Change Management Support project aims to facilitate a seamless transition to a new ERP system or significant updates to an existing one. In today's fast-paced business environment, organizations face the challenge of adapting to new processes and technologies. Our primary objective is to minimize disruption during this transition while enhancing user engagement and ensuring that the business fully realizes the benefits of its ERP investment. The project will involve a comprehensive assessment of the current ERP landscape, identification of key stakeholders, and the development of a tailored change management strategy. Key outcomes include effective training sessions for team members, ongoing support throughout the transition phase, and the creation of user-friendly documentation. By focusing on these areas, we aim to empower your organization to adapt effectively and efficiently to the new ERP system, ultimately leading to improved operational performance and user satisfaction.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current ERP Landscape**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the existing ERP system, including its functionalities, user engagement levels, and areas for improvement.
  
- **Stakeholder Identification**: Identify and engage key stakeholders who will be impacted by the ERP changes, ensuring their input and support throughout the process.

- **Change Management Strategy Development**: Create a customized change management strategy that addresses the specific needs of the organization, including communication plans, training schedules, and support mechanisms.

- **Training Sessions**: Develop and deliver comprehensive training sessions tailored to different user groups within the organization to ensure they are well-equipped to use the new ERP system effectively.

- **Ongoing Support**: Provide continuous support during the transition phase, addressing any issues or concerns that arise and ensuring a smooth adoption of the new system.

- **Documentation Creation**: Develop user-friendly documentation, including guides and FAQs, to assist users in navigating the new ERP system and to serve as a reference post-implementation.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current ERP Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing findings and recommendations.

- **Milestone 2**: Stakeholder Engagement and Identification
  - Deliverable: Stakeholder Engagement Plan outlining communication and involvement strategies.

- **Milestone 3**: Change Management Strategy Finalization
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Change Management Strategy Document.

- **Milestone 4**: Training Sessions Conducted
  - Deliverable: Training Completion Certificates and Feedback Reports.

- **Milestone 5**: Ongoing Support Initiated
  - Deliverable: Support Log documenting issues and resolutions.

- **Milestone 6**: User Documentation Delivered
  - Deliverable: User Manuals and Quick Reference Guides.
