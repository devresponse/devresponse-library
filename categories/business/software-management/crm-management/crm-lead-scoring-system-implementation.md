
# CRM Lead Scoring System Implementation Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The CRM Lead Scoring System Implementation project aims to enhance the efficiency of the sales process by developing a robust lead scoring system within the existing CRM framework. The primary objective is to analyze current customer data and sales processes to identify key attributes that signify high-value leads. By configuring the CRM to assign scores based on these attributes, the sales team will be empowered to prioritize leads that are most likely to convert, thereby maximizing revenue opportunities. Key outcomes of this project include the establishment of automated scoring rules, seamless integration with existing CRM functionalities, and comprehensive training for the sales team. Ultimately, this project will result in a fully functional lead scoring system that not only streamlines the sales process but also significantly improves overall sales performance.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Analysis Phase**
  - Conduct a comprehensive review of existing customer data and sales processes.
  - Identify key attributes that correlate with high-value leads.
  - Gather input from stakeholders to ensure alignment with business objectives.

- **Design Phase**
  - Develop a lead scoring model based on identified attributes.
  - Create a detailed plan for CRM configuration, including scoring rules and criteria.

- **Implementation Phase**
  - Configure the CRM to incorporate the lead scoring model.
  - Set up automated scoring rules to evaluate leads in real-time.
  - Integrate the lead scoring system with existing CRM functionalities.

- **Training Phase**
  - Develop training materials for the sales team.
  - Conduct training sessions to ensure effective use of the new lead scoring system.
  - Provide ongoing support and resources for the sales team post-implementation.

- **Evaluation Phase**
  - Monitor the effectiveness of the lead scoring system.
  - Gather feedback from the sales team and make necessary adjustments.
  - Report on key performance indicators to measure success.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Project charter and stakeholder alignment document.

- **Milestone 2: Completion of Analysis Phase**
  - Deliverable: Analysis report detailing key attributes and lead scoring model.

- **Milestone 3: Completion of Design Phase**
  - Deliverable: Design document outlining CRM configuration and scoring rules.

- **Milestone 4: Completion of Implementation Phase**
  - Deliverable: Fully configured lead scoring system within the CRM.

- **Milestone 5: Completion of Training Phase**
  - Deliverable: Training materials and completion of training sessions.

- **Milestone 6: Project Closure**
  - Deliverable: Final project report and evaluation of lead scoring system effectiveness.
