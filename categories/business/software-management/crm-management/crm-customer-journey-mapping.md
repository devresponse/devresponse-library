
# Project Plan Template: CRM Customer Journey Mapping

## 1. Executive Summary

The CRM Customer Journey Mapping project aims to enhance customer satisfaction and drive business growth by creating comprehensive customer journey maps within a CRM system. This project will provide a visual representation of the customer experience, detailing each phase from initial contact to post-purchase interactions. The primary objective is to identify key touchpoints, customer emotions, and potential pain points throughout the journey. By analyzing customer interactions and feedback, we will optimize these touchpoints to ensure a seamless experience. The key outcomes include detailed maps that illustrate the current state of customer interactions and actionable insights for improvement. Ultimately, this project will empower your business with the necessary tools to foster stronger customer relationships and increase retention rates, ensuring a competitive edge in today's market.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Define project objectives and success criteria.
  - Identify key stakeholders and assemble the project team.

- **Research and Analysis**
  - Conduct stakeholder interviews to gather insights on customer interactions.
  - Analyze existing customer data to identify trends and patterns.

- **Customer Journey Mapping**
  - Identify key customer touchpoints across various stages of the journey.
  - Map out the current state of customer interactions, highlighting emotions and pain points.
  - Develop detailed customer journey maps for different customer segments.

- **Optimization Recommendations**
  - Analyze the mapped journeys to identify areas for improvement.
  - Provide actionable recommendations to enhance customer experience at each touchpoint.

- **Final Deliverables**
  - Comprehensive customer journey maps.
  - A report summarizing findings and recommendations for optimization.
  - Presentation of the final deliverables to stakeholders.
