
# Project Plan Template: CRM GDPR Compliance Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

The CRM GDPR Compliance Audit project aims to ensure that your organization's customer relationship management system adheres to the stringent requirements set forth by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). In an era where data privacy is paramount, this audit will provide a comprehensive assessment of your CRM practices, focusing on data collection, storage, processing, and sharing. Our primary objective is to identify any compliance gaps and offer actionable recommendations to enhance your data security measures. Key outcomes of this project include a detailed compliance status report, identification of potential risks, and a strategic roadmap for achieving full GDPR compliance. By the conclusion of this project, your organization will not only meet regulatory requirements but also foster greater customer trust through responsible data handling practices.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the CRM GDPR Compliance Audit includes the following key activities:

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a preliminary review of the current CRM system and data management practices.
- **Data Mapping**: Identify and document all data collection points, storage locations, processing activities, and sharing practices.
- **Consent Mechanism Evaluation**: Review existing consent mechanisms to ensure they meet GDPR standards.
- **Data Subject Rights Assessment**: Evaluate how the organization handles requests related to data subject rights (e.g., access, rectification, erasure).
- **Gap Analysis**: Identify any areas of non-compliance or risk within the current CRM practices.
- **Recommendations Report**: Provide a detailed report outlining compliance status, identified risks, and actionable recommendations for improvement.
- **Implementation Support**: Assist in the implementation of necessary changes to enhance compliance and data security.
