
# Project Plan Template: CRM Data Cleaning and Deduplication

## 1. Executive Summary

The CRM Data Cleaning and Deduplication project aims to enhance the accuracy and reliability of customer data within the CRM system. In today's competitive business landscape, maintaining precise customer information is crucial for effective CRM management. This project will focus on identifying and eliminating duplicate entries, correcting inaccuracies, and standardizing data formats. By conducting a thorough analysis of the existing CRM data, we will utilize advanced tools and techniques to ensure that all customer records are unique and up-to-date. Key outcomes include a clean, organized database that improves customer interactions, supports better decision-making, and contributes to strategic planning. Ultimately, this project is designed to deliver tangible results that enhance the effectiveness of your CRM and foster overall business growth.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Assessment**: Conduct an initial assessment of the existing CRM data to identify the extent of duplicates and inaccuracies.
- **Data Cleaning**: Implement data cleaning processes to correct inaccuracies, including standardizing formats and updating outdated information.
- **Deduplication**: Utilize advanced deduplication techniques to identify and merge duplicate entries, ensuring that each customer record is unique.
- **Quality Assurance**: Perform quality checks to validate the accuracy and completeness of the cleaned data.
- **Documentation**: Provide comprehensive documentation of the data cleaning and deduplication processes, including methodologies used and results achieved.
- **Training**: Offer training sessions for staff on maintaining data integrity and best practices for future data entry.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Data Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing the current state of CRM data.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Data Cleaning Process Initiated
  - Deliverable: Cleaned data set with corrected inaccuracies.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Deduplication Process Completed
  - Deliverable: Finalized unique customer records with duplicates merged.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Quality Assurance Checks Completed
  - Deliverable: Quality Assurance Report confirming data integrity.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Documentation and Training Delivered
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive documentation and training materials provided to staff.
