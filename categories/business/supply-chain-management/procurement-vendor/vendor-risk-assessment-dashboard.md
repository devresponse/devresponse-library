
# Vendor Risk Assessment Dashboard Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Vendor Risk Assessment Dashboard project aims to develop a comprehensive tool that enables businesses to evaluate and monitor the risks associated with their vendors. The primary objective of this project is to create a user-friendly dashboard that integrates various data sources, providing insights into critical factors such as financial stability, regulatory compliance, and geopolitical influences that may affect vendor reliability. By identifying key risk indicators and designing intuitive visualizations, the dashboard will facilitate real-time monitoring and analysis of vendor performance. Key outcomes include improved decision-making capabilities for vendor selection and management, enhanced supply chain resilience, and increased operational efficiency. Ultimately, this project will empower organizations to proactively manage vendor risks, ensuring that they can maintain robust and reliable supply chains in an increasingly complex business environment.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Define project objectives and success criteria.
  - Identify stakeholders and establish communication channels.

- **Requirements Gathering**
  - Conduct workshops with stakeholders to gather requirements.
  - Identify key risk indicators relevant to vendor assessment.
  - Document functional and non-functional requirements.

- **Data Integration**
  - Identify and integrate various data sources (financial data, compliance records, geopolitical information).
  - Ensure data quality and consistency across sources.

- **Dashboard Design**
  - Develop wireframes and prototypes for the dashboard interface.
  - Design user-friendly visualizations for key risk indicators.
  - Incorporate feedback from stakeholders to refine designs.

- **Data Analytics Implementation**
  - Implement data analytics capabilities for real-time monitoring.
  - Develop algorithms for risk scoring and vendor evaluation.

- **Testing and Validation**
  - Conduct unit testing, integration testing, and user acceptance testing.
  - Validate the accuracy and reliability of the dashboard outputs.

- **Deployment**
  - Deploy the dashboard to the production environment.
  - Provide training sessions for end-users on dashboard functionality.

- **Post-Deployment Support**
  - Offer ongoing support and maintenance for the dashboard.
  - Gather user feedback for future enhancements.
