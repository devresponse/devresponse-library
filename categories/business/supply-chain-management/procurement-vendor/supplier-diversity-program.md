
# Supplier Diversity Program Project Plan Template

## 1. Executive Summary

The Supplier Diversity Program aims to enhance the inclusivity of the supply chain by actively seeking out and integrating minority-owned and small-scale suppliers into the procurement process. This initiative is designed to address the current gaps in diversity within our supplier base, ensuring that our procurement practices reflect our commitment to social responsibility and community engagement. The project will involve a comprehensive analysis of existing suppliers, followed by the development of a strategic plan to onboard diverse suppliers. Key outcomes include the establishment of clear criteria for supplier selection, the formation of partnerships with diversity-focused organizations, and the implementation of training sessions for procurement teams. Ultimately, this program will not only meet compliance standards but also foster innovation and strengthen community ties through diverse supplier relationships.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Current Supplier Analysis**: Conduct a thorough review of the existing supplier base to identify diversity gaps.
- **Criteria Development**: Create specific criteria for the selection of minority-owned and small-scale suppliers.
- **Partnership Establishment**: Identify and establish partnerships with organizations that focus on supplier diversity.
- **Training Sessions**: Develop and implement training sessions for procurement teams to emphasize the importance of supplier diversity.
- **Onboarding Process**: Design a structured onboarding process for new diverse suppliers.
- **Monitoring and Evaluation**: Establish metrics for measuring the success of the Supplier Diversity Program and create a feedback loop for continuous improvement.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Current Supplier Analysis
  - **Deliverable**: Report detailing gaps in supplier diversity.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Development of Supplier Selection Criteria
  - **Deliverable**: Document outlining the criteria for selecting diverse suppliers.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Establishment of Partnerships
  - **Deliverable**: List of partnered diversity-focused organizations.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Implementation of Training Sessions
  - **Deliverable**: Training materials and attendance records from sessions.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Onboarding of Diverse Suppliers
  - **Deliverable**: Onboarding process documentation and records of new suppliers.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Monitoring and Evaluation Framework
  - **Deliverable**: Metrics and evaluation report on the program's effectiveness.
