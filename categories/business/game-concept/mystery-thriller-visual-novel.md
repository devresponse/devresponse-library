
# Project Plan Template: Mystery Thriller Visual Novel

## 1. Executive Summary

The Mystery Thriller Visual Novel project aims to craft an engaging and immersive gaming experience that captivates players through a compelling narrative and rich character development. The primary objective is to create a branching storyline that allows players to make choices influencing the game's outcome, thereby enhancing replayability and player engagement. This project will focus on developing intriguing characters, each with unique backgrounds, motivations, and relationships that contribute to the overall mystery. Additionally, a distinctive visual style will be established, utilizing color palettes, art styles, and UI elements that evoke tension and intrigue, complementing the suspenseful atmosphere of the game. Key outcomes include a fully developed game prototype, a detailed narrative script, character designs, and a cohesive visual style that aligns with the game's theme. This project is ideal for businesses looking to enter the gaming market with a unique offering or to enhance their existing portfolio with a visually stunning and narratively complex game.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Game Concept Development**
  - Research and analysis of existing visual novels and mystery genres.
  - Brainstorming sessions to outline core themes, mechanics, and gameplay elements.

- **Narrative Design**
  - Creation of a branching storyline with multiple endings.
  - Development of character arcs and backstories.
  - Writing of dialogue and narrative scripts.

- **Character Design**
  - Concept art for main and supporting characters.
  - Development of character profiles, including motivations and relationships.

- **Visual Style Development**
  - Establishment of a color palette and art style that reflects the game's tone.
  - Design of UI elements that enhance user experience.

- **Prototyping**
  - Development of a playable prototype to test narrative flow and gameplay mechanics.
  - Iterative feedback sessions to refine the game based on player input.

- **Marketing Strategy**
  - Creation of a marketing plan to promote the game upon completion.
  - Development of promotional materials, including trailers and social media content.
