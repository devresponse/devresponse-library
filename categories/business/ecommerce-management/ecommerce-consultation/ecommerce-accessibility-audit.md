
# Ecommerce Accessibility Audit Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Ecommerce Accessibility Audit project aims to provide a comprehensive evaluation of your ecommerce website's accessibility for users with disabilities. The primary objective is to assess the site against established accessibility standards, specifically the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG). This project will involve a meticulous review of the website to identify barriers that may impede users with disabilities from navigating effectively. Key outcomes include a detailed report that outlines our findings, highlighting specific areas of non-compliance, and offering actionable recommendations for improvement. By enhancing the usability of your ecommerce platform, we aim to ensure a seamless shopping experience for all customers. This initiative not only aligns with legal compliance but also expands your customer base and improves overall customer satisfaction, ultimately contributing to the success of your business.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a kickoff meeting to understand the specific needs and goals of the client regarding accessibility.
  
- **Website Evaluation**: Perform a thorough audit of the ecommerce website using automated tools and manual testing methods to assess compliance with WCAG standards.

- **Barrier Identification**: Identify and document barriers that users with disabilities may encounter while navigating the site, including issues related to visual, auditory, and motor accessibility.

- **Reporting**: Develop a comprehensive report detailing the findings of the audit, including:
  - Areas of non-compliance with WCAG standards
  - Specific examples of accessibility barriers
  - Prioritized recommendations for remediation
  
- **Presentation of Findings**: Schedule a meeting to present the findings to the client, allowing for discussion and clarification of the report.

- **Follow-Up Support**: Provide a follow-up consultation to assist the client in understanding the recommendations and planning for implementation.
