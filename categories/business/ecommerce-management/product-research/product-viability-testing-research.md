
# Project Plan Template: Product Viability Testing Research

## 1. Executive Summary

The Product Viability Testing Research project aims to provide a comprehensive analysis of potential products for {{Company Name}}’s ecommerce business. The primary objective is to conduct an in-depth demand analysis to gauge market interest, evaluate competitive pricing strategies, and assess customer preferences. This research will utilize various methodologies, including surveys, competitor analysis, and market trend evaluations, to deliver actionable insights. Key outcomes of this project include a detailed report outlining market demand, recommended pricing strategies, and a summary of customer preferences. By minimizing risks associated with new product launches and enhancing inventory management strategies, this project will empower {{Company Name}} to make informed decisions regarding product selection, ultimately driving profitability and growth for the ecommerce venture.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Product Viability Testing Research project includes the following key activities:

- **Demand Analysis**: Conduct surveys and focus groups to gather data on consumer interest in potential products.
- **Competitor Analysis**: Evaluate competitors' product offerings, pricing strategies, and market positioning to identify gaps and opportunities.
- **Market Trend Evaluation**: Analyze current market trends and consumer behavior to forecast future demand and preferences.
- **Data Compilation**: Aggregate and analyze data collected from various research methodologies to identify actionable insights.
- **Reporting**: Prepare a comprehensive report summarizing findings, insights, and recommendations for product selection and pricing strategies.
- **Presentation**: Present findings to stakeholders, highlighting key insights and recommendations for product viability.
