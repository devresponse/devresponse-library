
# E-Commerce Store Accessibility Audit Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The E-Commerce Store Accessibility Audit project aims to ensure that your online store is fully accessible to all users, including those with disabilities. In today's digital landscape, accessibility is not merely a legal obligation but a fundamental aspect of user experience and inclusivity. This project will involve a thorough evaluation of your e-commerce platform against established standards such as the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG). Our team will assess critical components including site navigation, content readability, and interactive elements to identify any barriers that may impede users with disabilities. The key outcomes of this audit will include a detailed report outlining the current accessibility status of your store, a prioritized list of actionable recommendations for improvement, and a roadmap for implementation. By enhancing accessibility, you will not only comply with legal requirements but also improve the overall shopping experience for all customers, expand your market reach, and strengthen your brand reputation.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with stakeholders to understand specific accessibility concerns and project objectives.
  
- **Accessibility Evaluation**: Perform a comprehensive audit of the e-commerce store, focusing on:
  - Navigation structure and ease of use
  - Content readability (text size, contrast, etc.)
  - Interactive elements (forms, buttons, etc.)
  - Compatibility with assistive technologies (screen readers, etc.)
  
- **Compliance Assessment**: Evaluate the store's compliance with WCAG 2.1 standards and other relevant accessibility laws.

- **Reporting**: Compile findings into a detailed report that includes:
  - Current accessibility status
  - Identified barriers and issues
  - Prioritized recommendations for enhancements
  
- **Action Plan Development**: Create a roadmap for implementing the recommended changes, including timelines and responsible parties.

- **Follow-Up Consultation**: Schedule a meeting to discuss the report findings and action plan with stakeholders.
