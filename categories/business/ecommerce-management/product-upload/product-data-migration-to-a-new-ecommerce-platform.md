
# Project Plan Template: Product Data Migration to a New Platform

## 1. Executive Summary

The **Product Data Migration to a New Ecommerce Platform** project aims to facilitate the seamless transfer of product information from an outdated ecommerce system to a modern platform. The primary objective is to enhance the online presence of the business while streamlining inventory management processes. This project will involve a comprehensive audit of existing product data, ensuring that critical details such as product names, descriptions, prices, SKUs, and images are accurately captured and formatted according to the new platform's specifications. Key outcomes include improved data integrity, enhanced customer experience through accurate product information, and minimized downtime during the transition. By implementing rigorous quality checks post-migration, we will ensure that customers have access to up-to-date product information, thereby fostering trust and satisfaction. This project is essential for businesses looking to remain competitive in the digital marketplace and to provide a superior shopping experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Audit Existing Product Data**: Conduct a thorough review of the current product database to identify all necessary information for migration.
- **Data Mapping**: Create a mapping document that outlines how existing data fields will correspond to the new platform's requirements.
- **Data Cleanup**: Identify and rectify any inconsistencies or errors in the existing product data to ensure accuracy.
- **Migration Process**: Execute the data migration using automated tools and manual processes as needed to ensure completeness.
- **Quality Assurance Checks**: Implement a series of quality checks post-migration to verify the accuracy and integrity of the data.
- **Training and Documentation**: Provide training for staff on the new platform and create documentation for future reference.
- **Go-Live Support**: Offer support during the transition period to address any issues that may arise post-migration.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Product Data Audit  
  **Deliverable**: Audit report detailing existing product data and identified issues.

- **Milestone 2**: Data Mapping Document Finalization  
  **Deliverable**: Approved data mapping document outlining field correspondences.

- **Milestone 3**: Data Cleanup Completion  
  **Deliverable**: Cleaned and validated product data ready for migration.

- **Milestone 4**: Successful Data Migration  
  **Deliverable**: Confirmation of successful migration with all data fields populated.

- **Milestone 5**: Quality Assurance Report  
  **Deliverable**: Report detailing the results of quality checks and any discrepancies found.

- **Milestone 6**: Staff Training Completion  
  **Deliverable**: Training session completed with feedback collected from participants.

- **Milestone 7**: Go-Live Support Period  
  **Deliverable**: Support log documenting any issues and resolutions during the go-live phase.
