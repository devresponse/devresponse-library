
# Project Plan Template: Operational Risk Management Dashboard

## 1. Executive Summary

The Operational Risk Management Dashboard project aims to create a comprehensive and user-friendly interface that enables businesses to monitor and manage their operational risks effectively. The primary objective of this project is to develop a dashboard that integrates various data sources, providing real-time insights into potential risks. This will empower decision-makers to act swiftly and strategically, enhancing their ability to mitigate risks before they escalate. Key outcomes include the implementation of risk identification metrics, assessment tools, and mitigation strategies, all presented in an intuitive layout. By facilitating easy navigation and customization, users will be able to tailor the information to their specific needs. Ultimately, this dashboard will enhance risk management processes, improve operational efficiency, and safeguard the growth trajectory of businesses.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Define project goals and objectives.
  - Identify stakeholders and establish communication channels.
  
- **Requirements Gathering**
  - Conduct interviews and workshops with stakeholders to gather requirements.
  - Document functional and non-functional requirements for the dashboard.

- **Design Phase**
  - Create wireframes and prototypes for the dashboard interface.
  - Develop a user experience (UX) design that emphasizes usability and accessibility.

- **Development Phase**
  - Integrate various data sources to ensure real-time data availability.
  - Develop risk identification metrics and assessment tools.
  - Implement mitigation strategies within the dashboard.

- **Testing Phase**
  - Conduct unit testing, integration testing, and user acceptance testing (UAT).
  - Gather feedback from stakeholders and make necessary adjustments.

- **Deployment**
  - Deploy the dashboard in a production environment.
  - Provide training sessions for end-users to ensure effective utilization.

- **Maintenance and Support**
  - Establish a support plan for ongoing maintenance and updates.
  - Monitor dashboard performance and user feedback for continuous improvement.
