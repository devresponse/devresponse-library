
# Project Plan Template: Manager Training for Performance Reviews

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Manager Training for Performance Reviews" project aims to develop a comprehensive training program tailored specifically for managers to enhance their capabilities in conducting effective performance reviews. The primary objective is to equip managers with essential skills such as setting clear performance expectations, delivering constructive feedback, and utilizing performance metrics effectively. This training will incorporate interactive workshops featuring role-playing scenarios, case studies, and group discussions to reinforce learning and application. Key outcomes include improved manager confidence in conducting performance reviews, increased employee engagement, and alignment of individual goals with organizational objectives. By the end of the program, participants will possess the tools and techniques necessary to facilitate meaningful performance conversations that not only assess employee performance but also promote professional development.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of this project includes the following key components:

- **Needs Assessment**: Conduct surveys and interviews with stakeholders to identify specific training needs and expectations.
- **Curriculum Development**: Design a training curriculum that covers essential topics such as:
  - Setting clear performance expectations
  - Providing constructive feedback
  - Utilizing performance metrics effectively
  - Conducting performance conversations
- **Training Delivery**: Organize and facilitate interactive workshops that include:
  - Role-playing scenarios to practice feedback delivery
  - Case studies for real-world application
  - Group discussions to share experiences and insights
- **Evaluation and Feedback**: Implement a feedback mechanism to assess the effectiveness of the training and identify areas for improvement.
- **Follow-up Support**: Provide additional resources and support for managers post-training to ensure continued development and application of skills.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Needs Assessment
  - Deliverable: Needs Assessment Report
- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of Training Curriculum
  - Deliverable: Training Curriculum Document
- **Milestone 3**: Delivery of Training Workshops
  - Deliverable: Workshop Materials and Attendance Records
- **Milestone 4**: Collection of Participant Feedback
  - Deliverable: Feedback Summary Report
- **Milestone 5**: Provision of Follow-up Resources
  - Deliverable: Resource Guide for Managers
