
# Project Plan Template: Employee Goal-Setting Workshop

## 1. Executive Summary

The Employee Goal-Setting Workshop aims to empower employees by equipping them with the essential skills and knowledge to set SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) goals that align with the overarching objectives of {{Company Name}}. This interactive workshop will engage participants through a series of activities, discussions, and practical exercises, encouraging them to reflect on their individual roles and contributions to the organization. Key outcomes include enhanced goal-setting capabilities, improved alignment of personal and organizational objectives, and a stronger culture of accountability and continuous improvement. By the end of the workshop, employees will possess a clear understanding of how to formulate effective goals that not only support the company's mission but also foster their personal growth and career aspirations. The workshop will be facilitated by experienced HR consultants who will share insights and best practices in performance management, ensuring a comprehensive learning experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Employee Goal-Setting Workshop includes the following components:

- **Workshop Design**: Develop a structured agenda that includes interactive sessions, group discussions, and practical exercises focused on SMART goal-setting.
- **Facilitation**: Engage experienced HR consultants to lead the workshop, ensuring they are well-versed in performance management principles and practices.
- **Materials Development**: Create and distribute workshop materials, including handouts, worksheets, and presentation slides that participants can reference during and after the workshop.
- **Participant Engagement**: Implement strategies to encourage active participation, such as breakout sessions, role-playing scenarios, and peer feedback.
- **Follow-Up**: Establish a follow-up mechanism to assess the application of learned skills and provide additional support as needed.
