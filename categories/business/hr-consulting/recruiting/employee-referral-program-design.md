
# Project Plan Template: Employee Referral Program Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Employee Referral Program Design project aims to create a structured and effective referral program that actively engages current employees in the recruitment process. The primary objective is to leverage the existing workforce's networks to attract high-quality candidates who align with our organization's culture and values. By conducting a thorough analysis of workforce dynamics and identifying key roles that require filling, we will develop a comprehensive referral framework tailored to our talent acquisition strategy. Key outcomes of this project include the creation of promotional materials to encourage employee participation, the establishment of clear guidelines for referrals, and the implementation of an incentive structure to reward successful hires. Additionally, training sessions will be provided to ensure employees understand the program's benefits and processes. The final deliverable will be a detailed program guide that outlines all aspects of the referral program, facilitating easy implementation and management by our team.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Analysis of Current Workforce Dynamics**
  - Assess existing employee demographics and skills.
  - Identify key roles and departments with recruitment needs.
  
- **Development of Referral Framework**
  - Create a structured referral process that includes:
    - Clear guidelines for employee referrals.
    - Criteria for eligible candidates.
  
- **Incentive Structure**
  - Design an attractive incentive program to motivate employees to participate.
  - Define reward mechanisms for successful hires.
  
- **Promotional Materials**
  - Develop engaging materials to promote the referral program, including:
    - Posters, emails, and digital content.
  
- **Training Sessions**
  - Organize training workshops for employees to explain the program.
  - Provide resources and FAQs to address common questions.
  
- **Final Deliverable**
  - Compile a comprehensive program guide that includes:
    - Overview of the referral program.
    - Detailed processes and guidelines.
    - Incentive structure and promotional materials.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Completion of Workforce Analysis**
  - Deliverable: Report on current workforce dynamics and key roles identified.
  
- **Milestone 2: Development of Referral Framework**
  - Deliverable: Draft of the referral process and guidelines.
  
- **Milestone 3: Incentive Structure Finalization**
  - Deliverable: Document outlining the incentive program.
  
- **Milestone 4: Creation of Promotional Materials**
  - Deliverable: Set of promotional materials ready for distribution.
  
- **Milestone 5: Training Sessions Conducted**
  - Deliverable: Training session materials and attendance records.
  
- **Milestone 6: Final Program Guide Delivery**
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive program guide for implementation.
