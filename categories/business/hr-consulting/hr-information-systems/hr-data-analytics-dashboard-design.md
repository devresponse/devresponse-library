
# Project Plan Template: HR Data Analytics Dashboard Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The HR Data Analytics Dashboard Design project aims to create a custom dashboard that provides a comprehensive visualization of critical HR metrics tailored to the specific needs of {{Organization Name}}. In today's data-driven business environment, understanding human resources metrics is essential for strategic decision-making. This project will facilitate the visualization of key indicators such as employee turnover rates, recruitment efficiency, and workforce demographics. The primary objective is to empower stakeholders with easily interpretable data, enabling informed decisions that enhance organizational performance. The project will commence with a detailed consultation to gather requirements and assess the current HR data landscape. Following this, wireframes and prototypes will be developed for stakeholder review, ensuring alignment with expectations. Upon approval, the dashboard will be developed and integrated into existing HR systems. Finally, comprehensive training sessions will be conducted to ensure the team can effectively utilize the dashboard, promoting a culture of data-driven insights across the organization.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct meetings with key stakeholders to gather requirements.
  - Assess the current HR data landscape and identify existing data sources.
  
- **Design Phase**:
  - Create wireframes and prototypes of the dashboard for stakeholder review.
  - Incorporate feedback and finalize the design.

- **Development Phase**:
  - Develop the dashboard using appropriate technologies and tools.
  - Integrate the dashboard with existing HR systems to ensure seamless data flow.

- **Testing Phase**:
  - Conduct thorough testing to ensure functionality and accuracy of the dashboard.
  - Address any issues or bugs identified during testing.

- **Training Phase**:
  - Develop training materials and conduct training sessions for end-users.
  - Provide ongoing support and resources for effective dashboard utilization.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the Consultation Phase
  - Deliverable: Requirements Document

- **Milestone 2**: Finalization of Dashboard Design
  - Deliverable: Approved Wireframes and Prototypes

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Development Phase
  - Deliverable: Functional HR Analytics Dashboard

- **Milestone 4**: Successful Testing Completion
  - Deliverable: Testing Report and Bug Fixes

- **Milestone 5**: Training and Handover
  - Deliverable: Training Materials and Session Completion Certificate
