
# Project Plan Template: HRIS Feedback and Survey Integration

## 1. Executive Summary

The HRIS Feedback and Survey Integration project aims to enhance the existing HR Information System by incorporating robust feedback and survey tools. The primary objective of this project is to enable organizations to systematically collect employee insights, fostering a culture of open communication and continuous improvement. By integrating suitable survey platforms, we will customize survey templates that align with organizational goals, ensuring that the feedback collected is relevant and actionable. Key outcomes include a seamless data flow between the HRIS and the feedback tools, which will empower HR personnel to analyze results effectively. Additionally, we will conduct training sessions to equip HR staff with the skills necessary to utilize the new features and implement strategies based on employee feedback. By the end of this project, clients will have a fully integrated system that captures employee sentiments and translates them into strategic engagement initiatives, ultimately driving higher employee satisfaction and retention.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Identification of Survey Platforms**: Research and evaluate various survey tools that can be integrated with the existing HRIS.
- **Customization of Survey Templates**: Develop and customize survey templates to align with the organization's specific goals and objectives.
- **Integration Development**: Ensure seamless integration between the HRIS and selected survey platforms, focusing on data flow and user experience.
- **Testing and Quality Assurance**: Conduct thorough testing of the integration to ensure functionality and reliability.
- **Training Sessions**: Develop and deliver training sessions for HR personnel on how to utilize the new feedback and survey features effectively.
- **Post-Implementation Support**: Provide ongoing support and maintenance for the integrated system to address any issues and ensure optimal performance.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of survey platform identification
  - **Deliverable**: Report on selected survey platforms with recommendations
- **Milestone 2**: Customization of survey templates
  - **Deliverable**: Set of customized survey templates ready for deployment
- **Milestone 3**: Successful integration of survey tools with HRIS
  - **Deliverable**: Integrated system with documented data flow processes
- **Milestone 4**: Completion of testing and quality assurance
  - **Deliverable**: Testing report with identified issues and resolutions
- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of training sessions
  - **Deliverable**: Training materials and attendance records
- **Milestone 6**: Post-implementation support initiation
  - **Deliverable**: Support plan and contact information for ongoing assistance
