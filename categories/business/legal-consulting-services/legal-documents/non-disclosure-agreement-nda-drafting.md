
# Project Plan Template: Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) Drafting

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with {{Client Name}} to gather information about the sensitive information to be protected and the specific context of the NDA.
  
- **Research and Analysis**: Review relevant laws and regulations applicable to NDAs in the jurisdiction of {{Client Name}}. Analyze industry standards and best practices for NDA clauses.

- **Drafting the NDA**: Create a draft NDA that includes:
  - Definition of confidential information
  - Obligations of the receiving party
  - Duration of confidentiality
  - Exclusions from confidentiality
  - Remedies for breach of agreement
  - Governing law and jurisdiction

- **Review and Revisions**: Present the draft NDA to {{Client Name}} for feedback. Make necessary revisions based on the feedback received.

- **Finalization**: Prepare the final version of the NDA, ensuring all legal requirements are met and that it reflects the agreed-upon terms.

- **Delivery**: Provide the finalized NDA document to {{Client Name}} in both digital and hard copy formats.
