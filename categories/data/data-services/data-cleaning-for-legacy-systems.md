
# Project Plan Template: Data Cleaning for Legacy Systems

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Data Cleaning for Legacy Systems" project aims to enhance the data integrity and usability of outdated systems that often impede a business's ability to extract valuable insights. In today's data-driven landscape, the quality of data is paramount for informed decision-making. This project will involve a comprehensive assessment of existing data quality, focusing on identifying inconsistencies, duplicates, and inaccuracies within the legacy systems. Our specialized data cleaning services will employ advanced techniques to rectify these issues, ensuring that the data is reliable and actionable. Key outcomes of this project include a thorough cleaning of the data, a detailed report outlining the cleaning process, the modifications made, and strategic recommendations for ongoing data management practices. By modernizing their data infrastructure, clients will be better positioned to leverage their data for competitive advantage and operational efficiency.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment Phase**: Conduct a thorough evaluation of the existing data within the legacy systems to identify quality issues such as duplicates, inconsistencies, and inaccuracies.
- **Data Cleaning Techniques**: Implement advanced data cleaning methodologies, including but not limited to:
  - Deduplication processes
  - Standardization of data formats
  - Validation of data accuracy against reliable sources
- **Documentation**: Maintain detailed records of the cleaning process, including the specific issues identified and the actions taken to resolve them.
- **Reporting**: Prepare a comprehensive report that includes:
  - An overview of the data quality assessment
  - A summary of the cleaning activities performed
  - Recommendations for ongoing data management and best practices
- **Client Training**: Provide training sessions for client staff on maintaining data integrity and managing data going forward.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Data Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing identified data quality issues.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Implementation of Data Cleaning Techniques
  - Deliverable: Cleaned Data Set with documented changes made.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Final Reporting and Recommendations
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Final Report including assessment, cleaning process, and recommendations.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Client Training Session
  - Deliverable: Training Materials and Session Completion Certificate.
