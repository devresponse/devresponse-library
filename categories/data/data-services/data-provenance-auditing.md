
# Project Plan Template: Data Provenance Auditing

## 1. Executive Summary

The Data Provenance Auditing project aims to establish a comprehensive framework for tracing the origin and transformation of datasets throughout their lifecycle. The primary objective is to enhance data integrity and ensure compliance with industry standards by meticulously documenting data lineage. This project will provide stakeholders with transparent insights into data usage, thereby fostering trust and accountability in data management practices. Key outcomes include the development of a detailed data provenance map, the creation of standardized documentation templates, and the establishment of a robust monitoring system to track data changes over time. By implementing these auditing practices, organizations will mitigate risks associated with data mismanagement and strengthen their overall data governance strategy.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Lineage Mapping**: Develop a comprehensive data provenance map that outlines the flow of data from its origin through various transformations to its final output.
  
- **Documentation Templates**: Create standardized templates for documenting data sources, transformations, and outputs to ensure consistency and clarity in data reporting.

- **Monitoring System**: Establish a monitoring system that tracks changes in data over time, providing real-time insights into data integrity and lineage.

- **Stakeholder Engagement**: Conduct workshops and meetings with stakeholders to gather requirements, present findings, and ensure alignment with organizational goals.

- **Compliance Assessment**: Evaluate current data management practices against industry standards and regulations to identify gaps and areas for improvement.

- **Training and Support**: Provide training sessions and ongoing support for staff to ensure effective implementation and adherence to the new data provenance auditing framework.

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of initial stakeholder meetings and requirements gathering.
