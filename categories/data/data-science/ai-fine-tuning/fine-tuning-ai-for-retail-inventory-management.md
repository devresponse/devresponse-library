
# Project Plan Template: Fine-Tuning AI for Retail Inventory Management

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Fine-Tuning AI for Retail Inventory Management" project aims to enhance the capabilities of a pre-trained AI forecasting model specifically designed for retail inventory management. The primary objective is to improve the model's accuracy in predicting demand, which will lead to optimized inventory levels and a significant reduction in waste. This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of historical sales data, identification of seasonal trends, and the integration of external factors such as promotions and market conditions. The fine-tuning process will encompass adjusting hyperparameters, retraining the model with updated datasets, and validating its performance through rigorous testing. By the conclusion of this project, the client will receive a customized forecasting model tailored to their unique operational needs, ultimately contributing to more efficient inventory management and substantial cost savings.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Collection and Preparation**
  - Gather historical sales data from the client’s database.
  - Clean and preprocess the data to ensure quality and consistency.
  - Identify and extract relevant external factors (e.g., promotions, market conditions).

- **Analysis of Historical Data**
  - Conduct exploratory data analysis (EDA) to identify trends and patterns.
  - Analyze seasonal variations and their impact on inventory levels.

- **Model Fine-Tuning**
  - Adjust hyperparameters of the pre-trained AI model to enhance performance.
  - Retrain the model using the prepared datasets, incorporating insights from the EDA.
  - Implement techniques such as cross-validation to ensure robustness.

- **Performance Validation**
  - Develop a testing framework to evaluate the model’s forecasting accuracy.
  - Compare the fine-tuned model’s predictions against actual sales data.
  - Iterate on the model based on validation results to achieve optimal performance.

- **Delivery and Documentation**
  - Provide the client with the customized forecasting model.
  - Create comprehensive documentation detailing the model’s functionality and usage.
  - Conduct a training session for the client’s team on how to utilize the model effectively.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Data Collection Completed**
  - Deliverable: Cleaned and prepared dataset ready for analysis.
  
- **Milestone 2: Exploratory Data Analysis Completed**
  - Deliverable: EDA report highlighting key trends and insights.
  
- **Milestone 3: Model Fine-Tuning Completed**
  - Deliverable: Fine-tuned AI forecasting model with adjusted hyperparameters.
  
- **Milestone 4: Performance Validation Completed**
  - Deliverable: Performance validation report with accuracy metrics.
  
- **Milestone 5: Final Delivery**
  - Deliverable: Customized forecasting model and comprehensive documentation.
