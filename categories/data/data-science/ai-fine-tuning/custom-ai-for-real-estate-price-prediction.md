
# Project Plan Template: Custom AI for Real Estate Price Prediction

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Custom AI for Real Estate Price Prediction" project aims to enhance the predictive capabilities of a pre-trained regression model by fine-tuning it specifically for the real estate market. The primary objective is to incorporate local market trends, property features, and other relevant variables that significantly influence real estate prices. This project will involve a comprehensive approach that includes data collection, preprocessing, and feature engineering to ensure the model accurately reflects the nuances of the real estate market. Key outcomes will include a robust model capable of making precise predictions, a detailed report on performance metrics, insights gained during the fine-tuning process, and actionable recommendations for future improvements. By leveraging AI, clients will be empowered to make informed real estate investment decisions, ultimately leading to better financial outcomes and strategic market positioning.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Collection**: 
  - Identify and gather relevant datasets, including historical property prices, local economic indicators, and demographic data.
  - Ensure data diversity to capture various market conditions and property types.

- **Data Preprocessing**: 
  - Clean and preprocess the collected data to handle missing values, outliers, and inconsistencies.
  - Normalize and standardize data to prepare it for model training.

- **Feature Engineering**: 
  - Develop new features that capture local market trends and property characteristics.
  - Select and transform features to enhance model performance.

- **Model Fine-Tuning**: 
  - Fine-tune the pre-trained regression model using the prepared dataset.
  - Experiment with different hyperparameters and training techniques to optimize model performance.

- **Testing and Validation**: 
  - Conduct rigorous testing to evaluate model accuracy and reliability.
  - Validate the model against a separate test dataset to ensure generalizability.

- **Reporting**: 
  - Compile a comprehensive report detailing model performance metrics, insights from the fine-tuning process, and recommendations for future improvements.
  - Present findings to stakeholders and provide guidance on implementation.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Data Collection

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Feature Engineering

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Model Fine-Tuning
  - Deliverable: Fine-tuned regression model.

- **Milestone 5**: Completion of Testing and Validation
  - Deliverable: Validation report with performance metrics.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Report Submission
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report with insights and recommendations.
