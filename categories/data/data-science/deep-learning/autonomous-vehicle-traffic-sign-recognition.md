
# Project Plan Template: Autonomous Vehicle Traffic Sign Recognition

## 1. Executive Summary

The Autonomous Vehicle Traffic Sign Recognition project aims to develop a sophisticated neural network capable of accurately identifying and classifying traffic signs in real-time. This initiative is critical for enhancing the safety and efficiency of autonomous driving systems. The project will encompass several key objectives, including the collection of a diverse dataset of traffic signs, preprocessing of the data to ensure quality and consistency, and the design and implementation of a convolutional neural network (CNN) specifically tailored for image recognition tasks. Key outcomes of this project will include a fully functional model that demonstrates high accuracy and speed in recognizing traffic signs under various environmental conditions. Additionally, comprehensive documentation will be provided, detailing the methodology, training processes, and usage guidelines to facilitate further development and integration into autonomous vehicle systems. By achieving these objectives, the project will contribute significantly to the advancement of autonomous driving technology, ensuring safer roads and improved traffic management.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Collection**: 
  - Gather a diverse dataset of traffic signs from various sources, including public datasets and custom data collection.
  - Ensure the dataset includes images taken under different lighting conditions, angles, and distances.

- **Data Preprocessing**:
  - Clean and preprocess the collected images to standardize size, resolution, and format.
  - Augment the dataset to increase variability and robustness, including techniques such as rotation, flipping, and color adjustments.

- **Model Design**:
  - Design a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture suitable for image recognition tasks.
  - Implement layers and configurations that optimize performance for traffic sign classification.

- **Model Training**:
  - Train the CNN on the prepared dataset, utilizing techniques such as transfer learning and hyperparameter tuning to enhance model performance.
  - Monitor training progress and adjust parameters as necessary to prevent overfitting.

- **Performance Evaluation**:
  - Establish performance metrics, including accuracy, precision, recall, and inference speed.
  - Conduct rigorous testing of the model across various scenarios to ensure reliability and effectiveness in real-time applications.

- **Documentation**:
  - Prepare comprehensive documentation that includes the methodology, training processes, and guidelines for model usage and integration.
  - Provide a user manual for developers and engineers who will implement the model in autonomous vehicle systems.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Data Collection
  - Deliverable: A comprehensive dataset of traffic sign images.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Data Preprocessing
  - Deliverable: A cleaned and augmented dataset ready for training.

- **Milestone 3**: Model Design Finalization
  - Deliverable: Documented CNN architecture and design specifications.

- **Milestone 4**: Model Training Completion
  - Deliverable: A trained CNN model with performance metrics.

- **Milestone 5**: Performance Evaluation
  - Deliverable: Evaluation report detailing model accuracy and speed.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Documentation
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive documentation and user manual.
