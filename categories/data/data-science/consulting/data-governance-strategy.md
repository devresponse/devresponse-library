
# Project Plan Template: Data Governance Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Data Governance Strategy project is to establish a robust framework that ensures data integrity, compliance, and effective management across the organization. In today's data-driven landscape, organizations face increasing challenges related to data quality and regulatory compliance. This project will provide a comprehensive assessment of your current data management practices, identifying gaps and proposing actionable recommendations tailored to your business needs. Key outcomes will include the development of policies, procedures, and standards that govern data management practices, the creation of a data stewardship model, and the establishment of data quality metrics and compliance guidelines. By the end of this project, your organization will possess a clear roadmap for effective data governance, enabling enhanced data quality, risk mitigation, and informed decision-making based on reliable data.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Assessment**: Conduct a thorough evaluation of current data management practices within the organization.
- **Gap Analysis**: Identify gaps in existing data governance practices and compliance with relevant regulations.
- **Framework Development**: Create a comprehensive data governance framework that includes:
  - Policies for data management
  - Procedures for data handling and processing
  - Standards for data quality and compliance
- **Data Stewardship Model**: Develop a model that outlines roles and responsibilities for data stewardship within the organization.
- **Data Quality Metrics**: Establish metrics to measure and monitor data quality across various data sets.
- **Compliance Guidelines**: Create guidelines to ensure adherence to relevant regulations and standards.
- **Training and Support**: Provide training sessions for staff on the new data governance framework and ongoing support for implementation.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Initial Assessment
  - Deliverable: Assessment Report detailing current practices and identified gaps.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Gap Analysis Report
  - Deliverable: Gap Analysis Report with recommendations for improvement.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Draft Data Governance Framework
  - Deliverable: Draft document outlining policies, procedures, and standards.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Data Governance Framework
  - Deliverable: Finalized Data Governance Framework document.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Data Stewardship Model
  - Deliverable: Document outlining roles and responsibilities for data stewardship.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Data Quality Metrics
  - Deliverable: Report on established data quality metrics.
  
- **Milestone 7**: Compliance Guidelines
  - Deliverable: Document detailing compliance guidelines.
  
- **Milestone 8**: Training Sessions
  - Deliverable: Training materials and completion of training sessions.
