
# Project Plan Template: Retail Sales Demand Prediction

## 1. Executive Summary

The Retail Sales Demand Prediction project aims to develop a robust time series model that accurately forecasts future retail sales by analyzing historical sales data and identifying seasonal trends. The primary objective of this project is to provide retailers with a reliable tool for predicting sales, which will enable them to optimize inventory management, enhance marketing strategies, and improve overall business planning. The project will involve the collection and cleaning of historical sales data, followed by exploratory data analysis to uncover patterns and insights. A selected time series model will be trained and validated using appropriate statistical techniques, ensuring that it captures the underlying trends and seasonality inherent in the data. Key outcomes will include a comprehensive report detailing the model's performance, along with visualizations that illustrate the predicted sales trends. By leveraging advanced data science techniques, clients will gain actionable insights that can significantly impact their sales forecasting accuracy.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Collection**: Gather historical retail sales data from various sources, including internal databases and external market data.
- **Data Cleaning**: Process the collected data to remove inconsistencies, handle missing values, and ensure data quality.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**: Conduct EDA to identify patterns, trends, and seasonal effects in the sales data.
- **Model Selection**: Evaluate and select appropriate time series forecasting models (e.g., ARIMA, Exponential Smoothing, Prophet).
- **Model Training and Validation**: Train the selected model using historical data and validate its performance using statistical metrics (e.g., RMSE, MAE).
- **Performance Reporting**: Generate a comprehensive report detailing the model's performance, including insights from the EDA and validation results.
- **Visualization**: Create visualizations to illustrate predicted sales trends and seasonal patterns.
- **Client Presentation**: Present findings and recommendations to stakeholders, ensuring clarity and actionable insights.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Data Collection
  - Deliverable: Collected and cleaned dataset ready for analysis.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Exploratory Data Analysis
  - Deliverable: EDA report highlighting key patterns and insights.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Model Selection and Training
  - Deliverable: Selected time series model with training results.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Model Validation
  - Deliverable: Validation report with performance metrics.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Report and Visualizations
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report and visualizations illustrating predicted sales trends.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Client Presentation
  - Deliverable: Presentation to stakeholders summarizing findings and recommendations.
