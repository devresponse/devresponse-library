
# Project Plan Template: Electric Vehicle Charging Demand Forecasting

## 1. Executive Summary
The Electric Vehicle Charging Demand Forecasting project aims to accurately predict the future demand for electric vehicle (EV) charging stations by leveraging historical usage data. The primary objective is to analyze various data sources, including charging station usage logs, geographical data, and demographic information, to derive actionable insights. By employing advanced time series analysis techniques, we will identify key trends, seasonal patterns, and anomalies that influence charging station usage. The expected outcomes include a comprehensive report detailing our methodology, findings, and a predictive model that highlights peak usage times and potential growth areas for new charging stations. This project is crucial for stakeholders in the EV industry, such as charging station operators, urban planners, and policymakers, as it will provide the necessary insights to optimize existing infrastructure and ensure that adequate resources are in place to meet the anticipated demand for EV charging.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work
- **Data Collection**: Gather historical usage data from charging station logs, geographical data, and demographic information from relevant sources.
- **Data Cleaning**: Process and clean the collected data to ensure accuracy and consistency for analysis.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**: Conduct EDA to understand data characteristics, identify trends, and detect anomalies.
- **Time Series Analysis**: Apply advanced time series analysis techniques to model the data, focusing on identifying seasonal patterns and forecasting future demand.
- **Model Development**: Develop a predictive model that estimates future charging station usage based on historical data.
- **Validation**: Validate the model using appropriate statistical methods to ensure reliability and accuracy.
- **Reporting**: Compile a comprehensive report that includes methodology, findings, visualizations, and actionable recommendations for stakeholders.
- **Presentation**: Present the findings to stakeholders, highlighting key insights and recommendations for infrastructure optimization.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1**: Completion of data collection  
  **Deliverable**: Data collection report detailing sources and datasets used.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of data cleaning

  **Deliverable**: Predictive model with documentation of methodology.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Model validation

- **Milestone 6**: Final report and presentation  
  **Deliverable**: Comprehensive report and presentation slides for stakeholders.
