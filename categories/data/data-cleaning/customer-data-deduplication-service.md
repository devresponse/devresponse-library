
# Project Plan Template: Customer Data Deduplication Service

## 1. Executive Summary

The Customer Data Deduplication Service aims to enhance the integrity of your customer database by meticulously identifying and eliminating duplicate records. In today's data-driven landscape, businesses rely heavily on accurate customer information for effective marketing and customer relationship management (CRM). This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of your existing customer data, employing advanced algorithms alongside manual verification techniques to ensure that every duplicate entry is identified and removed. The key outcomes of this project include a detailed report of the findings, which will outline the number of duplicates found and the actions taken. By the conclusion of this project, you will possess a clean and reliable customer database that not only enhances your marketing efforts but also improves customer interactions, ultimately leading to better business outcomes. Our objective is to provide you with a streamlined data set that supports informed decision-making and fosters stronger customer relationships.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Collection**: Gather existing customer data from various sources, including CRM systems, spreadsheets, and databases.
- **Data Analysis**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the collected data to identify potential duplicate records using advanced algorithms.
- **Manual Verification**: Implement manual verification techniques to confirm the accuracy of identified duplicates.
- **Data Cleaning**: Remove or merge duplicate records based on predefined criteria and client preferences.
- **Reporting**: Generate a detailed report summarizing the findings, including the number of duplicates identified, actions taken, and recommendations for future data management practices.
- **Client Review**: Present the findings and report to the client for review and feedback.
- **Final Adjustments**: Make any necessary adjustments based on client feedback before finalizing the cleaned database.

- **Milestone 1**: Data Collection Completed

  - Deliverable: Final report detailing findings and actions taken
- **Milestone 6**: Client Review and Feedback

  - Deliverable: Final cleaned database and project closure report
