
# Project Plan Template: Real Estate Listings Data Cleanup

## 1. Executive Summary

The Real Estate Listings Data Cleanup project aims to significantly enhance the quality and usability of real estate data by implementing a comprehensive data cleaning strategy. The primary objective is to conduct a thorough examination of existing listings to identify and eliminate duplicate entries, ensuring that each property is represented uniquely within the dataset. This project will also address various formatting issues that may impede effective data analysis, including inconsistent address formats, incorrect pricing notations, and varying units of measurement. Furthermore, we will standardize property attributes such as property type, square footage, and amenities to create a cohesive dataset that is easy to navigate and analyze. By the conclusion of this project, stakeholders will receive a clean, reliable dataset that supports accurate decision-making and enhances overall real estate operations. Key outcomes include improved data integrity, streamlined data management processes, and enhanced analytical capabilities, ultimately leading to better strategic decisions in real estate investments.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Assessment**: Conduct an initial assessment of the existing real estate listings dataset to identify the extent of duplicates and formatting issues.
- **Duplicate Removal**: Implement algorithms and manual checks to identify and remove duplicate entries, ensuring each property is listed only once.
- **Formatting Standardization**: Review and correct inconsistent address formats, pricing notations, and units of measurement to ensure uniformity across the dataset.
- **Attribute Standardization**: Standardize property attributes such as property type, square footage, and amenities to ensure consistency and ease of analysis.
- **Quality Assurance**: Perform quality checks throughout the data cleaning process to ensure accuracy and completeness of the dataset.
- **Final Review and Delivery**: Conduct a final review of the cleaned dataset and deliver the final product to stakeholders, along with documentation outlining the changes made.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Data Assessment  
  **Deliverable**: Assessment report detailing the current state of the dataset and identified issues.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Duplicate Removal  
  **Deliverable**: Cleaned dataset with duplicates removed, along with a summary of duplicates identified.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Formatting Standardization  
  **Deliverable**: Dataset with standardized formatting, including corrected addresses and pricing notations.

- **Milestone 4**: Completion of Attribute Standardization  
  **Deliverable**: Fully standardized dataset with consistent property attributes.

- **Milestone 5**: Quality Assurance Review  
  **Deliverable**: Quality assurance report confirming the accuracy and completeness of the cleaned dataset.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Review and Delivery  
  **Deliverable**: Final cleaned dataset and documentation outlining the changes made, delivered to stakeholders.
