
# Project Plan Template: Healthcare Patient Record Cleaning

## 1. Executive Summary

The Healthcare Patient Record Cleaning project aims to enhance the quality and usability of patient data by meticulously cleaning and organizing records. The primary objective of this project is to identify and resolve duplicate entries, correct inaccuracies, and standardize data formats to ensure consistency across the dataset. Accurate patient records are vital for effective healthcare analytics, as they directly impact decision-making and patient care outcomes. This project will follow a structured approach, including data assessment, error identification, and the implementation of cleaning techniques. Key outcomes include a comprehensive, error-free dataset that is ready for analysis, enabling better insights and improved healthcare services. By the end of this project, clients will benefit from enhanced data integrity, which will support better patient care and operational efficiency.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Assessment**: Conduct an initial review of the existing patient records to understand the current state of the data and identify areas that require cleaning.
  
- **Error Identification**: Utilize data profiling techniques to identify duplicate entries, inaccuracies, and inconsistencies in data formats.

- **Data Cleaning Techniques**:
  - **Deduplication**: Implement algorithms to identify and merge duplicate records.
  - **Correction of Inaccuracies**: Validate data against reliable sources to correct any inaccuracies found.
  - **Standardization**: Apply standard formats for dates, addresses, and other relevant fields to ensure consistency across the dataset.

- **Quality Assurance**: Perform quality checks throughout the cleaning process to ensure that the data meets the required standards.

- **Final Review and Delivery**: Compile the cleaned dataset and conduct a final review before delivering it to the client.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Data Assessment

- **Milestone 3**: Implementation of Cleaning Techniques
