
# Project Plan Template: Survey Response Data Validation

## 1. Executive Summary

The Survey Response Data Validation project aims to significantly enhance the quality and reliability of survey data collected. The primary objective is to conduct a meticulous examination of the dataset to identify and eliminate incomplete entries, ensuring that only complete and valid responses are retained. This process is crucial for maintaining the integrity of the data, as it directly impacts the accuracy of subsequent analyses. Additionally, the project will address any formatting issues to ensure uniformity across the dataset, which is essential for accurate interpretation of results. We will also focus on ensuring data consistency by identifying and rectifying discrepancies within the responses. By the conclusion of this project, stakeholders will receive a clean, reliable dataset that is primed for insightful analysis, empowering them to make informed decisions based on accurate survey results. The key outcomes include a validated dataset, improved data quality, and enhanced decision-making capabilities.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Examination**: Conduct a thorough review of the survey dataset to identify incomplete and invalid entries.
- **Data Cleaning**: Remove incomplete entries and correct any formatting issues to ensure uniformity.
- **Data Consistency Checks**: Identify and rectify discrepancies in the responses to ensure data consistency.
- **Documentation**: Provide detailed documentation of the cleaning process, including methodologies used and changes made to the dataset.
- **Final Dataset Delivery**: Deliver a clean and validated dataset ready for analysis, along with a summary report outlining the cleaning process and key findings.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Data Review
  - Deliverable: Report on data quality assessment and identified issues.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Data Cleaning Process
  - Deliverable: Cleaned dataset with all incomplete entries removed and formatting issues corrected.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Consistency Checks
  - Deliverable: Report on data consistency checks and discrepancies resolved.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Final validated dataset and comprehensive summary report.
