## 1. Executive Summary

The Facial Emotion Annotation project aims to enhance the capabilities of AI models in recognizing human emotions through facial expressions. The primary objective of this project is to meticulously annotate a diverse dataset of facial images, labeling each image with specific emotions such as {{emotion_1}}, {{emotion_2}}, {{emotion_3}}, {{emotion_4}}, and {{emotion_5}}. ## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Facial Emotion Annotation project includes the following key activities:

- **Dataset Collection**: Gather a diverse set of facial images representing a wide range of emotions. This dataset should include images from various demographics to ensure inclusivity and representation.
  
- **Annotation Guidelines Development**: Create comprehensive guidelines for annotators to ensure consistency in labeling emotions. This will include definitions and examples for each emotion category: {{emotion_1}}, {{emotion_2}}, {{emotion_3}}, {{emotion_4}}, and {{emotion_5}}.

- **Annotation Process**: Employ trained annotators to label the images according to the established guidelines. Each image will be reviewed to ensure accuracy and adherence to the guidelines.

- **Quality Assurance**: Implement a quality assurance process to review a sample of the annotated images for accuracy. This may involve cross-checking annotations and providing feedback to annotators.

- **Final Dataset Compilation**: Compile the annotated images into a structured dataset, ensuring that it is easily accessible for machine learning model training.

- **Documentation**: Provide detailed documentation of the dataset, including the annotation process, guidelines, and any relevant metadata.
