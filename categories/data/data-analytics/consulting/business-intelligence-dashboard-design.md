
# Project Plan Template: Business Intelligence Dashboard Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The Business Intelligence Dashboard Design project aims to create a comprehensive and interactive dashboard tailored specifically to the unique needs of {{Client Organization}}. The primary objective is to empower your team with a powerful tool for visualizing key performance indicators (KPIs) that facilitate data-driven decision-making. This project will begin with an in-depth consultation to understand your business objectives, data sources, and the critical metrics that drive your operations. Key outcomes include the delivery of wireframes and prototypes for stakeholder feedback, followed by the final interactive dashboard that integrates seamlessly with your existing data systems. Additionally, we will provide thorough documentation to ensure your team can effectively use and maintain the dashboard. Ultimately, this project is designed to enhance your strategic planning and operational efficiency, enabling your organization to leverage data effectively for informed decision-making.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct meetings with key stakeholders to gather requirements and understand business objectives.
  - Identify and document existing data sources and the specific metrics that are crucial for operations.

- **Design Phase**: 
  - Develop wireframes and prototypes of the dashboard for review and feedback.
  - Iterate on designs based on stakeholder input to ensure alignment with the organization's vision.

- **Implementation Phase**: 
  - Build the final interactive dashboard using the approved designs.
  - Integrate the dashboard with existing data systems to ensure real-time data visualization.

- **Documentation and Training**: 
  - Create comprehensive documentation on dashboard usage and maintenance.
  - Conduct training sessions for end-users to ensure effective utilization of the dashboard.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the consultation phase
  - Deliverable: Requirements document outlining business objectives and data sources.

- **Milestone 2**: Approval of wireframes and prototypes
  - Deliverable: Set of wireframes and prototypes for stakeholder review.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of the dashboard development
  - Deliverable: Fully functional interactive dashboard.

- **Milestone 4**: Delivery of documentation and training
  - Deliverable: User manual and training session completion.
