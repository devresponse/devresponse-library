
# Project Plan Template: Predictive Sales Forecasting Model

## 1. Executive Summary

The Predictive Sales Forecasting Model project aims to develop a robust Business Intelligence (BI) analytics model that leverages historical sales data to predict future sales trends. The primary objective of this project is to provide the client with accurate sales forecasts that can inform strategic decision-making and optimize sales strategies. By collecting and cleaning historical sales data, we will apply advanced predictive algorithms to generate insights that are both actionable and relevant. Key outcomes of this project include a comprehensive analysis of factors influencing sales trends, which will enable the client to understand underlying patterns in their data. The final deliverable will be a user-friendly dashboard that visualizes forecasted sales trends and key performance metrics, ensuring that stakeholders can easily interpret results and make informed decisions. This project is essential for businesses seeking to enhance operational efficiency and drive sales growth.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Collection**: Gather historical sales data from various sources, including CRM systems, sales databases, and external market data.
- **Data Cleaning**: Perform data cleaning to ensure accuracy and consistency, addressing any missing or erroneous data points.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**: Conduct EDA to identify trends, patterns, and correlations within the historical sales data.
- **Model Development**: Apply advanced predictive algorithms (e.g., time series analysis, regression models, machine learning techniques) to develop the forecasting model.
- **Factors Analysis**: Analyze external and internal factors influencing sales trends, such as seasonality, market conditions, and promotional activities.
- **Dashboard Creation**: Design and develop a user-friendly dashboard that visualizes forecasted sales trends and key performance metrics.
- **Stakeholder Training**: Provide training sessions for stakeholders on how to use the dashboard and interpret the results.
- **Documentation**: Prepare comprehensive documentation detailing the methodology, model assumptions, and user guide for the dashboard.
