
# Project Plan Template: Supplier Diversity Analytics Dashboard

## 1. Executive Summary

The Supplier Diversity Analytics Dashboard project aims to create a comprehensive and user-friendly dashboard that enables organizations to effectively analyze and monitor their supplier diversity metrics. The primary objective of this project is to integrate various data sources to provide real-time insights into supplier demographics, performance, and engagement levels. By leveraging advanced data visualization techniques, the dashboard will highlight key performance indicators (KPIs) related to supplier diversity, allowing stakeholders to identify trends, track progress towards diversity goals, and make data-driven decisions. Key outcomes include enhanced visibility into supplier diversity efforts, improved tracking of diversity-related KPIs, and actionable insights that drive equitable business practices. Ultimately, this dashboard will empower organizations to enhance supplier inclusivity, foster equitable business practices, and drive meaningful change within their supply chains.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Collection**: Gather relevant data from various internal and external sources, including supplier databases, procurement systems, and diversity reports.
- **Data Cleaning**: Ensure the accuracy and consistency of the collected data by performing data cleaning and validation processes.
- **Dashboard Design**: Create intuitive visualizations that effectively communicate supplier diversity metrics, including charts, graphs, and tables.
- **KPI Development**: Identify and define key performance indicators (KPIs) that will be tracked within the dashboard.
- **User Engagement Features**: Implement interactive features such as filters, drill-down capabilities, and customizable views to enhance user engagement.
- **Testing and Validation**: Conduct thorough testing of the dashboard to ensure functionality, usability, and accuracy of the displayed data.
- **Training and Documentation**: Provide training sessions for end-users and develop comprehensive documentation to support dashboard usage.
- **Deployment**: Launch the dashboard and ensure it is accessible to all relevant stakeholders.
