
# Project Plan Template: Contact List Organization

## 1. Executive Summary

The Contact List Organization project aims to streamline and enhance the management of contact information by compiling and organizing details from various sources into a cohesive and well-structured address book. This project is essential for businesses and individuals who require quick access to accurate contact information. The primary objective is to gather data from emails, spreadsheets, and other digital platforms, ensuring that all entries are verified for accuracy and completeness. Key outcomes include a user-friendly address book that is free of duplicates and errors, allowing for efficient communication and improved productivity. By focusing on attention to detail, this project will deliver a reliable and efficient solution to clients' contact management needs, ultimately facilitating better networking and relationship management.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Collection**: 
  - Gather contact information from various sources including emails, spreadsheets, and digital platforms.
  - Identify and categorize contacts based on predefined criteria (e.g., personal, professional, etc.).

- **Data Verification**: 
  - Verify the accuracy and completeness of collected data.
  - Cross-check entries to eliminate duplicates and errors.

- **Data Organization**: 
  - Structure the contact information into a user-friendly format.
  - Create categories and tags for easy navigation and retrieval.

- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Provide the organized address book in a format agreed upon with the client (e.g., Excel, Google Sheets, etc.).
  - Ensure that the final product is easy to use and accessible for future communications.

- **Post-Delivery Support**: 
  - Offer a brief training session on how to use the organized address book.
  - Provide a support period for any questions or issues that may arise post-delivery.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Project Kickoff
  - Deliverable: Project initiation meeting and confirmation of project scope.

- **Milestone 2**: Data Collection Completion
  - Deliverable: Compilation of all contact information from identified sources.

- **Milestone 3**: Data Verification Completion
  - Deliverable: Verified and cleaned contact list ready for organization.

- **Milestone 4**: Data Organization Completion
  - Deliverable: Structured and categorized address book.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Organized address book delivered in the agreed format.

- **Milestone 6**: Post-Delivery Support
  - Deliverable: Completion of training session and support period.
