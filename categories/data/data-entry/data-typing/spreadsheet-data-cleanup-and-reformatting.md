
# Project Plan Template: Spreadsheet Data Cleanup and Reformatting

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the Spreadsheet Data Cleanup and Reformatting project is to enhance the accuracy and usability of the provided spreadsheet data. This project will involve a thorough review of the existing spreadsheet to identify inconsistencies, errors, and formatting issues. The selected freelancer will manually input corrections, which includes rectifying typos, standardizing formats for dates, currency, and numerical values, and ensuring that all data entries are consistent and properly aligned. The key outcomes of this project will be a polished spreadsheet that meets the client's specifications, is free from errors, and is ready for analysis or reporting. By improving the quality of the data, this project will support businesses in making informed decisions and generating reliable reports. Effective communication regarding significant findings or challenges encountered during the cleanup process will also be a critical component of this project, ensuring transparency and collaboration between the freelancer and the client.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Review**: Conduct a comprehensive review of the provided spreadsheet to identify inconsistencies, errors, and formatting issues.
- **Error Correction**: Manually correct typos and inaccuracies found in the data entries.
- **Standardization**: Standardize formats for:
  - Dates (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY)
  - Currency (e.g., USD, EUR)
  - Numerical values (e.g., decimal places, thousand separators)
- **Data Alignment**: Ensure all data entries are consistent and properly aligned within the spreadsheet.
- **Quality Assurance**: Perform a final review of the cleaned and reformatted spreadsheet to ensure all corrections have been made and that the data is ready for analysis.
- **Communication**: Provide regular updates to the client regarding significant findings or challenges encountered during the cleanup process.
- **Final Deliverable**: Submit a polished spreadsheet that meets the client's specifications and is free from errors.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Review of Spreadsheet
  - **Deliverable**: Report on identified inconsistencies and errors.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Data Cleanup
  - **Deliverable**: Cleaned and reformatted spreadsheet with corrections made.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Final Quality Assurance Review
  - **Deliverable**: Final report confirming the accuracy and usability of the spreadsheet.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Project Closure
  - **Deliverable**: Submission of the final polished spreadsheet and project completion report.
