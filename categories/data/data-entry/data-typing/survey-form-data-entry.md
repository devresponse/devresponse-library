
# Project Plan Template: Survey Form Data Entry

## 1. Executive Summary

The Survey Form Data Entry project aims to facilitate the transition of physical survey data into a digital format, ensuring that organizations can effectively analyze and utilize the information gathered. The primary objective is to accurately transfer responses from paper forms into structured digital files, such as spreadsheets or databases, while maintaining the integrity and accuracy of the data. Key outcomes of this project include a comprehensive digital dataset that is free from errors, organized according to specified fields, and ready for analysis and reporting. This meticulous process not only enhances data accessibility but also supports informed decision-making for organizations. By employing skilled freelancers with strong typing abilities and data entry experience, we aim to deliver a high-quality final product that meets the client's expectations and requirements.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Entry**: Accurately type responses from physical survey forms into digital formats, ensuring no errors are made during the process.
- **Data Categorization**: Organize and categorize responses into specified fields as per the project requirements.
- **Quality Assurance**: Implement a quality control process to verify the accuracy of the entered data, including cross-referencing with original survey forms.
- **Final Deliverable**: Provide a clean, organized digital file (e.g., Excel spreadsheet or database) that reflects all survey responses, ready for analysis and reporting.
- **Communication**: Maintain regular communication with the project stakeholders to provide updates on progress and address any queries or concerns.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Project Kick-off
  - Deliverable: Project initiation meeting and confirmation of project scope and timelines.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Data Entry Completion
  - Deliverable: Completion of data entry for all survey forms.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Quality Assurance Review
  - Deliverable: Quality assurance report detailing the accuracy of the data entered.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Submission
  - Deliverable: Submission of the final organized digital file to the client.
