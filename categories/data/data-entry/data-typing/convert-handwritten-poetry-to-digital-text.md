
# Project Plan Template: Handwritten Poetry to Digital Text

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Handwritten Poetry to Digital Text" project aims to meticulously convert handwritten poetry into a digital format while preserving the essence, formatting, and stylistic elements of the original pieces. The primary objective is to create a clean, editable digital document that accurately reflects the unique characteristics of each poem, including line breaks, indentation, and any artistic flourishes present in the handwriting. This project will not only enhance accessibility but also ensure that the beauty and nuances of the handwritten form are maintained in the digital realm. Key outcomes include a comprehensive digital archive of the poetry, improved readability, and the potential for further dissemination and sharing of the poetic works. By engaging freelancers who appreciate the intricacies of poetry and possess a keen eye for detail, we aim to deliver a final product that honors the original artistry while making it available for a broader audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Define project objectives and deliverables.
  - Identify and onboard qualified freelancers with expertise in transcription and poetry.

- **Data Collection**
  - Gather all handwritten poetry pieces to be converted.
  - Ensure that all materials are organized and accessible for transcription.

- **Transcription Process**
  - Carefully transcribe each poem, maintaining original formatting, line breaks, and stylistic elements.
  - Utilize software tools that facilitate accurate transcription while allowing for manual adjustments as needed.

- **Quality Assurance**
  - Implement a review process to ensure accuracy and fidelity to the original handwritten works.
  - Conduct peer reviews where applicable to validate the transcription quality.

- **Final Deliverables**
  - Produce a clean, editable digital document for each poem.
  - Provide a summary report detailing the transcription process and any challenges encountered.

- **Project Closure**
  - Conduct a final review meeting with stakeholders to present deliverables.
  - Gather feedback and document lessons learned for future projects.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Project initiation document and team onboarding completed.
  
- **Milestone 2: Data Collection Completion**

- **Milestone 3: Transcription Phase Completion**
  - Deliverable: Initial transcription of all poems completed.

- **Milestone 4: Quality Assurance Review**
  - Deliverable: Quality assurance report and revisions made based on feedback.

- **Milestone 5: Final Deliverables Submission**

- **Milestone 6: Project Closure**
  - Deliverable: Final project report and feedback session completed.
