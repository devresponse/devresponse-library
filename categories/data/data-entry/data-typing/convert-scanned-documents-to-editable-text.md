
# Project Plan Template: Scanned Documents to Editable Text

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Scanned Documents to Editable Text" project aims to transform scanned paper documents into accurate and editable text files. The primary objective is to provide clients with a reliable service that digitizes their physical records, making them easily accessible and modifiable. This project will involve meticulous manual typing to ensure that every detail is captured accurately, followed by a thorough verification process to cross-check the typed content against the original documents. Key outcomes include the delivery of high-quality, error-free text files that maintain the integrity of the original information. By the end of this project, clients will have a digital repository of their documents that enhances their ability to manage and utilize their data effectively. The project is expected to be completed within a specified timeframe, ensuring timely delivery while upholding the highest standards of accuracy and detail.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Document Review**: Each scanned document will be carefully reviewed to understand its content and structure.
- **Manual Typing**: The freelancer will manually type the content from the scanned documents into a text format, ensuring accuracy and attention to detail.
- **Cross-Referencing**: After typing, the output will be cross-referenced with the original scanned document to identify and correct any discrepancies.
- **Quality Assurance**: A final quality check will be conducted to ensure that the editable text files are free from errors and accurately reflect the original documents.
- **File Formatting**: The final text files will be formatted according to client specifications, ensuring they are ready for use in various applications.
- **Delivery**: Completed files will be delivered to the client in the agreed-upon format and within the established timeframe.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Document Review
  - Deliverable: Summary report of documents to be typed and any potential challenges identified.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Typing Phase
  - Deliverable: First draft of typed documents for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Cross-Referencing and Quality Assurance
  - Deliverable: Error report and final version of documents after corrections.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: All completed editable text files delivered to the client.
