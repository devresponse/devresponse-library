
# Project Plan Template: Genealogical Records Typing

## 1. Executive Summary
The Genealogical Records Typing project aims to meticulously transcribe genealogical records into digital formats, enhancing accessibility for family history research and preservation. The primary objective is to convert handwritten or printed documents into accurate digital representations, ensuring that all names, dates, and pertinent information are captured with precision. This project will result in a comprehensive digital archive that can be easily accessed and shared among family members and researchers. Key outcomes include the creation of organized and formatted digital files in various formats such as spreadsheets, word documents, or specialized genealogy software. Attention to detail is paramount, as these records serve as critical resources for historical research and family lineage verification. Ultimately, the project seeks to facilitate the preservation of family histories and improve the usability of genealogical data for future reference.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work
The scope of work for the Genealogical Records Typing project includes the following tasks:
- **Document Collection**: Gather all relevant genealogical records from the client, including handwritten and printed documents.
- **Transcription**: Accurately type the collected records into specified digital formats, ensuring the inclusion of all names, dates, and relevant information.
- **Quality Assurance**: Implement a quality control process to verify the accuracy of the transcriptions, including proofreading and cross-referencing with original documents.
- **Formatting**: Organize and format the digital files according to the client's specifications, ensuring user-friendliness for future reference.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the completed digital archive to the client in the agreed-upon formats, along with any necessary documentation or instructions for use.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1**: Document Collection Completed
  - Deliverable: All genealogical records gathered and ready for transcription.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Initial Transcription Completed
  - Deliverable: First draft of transcribed records in digital format.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Quality Assurance Review Completed
  - Deliverable: Verified and corrected transcriptions ready for final formatting.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Formatting Completed
  - Deliverable: Organized digital files formatted according to client specifications.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Project Completion
  - Deliverable: Final digital archive delivered to the client, along with any necessary documentation.
