
# Project Plan Template: Digital Advertising Benchmarking

## 1. Executive Summary

The Digital Advertising Benchmarking project aims to conduct comprehensive web research to analyze digital advertising campaigns executed by key competitors within the industry. The primary objective is to identify prevailing trends, effective strategies, and performance benchmarks that can inform our client's advertising efforts. This project will involve a thorough examination of various digital platforms, including social media, search engines, and display networks, to gather data on ad formats, messaging, targeting strategies, and engagement metrics. By synthesizing this information, we will provide actionable insights that will help the client optimize their digital advertising strategy, enhance their competitive positioning, and improve overall campaign performance. Key outcomes will include a detailed report summarizing findings, visual representations of data trends, and strategic recommendations based on the analysis, ultimately empowering the client to make informed decisions in their advertising initiatives.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Objectives**: 
  - Analyze digital advertising campaigns of key competitors.
  - Identify trends, strategies, and benchmarks in digital advertising.

- **Research Methodology**:
  - Conduct qualitative and quantitative analysis of digital ads across various platforms.
  - Utilize tools for data collection and analysis, including social media analytics, SEO tools, and advertising platforms.

- **Data Collection**:
  - Gather data on ad formats, messaging, targeting strategies, and engagement metrics.
  - Focus on platforms such as Facebook, Google Ads, Instagram, and display networks.

- **Analysis**:
  - Perform comparative analysis of competitors' advertising strategies.
  - Identify successful tactics and areas for improvement.

- **Deliverables**:
  - A comprehensive report detailing findings and insights.
  - Visual representations of data trends (charts, graphs).
  - Strategic recommendations tailored to the client's needs.
