
# Project Plan Template: Data Archiving and Retention Policy

## 1. Executive Summary

The Data Archiving and Retention Policy project aims to establish a robust framework for managing historical data within {{Organization Name}}. In today's data-driven environment, it is crucial for organizations to effectively manage their data to ensure compliance with legal and regulatory standards while optimizing storage resources. This project will focus on creating a comprehensive policy that outlines procedures for data classification, archiving, and retention timelines tailored to the specific needs of {{Organization Name}}. Key outcomes include a thorough assessment of current data management practices, actionable recommendations for improvements, and the implementation of necessary tools and technologies to support the policy. By the end of this project, {{Organization Name}} will have a clear and efficient framework for managing historical data, minimizing risks associated with data loss, and ensuring compliance with applicable regulations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Assessment of Current Data Management Practices**  
  - Conduct interviews with key stakeholders to understand existing data management processes.
  - Review current data storage solutions and practices.
  - Identify gaps and areas for improvement in data management.

- **Development of Data Classification Framework**  
  - Define categories for data classification based on sensitivity and regulatory requirements.
  - Create guidelines for data labeling and categorization.

- **Creation of Archiving Procedures**  
  - Outline procedures for data archiving, including methods for data transfer and storage.
  - Develop a timeline for archiving data based on classification.

- **Establishment of Retention Timelines**  
  - Define retention periods for different categories of data.
  - Create guidelines for data disposal and destruction.

- **Implementation of Tools and Technologies**  
  - Identify and recommend tools that support data archiving and retention.
  - Develop a plan for the integration of these tools into existing systems.

- **Training and Documentation**  
  - Create training materials for staff on the new data archiving and retention policy.
  - Document the policy and procedures for future reference.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Completion of Current Data Management Assessment**  
  - Deliverable: Assessment report outlining current practices and identified gaps.

- **Milestone 2: Development of Data Classification Framework**  
  - Deliverable: Document detailing the data classification categories and guidelines.

- **Milestone 3: Creation of Archiving Procedures**  
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive archiving procedures document.

- **Milestone 4: Establishment of Retention Timelines**  
  - Deliverable: Retention schedule document for various data categories.

- **Milestone 5: Implementation of Recommended Tools**  
  - Deliverable: Implementation plan and tool recommendations report.

- **Milestone 6: Training and Documentation Completion**  
  - Deliverable: Training materials and finalized policy document.
