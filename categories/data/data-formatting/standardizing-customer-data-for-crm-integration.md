
# Project Plan Template: Standardizing Customer Data for CRM Integration

## 1. Executive Summary

In today's data-driven business environment, the accuracy and standardization of customer data are paramount for effective customer relationship management (CRM). The objective of this project, "Standardizing Customer Data for CRM Integration," is to meticulously format and clean existing customer data to ensure compatibility with various CRM systems. This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of current data sets, identifying inconsistencies, and applying data cleaning techniques to eliminate duplicates, correct errors, and standardize formats. By transforming the data into a structure that aligns with the requirements of the chosen CRM platform, we aim to facilitate seamless integration. Key outcomes include improved data accessibility and enhanced customer interactions, ultimately leading to more effective CRM efforts. Upon project completion, clients will receive a comprehensive and reliable data set, ensuring their CRM initiatives are built on a solid foundation of accurate information.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Analysis**: Review existing customer data sets to identify inconsistencies and areas for improvement.
- **Data Cleaning**: 
  - Remove duplicate entries.
  - Correct errors in customer information (e.g., misspellings, incorrect formats).
  - Standardize data formats (e.g., date formats, address structures).
- **Data Transformation**: 
  - Restructure data to meet the specific requirements of the selected CRM platform.
  - Ensure all fields are correctly mapped to the CRM's data model.
- **Quality Assurance**: Conduct thorough testing to ensure data integrity and accuracy post-cleaning and transformation.
- **Documentation**: Provide detailed documentation of the data cleaning and transformation processes for client reference.
- **Training**: Offer training sessions for client staff on how to maintain data quality moving forward.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Project Kickoff

- **Milestone 2**: Data Analysis Completion

- **Milestone 5**: Quality Assurance Review

- **Milestone 6**: Final Documentation and Training
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive project documentation and training materials.
