
# Project Plan: Employee Productivity Tracker

## 1. Executive Summary

The Employee Productivity Tracker project aims to create a comprehensive visual representation of key productivity metrics for employees within an organization. The primary objective is to design a series of bar and pie charts that effectively illustrate important data points, including the number of tasks completed, total hours worked, and significant project milestones achieved. By utilizing visually appealing and easily interpretable charts, the final deliverables will enable stakeholders to quickly assess employee performance and productivity trends. This project will not only enhance the visibility of employee contributions but also facilitate informed decision-making by transforming complex data sets into straightforward visuals. The project will include a consultation phase to gather specific requirements and preferences from the client, ensuring that the final product aligns with organizational needs. Key outcomes will include improved understanding of productivity metrics, enhanced reporting capabilities, and a user-friendly interface for stakeholders to access and interpret data efficiently.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct meetings with stakeholders to gather requirements and preferences.
  - Identify key productivity metrics to be visualized.
  - Define the target audience for the charts and their specific needs.

- **Design Phase**:
  - Create initial design concepts for bar and pie charts.
  - Develop a style guide to ensure consistency in visuals.
  - Incorporate feedback from stakeholders to refine designs.

- **Data Collection and Analysis**:
  - Gather necessary data on employee productivity metrics.
  - Analyze data to determine the most effective way to represent it visually.

- **Chart Creation**:
  - Develop final versions of bar and pie charts based on approved designs.
  - Ensure charts are clear, simple, and visually appealing.

- **Review and Finalization**:
  - Present final charts to stakeholders for review.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on feedback.
  - Deliver final charts in the agreed-upon format.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the consultation phase
  - Deliverable: Requirements document outlining key metrics and preferences.

- **Milestone 2**: Approval of design concepts
  - Deliverable: Style guide and initial design mock-ups.

- **Milestone 3**: Data collection and analysis completed
  - Deliverable: Data analysis report summarizing findings.

- **Milestone 4**: Final charts created
  - Deliverable: Set of bar and pie charts ready for review.

- **Milestone 5**: Final review and adjustments
  - Deliverable: Finalized charts delivered to stakeholders.
