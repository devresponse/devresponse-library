
# Project Plan Template: Market Share Comparison

## 1. Executive Summary

The Market Share Comparison project aims to deliver a comprehensive visual representation of market share data for various competitors within the {{industry}}. The primary objective is to collect and analyze relevant market data to create visually engaging pie charts that illustrate the percentage breakdown of market shares among competitors. Additionally, we will develop bar charts to depict year-over-year changes in market share, providing a clear visual narrative of trends over time. This project will not only enhance the client's understanding of the competitive landscape but also support strategic decision-making through effective data visualization. Key outcomes include the delivery of high-quality, informative visualizations tailored to the client's specific requirements, ensuring that the final product is both aesthetically pleasing and easy to interpret. By the end of this project, stakeholders will have a clearer insight into market dynamics, enabling them to make informed decisions that drive business growth.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Collection**: 
  - Identify and gather relevant market data from reliable sources.
  - Ensure data accuracy and completeness through validation processes.

- **Data Analysis**: 
  - Analyze the collected data to determine market share percentages for each competitor.
  - Identify trends and patterns in market share over the specified time frame.

- **Visualization Design**: 
  - Create visually engaging pie charts to represent the percentage breakdown of market shares.
  - Develop bar charts to illustrate year-over-year changes in market share.

- **Client Collaboration**: 
  - Conduct meetings with the client to gather specific requirements and preferences for visualizations.
  - Provide drafts for client feedback and make necessary revisions based on input.

- **Final Deliverables**: 
  - Deliver a comprehensive report that includes all visualizations and an analysis of findings.
  - Ensure all visualizations are formatted for clarity and ease of interpretation.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Data Collection Completed
  - Deliverable: Compiled dataset of market share information.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Data Analysis Completed
  - Deliverable: Analysis report detailing market share percentages and trends.

- **Milestone 3**: Initial Visualization Drafts
  - Deliverable: Draft pie charts and bar charts for client review.

- **Milestone 4**: Client Feedback Incorporated
  - Deliverable: Revised visualizations based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Report and Visualizations Delivered
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report with final visualizations and insights.
