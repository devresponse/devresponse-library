
# Project Plan Template: Historical Map Digitization

## 1. Executive Summary

The Historical Map Digitization project aims to transform historical maps into interactive digital formats that enhance the understanding of geographical changes over time. The primary objective is to digitize and georeference a collection of high-resolution scans of historical maps, allowing users to visualize and analyze urban development and landscape transformations. This project will involve meticulous attention to detail during the digitization process to ensure that all intricate features of the maps are accurately captured. Key outcomes include the creation of interactive overlays compatible with GIS applications, which will serve as valuable resources for historians, urban planners, and researchers. By providing a dynamic platform for exploration, this project will facilitate a deeper understanding of historical geography and its implications for contemporary urban planning and development.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Collection of Historical Maps**: Identify and gather high-resolution scans of historical maps from various archives and libraries.
- **Digitization Process**: 
  - Utilize advanced scanning technology to ensure high-quality digital representations of the maps.
  - Employ GIS software to meticulously digitize the maps, capturing all relevant details, including topographical features, landmarks, and urban layouts.
- **Georeferencing**: 
  - Align the digitized maps with current geographical coordinates to ensure accurate spatial representation.
  - Validate the georeferencing process through comparison with contemporary maps and geographical data.
- **Creation of Interactive Overlays**: 
  - Develop interactive features that allow users to toggle between historical and current maps.
  - Ensure compatibility with popular GIS applications for ease of access and use.
- **Quality Assurance**: Conduct thorough testing to ensure the accuracy and functionality of the digitized maps and overlays.
- **Documentation and Training**: Provide comprehensive documentation and training materials for end-users to maximize the utility of the interactive overlays.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Historical Map Collection
  - Deliverable: Compiled repository of high-resolution scans of historical maps.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Digitization Process
  - Deliverable: Digitized versions of all collected historical maps.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Georeferencing
  - Deliverable: Georeferenced maps aligned with current geographical coordinates.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Development of Interactive Overlays
  - Deliverable: Interactive overlays ready for integration into GIS applications.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Quality Assurance and Testing
  - Deliverable: Quality assurance report and user feedback documentation.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Final Documentation and Training
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive user manual and training materials for end-users.
