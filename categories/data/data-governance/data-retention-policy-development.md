
# Project Plan Template: Data Retention Policy Development

## 1. Executive Summary

The Data Retention Policy Development project aims to establish a comprehensive framework for managing data retention within the organization. In today's data-driven environment, it is crucial for organizations to implement a robust data retention policy that not only meets legal and regulatory requirements but also aligns with business objectives. This project will focus on defining specific timeframes for data storage, establishing criteria for data classification, and outlining procedures for secure data deletion. The key outcomes of this project will include a detailed policy document that serves as a guideline for the organization, ensuring that sensitive information is retained only as long as necessary and disposed of securely when no longer required. Additionally, the project will provide recommendations for implementation and ongoing monitoring to ensure compliance and effective data protection. By the end of this project, the organization will have a clear and actionable data retention policy that mitigates risks associated with data management.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Data Retention Policy Development project includes the following key activities:

- **Assessment of Current Data Management Practices**: Review existing data management policies and practices to identify gaps and areas for improvement.
- **Legal and Regulatory Compliance Review**: Research applicable laws and regulations related to data retention to ensure the policy meets all legal obligations.
- **Stakeholder Engagement**: Conduct interviews and workshops with key stakeholders to gather input on data retention needs and concerns.
- **Policy Development**: Draft a comprehensive data retention policy that includes:
  - Specific timeframes for data storage based on data classification.
  - Criteria for classifying data types (e.g., sensitive, non-sensitive).
  - Procedures for secure data deletion and archiving.
- **Implementation Recommendations**: Provide actionable steps for implementing the policy across the organization.
- **Monitoring and Compliance Framework**: Develop guidelines for ongoing monitoring and compliance checks to ensure adherence to the policy.
