
# Project Plan Template: Lead Scoring Data Refinement

## 1. Executive Summary

The Lead Scoring Data Refinement project is designed to enhance the effectiveness of our sales strategy by refining existing lead scoring data. The primary objective is to append critical firmographic data, including {{company size}}, {{industry}}, and {{location}}, alongside behavioral insights that track {{user engagement}} and {{interaction patterns}}. By integrating these additional data points, we aim to create a more comprehensive view of potential leads, enabling more precise targeting and improved conversion rates. The project will involve a thorough analysis of the current lead scoring system, data cleansing to ensure accuracy, and the application of advanced data enrichment techniques. The key outcomes will include a refined lead scoring model that is actionable and tailored to our specific sales objectives, ultimately driving better business outcomes. This initiative is expected to significantly improve our lead qualification process, allowing the sales team to focus on high-potential leads and increase overall sales efficiency.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Current Lead Scoring Analysis**: Evaluate the existing lead scoring model to identify gaps and areas for improvement.
- **Data Cleansing**: Conduct a thorough data cleansing process to ensure the accuracy and reliability of existing lead data.
- **Data Enrichment**: Append critical firmographic data ({{company size}}, {{industry}}, {{location}}) and behavioral insights to the existing lead data.
- **Model Refinement**: Develop a refined lead scoring model that incorporates the enriched data points.
- **Testing and Validation**: Test the new lead scoring model against historical data to validate its effectiveness.
- **Implementation Plan**: Create a detailed implementation plan for rolling out the refined lead scoring model within the sales team.
- **Training and Support**: Provide training sessions and ongoing support for the sales team to ensure successful adoption of the new model.

- **Milestone 6**: Implementation Plan Finalization

- **Milestone 7**: Training and Support Completion
