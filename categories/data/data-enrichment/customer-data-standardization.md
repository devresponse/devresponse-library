
# Project Plan Template: Customer Data Standardization

## 1. Executive Summary

The Customer Data Standardization project aims to enhance the quality and usability of the customer database by addressing inconsistencies and inaccuracies within the data. The primary objective is to conduct a comprehensive analysis of existing data entries to identify and rectify discrepancies, including duplicate records, varying formats, and incomplete information. Our approach will involve a systematic data cleaning process, ensuring that all entries are uniformly formatted and normalized to meet industry standards. Key outcomes of this project include a reliable and accurate customer database that supports informed decision-making, effective marketing strategies, and improved customer relationship management. Ultimately, this project will provide your organization with a clean, usable database that enhances operational efficiency and aligns with your business objectives.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Assessment**: Conduct an initial assessment of the existing customer database to identify inconsistencies, duplicates, and inaccuracies.
- **Data Cleaning**: Implement a systematic approach to clean the data, which includes:
  - Removing duplicate records
  - Standardizing formats (e.g., phone numbers, addresses)
  - Filling in missing information where possible
- **Data Normalization**: Normalize data entries to align with industry standards, ensuring consistency across all records.
- **Quality Assurance**: Perform quality checks to validate the accuracy and completeness of the cleaned data.
- **Documentation**: Provide detailed documentation of the processes used, including methodologies and any changes made to the data.
- **Training**: Offer training sessions for staff on maintaining data quality and best practices for data entry moving forward.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Data Assessment
  - Deliverable: Data Assessment Report
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Data Cleaning
  - Deliverable: Cleaned Customer Database
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Data Normalization
  - Deliverable: Normalized Data Set
- **Milestone 4**: Quality Assurance Checks
  - Deliverable: Quality Assurance Report
- **Milestone 5**: Final Documentation and Training
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive Project Documentation and Training Materials
