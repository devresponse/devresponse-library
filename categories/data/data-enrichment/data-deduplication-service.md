
# Project Plan Template: Data Deduplication Service

## 1. Executive Summary

The Data Deduplication Service project aims to enhance the integrity and usability of your datasets by meticulously identifying and eliminating duplicate entries. In today's data-driven landscape, the accuracy of information is crucial for effective decision-making and operational efficiency. This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of your existing datasets using advanced algorithms and techniques to pinpoint duplicates. The key outcomes include a detailed report outlining the duplicates found, the actions taken to remove them, and the delivery of a clean, deduplicated dataset. By ensuring that your datasets are free from duplicates, we will significantly improve data quality, enabling your organization to make informed decisions and support strategic initiatives effectively. Ultimately, this service is designed to provide you with a high-quality dataset tailored to your specific business needs, fostering improved operational efficiency and data reliability.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Data Analysis**: Conduct a thorough analysis of existing datasets to identify potential duplicates.
- **Algorithm Implementation**: Utilize advanced algorithms and techniques for duplicate detection.
- **Duplicate Identification**: Generate a list of duplicate entries found within the datasets.
- **Reporting**: Provide a comprehensive report detailing the duplicates identified and the rationale behind their classification.
- **Data Cleaning**: Execute actions to remove or consolidate duplicate entries as per the agreed-upon strategy.
- **Final Dataset Delivery**: Deliver a clean, deduplicated dataset that meets the specific requirements of the client.
- **Post-Project Support**: Offer a brief support period post-delivery to address any questions or issues related to the deduplicated dataset.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Project Kickoff
  - Deliverable: Project initiation meeting and confirmation of project scope.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Data Analysis Completion

- **Milestone 4**: Data Cleaning Execution
  - Deliverable: Cleaned dataset with duplicates removed or consolidated.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Report Submission
  - Deliverable: Final report detailing the duplicate entries, actions taken, and the final dataset.
  
- **Milestone 6**: Project Closure
  - Deliverable: Project closure meeting and feedback collection.
