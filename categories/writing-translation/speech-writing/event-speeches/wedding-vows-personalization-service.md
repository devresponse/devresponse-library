
# Wedding Vows Personalization Service Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Wedding Vows Personalization Service aims to assist couples in articulating their love and commitment through uniquely crafted wedding vows that reflect their individual relationship. The project will involve a collaborative process where experienced speechwriters engage with couples to gather insights about their love story, values, and aspirations. The primary objective is to create heartfelt vows that resonate deeply with both the couple and their guests, ensuring that this pivotal part of the ceremony is memorable and meaningful. Key outcomes include the delivery of personalized vows that cater to various styles—traditional, modern, humorous, or sentimental—along with multiple revisions to guarantee complete satisfaction. By the end of this project, couples will feel confident and excited about their vows, contributing to a beautiful and memorable wedding ceremony.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Schedule a meeting with the couple to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific themes they wish to incorporate into their vows.
- **Information Gathering**: Conduct interviews or questionnaires to collect insights about the couple's love story, shared values, and aspirations.
- **Draft Creation**: Develop an initial draft of the wedding vows based on the information gathered, ensuring it reflects the couple's unique relationship.
- **Review and Revisions**: Present the draft to the couple for feedback and make necessary revisions. This process will include up to {{number_of_revisions}} rounds of edits to ensure satisfaction.
- **Finalization**: Deliver the final version of the wedding vows in a format suitable for the couple to use during their ceremony.
- **Post-Ceremony Follow-Up**: Check in with the couple after the wedding to gather feedback on their experience and the impact of the vows during the ceremony.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of couple's preferences and vision for vows.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Information Gathering Completed
  - Deliverable: Compiled insights document detailing the couple's love story and values.
  
- **Milestone 3**: First Draft of Vows Delivered
  - Deliverable: Initial draft of the wedding vows for couple's review.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Finalized wedding vows after all revisions have been made.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery of Vows
  - Deliverable: Final version of wedding vows provided to the couple.
