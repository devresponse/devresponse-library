
# Project Plan Template: Obituary and Memorial Tribute Writing

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Obituary and Memorial Tribute Writing" project aims to create personalized obituaries and memorial tributes that honor the life and legacy of individuals who have passed away. Our primary objective is to provide families and friends with a heartfelt narrative that reflects the unique personality, achievements, and cherished memories of their loved ones. Through a thorough consultation process, we will gather essential details about the individual, ensuring that the tone and language used in the tribute resonate with their character and the sentiments of those left behind. Key outcomes of this project include the delivery of a beautifully crafted obituary or memorial tribute that can be utilized for publication or personal remembrance. By focusing on the emotional aspects of the writing process, we aim to offer comfort and solace to grieving families while celebrating the life lived.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct an initial meeting with the family to discuss the deceased's life, achievements, passions, and memorable experiences.
  - Gather information through questionnaires and interviews to ensure a comprehensive understanding of the individual.

- **Writing Phase**: 
  - Draft the obituary or memorial tribute, incorporating the gathered information while maintaining a respectful and sensitive tone.
  - Ensure that the narrative reflects the personality and legacy of the deceased.

- **Review Phase**: 
  - Present the draft to the family for feedback and revisions.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on family input to ensure satisfaction with the final product.

- **Finalization Phase**: 
  - Deliver the final version of the obituary or memorial tribute in the agreed-upon format (digital or print).
  - Provide guidance on publication options if requested.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Completed questionnaire and notes from the consultation session.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Draft Submission
  - Deliverable: First draft of the obituary or memorial tribute for family review.

- **Milestone 3**: Revision Completion
  - Deliverable: Revised draft based on family feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized obituary or memorial tribute in the requested format.
