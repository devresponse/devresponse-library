
# Whisper Campaign Audio Ad Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Whisper Campaign Audio Advertisement project aims to create a captivating audio ad that engages listeners through a soft, whisper-like delivery. The primary objective of this project is to evoke curiosity and a sense of intimacy, making the audience feel as though they are being let in on a secret. By employing a warm and inviting tone, the ad will encourage listeners to lean in and pay attention, fostering a deeper connection with the brand. The final product will be designed for use across various platforms, including radio, podcasts, and digital media, ensuring broad reach and engagement. Key outcomes include a polished audio ad lasting between 30 to 60 seconds, featuring a clear call to action that prompts listeners to seek more information about the brand. The project will involve scripting, voice recording, sound design, and editing, all aligned with the brand's identity and messaging to ensure a professional and impactful final product.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Project Initiation**
  - Define project objectives and success criteria.
  - Identify stakeholders and establish communication channels.

- **Scripting**
  - Develop a script that embodies the whisper-like delivery and aligns with brand messaging.
  - Review and revise the script based on stakeholder feedback.

- **Voice Recording**
  - Select a voice talent that matches the desired tone and style.
  - Schedule and conduct recording sessions to capture the audio.

- **Sound Design**
  - Create a soundscape that complements the whisper delivery and enhances the overall mood.
  - Incorporate background music and sound effects as necessary.

- **Editing**
  - Edit the recorded audio to ensure clarity and professionalism.
  - Finalize the audio ad, ensuring it meets the specified duration and quality standards.

- **Review and Approval**
  - Present the final audio ad to stakeholders for feedback.
  - Make necessary adjustments based on feedback and obtain final approval.

- **Distribution**
  - Prepare the audio ad for distribution across selected platforms (radio, podcasts, digital media).
  - Develop a distribution plan to maximize reach and engagement.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**
  - Deliverable: Project initiation document and stakeholder list.

- **Milestone 2: Script Development**
  - Deliverable: Finalized script ready for recording.

- **Milestone 3: Voice Recording**
  - Deliverable: Raw audio files from recording sessions.

- **Milestone 4: Sound Design Completion**
  - Deliverable: Completed sound design elements.

- **Milestone 5: Audio Editing**
  - Deliverable: Polished audio ad ready for review.

- **Milestone 6: Final Review and Approval**
  - Deliverable: Approved final audio ad.

- **Milestone 7: Distribution Plan**
  - Deliverable: Distribution strategy document and audio ad files prepared for release.
