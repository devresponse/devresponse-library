
# Script Conflict and Tension Workshop Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Script Conflict and Tension Workshop aims to empower writers with the essential tools and techniques necessary for crafting compelling narratives that captivate audiences. This workshop will provide an in-depth exploration of the intricacies of conflict and tension, two critical elements that drive a story forward and maintain viewer interest. Participants will engage in interactive sessions that cover various types of conflict—internal, external, and relational—while learning how to effectively weave these elements into their scripts. Through practical exercises, attendees will apply the concepts learned in real-time, enhancing their understanding of how to construct and sustain tension throughout their narratives. By the end of the workshop, participants will leave with a clearer understanding of how to create stories that resonate with audiences and leave a lasting impact. The key outcomes include improved writing skills, enhanced narrative techniques, and a deeper appreciation for the role of conflict in storytelling.
