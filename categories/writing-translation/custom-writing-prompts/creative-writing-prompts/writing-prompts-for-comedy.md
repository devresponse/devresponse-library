
# Project Plan Template: Writing Prompts for Comedy

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Writing Prompts for Comedy" project aims to create a vibrant and engaging collection of writing prompts that inspire comedic storytelling. The primary objective is to develop a diverse set of prompts that range from quirky character ideas to absurd situations, encouraging writers to think outside the box and embrace their sense of humor. This project will provide writers with the tools they need to explore various comedic styles, including slapstick, satire, and witty dialogue. Key outcomes include a comprehensive list of prompts, each accompanied by a brief explanation to guide writers in their creative process. By the end of this project, we will deliver a valuable resource that caters to both seasoned authors and newcomers, helping them inject humor into their work and develop their unique comedic voice.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Ideation**: Conduct research on existing comedic writing prompts and gather inspiration from various comedic genres.
- **Prompt Development**: Create a collection of at least {{number_of_prompts}} unique writing prompts that cover a wide range of comedic styles.
- **Explanatory Notes**: For each prompt, write a brief explanation that provides context and guidance for writers.
- **Review and Feedback**: Share the prompts with a select group of writers for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Final Compilation**: Compile the final list of prompts and explanations into a cohesive document.
- **Formatting and Design**: Format the document for clarity and ease of use, ensuring it is visually appealing and user-friendly.
- **Distribution**: Determine the best channels for distributing the final product to reach the target audience effectively.
