
# Project Plan Template: Writing Prompts for Moral Dilemmas

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Writing Prompts for Moral Dilemmas" project aims to create a comprehensive catalog of thought-provoking writing prompts that center around complex ethical questions and moral dilemmas. The primary objective is to inspire writers to explore the intricacies of human decision-making and the repercussions of their choices through engaging scenarios. This project will provide a diverse range of prompts that challenge writers to delve into themes such as justice, loyalty, sacrifice, and the greater good. By doing so, we aim to foster deeper character development and narrative tension, ultimately enhancing the storytelling skills of writers. Key outcomes include a curated list of prompts that not only ignite creativity but also encourage writers to tackle challenging ethical questions, resulting in narratives that resonate profoundly with readers. This project is designed to be a valuable resource for writers seeking to enrich their craft and engage with the moral complexities of their characters.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of this project includes the following key activities:

- **Research and Development**: Conduct thorough research on various moral dilemmas and ethical questions to ensure a rich variety of prompts.
- **Prompt Creation**: Develop a series of writing prompts that encapsulate different themes related to moral dilemmas, ensuring each prompt is thought-provoking and conducive to character development.
- **Categorization**: Organize the prompts into thematic categories for easy navigation and selection by writers.
- **Feedback and Revision**: Gather feedback from a select group of writers and revise prompts as necessary to enhance clarity and impact.
- **Final Compilation**: Compile the final list of prompts into a comprehensive document, including guidelines on how to use the prompts effectively in writing.
