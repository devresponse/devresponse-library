
# Project Plan Template: Horror Writing Prompts

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Horror Writing Prompts" project aims to inspire and challenge writers to delve into the chilling and suspenseful realms of horror literature. The primary objective is to create a comprehensive catalog of unique writing prompts that evoke fear, suspense, and psychological tension. This project will provide writers with a diverse range of scenarios, from supernatural hauntings to psychological thrillers, encouraging them to explore various facets of horror storytelling. Key outcomes include a curated collection of at least {{number_of_prompts}} prompts, a user-friendly format for easy access, and supplementary resources such as tips on crafting horror narratives. By the end of this project, participants will have a wealth of inspiration to enhance their writing skills and produce captivating horror stories that resonate with readers. 

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the "Horror Writing Prompts" project includes the following key components:

- **Research and Development**: Conduct thorough research on horror themes, tropes, and writing techniques to inform the creation of prompts.
- **Prompt Creation**: Develop a minimum of {{number_of_prompts}} unique horror writing prompts, ensuring a variety of themes such as supernatural, psychological, and survival horror.
- **Format and Presentation**: Design an engaging and accessible format for the prompts, which may include a digital catalog, printable worksheets, or an interactive online platform.
- **Supplementary Resources**: Create additional materials such as writing tips, examples of horror narratives, and exercises to help writers effectively utilize the prompts.
- **Feedback and Revision**: Gather feedback from a select group of writers to refine the prompts and resources, ensuring they meet the needs of the target audience.
