
# Project Plan Template: Sci-Fi Writing Prompts

## 1. Executive Summary

The Sci-Fi Writing Prompts project aims to create a diverse and imaginative collection of writing prompts specifically tailored for science fiction enthusiasts. The primary objective is to inspire writers by providing them with unique scenarios that explore various themes within the genre, such as space exploration, advanced technology, alien civilizations, and dystopian futures. By crafting prompts that challenge the boundaries of reality, this project seeks to ignite the imagination of both novice and experienced writers alike. Key outcomes include a comprehensive collection of prompts that not only stimulate creativity but also serve as a valuable resource for writers looking to enhance their skills and produce compelling narratives. Ultimately, this project will foster a deeper appreciation for science fiction writing and encourage the exploration of innovative concepts within the genre.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Sci-Fi Writing Prompts project includes the following key components:

- **Research and Theme Exploration**: Conduct thorough research on various themes within the science fiction genre to ensure a diverse range of prompts.
- **Prompt Development**: Create a collection of {{number}} writing prompts, each designed to inspire creativity and encourage exploration of complex narratives.
- **Feedback and Revision**: Gather feedback from a select group of writers and revise prompts based on their insights to enhance clarity and effectiveness.
