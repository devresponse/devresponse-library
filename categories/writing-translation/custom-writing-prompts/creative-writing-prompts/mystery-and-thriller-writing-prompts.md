
# Project Plan Template: Mystery and Thriller Writing Prompts

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Mystery and Thriller Writing Prompts" project aims to create an engaging and diverse collection of writing prompts specifically designed for writers in the mystery and thriller genres. The primary objective is to ignite the imagination of writers by providing them with carefully crafted prompts that incorporate intriguing clues, deceptive red herrings, and unexpected twists—elements that are essential to captivating storytelling in these genres. This project will not only enhance writers' narrative techniques but also challenge them to think critically about plot development and character motivations. By the end of the project, clients will receive a comprehensive set of prompts that cater to various sub-genres within mystery and thriller, ensuring a rich and varied source of inspiration. Key outcomes include a finalized collection of prompts, feedback from a focus group of writers, and a marketing strategy for distribution.
