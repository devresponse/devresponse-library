
# Project Plan Template: Simplify a Research Paper into a Technical Summary

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of this project is to transform a detailed and complex research paper into a clear and concise technical summary that is accessible to a broader audience. The project involves a thorough analysis of the original research document to distill its essential findings, methodologies, and implications while eliminating jargon and overly technical language. The key outcomes of this project will include a well-structured summary that maintains the integrity of the original research, engages the reader, and is suitable for publication in a technical newsletter or as part of a broader communication strategy. By focusing on clarity and accessibility, this project aims to bridge the gap between technical research and public understanding, ensuring that the valuable insights from the research can be appreciated by non-experts. The final deliverable will be a polished technical summary that reflects both the depth of the research and the needs of the intended audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Analysis of Original Research Paper**: 
  - Review the complete research paper to understand its objectives, methodologies, findings, and implications.
  - Identify key points and themes that are essential for the summary.

- **Drafting the Technical Summary**: 
  - Create an outline based on the identified key points.
  - Write the initial draft of the technical summary, ensuring clarity and conciseness.
  - Use language that is accessible to non-experts while maintaining the integrity of the research.

- **Review and Revisions**: 
  - Conduct a peer review of the draft to gather feedback.
  - Revise the summary based on feedback to enhance clarity and engagement.

- **Finalization**: 
  - Prepare the final version of the technical summary.
  - Ensure that the summary is formatted appropriately for publication.

- **Delivery**: 
  - Submit the final technical summary to the client in the agreed format.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the analysis of the original research paper.
  - **Deliverable**: Summary of key points and themes identified from the research paper.

- **Milestone 2**: Drafting of the initial technical summary.
  - **Deliverable**: First draft of the technical summary.

- **Milestone 3**: Peer review and feedback collection.
  - **Deliverable**: Feedback report and revised draft.

- **Milestone 4**: Finalization of the technical summary.
  - **Deliverable**: Final version of the technical summary ready for publication.
