
# Project Plan Template: Create a Knowledge Base Article for a Tech Support Portal

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of this project is to create a comprehensive knowledge base article specifically designed for a tech support portal. This article will address a frequently encountered technical issue faced by users, providing them with clear, step-by-step instructions and solutions. The project aims to enhance user experience by simplifying complex technical information into easily digestible content. Key outcomes include a well-structured article that incorporates headings, bullet points, and visuals where necessary, ensuring that users can quickly find the information they need. The final deliverable will not only resolve the identified technical issue but will also serve as a valuable resource that improves the overall support experience for users. By conducting thorough research and employing clear language, the project will contribute to a more effective tech support portal, ultimately leading to increased user satisfaction and reduced support inquiries.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Identify the specific technical issue to be addressed.
  - Conduct thorough research to gather accurate and relevant information.
  - Review existing documentation and user feedback to understand common pain points.

- **Content Development**:
  - Draft the knowledge base article, ensuring clarity and conciseness.
  - Structure the article with appropriate headings and subheadings.
  - Use bullet points for easy readability and comprehension.
  - Incorporate visuals (e.g., screenshots, diagrams) to enhance understanding.

- **Review and Revision**:
  - Share the draft with stakeholders for feedback.
  - Revise the article based on feedback received.
  - Ensure that all technical information is accurate and up-to-date.

- **Finalization**:
  - Prepare the final version of the article for publication.
  - Ensure that the article is formatted correctly for the tech support portal.
  - Publish the article and verify its accessibility on the platform.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research summary document outlining findings and the chosen technical issue.

- **Milestone 2**: Draft of Knowledge Base Article
  - Deliverable: First draft of the article for stakeholder review.

- **Milestone 3**: Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised article based on stakeholder feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Article Submission
  - Deliverable: Finalized knowledge base article ready for publication.

- **Milestone 5**: Article Publication
  - Deliverable: Published article on the tech support portal.
