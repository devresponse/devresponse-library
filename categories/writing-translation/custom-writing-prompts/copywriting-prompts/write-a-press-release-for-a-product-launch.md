
# Project Plan Template: Write a Press Release for a Product Launch

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of this project is to craft a compelling press release for the launch of a fictional product or service, aimed at effectively communicating its key features and benefits to both journalists and potential customers. This project will involve thorough research and understanding of the product, as well as the target audience, to ensure that the press release captures attention and conveys the newsworthiness of the launch. The final document will adhere to standard press release formatting, which includes a catchy headline, a strong lead paragraph, supporting details, and a boilerplate about the fictional company. Key outcomes of this project include a well-structured press release that not only informs but also engages the audience, thereby enhancing the visibility of the product in the market. This project is particularly beneficial for individuals looking to refine their copywriting skills in a business context and create impactful marketing content that resonates with readers.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct research on the fictional product/service to gather key features, benefits, and unique selling points.
  - Analyze the target audience to tailor the messaging appropriately.
  
- **Drafting Phase**: 
  - Create an outline for the press release, ensuring all essential elements are included.
  - Write the first draft of the press release, focusing on clarity and engagement.
  
- **Review Phase**: 
  - Share the draft with stakeholders for feedback.
  - Revise the press release based on feedback received, ensuring all comments are addressed.
  
- **Finalization Phase**: 
  - Format the press release according to standard press release guidelines.
  - Prepare a final version for distribution, including a catchy headline, strong lead paragraph, supporting details, and a boilerplate about the fictional company.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research summary document outlining key features and target audience insights.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Drafting Phase
  - Deliverable: First draft of the press release.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Completion of Review Phase
  - Deliverable: Revised press release incorporating stakeholder feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Finalization of Press Release
  - Deliverable: Final formatted press release ready for distribution.
