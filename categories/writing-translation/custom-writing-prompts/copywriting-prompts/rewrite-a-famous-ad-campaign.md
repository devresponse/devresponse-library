
# Project Plan Template: Rewrite a Famous Ad Campaign

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the "Rewrite a Famous Ad Campaign" project is to creatively reinterpret a well-known advertising campaign, adapting it to appeal to a different demographic or market segment. This project will involve a thorough analysis of the original campaign's messaging, target audience, and creative elements, allowing for a deep understanding of what made the original successful. The key outcomes will include a comprehensive document that outlines the rewritten campaign, maintaining the essence of the original while innovating in tone, style, and medium. By engaging in this project, copywriters will enhance their skills in creative thinking and audience adaptation, ultimately producing a fresh campaign that resonates with a new audience. The project aims to foster a deeper appreciation for the art of copywriting and the strategic decisions that underpin effective advertising.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Campaign Selection**: Identify and select a famous advertising campaign that has had a significant impact on the marketing world. 
- **Analysis**: Conduct a detailed analysis of the original campaign, focusing on:
  - Messaging: What was the core message of the original campaign?
  - Target Audience: Who was the original target audience, and what demographics were they aiming to reach?
  - Creative Elements: What creative strategies (visuals, tone, style) were employed in the original campaign?
- **Rewriting the Campaign**: Develop a new version of the campaign that:
  - Adapts the messaging to resonate with a different demographic or market segment.
  - Changes the tone and style as necessary to fit the new audience.
  - Considers alternative mediums for delivery (e.g., social media, print, video).
- **Documentation**: Create a comprehensive document that includes:
  - Key messaging for the rewritten campaign.
  - Proposed visuals and creative elements.
  - A rationale for the creative choices made in the rewriting process.
