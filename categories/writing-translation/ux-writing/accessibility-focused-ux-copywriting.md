
# Project Plan Template: Accessibility-Focused UX Copywriting

## 1. Executive Summary

The Accessibility-Focused UX Copywriting project aims to create user-centric copy that adheres to established accessibility standards, particularly the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG). The primary objective is to enhance the digital experience for individuals with disabilities by crafting clear, concise, and meaningful text. This project will involve a thorough analysis of existing content to identify areas for improvement, followed by the development of new copy that meets accessibility criteria. Key outcomes will include an improved user experience for all users, increased inclusivity in digital communication, and a comprehensive report detailing recommendations and newly created content. By prioritizing accessibility in UX writing, businesses can demonstrate their commitment to inclusivity and ensure that their digital interfaces are navigable and engaging for everyone, regardless of their abilities.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Content Analysis**: Review existing UX copy across digital platforms to assess current accessibility compliance and identify gaps.
- **User Research**: Conduct interviews and surveys with users, particularly those with disabilities, to gather insights on their experiences and needs.
- **Accessibility Standards Review**: Familiarize the project team with WCAG guidelines and other relevant accessibility standards.
- **Recommendations Report**: Develop a detailed report outlining specific recommendations for improving existing content and creating new copy.
- **Copywriting**: Create new UX copy that adheres to accessibility standards, ensuring clarity and conciseness.
- **Testing and Feedback**: Implement usability testing with users who have disabilities to gather feedback on the new copy.
- **Final Deliverables**: Provide a comprehensive document that includes the analysis, recommendations, and newly created copy, along with a summary of user feedback.
