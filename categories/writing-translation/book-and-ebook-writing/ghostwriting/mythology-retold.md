
# Project Plan Template: Mythology Retold

## Executive Summary
The 'Mythology Retold' project aims to breathe new life into ancient myths by crafting a modern retelling that resonates with contemporary audiences. The primary objective is to create a compelling manuscript that honors the original tales while making them accessible and engaging for today's readers. This project will involve in-depth research into selected myths, understanding their cultural significance, and interpreting them through a modern lens that aligns with the client's creative vision. Key outcomes include a polished manuscript that reflects the client's unique voice and perspective, multiple drafts and revisions based on client feedback, and a final product that captivates readers. By collaborating closely with the client throughout the process, we will ensure that the narrative not only preserves the essence of the original myths but also connects with modern sensibilities, ultimately enriching the literary landscape.

## Detailed Scope of Work
1. **Research Phase**
   - Conduct in-depth research on selected myths, including historical context, cultural significance, and existing interpretations.
   - Identify themes and motifs that resonate with contemporary audiences.

2. **Concept Development**
   - Collaborate with the client to define the creative vision and direction for the retelling.
   - Develop an outline that captures the essence of the myths while integrating modern elements.

3. **Drafting**
   - Write the first draft of the manuscript based on the approved outline.
   - Incorporate the client's voice and perspective throughout the narrative.

4. **Revisions**
   - Present the first draft to the client for feedback.
   - Revise the manuscript based on client input, ensuring alignment with their vision.

5. **Finalization**
   - Prepare the final manuscript, incorporating all revisions and feedback.
   - Conduct a final review to ensure quality and coherence.

6. **Delivery**
   - Deliver the completed manuscript to the client in the agreed-upon format.

## Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1:** Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and insights.

- **Milestone 2:** Approval of Concept Development
  - Deliverable: Outline of the manuscript for client approval.

- **Milestone 3:** Submission of First Draft
  - Deliverable: First draft of the manuscript.

- **Milestone 4:** Completion of Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised manuscript based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 5:** Final Manuscript Delivery
  - Deliverable: Completed manuscript delivered to the client.
