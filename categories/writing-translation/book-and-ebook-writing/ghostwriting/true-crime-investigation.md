
# Project Plan Template: True Crime Investigation

## 1. Executive Summary

The "True Crime Investigation" project aims to create a compelling true crime book that meticulously explores the intricacies of a specific real-life criminal case. The primary objective is to craft a narrative that not only presents factual details but also engages readers by delving into the various theories surrounding the case. This project will involve extensive research, including interviews with key individuals, analysis of court documents, and a thorough review of media coverage to ensure the accuracy and depth of the narrative. Key outcomes include a professionally written manuscript that reflects the client's voice and vision, regular updates and drafts for client feedback, and a final product that captivates the target audience. By maintaining open communication and alignment with the client's expectations throughout the writing process, this project seeks to deliver a high-quality book that resonates with true crime enthusiasts and provides a comprehensive understanding of the events.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct interviews with relevant individuals, including law enforcement, witnesses, and family members.
  - Analyze court documents related to the case.
  - Review media coverage, including articles, documentaries, and podcasts.

- **Writing Phase**: 
  - Develop an outline based on research findings.
  - Write the first draft of the manuscript, ensuring the narrative is engaging and maintains the client's voice.
  - Incorporate feedback from the client at various stages of the writing process.

- **Editing Phase**: 
  - Revise the manuscript based on client feedback and additional research.
  - Conduct a thorough proofreading to ensure grammatical accuracy and coherence.

- **Finalization Phase**: 
  - Prepare the final manuscript for publication, including formatting and any necessary illustrations or photographs.
  - Deliver the completed manuscript to the client.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing findings and interview notes.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Submission of the First Draft
  - Deliverable: First draft of the manuscript for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Completion of the Editing Phase
  - Deliverable: Revised manuscript incorporating client feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Manuscript Delivery
  - Deliverable: Completed and formatted manuscript ready for publication.
