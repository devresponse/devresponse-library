## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct thorough research on {{Sports Legend's Name}}’s life, career, and achievements.
  - Gather existing materials, such as articles, interviews, and documentaries.
  
- **Interview Phase**: 
  - Schedule and conduct interviews with {{Sports Legend's Name}}, family members, and close associates.
  - Prepare interview questions that elicit personal anecdotes and insights.

- **Writing Phase**: 
  - Draft the biography, ensuring it captures the essence of {{Sports Legend's Name}}’s voice and experiences.
  - Organize the manuscript into coherent chapters that chronologically or thematically present the life story.

- **Review and Revision Phase**: 
  - Present the first draft to the client for feedback.
  - Incorporate revisions based on client input to refine the manuscript.

- **Finalization Phase**: 
  - Prepare the final manuscript for publication, ensuring formatting is suitable for both print and digital formats.
  - Collaborate with publishing professionals if necessary for layout and design.
