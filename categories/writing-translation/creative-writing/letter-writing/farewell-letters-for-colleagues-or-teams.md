
# Project Plan Template: Farewell Letters for Colleagues or Teams

## 1. Executive Summary

The Farewell Letters for Colleagues or Teams project aims to create personalized and heartfelt farewell letters that honor the contributions of departing team members. In today's fast-paced work environment, it is essential to acknowledge the bittersweet nature of farewells, ensuring that each letter encapsulates the unique journey of the individual within the organization. The project will involve a comprehensive consultation process to gather insights about the individual’s achievements, experiences, and relationships within the team. The key outcomes include the delivery of professionally crafted letters that resonate with warmth and appreciation, leaving a lasting impression on both the departing individual and their colleagues. By focusing on personalization and emotional resonance, we aim to foster a positive farewell experience that reflects the values of our organization and strengthens team bonds.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Schedule and conduct one-on-one consultations with stakeholders to gather insights about the departing individual.
  - Discuss key achievements, memorable moments, and personal anecdotes to be included in the letter.

- **Drafting Phase**: 
  - Create a draft of the farewell letter based on the information gathered during the consultation.
  - Ensure that the tone is appropriate, balancing professionalism with warmth.

- **Review Phase**: 
  - Share the draft with stakeholders for feedback and revisions.
  - Incorporate any necessary changes to ensure the letter meets expectations.

- **Finalization Phase**: 
  - Prepare the final version of the farewell letter, ensuring it is polished and error-free.
  - Format the letter for presentation, including any necessary branding or personal touches.

- **Delivery Phase**: 
  - Deliver the finalized farewell letter to the appropriate stakeholders.
  - Optionally, provide a printed version for presentation during farewell gatherings.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of stakeholder consultations
  - **Deliverable**: Summary of insights gathered from consultations

- **Milestone 2**: Drafting of farewell letters
  - **Deliverable**: First draft of the farewell letter

- **Milestone 3**: Review and feedback session
  - **Deliverable**: Revised draft based on stakeholder feedback

- **Milestone 4**: Finalization of farewell letters
  - **Deliverable**: Polished and formatted farewell letter

- **Milestone 5**: Delivery of final letters
  - **Deliverable**: Completed farewell letters delivered to stakeholders
