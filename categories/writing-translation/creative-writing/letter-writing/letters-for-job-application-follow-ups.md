
# Project Plan Template: Letters for Job Application Follow-Ups

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Letters for Job Application Follow-Ups" project aims to provide job seekers with expertly crafted follow-up letters that enhance their candidacy in a competitive job market. The primary objective is to create personalized and professional letters that reflect the unique voice of each client while adhering to established business etiquette. This project will involve close collaboration between our expert writers and clients to capture the nuances of their interview experiences, the specific position applied for, and the culture of the hiring organization. Key outcomes include the delivery of tailored follow-up letters that express gratitude for the interview opportunity, reiterate interest in the position, and highlight significant discussion points from the interview. By ensuring that these letters stand out, we aim to reinforce the client's candidacy and leave a lasting positive impression on hiring managers. Ultimately, this service will empower job seekers to take a crucial step in their job search with confidence and professionalism.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Client Consultation**: Conduct an initial consultation with the client to gather information about the interview, the position, and the company culture.
- **Research**: Perform background research on the company and the role to ensure the letter is relevant and tailored.
- **Drafting**: Create a personalized follow-up letter that includes:
  - A thank-you note for the interview opportunity.
  - A reiteration of interest in the position.
  - Key points discussed during the interview that align with the client's qualifications.
- **Revisions**: Provide up to two rounds of revisions based on client feedback to ensure satisfaction with the final product.
- **Final Delivery**: Deliver the final follow-up letter in a professional format suitable for email or print.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Client Consultation
  - Deliverable: Summary of client interview details and expectations.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Research Completion
  - Deliverable: Research notes on the company and position.

- **Milestone 3**: Draft Submission
  - Deliverable: First draft of the follow-up letter.

- **Milestone 4**: Revision Completion
  - Deliverable: Revised letter based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized follow-up letter in the agreed format.
