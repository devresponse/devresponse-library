
# Project Plan Template: Poetry for Wedding Invitations

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Poetry for Wedding Invitations" project aims to create exquisite and heartfelt poetry tailored specifically for wedding invitations. The primary objective is to craft verses that not only convey the joy and love of the occasion but also resonate with the couple's unique story and style. This project will involve close collaboration between the selected poet and the client to understand their vision, preferences, and any specific themes they wish to incorporate. Key outcomes include a selection of poetic lines that can be elegantly integrated into the wedding invitations, enhancing both the aesthetic and emotional appeal of the invites. By ensuring that the verses reflect the couple's personality and the romantic essence of their celebration, this project will contribute to making their invitations truly memorable. Ultimately, the goal is to deliver a product that encapsulates the couple's love story in a way that is both artistic and meaningful.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the couple to discuss their vision, preferences, and any specific themes or styles they wish to incorporate into the poetry.
  
- **Research and Inspiration**: Gather inspiration from the couple's love story, interests, and any relevant cultural or personal elements that can be woven into the poetry.

- **Drafting Poetry**: Create multiple drafts of poetic lines that capture the essence of the couple's relationship and the theme of the wedding.

- **Client Review**: Present the drafts to the couple for feedback and revisions. This step may involve several iterations to ensure satisfaction.

- **Finalization**: Finalize the selected poetic lines, ensuring they are polished and ready for integration into the wedding invitations.

- **Delivery**: Provide the final poetic lines in a format suitable for the couple to share with their invitation designer.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - **Deliverable**: Summary of couple's vision and preferences.

- **Milestone 2**: Research and Inspiration Gathering Completed
  - **Deliverable**: Inspiration board or notes reflecting the couple's story and themes.

- **Milestone 3**: First Draft of Poetry Completed
  - **Deliverable**: Initial drafts of poetic lines for client review.

- **Milestone 4**: Client Feedback Received
  - **Deliverable**: Documented feedback and requested revisions.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Poetry Lines Delivered
  - **Deliverable**: Polished poetic lines ready for integration into wedding invitations.
