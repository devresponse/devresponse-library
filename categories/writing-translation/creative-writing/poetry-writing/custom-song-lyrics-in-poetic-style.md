
# Project Plan: Custom Song Lyrics in Poetic Style

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Custom Song Lyrics in Poetic Style" project aims to create unique and emotionally resonant song lyrics that embody the essence of poetry. The primary objective is to craft lyrics that not only tell a compelling story but also evoke deep emotions, making them relatable and impactful for listeners. This project will begin with an in-depth consultation to understand the client's vision, themes, and specific emotions they wish to convey through their music. The outcome will be a polished set of song lyrics that incorporate various poetic techniques, such as imagery, metaphor, and rhythm, ensuring that the lyrics flow seamlessly with the intended melody. Key outcomes include a series of lyrical drafts, client feedback sessions, and a final set of lyrics that can be easily adapted for musical composition. This project is particularly suited for musicians, bands, or anyone seeking to enhance their music with a poetic touch.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Schedule a meeting with the client to discuss their vision, themes, and desired emotions.
  - Gather any reference materials or examples of lyrics that resonate with the client.

- **Draft Development**:
  - Create an initial draft of song lyrics based on the consultation.
  - Incorporate poetic techniques such as imagery, metaphor, and rhythm.
  - Prepare multiple drafts to explore different lyrical approaches.

- **Feedback and Revisions**:
  - Present the initial drafts to the client for feedback.
  - Conduct up to three rounds of revisions based on client input.
  - Ensure that the final lyrics align with the client's vision and musical style.

- **Final Deliverable**:
  - Provide a polished set of song lyrics that can be easily adapted to a musical composition.
  - Include a brief explanation of the poetic techniques used in the lyrics.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Summary of client vision and themes.
  
- **Milestone 2**: First Draft Submission
  - Deliverable: Initial draft of song lyrics.

- **Milestone 3**: Feedback Session
  - Deliverable: Client feedback report.

- **Milestone 4**: Revised Draft Submission
  - Deliverable: Revised draft of song lyrics after first round of feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Review
  - Deliverable: Final set of song lyrics, polished and ready for musical adaptation.
