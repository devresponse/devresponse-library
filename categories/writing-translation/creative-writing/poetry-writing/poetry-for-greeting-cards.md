
# Project Plan Template: Poetry for Greeting Cards

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Poetry for Greeting Cards" project aims to create heartfelt and concise poems tailored specifically for greeting cards, encapsulating emotions and sentiments appropriate for various occasions such as anniversaries, holidays, and expressions of gratitude. The primary objective is to craft poems that resonate deeply with recipients, ensuring that each message is both personal and impactful. This project will involve a thorough understanding of the desired themes and tones from the client, followed by the creation of multiple drafts for review. Key outcomes include a collection of polished poems ready for printing or digital use, enhancing the overall greeting card experience. By focusing on creativity and emotional connection, this project seeks to elevate the art of greeting cards, making them more memorable and meaningful for recipients.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Client Consultation**: Conduct initial meetings with the client to understand their vision, themes, and specific occasions for the poems.
- **Research**: Gather insights on popular sentiments and styles in greeting card poetry to inform the writing process.
- **Draft Creation**: Develop multiple drafts of poems for various occasions, ensuring a range of tones and styles to choose from.
- **Client Review**: Present the drafts to the client for feedback and revisions.
- **Finalization**: Revise the poems based on client feedback, ensuring they meet the desired emotional and thematic criteria.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final polished versions of the poems in both print-ready and digital formats.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Client Consultation  
  **Deliverable**: Summary document outlining client preferences and themes.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of Research  
  **Deliverable**: Research report on popular sentiments and styles in greeting card poetry.

- **Milestone 3**: Submission of Draft Poems  
  **Deliverable**: Initial drafts of poems for client review.

- **Milestone 4**: Client Feedback Session  
  **Deliverable**: Documented feedback from the client.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Poem Revisions  
  **Deliverable**: Polished versions of the poems based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 6**: Project Completion  
  **Deliverable**: Final delivery of poems in print-ready and digital formats.
