
# Project Plan: Custom Poems for Proposals

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Custom Poems for Proposals" project aims to create a personalized poem that encapsulates the unique love story of the client and their partner, serving as a heartfelt centerpiece for a marriage proposal. The primary objective is to collaborate closely with the client to gather insights about their relationship, including key emotions, themes, and memorable moments that they wish to convey through the poem. This project will result in a beautifully crafted piece of poetry, tailored to resonate deeply with the partner, enhancing the emotional impact of the proposal. Key outcomes include a finalized poem in the chosen style—be it a sonnet, free verse, or haiku—delivered in a polished format suitable for presentation. By focusing on the creative expression of love, this project allows the client to concentrate on the joy of the occasion, ensuring that the proposal is not only memorable but also a true reflection of their commitment.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their vision for the poem, including themes, emotions, and any specific memories they wish to include.
- **Research and Inspiration Gathering**: Collect relevant information about the couple's relationship, including anecdotes, shared experiences, and significant milestones.
- **Drafting the Poem**: Create an initial draft of the poem based on the gathered information, incorporating the chosen poetic style.
- **Client Review**: Present the draft to the client for feedback and suggestions for revisions.
- **Revisions**: Make necessary adjustments to the poem based on client feedback, ensuring it aligns with their vision.
- **Finalization**: Prepare the final version of the poem, ensuring it is polished and formatted for presentation.
- **Delivery**: Provide the completed poem to the client in a suitable format (e.g., printed, digital) for their proposal.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - **Deliverable**: Summary of client’s vision and key themes.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Research and Inspiration Gathering Completed
  - **Deliverable**: Collection of anecdotes and significant relationship milestones.

- **Milestone 3**: Draft of the Poem Completed
  - **Deliverable**: Initial draft of the poem in the chosen style.

- **Milestone 4**: Client Review Completed
  - **Deliverable**: Feedback from the client on the draft.

- **Milestone 5**: Revisions Completed
  - **Deliverable**: Revised poem based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 6**: Final Version Delivered
  - **Deliverable**: Polished poem ready for presentation.
