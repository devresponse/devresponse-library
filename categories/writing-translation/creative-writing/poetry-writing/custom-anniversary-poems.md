
# Project Plan Template: Custom Anniversary Poems

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Anniversary Poems project aims to celebrate the unique love story of couples by crafting personalized poetry that encapsulates the essence of their relationship. The primary objective is to create a heartfelt piece that reflects significant milestones, cherished memories, and the emotions that define the couple's bond. This project will involve close collaboration with clients to gather details about the couple's history, favorite moments, and any specific themes or messages they wish to convey. Key outcomes include the delivery of a beautifully written poem tailored to the couple's preferences, which can be presented as a memorable gift. By offering various styles such as sonnets, haikus, or free verse, we ensure that the final product resonates deeply with the couple's journey together. This project guarantees a polished and meaningful piece that not only serves as a celebration of love but also as a lasting keepsake for years to come.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Client Consultation**: Schedule an initial meeting with the client to discuss the couple's story, preferences, and any specific themes or messages they want to include in the poem.
  
- **Information Gathering**: Collect detailed information about the couple's significant milestones, cherished memories, and favorite moments. This may include:
  - Important dates (e.g., first date, wedding date)
  - Shared experiences (e.g., trips, hobbies)
  - Personal anecdotes and inside jokes
  
- **Poem Drafting**: Create a first draft of the poem based on the gathered information. The style will be determined in consultation with the client.

- **Client Review**: Present the first draft to the client for feedback. Discuss any revisions or adjustments needed to better capture the couple's story.

- **Final Edits**: Make necessary revisions based on client feedback and finalize the poem.

- **Presentation Options**: Offer options for presenting the poem, such as:
  - Framed print
  - Digital format
  - Custom card
  
- **Delivery**: Ensure timely delivery of the final product in the agreed format.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Client Consultation
  - **Deliverable**: Summary of client preferences and information gathered.
  
- **Milestone 2**: First Draft Completion
  - **Deliverable**: First draft of the poem for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback Session
  - **Deliverable**: Documented feedback and requested revisions.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Poem Delivery
  - **Deliverable**: Finalized poem in the chosen format, ready for presentation.
