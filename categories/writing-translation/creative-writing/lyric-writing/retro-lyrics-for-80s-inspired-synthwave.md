
# Project Plan Template: Retro Lyrics for 80s-Inspired Synthwave

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Retro Lyrics for 80s-Inspired Synthwave" project aims to craft evocative and vibrant lyrics that capture the essence of the 1980s, specifically tailored for the synthwave music genre. The primary objective is to create a lyrical narrative that resonates with listeners by drawing inspiration from iconic elements of 80s pop culture, such as retro technology, neon aesthetics, and the vibrant nightlife of the era. This project will not only enhance the emotional impact of the synthwave tracks but also evoke a sense of nostalgia among the audience. Key outcomes include a set of high-quality lyrics that align with the artist's vision and style, fostering a collaborative environment to ensure the final product is personalized and authentic. By integrating these lyrics seamlessly with the musical composition, we aim to create an immersive experience that transports listeners back to the vibrant 80s, making it a memorable addition to the synthwave genre.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct in-depth research on 80s pop culture, including music, fashion, technology, and nightlife.
  - Gather references and materials that inspire the lyrical themes.

- **Lyric Development**:
  - Brainstorm and outline potential themes and narratives for the lyrics.
  - Draft initial lyrics that reflect the chosen themes and resonate with the synthwave sound.

- **Collaboration**:
  - Schedule meetings with the artist to discuss their vision and style.
  - Revise lyrics based on feedback from the artist to ensure alignment with their musical composition.

- **Finalization**:
  - Polish the lyrics for clarity, rhythm, and emotional impact.
  - Prepare the final set of lyrics for delivery to the artist.

- **Feedback and Revisions**:
  - Allow for a feedback period post-delivery for any last-minute adjustments.
  - Implement any necessary changes based on the artist's feedback.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report summarizing key findings and inspirations.

- **Milestone 2**: Draft of Initial Lyrics
  - Deliverable: First draft of lyrics for review by the artist.

- **Milestone 3**: Collaborative Review Session
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and revised lyrics based on artist feedback.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Lyrics Submission
  - Deliverable: Finalized lyrics document delivered to the artist.

- **Milestone 5**: Feedback Implementation
  - Deliverable: Revised lyrics based on final feedback, if necessary.
