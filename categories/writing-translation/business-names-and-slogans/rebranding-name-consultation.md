
# Project Plan Template: Rebranding Name Consultation

## 1. Executive Summary

The Rebranding Name Consultation project aims to assist businesses in selecting a new brand name that effectively resonates with their target audience while embodying their core values. In today's competitive market, a strong brand identity is crucial for success, and this consultation service is designed to provide expert guidance throughout the rebranding process. Our objectives include analyzing the current brand positioning, understanding the client's business goals, and exploring the industry landscape to identify unique and impactful name options. Key outcomes of this project will include a comprehensive analysis of potential names, consideration of trademark implications, and a shortlist of compelling name options that align with the client's vision. By the conclusion of this consultation, clients will be equipped with a clear direction for their rebranding efforts, ensuring that their new name will enhance their market presence and contribute to a successful rebranding campaign.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a kickoff meeting with the client to understand their current brand, business goals, and vision for the rebranding.
- **Brand Analysis**: Evaluate the existing brand positioning, including strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats (SWOT analysis).
- **Industry Research**: Analyze the competitive landscape to identify naming trends and potential gaps in the market.
- **Name Generation**: Develop a list of potential names that are unique, memorable, and reflective of the client's values and goals.
- **Name Evaluation**: Assess each name option for availability, trademark considerations, and marketability.
- **Feedback Session**: Present the shortlisted names to the client and gather feedback for further refinement.
- **Final Recommendations**: Provide a final report with the selected name options and rationale for each, along with next steps for the rebranding process.
