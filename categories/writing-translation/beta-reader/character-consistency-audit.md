
# Project Plan Template: Character Consistency Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

The Character Consistency Audit is a specialized beta reading service aimed at authors who wish to enhance the authenticity and relatability of their characters prior to publication. The primary objective of this project is to conduct a thorough analysis of character arcs, motivations, and interactions within the manuscript. By meticulously reviewing the text, the beta reader will identify inconsistencies in character behavior, dialogue, and development. Key outcomes of this project include providing authors with detailed feedback that highlights specific areas where characters may feel underdeveloped or contradictory, along with actionable suggestions for improvement. This service is crucial for writers striving to create compelling and believable characters that resonate with readers, ultimately elevating the overall quality of the manuscript. The Character Consistency Audit not only aims to refine individual characters but also to enhance the narrative as a whole, ensuring that the story captivates its audience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the author to understand their vision for the characters and any specific concerns they may have regarding character consistency.
  
- **Manuscript Review**: Perform a comprehensive read-through of the manuscript, focusing on character development, arcs, motivations, and interactions.
  
- **Character Analysis**: Identify inconsistencies in character behavior, dialogue, and development. This includes assessing character motivations and how they align with the overall narrative.
  
- **Feedback Report**: Prepare a detailed feedback report that includes:
  - A summary of findings regarding character consistency.
  - Specific examples from the manuscript where characters may feel underdeveloped or contradictory.
  - Actionable suggestions for improvement to enhance character authenticity and relatability.
  
- **Follow-Up Consultation**: Schedule a follow-up meeting with the author to discuss the feedback report and answer any questions.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and agreed-upon objectives.

- **Milestone 2**: Manuscript Review Completed
  - Deliverable: Completed read-through of the manuscript with notes.

- **Milestone 3**: Character Analysis Completed
  - Deliverable: Draft of character analysis findings.

- **Milestone 4**: Feedback Report Delivered
  - Deliverable: Final feedback report with detailed analysis and suggestions.

- **Milestone 5**: Follow-Up Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of follow-up discussion and any additional recommendations.
