
# Project Plan Template: Series Continuity Check

## 1. Executive Summary

The Series Continuity Check project aims to provide a specialized beta reading service for authors engaged in multi-book series. The primary objective is to ensure that all elements of the narrative remain consistent throughout the series, thereby enhancing the overall quality and coherence of the final product. This project will involve a meticulous review of manuscripts, focusing on recurring characters, plotlines, and world-building elements. Key outcomes include detailed feedback on character development, plot arcs, and thematic consistency, which will help authors identify and rectify any discrepancies or contradictions that may confuse readers. By delivering comprehensive and constructive feedback, the Series Continuity Check will empower authors to create a polished and engaging reading experience, ultimately increasing reader satisfaction and loyalty.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Series Continuity Check includes the following key activities:

- **Initial Consultation**: Engage with the author to understand the series' overarching themes, character arcs, and specific concerns regarding continuity.
  
- **Manuscript Review**: Conduct a thorough reading of each manuscript in the series, focusing on:
  - Character consistency (development, traits, and arcs)
  - Plot continuity (events, timelines, and resolutions)
  - World-building elements (settings, rules, and lore)
  
- **Feedback Compilation**: Provide detailed notes and suggestions for each manuscript, highlighting:
  - Areas of inconsistency or confusion
  - Recommendations for improvement
  - Observations on character and plot development
  
- **Follow-Up Consultation**: Schedule a meeting with the author to discuss feedback, clarify any points, and outline next steps for revisions.

- **Final Review**: Optionally, conduct a second round of feedback after the author has made revisions to ensure continuity is maintained.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of author’s objectives and concerns.

- **Milestone 2**: Manuscript Review Completed
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive feedback document for each manuscript.

- **Milestone 3**: Follow-Up Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and action items for revisions.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Review (if applicable)
  - Deliverable: Updated feedback document post-revisions.
