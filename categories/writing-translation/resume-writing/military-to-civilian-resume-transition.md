
# Project Plan Template: Military-to-Civilian Resume Transition

## 1. Executive Summary

The Military-to-Civilian Resume Transition project aims to empower veterans by transforming their military experiences into compelling civilian job qualifications. The primary objective is to provide a comprehensive service that includes an in-depth analysis of the client's military background, identification of transferable skills, and the creation of a tailored resume that resonates with civilian employers. This project will involve a detailed consultation to understand the client's career aspirations and the specific industries they wish to enter. Key outcomes include a professionally crafted resume optimized for applicant tracking systems (ATS) and designed to enhance the client's chances of securing interviews. By focusing on relevant experiences, skills, and achievements, this project not only aims to improve job prospects for veterans but also to instill confidence in their ability to navigate the civilian job market.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a one-on-one consultation with the client to discuss their military background, career goals, and target industries.
  
- **Assessment of Military Experience**: Analyze the client's military service record to identify transferable skills and relevant experiences that align with civilian job requirements.

- **Resume Drafting**: Create a tailored resume that highlights the client's unique qualifications, including:
  - Relevant military roles and responsibilities
  - Transferable skills applicable to civilian jobs
  - Achievements and recognitions received during service

- **ATS Optimization**: Incorporate industry-specific keywords and phrases to ensure the resume is optimized for applicant tracking systems, increasing visibility to potential employers.

- **Review and Revisions**: Provide the client with a draft of the resume for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.

- **Final Delivery**: Deliver the final version of the resume in both PDF and Word formats, along with a cover letter template tailored to the client's job search.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Consultation notes and agreed-upon career goals document

- **Milestone 2**: Military Experience Assessment Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of transferable skills and relevant experiences

- **Milestone 3**: Resume Draft Completed
  - Deliverable: First draft of the tailored resume

- **Milestone 4**: Client Review and Feedback
  - Deliverable: Client feedback document

- **Milestone 5**: Final Resume Delivered
  - Deliverable: Final resume in PDF and Word formats, along with a cover letter template
