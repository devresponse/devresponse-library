
# Project Plan Template: Resume for Retail and Customer Service Roles

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of this project is to create tailored resumes for professionals in the retail and customer service sectors, ensuring that their unique skills and experiences are effectively showcased. This project will focus on highlighting key achievements such as sales performance, customer satisfaction metrics, and problem-solving abilities that demonstrate the candidate's value to potential employers. The project will begin with a thorough consultation to gather relevant information from the client, allowing us to understand their career goals and experiences. Following this, we will craft a professional resume that adheres to industry standards, ensuring it is visually appealing and optimized with keywords to pass through Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). The key outcomes of this project include a polished resume that stands out in a competitive job market, significantly increasing the chances of landing interviews and job offers for our clients.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct an initial consultation with the client to gather information about their work history, skills, and career aspirations.
  - Discuss specific achievements in sales and customer service that should be highlighted.

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Analyze industry standards for resumes in the retail and customer service sectors.
  - Identify relevant keywords and phrases that are commonly used in job descriptions within these fields.

- **Resume Development**: 
  - Create a draft of the resume that includes:
    - Contact information
    - Professional summary
    - Work experience
    - Education
    - Skills section
    - Achievements and metrics
  - Ensure the resume is visually appealing and formatted correctly.

- **Review and Revision**: 
  - Present the draft to the client for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on client input.

- **Finalization**: 
  - Deliver the final version of the resume in both PDF and Word formats.
  - Provide tips for optimizing the resume for ATS and preparing for interviews.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Completed client questionnaire and notes from the consultation.

- **Milestone 2**: Research and Analysis
  - Deliverable: List of industry-standard keywords and phrases.

- **Milestone 3**: Draft Resume Creation
  - Deliverable: First draft of the resume for client review.

- **Milestone 4**: Client Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised resume based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Resume Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized resume in PDF and Word formats, along with ATS optimization tips.
