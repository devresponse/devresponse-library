
# Project Plan Template: Academic CV to Corporate Resume Conversion

## 1. Executive Summary

The objective of the "Academic CV to Corporate Resume Conversion" project is to assist individuals transitioning from academia to the corporate sector by transforming their detailed academic CVs into streamlined corporate resumes. This project aims to highlight relevant skills and experiences that align with corporate job requirements, ensuring that clients can effectively present their qualifications to potential employers. The process begins with a comprehensive review of the existing academic CV, identifying key achievements and skills that resonate with corporate roles. The final resume will be crafted using results-oriented language and modern formatting, incorporating industry-standard practices to enhance visibility in applicant tracking systems. Key outcomes include a tailored resume that captures the attention of hiring managers, increased confidence for clients in their job applications, and a clear pathway for transitioning into corporate roles. This transformation is essential for individuals seeking to make a successful shift in their careers, ensuring they stand out in a competitive job market.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to discuss their career goals, target positions, and specific requirements for the corporate resume.
  
- **Review of Existing CV**: Analyze the client's academic CV to identify relevant skills, experiences, and achievements that can be highlighted in the corporate resume.

- **Resume Drafting**: Create a first draft of the corporate resume, focusing on:
  - Results-oriented language
  - Modern and professional layout
  - Industry-standard formatting
  - Keyword optimization for applicant tracking systems

- **Client Feedback**: Present the draft to the client for feedback and suggestions for revisions.

- **Revisions**: Make necessary adjustments based on client feedback to ensure the final product meets their expectations.

- **Final Review**: Conduct a final review of the resume to ensure accuracy, clarity, and professionalism.

- **Delivery of Final Resume**: Provide the client with the final version of the corporate resume in both PDF and Word formats.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Meeting notes and agreed-upon objectives

- **Milestone 2**: CV Review Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of key achievements and skills identified

- **Milestone 3**: First Draft of Corporate Resume Completed
  - Deliverable: Draft version of the corporate resume

- **Milestone 4**: Client Feedback Received
  - Deliverable: Documented feedback from the client

- **Milestone 5**: Final Resume Delivered
  - Deliverable: Final corporate resume in PDF and Word formats
