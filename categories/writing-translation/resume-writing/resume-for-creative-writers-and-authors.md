
# Project Plan Template: Resume for Creative Writers and Authors

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Resume for Creative Writers and Authors" project aims to develop a compelling and tailored resume that effectively highlights the unique voice and style of creative professionals in the writing industry. The primary objective is to create a resume that not only showcases the client's published works, writing awards, and notable creative projects but also aligns with their career aspirations. Through an in-depth consultation, we will gather insights into the client's writing style, key achievements, and career goals. The project will culminate in a polished resume that employs strategic keyword optimization to enhance visibility in applicant tracking systems, ensuring that the client stands out in a competitive job market. Key outcomes include a professionally crafted resume that communicates the client's qualifications and enhances their chances of securing desirable opportunities in their field.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a comprehensive meeting with the client to discuss their career goals, writing style, and key achievements. This will involve:
  - Understanding the client's target job market.
  - Gathering information on published works and awards.
  - Discussing the client's unique voice and style preferences.

- **Research and Analysis**: Analyze industry standards for resumes in the creative writing field, including:
  - Reviewing successful resumes of similar professionals.
  - Identifying relevant keywords and phrases for optimization.

- **Draft Resume Creation**: Develop a first draft of the resume that includes:
  - A tailored summary that reflects the client's unique voice.
  - Sections for education, experience, publications, and awards.
  - Strategic keyword integration for applicant tracking systems.

- **Client Review and Feedback**: Present the draft to the client for feedback, allowing for:
  - Revisions based on client input.
  - Adjustments to better reflect the client's style and preferences.

- **Finalization**: Deliver the final polished resume, ensuring:
  - Formatting is professional and visually appealing.
  - All elements are optimized for both human readers and applicant tracking systems.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completion
  - Deliverable: Summary of client insights and goals.

- **Milestone 2**: Research and Analysis Completion
  - Deliverable: Document outlining industry standards and keyword analysis.

- **Milestone 3**: Draft Resume Submission
  - Deliverable: First draft of the resume for client review.

- **Milestone 4**: Client Feedback and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised resume based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Resume Delivery
  - Deliverable: Polished resume in multiple formats (PDF, Word).
