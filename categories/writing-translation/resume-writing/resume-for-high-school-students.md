
# Project Plan Template: Resume for High School Students

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Resume for High School Students" project aims to create tailored resumes that empower high school students seeking internships, part-time employment, or admission into college programs. The primary objective is to highlight each student's unique academic achievements, relevant coursework, and extracurricular activities, ensuring they stand out to potential employers and admissions committees. This project will involve a comprehensive consultation process to gather essential information about the student's experiences and aspirations. The final deliverable will be a professionally crafted resume that adheres to industry standards, optimized for clarity and impact. Key outcomes include increased confidence for students in their job applications and a higher likelihood of securing interviews and opportunities. By providing a polished resume, we aim to enhance the students' ability to effectively communicate their strengths and potential, ultimately supporting their career and educational aspirations.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Schedule a one-on-one consultation with each student to discuss their academic background, extracurricular activities, work experience, and career aspirations.
  - Gather relevant information through a structured questionnaire to ensure all pertinent details are captured.

- **Resume Development**:
  - Create a tailored resume for each student that includes sections such as contact information, objective statement, education, relevant coursework, extracurricular activities, and skills.
  - Utilize effective layout design principles to ensure the resume is visually appealing and easy to read.
  - Incorporate strategic keyword placement to enhance visibility and alignment with job descriptions or college admission criteria.

- **Review and Revisions**:
  - Provide the initial draft of the resume to the student for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on the student's input to ensure satisfaction with the final product.

- **Final Delivery**:
  - Deliver the final resume in both PDF and editable formats.
  - Offer tips for using the resume effectively in job applications and interviews.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Completed questionnaire and notes from the consultation.

- **Milestone 2**: First Draft of Resume
  - Deliverable: Initial resume draft provided to the student for review.

- **Milestone 3**: Final Resume Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized resume in PDF and editable formats.

- **Milestone 4**: Follow-Up Consultation
  - Deliverable: A follow-up session to discuss resume usage and job application strategies.
