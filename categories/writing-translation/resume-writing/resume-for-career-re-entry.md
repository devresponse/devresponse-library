
# Project Plan Template: Resume for Career Re-Entry

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Resume for Career Re-Entry" project aims to assist individuals who are looking to re-enter the workforce after a career break by crafting a compelling and professionally tailored resume. The primary objective is to understand the unique backgrounds of clients, including their skills, volunteer experiences, and any certifications acquired during their time away from the job market. This project will involve a comprehensive consultation process to gather relevant information and insights about the client’s career aspirations. Key outcomes include the delivery of a polished resume that highlights transferable skills and relevant experiences, ensuring alignment with the client's desired job roles. Additionally, the resume will be optimized with industry-specific keywords to enhance its visibility in Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), thereby increasing the chances of catching the attention of hiring managers. Ultimately, this project seeks to empower clients to make a strong first impression and successfully navigate their return to the workforce.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: Conduct an in-depth consultation with the client to gather information about their career history, skills, volunteer work, and any certifications obtained during their career break.
- **Research and Analysis**: Analyze the job market and identify key skills and qualifications relevant to the client’s desired job roles.
- **Resume Drafting**: Create a first draft of the resume that emphasizes transferable skills, relevant experiences, and aligns with the client's career objectives.
- **Keyword Optimization**: Incorporate industry-specific keywords to ensure the resume is ATS-friendly and increases visibility to hiring managers.
- **Client Review**: Present the draft to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Finalization**: Deliver the final polished resume in both digital and print formats, ensuring it meets professional standards.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client’s background and career goals.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Resume Draft Completed
  - Deliverable: First draft of the resume for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback Received
  - Deliverable: Documented feedback and requested changes.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Resume Delivered
  - Deliverable: Polished resume in digital and print formats.
