
# Project Plan Template: Resume for Skilled Trades Workers

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Resume for Skilled Trades Workers" project aims to create tailored resumes for individuals in skilled trades such as electricians, plumbers, and carpenters. The primary objective is to develop professional resumes that effectively highlight the unique skills, qualifications, and experiences of each tradesperson. This project will involve a comprehensive process of gathering detailed information about the individual's work history, certifications, technical skills, and notable projects. Key outcomes include the delivery of visually appealing and industry-standard resumes that are optimized for applicant tracking systems (ATS) through the incorporation of relevant keywords. By enhancing the visibility of these resumes, we aim to increase the chances of our clients securing interviews and ultimately landing positions that align with their expertise. This service is crucial for tradespeople looking to differentiate themselves in a competitive job market, ensuring they present their qualifications in the best possible light.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Client Consultation**: Conduct an initial consultation with each tradesperson to understand their career goals, work history, and specific skills.
- **Information Gathering**: Collect detailed information including:
  - Work history (job titles, employers, dates of employment)
  - Certifications and licenses relevant to the trade
  - Technical skills and proficiencies
  - Notable projects and achievements
- **Resume Drafting**: Create a tailored resume draft that includes:
  - A professional summary
  - Skills section
  - Work experience section
  - Education and certifications section
- **Design and Formatting**: Ensure the resume is visually appealing and adheres to industry standards for readability and professionalism.
- **Keyword Optimization**: Research and incorporate relevant keywords to enhance ATS compatibility.
- **Client Review**: Present the draft to the client for feedback and revisions.
- **Finalization**: Make necessary adjustments based on client feedback and finalize the resume.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final resume in multiple formats (PDF, Word) as per client preference.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Client Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client needs and information gathered
- **Milestone 2**: Resume Draft Created
  - Deliverable: First draft of the tailored resume
- **Milestone 3**: Client Review Completed
  - Deliverable: Feedback received from the client
- **Milestone 4**: Final Resume Delivered
  - Deliverable: Finalized resume in requested formats
