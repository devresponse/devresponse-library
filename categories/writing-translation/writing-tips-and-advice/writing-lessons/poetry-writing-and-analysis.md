
# Project Plan Template: Poetry Writing and Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Poetry Writing and Analysis" project aims to provide a comprehensive course that immerses participants in the art of poetry. The primary objective is to equip individuals with the skills necessary to understand and create poetry, focusing on essential elements such as rhyme schemes, meter, and vivid imagery. This course will not only cover the technical aspects of poetry writing but also facilitate an in-depth analysis of famous poems, allowing learners to appreciate the techniques employed by renowned poets. Key outcomes include the development of personalized poetry portfolios, enhanced writing skills, and a deeper understanding of poetic forms and styles. By the end of the course, participants will have the confidence to express their creativity through poetry, whether they are beginners or seasoned writers looking to refine their craft.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the "Poetry Writing and Analysis" project includes the following components:

- **Course Development**: Create instructional materials covering various poetry elements, including:
  - Rhyme schemes
  - Meter
  - Imagery and symbolism
  - Poetic forms (sonnets, haikus, free verse, etc.)

- **Interactive Exercises**: Design engaging activities that encourage participants to practice writing poetry and analyzing existing works.

- **Famous Poem Analysis**: Curate a selection of renowned poems for in-depth analysis, focusing on techniques and themes.

- **Personalized Feedback**: Implement a system for providing constructive feedback on participants' poetry submissions.

- **Course Delivery**: Utilize an online platform for course delivery, ensuring accessibility and engagement.

- **Assessment**: Develop assessment criteria to evaluate participants' progress and understanding of the material.
