
# Project Plan Template: Journalistic Writing for Beginners

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Journalistic Writing for Beginners" project aims to provide aspiring journalists with a comprehensive understanding of the essential skills required in the field of journalism. This course will offer participants an in-depth exploration of news writing fundamentals, focusing on how to craft compelling stories that captivate and engage readers. Key objectives include teaching critical interviewing techniques, enabling students to effectively gather information and conduct insightful interviews. Furthermore, the course will emphasize ethical reporting, instilling the importance of integrity and accuracy in journalism. By blending theoretical knowledge with practical exercises, this project seeks to establish a strong foundation in journalistic practices, making it an ideal opportunity for individuals looking to embark on a journalism career or enhance their writing skills. The key outcomes will include improved writing proficiency, enhanced interviewing capabilities, and a solid understanding of ethical journalism standards.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the "Journalistic Writing for Beginners" project includes the following components:

- **Course Development**: Creation of a structured curriculum that covers the fundamentals of journalistic writing, interviewing techniques, and ethical reporting.
- **Content Creation**: Development of course materials, including lecture notes, reading materials, and practical exercises.
- **Workshops and Practical Sessions**: Organization of interactive workshops where participants can practice writing and interviewing skills in real-world scenarios.
- **Assessment and Feedback**: Implementation of assessments to evaluate participants' understanding and skills, along with providing constructive feedback.
- **Marketing and Enrollment**: Strategies to promote the course and manage participant enrollment, ensuring a diverse group of aspiring journalists.
- **Course Delivery**: Execution of the course through a combination of in-person and online sessions, accommodating various learning preferences.
