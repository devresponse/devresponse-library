
# Poetry Writing Workshop Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

The Poetry Writing Workshop is designed to provide an immersive experience for writers seeking to enhance their poetry skills through personalized mentorship. The primary objective of this project is to create a supportive environment where participants can explore the intricacies of poetic structure, rhythm, and imagery under the guidance of an experienced poetry mentor. This workshop will include interactive sessions featuring writing exercises, constructive feedback, and discussions on various poetic forms and techniques. Key outcomes include improved writing skills, a deeper understanding of poetic elements, and the development of each participant's unique voice and style. By the end of the workshop, participants will have produced a portfolio of original poetry and gained the confidence to continue their writing journey.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Poetry Writing Workshop includes the following components:

- **Mentorship Sessions**: One-on-one and group sessions with an experienced poetry mentor focusing on personalized guidance.
- **Writing Exercises**: Structured activities designed to stimulate creativity and encourage experimentation with different poetic forms.
- **Feedback Mechanism**: Regular opportunities for participants to share their work and receive constructive feedback from both the mentor and peers.
- **Discussion Topics**: Exploration of various poetic techniques, styles, and historical contexts to enrich participants' understanding of poetry.
- **Portfolio Development**: Assistance in compiling a portfolio of original poetry created during the workshop.
- **Final Showcase**: An event where participants can present their work to an audience, fostering a sense of community and accomplishment.
