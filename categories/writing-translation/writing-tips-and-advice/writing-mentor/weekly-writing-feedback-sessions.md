
# Project Plan Template: Weekly Writing Feedback Sessions

## 1. Executive Summary

The Weekly Writing Feedback Sessions project aims to provide participants with personalized, in-depth critiques of their writing on a consistent weekly basis. Each participant will submit a piece of writing—whether it be an article, essay, story, or other formats—and receive comprehensive feedback from an experienced writing mentor. The primary objective is to enhance the participants' writing skills by focusing on various critical aspects, including style, tone, structure, grammar, and overall effectiveness. Key outcomes of this project include improved writing proficiency, the development of a unique voice, and increased confidence in writing abilities. By engaging in these sessions, participants will embark on a transformative writing journey, gaining the necessary guidance and support to succeed in their writing endeavors.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Weekly Writing Feedback Sessions includes the following components:

- **Participant Enrollment**: Open registration for aspiring authors, professionals, and anyone looking to improve their writing skills.
- **Weekly Writing Submission**: Participants will submit one piece of writing each week by {{submission_deadline}}.
- **Feedback Process**: Each submission will be reviewed by a designated writing mentor, who will provide detailed feedback covering:
  - Style and tone
  - Structure and organization
  - Grammar and mechanics
  - Overall effectiveness and impact
- **Feedback Delivery**: Written feedback will be provided to participants within {{feedback_turnaround_time}} days of submission.
- **Follow-Up Sessions**: Optional follow-up discussions will be available to clarify feedback and discuss further improvements.
- **Progress Tracking**: Participants will have access to a progress tracking system to monitor their development over time.

The pricing structure for the Weekly Writing Feedback Sessions is as follows:

- **Session Fee**: Each participant will be charged {{session_fee}} per week for the feedback sessions.
- **Payment Terms**: Payments are due at the beginning of each month for the upcoming sessions. Participants can choose to pay for a single session or opt for a discounted package for {{number_of_sessions}} sessions.
