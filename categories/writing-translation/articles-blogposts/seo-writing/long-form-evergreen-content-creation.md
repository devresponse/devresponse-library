
# Project Plan Template: Long-Form Evergreen Content Creation

## 1. Executive Summary

The Long-Form Evergreen Content Creation project aims to develop a series of meticulously crafted articles that remain relevant and valuable over time. The primary objective is to produce comprehensive, engaging content that adheres to best practices in SEO, ensuring high visibility in search engine results. This project will focus on thorough research and the incorporation of relevant keywords and phrases that align with current trends and user intent. By structuring the content for enhanced readability and user experience—utilizing headings, subheadings, and bullet points—we will create articles that not only attract readers but also encourage them to engage with the material. Additionally, we will implement strategic internal and external linking to boost authority and drive traffic. The key outcomes of this project include a set of well-optimized articles designed to attract consistent organic traffic, ultimately serving as a valuable asset for your online presence.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Conduct keyword research to identify high-traffic, relevant keywords.
  - Analyze competitor content to determine gaps and opportunities.
  - Gather data and insights on current trends and user intent.

- **Content Creation**:
  - Develop a content outline for each article, including headings and subheadings.
  - Write comprehensive articles (minimum {{word_count}} words each) that are engaging and informative.
  - Ensure the content is optimized for SEO, incorporating identified keywords naturally.

- **Editing and Review**:
  - Perform thorough editing for grammar, style, and SEO compliance.
  - Conduct a peer review process to ensure quality and accuracy.

- **Implementation of Linking Strategies**:
  - Identify opportunities for internal linking to existing content.
  - Include external links to authoritative sources to enhance credibility.

- **Final Delivery**:
  - Provide a set of finalized articles in a format suitable for publication (e.g., Google Docs, Word).
  - Include a summary report detailing keyword strategies, linking strategies, and expected outcomes.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Research report including keyword analysis and competitor insights.

- **Milestone 2**: Submission of Content Outlines
  - Deliverable: Outlines for each article, including proposed headings and subheadings.

- **Milestone 3**: Draft Articles Completion
  - Deliverable: First drafts of all articles for review.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Edits and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Edited articles ready for publication.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Set of finalized articles and summary report.
