
# Project Plan Template: Interview Preparation and Question Design

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Interview Preparation and Question Design" project aims to elevate the quality of podcast interviews through meticulous preparation and tailored question design. The primary objective is to create an engaging and insightful experience for both the podcast host and the audience. This project will begin with an in-depth consultation to understand the podcast's theme, target audience, and specific goals of the interview. Based on this understanding, a customized set of compelling interview questions will be crafted to elicit thoughtful responses from guests. Additionally, a preparation guide will be provided for the host, offering tips on creating a comfortable environment, managing interview pacing, and effectively following up on responses. Key outcomes include enhanced interview quality, improved audience engagement, and a more enriching overall podcast experience.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Consultation Phase**: 
  - Conduct an initial consultation with the podcast host to discuss the podcast's theme, target audience, and interview goals.
  - Gather information about potential guests and their backgrounds.

- **Question Design**:
  - Develop a tailored set of interview questions that align with the podcast's objectives and resonate with the target audience.
  - Ensure questions are open-ended to encourage thoughtful responses and natural conversation flow.

- **Preparation Guide Creation**:
  - Create a comprehensive preparation guide for the host, including:
    - Tips for creating a comfortable environment for guests.
    - Strategies for managing the pacing of the interview.
    - Techniques for effectively following up on guest responses.

- **Review and Feedback**:
  - Present the crafted questions and preparation guide to the host for feedback.
  - Make necessary revisions based on the host's input to ensure alignment with their vision.

- **Final Deliverables**:
  - Deliver the final set of interview questions and the preparation guide to the podcast host in a user-friendly format.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Summary of consultation notes and agreed-upon objectives.

- **Milestone 2**: Draft Interview Questions
  - Deliverable: Initial draft of tailored interview questions.

- **Milestone 3**: Preparation Guide Draft
  - Deliverable: Draft of the preparation guide for the host.

- **Milestone 4**: Review and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised interview questions and preparation guide based on host feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Delivery
  - Deliverable: Finalized interview questions and preparation guide delivered to the host.
