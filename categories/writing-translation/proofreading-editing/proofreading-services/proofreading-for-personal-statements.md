
# Project Plan Template: Proofreading for Personal Statements

## Executive Summary

The "Proofreading for Personal Statements" project aims to provide a comprehensive proofreading service tailored for individuals seeking to enhance their personal statements for college admissions or job applications. In today's competitive landscape, a well-crafted personal statement can significantly influence the success of an application. Our primary objective is to refine each document to ensure it is free from grammatical errors and typos while also ensuring that it resonates with the intended audience. The project will involve meticulous reviews focusing on clarity, coherence, and overall impact, allowing the unique voice of the applicant to shine through. Key outcomes include delivering a polished personal statement that adheres to specific application requirements, providing constructive feedback, and offering suggestions for improvement. Ultimately, our goal is to empower applicants to present their best selves, making their personal statements compelling and memorable.

## Detailed Scope of Work

1. **Initial Consultation**
   - Conduct a brief consultation with the client to understand their goals, target audience, and specific requirements for the personal statement.

2. **Document Submission**
   - Clients will submit their personal statements in a specified format (e.g., Word document, PDF).

3. **Proofreading Process**
   - Review the document for grammatical errors, typos, and punctuation issues.
   - Assess the overall structure and flow of the statement.
   - Evaluate the clarity and coherence of the content.
   - Ensure that the personal statement aligns with the specific requirements of the application.

4. **Feedback and Suggestions**
   - Provide detailed feedback on areas for improvement.
   - Suggest revisions to enhance clarity, impact, and engagement.

5. **Final Review**
   - After the client implements suggested changes, conduct a final review to ensure all feedback has been addressed.

6. **Delivery of Final Document**
   - Deliver the final, polished personal statement to the client in the agreed format.

## Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1: Initial Consultation Completed**
  - Deliverable: Summary of client goals and requirements.

- **Milestone 2: Document Submission Received**
  - Deliverable: Acknowledgment of receipt and timeline for proofreading.

- **Milestone 3: First Draft Proofreading Completed**
  - Deliverable: Proofread document with comments and suggestions.

- **Milestone 4: Client Feedback Received**
  - Deliverable: Summary of client revisions and additional requests.

- **Milestone 5: Final Review Completed**
  - Deliverable: Final polished personal statement delivered to the client.
