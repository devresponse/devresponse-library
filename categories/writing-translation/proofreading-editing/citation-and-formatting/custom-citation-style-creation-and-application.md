
# Project Plan Template: Custom Citation Style Creation and Application

## 1. Executive Summary

The Custom Citation Style Creation and Application project aims to develop a tailored citation style that meets the unique requirements of {{Organization Name}}. In the realm of academic and professional writing, adherence to specific citation styles is crucial for maintaining credibility and professionalism. This project will involve a comprehensive analysis of existing materials, followed by the creation of a custom style guide that aligns with the organization's objectives and preferences. Our expert team will collaborate closely with stakeholders to ensure that all specific guidelines are incorporated into the new citation style. Key outcomes of this project include a fully functional citation style, a detailed style guide, and the application of the new format to existing documents. By the end of the project, {{Organization Name}} will benefit from enhanced consistency and accuracy in its written work, ultimately elevating the quality and professionalism of its publications.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with stakeholders to gather requirements and understand specific citation preferences.
- **Review of Existing Materials**: Analyze current documents to identify citation inconsistencies and areas for improvement.
- **Development of Custom Citation Style**: Create a tailored citation style that adheres to the identified requirements and guidelines.
- **Creation of Style Guide**: Develop a comprehensive style guide that outlines the rules and examples of the new citation format.
- **Application of Citation Style**: Implement the new citation style across existing documents, ensuring consistency and accuracy.
- **Feedback and Revisions**: Present the new style guide and applied documents to stakeholders for feedback and make necessary revisions.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the final style guide and updated documents to {{Organization Name}}.
