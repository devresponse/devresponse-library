
# Project Plan Template: Chicago Style Footnote and Bibliography Formatting

## 1. Executive Summary

The Chicago Style Footnote and Bibliography Formatting project aims to provide expert assistance in ensuring that all footnotes and bibliographies in your document are formatted according to the Chicago Manual of Style. This project is essential for individuals in the humanities, particularly in fields such as history, literature, and the arts, where accurate citation is crucial for maintaining academic integrity and credibility. Our team of experienced professionals will meticulously review your document, focusing on the correct formatting of footnotes and the bibliography, including consistency in citation formats, proper punctuation, and the correct order of entries. The key outcomes of this project include a polished and compliant document that enhances the professionalism of your work and allows readers to easily locate your sources. By choosing our service, you will ensure that your references are presented accurately, thereby elevating the quality of your academic writing.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for this project includes the following tasks:

- **Initial Document Review**: Assess the document to identify areas requiring citation formatting.
- **Footnote Formatting**: Ensure that all footnotes are formatted according to the Chicago Manual of Style, including:
  - Correct placement and numbering of footnotes.
  - Consistency in citation formats across all footnotes.
  - Proper punctuation and style adherence.
  
- **Bibliography Formatting**: Review and format the bibliography to meet Chicago style guidelines, including:
  - Correct order of entries (alphabetical by author’s last name).
  - Consistency in citation formats for all sources.
  - Proper punctuation and formatting for different types of sources (books, articles, websites, etc.).

- **Final Review and Quality Assurance**: Conduct a thorough review of the formatted document to ensure all guidelines have been met and that the document is polished and ready for submission.
