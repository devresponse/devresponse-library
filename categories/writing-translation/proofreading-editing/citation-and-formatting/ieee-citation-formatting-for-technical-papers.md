
# Project Plan Template: IEEE Citation Formatting for Technical Papers

## 1. Executive Summary

The IEEE Citation Formatting for Technical Papers project aims to provide comprehensive formatting services to ensure that technical papers adhere to the stringent IEEE citation standards. The primary objective is to enhance the credibility and professionalism of academic and technical documents by meticulously reviewing and formatting citations and references. This project will involve a thorough examination of the manuscript to ensure that all citations are correctly numbered and formatted according to IEEE guidelines. Key outcomes include a well-organized reference list that meets IEEE style requirements, detailed feedback on citation accuracy, and corrections that improve the overall clarity and impact of the paper. By the end of this project, clients will receive a polished manuscript that not only meets necessary standards but also effectively communicates their research findings.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Review of Manuscript**: Conduct a comprehensive review of the submitted technical paper to identify all citations and references.
- **Citation Formatting**: Ensure that all in-text citations are correctly numbered and formatted according to IEEE standards.
- **Reference List Organization**: Organize the reference list to comply with IEEE style, ensuring that all sources are accurately represented and formatted.
- **Feedback and Corrections**: Provide detailed feedback on citation accuracy and suggest corrections to enhance clarity and impact.
- **Final Review**: Conduct a final review of the formatted manuscript to ensure compliance with IEEE guidelines before delivery.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Manuscript Submission
  - **Deliverable**: Receipt of the manuscript for review.
  
- **Milestone 2**: First Review Completion
  - **Deliverable**: Completed review with initial feedback on citations and references.

- **Milestone 3**: Formatting Implementation
  - **Deliverable**: Formatted manuscript with citations and reference list organized according to IEEE standards.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Feedback and Corrections
  - **Deliverable**: Detailed feedback report and corrections provided to the client.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Submission
  - **Deliverable**: Delivery of the final formatted manuscript to the client.
