## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Turabian Style Thesis Formatting project includes the following tasks:

- **Initial Consultation**: Discuss the specific requirements and expectations for the thesis formatting.
- **Document Review**: Conduct a thorough review of the thesis document to identify areas needing formatting adjustments.
- **Formatting Adjustments**:
  - Title Page: Ensure proper title, subtitle, author name, and institutional affiliation are formatted correctly.
  - Table of Contents: Create or adjust the table of contents to reflect accurate page numbers and headings.
  - Main Body: Format headings, subheadings, and text according to Turabian guidelines.
  - Footnotes and Endnotes: Ensure all citations are correctly formatted and placed according to Turabian style.
  - Bibliography: Compile and format the bibliography to meet Turabian standards.
  - Appendices: Format any appendices as required by the Turabian style guide.
- **Final Review**: Conduct a final review of the formatted document to ensure all elements comply with Turabian standards.
- **Delivery**: Provide the final formatted thesis document in the agreed-upon format (e.g., Word, PDF).
