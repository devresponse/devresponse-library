
# Project Plan Template: MLA Annotated Bibliography Formatting

## 1. Executive Summary

The MLA Annotated Bibliography Formatting project aims to provide a comprehensive service for students, researchers, and professionals seeking to create meticulously formatted annotated bibliographies in accordance with the Modern Language Association (MLA) style guidelines. The primary objective of this project is to ensure that all citations are accurately formatted, including proper punctuation, italics, and hanging indents, while also providing concise annotations that summarize the content and relevance of each source. Key outcomes include a polished final product that adheres to academic and professional standards, enhancing the credibility and scholarly value of the client's work. By delivering a well-structured bibliography, this project will not only save clients time but also improve their understanding of MLA formatting, ultimately contributing to their academic success.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research and Collection of Sources**: Gather the necessary sources provided by the client or recommended based on the research topic.
- **Citation Formatting**: Format each source according to MLA guidelines, ensuring correct punctuation, italics, and hanging indents.
- **Annotation Creation**: Write concise annotations for each source, summarizing the content and relevance to the overall research topic.
- **Review and Revision**: Conduct a thorough review of the formatted bibliography and annotations, making necessary revisions to ensure clarity and adherence to MLA standards.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the client with a polished annotated bibliography in a specified format (e.g., Word document, PDF).
- **Client Feedback**: Allow for client feedback and make any final adjustments as needed.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation
  - Deliverable: Summary of client requirements and source materials.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Source Formatting
  - Deliverable: Draft of formatted citations for client review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Annotation Development
  - Deliverable: Draft of annotations for each source.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Review and Revisions
  - Deliverable: Revised annotated bibliography based on client feedback.
  
- **Milestone 5**: Final Submission
  - Deliverable: Completed annotated bibliography delivered to the client.
