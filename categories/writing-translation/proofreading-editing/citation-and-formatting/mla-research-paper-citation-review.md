
# MLA Research Paper Citation Review Project Plan

## Executive Summary
The MLA Research Paper Citation Review project aims to provide comprehensive support for students and professionals who require meticulous attention to detail in their research papers. The primary objective is to ensure that all citations, both in-text and on the works cited page, are formatted according to the latest MLA guidelines. This project will involve a thorough examination of submitted papers, identifying and correcting any citation errors while also offering guidance on best practices for citation. Key outcomes include enhanced credibility of the research work, adherence to academic standards, and a detailed report that highlights corrections made along with suggestions for future citation practices. By focusing on precision and integrity, this project guarantees that clients' research papers will meet the expectations of their audience and uphold the standards of scholarly work.

## Detailed Scope of Work
1. **Initial Consultation**: 
   - Discuss project requirements with the client.
   - Gather necessary documents and papers for review.

2. **Citation Review**:
   - Conduct a thorough examination of in-text citations and the works cited page.
   - Identify errors in formatting according to the latest MLA guidelines.

3. **Correction Process**:
   - Correct identified errors in citations.
   - Ensure consistency and accuracy throughout the document.

4. **Best Practices Guidance**:
   - Provide clients with guidance on best practices for citation.
   - Offer tips for maintaining citation integrity in future works.

5. **Final Report**:
   - Prepare a detailed report summarizing corrections made.
   - Include suggestions for improving citation practices in future research.

## Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completion
  - Deliverable: Summary of client requirements and documents received.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Citation Review Completion
  - Deliverable: List of identified errors and issues in citations.

- **Milestone 3**: Correction Process Completion
  - Deliverable: Revised document with corrected citations.

- **Milestone 4**: Best Practices Guidance Provided
  - Deliverable: Document outlining best practices for citation.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Report Submission
  - Deliverable: Comprehensive report detailing corrections and future suggestions.
