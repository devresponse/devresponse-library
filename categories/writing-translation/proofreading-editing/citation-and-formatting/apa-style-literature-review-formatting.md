
# Project Plan Template: APA Style Literature Review Formatting

## 1. Executive Summary

The APA Style Literature Review Formatting project aims to provide a comprehensive service that ensures your literature review adheres to the American Psychological Association (APA) style guidelines. The primary objective is to meticulously format your document, focusing on critical elements such as in-text citations, reference lists, and overall document structure. This project will enhance the credibility and professionalism of your academic work, making it ready for submission. Key outcomes include a thoroughly reviewed document that meets all APA requirements, a polished final product, and a collaborative approach that ensures your research findings are effectively communicated. By the end of this project, you will have a literature review that not only complies with APA standards but also reflects the quality of your research.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of this project includes the following tasks:

- **Initial Consultation**: Discuss the specific requirements and expectations for the literature review formatting with the client.
- **Document Review**: Conduct a thorough review of the existing literature review document to identify areas needing formatting adjustments.
- **APA Formatting**: Apply APA style guidelines to the document, including:
  - Proper in-text citations
  - Structuring the reference list according to APA standards
  - Ensuring correct margins, font size, and headings
- **Final Review**: Perform a final check to ensure all formatting is consistent and compliant with APA guidelines.
- **Client Feedback**: Present the formatted document to the client for feedback and make necessary revisions based on their input.
- **Final Delivery**: Provide the polished final product, ready for submission.
