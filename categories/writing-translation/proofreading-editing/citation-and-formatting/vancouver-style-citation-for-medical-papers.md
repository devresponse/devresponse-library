
# Project Plan Template: Vancouver Style Citation for Medical Papers

## 1. Executive Summary

The Vancouver Style Citation for Medical Papers project aims to provide comprehensive formatting services to ensure that medical papers meet the stringent requirements of the Vancouver citation style. This project is designed for researchers and professionals in the medical and health sciences fields who seek to enhance the credibility of their work through meticulous citation practices. The primary objective is to ensure that all in-text citations are accurately numbered and correspond to a well-organized reference list, thereby upholding academic integrity. Key outcomes of this project include a fully formatted document that adheres to the Vancouver style guidelines, consistent presentation of headings, subheadings, and tables, and a final review to guarantee compliance with publication standards. By delivering these services, we aim to facilitate the successful publication of our clients' research in reputable journals, ultimately contributing to the advancement of knowledge in the medical community.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Initial Consultation**: Conduct a meeting with the client to understand specific requirements and expectations regarding the document.
- **Document Review**: Analyze the submitted medical paper for content, structure, and existing formatting.
- **Citation Formatting**: 
  - Ensure all in-text citations are numbered correctly according to the Vancouver style.
  - Organize the reference list in accordance with Vancouver guidelines.
- **Formatting Consistency**: 
  - Review and format headings, subheadings, and tables to ensure uniformity throughout the document.
  - Check for font consistency, line spacing, and margin settings as per the Vancouver style.
- **Final Review**: Conduct a thorough review of the entire document to ensure compliance with all Vancouver style requirements.
- **Client Feedback**: Present the formatted document to the client for feedback and make necessary adjustments.
- **Final Submission**: Deliver the final formatted document to the client in the agreed-upon format.
