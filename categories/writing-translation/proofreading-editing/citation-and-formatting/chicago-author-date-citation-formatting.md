
# Project Plan Template: Chicago Author-Date Citation Formatting

## 1. Executive Summary

The Chicago Author-Date Citation Formatting project aims to provide comprehensive formatting services for documents in accordance with the Chicago Author-Date citation style. This style is essential for academic writing, particularly in the sciences and social sciences, where precision and adherence to formatting guidelines are paramount. The project will involve a meticulous review of each document to ensure that all in-text citations and reference lists are formatted correctly, with particular attention to author names, publication dates, and titles. Additionally, our experts will ensure that the overall document complies with required formatting standards, including margins, font styles, and spacing. The key outcomes of this project include a polished document ready for submission or publication, enhanced professionalism, and assurance that the work meets the rigorous standards expected in academic and professional settings. By choosing our service, clients can focus on their research and writing, confident that their citation and formatting needs are in expert hands.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Chicago Author-Date Citation Formatting project includes the following tasks:

- **Document Review**: A thorough examination of the submitted document to identify areas requiring citation and formatting adjustments.
- **In-Text Citation Formatting**: Ensuring that all in-text citations adhere to the Chicago Author-Date style, including proper placement and formatting of author names and publication years.
- **Reference List Formatting**: Compiling and formatting the reference list according to Chicago guidelines, ensuring accuracy in author names, publication dates, titles, and other relevant details.
- **Overall Document Formatting**: Checking and adjusting the document’s overall formatting, including margins, font styles, line spacing, and any other specific requirements outlined by the client.
- **Final Quality Check**: Conducting a final review of the document to ensure all elements meet the Chicago Author-Date citation standards before delivery to the client.
