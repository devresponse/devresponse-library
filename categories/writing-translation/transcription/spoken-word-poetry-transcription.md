
# Project Plan Template: Spoken Word Poetry Transcription

## 1. Executive Summary

The Spoken Word Poetry Transcription project aims to provide a comprehensive transcription service for live or recorded spoken word poetry performances. The primary objective is to convert these performances into written format while preserving the unique rhythm, tone, and emotional nuances that define spoken word poetry. This project is essential for poets who wish to publish their work, share it with a broader audience, or maintain a record of their performances for future reference. Key outcomes include the delivery of polished written documents that are ready for publication or sharing, along with the incorporation of client feedback through necessary revisions. By ensuring clarity and readability in the final product, we aim to authentically convey the artist's voice and enhance the accessibility of their work. This project is fixed in scope, with clear expectations and timelines established to ensure timely delivery and client satisfaction.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of the Spoken Word Poetry Transcription project includes the following key activities:

- **Initial Consultation**: Meet with the client to understand their specific needs, preferences, and the context of the spoken word performance.
- **Transcription Process**: 
  - Listen to the recorded performance or attend the live event.
  - Transcribe the spoken word poetry, capturing not only the words but also the rhythm, tone, and emotional nuances.
  - Format the transcription for clarity and readability.
- **Review and Revisions**: 
  - Provide the initial draft to the client for feedback.
  - Incorporate any necessary revisions based on client input.
- **Final Delivery**: 
  - Deliver the polished written document in the agreed format (e.g., Word document, PDF).
  - Ensure the final product is ready for publication or sharing.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Initial Consultation Completed
  - Deliverable: Summary of client requirements and expectations.
  
- **Milestone 2**: Transcription Draft Completed
  - Deliverable: Initial draft of the transcription for client review.

- **Milestone 3**: Client Feedback Received
  - Deliverable: Documented feedback from the client.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Revisions Completed
  - Deliverable: Revised transcription based on client feedback.

- **Milestone 5**: Final Document Delivered
  - Deliverable: Polished written document ready for publication or sharing.
