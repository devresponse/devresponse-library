
# Project Plan Template: Journalistic Interview Transcription

## 1. Executive Summary
The Journalistic Interview Transcription project aims to provide high-quality transcription services for journalists conducting interviews. The primary objective is to convert audio recordings of interviews into accurate and coherent written documents that serve as essential resources for article writing and fact-checking. This project will ensure that journalists have reliable access to quotes and information, enhancing the integrity of their reporting. Key outcomes include the delivery of meticulously transcribed documents that adhere to strict guidelines for accuracy, including proper punctuation, speaker identification, and formatting. By facilitating this process, the project will enable journalists to focus on crafting compelling narratives while relying on precise transcriptions of their interviews. The final deliverables will be provided in a digital format, ensuring easy access and usability for the client.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work
- **Audio Review**: The transcriber will listen to the audio recordings of interviews to familiarize themselves with the content and context.
- **Transcription Process**: The transcriber will accurately convert spoken dialogue into written format, ensuring clarity and coherence.
- **Speaker Identification**: Each speaker will be clearly identified in the transcription to maintain context and accuracy.
- **Punctuation and Formatting**: The transcription will adhere to strict guidelines for punctuation and formatting to ensure readability.
- **Quality Assurance**: A quality check will be performed to verify the accuracy of the transcription before delivery.
- **Final Delivery**: The completed transcription will be delivered in a digital format, such as a Word document or PDF, as per the client's preference.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1**: Initial Audio Review Completion
  - **Deliverable**: Summary of key points from the audio recordings.
  
- **Milestone 2**: First Draft Transcription
  - **Deliverable**: First draft of the transcription for review by the client.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Quality Assurance Review
  - **Deliverable**: Revised transcription incorporating feedback from the client.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Delivery
  - **Deliverable**: Finalized transcription delivered in the agreed digital format.
