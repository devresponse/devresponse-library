
# Project Plan Template: Focus Group Transcription

## 1. Executive Summary

The Focus Group Transcription project aims to provide high-quality transcription services for recorded focus group discussions. The primary objective is to deliver an accurate and comprehensive written record that captures every participant's voice, ensuring that qualitative data can be effectively analyzed by researchers and organizations. This project will involve meticulous attention to detail, including speaker identification, timestamps, and relevant non-verbal cues, to enhance the understanding of the dialogue. Key outcomes include a formatted final document that allows clients to extract valuable insights and themes, ultimately supporting their research objectives and decision-making processes. By ensuring precision and clarity in the transcription, we aim to facilitate a deeper understanding of the discussions and contribute to the overall success of our clients' research initiatives.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

The scope of work for the Focus Group Transcription project includes the following key activities:

- **Audio Review**: Initial review of the recorded focus group discussions to understand context and identify any potential challenges in transcription.
- **Transcription Process**: 
  - Transcribing the audio recordings verbatim, ensuring that every participant's contributions are accurately captured.
  - Including speaker identification for clarity on who is speaking at any given time.
  - Adding timestamps at regular intervals to facilitate easy navigation through the document.
  - Noting relevant non-verbal cues (e.g., laughter, pauses) that may provide additional context to the dialogue.
- **Quality Assurance**: Implementing a thorough review process to ensure the accuracy and completeness of the transcription, including proofreading and editing.
- **Final Formatting**: Preparing the final document in a clear, organized format that enhances readability and usability for clients.
- **Delivery**: Providing the completed transcription document in the agreed-upon format (e.g., Word, PDF) within the specified timeline.
