
# Film Script Transcription Project Plan

## 1. Executive Summary
The Film Script Transcription project aims to provide high-quality transcription of spoken dialogue from films and television shows into a written script format. The primary objective is to create accurate subtitles and dubbing scripts that enhance accessibility and viewer understanding. This project will involve a meticulous transcription process that captures not only the dialogue but also relevant non-verbal cues and contextual elements, ensuring that the essence of the original performance is preserved. Key outcomes include the delivery of a clean, formatted script that adheres to industry standards, making it suitable for post-production processes. The final deliverable will be provided in a specified file format, ensuring compatibility with various editing software. Timely delivery and accuracy are paramount, as the scripts will be utilized for professional purposes, contributing to the overall quality and accessibility of the film or television content.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work
- **Transcription Process**: 
  - Listen to the audio of the film or television show.
  - Transcribe spoken dialogue verbatim, including pauses, interruptions, and non-verbal cues.
  - Ensure that the transcription captures the tone and context of the dialogue.
  
- **Formatting**:
  - Format the transcription according to industry standards for scripts.
  - Include character names, dialogue, and any relevant stage directions or notes.
  
- **Quality Assurance**:
  - Review the transcription for accuracy and completeness.
  - Conduct a second round of proofreading to ensure no errors are present.
  
- **Final Deliverables**:
  - Provide the final script in the specified file format (e.g., .docx, .pdf, .txt).
  - Ensure the script is compatible with common editing software used in post-production.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables
- **Milestone 1**: Initial Audio Review
  - Deliverable: Summary of audio content and transcription plan.
  
- **Milestone 2**: First Draft Transcription
  - Deliverable: Complete first draft of the transcription for review.
  
- **Milestone 3**: Quality Assurance Review
  - Deliverable: Revised transcription after quality checks and feedback.
  
- **Milestone 4**: Final Script Delivery
  - Deliverable: Clean, formatted script delivered in the specified file format.
