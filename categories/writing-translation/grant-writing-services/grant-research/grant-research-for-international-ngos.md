
# Project Plan Template: Grant Research for International NGOs

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Grant Research for International NGOs" project aims to conduct comprehensive research on global grant opportunities specifically tailored for international non-governmental organizations (NGOs). The primary objective is to identify cross-border funding sources and international development programs that align with the mission and objectives of the organization. This project will involve a thorough analysis of various funding databases, government and private sector grants, and philanthropic foundations that support international initiatives. Key outcomes will include a detailed report that outlines potential funding opportunities, eligibility criteria, application processes, and deadlines. By providing NGOs with a clear understanding of viable funding options, this project is crucial for expanding their funding base and enhancing their capacity to implement impactful programs across borders. Ultimately, the research will empower NGOs to strategically pursue funding opportunities that align with their goals and contribute to sustainable development.

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

- **Research Phase**: 
  - Identify and analyze global funding databases relevant to international NGOs.
  - Investigate government and private sector grants available for cross-border initiatives.
  - Explore philanthropic foundations that support international development programs.

- **Analysis Phase**: 
  - Evaluate the alignment of identified funding sources with the mission and objectives of the organization.
  - Assess eligibility criteria for each funding opportunity.
  - Compile information on application processes and deadlines.

- **Reporting Phase**: 
  - Develop a comprehensive report that includes:
    - A list of potential funding opportunities.
    - Detailed descriptions of eligibility criteria.
    - Step-by-step application processes.
    - Important deadlines for each funding source.

- **Review and Feedback**: 
  - Present the draft report to stakeholders for feedback.
  - Incorporate feedback and finalize the report.

## 3. Milestones and Deliverables

- **Milestone 1**: Completion of the Research Phase
  - Deliverable: Preliminary list of identified funding sources.

- **Milestone 2**: Completion of the Analysis Phase
  - Deliverable: Detailed analysis of funding opportunities, including eligibility criteria and application processes.

- **Milestone 3**: Draft Report Submission
  - Deliverable: Draft report for stakeholder review.

- **Milestone 4**: Final Report Submission
  - Deliverable: Final comprehensive report outlining funding opportunities, eligibility, application processes, and deadlines.
